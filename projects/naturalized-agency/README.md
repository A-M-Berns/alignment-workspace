# Naturalized agency

This line asks for formal tools that, given a description of a world with no agents
marked in it, say where the loci of agency are and what their parts are for, and for
a theorem bounding how far two such descriptions of one system can disagree. Its
notions of function and purpose follow the organisational account (a part's function is
its contribution to maintaining a whole that in turn maintains the part), and its
notion of an aim follows Dennett's intentional strategy.

The line's name is provisional.
