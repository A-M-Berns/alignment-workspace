# Interpretation

**Superseded-by:** [Normativity wiki](https://github.com/A-M-Berns/alignment-workspace/wiki/Normativity).

**Non-authoritative.** Nothing here is cited by a theory part, and nothing here
is a result. This is the three-zone map: what is built, what is scoped but not
built, and what is unscoped. Design commentary lives here and nowhere else.

## Zone I — built

What this package actually contains, as an inventory rather than a summary.

**A settlement interface as a formal object.** Eighteen clause texts, twenty
predicates, each checkable over a finite record prefix or carried as a named
hypothesis object. A reference engine satisfying every checkable clause, so the
checkable fragment is known to be jointly satisfiable rather than hoped to be.

**A finite-time measure for the tolerance clause.** The incoherence functional,
with a normalization forced by duality rather than stipulated, its two sanity
theorems, a tight certificate on a displayed instance, and the guard showing why
it must be measured against logic-plus-settlement rather than against the book.

**The core condition under settlement.** The ambient reading displayed failing
at every positive coefficient; the relative reading, its linear reformulation,
the per-date program that reformulation buys, and the transport lemma across
confirmed settlements. The dependent-settlement failure, displayed.

**A composite that consumes the interface.** The correspondence with the
previous consolidation's condition bundle, including its three uncovered
conditions and the twelve clauses it never needed; the composite restated over
any engine satisfying the relevant clauses, with thirteen hypothesis objects
each individually load-bearing; and the instance corollary with its three named
conditionals.

**Constructions.** A funded empirical channel end to end, the adequacy
inequality proved for it, conduct witnesses each paired with a control, and a
two-engine instance with disjoint jurisdictions, breach isolation, and a fenced
composed purse.

**The four earlier layers**, restated folder-locally: the objection grammar's
footprint discipline; diachronic identity across migration, composition,
transport and the ledger; practical demand; and joint composition with its
repaired tightening.

## Zone II — scoped, not built

Ordered by dispatchability: the first could be started tomorrow, the last needs
a decision first.

1. **Registry completeness for the objection grammar.** A finite program over a
   finite registry. Nothing conceptual is missing; it is work.
2. **Higher-dimensional sharpness for the movement cap.** The vertex formulation
   already generalizes; only the exercised instances are scalar.
3. **A minimal constructed instance of the audited pair.** Enough of a market
   over a declared process to evaluate the interface predicates against. This is
   the highest-value item in the zone, because it moves the package's strongest
   evidence out of its weakest class.
4. **The persistence question**, as a search rather than a proof: a systematic
   sweep over settlement trajectories, looking for a structural condition
   separating the trajectories that preserve the coefficient from those that
   collapse it.
5. **Multi-engine generalization** beyond two.
6. **The coherence modulus**, which needs a genuine idea rather than more
   searching, and may be impossible.
7. **The deduction budget**, which needs the acceleration-versus-attention fork
   decided before any formalization is meaningful.

## Zone III — unscoped

Stated honestly: nothing in this package bears on these, and no work here is
evidence about them.

**Whether the interface is the right interface.** Every result is of the form
*given an engine satisfying these clauses*. That the clauses carve the right
joint is supported only by the observation that existing frameworks inhabit
different subsets of them — suggestive, and not an argument.

**Whether the two assumed residues are the right two.** Procedural faithfulness
and checker soundness are argued to be unavoidable, from inside the mechanism by
record extensionality and from outside by the deference results. Neither argument
is formalized here.

**Whether any of this bears on alignment.** The package is accounting: it says
what a record must show. It contains no behavioural, convergence, incentive, or
learning claim, and it would be a mistake to read the composite's cap as a
safety property. It limits what a record can show about gain under stated
hypotheses; it predicts nothing about what any system does.

**The seed.** The third unpayable debt — the originative one — never touches the
interface at all. It is not scoped and is not approached.

## A commentary the theory parts deliberately omit

The most interesting thing in this package is probably not a theorem. It is that
**the enforcement clause turned out to be checkable at a date and open only over
time**, and that this was invisible until the core condition was written down as
a containment and noticed to be linear in the reference at fixed coefficient.
The clause had been carried as a single open assumption; it is really a program
plus a strictly smaller assumption. Whether other clauses hide the same
structure is the question that observation raises, and this package does not
answer it.
