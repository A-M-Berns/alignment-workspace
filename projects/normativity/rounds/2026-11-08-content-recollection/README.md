# Content recollection — four tools for a notion of content that survives change

Verdict: CONTENT-BY-ROWS — of four tools tried, two fit and two fit in part. Conservativity fits: over rational rows a vocabulary change keeps every old commitment iff the image of the new region lies in the old region, and adds none iff the old region lies in the image, the second by the rational alternative read in from a pinned dependency; a new term whose introduction and elimination rows force P(Y) ≥ 3/10 on an old coordinate is the non-conservative fixture, and seed-independence is conservativity on pins only. Incompatibility semantics fits: incompatibility as a valid conflict is exactly infeasibility in the cube and incompatibility entailment coincides with certificate entailment with no nonemptiness hypothesis, which answers item 118 (every valid bound is certified; the computed forced interval is attained at rational points); Expresses defined by incompatibility role against the robust reading is inhabited with content — the row the rebuttal rule keeps passes, the plain row fails because it contraposes, and the row at 3/4 has no entitlement — while the form language alone is degenerate and the same-role strength is not established for the exact row. Recollection as a stochastic map fits in part: on world distributions it is a translation, so pullback, soundness, composition, the exact transport step with constant 1 and slack 0 and a slack of the row's oscillation times the mass on which two maps differ all hold, and it is the garbling of the thin-legitimacy module; but it checks a proposed map and chooses none, under an uninformative map every old row is kept or broken wholesale, and the faithful transport of Integrity is the same shape on accounts and not a consequence. Precedent fits in part: read off the record, with survivors prevailing and undercut occurrences outweighed, a case base is rationalized by one strict order on reasons iff the graph on its cases has no cycle, the pairwise check being necessary and not sufficient, with a forbidden and two distinguished continuations; it allows and does not rank, sits upstream of the survivor rule, and the record has no decision object to read a case from. Follow-up of 2026-11-09, on finite memory: of four further matters one fits and three fit in part. The holding fits in part: permission reads a case base only through the reach of its holding — the transitive closure of the cases' decisions, with covering — so the cases may be forgotten and the holding kept, and over a finite set of forms it is eventually constant; over one pair of kinds, n strengths carry a different holding for each set of steps, so the kinds of reasons alone give no finite representation. Decisions on the record fit in part: an assessed act — grounds, surviving grounds against, the assessor's verdict fixing which side won — is a decision object beside the docket, conduct can be held to the holding of its own assessed past, and a verdict against a choice that past backs leaves nothing permitted; the constraint still allows and does not rank. Forgetting fits: one Fourier–Motzkin step computes the projection that drops a coordinate, forgetting is exact and conservative on the retained fragment, a settled coordinate may be forgotten under every later docket state and settlement and a closed account keeps its receipts and its denotation, while forgetting an unsettled coordinate can cost the later forced interval, [3/4, 1] against [0, 1]; loss relative to a reference schedule composes as slack and is bounded when summable; and a reasoner choosing what to drop after seeing the record obtains [0, 1] where each coordinate fixed in advance leaves [3/4, 1] on one record. A vocabulary change as an act on the docket fits in part: with a declared protected fragment its licence is a refinement certificate and its challenge a broken-commitment witness with decidable validity, a licensed change admitting no valid challenge on the fragment; who may issue one is not determined, and none of three readings of a broken commitment — bars, accounted, the split by liveness — registers a live obligation shed by retelling its antecedent as false, which each admits at no charge.
