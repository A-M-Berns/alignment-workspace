# Content recollection — four tools for a notion of content that survives change

Verdict: CONTENT-BY-ROWS — of four tools tried, two fit and two fit in part. Conservativity fits: over rational rows a vocabulary change keeps every old commitment iff the image of the new region lies in the old region, and adds none iff the old region lies in the image, the second by the rational alternative read in from a pinned dependency; a new term whose introduction and elimination rows force P(Y) ≥ 3/10 on an old coordinate is the non-conservative fixture, and seed-independence is conservativity on pins only. Incompatibility semantics fits: incompatibility as a valid conflict is exactly infeasibility in the cube and incompatibility entailment coincides with certificate entailment with no nonemptiness hypothesis, which answers item 118 (every valid bound is certified; the computed forced interval is attained at rational points); Expresses defined by incompatibility role against the robust reading is inhabited with content — the row the rebuttal rule keeps passes, the plain row fails because it contraposes, and the row at 3/4 has no entitlement — while the form language alone is degenerate and the same-role strength is not established for the exact row. Recollection as a stochastic map fits in part: on world distributions it is a translation, so pullback, soundness, composition, the transport step with constant 1 and slack 0 and a slack equal to the mass on which two maps differ all hold, and it is the garbling of the thin-legitimacy module; but it checks a proposed map and chooses none, under an uninformative map every old row is kept or broken wholesale, and the faithful transport of Integrity is the same shape on accounts and not a consequence. Precedent fits in part: read off the record, with survivors prevailing and undercut occurrences outweighed, consistency of a case base is a pairwise check with a forbidden and two distinguished continuations; it allows and does not rank, sits upstream of the survivor rule, and the record has no decision object to read a case from.
