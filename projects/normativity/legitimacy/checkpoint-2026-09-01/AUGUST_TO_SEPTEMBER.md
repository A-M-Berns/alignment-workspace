# August to September

Two prior checkpoints are reconciled here: the **August 9 consolidation**
(`projects/normativity/consolidation-aug9/`, `agent-consolidated`) and the
**diachronic-answerability note** of 31 August 2026. Neither is discarded and
neither is rewritten.

- The August consolidation remains **authoritative for its own content**. Its
  theory parts 1–12 are not restated here except where a later result modifies
  one, and each such place says so.
- The note is a **working mathematical synthesis; paper proofs, not
  Lean-verified**, by its own statement, and is treated at that strength. Its
  repository counterparts are the `2026-08-30-answerability-carriers`,
  `2026-08-30-anchored-slices-auth-transfer` and
  `2026-08-31-faithful-semantic-preservation` rounds.

## Part A — the August 9 consolidation

### Still current

- **The settlement interface.** Reports, timing, enforcement as the three and only
  three things an engine supplies. The `J/C/P/T/F` clause letters remain frozen
  opaque identifiers.
- **The vocabulary discipline.** *settlement event* not *pin*; *frozen* for
  repository artifacts; *incoherence*, *downside limit*, *core minimum*. Later
  rounds have honoured this and it should continue.
- **The objection grammar, diachronic identity, practical demand and joint
  composition** theory parts, for their own content.
- **The certification layering** — the mechanism operates without a coherence
  modulus at the price of the book carrying the liability. This is the structural
  ancestor of everything the liability line later did.
- **Quarantine of operative force on region emptiness.** Still the declared branch,
  and it is what `OVERLOAD_TARGET.md` later restated as "an empty region fails
  before any account is consulted".

### Refined — same idea, better theorem or more exact interface

| August | September |
|---|---|
| Bounded liability as an assumed input to preservation | Reasonwise accounting: per-row floors give the uniform subset ceiling `U + B_tot`, and aggregate safety alone provably does not suffice |
| Liability as a per-date certificate | The account is **signed and cumulative**; the identity needs the signed misfit, and per-date conservatism is strictly and unboundedly weaker |
| Affordability as a schematic notion | Exactly characterized for exogenous date costs: `liminf L_t(1) = 0`, for any star-shaped date cost, with the finite-horizon optimum and the online equivalence |
| "The mechanism can operate" | Sharp Timely Service: it can operate *and* deliver obligation-weighted Progress up to semantic delay only |
| Force emits a liability obligation | Unchanged, and now with the surrounding layer's discharge theory written down |

### Superseded

Nothing in the August consolidation is superseded by later work. The supersessions
of this program (`SUPERSESSION.md`) are all of statements made *after* August 9, in
rounds that were themselves later corrected. **This is worth saying plainly**: the
August package's caution — stating open questions as open, and refusing to promote
— is why it has needed no retraction, and it is the standard the later rounds
should have been held to.

### Still open, unchanged since August

- **Persistence of the core minimum** — the infimum over dates. Still the one open
  item the parametric composite leans on. No later round addressed it.
- **A computable coherence modulus, or a proof there is none** — open in both
  directions, with the Gaifman caution intact.
- **The deduction-budget clause**, and the *acceleration* versus *attention* fork
  in funding-responsive deduction. Only the second makes the plausible-world set
  funder-dependent, and only the second threatens the criterion's statement.
  Reserved in August, still reserved.

### Newly opened by later work — not visible in August

- **Which weighting Progress uses.** August had no obligation measure, so the
  service-weighted / obligation-weighted distinction could not arise. It was the one
  question the checkpoint reserved, and it is now settled: **Progress is the
  obligation-weighted form** (`DECISIONS.md`, 2026-09-01), so the transport interface sits
  inside the settled statement rather than downstream of it.
- **Transport, and its constants.** No August object corresponds to `T(t,s)`,
  `eps(t,s)`, or `epsbar_N(T)`.
- **Timeliness as a separate axis from persistence.** August's service notions do
  not distinguish them; the September separation (`persistence == eventual full
  service ⊊ uniform bounded delay`) is new.
- **Insolvency, and the certificate split.** Deadline insolvency is finite and
  authenticated; persistence insolvency needs a tail proof. Neither notion existed
  in August.
- **The closed-loop question, stated as a hierarchy.** E0–E5, so that a benchmark
  result cannot be silently imported into the closed loop.
- **The two routes to a cheap date**, and with them the slogan that *cheap
  enforcement is not always conforming enforcement* — a hazard August had no
  vocabulary to state.

## Part B — the diachronic-answerability note

### Still current

Everything structural, and it is the larger half of Layer II: grounded replay;
prospective revision under anchored protocols; the matter / slice / issue
separation; slice-wise conservation `c_alpha = Satisfied ∨ Disposed ∨ Remaining`;
remaining content cannot increase; no silent terminal closure; the persistent-wait
theorem; the answerability–service dichotomy; and the list in its §10 of what is
fixed versus revisable.

### Refined by the September mathematics

| the note | September |
|---|---|
| §11: *"Unbounded attention is not improvement. A progress theorem needs a reason-to-response structure … and a dynamic uptake condition"* | **Supplied.** The structure is Actionability's coercivity modulus `phi` with feasible repair and admissible region; the uptake condition is the MarketMaker cumulative cap. F1 converts divergent attention into vanishing defect; F2 gives the rate `A_N^{-1/2}` |
| §11: *"does not show that many simultaneous normative demands can exert sustained pressure while preserving viability"* | **Partly supplied.** Persistence does not compete across reasons and needs no Hall condition; timely service does compete and the joint condition is a single sum; preservation is what the liability floor buys |
| Def. 8.15: a **renewable** per-stage attention budget `sum_m a_n(m) <= 1`; Prop. 8.17 witnesses non-starvation with summable weights | **Repriced.** The liability budget is a **consumable stock** `sum_t L_t(a_t) <= B`. Theorem EV1 is the liability-priced version of Prop. 8.17: the same geometric-tranche construction, now paying for cost rather than dividing a share, and non-starvation survives at exactly the price `liminf L_t(1) = 0` |
| Thm 9.2: the dichotomy ends at *"attention diverges"* | **Continued.** The mathematics begins at `A_N -> infinity`. The two documents meet exactly there |
| §11: *"bounded cumulative liability is one concrete certificate of affordability; the liability theory should be treated as a realization of the broader schematic concept, not as its definition"* | **Adopted unchanged.** This typing is correct and this checkpoint keeps it |

### Where the note is richer than the mathematics

Three things the note has that Layer I has no analogue for, listed in decreasing
order of how much they matter:

1. **Disposition** — content that legitimately ceases to be owed without being
   answered. Layer I obligation mass is served or it remains; there is no third fate.
   This is the largest single gap and `ANSWERABILITY_AND_SERVICE.md` §6 argues it
   is also where defeat lives.
2. **Carriers and representations** — the whole `Rep_alpha` / `J_alpha` /
   fidelity-preorder layer. Layer I has a defect number and no representation.
3. **Prerequisites, routes, readiness** — dependency structure among issues. Layer
   I scheduling has none: every claim is independently servable at any legal date.

### Where the mathematics is richer than the note

1. **A defect**, as distinct from attention. The note tracks whether a matter was
   attended to; the mathematics tracks how badly the reason is answered, and
   Progress is a statement about the second.
2. **A price.** Attention costs liability, so there are norms no schedule can
   discharge at any budget — a failure mode the note's renewable budget cannot
   express.
3. **Timeliness as a separate axis**, with the exact criterion `D4` and the
   theorem that unlimited deferral makes eventual answering no harder than
   persistence.

### Still open in both

Semantic authentication and slice faithfulness are **application-supplied
assumptions** in the note and **hypothesis (T)** in the mathematics. They are the
same hole seen from two sides, and closing it is the top item of `ROADMAP.md`.

## The movement, in three sentences

August fixed the *interface*: what a settlement engine must supply before its
writings get force, and what force then owes the surrounding layer. The
answerability line fixed the *audit structure*: what revision may not do to
already-incurred content, and that a live matter cannot be starved. September
fixed the *economics*: what service costs, when it can persist, when it can be
timely, and what all of that buys — which turned out to be obligation-weighted Progress
up to semantic delay only, leaving the semantics of that delay as the frontier.
