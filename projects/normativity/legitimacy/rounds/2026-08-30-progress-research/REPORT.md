## Executive result

Progress plugs cleanly into Normative Continuity without another lifecycle. The
smallest schematic addition is a decision-local response menu, an admissible valuation
region, predictable repair programs and applicability, and signed Uptake. Uptake plus
an eventually nonnegative Sensitivity witness gives a clean defect-elimination theorem:

\[
W_N\to\infty\quad\Longrightarrow\quad D_N/W_N\to0.
\]

The stagnant-tail idea is decisive. It removes the negative-credit objection to signed
regret and avoids dynamic regret for the basic theorem: after a hypothetical last
legitimate progress event, one stable sensitive repair has nonnegative gain, so all
earlier comparator credit is finite. The weakest sufficient Stagnation Witness uses
positive `limsup`, not the prompt's stronger positive `liminf`.

The compression nevertheless remains conditional. Nothing in the current reason
multihypergraph or Continuity structure proves that a genuinely unanswered reason
entails one stable robust repair on the stagnant tail. That **normative witness
completeness** bridge is the single hard open problem. The value-security,
projection-traderization, and confidence-rated Phi-regret realization is coherent and
supports a nontrivial joint toy model, but it assumes that bridge.

## The theorem chain that survived

### Schematic

1. Continuity: eventual liveness gives `A_N(m)->infinity`.
2. Stagnation Witness: genuine stagnant tail gives a fixed registered `rho` with
   `W_N->infinity`, eventual `g_n>=gamma d_n>=0`, and
   `limsup D_N/W_N>0`.
3. Uptake: signed weighted average robust gain has limsup at most zero.
4. Sensitivity + Uptake: `D_N/W_N->0`.
5. Contradiction: the stagnant tail cannot exist.

Only steps 1 and 4 are presently established from their displayed premises. Step 2 is
the open normative bridge. Step 3 is an abstract axiom with a plausible realization.

### Candidate realization

1. Enabled reason occurrences compile, with episode provenance, to nonempty rational
   affine constraints on bounded value securities.
2. LP duality certifies robust repair gain.
3. Projection traderization provides `dist_infinity(hat v_n,K_n)<=tau_n`.
4. Confidence-rated Phi-regret bounds estimated repair gain.
5. Holder's inequality gives

   \[
   \sum w_ng_n\le B_\rho(W_N)+
   \sum w_n\|\Phi_n(p_n)-p_n\|_1\tau_n.
   \]

6. Sublinear regret and service-weighted vanishing error imply Uptake.

Steps 2 and 5 are elementary and derived in `MATH_NOTES.md`. Projection enforcement is
available upstream. Steps 1, 3-to-4 synchronous composition, and the exact weighted
learner instantiation remain realization obligations.

## Governing questions

### 1. Does Progress plug into Normative Continuity without duplicating lifecycle machinery?

Yes. It consumes `Live`, `Reach`, `Work`, `o`, `a`, cumulative attention, and recorded
resolution/succession. Progress adds only decision scoring and response objects. An
answer, defeater, evaluator revision, or reframing is represented through existing
Continuity events. `c_n` is a scoring weight, not a scheduler or pending state.

### 2. Is Uptake right, or is regret unavoidable schematically?

Signed Uptake is the right thin abstract condition. Regret is not part of what Progress
means; it is one realization. Signed Uptake is weaker than positive-part Uptake and can
permit negative credit, but the primary theorem's eventual Sensitivity premise removes
that problem on a stagnant tail. A stronger intermittent-reason theory would need
positive-part Uptake or interval/strongly-adaptive structure.

### 3. What is the weakest plausible Stagnation Witness?

`SW-density`:

\[
\text{eventual genuine stagnation}\Longrightarrow
\exists\rho,\gamma>0:\quad W_N\to\infty,quad
g_n\ge\gamma d_n\text{ eventually},\quad
\limsup D_N/W_N>0.
\]

The witness is one fixed causal repair program registered in history. Positive limsup
is sufficient; positive liminf is an optional stronger notion of persistent-density
stagnation. `W_N->infinity` is load-bearing because Continuity supplies service, not
relevance of that service to every reason.

### 4. Can legitimate evaluator revision occur indefinitely without evading basic Progress?

Yes, at the basic level. Infinite accepted prospective revisions are infinitely many
explicit answers, not a stagnant tail. This does not establish improvement, convergence,
or non-laundering across eras. Those are stronger diachronic Progress questions.

### 5. Does a stagnant tail avoid dynamic regret?

Yes, conditionally. If explicit legitimate progress stops, a stable episode-local
witness can be required afterward. Eventual Sensitivity makes its gain nonnegative;
finite pre-tail loss credit washes out. Dynamic regret is unnecessary for this theorem.
If no stable witness exists even on the tail, SW-density fails and dynamic regret alone
does not repair the normative incompleteness.

### 6. Are repair and normative witness completeness separate?

Yes. On finite `X`, pairwise maps expose any already-known pairwise robust dominance;
all deterministic maps give broad combinatorial expressivity. The difficult claim is
that an unresolved substantive reason entails any robust dominance over the admissible
region. Countermodel CM13 separates them directly.

### 7. Can value securities and traderized constraints realize `K_n`?

Plausibly. Episode-local anchored-score securities are enough for the first theorem;
they need not represent objective utility. Rational affine reason constraints yield
bounded polytopes, and existing projection enforcement gives intrinsic distance bounds.
The missing piece is the authenticated reason/episode-to-security-row compiler and its
conflict/nonemptiness discipline.

### 8. Can standard confidence-rated Phi-regret realize Uptake?

Plausibly, under a fixed finite alphabet or a proved encode/decode bridge, predictable
weights and maps, bounded full-information loss, and a fixed finite/countable repair
class. External regret alone is insufficient for conditional replacements. The exact
anytime/countable/episode-local combination is not already proved by one workspace
artifact, so it remains a bounded learning-interface lemma rather than a settled import.

### 9. What liability/traderization theorem is needed?

Existing bounded-liability enforcement preservation is necessary for the market-safety
route and already Lean-proved. It does not itself preserve a quantitative repair-regret
bound. For an external repair learner, add a synchronous composition lemma showing
that enforced authenticated prices provide its full-information losses without a
same-round circularity; adversarial regret then survives any loss sequence. For
repair-as-LI-trader realization, add a substantially stronger quantitative
repair-regret-to-exploitation lemma. The external learner route is currently cleaner.

### 10. Is there a nontrivial end-to-end toy reasoner?

Yes. `REALIZATION_MAP.md` gives one with a defeated old reason, explicit evaluator
revision into a fresh successor, a persistent new affine reason, two value securities,
a dual-certified pairwise repair, vanishing traderization error, and sublinear repair
regret. It yields vanishing defective response mass while the successor remains live.
The example proves joint satisfiability, not general witness completeness.

### 11. What is the hardest unresolved mathematical obstacle?

Normative witness completeness: derive, rather than stipulate, that a genuinely
unanswered reason on a stagnant Continuity tail produces one stable predictable repair,
an unbounded applicability-weighted service stream, and a robust Sensitivity certificate
over the operative admissible value regions. This is exactly the Progress analogue of
Persistent-Wait, but unlike Persistent-Wait it is not currently a structural graph
theorem.

## What failed and what did not

- Whole-region infimum survives as the notion of a **robust unanimity** reason, not as
  the semantics of every pro tanto reason.
- Signed Uptake survives for the defect theorem; positive-part Uptake is rejected as an
  unearned standard-regret consequence.
- The prompt's `liminf>=delta` witness is sufficient but not minimal; positive limsup
  is enough.
- A fixed stable repair on a stagnant tail survives; “some repair wins each date” fails
  under repair churn.
- A second repair scheduler is unnecessary under full information; `W_N` is a
  counterfactual evaluation mass.
- Dynamic regret is not needed for basic Progress; it may be needed for stronger
  cross-era or intermittent-reason guarantees.
- Existing liability preservation survives exactly at its declared scope; it is not
  silently upgraded to a regret theorem.

## Boundary with Coverage

Coverage hands off a represented challenge/reason that has acquired the standing and
answerability needed to become a Continuity matter. Progress requires that the handoff
retain stable reason identity, applicability provenance, typed service targets, and
enough content to attempt a value-constraint/repair certificate. This pass does not say
how Coverage finds or validates such criticism.

## Recommended next research theorem

Do not formalize the whole layer yet. The next pass should attempt one bounded
**Stagnant-Tail Witness Lemma** for a deliberately restricted reason language:

> For a fixed finite service alphabet, stable episode anchor, nonempty rational
> reason polytope, and complete pairwise repair family, characterize a semantic
> “unanswered” predicate under which absence of recorded answer events and persistent
> positive-density defect yields a fixed dual-certified pairwise repair.

Success would close the open bridge in a nontrivial fragment. Failure should identify
whether the obstacle is conflict/incomparability, unstable applicability, or the lack of
a semantic link from criticism to service-value inequalities.
