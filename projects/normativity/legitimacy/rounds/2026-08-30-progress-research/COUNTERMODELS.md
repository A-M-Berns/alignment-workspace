# Countermodels and failed candidates

Each trace separates a structural failure from its smallest reusable response. A
condition is not adopted merely because it blocks one trace.

## CM1 — attention theater

One matter is live and ready forever, `a_n=1`, but every service response concerns
administrative bookkeeping unrelated to the represented criticism. Hence `A_N=N`
while the criticism is inert.

`c_n(rho)=0` correctly says these choices are not counterfactually evaluable for the
repair, but then `W_N=0`; applicability alone does not create progress. The reusable
principle is in SW-density: genuine stagnation must yield a witness with unbounded
**service-weighted applicability**. This needs no second scheduler when all repair
counterfactuals are scored from the same serviced choice. If no relevant choice is ever
made, SW-density fails visibly rather than attention theater passing as progress.

## CM2 — evaluator laundering

Let `X={x,y}`. Whenever current `K_n` entails `v(y)>v(x)`, replace it before scoring
with a region entailing the reverse. No fixed repair gains.

Decision-local `K_n` prevents retroactive rescoring but not prospective churn. The
reusable principle is **episode provenance plus explicit transition**: a change of the
anchored evaluative procedure must occur through resolution/successor or another
recorded answer event. If such transitions occur forever, basic Progress treats them
as explicit revisions; if they stop, SW-density applies on the stagnant tail. A future
diachronic theory is needed to judge infinite revision itself.

## CM3 — repair shedding

Register pairwise repair `x->y`; once it has positive robust gain, delete its identity
from the class. A no-regret guarantee over the current class is vacuous.

Repairs need historically stable program identities. Unavailability returns identity;
it does not delete the comparator or its record. Legitimate withdrawal can be an
explicit answer event, as in prior Legitimate Improvement, but does not rewrite past
gain or erase a challenge. This is registry/provenance, not another lifecycle.

## CM4 — applicability laundering

Set `c_n(rho)=0` exactly on dates where the just-chosen `p_n` gives `g_n>0`, and one
otherwise. Then every positive opportunity is removed from `W_N`.

The repair is predictability: `c_n` is fixed from `H_n` before `p_n`. Predictability
alone permits a process to announce zero forever, so SW-density must additionally
derive unbounded weighted applicability from genuine stagnation. Anchoring
applicability to enabled reason occurrences makes changes provenance-visible.

## CM5 — signed-regret credit

For one fixed repair, let `g_n=-1` for the first million awake dates and `g_n=1/2` for
the next million. Signed cumulative gain is negative, although the later reason is
ignored throughout its episode.

Failed definition: signed Uptake by itself as a response to every intermittent reason.
Positive-part Uptake blocks the trace but is not standard-regret realizable. For basic
Progress, start after the hypothetical last progress event and require eventual
Sensitivity `g_n>=gamma d_n>=0`; the finite earlier credit washes out. Stronger
interval-responsive Progress would require interval/strongly-adaptive regret.

## CM6 — repair churn

At every date, one repair has gap `1`, but it is a new repair identity and all earlier
repairs are neutral or harmful. Every fixed comparator has sublinear signed gain while
the service is always locally dominated.

“Some positive repair always exists” is therefore not a sufficient witness. A fixed
finite class plus nonnegative gains may permit a pigeonhole argument; varying menus do
not. Basic Progress requires one stable tail witness. Dynamic regret or a compactness
theorem is optional stronger machinery, not an automatic patch.

## CM7 — defeater spam

Every criticism produces a new prerequisite, counter-criticism, or defeater issue. The
process services and succeeds them forever but never changes the target conduct.

Continuity ensures these issues and routes stay answerable and receive service; it does
not make their content responsive. If every creation is an accepted explicit revision,
basic Progress does not call the trace structurally stagnant. If the target defect and
one robust reason survive a tail, SW-density plus Uptake eliminates it. If spam itself
counts as endless “progress,” a stronger anti-laundering/diachronic criterion is needed.
No queue condition repairs the semantic gap.

## CM8 — infinite explicit revision

`q_0->q_1->...` is an infinite chain of fresh successors, each legitimately anchored,
with changed evaluator and state continuation. No episode lasts long enough for one
comparator to accumulate weight.

This is not a countermodel to **basic** Progress as scoped: the trace contains infinitely
many explicit answers/revisions. It is a countermodel to the stronger slogan that
revision must converge or improve under a cross-era measure. Such a theorem needs a
diachronic evaluator, anti-laundering rule, or switching comparator and is deferred.

## CM9 — whole-region robustness is too strong for ordinary reasons

Let `K` contain two admissible valuations, one preferring `x` and one preferring `y`.
A strong pro tanto reason favors `y` in one face, but `inf_K(v(y)-v(x))<0`. No robust
repair exists.

This does not refute the definition: `g>0` was deliberately read as unanimity among
operative admissible valuations. It refutes identifying every ordinary reason with a
whole-region robust gain. Conditional/local-face reasons require a predictable
applicability proposition selecting a subregion, or an explicit conflict-resolution
issue. Silently discarding the dissenting face would beg the normative question.

## CM10 — empty region explosion

Two operative rows require `v(y)-v(x)>=1/2` and `v(x)-v(y)>=1/2`. If one takes an
infimum over the empty region with extended-real conventions, every repair can become
vacuously robust.

Nonemptiness is mandatory. Conflict creates a represented inquiry/defeater/successor
event. Traderization cannot enforce an empty target, and an LP certificate is rejected
before scoring.

## CM11 — varying-menu hindsight

At date `n`, define the comparator only after seeing `p_n`, mapping whichever action got
the most mass to the best-valued label. It has linear advantage but is not a lawful
online comparator.

A repair is a fixed causal program that emits the entire date-`n` map from `H_n`; it may
take a candidate action as its argument but cannot inspect the distribution chosen
afterward. A growing union of occasion-specific labels also fails standard finite-action
regret unless an encode/decode bridge keeps the semantic alphabet fixed.

## CM12 — finite enforcement error floor

Let `g_n=gamma d_n` and `d_n=epsilon>0` forever, while `tau_n=gamma epsilon/2`.
The price estimate can erase the entire repair margin, and regret on estimated values
sees no gain.

Pointwise fixed tolerance cannot prove defect elimination. Service-weighted error must
vanish for the zero-defect theorem, or the theorem must weaken to ruling out defects
whose margin stays strictly above the error floor.

## CM13 — repair expressivity without normative completeness

Include every deterministic map on finite `X`, but let the unresolved criticism merely
say “investigate whether x harms a third party,” with `K` allowing every ordering of the
current service moves. The repair family is complete, yet no robust dominance follows.

This decisively separates repair expressivity from normative witness completeness. The
former is combinatorial; the latter is SW-density and remains open.

## Conditions retained after prosecution

| Failure | Reusable response |
|---|---|
| attention/applicability theater | SW-density includes `W_N->infinity`; predictable anchored confidence |
| silent evaluator or repair reset | episode provenance and Continuity-visible transition; stable program registry |
| signed negative credit | eventual nonnegative Sensitivity on a stagnant tail |
| repair churn | one stable tail witness, or explicitly stronger dynamic theory |
| varying menus/hindsight | fixed causal programs plus fixed-label/encode-decode bridge |
| empty/inconsistent values | nonempty bounded region and explicit conflict disposition |
| finite price error | weighted vanishing error or a margin-qualified weaker result |
| semantic reason without dominance | normative witness completeness, not more repair maps |
