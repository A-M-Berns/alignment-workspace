# Progress research pass

Status: research round; unregistered; not frozen or settled.

This round asks whether Progress can consume Normative Continuity's unbounded service
without duplicating its lifecycle. It develops the abstract schematic separately from a
candidate reasons/value-security/traderization/Phi-regret realization.

## Result

A clean defect-elimination theorem survives. A stagnant-tail formulation makes signed
Uptake sufficient and avoids dynamic regret for basic Progress. The architecture remains
coherent, but the central bridge is open: current theory does not derive a stable robust
repair witness from the semantic claim that a reason remains genuinely unanswered.
