# Normative Affordability

Research round: unregistered, no Lean, no settled definition changed. It fixes
the schematic interface between Answerability's standing load and Progress's
claim-weighted conclusion, and audits the composition with Actionability, Uptake
and settlement-relative safety.

**The fixed-era composition is frozen.** `FIXED_ERA_THEOREM.md`, the S1-S6
schematic stack of `CORRECTED_STACK.md`, the allocated-service typing, the
row-indexed accounting and Service Transport are settled for this round; later work
here is existence and characterization, which consume them rather than amend them.

Read `REPORT.md` first. Run the exact fixtures with

    python3 tests/run.py

from this directory.

## Verdict

AFFORDABILITY-COMPOSES-BUT-THE-SERVICE-CONDITION-IS-TRANSPORT-NOT-CONTIGUITY — the proposed architecture survives with one component replaced, one conclusion weakened and one inherited hypothesis refuted; its realization map survives an audit against the traderized mathematics with three withdrawals; and the remaining type mismatch between the schematic and the concrete learner is closed by separating allocated service from realized force. Contiguity of the claim measure against the service measure is exactly the weakest condition under which service-weighted learning transfers to claim-weighted defect, necessary and sufficient for triangular arrays, and necessary because a settlement-relative defect is a triangular array; for a defect sequence fixed independently of the horizon the exact condition is instead fixed-set contiguity, which is strictly weaker, and one-step delay separates them. Contiguity is nevertheless the wrong interface primitive: not checkable at a finite horizon, not quantitative, and false of bounded-delay service. The Deferred Service Transfer theorem reaches the same conclusion from a feasible adapted transport plan with a stability inequality and a service-to-claim cap; the two routes are incomparable rather than one deriving the other, since what the plan bounds is the density of the transported claim measure. Progress is staged rather than redefined, so no settled definition changes. Bounded-deficit Surface Fairness is a condition on total mass and does not control the claim-weighted defect at all: a two-surface fair rotation satisfies it with service-weighted defect exactly zero at every horizon while the claim-weighted defect tends to one half. Joint Actionability does not follow from individual Actionability when the gain is read against reason-relative regions and does follow, with no convexity and no separability, against the common region; in the traderized realization it is true and unused, because the compiled position is reactive. Three realization claims are withdrawn: per-reason Uptake is not free from the criterion, which does not quantify over the enforcement position at all, and comes instead from the market maker's cumulative cap; the criterion's preservation theorem is substrate preservation and not Uptake; and pointwise self-financing is sufficient and not necessary for persistent affordability, refuted by an alternating norm whose every date is robustly loss-making while the signed account stays inside a fixed band. The service variable is the allocated authority and not the realized force: the multiplier is fixed before the market maker picks a price and is freely schedulable inside a per-date liability capacity, while the realized position is decided at the fixed point, is zero exactly when the reason is perfectly satisfied, and counts a date as better served because the reasoner did worse on it. Against the allocated-service measure the per-date enforcement modulus gives service-weighted Progress outright, with rate the inverse square root of allocated service and residual the norm's own weighted mean-square exclusion of the still-live worlds; one compatible live world suffices and gives the fast rate, a certified core minimum caps the residual, and the liability charge grows like the square root of allocated authority, so a reason can receive divergent service on a finite lifetime budget because the realized force dies away as compliance improves. Actionability generalizes to a convex coercivity modulus that subsumes the linear and projection-quadratic cases in one theorem. Finite-horizon overload admits a sound Farkas certificate and no converse, and it is not the highest-value next target: the necessity of bounded liability is, followed by the existence of an affordable schedule, now posed in the allocated-service space where it is well typed. The construction is complete for a fixed era: conditional on a service-faithful authority schedule, per-row liability floors summing to a finite total, and the two residual assumptions, every arrow from the claim stream to claim-weighted Progress and to preservation of the original criterion is proved, with a finite-horizon inequality and an explicit dependency graph. The aggregate market maker cap becomes a uniform per-subset ceiling U plus the total floor exactly when the complementary rows carry summable liability floors, and aggregate safety alone does not suffice: two books with increments plus one and minus one have an identically zero aggregate account and no reason-level ceiling at all. Local authority capacity and lifetime SafeCert are different objects — a policy inside its per-date capacity at every date has an account unbounded below when the allowances are not summable, and spending the realized account slack instead strictly enlarges the viable region, quadratically. Coercive Actionability converges exactly when the modulus is bounded away from zero away from zero; convexity is not needed for convergence and buys only the rate. Conditional on force feasibility there is no service-capacity competition between reasons at all — the per-row conformance bound is independent of how many rows are active — so overload within that qualification is liability overload, while an empty region, an unpriceable row or an illegal compiled control is a failure before any account is consulted. Authority is quadratic in the liability allowance, so the per-date capacity region is non-convex and concentration beats proportional splitting by the reason count. The sustainable authority-rate region is neither convex nor the right object: time-sharing convexifies a renewable per-date resource and the liability budget is a consumable stock, and the rate region reports zero on friction sequences where the reason is in fact persistently served. Replacing it with the persistence region gives an exact answer. Writing the friction as the exclusion depth times the square root of the date's slack-plus-volume, a schedule with divergent cumulative authority and finite lifetime charge exists if and only if the friction has liminf zero; the maximum cumulative authority through any horizon is exactly the squared budget over the squared running minimum friction, attained by putting the whole budget on one least-friction date. Persistence is therefore budget-free and does not compete across reasons, so every individually persistable reason can be served simultaneously with all the others and there is no Hall-type condition to check; the persistence region is the full power set of the persistable reasons. A causal scheduler seeing the friction only at its own date achieves persistence exactly when an offline one can, by a doubling-threshold rule spending geometric tranches, and any scheduler with a positive floor on its per-date allocation is defeated by a friction sequence bounded away from zero. Conservative underwriting is strictly smaller than signed-account affordability by an unbounded margin: a satisfied row takes a zero position and costs nothing however deep its exclusion of the live worlds, so a floored friction that caps conservative authority at a constant permits linear growth on the realized trajectory. The scalar account slack is not sufficient state, because the world attaining the minimum can settle away. The fixed-era construction is frozen; what remains is the rate problem, the signed class, adversarial arrivals, the necessity of bounded liability, and transport-stability certificates.
