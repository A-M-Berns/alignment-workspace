# Normative Affordability

Research round: unregistered, no Lean, no settled definition changed. It fixes
the schematic interface between Answerability's standing load and Progress's
claim-weighted conclusion, and audits the composition with Actionability, Uptake
and settlement-relative safety.

Read `REPORT.md` first. Run the exact fixtures with

    python3 tests/run.py

from this directory.

## Verdict

AFFORDABILITY-COMPOSES-BUT-THE-SERVICE-CONDITION-IS-TRANSPORT-NOT-CONTIGUITY — the proposed architecture survives with one component replaced, one conclusion weakened and one inherited hypothesis refuted, and its realization map survives an audit against the traderized mathematics with three withdrawals. Contiguity of the claim measure against the service measure is exactly the weakest condition under which service-weighted learning transfers to claim-weighted defect, necessary and sufficient for triangular arrays, and necessary because a settlement-relative defect is a triangular array; for a defect sequence fixed independently of the horizon the exact condition is instead fixed-set contiguity, which is strictly weaker, and one-step delay separates them. Contiguity is nevertheless the wrong interface primitive: not checkable at a finite horizon, not quantitative, and false of bounded-delay service. The Deferred Service Transfer theorem reaches the same conclusion from a feasible adapted transport plan with a stability inequality and a service-to-claim cap; the two routes are incomparable rather than one deriving the other, since what the plan bounds is the density of the transported claim measure. Progress is staged rather than redefined: Actionability and Uptake give the service-weighted statement, transport carries it to the claim-weighted one at a residual equal to the transport stability error, and no settled definition changes. Bounded-deficit Surface Fairness is a condition on total mass and does not control the claim-weighted defect at all: a two-surface fair rotation satisfies it with service-weighted defect exactly zero at every horizon while the claim-weighted defect tends to one half, and of the four Persistent Relevance interfaces offered there only the one exposing a surface on every service date transfers. Joint Actionability does not follow from individual Actionability when the gain is read against reason-relative regions and does follow, with no convexity and no separability, against the common region; in the traderized realization it is true and unused, because the compiled position is reactive. Three realization claims are withdrawn: per-reason Uptake is not free from the criterion, which does not quantify over the enforcement position at all, and comes instead from the market maker's cumulative cap; the criterion's preservation theorem is substrate preservation and not Uptake; and pointwise self-financing is sufficient and not necessary for persistent affordability, refuted by an alternating norm whose every date is robustly loss-making while the signed account stays inside a fixed band. The account is signed and cumulative, so the necessary condition is worldwise viability rather than per-round non-loss, and the resulting realization is sharper and weaker than the schematic: the service-weighted defect converges to the service-weighted misfit between the norm and the still-live worlds, so force drives conformance exactly to the level at which the norm is consistent with what can still be true. Finite-horizon overload admits a sound Farkas certificate and no converse, and it is no longer the highest-value next target: the necessity of bounded liability is.
