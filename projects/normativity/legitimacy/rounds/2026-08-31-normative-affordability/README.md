# Normative Affordability

Research round: unregistered, no Lean, no settled definition changed. It fixes
the schematic interface between Answerability's standing load and Progress's
claim-weighted conclusion, and audits the composition with Actionability, Uptake
and settlement-relative safety.

Read `REPORT.md` first. Run the exact fixtures with

    python3 tests/run.py

from this directory.

## Files

- `SERVICE_TRANSFER.md` — the transfer theorem, the exact characterization by
  contiguity, the array-versus-fixed-sequence dichotomy, two starvation attacks,
  and the transport theorem that replaces contiguity as the interface primitive.
- `JOINT_ACTIONABILITY.md` — the interference countermodel, composition by
  common-region superposition, the vanishing-share failure of aggregate Uptake,
  and the minimal type of the joint response correspondence.
- `SAFECERT.md` — why actual-path safety is not a candidate, the robust form, and
  the non-revocation theorem.
- `AFFORDABILITY.md` — the revised definition, the Sustainable Progress theorem,
  and five premise-removal countermodels.
- `EXISTENCE_AND_DUALITY.md` — existence as a causal viability problem, a drift
  sufficiency sketch, and finite-horizon overload certificates.
- `REALIZATION_MAP.md` — the traderized mapping, the score identity and friction
  inequality, the core minimum as slack, and what immutable history alone secures.
- `src/`, `tests/` — forty-four exact-rational checks.

## Verdict

AFFORDABILITY-COMPOSES-BUT-THE-SERVICE-CONDITION-IS-TRANSPORT-NOT-CONTIGUITY — the proposed architecture survives with one component replaced, one conclusion weakened and one inherited hypothesis refuted. Contiguity of the claim measure against the service measure is exactly the weakest condition under which service-weighted learning transfers to claim-weighted defect, necessary and sufficient for triangular arrays, and necessary because a settlement-relative defect is a triangular array; for a defect sequence fixed independently of the horizon the exact condition is instead fixed-set contiguity, which is strictly weaker, and one-step delay separates them. Contiguity is nevertheless the wrong interface primitive: not checkable at a finite horizon, not quantitative, and false of bounded-delay service, which the theory has no reason to forbid. The Deferred Service Transfer theorem derives it instead from a feasible adapted transport plan with a stability inequality and a service-to-claim cap, and the derived bound is quantitative. The composition then delivers not claim-weighted defect tending to zero but limsup at most the transport stability error, which vanishes only when deferred service is exactly defect-preserving. Bounded-deficit Surface Fairness, the service hypothesis the merged Progress schematic carries, is a condition on total mass and does not control the claim-weighted defect at all: a two-surface fair rotation satisfies it with service-weighted defect exactly zero at every horizon while the claim-weighted defect tends to one half, and of the four Persistent Relevance interfaces offered there only the one exposing a surface on every service date transfers, which the recommended pairing is not. Joint Actionability does not follow from individual Actionability when the gain is read against reason-relative regions — an exhibited pair with jointly satisfiable demands, each meeting its margin exactly, has per-reason gain minus one eighth after aggregation — and does follow, with no convexity and no separability, when the gain is read against the common region, since superadditivity of an infimum over a smaller set is the whole proof. Traderized Logical Induction supplies the common scoring set, additivity and per-reason Uptake for free; what it does not supply is nonemptiness of the intersection, which is covered compatibility and already carries an exact failure certificate. Existence is a causal viability problem in which a fixed liability budget funds only a transient, so persistent service must be self-financing, and the sufficient condition offered is a drift sketch rather than a theorem. Finite-horizon overload admits a sound Farkas certificate pricing reasons against a liability price; the converse is not established, and that gap is the main question the round leaves open.
