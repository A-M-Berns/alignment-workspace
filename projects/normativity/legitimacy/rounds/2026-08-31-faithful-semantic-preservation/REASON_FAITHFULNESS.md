# Reason faithfulness

For a reason slice, the answerability-relevant quotient must reflect:

- provenance or authenticated provenance transport;
- target proposition and semantic content;
- answer mode;
- unresolved/represented status;
- the criterion for an adequate answer.

A proposition-only map can correctly preserve the text “claim C” while
collapsing “supply evidence for C” and “acknowledge receipt of C.” It is sound
about proposition identity and inadequate for inherited answerability.

Implementation format, wording, or document location may remain irrelevant.
The admissible quotient records that choice explicitly, permitting a
noninjective but slice-faithful map.

This adequacy condition is weaker than Progress faithfulness. Cross-era Progress
also needs a preserved comparison/correction surface and possibly a margin.
Nothing here proves that an answer is an improvement; it proves only that the
inherited reason can still receive the kind of answer it was owed.
