What the trajectory shows
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
  A -> B: the active value specification changes (v0 -> v1) while the
          operative projection does not: ['J0'] -> ['J0'].
          B's compiled fragment still names v0's thresholds:
          ['X[v0:q]>0', 'X[v0:q]>1/2', 'phi']
  B -> C: an explicit normative event moves force: ['J0'] -> ['J1'],
          and only now does the fragment change to v1's thresholds:
          ['X[v1:q]>0', 'X[v1:q]>1/3', 'X[v1:q]>2/3']
  every day: admissibility fails and the region is not inert --
          the two are the same fact, and neither day gets the
          unconditional branch of the traderization theorem.
