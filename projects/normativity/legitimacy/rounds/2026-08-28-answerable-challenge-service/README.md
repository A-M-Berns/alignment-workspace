# Answerable challenge service

Status: **specification, reference models and a prosecution record;
unregistered.** All names are provisional under `AGENTS.md` §6. Nothing here is
Lean-checked and no claim is registered.

## Verdict

CHALLENGE-CONTINUITY-SURVIVES-SERVICE-IS-THE-NEW-CONTENT — the registered challenge is the right object between criticism and promoted reason, and pinning its adjudication terms defeats the moving-goalpost attack: the same trace that never promotes anything under a threshold moving from 10 to 100 to 1000 promotes at t=11 when the episode's terms are fixed at registration, with the two fixtures differing in exactly one flag. Pinning binds the episode rather than the process, so prospective revision stays available and moving an open challenge onto new terms requires an explicit transfer, which frozen carry already expresses. The continuity half is nearly free and is frozen A1 for the third round running, which the document says in three places rather than presenting as discovery. The genuinely new mathematical burden is service: a challenge left open forever with opportunity recurring forever and nothing ever served satisfies every premise in this round and in the frozen package, because an outstanding claim nothing is done about is exactly what A1 is content with. The round does not solve self-sealing and says so: a general rule installed before any challenge, holding that criticisms threatening authority are rejected, is temporally clean and inferentially relevant and every structural condition here accepts it, and no premise was added to make it fail because every candidate considered would also have refused a legitimate resource-priority deferral. The outer boundary is an indistinguishability observation rather than a theorem: no predicate over represented history separates a world with no criticism from one whose decisive criticism was never registered.

## The question

Two rounds above this one found the same boundary from different directions.
`2026-08-27` could not catch a repair retired before it was demonstrated;
`2026-08-28-answerable-revision` could not catch a criticism sealed off before it
was promoted. Both stop where nothing has yet been promoted or demonstrated.

This round asks whether one intermediate object closes that gap:

```text
potential criticism      may exist and be wholly unrepresented
REGISTERED CHALLENGE     has procedural standing
adjudicated evidence     accumulated under the challenge
promoted reason          -> the round above
```

A registered challenge is **not yet a reason for revision**. It may be wrong,
duplicative or unsafe to test. The thesis is only that non-address requires an
answer too.

## What survives

**The pinned episode.** An open challenge is adjudicated under the terms fixed at
its registration. The moving-goalpost attack — threshold 10, evidence 9,
threshold 100, evidence 90, threshold 1000 — never promotes anything when terms
float, and promotes at `t = 11` when they are pinned. Prospective revision stays
available; moving an open challenge onto new terms requires an explicit transfer.

**Service is the new content.** A challenge open forever, with opportunity
recurring forever and nothing served, satisfies every premise here and in the
frozen package. That is `A1` working as designed, and it is exactly the problem.
Only a service premise sees it.

## What does not survive

**Registration does not solve self-sealing.** A general rule installed before any
challenge — *criticisms threatening authority are rejected* — is temporally clean
and inferentially relevant, and every structural condition here accepts it. No
premise was added to make it fail, because every candidate would also have
refused a legitimate resource-priority deferral.

**Coverage stays outside.** Two dockets with identical represented history, one
with decisive criticism never registered, are indistinguishable to every check in
the module.
