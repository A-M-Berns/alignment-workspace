# Integration with legitimacy and Progress

## 1. The constrained object

Liability is not primarily a condition on one reason or one Answer-Mode certificate.
It constrains the **joint accumulated authority portfolio** generated by all operative
rows and all historical revisions. Rows may each be grounded and individually
underwritable while their combination has an unsupported-authority certificate.

The safe Progress realization interface is:

\[
\begin{aligned}
&\text{eligible Answer-Mode rows}\to\text{joint region/schedule},\\
&\text{joint region/schedule}\to
 \text{common or controlled-drift underwriting certificate},\\
&\text{certificate}+\text{market upper envelope}
 \to\text{bounded authority liability},\\
&\text{bounded liability}\to
 \text{EnforcementPreservation}\to\text{Uptake realization}.
\end{aligned}
\]

Schematic Progress does not consume liability. Traderized realization does.

## 2. Individually legitimate, jointly unaffordable

The anti-correlated two-profile example in `DUALITY.md` shows the distinction. The
right conclusion is not automatically that either reason is illegitimate. Possibilities
include unresolved conflict among demands, a poor descriptive settlement model, or
the need to adjudicate which rows may exert authority simultaneously. Liability
compatibility is therefore a Proper-Exercise constraint on joint leverage.

An infeasibility certificate is a reason to acknowledge and service the conflict. It
does not itself authorize weakening a row.

## 3. Authority revision and anti-reset

Three historical invariants have parallel interpretations:

| layer | retained fact |
| --- | --- |
| Grounded Replay | past authorization remains attributable |
| Continuity | outstanding matters remain answerable or explicitly disposed/carried |
| cumulative liability | past exercises of force remain financially attributable |

The third presently follows from the market ledger; it is not a new liveness pillar.
A successor may acquire a new license and region, but historical authority payoff
must remain in the consolidated account. Potential switching then prices the cost of
self-revision rather than pretending the past vanished.

## 4. Settlement types

### Descriptive/evaluative securities

They settle by a future evaluator or world fact. Covered compatibility is substantive:
future assessed outcomes may disagree with present normative rows. Synchronic or
diachronic failure may reveal either real normative conflict or semantic mismatch.

### Normative-status securities

If settlement independently records whether a reason/protocol status holds, world
inclusion may be natural and zero liability possible. That does not by itself prove a
status security supplies the service-value comparison needed for Progress.

### Synthetic learning feedback

Certified bounded losses can drive regret directly and carry no market settlement
liability. They preserve the Uptake algebra but give up market calibration and the
Logical Induction interpretation. Liability theory applies only if such scores are
turned into externally assessed securities.

## 5. Architectural placement

```text
Coverage -> represented reasons -> Continuity -> Progress
                  |                    |
          authority grounding     concrete leverage
                  |                    |
                  +------ Proper Exercise ------+
                              |
               joint underwriting / liability
```

- Coverage determines which reasons may acquire standing.
- Grounding explains why governing schemas are attributable.
- Continuity prevents lifecycle erasure.
- Progress prevents indefinitely inert service.
- Liability limits how the concrete authority implementing leverage may be subsidized
  or dominate the market.

By theorem dependency, liability is currently a Progress-realization and
Proper-Exercise condition, not a fourth schematic pillar. Its algebra is general
enough to apply to other authority exercises later.

## 6. Guarantees and non-guarantees

Liability adds a uniform downside bound and thereby allows the preservation theorem
to rule out authority-funded ordinary-trader exploitation. Grounding and Continuity
do not supply this financial invariant.

It does not prove correctness, legitimacy of source reasons, proper authorization,
Coverage, non-starvation, Uptake, conflict resolution, or eventual closure.
