issue descendants collectively realizes unresolved content. Neither part is sufficient
alone. The carrier frontier is

\[
C_n(m)=\{q\in Live_n(m):\lambda_n(m,q)\ne0\},

not raw `Live_n(m)`: structural helpers and empty shells need not carry semantic load.
This adds a semantic allocation relation, not a second lifecycle.

collective completeness projections. In the finite join model,

\[
\bigvee\text{parent load}
=Satisfy\vee Dispose\vee\bigvee\text{successor loads}.

The paper Answerability Conservation theorem follows by induction from this local
equation plus settled outstanding evolution, fresh successors, and matter ancestry. It
is not a Continuity restatement: a live bogus successor is a structural countermodel.
No settled Continuity or PR71 interaction definition changes.
