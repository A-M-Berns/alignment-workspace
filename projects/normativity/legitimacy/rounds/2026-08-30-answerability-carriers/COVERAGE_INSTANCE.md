# Coverage instance

For a coverage matter (m_σ), the anchored semantic domain contains the contract scope
and applicability semantics. At a prefix, its unresolved slice consists of criticisms
that are active and not represented. A matter-indexed issue load records which of those
criticisms a carrier remains answerable for. Route adequacy is servicing/implementation
state; it is neither semantic satisfaction nor disposition.

For a substantive contract transition (σ\rightsquigarrow σ'):

* `Satisfy(c)` requires qualifying representation/registration;
* `Dispose(c)` requires the anchored authorized-disposition and relevance evidence;
* `Transfer(c,c')` authenticates target/applicability translation and allocates (c') to
  a fresh successor carrier;
* collective completeness requires every live unresolved criticism in the affected
  scope to occur in one of those three components.

The finite fixture accepts translation of `{criticism-a, criticism-b}` and rejects a
successor contract containing only `criticism-a`.

## Exact NCSS

PR71's post-defect is

\[
Active_{n+1}(c)\land\neg Rep_{n+1}(c)
\land\neg\exists R\,Adeq_{n+1}(R,c).
\]

If a resolution affects the carrier for (c), the first two clauses rule out
Satisfaction; absence of authorized disposition rules out Disposition. Transfer
Completeness therefore requires (c)'s unresolved semantic load on a fresh live
successor. The third clause makes `Implements_{n+1}(σ)` false. Settled Continuity supplies
the child's matter ancestry. Thus Exact NCSS is the one-criticism instance of
Answerability Conservation plus the existing implementation definition.

No PR71 interaction structure changes. When a Transfer claim says only the inquiry
subsystem changed, it may consume PR71's authenticated receipt-conditioned residual
response-policy certificate. Different receipts may still produce different realized
actions. Locality is a domain-specific premise of that Transfer proof, not a field
required on every patch.

The generic theory sharpens one point already identified in PR71: an adequate route can
witness ongoing implementation while the claim stays carried; it cannot justify an
empty carrier frontier.
