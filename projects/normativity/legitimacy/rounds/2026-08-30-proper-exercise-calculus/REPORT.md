# Report: Proper Exercise refinement calculus

## Verdict in substance

A reusable calculus exists, but both objects under audit require local repair.  Proper
Exercise is a typed, proof-relevant judgment on an authenticated transition, with a
domain-specific completeness rule for affected burdens.  Its reusable semantic core is
many-to-many discharge/transport. A paper induction with unchanged Continuity yields the
proposed finite-horizon Answerability Conservation theorem. Liability shares the transition-
certificate shell but uses joint feasibility rather than burden transport.

PR71's `(BL)` preserves the wrong default object.  It is valid as an optional strong
realized-trace invariant, but legitimate inquiry variation can change information and
therefore the action selected by an unchanged residual response rule.  The minimal repair
is an authenticated receipt-conditioned response-rule interface; CF supplies the patch
decomposition but not that semantics.

Coverage closure also becomes cleaner: `CloseAdequate` is
`PE^resolve_coverage`.  Registration or authorized disposition can discharge the
coverage-to-registration burden.  An adequate route implements an ongoing entitlement
and must accompany a live carrier; it is not, by itself, a terminal discharge.

## Answers to the final questions

1. **Is PE transition-level?** Yes. Same-batch mutations make the post-state essential.
2. **Proof-relevant?** Yes. Transport, disposition, and feasibility claims need
   inspectable typed witnesses.
3. **Generic core?** Yes: affected-complete, many-to-many discharge/transport for
   answerability burdens. It is not the mathematics of every PE type.
4. **Reusable conservation theorem?** Yes. PE supplies semantic descent and Continuity
   supplies live structural carriers; finite induction yields Answerability Conservation.
5. **Is `CloseAdequate` an instance?** Yes, after distinguishing terminal discharge from
   nonterminal adequate-route carry.
6. **`PE^continue`?** It certifies target/applicability translation, live scope,
   exceptions, failures, response quality, and live successor carriers.
7. **`PE^met`?** It backs each false-to-true `Met` edge with receipt plus registration,
   direct satisfaction, authorized obsolescence, or target disposition—not channel loss.
8. **Keep `Permit` distinct?** Yes: authorization and proper use are independent.
9. **Liability?** It fits the same typed certificate shell but has different, joint-
   feasibility mathematics.
10. **Sequential composition?** Burden transports compose relationally when intermediate
    types and scope proofs match.
11. **Parallel composition failure?** Shared feasibility constraints can make two
    individually proper exercises jointly unsupported; the exact fixture has deficit
    \(1/4\).
12. **Is `(BL)` too strong?** Yes as default locality; no as an explicitly demanded trace
    invariant.
13. **Preserve response structure?** Yes: preserve the contingent rule, not necessarily
    its information-dependent realized action.
14. **Minimum structure?** Receipt type, downstream continuation type, authenticated
    residual rule semantics, and a realization/counterfactual-feeding certificate.
15. **More CF?** No structural surgery to CF is required; a better model-supplied
    semantic certificate is required.
16. **Where should locality live?** Keep the patch exterior-fixed; demand typed locality
    in route adequacy or PE exactly when a normative argument consumes it.
17. **Modify Continuity?** No. Existing exact evolution, resolution discipline, fresh
    successors, and ancestry are sufficient.

## Verification

`python3 tests/run.py` runs 16 exact finite tests. They instantiate authorization without
PE, same-batch closure, split/merge transport, ontology translation, `Met` witnesses,
joint liability, response-rule locality, predictor response, and typed self-modification.
`exhaustive_one_step_transport` enumerates all 16 Boolean combinations of affected,
changed, disposition, and post-carrier status in the one-burden fixture and checks that
every admitted step leaves a receipt or live frontier. The computation is test support,
not a registered checker verdict; the general theorem rests on the displayed induction.

## Deviations from the prompt

No Lean declaration was added because the semantic transport relation remains
model-supplied and unregistered. The pass also tightens terminal Coverage closure:
adequate-route existence is classified as carry rather than discharge. This follows from
the prompt's requirement to make `CloseAdequate` an exact PE instance.

## Boundary of the result

The theorem does not select morally correct burdens, prove certificate truth without a
model, provide exercise fairness, or force eventual repair.  It turns “semantic stuff”
into a checkable interface and a reusable conditional theorem, not a settled theory of
Proper Exercise.  All results remain unregistered.
