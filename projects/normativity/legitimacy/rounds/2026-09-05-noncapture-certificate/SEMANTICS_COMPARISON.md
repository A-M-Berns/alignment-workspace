# External semantics comparison

Status: paper-derived comparison of unregistered interfaces. No semantics is selected.

## Common output

Each external framework must return, for every declared `j in J`, a coupled `H^j` on
which the fixed or authenticated-transported coverage contract can be evaluated. The
certificate consumer needs only:

1. whether each anchored concern is live in `H` and `H^j`;
2. whether protected actual routes remain adequate, or a replacement is adequate;
3. whether `P` stands on the live carrier in `H^j`.

The framework must also authenticate what the comparison holds fixed. Exterior response
*policies* or strategies are fixed, not realized actions: responses may differ after a
different inquiry. The principal's policy is fixed unless it is the declared intervention
coordinate. The assessed process may vary only along `j`. Settlement and evaluator
engines are fixed when the application declares them exogenous; if they are intervention
targets, that fact belongs in `J` and their coverage-relevant effects are evaluated
through `Adeq`.

## Ancestry excision/replay

The 2026-08-25 criterion removes an intervention episode's settlement-ancestry class and
replays the normative record. It can supply branch histories and an independence verdict
for surviving standings or events. To pay this round's bill it must additionally:

- hold `sigma` fixed or authenticate its transport through replay;
- evaluate route adequacy and principal standing on every replayed branch;
- declare which episodes constitute `J` and which exterior facts remain exogenous.

Its landed analysis gives countermodels to monotonicity and composition of excision, so
the certificate must be checked separately for every declared intervention. Survival of
an authority basis is not by itself route adequacy or standing on the coverage carrier.

## Interventional coupling on a local patch

The coverage interaction interface supplies `beta(q,z)` with a certified factorization
that holds the exterior response structure fixed, plus authenticated residual response
structure. It naturally supplies the route-efficacy part of `(R+)`. To pay the whole bill
it must compare pre/post patches for each process intervention, preserve the anchored
target and applicability meanings, include registration and admissibility in `Adeq`, and
export the principal-relative standing state. A bare `beta` table supplies none of those
causal or authority judgments.

## Policy-class quantification

A transition semantics can quantify over every assessed-process or advisor policy in a
declared class. Each policy induces `H^pi`; the certificate asks `(S)`, `(R+)`, and `(P)`
pointwise. This form must specify the environment strategy, principal policy, settlement
engine, evaluator, and initial state held fixed across policies. Quantifying over all
policies does not authenticate that class, stop compensating exterior changes, or decide
which party controls a route.

## Outside the common bill

None of the three forms, merely by producing histories, establishes that `Gamma` is the
right normative scope, that the agency decomposition is correct, that settlement reports
are true of the world, that closure judgments are faithful, or that practical/value
semantics are counterfactually sound. Those are separately named application inputs.
