`x>=delta`.  Restrict the set to `[delta,D]` and the outer infimum to `0<delta<=D`.
The inverse corollary also needs a generalized inverse or the usual normalization
hypotheses stated explicitly.  This is an interface typing error.  All other gaps are
missing realization theorems or ambient assumptions, not reasons to redesign the
contract.
