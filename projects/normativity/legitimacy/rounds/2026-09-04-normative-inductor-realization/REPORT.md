# Report

Verdict: **ARCHITECTURE-SURVIVES; PUBLIC-NORMALIZATION-REPLACED;
AUTHENTICATED-VALUE-CALIBRATION-IS-THE-CRITICAL-OPEN-BRIDGE**.

The additive NI architecture remains the strongest construction supported by the
workspace: an ordinary assessment-process Logical Inductor plus one bounded-liability
joint projection enforcer and a separate decision adapter. The strict-prefix compiler
conjoins every simultaneously serviced obligation into one certified nonempty rational
polytope. Independent reason traders remain rejected by the exact interference
counterexample.

The original public convention
`d=dist_2/sqrt(m), a=m lambda` fails harmless-padding invariance. Padding a fragment
with zero-error coordinates lowers reported defect and raises reported service while
leaving Euclidean enforcement work unchanged. PR82 now uses

\[
d=\operatorname{dist}_\infty(b,K),\qquad a=\lambda.
\]

This is dimension-free and exactly invariant under enforcement-null product padding.
The existing projection-force theorem still supplies the needed work inequality because
`lambda d_infinity^2 <= lambda dist_2^2 <= rho`. Exact invariance does not cover adding
genuinely tradable securities that change the ordinary book; canonical security IDs
and an explicit harmless-presentation relation remain part of the compiler contract.

The action bridge is now semantically honest. A projection point is admissible, not
automatically a vector of true policy values. An exact example has `b in K` and zero
defect while its displayed argmax is the uniquely bad action. The sufficient bridge
therefore requires a separately authenticated set-valued counterfactual-value
correspondence with ambiguity `zeta`. For a randomized `eta`-approximate optimizer on
a finite policy menu, the new Lean algebra gives true-value regret at most

\[
2d+2\zeta+\eta.
\]

An anchored response-adequacy certificate then realizes the abstract response interface
with `M_es=2L_es` and
`epsilon_es=L_es(2 zeta_es+eta_s)+epsilon_resp_es`. The value correspondence is an
internal realization witness for the contract's already abstract response certificate;
it does not require a new public contract object.

Joint feasibility of `K_s` is a statement about market states and not about
responses.  One service occurrence realizes one decision distribution, so every
exposure matched to it needs its own anchored response certificate against that same
distribution; obligations demanding incompatible responses are separated into distinct
service contexts, adjudicated under a licensed upstream rule, given a common adequate
response, or left as residual evaluation mass.  The admissible-edge relation admits an
edge only when its certificate exists, so this is a realization obligation rather than
a contract change.

The compiler contract now separates correctness, completeness, convex
representability, and joint feasibility. A conflict or `Unknown` result conserves the
original obligations; it creates a new adjudication obligation only under an upstream
licensed rule. The feasibility hierarchy is

\[
\text{affordably enforceable}\subseteq\text{jointly feasible}
\subseteq\text{individually convex-representable}.
\]

Nonempty intersection suffices for projection, including singleton and
lower-dimensional regions. Interior/core hypotheses belong to liability arguments.

The old service-flow theorem normalizes into the contract's partial transport object.
The old `LK` hypotheses imply the new weighted-column bound, now with a Lean
normalization lemma. Shared service remains allowed. A concrete predictable-window
linear workload supplies a conditional adapted cheapest-window schedule with bounded
liability and zero terminal residual; arbitrary adversarial closed-loop scheduling is
not claimed.

Ordinary LI preservation is also stated more narrowly. Bounded assessed liability
Lean-proves nonexploitation for the added trader. A packaged Logical Inductor further
requires computability of the augmented market; the registered effective compiler
supplies this in the rational deductive/world-inclusive case, while the general
assessment-process realization retains it as an explicit premise.

## Contract finding

`phi` is declared only on `[0,D]`, but `check_phi(delta)` quantifies over unrestricted
`x>=delta`. Restrict it to `x in [delta,D]` and `0<delta<=D`. Use a generalized
inverse in the inverse corollary, or add continuity, strict monotonicity, and endpoint
hypotheses. No further public repair is required by this refinement.

## Deviations

The dispatch permitted retaining the old normalization if justified. It is replaced
because the exact padding counterexample shows that its public defect, service measure,
and mixed-date evaluation weights depend on redundant dimension. The architecture,
joint-region compiler, and abstract Progress bound are retained.

## Distance to an end-to-end theorem

The algebraic enforcement, normalization, randomized decision transfer, semantic
composition, and service normalization spine is now substantially stronger. The
end-to-end result remains conditional on a general compiler, an affordable scheduler
for the chosen workload, and external semantic assumptions. The single most important
unsolved bridge is a concrete authenticated counterfactual-value ecology proving value
calibration and anchored response adequacy. Without it, even zero operative defect can
induce a disastrous action.
