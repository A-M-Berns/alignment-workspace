# Witness-bridge countermodels, second pass

Status: research prosecution record. “Proves” below means the restricted paper theorem
applies under its displayed assumptions, not that a repository claim is registered.

## Classification key

- **PROVES:** the restricted witness theorem derives SW-density; PR69 Uptake therefore
  rules out the stagnant trace.
- **SILENT:** a load-bearing premise fails and the architecture makes no substantive
  claim. This is correct unless noted.
- **ANSWERED:** the reason is defeated, disposed, translated, or behaviorally taken up;
  no stagnation theorem should fire.

## H1 — persistent action-directed reason

Fixed `X={act-x,act-y}`, enabled reason row `v(act-y)-v(act-x)>=1/3`, full exposure,
and `p_n(act-x)>=1/2` forever.

Pairwise replacement has `g_n>=p_n(act-x)/3`, `W_N=A_N->infinity`, and positive defect
density. **PROVES.** This is the direct restricted lemma.

## H2 — persistent inquiry-directed reason

The external action remains uncertain. Fixed service labels are `ignore` and
`investigate`; a licensed inquiry-duty reason contributes
`v(investigate)-v(ignore)>=1/4`. Ignoring retains density `1/2`.

The same pairwise proof applies. **PROVES.** No claim about which external act is better
is used. This is the main positive service-response result.

## H3 — conflicting undefeated action reasons

One row requires `v(y)-v(x)>=1/2`; another requires `v(x)-v(y)>=1/2`. Their conjunction
is empty.

The compiler returns an infeasible conflict core and withholds `K`. The original action
witness theorem is **SILENT**, correctly. If a separate licensed procedural reason says
`open-adjudication > conceal-conflict`, the conflict-service surface is nonempty and its
restricted theorem **PROVES** uptake of adjudication. Conflict alone does not supply
that procedural comparison.

## H4 — changing applicability

The reason applies only on dates `2^k`; Continuity service is one unit every date. Then
`A_N=N` but `W_N=O(log N)`. This still diverges, so Persistent Relevance in its weakest
form holds and the theorem can **PROVE** if defect density on those dates is positive.

If applicability fires only finitely often, `W_N` is bounded and the theorem is
**SILENT**, correctly. Predictability prevents hindsight selection but does not imply
engagement.

## H5 — defect vanishes while the issue remains live

The reason and strict row persist, the surface is always exposed, but
`p_n(x)=1/(n+1)`. Then

\[
D_N/W_N\le(1+\log N)/N\to0.
\]

The reason is behaviorally answered even though no terminal resolution occurs.
**ANSWERED.** Event stagnation alone would incorrectly classify this trace, so the
failed formulation “no answer events means stagnant” is rejected.

## H6 — service never exposes the decision

`A_N=N`, but every service move is unrelated bookkeeping and `c_n=0`. Hence `W_N=0`.

Persistent Relevance fails and the theorem is **SILENT**. This is the exact relevance
obligation; burying it inside “unanswered” would misclassify attention theater.

## H7 — reason defeated after long service

For a million dates, the reason is active and ignored. An accepted defeater then removes
its applicability source; no later row is compiled.

There is no indefinitely normatively unanswered tail. **ANSWERED.** Past foregone gain
remains historically auditable but basic Progress makes no post-defeat demand. A new
occurrence with identical content would be a new reason identity.

## H8 — evaluator revision preserving the old reason

`q` resolves into `q'`; the transition records `r -> r'`, preserves the fixed service
surface, row, margin, and license ancestry, and does not defeat the burden. Defective
response retains positive density.

Under the special cross-episode preservation condition, one fixed semantic repair still
exists and the theorem **PROVES** across the translated tail. Without an explicit
surface-preservation equation, mere identity linkage is insufficient.

## H9 — evaluator revision explicitly defeating the reason

The transition's old anchored protocol accepts a defeater and records it before the new
episode opens. No translation is owed.

**ANSWERED.** The new evaluator is not retroactively deciding the old reason; the old
episode's accepted disposition does the work.

## H10 — endless successor churn

Every date opens a fresh legitimate successor and translates the unresolved burden, but
also changes the response labels or comparison. There is no stable repair program.

Reason Carry prevents disappearance by omission, but the fixed-witness theorem is
**SILENT**. Basic Progress continues to count explicit revisions. This remains the main
diachronic loophole for a stronger theorem; closing it needs translation invariance or a
larger cross-era theory, not merely provenance.

## H11 — incompatible affine rows

Rows require `v(a)>=3/4` and `v(a)<=1/4`. Any compiler that silently drops one can
manufacture whichever repair its policy prefers.

The feasibility gate returns `Infeasible`, records both row provenances, and creates a
Continuity conflict issue. Original region Progress is **SILENT**, correctly. A
separately licensed conflict-response comparison may prove progress in servicing the
conflict.

## H12 — dominance only on a face

`K` admits valuations on and off face `F`. On `F`, `v(y)-v(x)>=1/2`; off `F`, the
ordering reverses. The reason's prose mentions `F`, but no operative constraint selects
it.

Whole-region robust gain is negative, so the theorem is **SILENT**, correctly. If an
independent event/constraint establishes `K subset F`, the row becomes globally sound
for that episode and the theorem **PROVES**. Failed formulation: replace `K` by the
favorable face without an authenticated context-selection step.

## H13 — two intermittently useful repairs, no stable one

On even dates repair `rho_0` has positive gain and `rho_1` negative; on odd dates the
signs reverse. Each fixed repair has zero or negative signed average, while some repair
is always locally attractive.

No eventual Sensitivity witness exists. The restricted theorem is **SILENT**, correctly.
This remains repair churn, not a reason to reopen signed Uptake or the stagnant-tail
strategy. A typed reason with one stable source/target row would rule this trace out by
hypothesis.

## H14 — inquiry question without inquiry-duty comparison

A genuine unresolved question is represented and enabled, but its target is `Question`,
not `ServiceCompare`; the practice contains no norm ranking investigation over deferral.

The compiler emits no row and the theorem is **SILENT**. This is a minimal obstruction
to universal service witness completeness. Merely adding an `investigate` label to `X`
does not give it normative force.

## H15 — service response exists but is infeasible

A typed reason says “perform test T rather than ignore,” but T requires unavailable
equipment and the decoder rejects the label at the prefix.

The reason is not row-operative on that surface. **SILENT.** A different represented
answer mode—request equipment, record impossibility, or open a successor—could yield a
new comparison, but the compiler may not invent it.

## H16 — explicit acknowledgement without resolution

A conflict-handling reason ranks `acknowledge-unresolved-conflict` over concealment. The
reasoner assigns acknowledgement probability tending to one but the conflict issue stays
open.

The designated concealment defect vanishes. **ANSWERED** behaviorally for this reason;
continued inquiry can remain due for a different reason occurrence. Progress does not
equate acknowledgement with terminal resolution.

## H17 — burden translation without reason carry

Each successor retains semantically identical prose but omits the old reason identity,
its sources, and any disposition. A fresh evaluator then claims no reason is pending.

Continuity alone admits the issue transitions. The new Reason Carry condition rejects
the omission at the realization boundary. Without Reason Carry, basic Progress is
**SILENT** and burden laundering remains possible. This demonstrates a useful cheap
intermediate condition, not a complete diachronic theorem.

## Failed formulations retained

1. **Event-only stagnation.** Fails H5: behavior can answer without an event.
2. **Normative-unanswered alone.** Fails H5 and H6: neither defect nor engagement
   follows.
3. **Engagement inside the definition of unanswered.** Hides H6 instead of rejecting
   the service implementation.
4. **Every question compiles to investigate > ignore.** Fails H14 by adding a procedural
   norm absent from the reason state.
5. **Conflict rows are prioritized by the compiler.** Fails H3/H11 by silently doing
   adjudicative work.
6. **Local-face infimum.** Fails H12 by laundering dissenting admissible valuations.
7. **Reason identity carry alone gives a stable repair.** Fails H10 when translations
   change surfaces.
8. **Every affine row has a repair.** False unless the coefficient is a feasible
   distribution difference with the required response-dependent scaling.

## Overall prosecution result

No hostile example refutes PR69 or the restricted theorem. The service-response move
turns inquiry, defeater assessment, conflict handling, and revision into the same
pairwise mathematics **when** a typed licensed answer-mode comparison exists. H14 and
H15 show the remaining boundary exactly: availability of a service label and genuine
importance of a reason do not themselves entail strict comparative force.
