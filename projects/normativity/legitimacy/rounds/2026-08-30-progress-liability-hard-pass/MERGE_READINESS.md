# PR69 merge readiness after the hard pass

## What PR69 has now established

### Schematic Progress

The episode-local theorem is stable:

\[
Continuity\ service
+Surface\ Fairness
+Answer\text{-}Mode\ Adequacy
+signed\ Uptake
\Longrightarrow D_N/W_N\to0.
\]

Persistent Relevance is derived from a concrete service-interface condition. Finite
stochastic repair kernels supply Sensitivity. The conclusion is vanishing weighted
defect density, not closure or cross-era convergence.

### Liability realization

This pass proves a nontrivial finite multi-repair authority-safety route. One covered
assessed-world mixture whose barycenter satisfies every simultaneously enforced
Answer-Mode row gives

\[
E_{\le N}(\omega)\ge-3(1-\theta)/\theta.
\]

The proof composes current projection nonnegativity, the MarketMaker cap, the proved
TradingFirm floor, and `EnforcementPreservation`. It permits individual worlds that
violate normative rows and is independent of tolerance. Directional enforcement is
enough for the Progress master inequality.

### Exact negative boundary

Separate row affordability and per-date compatible mixtures do not compose. PR50's
pump violates temporal common compatibility because its low/high bands are disjoint.
Time multiplexing without a common potential can reproduce the pump. The broader
moving-region and cross-era problem remains real.

## Should this debt block merging the research checkpoint?

No. PR69 makes no claim that arbitrary descriptive value constraints are affordable,
and the hard pass now gives both:

- a proved meaningful finite multi-repair sufficient condition; and
- an exact explanation of the main counterexample outside that condition.

That is enough for a research checkpoint: the architecture is neither hand-waving at
liability nor claiming a universal theorem. The remaining debt should block a claim
that the full value-security realization is settled, but not merging the round that
records its valid fragment and boundary.
