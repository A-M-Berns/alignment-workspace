# What kind of security is being enforced?

## The liability problem is partly semantic

Projection geometry does not decide whether a service-value row is legitimate or
affordable. Affordability depends on how the security pays in assessed worlds. The
same syntactic comparison can be zero-liability, common-mixture affordable, or
incompatible depending on settlement semantics.

## 1. Descriptive or evaluative securities

These settle according to a future anchored evaluator, observed outcome, or policy
value that may disagree with today's reason rows.

- **Benefit:** prices aggregate contestable predictive/evaluative information; regret
  responds to an endogenous estimate rather than a command.
- **Cost:** `K` may exclude every mixture with adequate coverage, especially after the
  evaluator settles or revises. Liability is real rather than bookkeeping.
- **Correct condition:** a covered compatible mixture is a substantive calibration
  hypothesis about possible future evaluator outcomes. It must be checked, not
  declared by authority.

World inclusion is generally unnatural here: requiring every possible evaluator
settlement to satisfy the current normative comparison builds agreement into a
descriptive object.

## 2. Normative-status securities

These settle on facts such as “reason `r` remained legitimately operative,” “the
required adjudication occurred,” or “a licensed disposition was recorded.”

- **Benefit:** protocol rules may independently entail that certain settlement
  profiles satisfy the operative rows. World inclusion or a common mixture can then be
  natural rather than circular.
- **Risk:** a status price is not automatically a value for choosing one service mode
  over another. A separate theorem must translate status into the repair gain consumed
  by regret.

If the row merely restates the settlement definition, zero liability is legitimate
but may say little about behavioral value. If the protocol has an independent
semantics of proper response, it can be a useful restricted existence case.

## 3. Synthetic learning scores

These are bounded feedback vectors emitted by the licensed normative process. They do
not claim to predict an external quantity and have no eventual market settlement.

- **Benefit:** no authority portfolio, assessed loss, or Service-Value Liability
  problem arises.
- **Cost:** the feedback is normative control rather than market belief. It loses
  Logical Induction calibration, trader aggregation, and the ability of descriptive
  evidence to push back through prices.

This distinction diagnoses the hardest cases: imposing normative rows on descriptive
future-evaluator securities is what creates the nontrivial liability obligation. The
problem is not an inevitable feature of Progress itself.

## Direct certified-loss realization

Let the compiler choose predictably any rational `v_n in K_n` and send the external
repair learner

\[
\ell_n=\mathbf1-v_n.
\]

For every repair,

\[
\langle p_n-\Phi(p_n),\ell_n\rangle
=\langle\Phi(p_n)-p_n,v_n\rangle.
\]

Because robust gain is the infimum over `K_n`,

\[
g_n\le\langle\Phi(p_n)-p_n,v_n\rangle.
\]

Thus the same confidence-rated regret bound directly implies signed Uptake, with no
projection error or authority liability. A nonempty rational polytope supplies a
computable rational vertex or other canonical point.

This is a mathematically valid nonmarket realization of basic Progress. It does not
collapse semantic disagreement inside `K`: the schematic robust gain still requires
all admissible valuations to favor the repair. But the selected feedback does not
aggregate independent beliefs about which admissible valuation is descriptively
correct.

## What value securities buy

The market route adds:

1. endogenous numerical estimates connected to the rest of the belief state;
2. contestability by ordinary traders;
3. Logical Induction calibration/non-exploitation guarantees;
4. a common price language in which reasons, predictions and other constraints can
   interact.

The direct route adds none of these. It shows that failure of market affordability
would block one realization, not Progress or Uptake.

## Recommendation for the research architecture

Retain all three as explicitly different realization classes:

- use descriptive value securities only when covered compatibility or another proved
  liability certificate is available;
- use normative-status securities when settlement rules independently justify their
  payoff constraints and a status-to-gain bridge is proved;
- recognize direct certified losses as the minimal existence realization of Uptake,
  not as a Logical Induction realization.

The common-mixture theorem gives a real positive reason to keep the market route. No
redesign is forced. The compiler must nevertheless type the security semantics; it
cannot treat these objects as interchangeable.

## Revision and historical loss

A fixed episode can anchor one settlement language and one covered mixture. Across
revision:

- a single mixture compatible with all old and new rows preserves the theorem;
- a new mixture compatible only with new rows does not revalue old authority trades;
- summable evaluator movement needs an additional turnover/potential-switching bound;
- a fresh account changes attribution, not historical net worth.

Episode-local Progress is therefore compatible with the theorem proved here. General
cross-era descriptive value enforcement remains a stronger deferred theory.
