# Report

## Result

The phase is canonicalized. The theory a reader meets on the wiki's Legitimacy page
and its component pages is the final pressure pass's, with one interface change: the
intervention semantics is indexed by the accounted obligation state, not the history
prefix, so the carrier of a concern's load is read off the accounts. Sixteen claims
are registered as *The legitimacy spine*; the decision ledger carries a dated
canonicalization entry promoting the agent-decided entries and superseding two; the
vocabulary carries the preferred labels and the deprecated ones; the Roadmap's
legitimacy section is three lists.

## Landing

The three stacked pull requests were linear on top of `main`. Landing them in order
would have put the integrity synthesis's failing axiom audit on `main` for one merge.
Instead `round/2026-09-08-canonicalization` was branched from the pressure pass's
head, carries this round on top of the eleven inherited commits, and is merged with a
merge commit so that every commit — the six worker rounds, the synthesis, the
consolidation, the pressure pass — is reachable from `main` and the wiki's
commit-pinned citations into them resolve permanently. The three stacked pull
requests are closed as superseded, each with a comment naming the landing pull
request. The SHAs are in the pull request and the decision ledger entry.
