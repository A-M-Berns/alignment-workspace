## Result

`READINESS.md` §1 lists the six pressure points and their repairs; §7 is the verdict:
**READY FOR FULL CANONICALIZATION**. Two of the six were genuine type-level defects
rather than presentation: the counterfactual-only Robust Openness never contained the
actual-branch standing condition Diachronic Answerability was said to read from it
(`counterfactual_open_not_actual`), and a valid closure produced answer evidence
(`Evidence`, now `Resolution`). One was a false claim: `LocalLaw` does not express
many-to-one aggregation. The other three were missing structure now supplied: the
accounted state, the evolution relation with explicit intermediate states, and
openness at every state, with the exact trajectory endpoint-only openness wrongly
accepts.

On §2's alternatives: openness at every prefix, at every transition, and only while a
concern is live coincide here — every state of an evolution is adjacent to a
transition, and `Covered` and `OpenTo` are already conditional on liveness and
applicability — so per-state openness is the weakest form expressible in the
coverage-state vocabulary, not merely the simplest. All four named attacks (standing
lost, routes lost, settlement access suppressed where routed, representation delayed)
are failures of `RobustOpenActual` at the affected prefix.

On §3's options: B (actual branch explicit, `J` for proper interventions) is adopted;
A would put a distinguished element into every application's intervention index and
make `cf null = actual` a proof obligation of the semantics rather than a definition.
