# Report: CF Coverage Continuity interface

Status: research architecture and exact finite fixtures; unregistered. Coverage, Proper
Exercise, and the legitimacy theory remain open.

## Result

The proposed bare patch \((Q,Z,\beta,q^{act})\) is too weak: any comparison table has
that type. Ambient factorization `(CFP)` additionally fixes the exterior response
strategy, but does not fix residual-agent behavior. The minimum local repair adds a
contract-authenticated residual observation \(p_R\) and requires its outcome to be
invariant across queries against every exterior. Literal product decomposition of the
full agent is unnecessary and would not itself prove this invariance.

With this repair, Coverage is a realization layer for the settled Continuity interface.
No Continuity structure or theorem needs to change. A coverage Due rising edge opens an
ordinary anchored matter; interaction routes realize prerequisites and semantic
judgments but are not Continuity's procedural issue routes.

No Clean Self-Sealing has a sharp split. It is false from Continuity alone: an anchored
`Resolve` oracle may accept terminal closure immediately after route destruction, and the
trace satisfies every structural requirement. If no resolution is recorded, persistence
of the matter is only the existing bookkeeping theorem. A genuine safety theorem follows
from local closure adequacy plus unchanged resolution/successor continuity, provided the
criticism remains active after the transition. It concludes that the anchored matter
stays live while `Implements` is false, forbidding both silent deletion and explicit bogus
closure. A separately materialized failure issue needs an additional compiler condition.
Neither form implies route repair or representation.

Eventual representation requires separate repair, opportunity, exercise, and
registration fairness together with Progress on persistent coverage failures. NSA alone
allows unbounded attention theater.

## Answers to the final questions

1. **Minimal interaction interface.** A finite global history carrier with nested prefix
   cylinders; a behaviorally certified local patch; admissible continuation policies; a target and
   applicability preservation certificate; and a receipt map. Registration and contract
   quality sit one layer above.
2. **Is \(P=(Q,Z,\beta)\) right?** It is the right computational payload and an
   insufficient proof object. Export \((P,\mathcal L)\), where \(\mathcal L\) is the
   factorization `(CFP)` through an authenticated ambient frame plus behavioral locality
   relative to protected residual observations.
3. **Can CF generate/certify patches?** Partly. A local
   \(\alpha:Q\times R\to A\) and \(Z\to R\times E\) give a commuting outcome square.
   Composing outcomes with supplied residual observations certifies behavioral locality.
   CF and its subagent relations do not choose those observations or identify inquiry.
4. **What is fixed in \(Z\)?** A residual agent continuation policy and an exterior
   response strategy/function, plus any exogenous target state. A realized response or
   trajectory is not fixed when it legitimately reacts to \(q\).
5. **Is `(TP)` right?** It is the right exact target-relative invariance test, not a
   universal observation/intervention definition. Quotient, transported, and
   mechanism/potential-outcome variants are sometimes required; applicability needs its
   own preservation certificate.
6. **Probability-free accessibility.** For one executable adaptive policy,
   \(\exists q,d_q:T=d_q\circ Y_q\), equivalently
   \(\pi_{Y_q}\le\pi_T\) under the finer-than order. A join over policies describes a
   repeatable/parallel portfolio and is invalid for one-run learning unless those
   experiments are jointly executable.
7. **Exposure/Registration.** Yes. The split is necessary and independently witnessed.
   Exposure is target information in a receipt; registration is a policy/compiler event
   producing a stable criticism identity or an explicit admission issue.
8. **Coverage contract.** An anchored tuple
   \((a,\Gamma,Rel,Adeq,Disp)\) specifying stable criticism identities, auditable
   relevance, route quality, and authorized disposition.
9. **`Implements_h(σ)`.** Every active, undisposed, unrepresented criticism in the
   anchored scope has an end-to-end adequate certified route. Joint resource feasibility
   is an additional portfolio condition when routes compete.
10. **What belongs inside Continuity?** Only ordinary Due/issue/matter/prerequisite
    records and the existing semantic judgments. Patch, target, exposure, registration,
    and adequacy definitions stay outside.
11. **Can Continuity remain unchanged?** Yes. The exact Lean spine already exposes all
    required lifecycle hooks.
12. **Exact connection.** `Due(maintainCoverage,σ)` opens \(q_\sigma,m_\sigma\);
    `Resolve` receives local closure adequacy; `Continue` transports contract
    state on substantive succession; `Met(d_T)` is justified by successful
    receipt/registration or authorized obsolescence; existing prerequisites and
    procedural routes manage known inquiries. Coverage can help realize Wait
    Responsiveness only with a fairness rule that eventually satisfies, withdraws, or
    repairs every permanent no-route wait.
13. **Proper Exercise.** Best understood as typed semantic refinements of Continuity's
    abstract judgments plus joint constraints such as liability underwriting. A single
    undifferentiated predicate loses the consumers.
14. **Is NCSS genuine?** Not from Continuity alone. With post-transition activity and
    local closure adequacy it
    is a genuine one-step safety theorem ruling out an explicit false-success resolution
    and preserving a live defective matter; without it NCSS is false or bookkeeping.
    A dedicated failure locus additionally requires Failure Materialization.
15. **What yields eventual representation?** Coverage safety; persistent relevance;
    repair fairness; unbounded query opportunity; exercise fairness; receipt soundness;
    registration fairness; and Progress excluding permanently inert repair/admission
    states. Each has a separate countermodel.
16. **Is \(\Gamma\) the main genesis boundary?** Yes. This theory is relative to a
    supplied historically anchored family and does not derive which criticisms deserve
    inclusion.
17. **Role of FFS/FSM.** Optional realization/proof tools for macrovariable
    factorization, conditional independence, and construction of certified patches.
    They are unnecessary for target preservation, partition accessibility, the
    Continuity bridge, or the NCSS safety proof. They do not solve agency-boundary or
    target-authentication choices.

## CF versus FFS/FSM

The in-repository CF formalization supplies exactly the needed ambient type and confirms
that additive/multiplicative subagency are relative structural witnesses. No existing
FFS/FSM artifact was found in the workspace. Escalation to those formalisms would add a
factorization calculus but would not remove the missing legitimacy certificate. The
thin first interface should therefore remain CF-relative and admit FFS/FSM certificates
as optional implementations.

## Verification

`python3 tests/run.py` checks twenty complete finite beta tables and their target
preservation, single-policy accessibility, registration capability under the actual
policy, and contract
implementation classifications. It also checks the three key separations: target change
despite access, exposure without registration, and route existence without exercise. An
exhaustive 32-case Boolean model checks the NCSS safety skeleton and exhibits the clean
self-sealing step admitted when resolution soundness is absent.

The architectural theorem is a paper proof, not a statement of record. No Lean was added
because the interface is not stable and the proposed bridge changes no existing Lean
type.

## What was not shown

- The certified-patch legitimacy witness is supplied; no theorem derives the correct
  agent/exterior boundary.
- The global finite history carrier is a model-relative ontology, not a universal
  embedded-agency ontology.
- Target identity, applicability, criticism scope, and authorized disposition are
  semantic inputs.
- Failure materialization, resolution soundness, Wait Responsiveness, non-starvation,
  repair fairness, registration fairness, and Progress are not derived.
- NCSS does not guarantee discovery; NPS is conditional and unformalized.
- Universal, uncountable, probabilistic, and resource-competitive criticism families are
  not solved.
- FFS/FSM were not imported or formalized.

## Deviations

No Lean bridge lemma was added: inspection of `IssueTrace` shows Coverage supplies no new
field, so a typechecking lemma would only restate that the semantic realization is
external. The original FFS/FSM formalisms were not fetched because the workspace's CF
surface and elementary partition mathematics decide the interface question; their
possible role remains optional rather than load-bearing.
