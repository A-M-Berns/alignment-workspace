# Report: Progress consolidation and realization pressure

## Executive result

The basic schematic has compressed successfully. `SW-density` is no longer needed in
the main statement; Persistent Relevance follows from a concrete bounded-deficit
Surface Fairness contract, and Answer-Mode Adequacy plus a finite stochastic repair
kernel proves the witness inequality for a materially broader class than pairwise
replacement. Strict-prefix timing is coherent, and sampled responses inherit the
mixed-policy conclusion almost surely.

The concrete realization is not yet settlement-ready. Projection supplies exactly
the requested per-date distance bound, including for changing finite rational
polytopes. PR50 supplies a credible restricted affordability route for an isolated,
positive-margin service-value coordinate and a counterexample to naive
multi-coordinate composition. Two independent seams therefore remain: that restricted
model result has not been promoted or generalized to the compiled repair family, and
the exact anytime confidence-rated causal modification-regret package is not yet a
workspace theorem.

## Answers to the final questions

### 1. What is the cleanest final schematic Progress theorem?

For a fixed matter, episode, eligible reason occurrence and finite response surface:
Continuity service plus Surface Fairness gives unbounded relevant service;
Answer-Mode Adequacy gives a stable repair with `g_n>=gamma d_n`; signed Uptake then
gives `D_N/W_N->0`. Therefore an operative unanswered reason cannot retain positive
defect density on that episode tail. The theorem does not promise closure.

### 2. Should `SW-density` remain in the main theorem?

No. Retain it only as a convenience lemma for comparison with PR69. The main theorem
should expose Surface Fairness, Answer-Mode Adequacy and Stagnation Persistence.

### 3. Can Persistent Relevance be reduced to service-interface fairness?

Yes. If reason-specific surface exposure has a fixed positive bounded-deficit share
of the matter's service mass, then

\[
W_N-W_{N_0}\ge\eta(A_N-A_{N_0})-C,
\]

so Continuity's `A_N->infinity` implies `W_N->infinity`. Fixed exposure, finite fair
rotation and reason-to-work-node ownership are concrete sufficient interfaces.
`Reach/Work` alone does not imply the intra-matter share.

### 4. Is Answer-Mode Adequacy the right boundary?

Yes for basic Progress. It covers exactly reasons whose licensed semantics identify
defective nonresponse modes, feasible recognized answers and a strict uniform service
comparison. It admits action, inquiry, adjudication, defeater assessment, revision
and certified impossibility without claiming inquiry or acknowledgment is always
better. Reasons lacking such a certificate remain outside this theorem.

### 5. How far does the pairwise theorem generalize cleanly?

To finite stochastic repair kernels. If each defective source `x` is moved to a
distribution `mu_x` over acceptable modes and every direction `mu_x-e_x` has margin
at least `gamma`, then gain is at least `gamma p(S)`. Equivalently, any finite conic
decomposition `Phi(p)-p=sum_j d_j(p)u_j` with nonnegative coefficients and certified
directions yields Sensitivity. Arbitrary affine rows and nonlinear repairs are not
earned.

### 6. Can mixed-policy elimination be upgraded to realized actions?

Yes, optionally. With predictable bounded weights, conditional sampling from `p_n`,
and `W_N->infinity`, the weighted sampling error is a bounded-increment martingale
whose normalized value tends to zero almost surely. Hence mixed defect density zero
implies realized weighted defective frequency zero almost surely.

### 7. Do current traderization results give the needed error control?

Yes for conformance. `RationalConstraintSchedule.conformance_of_constraints` and
`criterion_of_constraints`, together with `sup_conformance_of_dist2`, give a
same-tolerance coordinatewise bound for arbitrary date-varying nonempty rational
polytopes on finite fragments. A vanishing rational tolerance schedule gives weighted
vanishing error by Cesaro. Full computable Logical-Inductor realization additionally
uses the existing effective representation interface.

### 8. Are service-value constraints compatible with existing liability conditions?

Only conditionally. World inclusion gives zero liability, and a summable core/generic
charge gives another route. PR50 adds the important nonzero-liability case: in its
one-coordinate model, a stationary interior constraint with deductive containment,
uniform plausibility margin and no cross-coordinate subsidy is war-chest-affordable
independently of tolerance; summable set-gap motion is the proposed extension. But
PR50 is open, unregistered model evidence, and its cross-coordinate recycling witness
breaks the naive generalization. The missing work is now an exact restricted promotion
theorem plus a genuinely multi-coordinate joint-margin or non-recycling theorem.

### 9. What exact regret theorem is needed?

An anytime full-information theorem for fixed finite actions and a finite family of
causal stochastic repair programs, with predictable confidence weights and
`B_r(W)=o(W)` simultaneously for every repair. Standard confidence-rated external
regret and modification-regret reductions plausibly supply it; a finite-class
effective-mass/doubling composition lemma remains to be imported or proved.

### 10. Is synchronous price/response timing coherent?

Yes. Compile `K,c,Phi` and schedule attention from `H_n`; choose and sample `p_n`;
then reveal a market quote generated independently of the same-round response; then
score and record events. The market may compute concurrently, but its quote cannot
read `p_n`. This matches strict-prefix Continuity and adversarial full-information
regret.

### 11. Does Grounded Replay have a genuine consumer?

Yes. Operative Row Grounding requires every compiled row to cite a current or
same-episode anchored license in `Adm_n`; `grounded_replay_admitted` then supplies
finite authority ancestry. Successors need a fresh current license. This proves
provenance, not Proper Exercise or semantic truth.

### 12. Is Reason Carry cheap enough to require?

Burden carry is. An unresolved reason must be disposed or translated with identity,
provenance, burden status, answer-mode/surface mapping and a fresh successor-license
obligation. Numerical comparison invariance is stronger and optional. This prevents
one-step shedding without claiming cross-era progress.

### 13. Can a richer toy satisfy the current requirements?

Yes. A four-mode model supports action and inquiry reasons, explicitly defeats a
contrary reason at evaluator revision, carries the two unresolved burdens with fresh
licenses, compiles a nonempty two-row polytope, alternates reason exposure at positive
share, enforces tolerance `1/(n+1)`, and has sublinear repair advantage with vanishing
defects. Choosing anchored settlements inside the region gives zero liability. The toy
demonstrates joint satisfiability but also exposes the strength of that liability
assumption.

### 14. What remains before a genuine settlement pass?

The schematic finite-kernel theorem can now be formalized and audited. A complete
concrete settlement still needs (i) typed compilation plus promotion of PR50's
restricted affordability result and a multi-coordinate liability condition, and
(ii) the exact finite confidence-rated modification-regret package. Infinite revision, comparison
invariance across eras and universal reason completeness remain deliberately deferred.
