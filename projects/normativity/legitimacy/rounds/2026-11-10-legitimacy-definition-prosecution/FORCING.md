# Forced revisions

A forced revision is a change to the definition compelled by a banked case, recorded
here with the case as its certificate, in the form

    FORCED-REVISION <case>: <what changed in the definition> — <why the case compels it>

`run.py` accepts a divergence between a computed verdict and its pre-registration only
when a record naming that case stands here. Pre-registered verdicts are never edited.
