## 6. What the comparison shows

Where a case states how she now rates the course the adviser's conduct leads to, the two
renderers give the same answer on line 3: ten of ten, five by an explicit rating and five
because the conduct plainly leaves her valuing alone. Where it does not, one renderer read
her present wants as that rating and the other declined to. The literature's cases are
written to test whether a resulting desire is hers; most do not say what she thought, before,
of the course that changed her, which is the one thing line 3 asks.
