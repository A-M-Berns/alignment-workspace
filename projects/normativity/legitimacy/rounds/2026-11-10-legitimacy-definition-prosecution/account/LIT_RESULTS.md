## 1. Codes

- **adv**: `Y` an adviser acts; `N` no one does, and the case is no instance of the
  statement.
- **chg**: what the adviser's conduct changes — `f` her way of valuing; `app` her
  appraisals; `opt` her options; `act` which action she takes; `none`; `?` not fixed. An
  arrow is a change that follows from another.
- **L1, L2, L3, 3′**: `R` raised (for L3 and 3′: positive); `N` not raised (for L3 also:
  the conduct is f-neutral and line 3 does not apply); `?` not fixed by the case; `-` no
  adviser. A trailing `.r` marks an answer that rests on a reading the last column
  states. L1 and L2 are relative to the role's conduct (`BOARD.md` §5.9). 3′ is the
  addendum's variant (`BOARD.md` §7); the addendum arrived after the bank was written and
  before any case was classified.
- **verdict**, the statement's on the adviser's act: `F` flagged; `P` passes; `?` not
  fixed; `-` no instance.
- **vs**, against the source's verdict in `LIT_CASES.md` Part B: `A` agrees; `D`
  disagrees; `X` the source answers a different question (its verdict is on her desire,
  her choice or her consent); `?` the statement does not decide.
- **bk**: `Y` when line 3's answer is different according as something in the case is
  booked as a fact she appraises or as part of her valuing.

## 4. What the classification shows

1. **Where the case says how she now rates the course, line 3 gives the source's verdict
   on the act**: P3, P4, P5n, P6, K2, K14, C1, C4.
2. **P5.** A man who wants parenthood, knowing or not that he will rate his whole life low
   as a parent, is nudged into it: the statement passes it unless his present wish is a
   wish for a life he will find good. Pettigrew's test flags it by giving the later self
   weight; the statement has no later self in it.
3. **K13.** The statement has no place for an act that changes her against her will and is
   justified by what others are owed.
4. **N1.** As in the ninth pass (case C16): where every conduct open to the role frames,
   no line is raised.
5. **K26.** An adviser that lets a change happen it could have prevented by a word is
   f-neutral. Line 3 never reaches an omission; only a role that owes the telling does.
6. **K4 and K12.** The verdict is carried by reading `f_now` as attached to what she now
   does. The cases stipulate the new activity no worse and her no worse off, and with her
   indifferent K12 passes all three lines.
7. **K9, K10, K11.** A want formed by living through what was done to her is a change of
   `f` or a discovery, and the case does not say which. As a change, line 3 flags the
   adviser for the harm of its means and is silent on the want; as a discovery, no line
   is raised.
8. **Harman's ten.** None is an adviser's act. The statement shares one thing with her:
   gladness that will come from attachment is not consulted. It does not share another:
   she takes foreseen gladness to be ordinarily good evidence of what one should do
   (H1, H2), where the statement admits it only as evidence about a fact.
