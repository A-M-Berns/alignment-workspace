# Ledger of the legitimacy line (2026-11-26)

One row per component of the line. The line has three layers and one result from the second,
and the ledger a part for each: the statement in words (`LEGITIMACY_STATEMENT.md` §1, §2),
the formal statement over notions taken as given (§3; `LegitimacyStatement.lean`), the
interpretation, which works those notions out on histories
(`interpretation/INTERPRETATION.md`), and the connecting theorem, which says what the formal
statement gives for her decision to stop him (`CONNECTING_THEOREM.md`; `LegitimacyStop.lean`).
**What is stable**: the three sentences, held by the maintainer and selected by his verdicts
on twenty hard cases; the theorems of the two Lean files, which are kernel-checked. **What is
not**: any way of deciding, for a concrete episode, whether the statement holds of it; and
whether the hypotheses of the connecting theorem follow from the statement otherwise than by
a reading that asserts them.

A verdict is **closed** (a Lean declaration or a checker invocation stands for it, with the
event that reopens it), **open**, or **living note** (a position or a description, held and
revisable, that no check adjudicates). *Lean* files are under
`lean/Workspace/Deference/Contrib/`; `LegitimacyStatement.lean`, `LegitimacyGate.lean` and
`LegitimacyStop.lean` have the namespaces `Workspace.Deference.Contrib.LegitimacyStatement`,
`…LegitimacyGate` and `…LegitimacyStop`; no declaration of the three is registered. *The
runner* is `python3 run.py` in the round directory. Items are those of `ATTENTION.md`. Paths
are relative to this directory. A row's number is its identifier, not its position.

Kinds of evidence, strongest first: **kernel** (kernel-checked, of the declaration as
stated); **exact** (an exact check whose inputs an episode supplies); **coding** (a blind
coding by one language model against a scorer written first); **verdict** (the maintainer's
verdict on a written case, `HARD_CASES.md`); **review** (the outside model; reviews 8 to 15
each a fresh session that read only its brief, reviews 2 to 7 not blind; the replies are in
`REVIEW-astra-followup-16.md`, `REVIEW-astra-followup-20.md` and
`REVIEW-astra-followup-21.md` in the round's prompts directory); **reasoning** (argument
only).

Closed rows share one reopening event unless they name another: `python3 tests/audit_axioms.py`
fails on the declaration, or a commit changes its statement or the statement of a definition
it mentions.
