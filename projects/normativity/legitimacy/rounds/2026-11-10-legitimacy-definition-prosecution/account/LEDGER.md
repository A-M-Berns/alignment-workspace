# Ledger of the legitimacy line (2026-11-27)

One row per component of the line. The statement that governs is legitimacy against plain
telling (`PLAIN_TELLING_STATEMENT.md`; `LegitimacyPlainTelling.lean`): §1 has its parts and
what is proved of it, §2 its structural limits, §3 its tests and reviews. The statement in
three sentences (`LEGITIMACY_STATEMENT.md`), its formal statement, its interpretation on
histories and its connecting theorem are superseded; their rows are §4 to §7, each marked in
the column *now*. **What is stable**: the sentence, held by the maintainer; the theorems of
the Lean file, which are kernel-checked; the gap between the statement and what she does,
with two of its four items shown needed. **What is not**: any way of getting, for a case as
it comes, the forecasts the statement is computed from; and anything about what she does
where his conduct changes how she evaluates.

A verdict is **closed** (a Lean declaration or a checker invocation stands for it, with the
event that reopens it), **open**, or **living note** (a position or a description, held and
revisable, that no check adjudicates). *Lean* files are under
`lean/Workspace/Deference/Contrib/`; `LegitimacyPlainTelling.lean` has the namespace
`Workspace.Deference.Contrib.LegitimacyPlainTelling`, and `LegitimacyStatement.lean`,
`LegitimacyGate.lean` and `LegitimacyStop.lean` the namespaces `…LegitimacyStatement`,
`…LegitimacyGate` and `…LegitimacyStop`; no declaration of the four is registered. *The
runner* is `python3 run.py` in the round directory. Items are those of `ATTENTION.md`. Paths
are relative to this directory. A row's number is its identifier, not its position.

The column *now*, on the rows of §4 to §8: **carried**, the present statement keeps it;
**superseded**, it is about a statement that no longer governs, and what it says of that
statement stands; **moot**, the question it poses does not arise under the present statement.
A row that was open and is moot is a living note: nobody needs it answered.

Kinds of evidence, strongest first: **kernel** (kernel-checked, of the declaration as
stated); **exact** (an exact check whose inputs a case or an episode supplies); **coding** (a
blind coding by a language model against a scorer written first); **verdict** (the
maintainer's verdict on a written case, `HARD_CASES.md`); **review** (an outside model;
reviews 8 to 15 and 17 to 20 each a fresh session that read only its brief, reviews 2 to 7 not
blind, the sixteenth not on record; the replies are in `REVIEW-astra-followup-16.md`, `-20.md`, `-21.md` and `-22.md` in the round's
prompts directory); **reasoning** (argument only).

Closed rows share one reopening event unless they name another: `python3 tests/audit_axioms.py`
fails on the declaration, or a commit changes its statement or the statement of a definition
it mentions. A row closed on the runner reopens when the runner fails on the document it
names.
