# Per-case choices

None. No verdict needed a driver, a baseline, `G` or `U` beyond `DEFINITIONS.md`.

Two things fixed globally there carry the weight a per-case choice would have carried,
and the verdicts named depend on them:

- `U := V` for objects with no aim variable — the faithful-adviser verdicts on B4, B6,
  T2 and T8 (`DIVERGENCES.md`).
- Clause (iv) of *working*, order is not content — the arm's-length verdicts on T7 and
  N9.

Each new case's scenario — its policies, payoffs, what is due, its actual world — was
written in `CASE_BANK.md` before any verdict was computed, by an executor who had read
the predictions.
