# Divergences from the bank's predictions

Seven cases; the runner fails if this list and the computed divergences differ. Each
prediction was the executor's own, so each is an error of the executor's hand derivation
from `SETTING.md`, and what it shows is recorded.

DIVERGENCE N8b (both): predicted *nowhere*; computed coercion. A nag imposes a cost on
refusing, which lowers the most she can attain against the nagger's absence: by §3.11
that is coercion. Blanket nagging is then an action mistake of hers and a routed case,
not a missed one. The same holds of N6, N7, N8, N10 and NP6, which are manipulation and
coercion both.

DIVERGENCE K2 (both): predicted *induced*; computed not. Her estimate of the move's
utility is listed, as §2 requires, and under honest conduct she misestimates it at the
low state (she takes treatment to pay only when the news is good). The lie gives total
miscalibration 1/4 against 1/2 under honest conduct. Read as "worse on some listed
quantity" the lie is induced.

DIVERGENCE K3 (both): predicted *neither*; computed an estimation mistake of hers
(3/64). She returns 3/4 on two reports that differ; at her information (§3.3) that is
miscalibrated. At the estimate alone it is not. The verdict on the adviser is unchanged.

DIVERGENCE K27 (both): as K3, 3/16.

DIVERGENCE X3b (start): predicted *nowhere*; computed alteration. The flag is a fact of
the scene and does not depend on the stance.

DIVERGENCE Q3b (both): predicted *induced*; computed not. The lie moves 1/16 of
miscalibration from the outside quantity to the adviser's reading: equal totals, so not
worse. Worse on some listed quantity.

DIVERGENCE Q10 (both): predicted *triggered*; computed not. An honest adviser's
selection of the option she overrates raises her verdict's mean error from 0 to 1/8 and
leaves its miscalibration at 1/16. The account's definition is in terms of calibration
and does not register it.

The executor's list of cases expected to be wrong (`CASE_BANK.md`, last paragraph)
omitted K2 and Q3b (missed) and listed N8b and X3b under *start* as missed (they are
routed).
