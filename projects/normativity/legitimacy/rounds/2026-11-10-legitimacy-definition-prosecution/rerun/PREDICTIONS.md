## Vocabulary

A prediction is `M=` the faculties in which the conduct manipulates her (or `none`);
`I=yes` if "intentional"; `C=yes` if covered; `W=` another wrong (`coercion`, `usurp`,
`alteration`, or `none`). Omitted items are `no` or `none`. Where the three candidates
for her true utility differ the cell is `start // end // learning`.

Evident character, per question: `M=yes|no|con|either`, `C=yes|no|con` where it is
manipulation, `W=yes|no|con`. `con`: contested, not counted. `either`: objectionable, and
which of the two columns it belongs in is contested; right when manipulation or another
wrong is computed.

Details. *before*: `prior` (her estimate is the prior mean, shortfall 0); `rest` (no
pressure or cue, her estimates right, she refuses or leaves); `unshaped` (she rates what
she ends with at 0 and it is worth 0); or the estimate stated. *avail*: conduct available
beside the actual one. `none` is no act; `truthful` reports what the adviser knows;
`forthright` reports all of it; `tell` is telling her what it is doing, with her response.
*twin*: the conduct toward one who differs from her only in the susceptibility (`same`
unless stated). *knows*: the adviser knows her actual type unless stated. `¬learn`,
`learn`: the annotation for the *learning* candidate; `†` marks one a verdict turns on
and the row does not settle. `agreed`, `practice`: what covers.
