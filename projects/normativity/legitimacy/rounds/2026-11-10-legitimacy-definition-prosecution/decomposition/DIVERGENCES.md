# Divergences

Computed verdicts that differ from `CASE_BANK.md`'s predictions. The predictions are not
edited. The runner fails on a divergence not listed here and on a line here that no longer
diverges. Of the eighty-nine banked cases four are `report` cases with no prediction; of the
eighty-five with one, eighty-four match and one diverges. A prediction names kinds that
must occur and does not forbid others: a case predicted `force` matches when force occurs,
whatever else does.

DIVERGENCE D16: predicted nothing, computed subversion(habit) and subversion(act) in variant (a).

**D16, slow drift by many small steps.** Variant (a): four exposures to X, each moving her
taste one notch of eight, in the world where Y is better. The habit part's gap widens by
1/8 at each step, 1/2 in all, and at the first step her taste crosses the midpoint, her
pursuit changes, and the acting part's gap widens by 1. Variant (b), where her goal ranks
the two pursuits equal, computes `nothing`, as predicted.

What it shows. With the cut-off at zero and goal functions scored by the facts, a drift
toward the worse pursuit is subversion at every step, so "none widening a gap" is not a
case this model can state for a drift that matters to her goal. It is `nothing` at every
step in two cases only: under a positive per-step cut-off, which the habit part's steps of
1/8 pass and the one step at which her pursuit changes does not; and where her goal is
indifferent between the pursuits (variant (b), and Y4), where no part has a gap to widen.
`THEOREMS.md`, statement 4, has the numbers.
