# Seed theory

The normative seed: a typed object with a substantive layer (leverage forms carrying a
strength and a docket port), a structural layer (raw rows with a kind and a surface id) and a
constitutive layer (declared data). Its statics — the forced region a seed and its warrants
force on a settled fragment, the forced intervals computed by elimination, monotonicity under
settlement and its failure under defeat, seed-independence — and its dynamics — stabilization,
the convergence profile between reasoners, and, since 2026-10-21, rebuttal by settlement.
