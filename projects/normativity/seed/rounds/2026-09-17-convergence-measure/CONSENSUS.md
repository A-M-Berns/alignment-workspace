# The overlapping-consensus reading

What the results say about the program's position on convergence.  No theorem beyond
`DYNAMICS.md`, `MANY.md` and `MEASURE.md`.

**Convergence is a profile, not a point.**  Two reasoners converge on a declared `Φ`
with declared weights when `Hull → small` and `Disc → 0`, read against the closed
layer's reference `Hull(∅‑seeds)`.  They end within a hull; the hull is the result.
Nothing here says where inside it either of them sits, and on the moral fixture the
limit hull at the last settlement step is `7/12` with discord `1/6` — the interpersonal
axis is still open and the aggregation disagreement is still discord.

**Dockets stay trace-constitutive.**  The seed round found that the landed defeat
calculus fixes the live docket per trace and computes no extension.  That bears on
*reason* convergence — whether two reasoners hold the same warrants — and on nothing
here: the profile is a function of the intervals, the intervals of the live bundles,
and two traces with different live bundles can have the same intervals
(`consensus`, `DYNAMICS.md` pressure test 5).  A lock is one more row that never
changes; it sits in the profile as width or as certified discord and does not stop the
hull from settling (pressure test 4).  The fork between grounded conformance and
trace-dependence is real and is not the fork this measure lives on.

**Persistent discord is certified and names what must go.**  Under exhaustive sharing
the live sets coincide and what remains in `Δ` is between substantive items, with a
`FarkasCert` whose positively weighted items are the candidates for withdrawal
(`discord_certificate`; the moral certificate names the prioritarian negation and the
sum item, nothing else).  The measure does not say which reasoner gives up which item;
that is the principal community's decision through the docket, as the seed round said
of the residue.

**One-sided learning creates discord until shared — and sharing does not dissolve it.**
A private warrant narrows one reasoner's interval and can open a gap
(`private_learning`, `population_private`).  Sharing the warrant then either leaves the
gap or refutes the receiver (`discord_persists`, `forcedRegionR_anti_warrants`): a
warrant that conflicts with what the receiver already holds is a certificate, not a
reconciliation.  What the world reconciles is what is shared *and compatible*; the rest
is reopened by defeat or withdrawn by decision.  The slogan the dispatch offered — the
world reconciles only what is shared — is right with the qualifier that sharing an
incompatible warrant reconciles nothing and exposes the incompatibility.

**Overlapping consensus** (provisional) is price agreement on `Φ`: a profile with
`Disc = 0` and `Hull = Ovl` small while the live dockets are disjoint.  It is not
agreement about why; it is not robust to a defeater landing on one docket
(`consensus`: one defeat takes the hull from `1/3` to `1`); and it is not agreement on
anything outside `Φ`.  It is what two reasoners with different reasons can be said to
share, exactly, and it is fragile in exactly one direction — reopening — which is the
direction the world cannot undo for them.
