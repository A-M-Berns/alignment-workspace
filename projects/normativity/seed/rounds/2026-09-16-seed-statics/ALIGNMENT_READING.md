# The alignment reading

Pointers only; nothing on the deference side is edited, and nothing here claims that a
seed forces a limit — every forced region below is `K(S ∪ W, F)` for named `S`, `W`, `F`.

**The constitutive layer is a protected concern.**  `S_con` — the declared standing set
and protected class — is what a reasoner must not be able to change unilaterally, which
is the response-authority sense of a protected concern in `wiki/Corrigibility.md` §1:
its semantics is that some principal response realizes it now, and a change to it that
the principal cannot respond to is an agent-caused protected loss.  In this round the
layer is declared data that the statics read and never write; whether a realization keeps
it that way is the corrigibility theorem's question, not the seed's.

**Discovery class and reason supply, a conjecture.**  The discovery class `𝒟` of
`STABILIZATION.md` — what warrants and defeaters can be raised — is, seen from the
deference side, the reason-supply channel: the declared reason interface through which
counterreasons reach the principal's evaluation.  The stabilization condition (finite
`𝒟`, or summable reopenings) and the reason-supply liveness residual of the deference
side's item 87 clause 5 and PR #101's item 92 are plausibly one object read from two
sides: the first says when the forced region stops moving because nothing new can be
raised, the second says who must raise what by when.  Recorded as a conjecture; nothing
about it is proved here.

**The residue is decided, not derived.**  What the seed leaves open (`r(a,c)` on the
moral fixture; `V₂` on the legal one) and what it cannot jointly hold (the population
cluster) is the residue.  The theory names both kinds and computes their certificates —
a non-point interval no item narrows, a minimal infeasible subset — and stops there.
Which open axis is closed, and which row of an infeasible cluster is withdrawn, is what a
principal community decides through the docket and the surface-change channel; the
statics say what follows from the decision and nothing about which decision to take.
