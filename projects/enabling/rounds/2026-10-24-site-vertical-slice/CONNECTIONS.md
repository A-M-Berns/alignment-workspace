# The five connections, by reading (2026-10-24)

The precedent every later connection follows (ruling S1; `prompts/2026-10-24-site-vertical-slice/PROMPT.md`, Part 1). For each registered result: the informal claim `C` it establishes (found or filed), `C` as the `and` of its formal result(s) and its own completeness leaf, the record's hypotheses filed by reading with their `discharge` tags, the bridge rewritten to name the gap between the claim and the declaration, and the routes of `C`'s improvement claim with their items. Everything here is at level 0 and AI-drafted; a human reading of a bridge or a tag is an `attribution` event, which this round did not write.

**The reading rule for tags**, applied throughout: `definitional` for a binder that says what the objects are or which case is examined (a schedule's data, a fixture, the antecedent that defines the case a conditional claim speaks about); `open` for an assumption the theory posits about the world and nothing here derives (consistency of the process, actual coverage, grade trust, the sincere reference, a calm exterior); `restates-conclusion` where a hypothesis is the conclusion read back; `proved-in-model` was not needed: no declaration here discharges a hypothesis by a model inside the record itself (the inhabitants and witnesses the registry notes name are companion declarations, not hypotheses).
