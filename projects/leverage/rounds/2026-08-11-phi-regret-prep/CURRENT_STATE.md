# What the line holds, and what this round could stand on

Authority is `RESEARCH_STATE.md`'s precedence order: `DECISIONS.md`, then
`projects/leverage/CLAIMS.md`, then the consolidation by claim identifier, then
round records. The forward tree says of itself that it is disposable and is
evidence for nothing, so nothing below rests on it as evidence — though the
round does adapt one of its interfaces, which is a separate matter recorded in
*Where the code is* below.

## Proved, and citable by identifier

Six layers of `projects/leverage/consolidation-aug9/`, 180 claims with statuses
agreeing between the theory parts and the ledger, re-verified by CI's
`consolidation-verification` gate on every push. What this round leans on:

- **The objection grammar.** A judge declares a footprint and the reader logs
  every table it is asked for, including membership probes; a read outside the
  declaration withholds the verdict (`GR-J1`). Static well-formedness is decided
  before any judge runs (`GR-J3`).
- **The practical-demand layer.** A merits direction exists exactly when the
  credal interval separates the bound threshold, and separation is strict
  (`CD-J2`). Refusal liability is elapsed time times a frozen tariff, derived
  from the record rather than supplied (`CD-J8`); tolling is subtraction on that
  quantity and needs no new primitive (`CD-J9`). A default creates no coverage,
  alters no interval, and cannot later be cited as merits support (`CD-J3`).
  Procedure is prospective: a query is bound to the schedule version live when it
  arrived (`CD-J4`).
- **The stream layer.** Persistent substantive silence is not cost-free
  (`CS-J1`). A transfer crosses a declared fence exactly when one endpoint is
  inside it, and a pooled system declares no fence, so the predicate is vacuous
  rather than universal (`CS-J5`). The liability trilemma (`CS-J2`) and the
  necessity of the solvency coupling (`CS-N1`).
- **Answerability.** Every open obligation has its own identity and receives an
  explicit authorized disposition; one response may discharge several only where
  its adequacy for each is separately represented.

## Architected, not proved

The answerability ledger and the case docket are designed objects with executable
implementations and no consolidated claim rows of their own beyond the
transcriptions in Theory 9. The certificate architecture — untrusted prover,
small trusted judge, certificate between — is a repository-level commitment in
`AGENTS.md`, not a theorem.

## Open, and load-bearing

Ranked by the consolidation's own `OPEN_PROBLEMS.md`: persistence of the
certified core minimum, where the per-date check is a linear program and the
infimum over dates is open; a computable coherence modulus, open in both
directions, which the adjacent impossibility results do **not** settle because
both turn on Gaifman inductivity and the modulus question does not mention it;
registry completeness for the objection grammar, the one gap that would make a
footprint declaration unsound rather than merely coarse.

## Reserved, and now opened

`OPEN_PROBLEMS.md` closes with a pointer list it explicitly declines to treat:
inquiry ontology and question records, and **the learning and installation
track**. This round enters the second of those. It is the first material in the
line about what the learner does over time as opposed to what its record must
show at a date, and nothing in the consolidation constrains it.

## What did not exist before this round

Searched across the repository: **no φ-regret material of any kind.** The word
appears nowhere. "Regret" appears in the deference line, where it means decision
regret against a comparator class of jurisdiction assignments, and where a
`DECISIONS.md` entry of 2026-08-11 rules that **competence may not be stated as a
regret bound** because pointwise, average and selector-relative forms are each
*equivalent* to the delegation inequality rather than sufficient for it.

**That ruling does not reach this round, and the reason matters.** It is about a
regret bound used as a *hypothesis* — assumed of an agent in order to derive that
delegating to it is worthwhile, where the assumption turns out to be the
conclusion. Here regret is a *conclusion*: something a learner is to be shown to
have, against a comparator class defined by a legality predicate that never
mentions charge. The two uses share a word and nothing else. A future round that
tried to assume low φ-regret in order to derive that a learner is
reasons-responsive would be committing the deference line's error exactly, and
this paragraph exists so that it is committed knowingly if at all.

## Where the code is, and a structural defect

The consolidation's `src/` carries the coherence functional, the interval
computer, the objection grammar, the composite and the constructions. It does
**not** carry the answerability ledger or the case docket. The only executable
version of either sits in `projects/leverage/forward/src/`, in a tree whose own
`FORWARD.md` says it "may be changed, rewritten, or deleted wholesale at any time
without loss" and that "nothing here is evidence for anything."

So the theory of the layer this round builds on is in the authoritative record
and the code for it is in a tree declared deletable. This round therefore adapts
rather than imports: `src/model.py` reimplements the obligation fields a lawful
edit check needs, and says so. The defect is filed in `PRIORITIES.md` under
*Workspace friction*; the workaround is recorded here rather than left silent.
