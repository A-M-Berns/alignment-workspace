# Open problems

Ranked by what the next round is blocked on. Nothing here is promoted; where both
directions are open that is said.

## 1. Does the reduction instantiate?

Whether Blum–Mansour's fixed-point step applies to a comparator class whose
elements are guarded swaps over a per-occasion action set that varies with the
bound schedule. The guard is measurable before the action, which is the condition
the standard statement needs; the varying action set is what the standard
statement does not have to handle.

**Highest priority**, because every other question about a bound is downstream of
it and it is the cheapest to settle. A negative would not be a setback: it would
say which structural feature of the substrate is the obstruction, and that is
what `ONLINE_LEARNING_MAP.md` exists to make findable.

## 2. Does φ-regret survive the solvency coupling?

The v1 environment declares `suspends=False`, and that is what buys horizon-free
counterfactual influence (`PR-L5`). But `CS-N1` is a necessity witness for the
coupling in the demand results: without it, liability accrues and no trilemma
branch closes. So the environment in which the learning statement is cleanest is
one in which a proved result of the same line has no content.

Open in both directions. Either there is a φ-regret statement robust to a
coupling — which would need a comparator class whose members cannot move a
suspension date, and it is not obvious such a class is large enough to be
interesting — or there is an impossibility, which would be a genuine result about
the shape of self-correction under shared solvency.

## 3. Is `BearsOn` the right shape?

The supplied default asks whether a ground declares the coordinate and names the
occasion or its target. That is a bookkeeping relation wearing the name of a
normative one. Whether a substantively adequate `BearsOn` exists, and whether it
is a relation between a reason and a coordinate at all rather than between a
reason and a *reason for the response*, is untouched.

The obvious move — grade relevance rather than deciding it — is unattractive for
the reason the deference line already found on a different question: a graded
predicate invites summing grades against savings, which is the collapse the
footprint exists to prevent.

## 4. Coverage, and what enlarging Φ_law costs

Sublinear φ-regret against a nine-element class is a weak statement. The standard
bounds carry `√(log|Φ|)`, so coverage buys scope at a rate. What is missing is any
account of where new comparators come from: an inquiry module would have to
propose them, and proposing a comparator is proposing a normative rule, which
ought to be answerable in its own right.

This is where the round most obviously stops short of the thing anyone actually
wants, and it is deliberately out of scope rather than overlooked.

## 5. Is `unresolved` a verdict or an evasion?

The magnitude predicate returns `unresolved` where a ground supports a direction
and not an endpoint. That is honest about the round's uncertainty. It is not
obviously a coherent third value: a comparator whose firing is `unresolved` does
not fire, so operationally `unresolved` and `rejected` are the same, and the
distinction lives entirely in the report.

Either it should become a real third status with consequences — a comparator that
fires provisionally and whose regret is reported separately — or it should be
admitted to be a label on a rejection. Both are defensible and this round chose
neither.

## 6. Is the obligation adapter faithful?

`src/model.py` reimplements the fields of the answerability algebra that a lawful
edit check needs, because the only executable version of that algebra sits in a
tree that declares itself deletable. No cross-check was run. The three burden
codes may be a coarsening of distinctions the ledger draws — the ledger
distinguishes discharge from withdrawal from loss from procedural closure, and
this substrate collapses the middle two.

Filed as workspace friction as well, because the underlying cause is structural
rather than mathematical.

## 7. The response set

Three bases and two dependent coordinates. Real dispositions have more: partial
service, referral, refinement of the question, suspension pending an inquiry. The
magnitude coordinate is `tolled` because that is the one continuous quantity the
existing theory already prices, not because tolling is where magnitude matters
most.

A round that added a coordinate would have to redo the scope-discipline
declaration and, probably, the `licenses` mechanism — which is the least examined
part of the interface.
