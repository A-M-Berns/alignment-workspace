# Claim ledger

One row for every formal claim of this monograph's six theory parts, restated
folder-locally. The previous consolidation's own ledger remains authoritative
for its six parts and is vendored intact at `vendor/consolidation_aug8.zip`;
nothing from it is duplicated here except where a claim below modifies or
repairs it, and those rows say so in their dependencies.

**Row discipline.** Every row carries its full hypotheses (or names the section
where they are displayed, when they are long), its conclusion, its status, its
necessity or sharpness note, a verification pointer, and its dependencies.

**Status vocabulary.** `PROVED (single derivation)` · `MACHINE-CHECKED (stated
finite scope)` · `NECESSITY WITNESS` · `REFUTED (witness displayed)` ·
`PROVED-CONDITIONAL (conditions listed)`. A row's status here and the status
printed beside its identifier in the theory part are checked equal by
`tests/run.py`; a mismatch fails the suite.

**Verification pointers.** `hand-derived` means the proof in the theory part is
the whole evidence: no code stands behind it, and none is implied. A test
pointer means the displayed finite instance is recomputed by Tier A or Tier B.
Passing code is evidence for displayed finite instances only.

**One row is a reading audit.** `NL-SI-SIM` records the outcome of reading one
candidate engine's source against the interface. It is neither machine-checked
nor derived from these definitions, it is labelled as such in its sharpness
column and in `VERIFICATION.md`, and everything depending on it inherits that
standing.

## Status boundary

Fifty rows are proved from the definitions of their part. Seventeen are
machine-checked over displayed finite instances and claim nothing beyond them.
Twelve are necessity witnesses: each displays an instance showing that dropping
a named condition breaks a named result. One is refuted with its witness
displayed. Seven are conditional, and each lists its conditions in its own row
and in its part.

The two heaviest conditionals are `NL-J3` and `NL-SI-X1`. `NL-J3` inherits the
previous consolidation's hypotheses wholesale. `NL-SI-X1` is a **substitution**
over `NL-J3` — it proves nothing new, and its content is that the antecedent is
a predicate over a class of engines that is evaluated rather than assumed.

`NL-SI-SIM` and everything downstream of it (`NL-SI-X4`) rest on a reading
audit. That is the weakest evidence class in this package and is marked
everywhere it is used.
