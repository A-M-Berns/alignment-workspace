# Deference

The deference and corrigibility program: when a bounded agent should defer to
another, what deference costs, and what an agent's own deliberation has to look
like for deference to be safe rather than merely obedient.

The thesis under test is that **epistemic superiority need not entail final
authority**. Several stages of work have run against it. Most of what they
established is negative, and the negative results are the substance.

## Research history

`../../prompts/` holds every round's dispatch and report, committed with the work
— including the rounds that failed, which on this line is most of the
interesting ones. Those are **evidence and history, not the current position**: a
later ledger row or `DECISIONS.md` entry governs where they disagree, and reports
are not edited when they are superseded.

Two rounds are worth reading as method rather than result. Stage III built a
comparator for fully updated deference, verified its Lean, and had an independent
adversarial review find that the comparator contained no future agent — the
mathematics survived under a corrected name, the interpretation did not. Stage IV
tried to supply the missing agent and failed in the mirror-image way, which
located the obstruction as **type-level**: two authorisation regimes inducing the
same realisation map are the same object in a signature whose only outputs are
such maps. Neither round was going to work, and the second failure is what
explains the first.

The line's recorded starting point is the note dump of 2026-06-27, carried beside
this file, with a statement-level audit and a Lean development covering deference,
a self-referential target, frozen deliberation, faithful acceleration, and a tower

**That development takes the Logical Induction theorems as named hypotheses and
proves what follows from them.** Its own audit names the complement as its largest
gap: the market and traders are unmodelled, so the inference from the criterion to
the forcing inequality is nowhere in it. The leverage line and the pinned
Formalized-Agent-Foundations development sit on the other side of exactly that
gap, which is why the two lines share a repository and a Lean project. It is
`PRIORITIES.md` item 7.
