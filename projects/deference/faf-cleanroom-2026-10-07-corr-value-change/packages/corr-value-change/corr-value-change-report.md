## Conventions chosen (and why)

- **Numbers.** ℝ for theorems and witnesses alike. Witnesses are rational literals in ℝ discharged by `norm_num`/`simp`, so every number in the fixture is checked exactly and the `LitDdbFrames` Total Trust definition (over ℝ) can be reused without a cast.
- **Conditional expectation once.** `Prob.condExp P U E = (∑_{ω∈E} P ω · U ω) / P(E)` in `Model.lean`; `x/0 = 0` is a junk value and every headline conditioning on `E` carries `0 < P(E)` (as a model field where the model supplies it: `Decision.pos`, `TwoStep.posK/posC`, `ProductModel.qK_pos/qC_pos`).
- **Decisions.** `Decision P A`: acts indexed by a type `A`, pairwise disjoint, covering, each of positive probability. The chosen acts of every headline are hypothesis-bearing variables (`hK : ∀ a, v_K a ≤ v_K aK`, `hhat : ∀ j a, v_j a ≤ v_j (â^j)`); nothing is picked by `Classical.choice`.
- **The decomposition is proved once, abstractly** (`Table.lean`, `ValueTable`): outcome weights `p_j > 0` summing to one, `v_K`, `v_j`. The evidential two-step model (`TwoStep.lean`) and the Savage model (`Savage.lean`, Appendix B) instantiate it, so the identity in §1.4 and in B.2 is literally the same theorem, as B.2 says ("goes through verbatim").
- **Reuse of other packages.** Nothing from `CorrLegitModif`/`CorrLegitGeneral`/`CorrReflectFrames`/`LegitNeg*` defines the note's objects (a joint on `Ω × Θ × I` with installed states `Q_i` on `Ω × Θ`, or a two-step evidential decision); those packages work over `LitDdbFrames` frames `F : Frame W` (rows indexed by the *same* world type). For Total Trust (T9(d), T12(i)) the definition of record `Cleanroom.Found.LitDdbFrames.TotalTrust` is reused as is, with the note's two-world source as a `Frame Bool`; DDB Theorem 4.1 (`totalTrust_iff_hullAndModestlyInformed`) is cited for the general "Total Trust ⇒ hull" (S1). Reflection (R⁺), (M⁺), (Z) and (R⁺∣L) are *defined here* (`Update.lean`), because the note's (R⁺) compares `P(· ∣ E_i)` on `Ω⁺` with a state `Q_i` on `Ω⁺`, a different carrier from the joint; the report says in T6 how the definition relates to `LitDdbFrames.Reflects`.
