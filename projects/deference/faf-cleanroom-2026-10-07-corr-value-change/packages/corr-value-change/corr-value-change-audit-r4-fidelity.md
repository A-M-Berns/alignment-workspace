## 3. Non-blocking issues

### N1. Claim 4's equality clause: the note's "iff the modification carries no information about `u_{θ,a}`" is false in its "only if" direction, and the findings file does not say so
§2.4 Claim 4: "Equality holds iff the modification carries no information about `u_{θ,a}`." Under the natural reading of "carries no information" (the outcome independent of `u_{θ,a}` given `ω`), the "only if" fails: equality in the mean-square statement needs only that the outcome leave the *conditional mean* of `u_{θ,a}` unchanged, and an outcome can be informative about `u_{θ,a}` (about its spread) while leaving its mean alone. The library already proves the right statement — `msErr_eq_iff`/`representation_eq_iff`: equality iff `Ū^{(i)}_a(ω) = Ū_a(ω)` on the support — and labels it `variant: "no information" made precise as equality of conditional means`, with the docstring saying independence "is sufficient, not necessary". But this is a finding about the note (severity: imprecision, §2.4), and the findings file has no entry for it, so a reader of F1–F15 would take Claim 4's equality clause as certified. Probe `audit-r4-probes/Claim4EqualityNotIndependence.lean` (elaborates, exit 0, log beside it): `Θ = Fin 3`, `u_θ = (−1, 0, 1)`, one act; outcome `true` (mass `1/2`) is `θ = 1`, outcome `false` is `θ ∈ {0, 2}` with `1/4` each; installed states the conditionals. Then `c4_reflection : Reflection` (so Claim 4 applies), `c4_informative : P(θ = 1 ∧ i = true) = 1/2 ≠ 1/4 = π_true · P⁺(θ = 1)` (the outcome is informative about `u`: `P(u = 0 ∣ i = true) = 1 ≠ 1/2`), `c4_equality : msErr(installed) = 1/2 = msErr(current)`, and `c4_eq_iff_instance` (the library's `representation_eq_iff` instantiated). Fix: add a finding (F16) "Claim 4's equality clause holds iff the outcome leaves the conditional mean of `u_{θ,a}` unchanged, not iff it carries no information; the ⇐ direction (independence ⇒ equality) is right", pointing to `representation_eq_iff`; and adopt the probe (say `representation_eq_informative` in `Variants.lean` or `Fine.lean`) as the N+ witness for the equality side of `representation_eq_iff` — the row's Witness cell names only `coin_pill_worse`, which exercises the strict side (`9/100 < 1/4`).
