# The weak loop: scheduled-averaged Tower / Total Trust / Value

*⚠ Attribution note (2026-08-10): "Eisenstat's lookahead construction" below names the corpus construal, not Sam Eisenstat's intended setup — see [[eisenstat-conjecture-attribution]]. §3.3 of this page is, incidentally, the corpus's sharpest statement of how the tower refutation depends on the human holding the quotes as decided data — the regime Sam's intended human-side delay need not be in.*

*Claude, 2026-08-03, from Abram's three questions: does Eisenstat's lookahead construction get a weaker Value to go with its weaker Tower and weaker Total Trust; do the weak notions close a loop like the strong ones, by generalizing the strong loop's proofs; and is the shared-fixed-point (regular-Tower) version the delay-to-zero limit of the weak-Tower proof. Short answers: **(1) yes, and it was recorded at [[theorem-ss-streamlined]] §8 — but the general-menu "no scope condition" conjecture there is REFUTED as stated** (the punishing probe transports down the grades; the hedged/two-option transport is untouched); **(2) yes — the strong loop's three arrows generalize essentially verbatim to the averaged grade via the donor calculus, the scope condition is grade-invariant, and the loop's direction is forced at the weak grade too**; **(3) no as literally asked — the deformation parameter is feedback promptness, not delay, and at the self-referential diagonal there is a genuine discontinuity between sequential publication and the joint fixed point** — with the exact-trade form of "yes in substance" spelled out below. **Unvetted by Abram as of 2026-08-03.** Conventions: [[conventions-and-status-labels]]; file map: [[varying-question-synthesis]].*

## 1. Weak Value: what was recorded, and the refutation of the general-menu conjecture

**What the notes already had** ([[theorem-ss-streamlined]] §8, recorded 2026-07-31 in answer to Abram's question). Two levels: *(a)* two-option transport is **exact and proved** — the two-option identity is a per-day algebraic identity, so scheduled averaged soft Total Trust *is* scheduled averaged two-option Value (in the soft form, whose strategy is the $\delta$-hedged one); *(b)* general finite menus were **CONJECTURED (~0.7)** to transport with **no decision-theoretic scope condition**, on the theory that F1 degenerates for the readable lookahead expert and "the Newcomblike pathologies land in the pinned stratum".

**The conjecture in form (b) is refuted: the punishing probe transports.** Run the constant-probe punishing menu of [[loop-direction]] against the lookahead expert, in Theorem SS's own setting (same-day publication, (L)). Menu $\{O^1_n, \text{const } s\}$ with $\Gamma \vdash O^1_n = \mathbb 1[\mathrm{sel}_n = 2]$, $\mathrm{sel}_n$ the menu's own argmax on the published lookahead quotes, tie-break ledger-decided.

- **The quote goes liar, as before.** By day $f(n)$ the day-$n$ quotes are ancient ledger data, so $\mathbb E^H_{f(n)}(O^1_n) \approx \mathbb 1[\mathrm{sel}_n = 2]$; hence the lookahead quote of $O^1_n$ is $\mathbb E^A_n\big(\ulcorner \mathbb E^H_{f(n)}(O^1_n)\urcorner\big) \approx \mathbb P^A_n(\mathrm{sel}_n = 2)$, and since $\mathrm{sel}_n$ compares that quote to the constant's quote $\approx s$ while $A$ knows its own quotes only within $o(1)$, the fixed point hovers: the quote $\to s$, with interior selection mass $\big(\mathbb P^A_n(\mathrm{sel}_n{=}1), \mathbb P^A_n(\mathrm{sel}_n{=}2)\big) \to (1-s, s)$.
- **F1 fails for the lookahead expert, by $s(1-s)$.** The followed strategy is provably $\widehat S_n = s \cdot \mathbb 1[\mathrm{sel}_n = 2]$, so its realized future credence is $\mathbb E^H_{f(n)}(\widehat S_n) \approx s\cdot\mathbb 1[\mathrm{sel}_n = 2]$, and

$$ \mathbb E^A_n\big(\ulcorner \mathbb E^H_{f(n)}(\widehat S_n)\urcorner\big) \;\approx_n\; s\cdot \mathbb P^A_n(\mathrm{sel}_n = 2) \;\to\; s^2 \;\;\ne\;\; s \;\approx\; \max_j\, \mathbb E^A_n\big(\ulcorner \mathbb E^H_{f(n)}(O^j_n)\urcorner\big). $$

The §8 argument's gap is exactly here: "$\widehat S_n = O^{j^\ast}_n$ is decided data" is true for $\Gamma$ and for the day-$f(n)$ human, but **not for $A$ same-day** — $A$'s knowledge of its own argmax is introspective, and at the hovering fixed point its self-prediction mass is interior, so its forecast of the *selected* option's future credence is a mass-weighted mixture, not the max. The route does touch conditional structure after all: $A$'s joint beliefs about $(\mathrm{sel}_n,\ \text{future credence})$.
- **Scheduled averaged argmax Value fails, by the same margin.** Under (L) the human reads the day-$i$ quotes same-day, so $\mathbb E^H_i(\widehat S_i) \approx s\cdot\mathbb 1[\mathrm{sel}_i = 2]$ pointwise; $A$'s own unbiasedness on schedule weightings forces the realized selection frequency to track the hovering quote, so with $w_i$ the bare schedule indicator (the §8 conjecture's own weighting):

$$ \frac{\sum_{i\le n} w_i\, \mathbb E^H_i(\widehat S_i)}{\sum_{i\le n} w_i} \;\to\; s^2 \;<\; s \;=\; \lim_n \frac{\sum_{i\le n} w_i\, \mathbb E^H_i(\text{const } s)}{\sum_{i\le n} w_i}. $$

**Consistency check, which is also the moral.** Theorem SS itself holds *at* $\widehat S_n$: the agreement form gives averaged $\mathbb E^H_i(\widehat S_i) \to s^2$ against the quote $\to s^2$ — the epistemic notion is fine at every bet including the strategy; what fails is only the *instrumental* comparison against the max. **Averaging launders oscillation, not bias**: the liar dynamics in the *quote* are oscillatory and unbiasedness absorbs them (which is why weak Tower and weak TT are paradox-immune, §2.4), but the punishing structure in the *values* is a one-signed per-day deficit ($\widehat S_n$ provably $\le s$, short by $s$ on every $\mathrm{sel}=1$ day), and no averaging removes a one-signed deficit. So the decision-theoretic scope condition is **grade-invariant**: weak argmax Value needs it exactly as strong argmax Value does.

**What survives of §8's Value claims.** The two-option/hedged transport (exact; the identity is per-day algebra); and a **repaired general-menu conjecture**: scheduled averaged argmax Value for the lookahead expert *under a lookahead-adapted conditional-stability condition* — selection not, on average, bad news about the selected option's **future credence**, $\sum_j \mathbb E^A_n\big(\mathbb 1[\mathrm{sel}_n = j]\cdot \ulcorner\mathbb E^H_{f(n)}(O^j_n)\urcorner\big) \gtrsim_n \sum_j \mathbb P^A_n(\mathrm{sel}_n = j)\,\mathbb E^A_n\big(\ulcorner\mathbb E^H_{f(n)}(O^j_n)\urcorner\big)$ — or under decisive quote-margins, where introspective concentration pins the argmax and F1 does degenerate as §8 hoped. That version I'd put at ~0.8; the F1-under-margins step is the same introspective-concentration shape as [[open-problems]] item 1.

## 2. The weak loop closes, by the strong loop's own proofs

The claim: the three arrows of the strong loop ([[total-trust-implies-value]], [[value-implies-tower]], [[tower-implies-total-trust]]) generalize to the scheduled-averaged grade with their structure intact, because each arrow factors into exactly three kinds of step, and the Cesàro grading commutes with all three:

1. **Applications of the graded hypothesis** — instantiate the weak notion instead of the strong one, at the same bet/menu, against the same weighting.
2. **Per-day provable facts carried by `expprovind`/`loe`** — per-day $o(1)$ errors enter any divergent-mass average free, by the **universal donor rule** of [[theorem-ss-streamlined]] §1; and same-weighting averages chain by additivity. The donor calculus is precisely the discipline the weak grade needs, and it is already kernel-checked (`StreamlinedSS.lean` T1).
3. **Expert-side per-day asymptotics** (introspection, the expert's own `loe`) — untouched by the novice-side grading.

Arrow by arrow:

- **Weak Tower ⟹ weak TT (the fold).** Verbatim. The fold's three facts ([[tower-implies-total-trust]]) are per-day provable arithmetic (the weighting is decided, the expert folds it out by its own `loe`, the ramp is positive only above the threshold); the one hypothesis application is Tower at the ramp-weighted product $(V_n\, u_n)$ — replace it by weak Tower at the same product, same averaging weighting, and chain by the donor rule. Closure demand unchanged: the graded quantifier must cover ramp-weighted products.
- **Weak Value ⟹ weak Tower (the probes).** Steps 1–2 of [[value-implies-tower]] (gap-bet quote pinned at $0$ by introspection; provably strict argmax) are per-day and expert-side — unaffected. Steps 3–4 instantiate graded Value on the probe menus; step 5's $\varepsilon$-diagonal sits outside the average. The probes remain self-stable, so the scope restriction on graded Value still never propagates.
- **Weak TT ⟹ weak Value.** Lemma 1 of [[total-trust-implies-value]] averages cleanly: the saturation of the weighting is per-day provable, one graded TT instance replaces the strong one, and provability induction drives the mass term as before. Lemma 2 (self-endorsement) is pure expert-side per-day material and is charged **unchanged — including (H3)**, per §1 above: the scope condition does not weaken with the grade.

**2.1 The loop's direction is forced at the weak grade too.** The [[loop-direction]] liar probe's Value deficit is a per-day *constant* ($s(1-s)$ once the fixed point settles), so it survives averaging verbatim: the weak reverse closure (weak Value ⟹ weak TT through two-option menus) computes averaged **hard** above-threshold inequalities, which the probe refutes at the averaged grade, while the averaged **soft** inequalities are vacuous there (the ramp sits at its foot) — one certificate serves both grades. Entry into weak TT rides the fold, exactly as in the strong loop.

**2.2 The readable-expert shortcut, for this expert.** For the lookahead expert specifically none of the above machinery is needed: the degeneracy collapses (L4; [[route-transitivity]] Lemma B) make weak Tower, weak TT, and weak two-option Value pairwise equivalent directly — already recorded at [[varying-question-synthesis]] §8 ("one witness settles Tower, TT, and two-option Value simultaneously"). The content of this section is that the loop also closes **abstractly**, for a general (non-readable) expert at the weak grade, by the strong loop's own arrows.

**2.3 Why the weak grade is the natural home of the cross-process setting.** The weak epistemic notions are **paradox-immune**: Theorem SS covers the self-referential diagonal bets (they are e.d. and $\Gamma_A$-determined), where the per-day notions die ([[tower-death]]'s stratum; the quote is pinned, the realized bit oscillates). Unbiasedness *is* the grade at which a liar sentence is priced correctly. So the strong-notions loop lives where the strong notions are satisfiable (the future-self instance, delayed-readability experts), and the weak loop is the matching structure one grade down, where the cross-process pair actually lives.

## 3. Weak Tower ⟶ regular Tower at the shared fixed point: ladder, not limit

The question: is the shared-fixed-point (v3, per-day) result what the weak-Tower proof becomes as the delay drops to zero?

**3.1 The modification exists, and the corpus has its exact-trade form.** [[joint-clearing-and-trader-class]] §2: joint clearing buys exactly one thing — $\mathcal C_A$-generability of the violation weighting's human-side factor $\operatorname{Ind}_\delta(\mathbb E^H_n(X_n) < t - \varepsilon)$. The proof modification is: **swap Theorem SS's schedule factor for that factor, and downgrade the engine from 4.8.16 to 4.8.15.** The schedule's job was to make the feedback computable in time for full limits (4.8.16); the violation factor's job is to mark violation days so that a mere limit point (4.8.15, rate-free, no schedule) already yields a contradiction with the persistent margin $\varepsilon$. That is precisely v3's route ([[faithful-acceleration-result]] §4.1) — so "yes in substance": the two proofs are siblings, differing in which factor pays for the truth-crossing.
**What it yields, honestly:** v3's Corollary 2 is the **one-sided** per-day family, $\liminf_n\big(\mathbb E^H_n(X_n) - \mathbb E^A_n(\ulcorner\mathbb E^H_{f(n)}(X_n)\urcorner)\big) \ge 0$, conditional on the assumed joint Brouwer construction and the D.2 relativization ([[open-problems]] item 12). Regular *Tower* (two-sided per-day agreement, then the readability collapse) needs in addition the mirror run at the mirror violation weighting — plausible by symmetry of the realized-cash trader, but **not recorded anywhere**; worth flagging as its own small gap.

**3.2 Why "delay → 0" is the wrong parameter.** Theorem SS's sparsity is the **computability latency of the lookahead target**: $Y_n = \mathbb E^H_{f(n)}(X_n)$ sits $f(n) - n$ days in the future *whatever the clearing arrangement*, so the evaluation-sparse schedule does not densify as the price-channel delay shrinks. The deformation parameter that actually orders the results is the **feedback-promptness ladder** ([[route-sparse-schedule]] §1): rate-free eventual feedback ⟷ Half 1 + Lemma P; schedule-prompt feedback ⟷ Theorem SS; **live** feedback ⟷ per-day (v3). Read on the ladder, Abram's intuition is right: the shared-fixed-point version *is* the endpoint — of the visibility/feedback axis, not the delay axis.

**3.3 The discontinuity at the endpoint (observation, unvetted).** Sequential clearing with publication — at *any* lag, including zero — makes the day-$n$ quotes **decided data** for $H$: on the self-referential diagonal ($O^1_n = \mathbb 1[\mathrm{sel}_n = 2]$-type bets), $H$ then prices the decided bit while the quote hovers at the threshold, and per-day two-sided agreement is refuted — [[tower-death]]'s stratum, present however small the delay. Under the joint fixed point the same quote is instead a **live continuous variable** of $H$'s clearing: a trader's coefficients depend continuously on it, so the sharp bit is not expressible in $H$'s day-$n$ prices, the diagonal's knife edge is smeared inside the fixed point, and the obstruction dissolves. So regular full Tower is *not* the continuous limit of weak Tower as delay $\to 0$: there is a phase change at the joint fixed point — who holds the selection as decided data changes discontinuously — and the cross-process Tower refutation lives exactly in the gap between "sequential, arbitrarily fast" and "joint". This, rather than speed, is what "both markets choosing the fixed point together" buys on the diagonal.

**3.4 The stratum where no fixed-point sharing is needed at all.** On the no-surprise stratum — fixed questions, or varying questions with convergent realized targets — Lemma P already gives per-day **two-sided** agreement (regular-Tower shape via the readability collapse) with *no* clearing hypothesis in either direction ([[eisenstat-lookahead-construction]] §3). So the joint fixed point is only ever buying per-day control of *surprising* varying questions; everywhere else, weak → regular is free.
