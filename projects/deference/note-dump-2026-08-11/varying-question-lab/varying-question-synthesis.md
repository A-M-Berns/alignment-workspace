# Varying-question trust: synthesis of the four-route exploration

*Claude, 2026-07-30; updated through 2026-07-31 (Abram's read-through — see §8 for the frontier it produced, and [[theorem-ss-streamlined]] for the canonical proof it converged on). Four parallel investigations of varying-question trust in the lookahead expert $E^\ast(X_n) := a_n = \mathbb E^A_n(\ulcorner \mathbb E^H_{f(n)}(X_n)\urcorner)$ under one-way visibility, with adversarial cross-verification between them (one route's verdict was reversed by it). Route files, all in this directory: [[route-sparse-schedule]] (positive, the headline), [[route-recurring-ccee]] (all-days analysis, non-derivability), [[route-transitivity]] (composition), [[route-negative-introspective]] (impossibility attempt — REVERSED in revision, now an obstruction result). ## 1. The headline: Theorem SS is the solid varying-question result

**Theorem SS** ([[route-sparse-schedule]] §10; PROVED modulo named hypotheses, ~0.82, independently re-verified): under one-way sequential clearing, for **every** e.d. sequence $(X_n)$ of $[0,1]$-LUVs, **every** rational $t$ and $\delta>0$, along one fixed evaluation-sparse schedule $g$ (uniform in $t,\delta$ and across description-size classes), with $w_n = \operatorname{Ind}_\delta(a_n>t)\cdot\mathbb 1[n\in\operatorname{im} g]$: if $\sum w_n = \infty$ then
$$\frac{\sum_{i\le n} w_i\,\Big(\mathbb E^A_i\big(\ulcorner \mathbb E^H_{f(i)}(X_i)\urcorner\big) - \mathbb E^H_i(X_i)\Big)}{\sum_{i\le n} w_i} \;\eqsim_n\; 0
\qquad\text{(two-sided full limit — quote–credence agreement)},$$ Tower-equivalent via the ledger collapse (the inner expectation of a readable expert's estimate is degenerate — [[route-sparse-schedule]] §8.2, corrected 2026-07-31: its literal shape is $\mathbb E^A\mathbb E^H_f$ vs $\mathbb E^H$, not the Tower's $\mathbb E^H\mathbb E^A$ vs $\mathbb E^H$), with both soft-TT inequalities as corollaries; if $\sum w_n<\infty$ both one-sided inequalities hold trivially per-day on the schedule. Named hypotheses: (S1) standing $\mathcal{BLCS}$/$\Gamma_A$-determinacy; (S2) mirror $\Gamma_H$-determinacy; **(L) ledger legibility** ($\mathcal C_H = \mathrm P^L$; see §3); (R) the E.12→D.4 relativization to $\mathrm P^L$ (~0.92). **Not** assumed: joint clearing, any Brouwer construction, *live* A-visibility of H, window-disjointness, fixedness/convergence of the questions, any environment hypothesis. **Assumed** (Abram's corrections, 2026-07-31): eventual empirical feedback (determinacy + $\Gamma_A$-completeness, rate-free) plus schedule-prompt *computability* of each forecast's outcome before the next scheduled bet; the sparsity is the computability latency, never a settlement deadline ([[route-sparse-schedule]] §1's ladder, §3's mechanism remark). Engine: erratum-corrected 4.8.16 on both markets + `cee`; 4.8.15 never appears.

A second, independent proof of the scheduled form: the **patient realized-cash bridge** ([[route-recurring-ccee]] Thm 5.2 + Prop 5.3) — v3's Theorem 1 trader survives the deletion of joint clearing (it is H-side only), and its sparsity is *forced* by risk budget ($f$-patient weightings accumulate only $O(\log^\ast n)$ mass), not an artifact.

**Presentation-canonical proof: [[theorem-ss-streamlined]]** — Tower-form packaging via the readability collapse (decided-atom arbitrage: a market's estimate of a published number is that number), with the human-side bridge by existing theorems per Abram's preference (`cee` + 4.8.16 applied to H — the pleasing symmetry: both halves are the same theorem, once about A's forecast of H's future, once about H's forecast of its own), plus a hypothesis-thinning remark showing (S2′)/(R′) are removable by a Kelly round-trip trader. ~0.84 citation route / ~0.85 trader route. **Analytic engine KERNEL-CHECKED** (`lean-deference/StreamlinedSS.lean`, T1–T5: weighted-average calculus + donor rule, Kelly ledger, arbitrage engine, full assembly with satisfiability witnesses, TT corollary arithmetic; sorry-free, standard axioms, independently rebuilt).

## 2. The trade is exact, and now proved from both sides

- **Per-day ⟷ joint clearing.** The violation weighting needs $\mathbb E^H_n(X_n)$ to be A-generable; that is precisely and only what joint clearing buys ([[route-sparse-schedule]] §8.3, [[route-recurring-ccee]] §5.6). The single unbiasedness application that would close the all-days gap exists exactly under joint clearing — it *is* v3.
- **All-days averaged: NOT derivable under one-way visibility** from the route's premise set ([[route-recurring-ccee]] Prop 5.4 — explicit witness with a permanent weighted deficit; the two 4.8.15 limit points land on disjoint subsequences). Open problem 7: **NO** (~0.85), with the caveat of §4 below (Lemma P shrinks the witness space; realizability of the witness by actual inductors requires its bad-day structure to be invisible to *every* A-generable weighting, including hard-coded ones — an immune-set-like condition, now the sharpest open question).
- **Soft conditioning collapses for a readable expert** ([[route-transitivity]] Lemma B): when the weighting is a generable rational feature of H's own prices — which the published quote is, under (L) — the ramp factors out, and soft TT at the quote weighting ≡ per-day dominance on the weighting's support (≡ v3's Corollary 2). So the deck's soft-TT notion, against a ledger-published expert, IS a per-day notion; the weighted family is a strictly weaker notion, not a bookkeeping variant.
- **What survives all-days, unconditionally**: the Oscillation Theorem ([[route-recurring-ccee]] T3) — a permanent weighted deficit of margin $c$ forces the weighted realized future credence to oscillate forever with amplitude $\ge c$; contrapositive T3′: if the weighted $Y$ converges, recurring soft TT holds (full limit if $\bar h$ also converges). Surprise, not variation, again.
- **The visibility axis is really a feedback-promptness axis** (Abram, 2026-07-31). Every result assumes H's deliberation outcomes eventually settle in A's world (determinacy via $\Gamma_A$ + $\Gamma_A$-completeness of $D_A$ — without which nothing constrains the quote, Half 1 included). An in-class H-simulator alone exerts no pressure: exploitation is assessed against $\mathrm{PC}(D_A^n)$, so unsettled bets are plausibly catastrophic and traders must be vindicated by $D_A$'s decisions. The rungs: rate-free eventual feedback ⟷ Half 1 / Lemma P / Theorem A (limit points, envelopes); rate-free settlement + schedule-prompt *computability* of the outcome ⟷ Theorem SS (full limit); live ⟷ v3 (per-day). Recorded at [[route-sparse-schedule]] §1, with the D.4 mechanism (round-trip realized cash; truth via rate-free PI) at §3.

## 3. The (L) convergence, and a wiki erratum of our own

All four routes independently converged on the same load-bearing hypothesis: **the quote ledger must be inside H's trader/generator class** ((L) / H⁺-CLASS), not merely "settled data for traders". One-way visibility is structurally cheap for *traders* and **not free for weightings**.

**Erratum (two independent derivations).** [[unbiasedness-theorem-families]] §6's ledger-weighting repair is invalid as stated: (i) its Provability Induction citation feeds 4.2.1 a *mixed* e.c. sequence of sentences, and selecting the true atoms is exactly the produce-hard step (Trap 4 inside the repair to Trap 2); (ii) the weighting it builds converges to the **hard** indicator, mismatching A's ramp on the band $a_n\in(t,t+\delta]$. Repair: assume (L); then the raw quote weighting is directly generable and $\tilde u$ is optional (and 4.2.1, relativized, then works if wanted). [[faithful-acceleration-result]] §4.3 step 3 needs the same amendment.

## 4. The negative side: reversed, and the obstruction looks structural

The impossibility attempt ([[route-negative-introspective]]) initially claimed a counterexample (~0.70) — questions $X_n = \ulcorner \mathbb P^H_{g(n)}(\psi_{g(n)})>\tfrac14\urcorner$ over a revisiting enumeration of hard sentences, H forced by an explicit two-line trader, A blinded by the one-way channel. Adversarial verification killed it, twice over:

1. **The hard-coded-index leak.** Family-C hypotheses (4.8.17's) quantify over weightings that *exist*, not weightings A can *find*: for any single false index $k_0$, the sparse e.c. indicator of its revisit days is patient, divergent (by revisiting), and carries $Y\to0$ — falsifying the opacity hypothesis in every variant (including PA-independent-carrier repairs).
2. **Lemma P (the replacement finding — a new free tool).** Expectation Preemptive Learning/Persistence (4.8.13/4.8.12) applied to A: on **every e.c. day-set where the realized target $Y_n$ converges to $c$, the quote $a_n\to c$** — pointwise, full limit, hypothesis-free given standing determinacy. This corrects [[unbiasedness-theorem-families]] §2's sorting ("family D cannot serve Half 1"): for *determined* targets it can, because there the limit expectation is the truth. And it collapses the construction: revisiting is *necessary* for the straddle (the §5.5 Uncomputable Convergence Rates proposition kills every "wait until resolved" schedule), but revisiting manufactures exactly the e.c. convergent day-sets on which Lemma P closes the weighting. **The straddle engine is symmetric between the two markets.**

Kept from the first draft (correct throughout): the three straddle-design kills (liar-like dies on forcing — pinning the price at the threshold destroys the margin; pseudorandom-as-question dies on base-rate; wait-for-resolution dies on uncomputable rates); the Lemma F forcing trader; and the **reversal of the surprise story** — a failure driven by the human being surprised cannot be built on this channel at all (`ceu`: H's credence already equals its own forecast of its future credence, the same object A forecasts); any failure driver must be the human's *private knowledge*.

Net: open problem 9 down to ~0.2 (introspective route) / ~0.3 (overall); the residual crack is the non-uniform per-index window between H's resolution and A's catch-up ($\bigcup_k [H_k, C_k)$), which must avoid containing any infinite e.c. subset with convergent $Y$ — immune-set territory. The plausibility of the **per-day positive** result under one-way visibility goes **up**, with Lemma P as the new full-limit tool pointed at open problem 7.

## 5. Composition: settled, with a refinement

Open problem 14 (does `st` + Half 1 compose?): **NO as an inference** (0.90; [[route-transitivity]], convergent with [[route-recurring-ccee]] §7.2). Refinements:

- **The corpus's "direction" worry is real** — theorem-level, not a vibe: the correctly-shaped finite-frame square is refuted by a 4-world witness (gap exactly $\tfrac18$, hand-verified) even with exact calibration on every weighting, and **proved under nesting** $\sigma(a)\subseteq\mathfrak M$. The LI mirror of nesting is a *timing* fact — publication before the lookahead — so the worry is neutralized here by the publication schedule, not by anything about trust. Checkable prediction: if $e(n) > F(n)$, this failure mode goes live.
- **What kills the composition here is the grain/strength mismatch, quantified**: Theorem B ([[route-transitivity]]; full limit, all days, no visibility hypotheses) bounds the deficit by $(1+t)\,\mathbb E^H_n(\ulcorner\min(1,|a_n-Y_n|/\delta)\urcorner)$ — H's per-day expectation of the **truncated absolute** forecast error. Half 1 supplies signed, weighted, limit-point control: mismatched in all three dimensions. Under per-day accuracy the composition closes fully (Cor. B.1).
- **Transitivity is not a fourth route** — its only non-vacuous repair collapses into the `cee` route, and literally it is strictly worse (absolute vs signed error control). The prior `deference-trust-lab/run3/work/tt-transitivity-forall/` work is correct but about the *chain* square (middle party a truster); logically independent of item 14 — do not cite it for item 14.

Also settled en route: open problem 13 — the weighted tower identity is **PROVED, full limit, all days** (and `cee`+4.8.10 suffices; `ccee` unnecessary at a day-$n$ weighting), but the "removes one limit point" accounting is **REFUTED** (the step it upgrades was already a full limit; the count stays at two).

## 6. What to say about Eisenstat's lookahead construction (replaces the old §6 story)

*Detailed version, with proofs, per-claim statuses, the loop transport, and the caveats: [[eisenstat-lookahead-construction]] — including a NEW two-sided upgrade of the fixed-question result via Lemma P.*

*⚠ Attribution correction (2026-08-10): the one-way setting this lab worked in does not match the information structure Sam Eisenstat intended (AI reads human beliefs immediately; humans see AI beliefs only at a delay) — read "Eisenstat's lookahead construction" throughout as the corpus construal; see [[eisenstat-conjecture-attribution]].*

1. **Unconditionally** — no visibility, no shared clearing, no delay assumption — the lookahead quotes are unbiased for the human's realized future credence on every weighting the forecaster can recognize (Half 1, limit point; engine kernel-checked), **and pointwise-exactly on every e.c. day-set where that future credence converges (Lemma P, full limit, new).**
2. **On a fixed question** the accelerator works under arbitrary delay (Theorem A, ~0.90).
3. **On varying questions, a publication channel plus an evaluation-sparse schedule buys two-sided quote–credence agreement in weighted average** (Theorem SS) — Tower-equivalent via the ledger collapse, both one-sided inequalities of scheduled soft Total Trust as corollaries — with the publication channel needed *inside the trader class* ((L)), which is what "the human has heard the AI" formally costs.
4. **The per-day varying-question form is exactly the price of the forecaster seeing the human**: provably not derivable from the one-way toolkit (Prop 5.4/5.5), available under joint clearing (v3), and the matching impossibility that would show one-way per-day trust *false* has itself died structurally (Lemma P) — so per-day one-way trust is genuinely open, with the tools now pointing slightly toward "true".

Caveats that travel: the forced family is weighted and scheduled, not the tower, not deck-TT; the "$B_n$ is itself an inductor" half of the merge conjecture remains untouched; the 4.8.15/4.8.16 erratum must be cited wherever the corrected statements are used.

## 8. Frontier after the read-through (2026-07-31)

- **Optimality of the weakened notions** (Abram's closing question). Two senses. *(a) Proof-relative*: the scheduled-averaged grade is provably the best obtainable from the current toolkit — witnessed ([[route-recurring-ccee]] Prop 5.4/5.5 for the all-days strengthening; [[route-transitivity]] §7's sparse-bad-set witness for the per-day strengthening, surviving even 4.8.16-strength premises) — and by the readable-expert collapses **one witness settles Tower, TT, and two-option Value simultaneously**, since the three are pairwise equivalent at each grade. *(b) Setting-relative*: OPEN — exhibiting an actual pair where the stronger grade fails is OP9's family, twice self-destructed, ~0.2–0.3; Lemma P's pressure means per-day one-way trust may simply be **true**, making the weakening proof-technology, not reality.
- **The two attack programs**: (i) realize the per-day witness with actual inductors — its bad-day set must be cheap for H's price-features yet contain no infinite e.c. subset with convergent target (the immune-set condition); success or principled failure both settle optimality; (ii) the opposite bet — prove per-day one-way trust outright, with Lemma P as the lever (pointwise full-limit currency; what it lacks is coverage of non-convergent stretches). Adversarial pairing of the two is the honest design.
- **The Value conjecture** ([[theorem-ss-streamlined]] §8, ~0.7, unvetted): F1 degenerates for the readable lookahead expert (selection by *published* quotes, ledger-decided tie-break), conjecturally yielding scheduled weighted-average Value over arbitrary e.d. bounded menus with **no decision-theoretic scope condition** — the Newcomblike pathologies landing in the pinned stratum rather than refuting the statement. Needs the F1 near-identity written out and a stress test against [[total-trust-implies-value]]'s selection-referencing counterexample. **⚠ 2026-08-03: the stress test failed — REFUTED as stated** (the constant-probe punishing menu transports to the averaged grade; F1 fails by $s(1-s)$ because $A$'s access to its own argmax is introspective, not decided); the two-option/hedged transport stands, and the repaired scope-conditioned form is ~0.8. [[weak-loop-and-value-transport]].
- **The session's organizing discovery, named**: *readable-expert degeneracy*. One mechanism — published quotes are decided data — collapsed, in turn, the Tower's inner expectation (L4), soft conditioning (Lemma B), and now conjecturally F1. Slogan: a readable expert has no private conditionals that matter; every notion of trust in it collapses toward cross-agent agreement.
