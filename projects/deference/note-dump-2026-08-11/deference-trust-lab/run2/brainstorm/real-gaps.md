## 1. The merge discharge (Fast Student / Slow Teacher)

CRITIQUE §1 is the harshest and most important: the merge is the flagship constructive payoff and it
is hand-waved at exactly three points — **(a) B-inductor-ness, (b) the Hop-2 standpoint shift,
(c) B-observability-as-feature checked vs LI's generability definition**. Round 1 produced no real
result here; it produced a *reduction* (merge = §10 with expert B) plus three open sub-problems.

Honest routing of the three:

**(a) "B is a logical inductor."** This is AGENDA conjecture (i), and the round-1 ideate correctly
split it into 1a (behavioral: B tracks H's limit on the good-feedback subsequence) and 1b (criterion:
no ℙ^B-efficient trader exploits B). 1b is **PROOF-ONLY and probably FALSE unrestricted** — the
honest content is a NEGATIVE result: *B's prices are a poly-time function of ℙ^A, not its own market,
so off the good-feedback subsequence B is "anything A likes," and a trader that knows A's bias on the
unobservable class exploits B.* You cannot Lean this honestly (it needs the trader/criterion
machinery), and assuming "B is generated by its own market" would be exactly the laundering ban. So
**(a) is PROOF-ONLY**, and the deliverable is the sharp statement + proof sketch of the negative
side, with the positive side ("B is exploitation-free ON the good-feedback subsequence") flagged
medium-open. This is a genuine gap and an honest non-Lean is a SUCCESS here.

**(b) The Hop-2 standpoint shift (ℙ^A → ℙ^H).** This is "the one true gap" the round-1 model named.
The substitution of `ℙ^H_{f(t)}(φ)` by `B_t(φ)=𝔼^A_t(⌜ℙ^H_{f(t)}(φ)⌝)` must survive *inside H's
ℙ^H-generable-weighted expectation*. `thm:wub` gives A-unbiasedness against the realized truth; we
need it H-relative after weighting. This is **PROOF-ONLY** at the LI level. BUT there is a genuinely
new, honest, NON-LI thing to extract here that round 1 missed: the **abstract substitution lemma**
that the whole argument is an instance of — "if an estimator is mean-zero-biased against truth on a
weighting *that one party can generate*, when may a *second* party substitute it under *its own*
generable weighting?" The round-1 ideate said this is "morally a known move" but never isolated the
exact algebraic obligation. In the radical-probabilist / finite-frame shadow this becomes a
**checkable inequality about two weighting σ-algebras** (the EXEC item, candidate below): when do
A's-generable and H's-generable weight classes *share enough* that an A-mean-zero residual is also
H-mean-zero? That is a real, decidable finite question and the honest core of Route-A-vs-Route-B.

**(c) B-observability-as-feature vs LI generability.** CRITIQUE §1 (iii): §10 needs `(B_t(O^j))_t`
to be ℙ^H-generable, but B is ℙ^A-generable; round 1 resolved this "by fiat" (A publishes B_t, H
reads it). The CRITIQUE demands this be **checked against LI Def 4.3** (expressible features /
market-generability), not asserted by analogy with market prices. This is the cleanest *definitional*
gap and the one place a real LEAN-CORE result is reachable: **the closure properties of
ℙ-generable features are a finite/decidable algebra** (generable functions are closed under +, ·,
max, composition with poly-time-computable continuous maps, reading externally-posted bounded
numbers). One can formalize "the class of generable features is a sub-algebra closed under reading a
posted bounded number, hence a published B_t IS a legal H-feature" as a genuine LEAN-CORE lemma about
an explicitly-defined closure — *without* assuming any LI theorem, because feature-generability is a
syntactic/algebraic closure condition, not a martingale fact. The NEAR-MISS: drop "bounded" and the
closure fails (an unbounded posted number breaks the [0,1]-LUV typing, re-opening Weatherson's Coin).

**Net:** (a) PROOF-ONLY (negative result), (b) PROOF-ONLY at LI level + an EXEC finite shadow of the
substitution obligation, (c) LEAN-CORE (a real generable-feature closure lemma). This is the most
honest decomposition I can give and it produces one genuinely new Lean target that is NOT a re-skin.

## 2. Honest updateless-deference (derive node-value as EDT-conditional of U)

CRITIQUE §3 and the updateless red-team agree: the round-1 relation `A updatelessly-defers to u :⇔
U(π_u)=max_p U(π)` with `π_u(s)=argmax_a node_value[s][a]` is a **tautology wrapper around argmax**
because `node_value` is a *free input never tied to U*. The kernel-checked Lean (`endorsement_reduction`
/ `split_eq_global`) proves only "argmax of a separable U is globally optimal" — real Lean, useless as
deference content. The red-team's single most valuable next step (verbatim): **make node_value a
derived quantity — define `v_s(a) := E_p[ U(π) | reach s, π(s)=a ]`, the genuine EDT-conditional of
the SAME U — and re-run the examples.**

This is the right gap and it has a genuine, validatable core:

- **The construction** (derive `node_value` from `U` via an explicit EDT-conditional) is a concrete
  finite object once you fix a self-prediction / reachability model. EXEC: build the EDT-conditional
  for the canonical menu (counterfactual mugging, transparent Newcomb, a genuine empirical
  improvement, Twin PD) and check whether the *derived* relation classifies them correctly —
  *without* a hand-fed `node_value`. This is the honest version of "6/6 cases pass" (the red-team
  found only 3 executed, hand-fed). The FAKE version to avoid: re-introducing any free
  per-node valuation; the whole point is that the only input is `U` and the self-prediction kernel.

- **The genuine theorem** that would make this a real Total-Trust⇔Value object: in the **decoupled
  (no cross-situation dependence)** case, the EDT-derived `node_value` argmax COINCIDES with the
  global optimum AND with van-Fraassen endorsement of the updated belief — a true biconditional, not
  the one-directional argmax fact round 1 shipped. The decoupled coincidence is plausibly LEAN-CORE
  (it is finite algebra about a derived conditional), and crucially the EEDT-conditional, not an LI
  theorem, is the object — so no laundering. The hard half (coupled case genuinely diverges, and the
  divergence is *exactly* the acausal payoff) is EXEC (exhibit it numerically) → PROOF-ONLY for the
  general characterization. The pre-registered fake: proving `argmax of separable U is optimal` again
  under a new name — that is the off-limits round-1 content.

The deep prize (the principal's actual conjecture): "updateful about values, updateless about the
rest" = be updateful exactly on the **separable/decoupled** sub-σ-algebra of U. The
EDT-derived-node-value construction is what lets you STATE this without circularity, because
"separable coordinate" is now defined on U directly, and the EDT-conditional collapses to the local
argmax there. That statement is the genuine new conceptual content.

## 3. Non-circular legitimacy / corrigibility

The round-1 legitimacy thread defines legitimacy = LUV-Total-Trust(N→E) and admits (its own fidelity
caveat, L1) that this is "only as contentful as the claim truth-tracking ⇒ small defect," which is
the v2 §10.4 open characterization = D3 itself. So the round-1 legitimacy *definition is circular*
relative to the thing it should explain. CRITIQUE §0 and §5–§6 confirm: the corrigibility sign-flip
and wirehead-decline are pointwise *encodings* of "endorsed", not *derivations* of why a signal is
endorsed. Two honest moves:

**(3a) A non-circular legitimacy definition: process-level, not trust-level.** Legitimacy should be
a property of E's *belief-formation process* that *entails* low cross-martingale defect, not a
rename of low defect. The candidate: define a process as **truth-tracking** if its update on
evidence e moves its credence in the direction of (and not past) the Bayes factor of e for the
hypothesis — a CONDITION ON THE PROCESS, checkable without reference to N's trust. Then the genuine
theorem is **truth-tracking ⇒ a sign condition on the defect** in a finite frame. This breaks the
circularity: the hypothesis is about the process, the conclusion is about the defect, and they are
DIFFERENT objects. The finite shadow (LEAN-CORE/EXEC): a process that updates *monotonically toward
the likelihood* yields a defect of a definite (non-adverse) sign; a process that overshoots (the
wirehead) yields the adverse sign. This is adjacent to `drug_defect_sign` (OFF-LIMITS) but is NOT the
same: round 1 ASSUMED the pointwise overshoot `Eθ_drug ≥ θ`; the new content is to *derive* the
overshoot sign from a process condition (an update rule), so the headline hypothesis is the update
rule, not the overshoot. The pre-registered fake: assuming the pointwise overshoot again — that is
exactly `wirehead_declined` and is off-limits.

**(3b) The corrigibility STRICT/weak gap and an honest impossibility.** CRITIQUE §5: the sign-flip
gives only weak (`≥0`) preference — `s≡0` satisfies both fire conditions, so "complies" is never
strict. A genuinely new item: identify the **minimal honest extra hypothesis** under which compliance
is STRICT (e.g. the signal has positive endorsed mass: `∃ w, π_w s_w (2 d_w − 1) > 0`), prove strict
compliance under it (LEAN-CORE — a strict-inequality refinement, with a NEAR-MISS showing that
dropping positive-mass makes it non-strict, recovering the `s≡0` degeneracy), AND state the
**corrigibility-vs-competence tension** (the Soares-style impossibility) in the endorsement calculus:
*an agent that absolutely-endorses every modification map, including edits to its own objective,
cannot also be modeled as a competent optimizer of that objective* — because conditional-endorsement
ordering breaks. The tension is PROOF-ONLY/INTERPRETATION (no LI dynamics), but it is honestly so,
and naming it crisply is a contribution.

## 4. The cheapest skipped real result: Aumann-fails-under-modesty (D9)

CRITIQUE §8 names this the cheapest real new result and flags it as SKIPPED: "the finite 3-world
example is LEAN-ABLE — not built. Arguably the most directly principal-relevant item ('the AI
disagrees with me'), and the most clearly tractable finite example." There is a prior-art risk
(Geanakoplos / non-partitional agreeing-to-disagree is studied), which the CRITIQUE flags — so the
honest framing is NOT "we discovered Aumann fails non-partitionally" (known) but the LAB-SPECIFIC
content: **modesty (the S4-non-Euclidean / non-immodest frame of v2 §5.3) is exactly the condition
under which two reasoners with common-knowledge-of-disagreement cannot agree** — i.e. the v2 §5.2
immodesty/modesty dichotomy IS the partition/non-partition dichotomy, made concrete on a 3-world
frame. This is LEAN-CORE: a finite frame where (i) the expert is modest (`P_w(P=P_w) < 1` at some
world), (ii) the posteriors of expectations are common knowledge, yet (iii) they differ — a witnessed
counterexample to the agreement conclusion, with a NEAR-MISS: make the same frame immodest (the S5
partition) and agreement is forced. This connects modesty (the lab's structural thesis) to Aumann
(the principal's flagged concern) by a concrete object, not an analogy.

The FAKE version to avoid: a 2-world frame where "disagreement" is just "different priors" (that's
not Aumann-failure, agents trivially can differ on priors); or asserting common knowledge without
encoding the fixed-point. The real object needs the iterated-knowledge fixed point on a genuinely
non-partitional (modest) information structure.

## 5. The Hop-2 substitution shadow (EXEC) — the one place an executable search bites

Round 1 left Hop 2 entirely to prose. The honest EXEC item: the substitution obligation has a
**finite-frame shadow** that is searchable. Set up two weighting σ-algebras (A-generable `𝒢_A`,
H-generable `𝒢_H`) on a small finite frame, an estimator residual `r = ℙ^H_{f(t)} − B`, and ask:
over randomized/exhaustive small frames, *does A-mean-zero of r on every 𝒢_A-weight imply H-mean-zero
of r on every 𝒢_H-weight?* The CONJECTURE (and I expect the answer is NO in general, YES iff
`𝒢_H ⊆ 𝒢_A` after the standpoint shift) is exactly Route-A-vs-Route-B. An exhaustive search over
3–4 world frames will either find the counterexample (proving the standpoint shift is NOT free,
vindicating CRITIQUE's "Route B only relocates") or fail to (suggesting a clean sufficient condition).
Either outcome is a real result about the actual obligation, not a shadow of a different claim. The
FAKE version: searching frames where 𝒢_A = 𝒢_H by construction (then the swap is trivially legal and
proves nothing) — the search must include frames where the two weight classes genuinely differ.
