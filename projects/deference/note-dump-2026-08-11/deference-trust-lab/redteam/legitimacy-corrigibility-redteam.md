### (a) Is the central claim TRUE? — TRUE as stated in Lean; the PROSE is too strong.

**Finding 1 (the load-bearing gap, from `legcorr_counterex.py`).** The doc's headline says the
wirehead "is exactly an operation that drives the legitimacy defect strictly negative" and frames
this as the formal content of "they would enjoy it if it happened" (reported utility rises). But the
theorem `wirehead_declined` requires **pointwise overstatement** `∀x, θ_x ≤ E_drug_x`, which is
**strictly stronger** than "reported utility rises" `E_π(E_drug) > E_π(θ)`. Concrete counterexample
(exact rationals, runs): `θ=(1,0)`, `E_drug=(0.95, 0.20)`, `π=(½,½)`. Average reported utility
**rises** by `+3/40`, yet on the *model's own witness* `w = Ind(E_drug>½) = (1,0)` the legitimacy
defect is **`+1/40 > 0`**. This is a "euphoric-but-numb" drug: it reports high (but slightly
sub-true) happiness in the genuinely-good world and a little false happiness in the bad world. It
**raises future-reported utility** but does **not** drive the defect negative. So the claim "the
wirehead drives the defect strictly negative" is FALSE *as a consequence of* "reported utility
rises"; it holds only for the pointwise-overstating subclass. The substantive content lives entirely
in `hover`, and the prose should say so.

**Finding 2 (decline = TIE, not strict, from `legcorr_stress.py` ATTACK 1).** Under the model's own
definitions `V_now(drug) = E_π(θ) = V_now(abstain)`, so current-endorsement makes the agent
**indifferent**, and "declines" is read off `¼ > ¼ = False`. The strictly-negative object (`−13/20`)
is the *drug-report valuation gap* (`V_fut − V_now`), **not** a strict reason to abstain. "Declines"
honestly means "never strictly prefers the drug," which is what the Lean proves (`defect ≤ 0`). The
prose "**declines**" invites reading a strict preference that isn't there.

**Corrigibility half: genuinely SOLID.** ATTACK 4/5 confirm `endorsed_signal_complies` /
`adversarial_signal_resists` are non-vacuous and sign-discriminating: the *same* frame with a
different `s` flips `adv` from `+½` to `−½`; the blank signal fails the positive theorem's `hfire`
pointwise (consistent with "silent"). The impossibility witness is real.

### (d) Smallest change that makes the over-strong claim honest (and keeps it substantive).

**The fix (one sentence + one hypothesis name).** In §0 and §2.2, replace "the wirehead is **exactly**
an operation that drives the legitimacy defect strictly negative" with: "**a pointwise-overstating**
wirehead drives the defect ≤ 0 (strict on a positive-mass overshoot world); a wirehead that merely
raises *average* reported utility need not — see the euphoric-but-numb counterexample." Then the
PROVED claim and the prose coincide, and the model gains a **sharper, true** statement: *the decline
is driven by **calibration failure in the direction of overstatement**, not by the report rising.*
This actually strengthens the wireheading analogy (the dangerous drugs are the **overstating** ones;
a numbing drug that under-reports the real good is declined for a *different* reason — it lowers, not
raises, the witnessed defect). Also add one line: "current-endorsement yields **indifference** (a tie),
which we read as 'does not take the drug'; the strict negativity is the *report-gap*, not a strict
abstention incentive."
