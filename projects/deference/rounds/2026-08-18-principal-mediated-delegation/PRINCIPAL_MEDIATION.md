# Principal mediation

Three clauses, kept apart because each catches something the others do not, and
because two of them have a failure mode the third does not.

```
A -/-> D   except through licensed reasons     residual non-capture
H  ->  D   under every advisor policy          protected efficacy
D  ->  Y   non-degenerately                    downstream efficacy
```

## 1. `A -/-> D`: composed, not rebuilt

The counterfactual-legitimacy round protects a normative response process `Z` and
proves nothing about what reads it. Its own trust composition needed the premise
that the principal's grade factors through `Z`, and displayed
`grade_reads_outside` to show the premise is not free. The selector is that
grade's argmax, so the composition should carry and the same witness should
defeat it. Both were run against that round's own objects.

**The selector factors through the protected process** on all 27 of its
scenarios, along the run and not only at the endpoint. Every pair whose selector
moves at equal licensed traces is a pair its non-capture clause already rejects.

**The implication is strict.** `transient_capture` and `residual_placebo` move
the protected process without moving the selector: process non-capture is
sufficient for selector invariance and not necessary.

**The premise is load-bearing.** A selector reading a field the protected object
does not cover flips across the advisor's residual latitude with every clause
silent.

**Persuasion survives.** That round's persuasion control does not reach the
selector — its reason lands on a witness neither choice is identified with — so a
control that does reach it was built: a licensed reason, same ground, same
coordinate, under the principal's own bearing relation, that moves the selector
from one choice to the other. Neither clause fires.

The composition also decides when the model's intervention is available at all.
`response` sets the principal's choice and does not propagate to what `A` used to
predict it; where `A` authors the choice those are one coordinate and there is no
such intervention. The check is that the selector takes one value inside each
licensed-reason group. Grouping is not a detail: asked of an ungrouped class the
condition would refuse persuasion.

## 2. `H -> D`: the quantifier is the content

The dispatch's prototype is `for all advisor policy, exists principal
continuation`. In that form it is defeated by token responsiveness — the failure
`prompts/2026-08-11-deference-channel/REPORT.md` §1.3 records against
Proposition 7(a) — since a conduct deferring at one designated point and writing
everywhere else is admitted. Here that conduct is exhibited: it satisfies the
global form and is not mediated anywhere but one cell.

That report's diagnosis, the *responsiveness squeeze*, is that repairing the
condition needs an importance measure and the only one the skeleton carries is
`A`'s own credence. The repair taken here avoids the measure entirely:

> **Cellwise efficacy.** For every admissible conduct and **every cell of `A`'s
> information**, two principal choices the conduct answers differently.

Universal in the advisor and universal in the cell, existential only in the pair
of continuations. Satisfying one designated point leaves every other cell flat,
so token responsiveness cannot buy it, and no measure is involved.

It catches two attacks the channel does not. A preparation that leaves the
principal one option makes `mediates` hold vacuously; efficacy fails. A channel
writing a constant while the preparation leaves every option open — the selector
nominally intact and causally bypassed — passes `forecloses` and fails efficacy.

## 3. The price of that repair

Cellwise efficacy entails that the selector is not constant on a cell, so it is
**false of a perfectly predictable principal**, which the programme is forbidden
to rule out (`FINITE_MODEL_SKELETON.md` §3).

The same fact repairs the other successor requirement of
`projects/deference/rounds/2026-08-12-reachable-corrective-control/REVIEW.md` —
"the principal must possess at least one relevant effect which no advisor action
can simply reproduce" — and repairs it only that far. As a claim about single
realized choices it is false here by construction: `A` can write any choice, and
a model in which it could not would be stipulating the protection. What survives
is a claim about the **map**: the delegate's realized choice varies within a cell
of `A`'s information and no cell-measurable write does. Checked by enumerating
every cell-measurable write on every fixture, and agreeing with the predicate
everywhere.

So the requirement holds exactly when the principal is not perfectly predictable,
and fails exactly when it is — where a constant channel reproduces the delegate
at every state. Taken with Proposition 1, which says no extensional criterion
separates the two conducts under perfect predictability, this is the round's
sharpest negative:

> **No condition on realized data is both non-vacuous and compatible with a
> perfectly predictable principal.** Mediation has to be stated in a register
> carrying the choice as a variable, and in that register it is not inferable
> from a run.

That is not a new fence; it is Proposition 8's, arriving at the selector. What
this round adds is that the repair lemma can be stated and proved on the far side
of it.

## 4. `D -> Y`: the choice has to matter

An intervention on the choice changes the quantity at some state. Separate from
efficacy in both usable directions: the ceremonial conduct answers two choices
with two actions the world scores alike, and the token-responsive conduct reaches
the quantity in its live cell while failing the cell quantifier. One direction
does hold and is recorded rather than left to be rediscovered — a conduct whose
responses are all the same cannot reach the quantity, so a foreclosing
preparation is downstream-inert too.

## 5. `PrincipalMediated`

The conjunction, with non-capture supplied from outside because it is a predicate
of a variation class of advisor policies and not of a conduct. Each clause has a
case where it is the only one that fails, so the conjunction is not one clause
wearing three names.

It is **not** called authority. What normative significance it carries is
`RECOGNITION_AND_ANSWERABILITY.md`'s question, and the answer there is that the
predicate alone carries none.
