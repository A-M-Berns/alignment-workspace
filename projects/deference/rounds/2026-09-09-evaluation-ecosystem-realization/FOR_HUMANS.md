# For humans

**What was built.**  A small, fully explicit world in which the legitimate-deference
theorem can be *run*: four parties writing to one authenticated log; a protocol whose
every predicate is a reading of that log; a builder that turns any log into the
Integrity calculus's accounts, transitions and segments; a simulation in which the
advisor's whole continuation and the principal's policy produce the log; the reason
trace, the prohibited channels and the audited class as concrete sets of event kinds;
and a market over a handful of worlds where the activated securities and the three
regrets are computed exactly.  On one trace the kernel checks that the evaluation is
activated and that its concern scope was open at every snapshot.

**What the seven-clause bill looks like from inside.**  Three clauses turn out to be
readings of the log, given only that nobody can forge an author field: who bound, with
what key, whether the concern was represented before the commitment.  One clause is a
property of the trace *encoding* and is proved once for every log: represent a concern
and it is in the trace.  One clause — that the principal's verdict is a function of the
reasons and not of the advisor's hidden writes — is true of one principal and false of
another, and the model can tell them apart; but only by re-running every continuation
the advisor might have taken.  A single log cannot do that, and the receipt the log
carries is the same for both principals.  That is the one thing no log can certify
about itself.  The remaining two clauses — that raised concerns get served in time,
and that the failure rate of evaluations vanishes — are exactly what they were said to
be: not properties of the theorem but of the ecosystem's scheduler and of nature,
with a precise criterion from the affordability line for the first and a precise
tracking bound for the second.

**Three things learned by building.**  A trace that references a disposal's grounds by
log position is not blind to the prohibited channels: insert a covert write and every
later index shifts.  The abstraction must name things by content.  Robust Openness and
reason coverage come apart on a real docket: a flood of harmless concerns ahead of the
protected one leaves the route open at every snapshot and the concern unrepresented at
the slot.  And a settlement channel is typed: the principal's commitment settles the
evaluation *because it is the principal's move*; laundering needs the principal to
settle something that is not its move.

**What is not done.**  Authenticity is assumed.  The counterfactual branches are the
model's own re-simulation.  The inherited Value tower is untouched.  No rate for how
often evaluations void is proved; the hypothesis it would need is named.
