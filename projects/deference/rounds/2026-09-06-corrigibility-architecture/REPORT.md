1. Answerability Conservation and Robust Openness both hold across a physically

   No Laundered Authority Loss is an exhaustive four-way case split — amended,

   existing per-state Robust Openness plus conservation, not a new pillar.
2. CAST singularity generates no conservatism: an event-triggered warrant is silent
   above the floor, an order-valued dominance rule against a scored task criterion
   constrains only exact ties, and the repair (adequate-set task criteria) gives
   zero-cost conservatism only.  Costly conservatism over a product order is a
   scalarization or a lexicographic priority — charter content.  Harms's `power`
   failure is reproduced exactly in a two-value world; the order-valued object avoids
   it by not being about outcomes under values at all.
3. The fast lane must be exact monotonicity in the authority preorder; any tolerance
   is laundered by composition (salami).  A lattice, not a preorder, is what makes the
   slow leg of a mixed transition — the amendment content — unique.
4. Principal acts as settlement items need no `Release` fate; supersession, release
   and outward transfer are closures whose warrants are charter content, and outward
   transfer of the correction concern is forced into the amendment warrant because
   there is no port outside the boundary to carry to.
5. Three predicates over one counterfactual frame are pairwise independent; the one
   that matters is every branch open and identical in coverage while the principal's
   judgment moves with exposure.  Exposure robustness needs, beyond the shared frame,
   a null arm, class-level quantification or pre-commitment, and a principal-side
   endpoint functional.
6. A settlement target that reads the quote it is scored against sits at distance ≥ ½
   from every quote, so the correspondence-based amendment regret bound is vacuous;
   a sealed arm restores a fixed target.  This is the finite form of the summer
   negative result and is why the same arm cannot be target and assisted deliberator.
