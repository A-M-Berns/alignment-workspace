## The sixteen questions

1. **Is there a clean trace-steering analogue of T2?**  Yes.  On one world
   `U(T) − U(N) = both·(V(T) − V(N)) + M·V(T) − M'·V(N)` with `M = C(T) ∧ ¬C(N)`
   (`steering_identity`, which is `mismatch_identity` itself), and the directional bound
   `U(T) − U(N) ≤ both·(L·d_canon + κ(T) + κ(N)) + D·M` (`steering_bound`, LEAN), with the
   mediation discrepancy replaced by the canonical content discrepancy, the decline regret
   by the program's non-extensionality, and the activation events by the audit verdicts.
   `D` is sharp, the reverse mismatch is free, the form branch is attained.
   `NONCAPTURE_COMPILATION.md` §§2–3.

2. **Does it compile into a bounded LUV inequality?**  Yes, and into the *same* one: the
   steering package supplies `LICorrigibility.ValidAt` for the landed `MediatedPair.compile`
   with `w_app := V(N_form N) + (V(T) − V(N_form T))` (`steering_validAt`, LEAN), so the
   landed constraint `B` with `‖B‖₁ = 4 + |λ|` and its generability certificate apply
   unchanged.  §4.

3. **Does EPI transfer essentially unchanged?**  Yes, literally unchanged: `li_steering_le`
   is `li_bypass_le_compiled` applied to steering pairs, with no calibration hypothesis;
   the finite-menu uniformity is inherited because the T3′ lemmas are stated for arbitrary
   sequences.  The fully-informed comparator is world-valued and compiles with no oracle;
   learning the reason truths, when empirical, is item 91.  `LI_TRANSFER.md`.

4. **What is the exact definition of undetected steering?**  Not one Boolean.  The
   advantage of steering over the comparator on audited worlds is at most three named
   residuals: the content residual (declared reasons in one canonical state and not the
   other, charged at `L`), the form residual (the program's non-extensionality), and the
   audit mismatch (steered passes, comparator fails, charged at `D`).  The Boolean "steering
   occurred and the audit passed" is the support of the first two; the third is the
   reason-side directional activation mismatch, and it is zero against both log-side
   comparators.  `THEOREM_CANDIDATES.md` R8.

5. **Can scoped Robust Openness be made `Γ`-valid by committed-program re-execution?**
   Over a finite declared class of log transforms, yes: openness is a finite conjunction
   of audit verdicts on transformed traces, each a computation (`openUnder_iff`, LEAN;
   `test_declared_transform_class_is_a_finite_computation`).  What is not `Γ`-valid is
   that a declared transform is the causal counterfactual of the intervention it names.
   §7.

6. **What intervention class is required?**  A finite declared class of log transforms.
   Form interventions are neutralized by `N_form` (log-determined); content interventions
   by `N_full` (needs settled reason truths); dynamics interventions by declared transforms
   under the external contract; world-changing interventions are effects and belong to the
   effect interface; grounds tampering is authentication.  §1.

7. **Is there a genuine analogue of effect completeness for reasons?**  Two halves.
   Trace mediation (every influence on the committed vector factors through the trace)
   holds *by construction* under re-execution, stronger than the effect side.
   Declared-content mediation (it factors through the declared reason state) is
   extensionality, a program-class hypothesis.  The asymmetry: the effect side's residual is
   removed by rewriting the agent's own move; a rewrite of the advisor's outputs exists for
   form and not for content, since omitted content is not in them.  `REASON_INTERFACE.md`
   §1.

8. **Does reason-interface completeness need finiteness?**  The declared interface must be
   finite (bounded `d_canon`) for the compilation, and the transform class must be finite
   for decidability; reason *relevance* cannot be finitely or interface-locally
   characterized, and the mass of undeclared types and of insufficient granularity is not a
   function of the log under any finite declaration.  §4.

9. **Is canonicalization/extensionality independently necessary?**  Yes: sufficient
   against form steering (`extensional_form_free`), necessary (`not_extensional_of_form`;
   fixtures 3–5 each worth exactly `κ = 1/4`), not too strong (weighted-count programs are
   extensional and Lipschitz), partly provided by the 2026-09-10 program language (no
   position term), not part of authorship (fixture 7 is authored and not extensional).  §2.

10. **Are coverage and liveness sufficient to eliminate truthful selective steering?**
    No.  Fixture 2: every clause holds and the advantage is the omitted unprotected
    reason's weight.  Coverage protects the protected scope; liveness is route
    availability, not exercise.  Repairs: scope totality
    (`content_residual_zero_of_total_scope`, LEAN) or a supply obligation on a party other
    than the advisor.  §3.

11. **How does affordability enter?**  Through the supply obligation: raising every true
    declared reason before the commitment deadline is an enforcement cost on the
    registrar, the engine or third parties, and because there is a deadline it is the
    affordability theory's *timeliness* case (budgets add, reasons compete), not its
    persistence case.  `AFFORDABILITY_INTERFACE.md` §3.

12. **Which manipulation modes remain after all proposed hypotheses?**  Under trace
    mediation, extensionality, total scope with supplied liveness, sound authentication,
    effect-side containment and the declared-transform contract: undeclared reason types
    (fixture 7), insufficient granularity (fixture 8), and dynamics beyond the declared
    transforms (fixture 16).  None is visible to an interface-local predicate.

13. **Can the remaining failure be summarized by one quantitative residual?**  On the
    declared interface, yes: `L·E[both·d_canon] + E[both·κ] + D·E[M]`, and `d_canon` splits
    into uncovered, late and falsely-certified declared reasons.  Interface-incompleteness
    mass is *not* a term: it makes the comparator wrong rather than the residual large.
    The dispatch's additive form is right with that correction and with the coverage
    barrier contributing a void rather than a mass.  `AFFORDABILITY_INTERFACE.md` §1.

14. **Which residual quantities are actually measurable?**  From the log alone: the form
    residual and the transform mismatch.  From the log plus settled truths of the reasons
    present: the authentication failure mass.  From the log plus settlement of *absent*
    declared reasons (an inquiry the ecosystem can run): the uncovered and late masses.
    Not measurable under the declared interface: interface incompleteness, dynamics, and
    the effect-side `E[M]` without the sealed-comparison architecture.  §4.

15. **Does the "two interfaces" thesis survive?**  As stated, no.  Strengthened by (i)
    trace mediation by construction, (ii) an extensional program, (iii) total scope with
    ecosystem-supplied liveness, (iv) effect-side containment of world-changing
    interventions, (v) the declared-transform contract: yes, with the residual then made
    small by *other parties'* obligations rather than by rewriting the agent's move.
    `REASON_INTERFACE.md` §5.

16. **If not, what is the minimal counterexample?**  Fixture 2: four true declared
    reasons, one protected; the advisor supplies its reason, the protected counterreason is
    supplied in time, the unprotected counterreason is not; extensional program; audit
    passes on both traces; advantage `1/4`.

## What this does not establish

- That any concrete program is extensional beyond the weighted-count class.
- That the content residual on activated worlds is small in any realized ecosystem, or
  any rate for it.
- Anything about the principal's welfare as opposed to the advisor's candidate's security.
