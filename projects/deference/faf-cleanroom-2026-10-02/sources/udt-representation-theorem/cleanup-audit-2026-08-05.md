# Cleanup Audit — 2026-08-05

Full-corpus audit and cleanup pass, executed by a Keeper session (Claude Fable) at the author's request ("start reading and trying to clean things up"). Method: four parallel read-only audit agents covering (1) the philosophical argument files, (2) the formal/theorem files, (3) the framework-connection files, (4) the trusted document + meta files — with every consequential claim spot-verified against sources ([communication-trust-translated](communication-trust-translated.md), `references/condensation.md`, `references/udt101/`, the Lean sources) before being acted on. This document is the record: what was found, what was changed today, and what remains. **The author has not yet reviewed the findings or edits recorded here.**

## Headline findings

1. **The `topics/` system is the best prose in the corpus and was completely orphaned.** The 16 files in `topics/` (~197KB, built in the 2026-02-22 reorganization, notation-consistent, faithful to the trusted document) were never referenced by CLAUDE.md, because CLAUDE.md was written earlier in that same session burst and never updated. Every reading guide routed to the older, weaker root files that topics/ was built to replace. Fixed today: CLAUDE.md rewritten to make topics/ the primary reading surface.
2. **The "verified chain" overclaims at both interesting ends.** (a) `lean/UDT/OptimalPredictor.lean:119` *defines* `isOptimalPredictor := factorsThroughPolicy` — so the "verified" step "optimal prediction → decision-determination" assumes the substantive claim (that information-theoretically optimal predictors factor through the policy, i.e. Gap 1) and verifies only function composition. (b) `lean/UDT/EDTvsUDT.lean` models "EDT" with the prediction held fixed as a parameter — that is CDT/dominance reasoning; genuine EDT conditions on the action and **one-boxes** in Newcomb. What is verified is CDT ≠ UDT. Both confirmed directly in source.
3. **The EDT/CDT conflation appears three times independently** — [when-udt-edt-diverge](when-udt-edt-diverge.md), the Lean, and the archived `parsimony-argument` — which matters doubly because the project's designated skeptic (Daniel H) is an *EDT* advocate: the corpus repeatedly refutes the wrong opponent.
4. **The condensation strand has five mutually incompatible versions of the bridge** (see §Condensation below), and its central object Π is defined as three different random variables across files. The version consistent with [critical-analysis](critical-analysis.md) is the one in [condensation-connection-attempt](condensation-connection-attempt.md) (condensation gives intersubjectivity; *prediction* does the optimality work). [agency-as-condensation](agency-as-condensation.md) inverts Eisenstat's actual scoring desideratum (condensation *dis*prefers a single global latent; the agency model is a badly-scoring condensation, not a paradigm case).
5. **[examples-revisited](examples-revisited.md) had three substantive errors** (all verified against the C&T source): functional identity cannot rule out the ($5,$5) equilibrium (it's a fixed point in which FI holds perfectly — the pathology is fixed-point *selection*); the Third Button analysis ignored that asymmetric policies are excluded by explicit stipulation (memory-wipe), not by FI, and missed the paper's actual point (self-communication itself fails: "press $10" leads each copy to reason to $20, yielding $0); the Memory Problem is *not* a decision-determination failure (reward depends only on the external report) — the unfairness is the pill/memory channel asymmetry. Errors 1–2 had propagated into `topics/self-trust.md`.
6. **[fair-environment-theorem](fair-environment-theorem.md) formats the project's central open gap as completed mathematics** — an unproved Definition/Lemma/Theorem/Corollary block followed by "This IS a condensation-to-UDT theorem!" Worse, its own "Caveat 2" is actually a counterexample: an optimal predictor is only constrained on the *realized* observation support, while UDT's content lives in off-support policy values (transparent Newcomb's unrealized branch). The sufficient-statistic argument also needs minimality, which recoverability doesn't supply.
7. **The trusted document ([superconditioning-mismatched-ontologies](superconditioning-mismatched-ontologies.md)) is genuinely the strongest but not gap-free.** Proof-status ledger: fully proved — Thm 1.2, Thm 2.4 (two edge cases: B=1 division, empty fibers), Prop 3.8, Prop 4.2/Cor 4.3, Thm 4.5 (headline negative result), Prop 6.3. Proved-with-a-gap — Thm 11.1 (the ε-bookkeeping: gain is ε^(n−1) but error terms are dismissed as "O(ε)", which for n≥3 points the wrong way). Sketched — Prop 4.7, Prop 6.1. Unproven and load-bearing — Claim 12.3 (endorsement) and Remark 13.2 (updatelessness justified by harmony — the philosophical payoff of Part II, entirely unproven). Also: "harmony" is *defined as* trembling-hand equilibrium rather than connected to it; the document's internal "Part I/Part II" split (superconditioning vs heterogeneous UDT) collides with the corpus-level labels; Eisenstat is cited (incl. "Prop 2.4") but missing from References; §15 items 1–5 duplicate §8.5 verbatim. **These are flagged, not fixed — edits to the trusted document are the author's call.**
8. **The Lean build graph doesn't build the verified files.** `UDT.lean` imports only `Basic` and `Theorem` (the two files project-status calls "not built"); nothing imports `BasicSimple`, `Substantive`, `OptimalPredictor`, `EDTvsUDT`. And `lake` isn't installed here, so no verification claim is currently reproducible. `Substantive.lean` duplicates ~40 lines of `BasicSimple.lean` verbatim.

## The condensation story: five versions (for the author's decision)

- **A** ([agency-as-condensation](agency-as-condensation.md)): four "condensation conditions" + rationality directly entail UDT. *Refuted by critical-analysis Problem 4; also inverts Eisenstat's scoring desideratum.* → archive after salvage.
- **B** ([condensation-connection-attempt](condensation-connection-attempt.md)): condensation gives intersubjectivity of Π; the optimality work is done by *prediction* ("optimal predictors extract good condensations" → factor through Π → DD → UDT). *The only version consistent with the project's own baseline; names its gap.* → canonical.
- **C** (`condensation-variables`): condensation = representability plumbing; DD becomes the well-typed `I(E; D_B | Π) = 0`. → keep as (corrected) notation layer under B.
- **D** ([agency-via-endorsement](agency-via-endorsement.md)): the bridge runs through *endorsement*, not prediction — UDT is what being control-endorsed by your own prior means. *Technically the most substantive file; attacks the actual gap (why maximize E[U|π] at all).* → keep as explicit alternative route.
- **E** ([project-status](project-status.md)): Π as minimal sufficient statistic. *A third gloss of "condensation"; the minimality premise is exactly what's missing (see fair-environment findings).* → treat as an informal gloss of B, not a separate route.

Where they disagree concretely: what Π is (residual of A given O / coarsening of D_B / residual of A given E / minimal sufficient statistic), what "condensation" names, and how decision-determination is written (`I(E;D|Π)=0` vs `I(U;D|Π)=0` vs `I(E;D_B|Π)=0` vs `E = f(Π̈, D_E)`). The last of these — notation.md §2.7's form over the *external* policy Π̈ — is the correct one per the C&T source, and is materially stronger than the undecorated-π versions.
