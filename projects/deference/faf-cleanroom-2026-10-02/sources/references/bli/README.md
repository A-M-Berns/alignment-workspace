# Bayesian Logical Induction (BLI): reference copies

Copies of notes relevant to **Bayesian Logical Induction** (BLI, Sam Eisenstat's unpublished construction; Logseq alias ``BLI`` of `Sam's Bayesian Logical Induction`). Gathered by a Keeper on 2026-09-29 at the author's request. Everything here is a **copy**: the originals stay where they were, and this folder does not track later edits to them.

**Why copies are useful here:** the originals of the Soto PDFs live in `Logsidian/assets/`, which the forge's sparse checkout excludes, so forge sessions could not read them before. They can read this folder.

**Renaming:** files are renamed so that every link name is unique in the repo. Copies under their original names would collide with `Logsidian/assets/` and `web-chats/`. In the Logseq page copies, `../assets/...` embeds became `!`wikilinks``. Soto PDFs point to the copies in `soto-2023/`, and everything else points to the original asset by name. Logseq block references `((uuid))` do not resolve outside Logseq.

## The `//////////////` argument

It is in the author's email to Martín of **Sep 18, 2023, 8:02 PM**, in [soto-email-thread-2023](soto-email-thread-2023.md): search for the line of slashes. The email opens with "If your time is limited, skip past the \"//////////////\" mark". The argument after the mark begins "So in particular, let us suppose the 100% correlated picture": a counterfactual-mugging calculation that decomposes P's expectation over Ask/Rec branches Q, using an extension of LUV coherence to small conjunctions of claims about Q. Later emails call it the argument for the **single-instance success thesis (SIST)**, and the author's notes also call it the "///////" or "/////" argument. The Logseq paste ([soto-email-thread-excerpts-2023](soto-email-thread-excerpts-2023.md)) lost the slash line, but its block `65392abe-0974-4591-b379-7ae788d649c1` is the same text.
