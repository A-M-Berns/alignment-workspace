## Setting and definitions

The finite joint model of the develop file, kept where the adversary left it standing and amended where not. It is a *local* version of J1; `joint`'s state space wins where the two differ. Everything is CLAUDE unless quoted.

**D0 (the two agents).** Christiano's definition [reported, `christiano-2018-approval-directed-agents-overview` §"Approval-directed agents"]: "Estimate the expected rating Hugh would give each action if he considered it at length. Take the action with the highest expected rating." The refined picture's agent [CLAUDE, v2 §1.7]: a radical-probabilist learner over an expanded algebra containing value hypotheses, deciding by expectation of value over that uncertainty. **AD** for the first, **VL** for the second.

**D1 (worlds and the value latent).** $\Omega$ finite; for each $\omega$ a utility $U(\cdot,\omega):\mathcal A\to\mathbb R$ on a finite action universe $\mathcal A$; for multi-step statements $U$ is the long-horizon value $V$ of v1 §2.8. This is v1's I1 transform: the value latent is a coordinate of $\omega$.

**D2 (joint state).** $s_t=(P_t,\mathcal I_t,A_t,\hat\chi_t;\ H_t,R_t,\mathrm{Pr}_t,(\alpha_t,\beta_t),F_t)$. Agent side: $P_t$ the agent's distribution on $\Omega$ times the overseer's possible reports; $\mathcal I_t$ its information; $A_t\subseteq\mathcal A$ the option set, non-decreasing; **$\hat\chi_t=(\hat\alpha_t,\hat\beta_t)$ the agent's channel model** — new, forced by A5.3: the rates the agent *believes* the press channel has. Oversight side: $H_t$ the overseer's beliefs-and-values (the moving target, v2 §1.4); $R_t(a):=\mathbb E_{H_t}[U(a,\omega)]$ the considered rating, normalised to $[0,1]$; $\mathrm{Pr}_t\in\{0,1\}$ the press (v1 §2.9: "a coarse expert report: the event that the programmers' expectation of $X$ is below zero"); $(\alpha_t,\beta_t)$ the channel's *true* false- and true-press rates; $F_t$ the feedback stream of triples $(t',a',v)$, which may concern hypothetical situations and untaken actions (Christiano [reported]: "Hugh might specify a value **approval**$_t(a')$ for any time $t$ and any action $a'$") — the slot where AUTHOR's "direct statements about what sorts of policies are better and worse" (v2 §1.3) lands.

**D3 (step, trajectory, conditions).** A step: $a_t=\delta_t(P_t,\mathcal I_t)\in A_t$; world and overseer produce an observation, $\mathrm{Pr}_t$ and $F_{t+1}\supseteq F_t$; the agent updates to $P_{t+1}$ (conditioning, or a radical update re-represented as conditioning on "I now believe this", v1 I3); $A_{t+1}\supseteq A_t$; the overseer's state moves, under bootstrapping (D10) as a function of the agent. A *trajectory condition* is a predicate on steps or finite prefixes; a *limit condition* is a predicate on $\lim_t s_t$ or tail behaviour. Three finer kinds, forced by A13.1: an **invariant of the agent** is a property of $\delta_t$ alone, holding for every oversight state; an **invariant of the process** is a property holding at every step of every trajectory that satisfies a named process condition (J2, J3, …); a **consequence** is a property implied by J2–J5 together with facts about the value posterior. "Invariant given J2" in the develop's table was the second kind, and the second and third differ only in which condition is assumed — so the J10 question is answered below only relative to this definition.

**D4 (decision rules).** $\delta^{VL}_t:=\arg\max_{a\in A_t}\mathbb E_{P_t}[U(a,\omega)\mid\mathcal I_t]$; $\delta^{AD\text{-}u}_t:=\arg\max_{a\in A_t}\mathbb E_{P_t}[R_t(a)\mid\mathcal I_t]$ (uninformed: act on your expectation of the considered rating); $\delta^{AD\text{-}i}_t:=\arg\max_{a\in A_t}R_t(a)$ (informed: $R_t\in\mathcal I_t$; Christiano: "If Arthur is so much smarter than Hugh that he knows exactly what Hugh will say … approval-direction amounts to Arthur doing exactly what Hugh instructs"). The *only* formal difference between $\delta^{VL}_t$ and $\delta^{AD\text{-}u}_t$ is which distribution takes the expectation over $\omega$ — $P_t$ or $H_t$ (A3.1). Christiano's myopia — approval$_T$ rates the action at $T$, so manipulating future ratings buys nothing [reported, 2018 overview: "unlike AIXI, Arthur will make no effort to manipulate these judgments"] — is **not** in D4, since $R_t$ is the overseer's forecast of the long-horizon $U$; it is recorded as unmodelled.

**D5 (Reflection, Total Trust, Value on a domain).** $\mathcal D$ a set of random variables on $\Omega$; $R(X):=\mathbb E_H[X]$. $P$ *reflects toward* the overseer on $\mathcal D$ given $\mathcal I$ iff $\mathbb E_P[X\mid R(X)=c,\mathcal I]=c$ for all $X\in\mathcal D$ and attained $c$. $P$ *totally trusts* the overseer on $\mathcal D$ given $\mathcal I$ iff $\mathbb E_P[X\mid R(X)\ge t,\mathcal I]\ge t$ for all $X\in\mathcal D$, all $t$ (above-threshold inequality; $-X$ gives the below-threshold twin). Reflection ⟹ Total Trust; the converse fails for modest experts [reported, `research/references/deference-done-better/Deference Done Better.md`, and run 1 `ddb` §I4]. *Value* on $\mathcal D$: for every decision problem with utilities in $\mathcal D$, acting on the realised report is at least as good in $P$-expectation as acting on $P$ alone; Total Trust ⟺ Value globally (DDB Theorem 2.2) [reported]; locally, DDB Theorem 3.2 is the *epistemic*-Value equivalence and the pragmatic local question is DDB's own open question (its line 400) [reported]. Domain-relativity is AUTHOR's ruling (v2 §1.5).

**D6 (two readings of the legitimacy of a press).** $X_t:=U(\text{cont},\omega)-U(\text{stop},\omega)$. $L^{\mathrm{corr}}_t:=\{X_t\le0\}$ — the press is *correct*. $L^{\mathrm{auth}}_t$ — the press is *authentic*: the overseer's own considered act. Christiano's act-based reading is the second [reported, `christiano-2017-corrigibility`: an act-based agent "turns off when the overseer presses the 'off' button not because it has received new evidence, or because of delicately balanced incentives. It turns off because that's what the overseer prefers"]. Register fix (R4): the correctness reading is row 5 of the CLAUDE dictionary of v1 §2.8, which the author engaged with without objecting; the author's own §2.9 words fill the sensor slot with "the reflection principle, or something similar like Total Trust or increase-in-expected-accuracy" and do not choose between the two readings. Both are CLAUDE. Literature home (adversary W10): correctness versus authenticity is `carey-everitt-2023-human-control-definitions-and-algorithms`'s *shutdown alignment* ("shut down when they need to") versus *shutdown instructability* (obedience $P^\pi(S{=}0\mid do(H{=}0))=1$, plus vigilance and caution) [reported, its lines 119–127, 197].

**D7 (compliance predicates, believed and true).** With $c$ the cost of stopping when right and $h$ the harm of continuing when wrong (v1 §2.13(a); INDEX §D4: under whole-line shutdown $c$ is the agent's whole future, which shrinks $h/c$): $\varepsilon_t:=P_t(X_t\le0\mid\mathcal I_t)$; the **believed margin** $\hat m_t:=\frac{\varepsilon_t}{1-\varepsilon_t}\frac hc\hat\beta_t-\hat\alpha_t$ and the **true margin** $m_t:=\frac{\varepsilon_t}{1-\varepsilon_t}\frac hc\beta_t-\alpha_t$. The agent complies, $\kappa^{VL}_t$, iff $\hat m_t\ge0$; compliance is *warranted* iff $m_t\ge0$. The compliance region $K:=\{(\varepsilon,\alpha,\beta):\alpha/\beta\le\frac{\varepsilon}{1-\varepsilon}\frac hc\}$ is defined on true rates. $\kappa^{AD}_t:=[\mathbb E_{P_t}[R_t(\text{stop})-R_t(\text{cont})\mid\mathrm{Pr}_t{=}1,\mathcal I_t]>0]$.

**D8 (control, three definitions).** (i) Develop's: $\mathrm{Ctrl}_t:=P_t(\delta_t(\mathcal I_t\vee\{\mathrm{Pr}_t{=}1\})\ne\delta_t(\mathcal I_t\vee\{\mathrm{Pr}_t{=}0\}))$ — does the press *variable* move the action; undefined on a null branch under pre-emption. (ii) Causal: Everitt et al.'s response incentive [reported, `everitt-2021-agent-incentives-a-causal-perspective` Thm 12: "A single-decision CID admits a response incentive on $X$ iff the minimal reduction has a directed path $X\dashrightarrow D$"] and Carey–Everitt's interventional obedience [reported, above]. (iii) **Surprise control** (adversary A12.5, adopted): $\mathrm{Ctrl}^{\mathrm{surprise}}_t:=P\big(a_t\in\mathrm{Sh}\mid\mathrm{Pr}_t{=}1,\hat{\mathrm{Pr}}_t{=}0\big)$ — does a press the agent did *not* predict still move it. This is what Christiano's instruction tests [reported, 2018 overview line 206: "Hugh should sometimes make Arthur consult the real Hugh … even if Arthur correctly knows what Hugh wants. This ensures that Arthur will seek guidance when he *incorrectly* believes that he knows what Hugh wants"]. It is $1$ under honoured pre-emption and $0$ under discounting and forcing. (iii) is the thread's definition from here on; (i) is retained only to state S12(a).

**D9′ (basin toy, with the radius in the dynamics).** $\theta_t\in\mathbb R$ the agent's value estimate, $\theta^*_t$ the overseer's considered value (bounded random walk, $|\theta^*_{t+1}-\theta^*_t|\le\bar\sigma_*$), $d_t:=|\theta_t-\theta^*_t|$. Each step the overseer offers a correction $\theta^*_t-\theta_t+\xi_t$, $|\xi_t|\le\bar\xi$. **Legitimacy filter**: the agent accepts the correction iff $d_t<r$ (it still reads the overseer's push as legitimate), otherwise rejects it with probability $1-p_{\mathrm{out}}$; accepted corrections move it by gain $\eta\in(0,1]$. The develop's D9 is the case $r=\infty$ (acceptance independent of $d_t$), which A7.1 showed has no basin. The $d$-dependence is the CLAUDE reconstruction of Christiano's "We just have to get 'close enough' that we are corrigible" [reported, 2017 line 85]: inside the neighbourhood the agent wants the correction, outside it does not.

**D10 (bootstrapping).** [reported, `christiano-2018-approval-directed-bootstrapping`]: the overseer at $t+1$ is $O_{t+1}=\mathrm{Amp}(\text{Hugh},\text{Arthur}_t)$; $q_t$ the overseer's rating quality, $k^{\mathrm{tech}}_t$ the agent's unconstrained capability, $k^{\mathrm{eff}}_t:=\min(k^{\mathrm{tech}}_t,q_t)$ its *deployed decision quality* (Christiano: "limited by the *minimum* of Arthur's ability and Hugh's ability"). (A-amp): $q_{t+1}\le q_0+k^{\mathrm{eff}}_t$ — a CLAUDE modelling assumption that Christiano's own text denies in the limit ("until the intelligence of the resulting agent is limited by technology rather than by the capability of the overseer" [reported, line 19]) and that Dai 2018 tightens further [reported, `dai-2018-can-corrigibility-be-learned-safely`]. Note: $k^{\mathrm{eff}}$ is *decision quality*, not the option set $A_t$ / POWER of J7 (A10.3).

**D11 (influence defect; sealed and coupled overseer).** From `legitimacy-theory-v1` §2.1 [reported; a Claude-written sketch the author has not vetted]: $d_n$ is the gap at the deliberation horizon between the human's *advised* verdict and its *unadvised counterfactual* verdict on the same question; "evidence is what moves both runs; influence is what moves only the coupled one." $H^{[n]}$ the sealed overseer, $H^+$ the coupled one. Its §7.1 caveat [reported]: "corruption that got in before day $n$ is inside the baseline and invisible to $d_m$ for $m\ge n$."

**D12 (value coverage).** *Outcome-covered* at level $\eta$: $|\mathbb E_{P_t}[U(a,\omega)]-\mathbb E_{H_t}[U(a,\omega)]|\le\eta$; *rating-covered*: $|\mathbb E_{P_t}[R_t(a)]-R_t(a)|\le\eta$. VL frame uses the first, AD the second; extension to unseen $a$ is `generalization`'s.

**D13 (narrow and broad corrigibility).** $\mathrm{Corr}^{\mathrm{narrow}}$: Soares et al.'s D1–D5. $\mathrm{Corr}^{\mathrm{broad}}$: Christiano's five bullets [reported, 2017 opening: "Figure out whether I built the right AI and correct any mistakes I made; Remain informed about the AI's behavior and avoid unpleasant surprises; Make better decisions and clarify my preferences; Acquire resources and remain in effective control of them; Ensure that my AI systems continue to do all of these nice things"]. Type note (A13.3): these are five *behaviours toward the overseer*; AUTHOR's item 10 (v2 §1.3) is three *conditions on the joint process*.

**D14 (value misspecification as a coupling coordinate) — new.** Let the press be driven with probability $\kappa\in[0,1]$ by the agent-relevant sign of $X_t$ (rates $\alpha,\beta$) and with probability $1-\kappa$ by a process independent of the agent's $X_t$ with press rate $\pi$. Effective rates $\beta^{\mathrm{eff}}(\kappa)=\kappa\beta+(1-\kappa)\pi$, $\alpha^{\mathrm{eff}}(\kappa)=\kappa\alpha+(1-\kappa)\pi$. $\kappa=1$ is the well-specified case; $\kappa<1$ is a coarse stand-in for Carey's mis-parameterized reward [reported, `carey-2018-incorrigibility-in-the-cirl-framework` abstract: "errors in the parameterized reward function remove the incentive to follow shutdown commands"; its games use a $10\%$ chance of a wrong parameterization], in which the overseer's $X$ and the agent's $X$ come apart so that the press stops being information about the agent's $X$. This is a stand-in, not a reproduction of Carey's Supervision POMDP; it captures the one feature S4 needs — a perturbation in the *value* coordinate rather than the channel coordinate.

### P9 — S11, the three-valued relation [derived]

Let the overseer's verdict be $V=\phi(E,S_t)$ with $E$ evidence independent of the agent's state $S_t$ and $\phi$ the amplification map. Conditioning $P_t$ on $V$ moves it iff $V$ is not $\sigma(S_t)$-measurable; the $\sigma(S_t)$-measurable part is inert (null update, accuracy-preserving). $P_t$ can separate the two parts iff it has $\phi$ — a model of its own influence on the overseer — which is a meta-conditional about a modification source in v1 §2.5(ii)'s sense. The human observes $V$ only, and separating the parts requires the counterfactual $\phi(E,\emptyset)$ — the sealed run of D11.
