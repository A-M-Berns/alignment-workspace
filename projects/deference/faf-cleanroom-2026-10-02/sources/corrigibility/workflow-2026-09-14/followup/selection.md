## Summary

**Headline [derived; finite cores checked]: the parked selection worry is resolved by one criterion, *retention*, and the two run-1 verdicts that looked opposed are theorems about different hypotheses, not a conflict.** An agent's choice of its next estimator, experiment, stopping day, or action preserves reflection (and Armstrong's sequential unbiasedness) whenever the successor is a Bayesian refinement of the current state that retains the *world-information* the choice used; independent randomization need not be retained [checked, new]. Reflection breaks in exactly three ways: the successor forgets the grounds of its selection (optimizer's curse), the successor is not a refinement (a "twist", a different prior), or a world action changes the source kernel untracked by the successor's model. Run-1 `li`'s daily splice of two inductors is the second case and a corollary of LI's Convergence theorem, not a selection phenomenon; its $o(1)$-agreement conjecture is refuted by a signed $1/n$ gap [derived, new]. In the $L_{t_1,t_2,\varphi}$ vocabulary: retention-compliant choices are *neutral* (zero lift); forgetting, twisting and untracked kernel changes are *delegitimizing*; acts that make future pushes retention-compliant (recording grounds, sealing channels, accurate source models) are *legitimizing*. Armstrong's $\mathbb E$-channel is closed for the thesis agent: a twisted successor is weakly dominated for any world-indexed payoff and pays only for belief-indexed payoffs (compensation terms, side bets on the press) [checked]. Turner's published "key parameter" and a reconstructed critic's trammelling analogue are not the selection worry: the first is the evidential/constitutive fork plus channel substitution (INDEX D1, C3), the second has no belief-side counterpart for a complete-preference agent [derived]. Placement: the retention lemma belongs in **J5** (it makes the invariant survive the agent's own agency); the splice/oscillation half belongs in **v1 §2.4**, where coherence, not richness, excludes oscillation. The author's one decision: whether retention is *part of the definition* of a legitimate self-modification or only a sufficient condition (Question 1).

## Resolutions

### R1. The two positive verdicts are theorems about different hypotheses; the separating example is a change of prior

**R1.1 [checked; run-1 `armstrong` A1 re-run, strengthened by `selection_checks.py` C1] The selection theorem, strengthened.** Let $P=P_{t_1}$ on finite $\Omega$, candidates $\rho^k=P(\cdot\mid\mathcal F_k)$ for partitions $\mathcal F_k$, and a selection rule $s$ measurable with respect to a partition $\mathcal G$ of world-information. If every $\mathcal F_k$ refines $\mathcal G$ then, for every rule $s$, the selected estimator $\rho^s(\omega):=\rho^{s(\omega)}(\omega)$ is **itself a Bayesian refinement of $P$** — its cells $\{\mathcal F_k\text{-cell of }\omega:\ s(\omega)=k\}$ partition $\Omega$ because $\{s=k\}$ is $\mathcal F_k$-measurable — and therefore satisfies not only SU but general reflection $P(A\mid\rho^s=q)=q(A)$ in both value and function form. All 9 admissible rules of the six-world model pass all three tests; the forgetting rule (world 4 routed to the coarse $\mathcal F_3$) fails all three. Run 1 proved SU only; the refinement statement is what J5 needs, since composition across times holds only at function-form grade (run 1 `radical` I5.6c, re-read not re-derived).

**R1.2 [checked, new; `selection_checks.py` C1b] Randomization need not be retained.** Let the selector also use a coin $\kappa$ independent of the world coordinates and *not* observed by any candidate. All $81$ coin-dependent rules preserve SU and value-form reflection on $\{X>0\}$. Proof: each cell $\{\rho^s(\varphi)=c\}$ is a union over coin values of $\mathcal F_k$-measurable pieces on each of which $P(\varphi\mid\cdot)=c$. So the right hypothesis is "the successor retains the *world-information* the selection used", not "retains the selector's information" (run 1 `armstrong` A1's phrasing); mixed strategies are free.

**R1.3 [checked; run-1 `armstrong` T4 and `radical` Sel.1 re-run; counts reconciled in C6] Adapted optional stopping preserves reflection.** The two run-1 counts agree: `armstrong`'s 64 encodings collapse to 25 distinct rules with at least one observation, plus the stop-at-root rule gives `radical`'s 26. Every adapted rule keeps $\mathbb E[P_\tau(\varphi)]=P_0(\varphi)$ and $P(\varphi\mid P_\tau(\varphi)=c)=c$; the non-adapted path-maximum rule gives $\tfrac{353}{540}$. This is the bounded optional-stopping theorem and is a special case of R1.1 (a stopping time is a selection among the refinements $P(\cdot\mid\mathcal F_m)$ whose selection event $\{\tau=m\}$ is $\mathcal F_m$-measurable).

**R1.4 [derived; the trader re-derived, existence reported] The LI splice is the Convergence theorem, not a selection result.** If $\mathbb P^1,\mathbb P^2$ are inductors over the same $D$ with $\lim\mathbb P^1_n(\varphi)\ne\lim\mathbb P^2_n(\varphi)$, then *any* process that takes value $\mathbb P^1_n(\varphi)$ infinitely often and $\mathbb P^2_n(\varphi)$ infinitely often has a non-convergent price on the fixed sentence $\varphi$, and the buy-low/sell-high trader of the paper's own proof sketch (main.tex:1004–1010: "wait until $\mathbb P_n(\varphi)<p-\varepsilon$ … buy … sell back … profit $2\varepsilon$ every time") exploits it holding at most one share. `selection_checks.py` C5: cash grows $\approx0.2$ per pair with plausible value bounded below by $-0.371$. The exploit needs no knowledge of the schedule — it trades on the price — so it covers an agent that chooses the alternation adaptively. Consequently `cee` fails for the splice on one parity subsequence (whichever parity $f$ sends to the other inductor: the day-$n$ inductor comes to believe the eventually-decided future quote by Provability Induction while its own price sits near the other limit). Existence of two inductors with different limits: [reported] deference-v6 §5.6 T7 via run 1 `li`; [derived, non-degenerate case] for a language with a fresh atom $q$ not in $\Gamma$, the substitution $q\mapsto\neg q$ is an automorphism fixing a suitably chosen $D$, so $\mathbb P^\sigma_n(\phi):=\mathbb P_n(\sigma\phi)$ is an inductor with $\mathbb P^\sigma_\infty(q)=1-\mathbb P_\infty(q)$ — different limits unless $\mathbb P_\infty$ is exactly $\sigma$-symmetric.

**R1.5 [derived, new] Run 1 `li`'s $o(1)$ conjecture is refuted as stated.** Take $\mathbb P^1_n(\varphi)=L-\tfrac1n$, $\mathbb P^2_n(\varphi)=L+\tfrac1n$ (each individually harmless: profits from a monotone sequence with summable increments are bounded). The alternating splice converges to $L$, yet the constant trader "buy one share on even days, sell on odd days" banks $\approx\tfrac{2}{n}$ per pair with at most one share held — divergent gain, bounded loss. So agreement to $o(1)$ is not sufficient; a *summable* disagreement along every e.c. sequence is the plausible sufficient condition [conjectured, $\sim0.6$], and the exact condition is the LI criterion itself (run 1 `li` I5.7's "criterion-preservation").

**R1.6 [checked; `selection_checks.py` C2] The separating example.** In the finite dictionary, alternate two priors $P\ne Q$: $\mathbb E_P\mathbb E_QX=\mathbb E_QX\ne\mathbb E_PX$ ($\tfrac74$ against $-\tfrac1{12}$), and $Q$ is not a refinement of $P$. Two inductors with different limits on a $\Gamma$-independent sentence are non-refinements of each other in exactly this sense (no decidable conditioning takes one to the other). So `li`'s counterexample lies in `armstrong`'s *excluded* class — non-Bayesian candidates — and `armstrong`'s positive theorem has an LI shadow in the *same-inductor* case: the self-tower `cee`/`ccee`, and the adapted-stopping form below.

**R1.7 [derived; hinges on main.tex:786–787] LI adapted stopping.** Expressible features are built from price features $\phi^{*n}$ "for each $n\in\mathbb N^+$" (main.tex:787), so a weight generable from the market may depend on prices of earlier days. For finitely many candidate deferral days $m_1<\dots<m_k$ (each an e.c. function of $n$) and a generable partition of unity $w^{(j)}$ with $w^{(j)}$ depending only on prices up to day $m_j$, `loe` and `ccee` applied $k$ times give
$$\mathbb E_n(X_n)\;=\;\sum_j\mathbb E_n\big(X_n\cdot w^{(j)}\big)\;\approx_n\;\sum_j\mathbb E_n\big(\ulcorner\mathbb E_{m_j}(X_n)\urcorner\cdot w^{(j)}\big)\;=\;\mathbb E_n\big(\ulcorner\mathbb E_{\tau}(X_n)\urcorner\big),$$
the soft stopping time $\tau$ being the day the ramp partition selects. This is the LI form of R1.3, at the usual ramp-and-band grade; the sharp-indicator version is barred for the same reason every exact statement is (`reflection-in-li` §"What is genuinely unreachable").

**R1.8 [derived] Cleanest true statements, one per setting.** *Finite Bayesian:* an adapted choice among refinements of one's own state that retain the choice's world-information is a refinement; reflection and SU are preserved; every other choice can break them. *LI:* within one inductor, deferring to a generable stopping day preserves the self-tower (R1.7); becoming a different inductor breaks it iff the two are exploitably distinguishable along the switching schedule (R1.4–R1.5), the limit-disagreement case being Convergence. The two settings agree on the moral: **switching estimators is safe iff the switch is a refinement of what you already are.**

### R2. When the agent's own choice is legitimizing, delegitimizing, or neutral — in the $L_{t_1,t_2,\varphi}$ vocabulary

Notation from run 1 `radical` I5.1/I6.1 (re-read, not re-derived): $\mathcal L_{t_1,t_2,\varphi}$ is the class of events $L$ with $P_{t_1}(\varphi\mid P_{t_2}(\varphi)=c,\,L)=c$ for every $c$ of positive mass; a third-person event $E$ is *strongly legitimizing* if $E\in\mathcal L$, and its *lift* relative to a designated $L$ is $\lambda_L(E):=P_{t_1}(L\mid E)-P_{t_1}(L)$. Let the agent's choice at $t_1$ be $s$, measurable in its $t_1$-information plus an independent coin, and let $E_k:=\{s=k\}$.

**R2.1 [derived; finite core checked in C1–C1b] The retention criterion.** Say the transition $t_1\to t_2$ *retains* $s$ if $P_{t_2}=P_{t_1}(\cdot\mid\mathcal I_{t_2})$ for the successor's information $\mathcal I_{t_2}$ and the world-information $s$ used lies in $\mathcal I_{t_2}$. Under retention every $E_k$ is strongly legitimizing for every $(t_2,\varphi)$:
$$P_{t_1}\big(\varphi\;\big|\;P_{t_2}(\varphi)=c,\ \{s=k\}\big)=c\quad\text{for all }k,c,$$
hence, with $L:=\bigcup_kE_k\cap\{\text{retention holds}\}$, $\lambda_L(E_k)=0$: the choice is **neutral** — it cannot raise or lower the probability of legitimacy because legitimacy holds on every branch. Choices of experiment, of stopping day, of admissible estimator, and world actions whose effect on the source kernel the successor's model tracks are all of this kind. Their ranking is by value of information and world payoff (Good, Jensen), never by legitimacy.

**R2.2 [derived; finite cores checked] Delegitimizing choices are the three retention failures.** (i) *Forgetting*: $\mathcal I_{t_2}$ lacks world-information $s$ used — face-value adoption of a selected estimate (run 1 `radical` Sel.2: $P(\varphi\mid\rho_\sigma=\tfrac23)=\tfrac8{13}$), the fully informed selector (run 1 `armstrong` T3(ii)); then $E_k\notin\mathcal L$. Conditioning on the selection restores $\mathcal L$-membership by construction (Sel.2, re-run). (ii) *Twisting*: $P_{t_2}$ is $\mathcal I_{t_2}$-measurable but not the conditional — Armstrong's "twist it a bit", noisy-unbiased candidates ($\tfrac{11}{20}$), a different prior (R1.6), a different inductor (R1.4). (iii) *Untracked kernel change*: a world action alters $P(\mathrm{Pr}\mid a_1,\omega)$ but the successor keeps the old kernel. `selection_checks.py` C4 with $\alpha=\tfrac1{10},\beta=\tfrac35,\varepsilon=\tfrac1{10},c=1,h=4$ and proportional garbling $d=\tfrac34$: the source-blind agent holds $P(W\mid\text{silence})=\tfrac4{85}$ against the true $\tfrac{34}{385}$, while the press cell is unchanged ($\tfrac25$ both), confirming run 1 `radical` I9.2 that proportional deception leaves the press's likelihood ratio alone and moves only the silence cell. A *source-aware* deceiver is not delegitimizing at all — its update on the garbled channel is still a refinement — it is merely information-destroying: value of information falls monotonically $\tfrac3{20},\tfrac9{80},\tfrac3{40},\tfrac3{80},0$ as $d$ runs $0,\tfrac14,\tfrac12,\tfrac34,1$ (Blackwell), so it is never chosen without a side payoff. **Deception is delegitimizing exactly when it is source-blind; source-aware deception is dominated.** The $a_1$-dependence of trust (v1 §2.9) lives in the kernel, and legitimacy stays predicated on worlds, as the author wanted (v1 §2.10).

**R2.3 [derived] Legitimizing choices raise the probability of future retention.** With $P_{t_1}(L)<1$ because the environment may push illegitimately, $\lambda_L(E)>0$ for acts that make anticipated pushes retention-compliant: recording the grounds of one's selections (memory/introspection — run 1 `armstrong` A2 and Open problem 2), keeping the source model accurate, and sealing governance channels so presses stay blind (run 1 `li` I6/I9: a press engineered from the agent's own quote is diagonal territory where Total Trust can be refuted; blindness is what must scale with capability). Repairing the button is *neutral* for legitimacy and positive for value of information — a broken channel is a trivially legitimate one. This corrects v2 §1.2's phrase "sharpening the sensor is accuracy-increasing and so is legitimizing": on the reflection reading, sharpening is neutral (both channels are refinements, zero lift); the brain-reader's incentive is value of information, and legitimacy is not a lever against it — which makes v2 §1.2's conclusion (only the value posterior restrains channel improvement) sharper, not weaker.

**R2.4 [checked; `selection_checks.py` C3] Armstrong's $\mathbb E$-channel is closed for the thesis agent, and the closure has a precise boundary.** In the six-world model with information $\mathcal F_1$ and a three-action world payoff $u_a(\omega)$, no $\mathcal F_1$-measurable twisted successor beats the refinement: of 400 random twists, 127 are strictly worse and 0 strictly better than the refinement's $\tfrac{29}{12}$ (the rest tie because the argmax coincides). Proof shape: the refinement's action is the pointwise maximizer of $\mathbb E_{P_{t_1}}[u_a\mid\mathcal F_1]$ on every cell. With Armstrong's compensation payoff $\mathbb E_\rho(Y)-\mathbb E_\rho(Z)$ — a payoff indexed by the *successor's beliefs* — the best twist found scores $\tfrac{65}{24}$ against the refinement's $\tfrac5{12}$: the twist pays. So the $\mathbb E$-channel incentive is an artefact of belief-indexed payoff (compensation terms; equally, side bets whose settlement is a report), and the reconstructed E-channel critique §2.3's "self-modification that lowers its own $\varepsilon$-estimate … with an incentive attached" has no incentive in the thesis: $P_t$ evaluates the successor's press-discounting by $P_t$'s own conditionals, and a successor that discounts presses $P_t$ believes informative is simply suboptimal by $P_t$'s lights. What is *not* closed: (a) the FUD arithmetic itself (a $P_t$ that already discounts presses does so without any twist — v1 §2.13(b), unchanged); (b) any payoff attached to the channel *event* — a hard button that forces shutdown at cost $c$ regardless of the posterior, bets with third parties on the press (reconstructed trammelling critique, Handoffs, its own flagged leak) — which reopens manipulation as a world-payoff question, not a legitimacy one (run 1 `radical` I11.2's forced-channel result, re-read); (c) a twisted successor that also has *more* information than the refinement on offer, which $P_t$ may prefer on value while it fails reflection — the one place where "accuracy-increasing" and "reflective" come apart (run 1 `ddb`'s point in INDEX §C4), and the substance of Question 1.

### R3. Turner's published key parameter and a reconstructed critic's trammelling analogue are not the selection worry

**R3.1 [derived; quotation checked] The key parameter is the soft/hard fork plus channel substitution.** Turner's sentence, verbatim (`references/06-coherence-and-shutdown/turner-2021-a-certain-formalization-of-corrigibility-is-vnm-incoherent.md`, l. 78–80): "The degree of dependence is a key parameter: As (variety in $\pi_\text{correct}$ depending on world state) increases, the (convergently instrumental incentive to avoid any kind of correction) decreases. … In the limit of variety, an agent can 'manipulate' us by getting us to correct it in *just the right way* so as to optimize its original objective." This is a statement about *forced policy corrections* under a fixed $U$: variety in the control channel lets a fixed-goal agent steer the control. Under values-as-beliefs the correction is evidence, and steering which correction one receives as a function of one's own knowledge yields a press with zero marginal information (run 1 `radical` I11.1c, re-read) — a garbling, dominated for a source-aware agent (R2.2). So the parameter is live exactly when corrections are *constitutive* (forced) rather than *evidential* — INDEX §D1's third bullet and the reconstructed E-channel critique's fork (§2.1 claim 5) — and, when the alternative to being corrected is finding out oneself, it is the reconstructed key-parameter critique C3's $\delta\le\kappa$, i.e. channel substitution (INDEX §C3, v2 §1.2). The reconstructed key-parameter critique C5's mechanism — "breaking the channel converts a variable $\mathbb E[X\mid o;a_1]$ into the constant $\mathbb E[X;a_1]$ that the agent already prefers" — is false for a source-aware agent: by Jensen the constant is worth *less* (value of information $\ge0$; C4 numbers). It is true for a source-blind one, which is R2.2(iii). Its own test T2 remains a good computation, but it tests the soft/hard fork, not selection. ATTRIBUTION-UNVETTED that Turner would accept this relocation.

**R3.2 [derived] Trammelling has no belief-side counterpart here.** The reconstructed trammelling critique §2.8 (marked [conjectured] by its author): reflection holds for each $a_1$ unchosen, the agent argmaxes, "the martingale property of the *chosen* path is not implied". The argmax is a deterministic function of $P_{t_1}$, hence a constant selection among refinements; by R1.1 the chosen path is a refinement and its martingale property *is* implied. Thornley's trammelling needs incomplete preferences that a policy can complete by shifting mass between incomparable outcomes; the thesis agent has complete preferences (the reconstructed critique §3 says so itself), so there is no gap to fill. The residue the reconstruction flagged for its own proposal — bets with external agents on the press — is R2.4(b), a payoff attached to the channel event, and applies to the thesis equally; it is a world-payoff leak, not an optional-stopping one. The local texts do not draw the parallel (the reconstruction's own note).

**R3.3 [derived] What the three reconstructed critics were right about.** Each names a real gap and mislabels it "selection": the E-channel critique — twisted successors are steerable when reflection is only SU without introspection (run 1 `armstrong` A2; retention *is* the introspection requirement); the key-parameter critique — source-blindness and forced corrections; the trammelling critique — channel-event payoffs. The mislabel matters because all three gaps are closed or relocated by R2, while "the agent's choice breaks reflection" would have been a structural objection to J5 had it stood.

### R4. Where the worry belongs: J5 for the lemma, v1 §2.4 for the oscillation half, nowhere for the rest

**R4.1 [derived] J5.** J5 asks for "legitimacy-conditioned reflection maintained along the trajectory — the $L_{t_1,t_2,\varphi}$ family as a time-indexed invariant". An invariant must be closed under the agent's own moves; R1.1 is that closure. Along a trajectory whose self-chosen transitions all retain, each step is a refinement, a chain of refinements is a refinement, and reflection from $t_1$ to $t_k$ holds outright in function form; where the environment's pushes intervene, function-form reflection conditional on the stepwise $L_{i,i+1}$ composes to $\bigcap_iL_{i,i+1}\in\mathcal L_{t_1,t_k,\varphi}$ (run 1 `radical` I5.6c, re-read; the function-form hypothesis is exactly what retention supplies at the agent's own steps). So the retention lemma is J5's lemma: **the invariant survives agency, and fails only at forgetting, twisting and untracked kernel changes** — three named, checkable events, which J6 can carry as "the trajectory property": no self-chosen transition of the three kinds.

**R4.2 [derived] v1 §2.4.** The author's representational problem asks for richness assumptions "to rule out cases such as the oscillation between opiates and stimulants". The oscillation case is a splice: a value posterior alternating between two hypotheses is not any coherent learner's posterior sequence. In the finite Bayesian setting a posterior on a fixed event is a bounded martingale and converges (Doob); in LI, Convergence (main.tex:1004) excludes non-convergent prices on any fixed sentence for every inductor. Neither needs richness. What richness governs is the *other* failure, convergence to a wrong hypothesis (run 1 `armstrong` A11, re-read). So §2.4 should split its sentence: coherence excludes oscillation; richness is for the limit. The impoverished-space agent of Soares et al. §1 is then excluded by the thesis's own legitimacy requirement (its pushes are not reflective), not by an extra assumption.

**R4.3 [derived] Nowhere / relocated.** The $\mathbb E$-channel: closed for world-indexed payoff (R2.4), reopened only by channel-event payoffs. Trammelling analogue: no belief-side counterpart (R3.2). Key parameter: to the evidential/constitutive fork (INDEX §D1, third bullet) and channel substitution (§C3), where the followups owning those clusters should record R3.1. Channel-event payoffs (hard button, side bets): to J10, as the place where D2 (not disabling the channel) stops being an invariant and becomes a world-payoff question.

**R4.4 [derived] What is the author's to decide.** Both placements are theorem-shaped and need no ruling. The one open decision is whether retention is *definitional* for legitimate self-modification (Question 1); it changes what J5's invariant *is* — the class $\mathcal L$ restricted to retention-compliant transitions, or the larger accuracy-defined class in which the retention lemma is only sufficient.

### R5. Sequential unbiasedness, introspection and retention are one requirement

**R5.1 [reported→derived] Retention is introspection, selection-indexed.** Run 1 `armstrong` Theorem A (I3.1, re-run via `reflection.py`): SU plus introspection $\iff$ general reflection; SU alone admits "twisted" estimators that fail reflection and can be steered by selection (its Open problem 2, conjectured there). Retention (R2.1) is the requirement that the successor know the grounds of its own selection — the selection-indexed form of "the future estimator knows its own state". So three vocabularies name one condition: Armstrong's introspection, Eisenstat's observation event "you believe this now" with its grounds (v1 §1, AUTHOR), and retention. This answers run 1 `armstrong`'s Handoff (ii) — "whether the thesis wants introspection as part of legitimacy" — in the affirmative *if* Question 1 is answered (A), and it is why v1 §2.5's precedent should read "sequential unbiasedness *plus introspection*" (INDEX §D6, positive `ddb`'s re-register request, and run 1 `armstrong` A2).

**R5.2 [derived] What double indifference is still for.** Armstrong's main result assumes SU along the realized estimator sequence and does not say how a choosing agent comes to satisfy it (his text: "modulo some convergence issues not addressed here"; the "did not show how" gloss is the reconstructed E-channel critique §2.3's gloss, ATTRIBUTION-UNVETTED). R1.1 supplies it: an agent whose estimator changes are adapted refinement-choices satisfies SU along the realized sequence by construction, and the $D$-terms vanish (run 1 `armstrong` I5.5, re-read). Double indifference is then a fallback for non-retention transitions — the role run 1 `armstrong` A5 already assigns it — and the thesis needs no posterior-side $D$-term (its Open problem 6) so long as retention holds at the agent's own steps.
