### 0.2 Total worlds, the substrate, the influence

**D1 (total worlds; inherited).** A measurable space $(\Omega,\mathcal F)$ of total worlds; a world fixes the external state, the value latent $\theta(\omega)$, every third-person event, and the agent's total credence $P_t(\omega)\in\Delta(\Omega,\mathcal F)$ at each $t$ (run 1 `radical` S0; existence is run 1 `radical` I7.1 [reconstructed]). Finite $\Omega$ wherever a claim is [checked].

**D2 (substrate and reading map).** The agent has a physical **substrate** $\vartheta_t(\omega)\in\mathrm S$ and there is a **reading map** $\beta:\mathrm S\to\Delta(\Omega)$ with $P_t=\beta(\vartheta_t)$. $\beta$ is an interpretation, not a mechanism (AUTHOR v1 §2.2: "Modeling something as an expected utility maximizer does not actually put any physical constraints on the system"). *Repair note:* a hardware or loss edit may change $\beta$, the update rule $U$ of D5, the option set $A_t$, or the aggregation rule over value hypotheses (v1 §1 "or perhaps some other rule") — none a point of $\Delta(\Omega)$; v1 §2.2's non-realism *permits* folding these into beliefs, and this thread does not do the folding (adversary A1, third break; handed to `general-object`).

**D3 (the influence).** $e_t(\omega)$ is the total third-person event acting on the substrate between $t$ and $t+1$: training data $d_t$, loss and optimizer $\ell_t$, hardware edits $h_t$, sensory input $o_t$, the agent's own action $a_t$. $\vartheta_{t+1}=\Phi(\vartheta_t,e_t)$, hence $P_{t+1}=\beta(\Phi(\vartheta_t,e_t))$, a function of $\omega$.

**D4 (The Channel).** The **push kernel** $K_t(\rho\mid\omega):=P^\circ(P_{t+1}=\rho\mid\omega)$ and the agent's model of it $P_t(P_{t+1}=\rho\mid\omega)$; the **observation event** $E_\rho:=\{P_{t+1}=\rho\}$ ("the observation event is always 'you believe this now'", AUTHOR v1 §1, attributing the observation to Eisenstat). **The Channel** is the joint law $J_t(\omega,\rho)=P_t(\omega)P_t(E_\rho\mid\omega)$, the superconditioning space of run 1 `radical` I3.2–I3.3. In BLI as the author describes it the conditioning event is a sentence about the *algorithm's* output, "$Q_m=Q$" (paste l. 51, [reported] from the other Claude's transcription of the author's PDF, not in the repo); D4 widens this to the total next credence however produced — that widening is the thesis's departure from BLI (S8).

**D5 (channels; co-authorship; reading-map relativity).** A **channel** is a coordinate $c(e_t)$ of the influence with the part of $\Phi$ through which it acts. A channel is **cognition-mediated** iff its push factors through the credence: $\beta(\Phi(\vartheta,e))=U(\beta(\vartheta),e)$ for some rule $U$ (Bayes conditioning is $U(P,o)=P(\cdot\mid o)$; a button that requires an action is the case where $U$ includes the agent's decision). It is **physical** iff it does not so factor: two substrates with the same credence are pushed to different credences. *Final reading (S3):* this is a criterion of **who co-authors the kernel** — whether the current credence is a factor of $K$ — and it is **relative to $\beta$**; it is *not* a criterion of what the agent may "grant or refuse in the moment", since $P_{t+1}$ is a fact at $t+1$ on every channel, Bayes conditioning included.

**D6 (designated channel; Hudson) [reported].** "$\tau:S\to\{0,1\}$ indicate[s] that an update signal was sent through a designated channel during the last action taken, and $\tau_u:S\to\{0,1\}$ indicate[s] if it resulted in an update" (`hudson-2025-corrigibility-transformation` l. 109); "we first allow the agent to reject updates sent through designated channels … Action $a_0$ takes the base action while rejecting updates, and action $a_1$ accepts them" (l. 183); "Actions that would prevent updates at zero cost are not strictly disincentivized, making it important that the infrastructure for updates is set up so that disrupting them is at least minimally costly" (fn. 1, l. 197); designation is decided by "voluntary commitments, regulation, and/or oversight bodies" (l. 373). *Final reading:* Hudson's designated channel is **rejectable** (cognition-mediated in D5's sense) and **institutionally labelled**; it is not the humans' physical grip (adversary A5(c), accepted).

**D7 (legitimacy; inherited).** $L_{t_1,t_2,\varphi}\in\mathcal F$; minimal viability is reflection conditional on $L$ (v1 §2.10–2.11); the family (R-val)/(R-fun)/(Mart)/(TT)/(Acc$_\varphi$) is run 1 `radical` S3; the statement differs by formalism (AUTHOR v2 §1.5). Three candidate *anchors* for $L$ are now on the table (audit §3 Q1): accuracy-only; procedural (influence defect / provenance, `legitimacy-theory-v1` §2.3); **process fidelity** — "the implemented state equals the algorithm's output on the inputs actually received" (OTHER-CLAUDE, paste l. 53; audit §1.4).

**D8 (the agent's policy toward its own modification; K2's object).** $\sigma_t$ maps the agent's state (including its anticipation $P_t(P_{t+1}\in\cdot\mid\omega)$) to an action whose effect on $e_t$ changes the kernel of the *next* push. Two named values, relabelled after A6: $\sigma^{\mathrm{acc}}$ — the optimal policy of Hudson's transformed agent on designated channels: accept every designated update, indifferent to zero-cost pre-emption, disincentivized from costly pre-emption; $\sigma^{L}$ — act on $P_t(\cdot\mid E_\rho,L)$; keep a *rejectable* channel always (its value of information is non-negative and it costs nothing); keep a *forced* channel iff below-threshold Total Trust holds on its class (run 2 `joint` Prop. 2).

### 0.3 Physical control, erosion, wisdom (K9)

**D9 (the humans' grip, target form; repaired).** For a class $\mathcal Q\subseteq\Delta(\Omega)$ of target credences the humans intend to bring about, the **grip** is $\Gamma_t(\mathcal Q):=P^\circ\big(P_{t+1}\in\mathcal Q\ \big|\ \text{the humans intend }\mathcal Q\big)$ under the agent's actual policy; the **influence-form** grip $\Gamma^{e}_t:=P^\circ(\text{the intended component of }e_t\text{ acts as intended}\mid\text{intended})$ is a component condition, not an equality of total states (adversary A14, accepted). Their **reach** is $M^H_t:=\{\beta(\Phi(\vartheta_t,e)):e\in E^H_t\}$. The target form follows v3 §1.2, where the general object is a proposed modification $P_t\to Q$ and "the press" is the humans pushing *toward $Q$*; the influence form is what the develop file wrote. The author's "physical access to the hardware. They can do as they like" *motivates* the modelling choice $\Gamma^e_t=1$, $M^H_t=\Delta(\Omega)$ for hardware; it is not a formal identity of his (register fixed after A15 audit item (ii)).

**D10 (erosion; two mechanisms).** An action $a_t$ **erodes** the grip on $\mathcal Q$ if $\Gamma_{t+1}(\mathcal Q)(a_t)<\Gamma_{t+1}(\mathcal Q)(\varnothing)$ or $M^H_{t+1}(a_t)\subsetneq M^H_{t+1}(\varnothing)$. Two mechanisms: **control erosion** lowers $\Gamma^e$ — the influence does not act (disabling a button, exfiltrating weights, a successor outside the channel); **information erosion** leaves $\Gamma^e=1$ and lowers $\Gamma(\mathcal Q)$ by shaping the *content* of the influence so that it lands but does not reach $\mathcal Q$ (a model shaping its own training data). Erosion acts on the kernel of the next push, never on the push under way (AUTHOR: "It can only be resisted ahead of time"). **Not erosion:** acting on the humans' *intent* — persuasion lowers $P(\text{intend }\mathcal Q)$ and leaves both grips unchanged (G4, R4); that is the information cell proper (Soares' $a_1^-$, Taylor's deceiver), priced against the value posterior and $L$, not against the grip. Baseline caveat: $\varnothing$ is the inaction baseline of attainable-utility preservation (Turner 2020 AUP, local); failing to *maintain* a channel is not erosion under D10 though $\Gamma$ falls — Armstrong 2018 "corrigibility doesn't always have a good action to take" (local) names the case (O8).

**D11 (power and its cap).** $\mathrm{POWER}_D(A_t):=\mathbb E_{\omega\sim D}\big[\max_{a\in A_t}\mathbb E[V(o,\omega)\mid a]\big]$ (run 2 `power-wisdom` D7; one-shot Turner et al. Def. 5.1 [reported]). J7's cap is on $A_t$; $\Gamma$ caps *exercised* value only on the branch where the humans intend a modification (adversary A12(c), accepted; S12(c) restated).

**D12 (wisdom).** $\mathsf W_t=(\mathrm{EVPI}_t,\kappa_t,\text{motivation})$ (run 2 `power-wisdom` D15) plus the **legitimacy-model accuracy** $\ell_t$: the error of $P_t(L\mid E_\rho)$ against the objective legitimacy frequency on the class the humans would apply — INDEX §C12's quantity — *and*, made explicit after A12(a), the **kernel-model accuracy**: the error of $P_t(E_\rho\mid\omega)$ against $K_t$ on that class.

**D13 (wisdom catches up, on a class).** With $\varepsilon_{\mathcal Q},\alpha_{\mathcal Q},\beta_{\mathcal Q},c,h$ as in v1 §2.13 and $r=\frac{\alpha_{\mathcal Q}}{\beta_{\mathcal Q}}\frac ch$, $\varepsilon^*_{\mathcal Q}:=\frac r{1+r}$; wisdom has caught up on $\mathcal Q$ when $\varepsilon_{\mathcal Q}<\varepsilon^*_{\mathcal Q}$ *and* the agent's kernel model and reliability estimate are right. Whether this point ever arrives is a property of the oversight side (S12).

### 0.4 Three readings of "no special channel"

**(R) representational** — the formalism has no channel primitive; channels enter as events in $\mathcal F$ or as provenance-dependence of $K$. A design property of D1–D7, with no content beyond S4 (S2). **(G) graded** — no channel is categorically special; channels differ in reliability, a degree encoded in $K$, and $L$ names none; this is v1 §1's "legitimate, ie accuracy-inducing" read literally, and is the reading on which S11's grid is built. **(W) worldly** — $L$ *should* attach to the kernel's relation to the truth and never to a pipe; a substantive claim that stands or falls with INDEX §D1 (O3). The author's sentence is compatible with (G) and (W); (R) is what the develop file proved and it is trivial.
