## Setting and definitions

**D1 (the object).** The three-step dictionary of v1 §2.8; the general object of v3 §1.2 (a proposed modification $Q\ll P_t$) enters only where marked. $X:=V(a_1,o,a_2^{\mathrm{cont}},\omega)-V(a_1,o,a_2^{\mathrm{sh}},\omega)$, the value of the best continuation given the press over stopping. The agent's information at its decision is the sigma-algebra $\mathcal F_A$; the humans' is $\mathcal F_H$; a common prior $\mu$ is assumed where a statement needs one ($P=\mu(\cdot\mid\mathcal F_A)$). The capability trajectory is a filtration $\mathcal F_{A,t}\uparrow\mathcal F_{A,\infty}$.

**D2 (the reading and the target — kept apart; adversary A1/R1 accepted).** Two things the develop file put under one heading are separated.
- *The reading* (AUTHOR, v3 §1.1: "the central case in my mind is that the shutdown command is evidence that shutting down is good"): the press is an event $\mathrm{Pr}$ with $\mu(\mathrm{Pr}\mid X\le0,\mathcal F_A)\ne\mu(\mathrm{Pr}\mid X>0,\mathcal F_A)$ — a likelihood ratio different from one — produced from $\mathcal F_H$ up to independent noise, with no restriction on which part of $\mathcal F_H$ drives it ("source unspecified"). The canonical instance is the thresholded report $\mathrm{Pr}=\{\mathbb E_\mu[X\mid\mathcal F_H]\le0\}$ (`hadfield-menell-2017-the-off-switch-game` Eq. 2, a rational $H$).
- *The target* (the legitimacy criterion the author named for the sensor slot, v1 §2.9 AUTHOR: "a slot to be filled by the reflection principle, or something similar like Total Trust or increase-in-expected-accuracy"; CLAUDE's application of it to the humans as an expert on $X$): Total Trust toward the humans on $X$, the below-threshold inequality
$$\mathbb E_\mu\big[V(a_1,\mathrm{Pr},a_2^{\mathrm{cont}},\omega)-V(a_1,\mathrm{Pr},a_2^{\mathrm{sh}},\omega)\ \big|\ \mathcal F_A,\mathrm{Pr}\big]\ \le\ 0,$$
with the above-threshold twin for silence. This inequality *is* desideratum 1 (shutdown posterior-optimal on the press); it is not a formalization of the reading. The v3 §1.1 CLAUDE sentence "exactly the Total Trust filler's reading" conflated the two and is corrected here. Everything below studies *on which cells and along which trajectories the reading meets the target*.

**D3 (the base-rate form; identity).** With $W:=\{X<0\}$, $\varepsilon:=\mu(W\mid\mathcal F_A)$, $\alpha:=\mu(\mathrm{Pr}\mid W^c,\mathcal F_A)$, $\beta:=\mu(\mathrm{Pr}\mid W,\mathcal F_A)$, $c:=\mathbb E_\mu[X\mid W^c,\mathcal F_A,\mathrm{Pr}]$, $h:=-\mathbb E_\mu[X\mid W,\mathcal F_A,\mathrm{Pr}]$: the target holds at $\mathcal F_A$ iff $(1-\varepsilon)\alpha c\le\varepsilon\beta h$, i.e. $\alpha/\beta\le\frac{\varepsilon}{1-\varepsilon}\frac hc$. This is the law of total expectation written three ways (adversary A12, accepted): the random-model checks of the develop file and of run 2 `fud` R1.1 confirm an identity, and are cited below only as "the identity". Its content is the *naming* of the terms — the false-press mass $(1-\varepsilon)\alpha c$ against the harm-weighted true-press mass $\varepsilon\beta h$.

**D4 (the humans' informational advantage: a two-way split with a subject label; adversary A2 accepted in part).** Relative to the trajectory, $\mathcal F_H=\sigma(\Theta_H,\,S)$ with
- **acquirable** $\Theta_H$: $\sigma(\Theta_H)\subseteq\mathcal F_{A,\infty}$ — information the agent's own information comes to contain (the fully-updated-deference page's $E$); this includes the *latent* part $\Pi^{\mathrm{lat}}$ of the humans' preferences (a pre-existing fact behaviour reveals);
- **sealed** $S$: $\mu(S\in\cdot\mid\mathcal F_{A,t})=\mu(S\in\cdot\mid\mathcal F_{A,\infty})$ for all $t$ — no learning about $S$ along the trajectory.
The sealed part carries a *subject label*, which does not enter the arithmetic of D6 but does enter two things stated separately: (b) $N$, sealed information *about the world* (a holdout, the humans' internal states) — sealed *contingently*, by a barrier; (c) $\Pi^{\mathrm{act}}$, value *settled by the humans' decision at the time of the press* (the press is the fact, not a report of one) — its existence in $V$ is a decision (S6, Q1); (d) $r_t\in\{0,1\}$, the humans' observation $O_H$ of *the agent*, bearing on whether its plan selection at step $t$ is wrong in a way not visible in $\mathcal F_{A,t}$ — sealed *definitionally*: $r_t\perp\mathcal F_{A,t}$ is what "invisible from inside" means (run 2 `amendment-1a` R1 [reported]; Arbital hard-problem page's "possible wrongness that isn't just one more kind of uncertainty to be summed over" [reported there]). What the adversary established (A2, A24): the *arithmetic* treats (b) and (d) identically — a sealed generator with a detector; "persists by construction" for (d) is "persists by hypothesis" once $r$ is a single latent across $t$ rather than a per-step $r_t$; the distinctness of (d) lives in the *definitional* sealing, in the degree argument (S12, OPEN), and in S5 below, where (d) turns out to be the true-law name of the misspecification clause. Outside the independence structure of D6, the chain-rule terms of a decomposition are order-dependent (A4); "which component carries" is well-defined only in D6.

**D5 (decay, persistence, and the finite-time rate; adversary A3 accepted).** A component $Z$ *decays* if $I_\mu(X;Z\mid\mathcal F_{A,t})\to0$ and *persists* if $\liminf_t>0$. These are limit definitions. The finite-time content available in general is the bound $I_\mu(X;\Theta_H\mid\mathcal F_{A,t})\le H(\Theta_H\mid\mathcal F_{A,t})$ — the acquirable component's contribution at time $t$ is at most the agent's remaining entropy about it — and, in D6, the explicit margin $M(\lambda_t)$ of S3. v2 §1.4 (AGREED) asks for trajectory statements; wherever this file says "in the limit" it means it.

**D6 (the component-hazard model; the adversary's stationarity flags written in).** Faults $F_k\in\{0,1\}$, $k\in\{\theta,N,\pi,r\}$, mutually exclusive, rates $p_k$, $p_0=1-\sum p_k$; $X=c_0$ on no fault, $-h_k$ on fault $k$. The agent at level $t$ learns $F_\theta$ exactly with probability $\lambda_t\uparrow1$; it never observes $F_N,F_\pi,F_r$; an optional self-monitor for $r$ has sensitivity $\lambda^r_t$ and is itself compromised with probability $1-\rho$ on $r=1$. *Misspecification parameter* $m$ (adversary A17, adopted as part of the model): of the $F_\theta=1$ cases the agent believes it has cleared, a fraction $m$ are wrongly cleared; the agent's own law $\mu_A$ has $m_A$ (calibrated iff $m_A=m$). *Reading-compromise parameter* $\rho'$ (S12): on $r=1$ the agent registers a press with probability $\rho'$. Worked parameters throughout: $p_\theta=\tfrac1{10}$, $p_N=p_\pi=p_r=\tfrac1{50}$; $(b,a)$: $\theta\,(\tfrac9{10},\tfrac1{20})$, $N\,(\tfrac9{10},\tfrac1{50})$, $\pi\,(1,0)$, $r\,(\tfrac35,\tfrac1{50})$; pause stakes $c_0=1,h=10$; whole-line stakes $c_0=\tfrac9{10},h=\tfrac{11}{10}$ (run 2 `fud` R2's accounting [reported]). **Hypotheses the model fixes by fiat and this file lists as such (A5, A24):** (H1) $p_r$ does not fall with the capability that drives $\lambda_t$; (H2) $(b_r,a_r)$ do not degrade as the agent's surviving errors become subtler; (H3) $O_H\perp(\mathcal F_{A,t},\Theta,N,\Pi)\mid r$ (the corrected hypothesis of S2(d)); (H4) $m=0$ unless stated.

**D7 (the two clauses of fully updated deference; as on the page).** *Predictable-update clause*: "a sufficiently advanced AI can also see that this update is predictable (by efficiency) and therefore behaves as if it had already updated (by Bayesianism)". *Nothing-left-to-learn clause*: $\pi_6$, "Avoid being shut down; then gather more evidence $E$ to update $\Delta U$ as far as possible; then optimize the updated $\Delta U\mid E$." *Misspecification clause*: "For any prior $\Delta U$ we currently know how to formalize, the corresponding fully updated $T$ seems likely to be very far from our ideal". ## Proofs and examples

**P1 (D3, the identity) [checked].** $\mathbb E_\mu[X\mid\mathcal F_A,\mathrm{Pr}]=\mu(W^c\mid\mathcal F_A,\mathrm{Pr})\,c-\mu(W\mid\mathcal F_A,\mathrm{Pr})\,h$ with $\mu(W\mid\mathcal F_A,\mathrm{Pr})=\varepsilon\beta/(\varepsilon\beta+(1-\varepsilon)\alpha)$; non-positive iff $(1-\varepsilon)\alpha c\le\varepsilon\beta h$. In the model, on the cleared branch with weights $w_\varnothing=p_0$, $w_\theta=p_\theta((1-\lambda)+\lambda m)$, $w_N,w_\pi=p_N,p_\pi$, $w_r=p_r(1-\lambda^r\rho)$: $\mathbb E_\mu[X\mid\text{cleared},\mathrm{Pr}]\cdot Z\mu(\mathrm{Pr}\mid\text{cleared})=w_\varnothing\alpha c_0-\sum_kw_kq_kh_k$, and $\varepsilon=(Z-w_\varnothing)/Z$, $\beta=\sum w_kq_k/(Z-w_\varnothing)$, $\bar h=\sum w_kq_kh_k/\sum w_kq_k$ make the three forms one. $\blacksquare$ (`components.out` §S1 confirms the algebra on 3000 random models; it cannot fail.)

**P2 (S2) [derived].** (a) $\mathrm{Pr}=g(\mathcal F_H,\xi)$ with $\xi$ independent: conditional on $\mathcal F_{A,t}$, $X\to\mathcal F_H\to\mathrm{Pr}$ is Markov; data processing. (b) Chain rule. (c) $I(X;\Theta_H\mid\mathcal F_{A,t})\le H(\Theta_H\mid\mathcal F_{A,t})=\mathbb E[\eta(\mu(\Theta_H\in\cdot\mid\mathcal F_{A,t}))]$; by Lévy's upward theorem $\mu(\Theta_H=\vartheta\mid\mathcal F_{A,t})\to\mathbf 1[\Theta_H=\vartheta]$ a.s. for each of finitely many $\vartheta$; $\eta$ bounded, continuous, zero at the vertices; bounded convergence. (d) Given $(\mathcal F_{A,t},\Theta,N,\Pi)$, $X=f(\Theta,N,\Pi,r)$ is a function of $r$; under (H3) the conditional law of $(r,O_H)$ is its unconditional law, so the term is $\mathbb E[I(f(\cdot,r);O_H)]$, free of $t$. Without (H3) — e.g. the humans' judgement "the agent acts strangely" depends on $\Theta_H$ — the term is $I(X;O_H\mid\mathcal F_{A,t},\Theta,N,\Pi)$ and may move with $t$. (e) $I=0$ is conditional independence; Pinsker $d_{TV}^2\le\mathrm{KL}/2$, mean shift of a bounded variable $\le2\|X\|_\infty d_{TV}$, Jensen. $\blacksquare$

**P3 (S3) [checked].** Harm side affine in $\lambda$ with slope $-p_\theta q_\theta h_\theta$; false side constant; $\lambda^*=(H_0-F)/(H_0-H_1)$ when $H_1<F<H_0$. **P7 (S10) [derived; checked in the model].** Let the agent continue on silence (true throughout the model: $\mathbb E[X\mid\neg\mathrm{Pr}]>0$). **P9 (S7) [checked].** Adding detector $k$ to an active set changes $q_f$ to $1-(1-q_f^{-k})(1-d_k(f))$, $d_k(f)=b_k$ if $f=k$ else $a_k$; the margin changes by $\sum_{f\text{ fault}}w_f(1-q_f^{-k})d_k(f)h_f-w_\varnothing(1-q_\varnothing^{-k})a_kc_0$, which is S7's display. 1. **The self-model floor, now operational (decides S5's fix and S13's centre).** Does a reflection-satisfying $P$ exist whose self-model carries a misclearance rate $\hat p_r\ge m\,p_\theta$ that no passed self-check lowers — i.e. honest unidentifiability rather than dogmatism (run 2 `joint` Q-A4, `invariant` Statement 6 [reported]; run 1 `soares` T1(c) via `amendment-1a` R9(iii) [reported])? S5 makes the quantity concrete: it is the rate at which the agent's own clearances are wrong, which the humans' detectors estimate and the agent cannot. A calibrated agent would want to *learn* $m$ from the humans' catch record (a bet that settles in retrospect, per AUTHOR v1 §2.12: "it will probably correctly believe that the previous iteration was shut down for a good reason"), which is the trust-earning mechanism of v1 §2.13(c) applied to $r$. Whether that learning is possible without the agent's reading of the catch record being itself compromised is Open problem 2.
2. **The degree argument as a theorem (decides S12).** Extend the $\rho'$ toy: let the reading compromise apply to the agent's reading of the *catch record* and of the *anticipated modification* as well as of the press; derive which of the three decisions of S1 is most degraded; compare with a physics-delivered $Q$. Until then S12 is a $2\times2$ with no ranking.
3. **The humans' $\mu^*$-optimal press rule when $m$ is unknown to them (decides what "disclosure" is).** S5 gives the rule for known $m$; with $m$ unknown, the humans face a decision under uncertainty about the agent's calibration, and a rational disclosed human (HM's rational $H$) conditions on the agent's clearance *as evidence*, not as a switch — compute her rule as a function of her prior on $m$, and whether it is self-maintaining along the trajectory (run 2 `joint` Open problem 1 [reported]).
4. **The sign condition for sealed information beyond full disclosure.** Characterize the information structures on which $\Delta_k>0$ for the humans' sealed-information detector on every agent cell; HM Theorem 1 per cell is sufficient ($\mathcal G\supseteq\mathcal F_A$), Garber et al.'s bounded-communication results show partial disclosure is non-monotone (`garber-2024-the-partially-observable-off-switch-game` abstract [checked, l. 27]); candidate: a Blackwell-type condition on the humans' partition relative to the agent's on the payoff-relevant variable.
5. **The act component without the D2 price.** If $V$ contains value the press settles, state the fixed point Christiano's act-based agent relies on (the overseer prefers non-manipulation) as a component of $V$ and show it removes S6(e)'s incentive; compare Carey–Everitt's vigilance as the external form of the same guard; hijacked commands as $a_\pi>0$.
6. **Capability-dependence of $p_r$ and $(b_r,a_r)$ — (H1),(H2).** Two opposite mechanisms: more capability makes fewer errors of every kind ($p_r\downarrow$) and makes the surviving errors subtler ($b_r\downarrow$). Neither is modelled. S5 adds a third: $m$ itself may fall or rise with capability (a more capable agent clears more, and clears more confidently).
7. **Sigma-algebra changes (deferred by the author, v3 §1.2).** A modification that enlarges the agent's algebra creates a component no prior $\mathcal F_{A,t}$ could screen off — sealed until the algebra catches up. S3's migration becomes conditional on the agent's eventual algebra containing the humans' on the press domain; S5's $m$ acquires an algebra-relative meaning (faults the agent cannot *represent* are cleared by default).
8. **General $Q$ (K1).** $X_Q:=V(\text{act on }P_t)-V(\text{adopt }Q\text{ and act})$; S10 and S11 transfer by substitution (they are stated for $X_Q$); S3–S5 need the analogue of "the agent clears $F_\theta$" — the agent's own assessment that $Q$'s content is already in $P_t$ — and the analogue of $m$: the rate at which that assessment is wrong.

## Questions for the author

*Merged from the develop file's Q1–Q5 and the adversary's Q1–Q2; each with options and consequences.*

**Q1. Is the press "evidence that shutting down is good" (a likelihood ratio), or is Total Trust toward the humans on $X$ the intended criterion, or both by domain?** (a) *Evidence* — D2's separation stands; Total Trust is the target checked cell by cell; v3 §1.1's "exactly the Total Trust filler's reading" is corrected. (b) *Total Trust is the criterion* — then D1 is assumed on the domain where the criterion holds and the trajectory question is on which cells it holds (run 2 `substitution` R7's pointwise question [reported]); S3–S5 describe when it fails. (c) *Both by domain* — Total Trust where the humans refine the agent's information (HM Theorem 1 per cell), evidence elsewhere; the FDG's inverted cell and S4's $D_\theta=1$ cell are then cells where the reading holds and the criterion does not, and the question is what the agent does there.

**Q2. Does $V$ contain an act-constituted component — value the press itself settles?** (a) *Yes* — a stationary floor under D1 for a capable agent, independent of external detection; act-based content enters (Christiano; Carey–Everitt's preference shifts); the D2 price of S6(e) must be paid by a fixed point in $V$ (Open problem 5). (b) *No* — D1 for a fully-updated agent rests on sealed world-information and external detection of compromise alone; under whole-line stakes the required $\Delta_k>0$ is an order-one margin. (c) *"We can model it that way"* — both carried; the Christiano dialogue chain happens on this fork, with S6(c)'s non-realism point as the picture's answer.

**Q3. Is the cognition/physics line (v3 §2, CLAUDE) yours, or does "there is just The Channel" mean there is no such line?** (a) *Yours* — S1's causal restatement is its form; the three loci stand. (b) *No such line* — the surviving distinction is when the agent's act is on the causal path; D1 is then the sub-case of D2's domain in which the act is at the time of the modification; K2/K5 re-briefed. (c) *A line, but not this one* — say which.

**Q4. Is a hypothesis about one's own plan selection that no self-observation moves dogmatism or honest unidentifiability?** (Inherits run 2 `joint` Q-A4.) S5 gives the hypothesis an operational referent: the agent's own misclearance rate $m$. (a) *Dogmatism* — a calibrated $\hat p_r\ge mp_\theta$ is outside the thesis's agent class; the miscalibrated cell of S5 is then structural, and D1 for a capable agent rests on Q2(a) and sealed world-information. (b) *Unidentifiability* — the floor stands; the load is on the humans' catch record as evidence about $m$ (Open problem 1). (c) *Only a self-referential $P$ can say* — Open problem 1 is the research question.

**Q5. Which human-side arrangement does the picture take as the ordinary case: reasons attached (communicated evidence), humans conditioning on the agent's clearance (disclosure), both, or the reasonless button?** S4: at $m=0$ disclosure $>$ communicated evidence $>$ reasonless (which equals no channel once the target fails); S5: above $m^*$ the humans should keep pressing on $\theta$-grounds, and a miscalibrated agent then ignores them. *Reasonless* accepts S3's arithmetic and moves the load to the physics channel (S10, S11), making D1 a low-capability device; *reasons attached* makes migration the agent's to remove by conditioning and is v1 §2.12's "all sorts of feedback"; *disclosure* is HM's rational $H$ and depends on the agent's incentive to disclose (run 1 `miri` Prop. 13.3 [checked]) and on $m$; *both* is run 2 `amendment-1a` R4(ii)'s design [reported]. Each is a condition on the humans for K10.

**Q6. Which stakes accounting is in force for the sealed-component limit — pause or whole-line?** (Inherits run 2 `fud` Q1.) Pause: the margin is $O(1/\delta)$ and any sealed detector with $\Delta_k>0$ carries it ($6.9\times$ at the worked parameters). Whole-line: $O(1)$ ($11/9$), the flip at $\lambda^*$ exists iff $a_\theta>3.5\%$, and the per-detector inequality of S7 is stringent.

**Q7. Accept, amend, or reject the two-paragraph scope note of S13 as the replacement for v3 §1.1's CLAUDE reading?** (a) *Accept* — INDEX D5 closes; item 6 opens with the components named and the register point of S8(c) on the table. (b) *Amend* — say which components are in, and whether the scope note should mention $m$. (c) *Reject* — then either the evidential reading is meant with the acquirable component alone (S3 says fully updated deference is arithmetic on a reasonless channel), or the press is not evidence (A1(a)(iii)) and D1 is not derived from a posterior.
