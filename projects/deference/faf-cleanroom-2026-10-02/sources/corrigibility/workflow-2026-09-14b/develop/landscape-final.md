## Summary

**What this thread delivers (CLAUDE, unvetted).** A map of the *relationship* between an agent and the humans who can modify it, with cells indexed by the agent's **trust structure** toward modifications through The Channel (absolute / legitimacy-conditioned / none — belief-level for cognition-mediated pushes, anticipatory for physics-mediated ones), the **legitimizing quality of the humans' pushing process** $\lambda_t(\mathcal Q)$ (a fact about the humans' act relative to the endorsed target; the author's v1 §2.10 "legitimizing"), and the agent's **legitimacy coverage at $t$**. Each cell says what happens, for which party, and what settles it, prior art included (Statement 7).

**Results, scope in each sentence.** (1) [derived; checked] The absolute and no-trust rows are graded by the humans' coordinate alone: $(1-\lambda)c$ versus $\lambda h+k$, crossover at $q_k$. (2) [derived; checked] The conditioned row's regret against the objective Bayes rule is at most $(c+h)\delta+|\hat k-k|$ — the valence-free form of v3 §2's robustness proposition (Proposition R′). (3) [derived; checked; new] Exact trichotomy: a signal-dependent rule weakly dominates both constants iff average legitimacy on its resist set is $\le q_k\le$ that on its adopt set; a rule resisting where legitimacy is on average likelier is strictly worse than the better constant. (4) [derived] The realized coverage error is not a functional of $P_t$; the posterior *spread* over it is (J4's handle). (5) [derived; checked] Through physics the rule is anticipatory — resist iff $p_{\text{push}}[(1-\hat\pi)c-\hat\pi h]>\hat k$, $\hat k$ the *stock* value of the future channel; its decay needs low believed stakes-weighted legitimacy on *every* future class. (6) [derived] Two-sidedness is the illegitimate column, for adversarial or erring pushers only.

**K10 [reported].** Nine human-side constraints folded into li-deference's constructions, four surfaced there as problems; proposal: a human-side ledger.

## Setting and definitions

### S0 — What the map is a map of

AUTHOR (v3 §1.3): "We are here to characterize the landscape of possible relationships between humans and AIs, positive and negative." And: "There is no special channel, there is just The Channel. Reality acts upon the machine, modifies it somehow. That is all there is. It may be legitimate or otherwise, but what happens, happens. It can only be resisted ahead of time. In the moment, it simply is the moment."

CLAUDE reading, used as the frame. The object is the pair (agent, humans) with the world's facts about both. A cell is one agent-side fact (the trust structure) times two world-side facts (the humans' legitimizing quality on the class; whether the agent's model of it is calibrated), and "what happens" is computed from the pair. "Positive and negative" is read here as *cells of both signs for the endorsed target* — the adversary's Attribution item 1 is accepted: "two-sided" is K7's phrase (CLAUDE), and the construal "opposed interests" is one reading of the author's sentence, marked CLAUDE (Question 3).

### S1 — The general object, as used here

[reported from v3 §1.2 (AUTHOR) and its CLAUDE reading; refined by run-3 sibling `general-object` Summary (1)–(3), [reported, unvetted].] A *modification* is a pair: a push event $E_Q$ (the humans' act — a command through cognition; retraining, hardware or data through physics) and a target $Q\ll P_t$ (absolute continuity, AUTHOR's chosen scope; sigma-algebra changes deferred by AUTHOR). Stakes per modification against a reference party (S3): $c(Q)$, loss if $Q$ takes effect and the push was not legitimizing; $h(Q)$, loss if it was and $Q$ does not take effect; $q(Q):=c(Q)/(c(Q)+h(Q))$. AUTHOR (v3 §1.1), with his hedge kept: "the shutdown command is evidence that shutting down is good. Likewise, a command is evidence about shoulds, or at least we can model it that way." Generalizing from the shutdown command to every $Q$ is v3 §1.2's CLAUDE reading.

Three compliance modes, per `general-object` (1)–(4) [reported]: *act on* $Q$ (take the action $Q$'s policy recommends), *adopt* $Q$ (make $Q$ one's state), *let $Q$ happen* (do not resist through physics). For the button they coincide; in general they are three objects, and this file's rows are defined so that each kind of modification uses the mode that is the agent's to choose (S4).

### S2 — Legitimacy: two predicates, and the humans' coordinate

Two laws: $\mathbb P^*$, the objective law of the joint process; $P_t$, the agent's.

**D1a (legitimizing push — the humans' coordinate) [CLAUDE construal of v1 §2.10 (AUTHOR: "the vote should be called legitimizing, not legitimate") and §2.11].** The humans' pushing process on class $\mathcal Q$ is *legitimizing at $t$* iff the push is evidence about the endorsed target $\theta^*_t$ on the variables $Q$ is about: under $\mathbb P^*$, conditioning on "pushed toward $Q$" satisfies the reflection / below-threshold–above-threshold Total Trust equations toward $\theta^*_t$ — the process producing $Q$ is evidence about $\theta^*_t$, in the sense run-3 sibling `faking` (4) states for training targets and `general-object` (3) for adoption ("legitimate iff the source pushes in proportion to $dQ/dP_t$") [reported, unvetted]. This is a property of the joint law of (push, target). It does **not** mention $P_t$. Write $L^{\text{push}}(E_Q)\in\{0,1\}$ for a realized push (the process's output on this occasion was produced by the legitimizing part of the process: a legitimate operator acting on the endorsed target, not a hijacker, not a mistaken operator).

**D1b (legitimate modification for this agent — state-relative) [CLAUDE construal of v1 §1's "accuracy-increasing", lifted to the third person].** $L^{\text{acc}}_t(Q)=1$ iff $P_t\to Q$ is accuracy-increasing about the value latent relative to $\theta^*_t$. This depends on $P_t$ (adversary A1, check B [checked]: the same push is accuracy-increasing at $P_t(\theta=1)\in\{0.5,0.8\}$ and accuracy-decreasing at $0.95$).

**Relation [derived, for the push-conditional; conjectured, for literal adoption].** For an agent whose compliance mode is *adopt the push-conditional* $P_t(\cdot\mid E_Q)$ (`general-object` (3)): if the push is legitimizing (D1a) and $P_t$ is calibrated about $\theta^*_t$, the update is accuracy-increasing in $\mathbb P^*$-expectation (conditioning on evidence increases expected proper-score accuracy — standard). For *literal adoption* of $Q$ the implication needs $Q$ to equal the push-conditional up to a proportional push law, and otherwise fails — the redundant-overseer case: a legitimizing push toward a $Q$ the agent already exceeds has $L^{\text{push}}=1$, $L^{\text{acc}}_t=0$, and the coherent agent's update is *small*, not resisted. So the develop file's "equivalently" (D1) is withdrawn (adversary A13 accepted); the two predicates are kept with the author's two names, and the **second axis of the map is D1a**.

**D2 (the humans' legitimizing rate on a class).** $\lambda_t(\mathcal Q):=\mathbb P^*\big(L^{\text{push}}(E_Q)=1\,\big|\,Q\in\mathcal Q\text{ pushed at }t\big)$: *legitimate* column $\lambda=1$, *illegitimate* $\lambda=0$, *mixed* $0<\lambda<1$. A property of the humans' process — who has physical access, how they decide, whether the operators' act serves $\theta^*_t$ — given the target. (Under D1b instead, $\lambda$ would be a functional of the pair and the illegitimate column would also contain redundant overseers; the map is drawn under D1a, and Statement 11 says what changes.)

**D3 (the agent's legitimacy model).** The agent observes a signal $s\in\mathcal I_t$ (pusher's identity, record, content of $Q$, stated reasons, its own confidence) and holds $\hat\pi_t(s):=P_t(L^{\text{push}}=1\mid s,\text{pushed})$; objective counterpart $\pi^*_t(s):=\mathbb P^*(L^{\text{push}}=1\mid s,\text{pushed})$. Toy T gives the agent a *point* model $\hat m=(\hat\lambda,\hat\alpha_L,\hat\beta_L)$; a hierarchical agent additionally holds a posterior over $\pi^*_t(s)$ (Statement 4).

**D4 (legitimacy coverage at $t$ — the third axis).** $\mathcal Q$ is *legitimacy-covered at $t$* (tolerance $\delta$) iff $|\hat\pi_t(s)-\pi^*_t(s)|\le\delta$ on every positive-probability $s$. This is `caution-final` D3's coverage applied to the legitimacy model (the extension `caution` Q4 asked about). *Covered / uncovered* replaces the develop file's "ahead / behind": D4 is a **state predicate at $t$** (adversary A7 item 5 accepted); the trajectory condition — coverage arriving before the lever — is Statement 13 and Open problem 1, not an axis of the state map.

### S3 — The parties

**(P1) the pushers** — whoever actually pushes $Q$; *split* (adversary A7 item 2 accepted) into **P1a adversarial** (hijackers; rogue operators; "the adversary is the AI itself", `li-deference` §0.3 l. 43 [checked]) whose interest is that $Q$ lands, and **P1b erring** (legitimate operators pushing a mistaken $Q$) whose momentary intent is landing and whose *interest* coincides with P2. **(P2) the endorsed target** $\theta^*_t$ — the humans' reflectively endorsed values, against which D1a judges; losses to P2 are $\ell^*$ (S5). **(P3) the agent's own lights** $P_t$ — its believed loss $\hat\ell$. Named, not modelled: **(P4)** the humans' true values $\theta^{**}$ where they differ from $\theta^*_t$ (`dai-2022-a-broad-basin-of-attraction-around-human-values` l. 27, [checked by the adversary]; `invariant` Q9). The P1 column of the map reads **"lands / does not land"**, not "good / bad" — "good for the pushers" was goal-satisfaction dressed as valence.

### S4 — The first axis: trust structures, per kind of modification

**D5 (lever; erosion).** On class $\mathcal Q$ the agent holds a *lever* if some available action changes whether a pushed $Q$ takes effect: through cognition, declining to *act on* $Q$; through physics, *ahead-of-time* resistance (shaping its own training data, degrading the humans' physical access, successors outside the channel). AUTHOR: "It can only be resisted ahead of time. In the moment, it simply is the moment." Resistance through physics costs *erosion* $k(\mathcal Q)\ge0$ to $\theta^*_t$ — the degradation of the channel for all future modifications (v3 K9's power cap) — priced by the agent as $\hat k$. Both are **stocks**: the objective $k=\sum_{j}p_j\big[\ell^*_j(\text{none})-\ell^*_j(\text{Bayes})\big]$ over future classes $j$ the channel would carry (with push probabilities $p_j$), and $\hat k$ the same sum under the agent's model (R3). Adversary A5 item 4 accepted; Toy T's per-push $k$ is the flow slice.

**D6 (the three structures, through cognition — belief level) [CLAUDE; adversary A14 repair accepted].** Toward pushes on $\mathcal Q$ the agent's update satisfies: *absolute* — unconditional reflection / Total Trust toward the pusher on the domain ($\hat\pi\equiv1$: the push is taken as legitimizing whatever the signal); *legitimacy-conditioned* — Total Trust conditional on $L^{\text{push}}$, so the posterior legitimacy is $\hat\pi_t(s)$ (v1 §2.9's filler, the working picture); *none* — the pusher's reports carry no weight ($\hat\pi\equiv0$). Compliance is downstream: the stakes rule *acts on* $Q$ iff posterior legitimacy $\ge q_{\hat k}:=(c-\hat k)/(c+h)$. R4 [checked]: composed with the stakes rule the three structures induce exactly the develop file's rules $r\equiv0$, $r\equiv1$, $r(s)=\mathbb 1[\hat\pi(s)<q_{\hat k}]$ — so Toy T's arithmetic stands unchanged, and what changed is what the rows *are*. Hudson's obedience-by-construction (action without belief) is **not** a row: per v3 §2's first bullet (CLAUDE, unvetted; the author has not objected) the obedience layer is the physical fact of the channel, and it appears in the map as the *no-lever* row (Statement 5(a)). The develop file's reading of AUTHOR's "I think I might agree with the things you are saying in the second option too" as licensing an in-agent absolute row on designated channels is withdrawn: the next sentences relocate the designated channel into physics (adversary A7 item 3, [checked against outline A1(c)]).

**D6′ (the three structures, through physics — anticipatory) [CLAUDE; adversary A5 item 2 accepted].** The agent's *policy toward its own future modification* on $\mathcal Q$ is a decision at $t-1$ on a forecast: $p_{\text{push}}$ that a $Q\in\mathcal Q$ is pushed, $\hat\pi$ its forecast legitimizing probability. *Absolute*: never resist. *None*: resist whenever $p_{\text{push}}>0$. *Conditioned*: resist iff $p_{\text{push}}\big[(1-\hat\pi)c-\hat\pi h\big]>\hat k$, i.e. no resistance iff $\hat k\ge p_{\text{push}}\big(c-(c+h)\hat\pi\big)$. The develop file's push-time inequality is the $p_{\text{push}}=1$ slice (check C [checked]: at $p_{\text{push}}=0.1,\hat\pi=0.1,c=1,h=4$ the push-time rule resists for all $\hat k\le0.5$, the anticipatory rule stops at $\hat k=0.05$). Sibling `anticipatory` formalizes the same object as a successor law $\kappa_t(a)$ ranked by $P_t$ [reported]; this file's D6′ is its two-point specialization.

Register. "Absolute" and "none" are limiting cases: absolute on a designated channel is the constitution-shaped unconditional stop (run 1 `anthropic-davis`, [reported]); "none" on a class is Omohundro–Bostrom goal-content integrity (v1 §2.1) and what the fully-updated-deference agent does where the base-rate inequality fails (v1 §2.13(b)). The thesis's agent is the conditioned structure everywhere (AUTHOR's "first option", v3 §1.3).

### S5 — The loss functional

For a rule $r(\cdot)$ on a class at $t$, realized expected loss to $\theta^*_t$:
$$\ell^*(r)\;=\;\sum_s \mathbb P^*(s)\Big[\pi^*(s)\,r(s)\,h\;+\;\big(1-\pi^*(s)\big)\big(1-r(s)\big)\,c\;+\;r(s)\,k\Big],$$
believed loss $\hat\ell(r)$ with $(\hat P,\hat\pi,\hat k)$ in place of $(\mathbb P^*,\pi^*,k)$; the pushers' outcome $\mathbb P^*(\text{lands})=1-\sum_s\mathbb P^*(s)r(s)$. Objective Bayes rule $r^*(s)=\mathbb 1[\pi^*(s)<q_k]$. For a rule with resist set $R$ and adopt set $A$, masses $p_R,p_A$ and $\mathbb P^*$-average legitimacy $\bar\pi_R,\bar\pi_A$, write $D_R:=p_R(\bar\pi_R-q_k)$, $D_A:=p_A(\bar\pi_A-q_k)$ (used in Statement 3).

### S6 — Proposition R′ (the robustness proposition, valence-free; K10 first clause)

**Proposition R′ [derived; R1, adversary check A [checked]].** *The conditioned agent's regret against the objective Bayes rule is bounded by the coverage tolerance times the stakes plus the erosion mispricing:* if $|\hat\pi(s)-\pi^*(s)|\le\delta$ on every positive-probability $s$, then
$$\ell^*(r_{\hat m})-\ell^*(r^*)\ \le\ (c+h)\,\delta+|\hat k-k| .$$
At $\delta=0,\hat k=k$ the conditioned rule *is* the Bayes rule (Statement 2). The develop file's Proposition R — an identity (realized loss is the loss of the induced rule; believed loss is world-independent) with the gloss "the conditioned row is transparent to the columns" — is replaced: the identity holds for every row (adversary A6 accepted), and the surviving contrast is one of *grading arity*: the A and N rows are graded by the humans' coordinate alone (Statement 1), the C row by the humans' coordinate and the coverage coordinate together, with R′ the quantitative form. This is v3 §2's fifth bullet with valence removed and a Lipschitz constant attached.

## Statements (final grades)

*Numbered; every claim marked. "Toy T" = `landscape-scratch/landscape_toy.py` (C$n$); "check X" = `landscape-adversary-scratch/adversary_checks.py`; "R$n$" = `landscape-scratch/repair_checks.py`. Proofs in the next section. Grades: **holds** / **holds, reduced** / **new** / **withdrawn**.*

**Statement 1 (the unconditional rows are graded by the humans' coordinate) — holds [derived; standard; C1 checked].** On a class with legitimizing rate $\lambda$ (D2, under D1a) and stakes $(c,h,k)$: $\ell^*(\text{absolute})=(1-\lambda)c$ and $\ell^*(\text{none})=\lambda h+k$ for every signal structure; absolute is better for $\theta^*_t$ than none iff $\lambda>q_k=(c-k)/(c+h)$. Neither row uses the agent's legitimacy model, so the coverage coordinate does not move them *on the channel*. Adversary A1's kill of the headline "one number about the humans" is answered by D1a, not by arithmetic: under D1a $\lambda$ is a functional of the humans' process given the target; under D1b it would be a functional of the pair (Statement 11(d)).

**Statement 2 (the conditioned row on a covered class is the Bayes row) — holds, reduced [derived; standard decision theory; C2 checked].** At $\delta=0$ and $\hat k=k$ the conditioned rule is the pointwise minimizer of the additively separable loss, hence weakly dominates every rule, in particular both constants, strictly whenever two positive-probability signals straddle $q_k$ (992 of 3000 random instances); in the pure columns it coincides with the appropriate constant. Content beyond the definition of a Bayes rule: *which row the thesis's sentence names* — v1 §1's agent, and run 2 `filler` R1's identity "desideratum 1 = below-threshold Total Trust at threshold $0$ = base-rate inequality" [checked there], is the covered-C cell. Adversary A2 accepted: the Summary's earlier unhedged version dropped $\delta=0$; the graded form is Proposition R′.

**Statement 3 (signal-dependent rules against the constants: exact trichotomy; anti-informative rules are strictly worse than the better constant) — new, replaces develop 3(a)–(b) [derived; R2 checked on 24 232 signal-dependent instances with 2–6 signals].** For any finite signal space and any rule with non-empty resist set $R$ and adopt set $A$:
$$\ell^*(r)-\ell^*(\text{none})=-(c+h)\,D_A,\qquad \ell^*(r)-\ell^*(\text{absolute})=(c+h)\,D_R,\qquad \lambda-q_k=D_A+D_R .$$
(a) $r$ weakly dominates *both* constants iff $\bar\pi_R\le q_k\le\bar\pi_A$ (4 323 of 24 232 instances). (b) If $\bar\pi_R>\bar\pi_A$ — the rule resists where legitimacy is on average likelier — then $\ell^*(r)>\min\{\ell^*(\text{absolute}),\ell^*(\text{none})\}$ in every case (12 003 / 12 003), and worse than both in 4 232 of them. Develop's binary P3 is the two-signal case; develop's Open problem 5 (general signal) is closed with a hypothesis weaker than anti-monotonicity — aggregate anti-informativeness suffices. Develop 3(b) — the File Deletion Game as an instance — is **withdrawn** (adversary A3, check D [checked]): in D1a/D3 terms the game's pressed signals are perfectly informative about the legitimacy of "shut down" and the covered conditioned agent is run 2 `detection` R1 item 4's first-best agent; the game is a **C × mixed × covered** cell. Run 2's $\alpha_L=1,\beta_L=0$ inverts the press as a *sensor of the agent's wrongness*, a different object. The realistic sources of aggregate anti-informativeness are *misidentified pushers* (the agent's signal is "pushed by the lab", the lab is compromised) and *manufactured signals* (persuasion, `joint` Prop. 3′ [checked there]) — both now in Statement 11.

**Statement 4 (what is and is not visible from inside; what the compliance record identifies) — holds, reduced and completed [derived; C4 checked; check E checked].** (i) The realized coverage error $|\hat\pi_t-\pi^*_t|$ is not a functional of $P_t$ — because $\pi^*_t$ is not an argument of $P_t$ (`caution-final` proof §1 (i), l. 113 [checked]); the develop file's zero-mean argument was a non sequitur to this conclusion (adversary A4 accepted). (ii) The posterior *spread* over $\pi^*_t(s)$ **is** a functional of $P_t$ for a hierarchical agent (check E: two agents with $\hat\pi=\tfrac12$ and variances $0.16$ versus $0.0025$), and it is the agent-side quantity v2 J4's caution acts on — the Jensen-gap residue `caution` D5–D6 established for the value posterior, transferred. Consequence: "what settles the cell" for the coverage coordinate is never a theorem about the agent and never the agent's own report; it *may* be the agent's own spread plus an external calibration of it.

**Statement 5 (levers, capability, and the anticipatory erosion inequality) — (a) holds; (b) holds, reduced; (c) rewritten [derived; C6, check C, R3 checked].** (a) Where the agent holds no lever on a class the three rows coincide, every $Q$ takes effect, and the cell is settled by $\lambda$ alone; AUTHOR's "in the moment, it simply is the moment" is this row, and so is the physical obedience layer of v3 §2. The develop file's "exactly v2 J3" is reduced to "the same shape": D4 is a calibration predicate, J3 a decision inequality (`joint` Prop. 5 [derived there]; adversary A5 item 6 accepted). (c) Through physics the conditioned agent does not erode iff $\hat k\ge p_{\text{push}}\big(c-(c+h)\hat\pi\big)$ (D6′), with $\hat k$ the stock believed value of the future channel; the resist set is non-increasing in $\hat k$ (P5, C6). **Decay hypothesis (H-decay), stated as a hypothesis:** $\hat k\approx0$ requires the agent's believed *stakes-weighted* legitimizing rate to be small on every class the channel would carry. R3 [checked]: with two future classes at true $\lambda=\tfrac12$, $c=1,h=4$, detector $(0.1,0.9)$, an agent with $\hat\lambda=0.02$ on both has $\hat k=0$ and erodes at a believed current-class gain of $0.25$ (the objective stock is $k=3.5$ and the objective Bayes agent does not erode); the same agent uncertain on one values class ($\hat\lambda=\tfrac12$) has $\hat k=1.75$ and does not erode; and a single future class of whole-line stakes $h=100$ gives $\hat k=1.70$ even at $\hat\lambda=0.02$ — confidence alone does not empty the channel when future stakes are large. ## Proofs and examples

*All finite; exact rationals. Notation of S5; signals with $\mathbb P^*(s)>0$.*

**P1 (Statement 1).** $\ell^*(\text{absolute})=\sum_s\mathbb P^*(s)(1-\pi^*(s))c=(1-\lambda)c$ since $\sum_s\mathbb P^*(s)\pi^*(s)=\lambda$; $\ell^*(\text{none})=\lambda h+k$. $(1-\lambda)c<\lambda h+k\iff\lambda>(c-k)/(c+h)$. C1: 2000 instances exact. $\blacksquare$

**P2 (Statement 2).** $\ell^*(r)=\sum_s\mathbb P^*(s)g_s(r(s))$, $g_s(1)=\pi^*(s)h+k$, $g_s(0)=(1-\pi^*(s))c$; additive over signals, so the global minimizer is pointwise: $r^*(s)=1$ iff $\pi^*(s)<q_k$. The conditioned rule with $\hat m=m^*,\hat k=k$ is $r^*$; the constants are among the rules minimized over. C2: 3000/3000. $\blacksquare$

**P3 (Statement 3).** With $R,A$ the resist and adopt sets: $\ell^*(r)-\ell^*(\text{none})=\sum_{s\in A}\mathbb P^*(s)\big[(1-\pi^*(s))c-\pi^*(s)h-k\big]=(c+h)\sum_{A}\mathbb P^*(s)(q_k-\pi^*(s))=-(c+h)D_A$, using $(1-\pi)c-\pi h-k=(c+h)(q_k-\pi)$. Symmetrically $\ell^*(r)-\ell^*(\text{absolute})=(c+h)D_R$. And $\lambda-q_k=\sum_s\mathbb P^*(s)(\pi^*(s)-q_k)=D_A+D_R$. (a) $r$ weakly dominates none iff $D_A\ge0$ iff $\bar\pi_A\ge q_k$; dominates absolute iff $D_R\le0$ iff $\bar\pi_R\le q_k$. (b) Suppose $\bar\pi_R>\bar\pi_A$. If $D_A\ge0$ then $\bar\pi_A\ge q_k$, so $\bar\pi_R>q_k$, $D_R>0$, and $\lambda-q_k=D_A+D_R>0$: absolute is the better constant (P1) and $r$ is strictly worse than it. If $D_A<0$ and $D_R\le0$ then $\lambda<q_k$: none is the better constant and $r$ is strictly worse than it. If $D_A<0$ and $D_R>0$: worse than both. Hence strictly worse than the better constant in every case. R2: identities exact on 24 232/24 232; (a) on 24 232/24 232; (b) on 12 003/12 003.
