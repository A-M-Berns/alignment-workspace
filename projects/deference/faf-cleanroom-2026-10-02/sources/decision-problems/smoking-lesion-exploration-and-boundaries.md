## 1. The corrected Smoking Lesion, made into a tree

The author's correction (email of Sep 18): lesion $\ell$ forces smoking with some probability; a mutually exclusive anti-lesion $\ell'$ forces abstaining with some probability; the lesion causes cancer, the anti-lesion is medically inert; as both forcings become rare "the agent must learn not to smoke". Parametrize: $P(\ell{=}1) = \rho$, $P(\ell'{=}1) = \rho_A$, $\rho + \rho_A < 1$; cancer $k \sim \mathrm{Bern}(\gamma_1)$ if lesioned, $\mathrm{Bern}(\gamma_0)$ otherwise; forcing probability $\delta$ for each; payoff $r = \alpha m - \beta k$.

**Two trees, one run law.** *Bypass* (coverage failure): root chance over $(\ell,\ell')$; on the lesion branch a chance node sends mass $\delta$ to a leaf-world with $m{=}1$ without consulting $d$; symmetrically on the anti-lesion branch with $m{=}0$; all other mass consults $d$. *Overwrite* (action-veridicality failure): every run consults $d$; after the draw a chance node replaces the drawn action with $m{=}1$ w.p. $\delta$ on the lesion branch and with $m{=}0$ w.p. $\delta$ on the anti-lesion branch. Both trees have the same $\nu_{B,C}$ over $(\ell,\ell',m,k)$; they differ in $\mathrm{occ}(d)$ and in whether the draw is a coordinate.

**Claim 1.1 [checked] — at the OC grade with $O_d=\top$, the state sees the population.** Definition 8 conditions on the world-event $\top$, so $P_{s_d} = \nu$ on both trees, forced runs included. Both actions have positive probability under every $C$ once $\delta > 0$, so strict OC already gives EDT content: no masking, no Definition-10 trembles needed. (Forcing does the job trembles do in Remark 3.9, from the environment's side.) The evidential gap $g(q) := P_s(k \mid m{=}1) - P_s(k \mid m{=}0)$ as a function of the deliberate smoking rate $q$ has, as $\delta \to 0$:

$$
g(0) \to (1-\rho)(\gamma_1 - \gamma_0), \qquad g(1) \to \rho\,(\gamma_1 - \gamma_0).
$$

At the abstain label the only smokers are lesion-forced, so smoking is near-perfect evidence of the lesion; at the smoke label the only abstainers are anti-lesion-forced, so abstaining is perfect evidence of *not* having the lesion, and smoking carries the lesion's base-rate excess. Neither gap depends on $\delta$ — this is the "does not shrink as $\delta \to 0$" of the earlier reply, confirmed. EDT abstains iff $\beta\, g > \alpha$. Hence:

- **abstain is a calibrated-and-approved label** iff $\beta(1-\rho)(\gamma_1-\gamma_0) > \alpha$ — essentially always with textbook numbers;
- **smoke is a calibrated-and-approved label** iff $\beta\rho(\gamma_1-\gamma_0) < \alpha$ — i.e. iff the lesion's *population-average* cancer excess is worth less than the pleasure of smoking.

**Correction to the earlier reply.** It claimed abstaining is the *unique* fixed point "with standard numbers". That needs $\beta\rho(\gamma_1-\gamma_0) > \alpha$. With $\alpha{=}1,\ \beta{=}10,\ \gamma_1{=}1,\ \gamma_0{=}0$ this is $\rho > 0.1$; at $\rho = 0.1$ the smoke label just fails to be a fixed point (gap $0.1005$ vs threshold $0.1$, a knife-edge that finite $\delta$ tips), and at $\rho = 0.05$ it clearly is one. Where both pure labels are fixed points there is an interior indifference $q^\ast$ (near $0.05$ in every case computed), so the abstain basin is small: an agent that deliberately smokes more than about one time in twenty is pushed to smoking [checked for the parameters in §8, not proved monotone]. Also the reply's "$P(\mathrm{cancer}\mid\mathrm{abstain}) = 0$" assumed $\gamma_0 = 0$. The *mechanism* — it is the anti-lesion's mutual exclusion that makes abstaining informative in the would-be smoke equilibrium — is right.

**Claim 1.2 [checked] — at the SSC grades, the bypass tree dissolves and the overwrite tree does not.** Per-run SSC conditions on $\mathrm{occ}(d)$. On the bypass tree the forced runs drop out, Lemma 3 applies to what remains, the gap is exactly $0$ for every label and every tremble rate, and EDT smokes; without trembles the unchosen action has no consulted mass and $T_{\mathrm{EDT}}$ approves vacuously (Remark 3.6's collapse). On the overwrite tree $\mathrm{occ}(d)$ is every run, the realized act $m$ is correlated with $\ell$ inside $\mathrm{occ}(d)$, and the OC analysis of Claim 1.1 repeats verbatim at the SSC grade. Adding a *draw* coordinate $a$ to the algebra and conditioning on $\{a{=}1\}$ instead of $\{m{=}1\}$ restores Lemma 3 and dissolves it.

So the author's "perhaps I should take a version of the problem seriously after all" is **grade-relative and algebra-relative**: yes at OC-with-$\top$ on either tree; yes at SSC on the overwrite tree read on the realized-act algebra; no at SSC on the bypass tree; no on any tree once the agent's own draw is an event it can condition on. [scrubbed]; if SSC is taken as the fairness criterion, the corrected problem survives only in the overwrite reading, and only until the agent can see what it decided as distinct from what it did.

**Where this sits in the run's catalogue.** The bypass tree is S2's *reference-class* row (others also write $\{m{=}1\}$) with a second writer added for $\{m{=}0\}$; the overwrite tree is S2's *compulsion* row with an anti-compulsion. The anti-lesion is what is new. Its job: in S2's one-sided rows the smoke label is trivially consistent (no one else writes $m{=}0$, so abstaining is uninformative). The anti-lesion makes abstaining informative *at the smoke label*, which is what turns "EDT could abstain" into "EDT must abstain" under the parameter condition above.

## 2. The agent boundary: the author's claim, confirmed and located

AUTHOR: "'the agent itself exploring' vs 'the Lesion doing it' is an agent boundaries question, ie somewhat arbitrary, so epsilon exploration is just another name for the lesion being less correlated with cancer."

There are three places to put lesion-*uncorrelated* noise of rate $\eta$ into the tree: (i) inside the procedure, $C^\eta = (1-\eta)C + \eta\,\mathrm{Unif}$ (Definition 10's tremble, "the agent's"); (ii) as a bypass chance node that writes a uniform $m$ without consulting $d$ ("the environment's", coverage failure); (iii) as an overwrite node that replaces the draw with a uniform action ("the environment's", action-veridicality failure). Matched rates give identical $\nu_{B,C}$.

**Claim 2.1 [checked for (i) vs (ii); derived for (iii)] — the three placements give the same verdict at every grade.** At OC-with-$\top$ the state is $\nu$, which is the same, so the verdicts coincide trivially: uncorrelated noise dilutes $g$ identically whoever owns it, and the earlier reply's crossover — abstain survives iff $\eta$ is small relative to $\delta$ — holds with the same numbers on all three (table in §8). At SSC: (i) is inside the draw, which Definition 6 makes independent of $\ell$, so it is screened; (ii) is outside $\mathrm{occ}(d)$, so it is excluded; (iii) is inside $\mathrm{occ}(d)$ but independent of $\ell$, so it is screened. Same verdict in each cell.

So the confirmation: **the axis that decides the verdict is correlation with the lesion, not ownership.** A lesion-correlated overwrite ($t_1 > t_0$ in the run's notation) *is* the lesion; adding an uncorrelated component to it — from either side of the boundary — is "the lesion being less correlated with the action". The author wrote "less correlated with cancer"; on the tree it is the action–lesion link that dilutes while the lesion–cancer link is untouched, and only the composite action–cancer correlation is what an $O_d=\top$ state can see. Same thing from the agent's side.

**Where the boundary is *not* idle.** It enters only through what "the action" is taken to be — the run's act-/draw-/disposition-event trichotomy (C1 Open 4). Under (i), draw and realized act coincide and both are Definition-6-independent of $\ell$. Under (iii), they come apart, and SSC on the realized act is not the same as SSC on the draw. The boundary question "is this noise mine?" is therefore the algebra question "is my draw a coordinate?" — Q1's relativity, S2's "enriching the algebra with a who or draw coordinate dissolves both homes". That is a modelling decision, which is the author's "somewhat arbitrary"; but once made it fixes which conditioning is available, so it is not idle for the *description*, only for the verdict of any description that is honest about it.

**The author already had the pieces, in three places** (all his own words):

- `03-smoking-lesion-steelman-iii-revenge-of-the-tickle-defense` line 71 (2017): "In Smoking Lesion Steelman, the lesion is still upstream of your decision… But this depends on my special version of Smoking Lesion. Perhaps in a different version of Smoking Lesion, where the increased tendency to smoke is more like a trembling hand after the decision is made, the influence is downstream rather than upstream." — the overwrite tree, named nine years early, as the version ratifiability does not dissolve.
- `MIRI-LOG` line 662 (2017): "Say we have a trembling hand which is biased a little toward smoking if we have the smoking lesion… We definitely don't want to place it downstream of us." — the lesion-*correlated* tremble, distinguished from exploration by exactly the correlation axis of Claim 2.1. Line 646 gives the criterion: events are downstream of acts iff correlated with the *exploration bits* — i.e. with the draw, not the realized act.
- [11-troll-bridge-5](sl-workflow/posts/11-troll-bridge-5.md) lines 130–145 (2019): the chain chicken rule → $P(A){=}0$ rule → ε-chicken → ε-exploration, with "(C) just supplies pseudorandomness via the agent's own probabilities… (D) supplies randomness from an external source instead." — the agent/environment continuum for *uncorrelated* noise, in one line.

## 3. VOI exploration: why it is "more of a real distinction"

AUTHOR: "If it faces the iterated problem with a gentle discount rate, it explores a lot before settling into a strategy. Of course those explorations could be correlated with the lesion too… Voi exploration is more a real distinction."

The v2 type has no exploration mandate: "trembles enter as state honesty (Definition 10) or as an evaluator's device (Remark 3.12), never as a behavioural mandate", and VOI exploration has no counterpart in the type (run, P13-13; the run marks VOI "unassessed, not refuted"). The author's own statement of the device is `13-my-current-take-on-counterfactuals` lines 81–83 (PCDT "naturally chooses some actions which are suboptimal in the short term, due to the long-term value of information"), with the convergence claim at line 687 and in `12-dutch-booking-cdt-revised-argument` lines 424–428: divergence between counterfactual and evidential expectations "is itself a reason why the action probability should be high, provided that the agent is not shortsighted and doesn't expect the action to be a trap." So what follows is outside v2's type and is argued at the learning level.

Three properties distinguish VOI exploration from an $\eta$-tremble, and each has a consequence for the lesion.

**(a) It is belief-dependent, so it cannot be relocated across the boundary.** An $\eta$-tremble is a fixed-rate mixture, which is why §2 could move it into a chance node with identical statistics. VOI exploration is a function of the agent's state — of what it does not yet know — and chance-node labels are not functions of $s_d$ (they can depend on $C$ only through the fixed-point construction of §5). So there is no environment-side tree that reproduces it. This is the precise content of "unambiguously the agent's": not a fact about ownership of randomness but about the *type* of the mechanism. [derived]

**(b) It is intentional, so it is tagged.** An agent that smokes *in order to find out what happens when it smokes* knows, at the time, that this draw was its own. That knowledge sits in the likelihood: if its hypothesis class contains "my draws are sometimes overwritten / sometimes bypassed", then the outcome of a *known-deliberate* draw discriminates the forcing hypotheses from "smoking causes cancer" even when past episodes are pooled. Over time the agent accumulates draw-tagged statistics — which are exactly SSC-on-the-draw, the sense in which both forcing trees dissolve (Claim 1.2, last sentence). So **VOI exploration is the learning mechanism that produces the calibration sense at which the corrected problem is screened.** The $\eta$-tremble produces no tag: the state knows the tremble *rate* (self-transparency, Remark 3.6) but not which episodes trembled, and under $O_d=\top$ it sees only pooled realized acts. [derived]

This needs introspective access to the draw. Without it — the agent sees only what it did, not what it decided — the tag is unavailable, its hypothesis class has no draw coordinate, and the VOI of one pooled data point is tiny: the population conditional it can learn about barely moves. A no-draw-access VOI explorer therefore barely explores, and reduces to the $\eta \to 0$ case of §2, i.e. to the abstain label. The steelman robots of `01-smoking-lesion-steelman` lines 49–53 are built without introspection specifically to block this ("The epistemic module cannot watch the decision module"); the author's remark at `MIRI-LOG` line 322 is the same fork from the other side ("if the agent itself can predict the exploration bits, then taking an action deliberately is different than taking it exploratorily"). [derived]

**(c) It vanishes, so under pooled statistics the lesion wins in the limit.** VOI exploration decays as the agent learns. At the OC-with-$\top$ grade with a fixed forcing rate $\delta$, the earlier reply's crossover applies: once the agent's exploration is small relative to $\delta$, confounded episodes dominate the pooled conditionals and the abstain label is selected regardless of path. So a VOI explorer *without* the tag converges to abstaining — the correction email's conclusion — while one *with* the tag converges to smoking. The two readings of the correction email are the two rows of §1. [derived; the crossover checked in §8]

**Lesion-correlated VOI exploration.** the author's caveat: the explorations "could be correlated with the lesion too". VOI exploration depends on $s_d$; if the lesion shifts $s_d$ (makes the agent curious, raises its estimate of smoking's pleasure), the exploration is lesion-correlated — but *through the state*, which in v2 means a distinct point, calibrated separately. That is Proposition 12's pattern: at the lesion-shifted point the calibrated state already carries the raised lesion posterior, and inside the point the draw is Definition-6-independent of $\ell$. The correlation lives in the population, not in any point's conditionals. So state-mediated correlation of exploration with the lesion is representable and dissolves. What does *not* dissolve is exploration correlated with the lesion *not through the state* — the VOI computation itself being corrupted — and that is §4's third channel. [derived]

**Related, on Troll Bridge.** the author worked VOI exploration into iterated Troll Bridge in `Logsidian/journals/2023-05-28.md` lines 29–35: a discounted logical inductor "should see value-of-information in crossing… So within the hypothetical reasoning needed for the usual Troll Bridge argument, the agent could choose to cross for reasons other than inconsistency. This blocks the conclusion that the bridge will blow up if crossed" — and the stricter troll that punishes crossing "for any other reason than high expected reward in the current instance… would not be such a compelling philosophical dilemma." That is (b) applied to the bridge: VOI crossing is tagged as *not-from-the-Löbian-reason*, and the tag is what the original troll cannot read. The same entry has the line that is §4's thesis in five words: "So EDT creates its own smoking lesion, so to speak" (line 43).

## 4. Three channels from lesion to action, and where the formalism's boundary actually is

Collecting §1–§3 with the draft's own machinery. A "lesion" — any upstream chance variable correlated with both the outcome and the action — can reach the action in three ways.

| channel | lesion reaches the action via… | v2 status | what dissolves it |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1. state** (tickle) | the observation / subjective state; the draw is $C(d)$ at a lesion-shifted point | representable (Prop 12) | calibration per point; nothing extra |
| **2a. bypass** | a chance node writes $m$ without consulting $d$ (coverage fails) | representable (S2 reference-class row) | any SSC grade; or a `who` coordinate |
| **2b. overwrite** | a chance node replaces the drawn action (action-veridicality fails) | representable (S2 compulsion row) | a *draw* coordinate; SSC on the realized act does not |
| **3. draw** | the deliberation's own output, not through the state | **not representable**: violates Definition 6 ("independently of everything so far") | — |

Channel 3 is Troll Bridge. The Löbian argument's conclusion is that the agent's *draw* — cross or not — is correlated with $\mathrm{Con}(\mathrm{PA})$ while its *state* cannot be (Gödel II), which is exactly a draw that depends on something upstream other than $d$. The run's shadow $\mathrm{TB}(\theta)$ models the bridge as channel 2a (`bot` w.p. $\theta$ → forced cross) and notes the mismatch itself: "forced crosser ≠ deliberator"; "the tickle defence Gödel-blocked in the post, stipulation-blocked in the tree" (`sl-workflow/notes/repair/P11.md:69`). That is the formal content of the author's "it loses features essential to my critique": not that the environment cannot be written down (it can, as $\mathrm{TB}(\theta)$), but that the *agent* cannot — a procedure whose draw at a fixed point is correlated with an upstream variable is not a Definition-4 procedure run under Definition 6.

Three earlier statements of the same boundary, all the author's:

- 2017, `05-mixed-strategy-ratifiability-implies-cdt-edt-1` line 46: *mixed-strategy implementability* — "**A** has no other direct parents… things correlated with our randomness must be downstream of it" — called "the shakiest assumption" at `07-a-rationality-condition-for-cdt-is-that-it-equal-edt-part-1` line 73. Definition 6 is implementability made into run semantics; channel 3 is its failure.
- 2023, `Logsidian/journals/2023-05-28.md` line 40–43: "The troll makes [the chicken condition] consequential. This is similar to entangling consequences with the random bits used for exploration. So an analogue of mixed-strategy-implementability is violated."
- 2026, `Logsidian/journals/2026-02-23.md` line 113: "The decision procedure itself might be violated by an environment which breaks the agent-environment boundary, such as in lesioning cases."

So the boundary that the formalism draws, and cannot move, is not agent-noise vs environment-noise (§2 showed that one is idle) but **state vs draw**: everything the lesion does to the agent must go through the point, or through the tree after the draw. The point from [another person in the thread] that embedded agents might have lesions to worry about is right and lands in two places: channel 2b (a body that does not do what the mind decided — representable, and honest at every grade until the draw is a coordinate) and channel 3 (a mind whose deciding is itself the lesion — not representable). The "tickle-free Smoking Lesion" idea ([scrubbed]: a bias not consciously introspectable becomes expressible in an embedded setting; repeated Smoking Lesion as "a completely different game" from one-shot) is, in these terms, channel 3: a tickle the state cannot carry is a correlation the draw carries without the state. [derived; the reading of the source is ATTRIBUTION-UNVETTED]

## 5. Back to the thread

**A question from [another person in the thread]** (a mixed CDT/EDT population, the agent uncertain of its own type). On the tree this is S2's reference-class row: the population statistics are written by agents at points the present agent never occupies. At OC-with-$\top$ it is *consistent* — the state "does not know it is the decider" (the run's binding register clause, `sl-workflow/notes/repair/C1.md:5`) and calibrated EDT refrains on others' statistics. At any SSC grade, or as soon as "I am deliberating the EDT way" is in the state, it is screened. So the answer to that question is not that the problem cannot be posed but that it is posed at a grade where the agent is calibrated to a population it is not part of; "one agent, not a superposition" is Definition 6 plus one $C$. The earlier reply's "an agent deliberating the EDT way, and can tell that's what it's doing, has learned it isn't a lesion-made CDT agent" is the SSC reading.

**The "unstable" objection from [another person in the thread].** Instability in the author's sense (the statistics change when you drop the agent in) shows up in the fixed-point structure of §1: with both pure labels consistent, the population statistic is *label-dependent* (the correlation is $(1-\rho)(\gamma_1-\gamma_0)$ at one label and $\rho(\gamma_1-\gamma_0)$ at the other), and the run's Corollary 12′ says this in general — the population correlation *is* the procedure's type-difference, sign $= \mathrm{sign}(q_1 - q_0)$ (S5, Lean-checked identity).

**The claim, credited to [another person in the thread], that Troll Bridge is just Smoking Lesion in this formalism.** Concede at the environment level: $\mathrm{TB}(\theta)$ is a forced-act lesion tree, S2's compulsion/bypass row with cross for smoke, and the run confirms "the real smoking lesion" in that precise sense (P11-13′). Deny at the agent level: §4, channel 3 vs channel 2. The earlier reply's finer point stands — Troll Bridge's correlation is *created by the agent's responsiveness to the proof* (the Löb premise: proving crossing is bad makes the agent not cross), so it holds for EDT-like and not for CDT-like reasoning; in tree terms, the label-dependence of $\nu$ that §5 of the draft externalizes is, in the bridge, generated inside the draw. [11-troll-bridge-5](sl-workflow/posts/11-troll-bridge-5.md) line 124 is the author's own statement: "The proof that things wouldn't be fine relies on the fact that the agent accepts that very proof as sufficient reason."

**The author's correction.** Holds as stated at the OC grade with an uninformative observation, under $\beta\rho(\gamma_1-\gamma_0) > \alpha$ for uniqueness (§1). Two of its four bullets are already in the draft: "credence that it will smoke could approach zero without ever being zero" is Definition 10 / Remark 3.9's "an evidential learner with vanishing exploration is $\varepsilon$-calibrated throughout training"; "if the agent has or lacks the lesion its whole life, the choices must be independent of the bad outcomes" is Lemma 3 applied to the agent's own history — under a fixed $\ell$ its own $(m,k)$ record is independent, so its learned conditionals screen off. "Let the agent have or lack the lesion over time" is what makes the agent's own record carry the population correlation, and it is the learning-side reason the corrected problem is a *repeated* problem — outside v2's type, consistent with the "completely different game" remark ([scrubbed]).

## 9. Follow-up exchange (2026-09-18/19): grounding calibration in learning, the question-begging worry, and Troll Bridge

The author's follow-ups after §1–§8, with the session's responses compressed. All [derived] unless marked; nothing here is machine-checked.

**9.1 the author's direction.** The analysis "tips me towards trembling-hand style definitions of calibration for the next version of the Coherent Care formalism, ie the 'ideal rationality picture' needs to have its justification grounded in the learning theoretic picture." Response: the draft is halfway there (Definition 10, Remark 3.9), but §1–§3 say the grounded object is not a uniform tremble. (i) Uniform trembles silently assume the off-path mass is the agent's own and lesion-independent — implementability again; the learning picture that licenses that is VOI exploration, which is tagged, so the grounded version conditions on the *draw* and is SSC-shaped. (ii) VOI exploration is non-uniform, so the limit becomes learner-dependent and prior-determined at acts the learner would not explore — the run's Q-traps cost; the static picture inherits the trap problem. (iii) Grounding relocates rather than removes the choice of calibration sense: the ideal picture is the shadow of a *class* of learners, and the grade is a parameter of the class (run: "which credit scheme = which conditioning fork", priced by regret). Two checks in favour: Troll Bridge comes out as the boundary for a stated reason (§9.3), and `fable-slop-notes` Claim 3.2 already needs trembles for "EDT = UDT on fair problems". Concrete proposals: the draw as a coordinate; limit calibration relative to a class of vanishing state-dependent exploration sequences (the run's test-sequence form), ray-dependence stated; the shadow theorem as a conjecture with visitation and no-trap hypotheses; §9 of the draft as the spine. Caution: "idealized rationality as the limit of bounded rationality" should not presume a unique limit — §1's multiplicity shows path-dependence, so the ideal object may be a set of limits (Appendix B's credal calibration) or a selection principle (Q5).

**9.2 The question-begging worry.** the author: treating every act as an experiment assumes the draw independent of its causal parents given the state, which "sorta begs the question on CDT = EDT"; the correct theory, "call it FDT", has counterfactual = conditional wherever the latter is defined; to represent classical CDT one might let its causal model be any one consistent with observed statistics (prior-selected), or give it interventional access to all variables. Response: the question is begged one level earlier — Definition 6 *is* implementability, and the run's S9 already has CDT = EDT at recorded points under it; the tagged grounding exposes the assumption rather than adding one. **Claim 9.1 [derived, not machine-checked].** Under the first proposal, at a recorded point Lemma 3 gives $m \perp (\ell,k)$ under $P_s$, and any causal model observationally consistent with $P_s$ then has no effective backdoor from $m$ to $k$, so $P(k \mid do(m)) = P(k \mid m)$ for every positive-probability $m$ and every graph prior. The prior matters only at null acts and at reference-class points (S2). Hence: *classical CDT is EDT calibrated to a population it is not part of, plus a graph prior about that population.* The second proposal learns the channel structure of §4 and reduces the residual dispute to the decision variable: intervening on the draw agrees with conditioning on it (Definition 6); intervening on the realized act disagrees with conditioning on it at channel 2. Two cautions from the run for the FDT property: the Dutch book has force against equilibrium mixtures but not against exploration-sourced probability (S12; AlexMennen's $O(\varepsilon^2)$ point), and null-act freedom must not be tremble-pinned — on $\mathrm{TB}(\theta)$ every tremble device approves not-crossing because fixed forced mass swamps vanishing trembles. Suggested for v3: *observational causal calibration* ($\mathrm{cf}_s$ consistent with $P_s$, nothing more) as Q6's neutral referent, with Claim 9.1 as its theorem.

**9.3 Troll Bridge: specifying FDT learning that passes where EDT fails.** the author: "I wonder if we can specify fdt learning in a way that avoids troll bridge where edt succumbs."

*The lemma the argument needs.* Every version of the Löbian argument uses (L): $\square(\mathrm{cross} \to \mathrm{bad}) \to (\mathrm{cross} \to T)$, where $T$ is the troll's trigger. For a rule that crosses iff its proof-respecting conditional expectation favours crossing, (L) holds with $T = \mathrm{Incon}(\mathrm{PA})$: given the proof, the only way to cross is a misfire. Löb then closes. `13-my-current-take-on-counterfactuals` line 62 names this step as the one a counterfactual agent blocks.

*It is the corrected Smoking Lesion's structure (§1) with the proof as selector.* The "forced mass" is $P(\mathrm{Incon})>0$, fixed by Gödel plus non-dogmatism; deliberate crossing at a vanishing rate is swamped by it, exactly the crossover of §8; the run's $\mathrm{TB}(\theta)$ has both pure labels calibrated (not-cross on the merits, cross vacuously) with separatrix $\theta/(1-\theta)$. What the tree lacks and the bridge has is a *selector*: the proof makes the not-cross label the only one available to a proof-respecting rule, whatever the empirical frequencies. [11-troll-bridge-5](sl-workflow/posts/11-troll-bridge-5.md) line 172's LIDT — crosses for a long time, finds the proof, stops — is a jump between the two basins triggered by the proof.

*Specification.* An **FDT learner** is one whose action-relevant expectation is
$$
\mathrm{cf}(U \mid do\,a) \;=\; \mathbb{E}_{w \sim P}\big[\,E(a, w)\,\big],
$$
the *marginal* belief over world-states $w$ — including the logical state $\mathrm{Incon}$ — composed with the environment's response function $E$; where $E(a,\cdot)$ is learned from tagged outcomes of the agent's own draws of $a$ and from proofs *about the environment's rule* (the troll's stated trigger), and $P(\mathrm{Incon})$ is small by non-dogmatism. Initial data comes from VOI exploration under a gentle discount. Three consequences:
1. **(L) fails.** Crossing given $\square(\mathrm{cross} \to \mathrm{bad})$ is consistent with the rule, since the rule never consults $P(\mathrm{bad} \mid \mathrm{cross})$; so cross does not imply misfire, Löb does not fire, and (PA being sound) no such proof exists. Counterfactual and conditional never *actually* disagree — the author's own point at line 787 of post 13 — the disagreement is only hypothetical.
2. **The exogeneity of the draw is a policy, not a belief.** The proof constrains $P(w \mid \mathrm{cross})$, which the formula never consults; it does not constrain $P(w)$ or $E(a,w)$. So no derivation-inspection rule is needed: proofs that route through the agent's own decision procedure are structurally inert for cf. This is Definition 6 adopted for channel 3 by the agent itself, and it is *the tickle defence adopted as a policy where Gödel makes it unavailable as a belief*: the agent does not believe its draw is sound; it declines to treat its draw as evidence about soundness.
3. **The FDT learner is a Definition-4 procedure under Definition 6; the proof-respecting EDT agent is not.** In consistent worlds the FDT learner crosses regardless of $\mathrm{Incon}$; the EDT agent's crossing implies $\mathrm{Incon}$, so its draw has a parent other than its state. "Troll Bridge is outside the type" (run, S23) is therefore a fact about the *agent*, and the type boundary is normative.

*Why VOI alone does not rescue EDT.* [derived] With the proof in hand a proof-respecting conditional has $P(\neg\mathrm{bad} \mid \mathrm{cross}) = 0$, so the information value of crossing is zero — there is nothing it expects to learn. The VOI escape in `Logsidian/journals/2023-05-28.md` (ll. 29–35) works because the conditional-contract market contains traders *not* bound by the proof, i.e. an FDT-like component whose disagreement the VOI resolves. So VOI exploration depends on the marginal formula; it is not an independent escape. Reading of the journal: ATTRIBUTION-UNVETTED.

*Bunthut's two objections (post 13, ll. 740–872), answered.* (i) A troll triggering on the inconsistency of the agent's counterfactual system $X$: against the marginal formula, a trigger on $P(\mathrm{bad} \mid \mathrm{cross})$ is inert, and a trigger on what the rule *does* consult — "$X \vdash \forall w\, E(\mathrm{cross},w) = \mathrm{bad}$" — makes $\varphi \leftrightarrow \square\varphi$ for $\varphi := \forall w\,E(\mathrm{cross},w)=\mathrm{bad}$, whence $\square\varphi$ by Löb and $\varphi$: the bridge is then *provably* out and not crossing is correct, which is the author's line-177 point that such a troll dissolves the dilemma. The author's concession at ll. 801–803 ("I don't have any proposal" for an $X$ that rules the proof out) is answered not by a weaker $X$ but by a *structured* one: cf respects proofs about $E$ and $P(w)$, not about the composite. (ii) "The refuser is right; the bridge would blow up if *he* crossed": yes — both fixed points are calibrated, so calibration does not adjudicate (Q6's verdict), value does (Definition 21), and Definition 16 says which event each rule makes inconsistent: the FDT learner makes $\mathrm{cross} \wedge \mathrm{bad}$ $P(\mathrm{Incon})$-inconsistent; the EDT rule makes $\mathrm{cross} \wedge \neg\mathrm{bad}$ inconsistent — the wrong one.

*Where it is prior-dependent, and why that is not "real Troll Bridge".* The stricter troll — blow up on any crossing against the agent's own *myopic* estimate — traps an FDT learner with a bad prior (VOI crossing against the estimate is punished, confirming the prior). By the second test in `Logsidian/journals/2020-10-19.md` (l. 35) that is a trap with no way out, not a dilemma; the 2023 journal says the same (l. 33). Against the original and the exploring-agent trolls the FDT learner passes from any prior that explores at all, since VOI crossing is deliberate and leaves no exploration bit for the troll to read.

*Cost.* The marginal formula cannot use proofs about the *composite* agent-plus-environment as shortcuts; a bridge that is really out costs one crossing unless its being out is provable as a fact about $E$ alone. And the FDT property (cf = conditional at positive-probability acts) holds for this agent because its tagged data really has draw $\perp w$ — it is self-vindicating, not a priori.

*Conjecture to try [guess].* Iterated bridge, slightly fallible troll, gentle discount, no-trap belief: every marginal-formula learner with positive initial crossing propensity crosses at all but finitely many rounds; every proof-respecting-conditional learner stops crossing after the round at which it finds the Löbian proof (the omitted proof of post 11, l. 174). The first half looks provable with logical-inductor machinery; the second half is the author's.

**9.4 Corrections from the directions run (2026-09-19).** Five subagent notes in `directions/` (index: `directions/README.md`) took the directions opened by §9.1–§9.3 and the [two-lesions-doc-2026-09-18](two-lesions-doc-2026-09-18.md) / [two-lesions-exchange-2026-09-19](two-lesions-exchange-2026-09-19.md) material. They correct this note in five places; the earlier text is left as written and these corrections govern.

1. **Claim 9.1 (§9.2) holds in scope and fails as stated.** [defining-cdt-in-the-learning-setting](directions/defining-cdt-in-the-learning-setting.md) §2, Theorem 3: at a recorded point, $P^{G,do(a)} = P_s(\cdot \mid a)$ *iff* the act is independent of its $G$-parents under $P_s$. Under (S1) with pre-query parents only this holds for every compatible structure (checked over all 25 DAGs). It fails for latent confounders whose effects cancel in the observational law and for reversed structures in which the act has real downstream effects — both observationally consistent with $P_s$ at a recorded point [checked]. So "for every graph prior" must read "for every structure whose act-parents are pre-query and observed"; the author's first proposal, if it admits latent parents of the act, is prior-relative even at recorded points.
2. **§9.3 point 2 over-claims.** The marginal formula does not by itself shield $P(\mathrm{Incon})$ from the composite theorem: if the marginal is an ordinary coherent credence over a language containing $\mathrm{Cross}$, a believed $\mathrm{Cross} \to \mathrm{Incon}$ forces $P(\mathrm{Incon}) \geq P(\mathrm{Cross}) - \varepsilon$, so the theorem reaches the marginal through self-prediction. What shields is the *language*: the marginal must be a credence over sentences that do not name the agent (NR3 in [non-responsiveness-learnability](directions/non-responsiveness-learnability.md) §1), with a second, full inductor doing self-prediction and response learning. That is the doc's Proposition 10 phrase "formed without regard to its own act" made precise, and the doc's own fragility remark ("causal about the bridge but evidential about itself") is the case where the shield is missing. Without provable self-prediction the unshielded agent still escapes the original troll, since under the hypothetical its crossings are self-mispredictions rather than $\mathrm{Incon}$; a troll that fires on self-mispredicted crossing re-closes the trap [derived; checked].
3. **"FDT learner" (§9.3) is a naming collision.** The object is the doc's $\mathfrak{B}$, and on the tree $\mathrm{TB}(\theta)$ it is CDT with $\square\bot$ in the fixed algebra ([clean-source-and-policy-responsiveness](directions/clean-source-and-policy-responsiveness.md) §3–4; [defining-cdt-in-the-learning-setting](directions/defining-cdt-in-the-learning-setting.md) §8 Q5). It acquires FDT's Newcomb behaviour only when lifted to the policy level with its exogenous roots fixed by responsiveness, where it becomes the deviation counterfactual of Definition 22 ([policy-level-fdt-learner](directions/policy-level-fdt-learner.md) §1–2). Read "FDT learner" above as "marginal-formula agent"; the policy-level lift is the FDT candidate.
4. **$\mathrm{TB}(\theta)$ as the run built it is bypass-shaped.** Its cross/not gap is $10(1-\theta)$. A tree faithful to the doc's troll — the forced crosser pays $-10$ only when $\square\bot$ — gives $10(1-2\theta)$, which is Proposition 10's crossing rule at $\theta < \tfrac12$ [clean-source-and-policy-responsiveness](directions/clean-source-and-policy-responsiveness.md) §4. The qualitative claims of §9.3 survive; the numbers differ.
5. **§1's fixed-point analysis sits outside the doc's hypothesis (H).** With this note's parameters $\beta\rho(\gamma_1-\gamma_0) = 0.55 < \alpha = 1$, so the smoke label is itself a fixed point; the doc's regime of three fixed points $0 < \check p < \hat p < 1$ with $\hat p = 1 - O(\delta)$ requires (H), i.e. $\alpha < \beta\rho(\gamma_1-\gamma_0)$. The two analyses agree where they overlap, and the exchange's "hovers near $\hat p$" holds under (H) and fails here ([iv-design-draw-as-instrument](directions/iv-design-draw-as-instrument.md) §5).

Two of the directions' positive results bear directly on §9.1's proposal: every note converges on the draw as a coordinate (§7 Q2 answered yes from five sides), and the deviation counterfactual is the referent that gets Newcomb and Troll Bridge both right, stated as *policy-responsiveness* — respond to $X$ iff the all-instance point-deviation moves $\nu(X)$. The open fork they leave is the level at which "cf = conditional wherever defined" is meant: the deviation referent satisfies it at the policy and violates it at explored acts against a label-probe Omega.
