I did not find a way to break the other clause ($U_r\supseteq\mathrm{unfair}(B)$) under EC+FRec: under EC two fiber members diverging at a chance node carry different chance coordinates in their leaf-worlds, and at a recorded decision node different act-events, so an unfair fiber cannot be made fair by relocating *other* points — unless the divergence node is itself an unrecorded simulation and the members differ only by spurious $U$-nodes below them [derived, not built]; the repaired definition covers that case as well.

## Closing

**The single most damaging objection.** The thread's two structural theorems — "LCA relocation is the least fair repair" (SP-5) and the licensing biconditional's law-side half (SP-9 (iv)⟹(iii), SP-13(a)) — both rest on reading FRec as "every decision node, simulations included, is action-veridical". Definition 7 says the opposite (Remark 3.4: simulations off $O_d$ are deliberately tolerated), and phase 1 killed exactly this strengthening in its observation form (`adversary-repair` A.2). The witness is not exotic: it is the notes' own decoupled mugging (P1 CF-15), which satisfies FRec at $d$ and has an inert simulation. Hung under a survivor $e$, it makes $e$ "exposed" yet leaves the output fair after relocating $d$ alone — so the fairness closure $\mathrm{cl}_f$ over-approximates and $r_{\mathrm{LCA}}$ is not least; standing alone, it makes "the other branch's law is $C(d)$-independent" true while the observation is unlicensed. The spectrum picture survives (every element is value-preserving; the verdict at $r_{\mathrm{LCA}}$ is unchanged; the crossing of licensed observations is inert), but the mandate's plank "LCA-relocation is the LEAST repair whose output is strongly fair" is proved only after inert queries are quotiented away — the equiv thread's job, which the notes flagged as needed only in the FRec-less case. Until that quotient is in place, "normatively distinguished point" should be read as "distinguished among data closed under *semantic* exposure", and every downstream use of "$r_{\mathrm{LCA}}$ = refrain exactly across couplings" should say so.

**The one thing to tell the synthesis agent not to repeat.** Do not repeat "under FRec, relocating $d$ resolves the $d$-nodes below a copied survivor differently and *therefore* the copies are non-isomorphic" (SP-4's proof, SP-9's (iv)⟹(iii), and anything that says "FRec stamps the drawn act into every leaf-world below a $d$-node"). Recording constrains the node met on $O_d$-runs only; a simulation's two resolutions may coincide. State "least fair repair" and "licensed ⟺ other-branch law independent of $C(d)$" only with "no inert $d$-nodes" (or "after the spurious-query quotient") in the same sentence. Secondarily: do not repeat "glomarization has no refuser trap / is self-correcting" without "the always-glomarizer is the sealed policy there".
