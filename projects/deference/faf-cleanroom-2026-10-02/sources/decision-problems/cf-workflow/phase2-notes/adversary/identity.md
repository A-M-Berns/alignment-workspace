## Claim 4 — ID-14: observation-identity as the coarsening that works. **SURVIVES, STRENGTHENED (a theorem-let the notes lacked); clause (ii) "imports no logical correlation" WOUNDED.**

**Strengthening [derived; toolkit sanity check in `idle_and_diag.py` §1].** On the class P1:synthesis item 20 uses — recorded at every queried point *for every procedure*, every observation realized — **$(O_d, A_d)$ already determines $d$**: two distinct points cannot share $(O,A)$. Proof. Let $d\ne d'$ share $O$ and $A$ (the same events, hence the same pairwise-disjoint action events). Under the generic full-support procedure every leaf has positive probability, and $O$ is realized, so there are $O$-runs; recording at $d$ and at $d'$ makes every $O$-run pass exactly one $d$-node and exactly one $d'$-node, so on every $O$-path one is an ancestor of the other; the upper node is met on an $O$-run, hence subtree-veridical, so every leaf below it is an $O$-leaf and passes the lower node too; action-veridicality at both makes each such leaf-world satisfy both drawn actions; under full support a path with mismatched draws $a\ne b$ has positive probability, and its leaf-world would satisfy two disjoint events. Contradiction. $\square$ Toolkit: the sequential same-coordinate configuration fails recording at the upper point ("action $a$ drawn but leaf-world does not satisfy it; leaf-world satisfies undrawn action $b$"); the parallel configuration fails coverage at both. Consequence: ID-14's coarsening is **idle on the honest class** — its "cost: none" and "every worked example unchanged" are theorems, not inspections — and PC-6's fragility attack lives *entirely off* that class. This is the cleanest available answer to PC-6 and the notes should state it.
