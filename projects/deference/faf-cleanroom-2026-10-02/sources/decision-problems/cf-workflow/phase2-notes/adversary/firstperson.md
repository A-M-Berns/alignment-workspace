## 12. Closing

**The single most damaging objection.** The mandate's central deliverable — brief A's "audit $s$ against $s^\circ(\cdot\mid\mathrm{occ}(s))$; on mismatch trust $s^\circ$" as an *algorithm* — is not actually specified. In the §6 pseudocode the audit is decision-irrelevant at every coupled point ($d\in U$ goes to the cluster argmax whether or not it passes) and its failure at an honest point routes to an undefined step ($\pi^*(d)$ for $d\notin U$).
