## 1. ZO-12 — chance-determined $\Rightarrow$ prefix-route function $\Rightarrow$ observable when no fiber straddles a cell. **SURVIVES** (two precision notes).

Attack 1 (re-derivation). Lemma A: for $\varepsilon, \varepsilon'$ with one prefix route ending at the first $\geq 2$-action decision node $q_1$, the hybrid $\varepsilon''$ (= $\varepsilon$ on $T_a$'s coordinates, $\varepsilon'$ on $T_b$'s) gives $\pi_a\cdot\varepsilon'' = \pi_a\cdot\varepsilon$ and $\pi_b\cdot\varepsilon'' = \pi_b\cdot\varepsilon'$, so chance-determinedness bridges the cells. Coordinates of subtrees off the route are never consulted; the leaf case (route ends at a leaf) is trivial. Sound. Theorem: $\pi_f(d) := f(\mathrm{cell}(q))(d)$ is a well-defined pure policy under the hypothesis; every decision node on the run of $(\pi_f,\varepsilon)$ has $\varepsilon$'s prefix route, hence $\varepsilon$'s cell, so $\pi_f\cdot\varepsilon = f(\mathrm{cell}(\varepsilon))\cdot\varepsilon$ — exactly the conditional-policy witness. The proof never uses non-nesting, veridicality or coverage. Strong fairness $\Rightarrow$ hypothesis: a common leaf label below $q$ and $q'$ lies in both $\mathrm{cell}(q)$ and $\mathrm{cell}(q')$ (on a strongly fair tree every leaf below $q$ is reached through $q$, there being no nesting), and cells are disjoint. Sound.
Attack 2 (independent brute force, toolkit-free: `a2_zo12_random.py`). Random binary trees with lazy 2-outcome chance coordinates, reused points (nesting allowed) and coarse outcome labels; for every 2-cell partition of $W$ that is column-determined I checked Lemma A (cell a function of the prefix route), the theorem (no straddle $\Rightarrow$ conditional policies exist), and fairness $\Rightarrow$ no straddle. Result: 3550 trees (1647 with nested fibers), 1358 column-determined partitions, **0 Lemma-A failures, 0 theorem failures**; 100 of the no-straddle partitions live on nested trees and were observable (the theorem exercised beyond strong fairness); 0 strongly fair trees with a straddling fiber, 0 with an unobservable column-determined partition.
Precision notes for the statement: (i) $\mathrm{cell}(q)$ is well defined only for nodes at or below the first $\geq 2$-action decision node of their path — single-action points on the prefix route must be excluded from the straddle hypothesis (they are forced anyway); (ii) the sweep's "chance-determined partitions exist only on trees with a chance-only prefix" is Lemma A's corollary and is fine. Verdict: SURVIVES; the cf-frontier handoff (open problem 1 true with "no fiber straddles a cell", fairness sufficient) stands.
