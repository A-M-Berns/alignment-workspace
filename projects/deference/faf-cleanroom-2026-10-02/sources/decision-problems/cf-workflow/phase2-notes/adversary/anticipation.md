## 2. Claim 2 — AN-3 / AN-4 / AN-5: occurrence-expressibility as Q3's criterion. **WOUNDED** (sufficiency survives; the converse and the "genuinely different" dichotomy are killed).

*What survives.* AN-3(i): if $\mathrm{occ}(d)=\lambda^{-1}(\mathrm{Occ}_d)$ mod $\mu$-null, then $\{\lambda\models X\}\cap\mathrm{occ}(d)=\{\lambda\models X\wedge\mathrm{Occ}_d\}$ mod null, so both Def-13 clauses at $d$ coincide with both Def-8 clauses at the relabelled point $(s_d,\mathrm{Occ}_d,A_d)$, and Prop 3 is the case $\mathrm{Occ}_d=O_d$. *What dies: the converse.* The notes assert (AN-3(ii)) that when $\mathrm{occ}(d)$ is not expressible "$\lambda_*\mu(\cdot\mid\mathrm{occ}(d))$ is not $\nu(\cdot\mid X)$ for any $X$ in general", call the instance-blind configuration "the only home of a genuinely non-OC SSC" (AN-4), and conclude "if $\mathrm{occ}(d)$ is not expressible … SSC is genuinely a different notion" (AN-5, bullet 2). Three trees [checked, `a1_expressibility.py`]:

(i) *The notes' own witness* (root coin; left a $d$-node with both answers leading to leaves carrying the single world $w_0$, payoffs $1$ and $0$; right the leaf $(w_0,0)$; $C=$ answer 1). Non-expressible. Per-run SSC state: $P=\delta_{w_0}$, $V(w_0)=\mathbb E_\mu[r\mid\mathrm{occ}(d)]=1$. Strict OC at the relabelled observation $\top$: $P=\nu=\delta_{w_0}$ — *the same*, which the notes report — but $V(w_0)=\mathbb E_\mu[r]=\tfrac12$. So in the notes' own example the two notions agree on the $P$-clause and differ only on the $V$-clause, i.e. by Remark 3.5's non-supervenient payoffs, not by anything anthropic. The notes discuss only $P$.

(ii) *Same tree, right leaf payoff $1$* (payoffs now supervene on worlds). Non-expressible ($w_0$ is realized on both sides), yet per-run SSC $=(\delta_{w_0},1)$ $=$ strict OC at $\top$ in **both** clauses. The dichotomy "non-expressible $\Rightarrow$ genuinely different" is false.

(iii) *Left $d$-node → $(c{=}0,\,1)$ / $(c{=}1,\,0)$, right leaf $(c{=}0,\,0)$, $C=(\tfrac12,\tfrac12)$.* $\nu=(\tfrac34,\tfrac14)$, per-run SSC $P=(\tfrac12,\tfrac12)$; no $X\in\mathcal E$ has $\nu(\cdot\mid X)=P$ (ratios $\tfrac23$ and $2$). A genuinely non-OC SSC exists — and it is also non-expressible. So non-expressibility is compatible with both outcomes; it decides nothing.

*The exact criterion* (verified on (i)–(iii) [checked, `a1_expressibility.py` §(iv)]): the per-run SSC $P$-state equals $\nu(\cdot\mid X)$ for some $X\in\mathcal E$ **iff** $\lambda_*\mu(\cdot\mid\mathrm{occ}(d))(w)/\nu(w)$ is constant over the state's support (then $X$ = that support); the $V$-clause additionally matches iff, for every world $w$ realized on occ-leaves, the $\mu$-mean payoff over the occ-leaves of $w$ equals the $\mu$-mean over all leaves of $w$. Expressibility implies both because no world straddles the two sides — that is the true content of AN-3. The criterion is *statistical* (about $\mu$ and $r$), not structural; the notes' "what I could not settle 1" asked for a structural iff, and there is none to find.

*Two smaller points.* AN-3(iii)'s structural sufficient condition (chance-only or action-recorded ancestors) does not cover TN-V1 at $d_E$ — the root is an unrecorded hypothetical decision edge — so on the flagship battery expressibility is established by inspection, not by the condition (the notes concede this in item 1 of their unsettled list; AN-5 does not). The proposed amendment 1 is carefully worded — "adds a genuinely new proposition … only when some world is realized both on runs that consult $d$ and on runs that do not" is a statement about the *ontology* and stands — but its gloss "SSC is genuinely a different notion" in AN-5 must not be carried into v2.

## 7. Claim 7 — AN-21 / AN-22 / AN-23 / AN-25: the stage-indexed prior.

**AN-22 KILLED.** Claim: with common information $\overline C=\mathcal E_{t_0}$, $\mathbf c=\mathrm{id}$, $\mathbf c'=\pi_{t_0,t'}$ (the deductive quotient killing digit$=7$), the stage transition $P_{t_0}\to P_{t'}=P_{t_0}(\cdot\mid X_3)$ "is a $\hat C$-compatible conditioning model iff $P_{t_0}(X_3)>0$, with $B=1/P_{t_0}(X_3)$". Refutation [checked, `a3_stage_compat.py`]: SC Def 2.3's (Compat) is the equality of homomorphisms $\overline C\to\overline L$, $\mathbf p\circ\mathbf c=\mathbf p'\circ\mathbf c'$, for **every** $\overline c\in\overline C$. Even a.e.-compatibility (SC §0.3) fails, since $\mathbf L(\mathbf p(X_7)\,\triangle\,\bot)=P_{t_0}(X_7)>0$. A brute-force search over all $(\mathbf p,\mathbf p')$ pairs on enlarged spaces of $\le6$ atoms finds no compatible pair whose $\mathbf p$ hits a digit-7 world. Running SC Thm 2.4's construction literally on this instance shows where it breaks: the $\mathbf c'$-fibers over the digit-7 atoms are **empty**, so the residual mass $\tfrac{B-1}{B}\mathbf C''$ has nowhere to go and $\mathbf p_*\mathbf L$ falls short of $P_{t_0}$ by exactly $P_{t_0}(X_7)$ ($\tfrac12,\tfrac9{10},\tfrac1{10}$ at $P_{t_0}(X_3)=\tfrac12,\tfrac1{10},\tfrac9{10}$). This is the "empty-fiber" gap the sibling's `cleanup-audit-2026-08-05.md` §7 lists for Thm 2.4 — the notes' header acknowledges the gap ("both touched below") and AN-22 then relies on the theorem inside it without a word.
