## 12. Closing items

**The single most damaging objection.** The thread's mandate was C2 — is the translation's virtue a universal property? — and its verdict (UN-9) is stated as a completed dichotomy: "the reflection exists exactly where it is empty and fails to exist where it would be informative". Neither half is established. Existence at the mixed grade is left open by the notes themselves (UN-3(ii) "if it exists", minimality [guess]); non-existence in the CF-native (lazy) $\mathbf{Chu}$ is not shown — UN-6 excludes only the *relocation* as unit, and the un-commit tree $\mathrm{Hon}_d$ (fair, `s7`'s own two-point tree) receives a strict Chu morphism from $\mathrm{Fr}(B_1)$, so $B_1$ does have a unit-shaped map to a fair object there and the reflection question is live, with a concrete candidate. The honest C2 verdict is narrower than UN-9: "every reflection established so far is trivial; the informative cases are open".

**The one thing to tell the synthesis agent not to repeat.** Do not repeat "UDT's extra cost is exactly the off-observation mass that makes $O_d$ a pseudo-observation / is nonzero exactly when some node simulates $d$" (UN-11(ii), UN-11(iv), amendment 1).
