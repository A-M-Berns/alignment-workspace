## Definitions carried (restated so the ledger stands alone)

- **Interaction models.** (a) Repeated independent episodes of one problem $B$: each episode is a Def-6 run under the learner's current procedure $C_t$, updated between episodes — the home of every claim below unless said otherwise (Remark 5.4's frequency reading). (b) Family model: episodes draw $B\sim\pi$ from a family; the learner sees only the point (Def 4). (c) Within-run repetition = self-succession (nested fibers), a property of $B$, not of learning.
- **Credit scheme $\kappa$** — which consultations $(d,a)$ an episode's return updates: $\mathrm{ev}$: iff the leaf-world satisfies $a\wedge O_d$ (Def 8's event; FR-11's tremble-EDT read dynamically); $\mathrm{occ}$: every consultation on the path (per-occurrence, SIA; under independent redraws the Monte-Carlo estimate of Theorem 1's $\sum_q R_qG_q(C,a)/\mathbb E[\#_d]$, P1 item 15); $\mathrm{dev}$: one credit per run to "my policy answered $a$ at $d$" (per-run, SSA; the shared-seed learner; estimates $V_B(C[d\mapsto a])$, Theorem 2 / Def 22, under P1 item 17's hypothesis); $\mathrm{pol}$: whole procedures as arms (finite bandit over pure procedures) or policy gradient on behavioural parameters.
- **Learner classes $\mathcal L_\kappa$ (repaired).** Episodic Monte-Carlo control with sample-average estimates credited by $\kappa$, split into **greedy members** ($\varepsilon$-greedy: the played policy is $(1-\varepsilon_t)\cdot$greedy $+\ \varepsilon_t\cdot\mathrm{Unif}$, i.e. Def 10's tremble of the greedy procedure) and **smooth members** (softmax with temperature $\tau_t\to0$; policy gradient / REINFORCE). $\mathcal L_{\mathrm{pol}}$ contains finite bandits over pure procedures and policy gradient.
- **Visitation hypothesis (the repaired "GLIE").** Exploration $\to0$ **and every $(d,a)$ is visited infinitely often a.s.** Sufficient: $\sum_t\varepsilon_t^{k}=\infty$ with $k$ the maximal number of decision nodes on a root-to-leaf path; or per-point count schedules $\varepsilon(d)=c/n(d)$ [reconstructed: Singh–Jaakkola–Littman–Szepesvári 2000, whose GLIE is a *visitation* property]. The bare pair "$\varepsilon_t\to0$, $\sum_t\varepsilon_t=\infty$" suffices only at decision-depth 1 (DY-9, DY-13). Optimistic initialization guarantees one visit per pair, not infinitely many.
- **Three senses of "converges".** (G) the greedy policy's limit; (T) the time-average per-occurrence frequency; (S) a smooth member's policy/parameter limit (its rest point as $\tau\to0$). Every statement below names its sense; the thread's headline definition used (G).
- **$\mathcal L$-learning-fair** (sense named): every learner in $\mathcal L$ satisfying the visitation hypothesis converges, in the named sense, to a $V_B$-optimal procedure ($T_{\mathrm{opt}}$, Defs 18/21). Coherence sense: global optimum (type-level coherence is automatic for any Def-4 procedure; Def 22's fixed-point set has bad members, P1 item 25).
- **Static shadow $S_\kappa(B)$**: the rest points of $\kappa$'s best-response map at $\varepsilon\to0$. $S_{\mathrm{ev}}$ = the tremble-EDT-consistent set in test-sequence form (DY-3); $S_{\mathrm{occ}}$ = Theorem 1's fixed points; $S_{\mathrm{dev}}$ = Def-22-coherent procedures with deviations over $\Delta(A_d)$ (P1 item 25's repair); $S_{\mathrm{pol}}$ for policy gradient = the local maxima of $V_B$ on the product of simplices — **not** $T_{\mathrm{opt}}$ in general.
