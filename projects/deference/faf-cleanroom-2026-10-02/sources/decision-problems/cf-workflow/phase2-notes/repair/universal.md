### Definitions (as the synthesis should use them)

**UN-A (marked problem).** A pair $(B,M)$: a concrete decision problem (Definition 5, evented chance) with a set $M$ of algebra coordinates declared disposition stamps (the fresh $\mathrm{pol}_d$ of P1:fair-repair R1). Fairness of $(B,M)$ is strong structural fairness with $M$ exempted (Claim B's sense). $\mathrm{Rel}_U(B,M):=(\mathrm{Rel}_UB,\;M\cup\{\mathrm{pol}_d\}_{d\in U})$.

**UN-B (graded law preorders).** $\mathrm{Law}_\gamma(B)$ = set of unmarked-coordinate joint world–payoff laws realized at grade $\gamma\in\{\mathrm{pure},\mathrm{mix},\mathrm{corr}\}$; $B\preceq_\gamma B'$ iff $\mathrm{Law}_\gamma(B)\subseteq\mathrm{Law}_\gamma(B')$; $\cong_{\mathrm{pure}}$ is P1:fair-repair §2.1's $\sim$. Thin categories; a reflection of $B$ into Fair is a least fair element of $B$'s up-set (unique up to $\cong_\gamma$). Remark [derived, definitional]: an arbitrary mixture of pure profiles has the mixture of their laws, so $\mathrm{Law}_{\mathrm{corr}}(B)=\mathrm{conv}\,\mathrm{Law}_{\mathrm{pure}}(B)$ and $B\cong_{\mathrm{pure}}B'\Rightarrow B\cong_{\mathrm{corr}}B'$ — the correlated grade is exactly as location-blind as the pure grade; only the mixed grade (independent redraws) separates.

**UN-C (Chu category of marked problems).** Morphisms $B\to B'$ are Chu morphisms $(g,h)$ between $\mathrm{Fr}(B)\to\mathrm{Fr}(B')$ (CF paper Definition 2: $g$ forward on pure policies, $h$ backward on chance profiles, $\pi\cdot h(\varepsilon')=g(\pi)\star\varepsilon'$), worlds coarsened by the stamps (CF-24). The environment convention is a *parameter*, not CF's choice: **lazy** (phase-1 CF-1: one coordinate per chance node of the tree) or **identified** (P1:adversary-repair D.2: one coordinate per original chance node, copies read it). $\mathbf{Chu}$ strict; $\mathbf{Chu}/\!\simeq$ up to homotopy (Definition 36), iso = biextensional equivalence.

**UN-D (data cost of UDT at $d$).** $B$ almost fair, $C$ a full-support procedure, $\hat d$ the relocated point of $d$'s scope-component at its LCA, $\hat s$ its strictly OC-calibrated state at an interior mixture (masked at the self-model where strictness is self-certain, Remark 3.6): $\mathrm{Cost}(B,d):=\big(P_{\hat s}(\neg O_d),\,(P_{\hat s},V_{\hat s})|_{\neg O_d}\big)$; proxy: number of $\hat s$-atoms outside $O_d$. Its support has **two components** (UN-11(ii)): simulation branches (nodes querying $d$ where $O_d$ fails) and uncovered off-observation branches of the scope (no $d$-node at all). Whether UN-D should be restricted to the covered scope is Open item 8.
