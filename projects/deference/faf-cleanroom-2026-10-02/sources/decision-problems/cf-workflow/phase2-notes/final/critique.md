## 0. Verdict in one paragraph

The mathematics I could spot-check holds, the sampled source paragraphs support the sentences built on them, no DO-NOT-USE toolkit function was called anywhere in the run, and the defensible-claims ledger's Lean lines match the lean-verify report clause by clause. The defects are of three kinds: (a) the three final documents were written in parallel and never reconciled — they disagree about whether Lean verification happened at all; (b) the synthesis states a rule about UNREVIEWED lemmas that its own headline items break, and drops scope in exactly the places the epistemic-discipline policy says scope must travel (the decay verdict, the tremble device's name, the rivalry claim's construal); (c) one mandate deliverable (the faithful thread's Q6 verdict, a correction to v2's Remark 3.11) reached the amendments and the ledger but never the synthesis. Underneath all three sits a systemic fact the finals state but do not headline: every [checked] mark in this run is an AI recomputation of an AI calculation, zero of roughly 140 KILLED/WOUNDED verdicts were contested, and the vetted-by-the-author bit is unset everywhere.

## 3. The single most important thing the author should not believe on the basis of this run

**That the run has shown "EDT = UDT on fair problems" in the sense of [scrubbed] — or that anything in it has been verified by a person.** What the run established is narrower and the finals say so, but a reader caching headlines will carry away the slogan. The theorem (A36 / L4 / item 15) is: on $\mathfrak F$ — strongly fair, recording at every queried point for every procedure, pruned chance, every queried observation realized — *event-tremble*-EDT-consistency implies $V$-optimality, as a **strict inclusion**, under **independent redraws**. Every untrembled EDT (strict, masked under either reading of Definition 9, limit), Definition-22 coherence, Theorem 1 and Theorem 2 approve a dominated profile *even on* $\mathfrak F$ (the threat tree; item 17, L4); on the almost-fair class even tremble-EDT approves the Stag Hunt's $(H,H)$ (L9); on the fully evented honest class every fair fiber is a singleton, so "identical subjective states must have equivalent subtrees" does no work there and the perfect-copy case lives only on instance-blind chance, where Seam 4's settling calculation "was not run" (synthesis §7); the fair form of an unfair problem preserves pure-profile laws (or the Definition-6′ law), not the AMD's $\tfrac43$, and is not canonical inside an entangled component (items 8, 12, UN-5); and the individuation question — is touched by no theorem (Seam 1, D5). Finally: "Vetted-by-the-author: ☐" on all 28 ledger entries is the truth about every [checked] in this run.
