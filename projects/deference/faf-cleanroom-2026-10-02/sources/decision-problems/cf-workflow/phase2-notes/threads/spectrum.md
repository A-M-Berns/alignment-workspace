## 0. Setup: what a partial repair is

Throughout, $B$ is a concrete decision problem (Def 5) that is **almost fair** (no fiber nested; every point consulted at most once per run) with (EC) and (FRec) at every point that gets relocated; where a hypothesis is dropped I say so. Two standing facts from phase 1 that everything below builds on: relocation (P1:fair-repair R1/R2/E2) is value- and law-preserving at all grades exactly on almost-fair inputs (FR-6, FR-7; adversary-repair Claim F), and the output is strongly fair only in the pre-enrichment-marginal sense (Claim B). Nested fibers are outside the poset's domain at the mixed grade (FR-7(c): no fair tree matches the AMD's mixed value function); at the pure grade every construction below still preserves the law (FR-7(a)), and the shared-seed semantics of Q12 would make nested fibers mixed-grade relocatable (P1:synthesis open problem 4 — **seeds** thread).

**Definition SP-D1 (relocation datum, partial repair).** A *relocation datum* on $B$ is a pair $r = (U_r, t_r)$: a set $U_r$ of queried points and, for each $u \in U_r$, a *target* node $t_r(u)$ that is an ancestor-or-equal of $\mathrm{lca}(F_u)$. Its output $\mathrm{Rel}_r(B)$ is obtained by two elementary moves, applied top-down:

- **(R) relocate at the lca**: P1's joint product relocation of a set at $\mathrm{lca}(\bigcup F_u)$ — a new node $\hat q$ with action set $\prod_u A_u$ realized as pol-coordinate events, every $U$-node resolved and stamped, $O_{\hat d}$ := the upstream context of the site (conjunction of the EC chance events and, if a decision node lies above, its action event — the toolkit's `close='force'` behaviour; E2 proper adds those decision nodes to $U$ instead, §1.4);
- **(H) hoist**: move a relocated node one level up, above its parent. For a chance parent, $\mathrm{Ch}\big(b_1 : \mathrm{Dec}(\hat d, \{a : S_a\});\ b_i : T_i\big) \;\mapsto\; \mathrm{Dec}\big(\hat d', \{a : \mathrm{Ch}(b_1 : S_a;\ b_i : T_i[\mathrm{pol} := a])\}\big)$, where $T_i[\mathrm{pol} := a]$ stamps the disposition into leaf-worlds that never consult the point (a "would-have" record) and $O_{\hat d'}$ is the parent's upstream context (one event fewer); for a decision parent $e$ likewise, with $\hat d'$ and $e$ commuting. Two relocated nodes that arrive at one position merge into their joint product node. Implemented as `sp_common.hoist`.

Points with the same target are relocated jointly; a relocated node copied into the branches of an outer relocated node is E1-refined (P1:fair-repair Exit E1) or, equivalently up to $\sim$ (P1 §2.1), merged into the outer product. The **null repair** is $(\emptyset, -)$; the **LCA repair** is $r_{\mathrm{LCA}} := (\mathrm{cl}_f(\mathrm{unfair}(B)),\ \mathrm{lca})$ with $\mathrm{cl}_f$ the fairness closure of SP-D3; the **top repair** is $(\mathcal D_B, \mathrm{root})$ — every queried point relocated to the root, i.e. one decision node with action set $\prod_d A_d$ (the deterministic procedures) and pure chance below: the *full normal form*.

**Definition SP-D2 (the order).** $r \le r'$ ("$r'$ relocates more") iff $U_r \subseteq U_{r'}$ and, for every $u \in U_r$, $t_{r'}(u)$ is an ancestor-or-equal of $t_r(u)$. This is the product, over points, of the chains $\{\text{not relocated}\} < \mathrm{lca}(F_u) < \cdots < \mathrm{root}$ — a finite distributive lattice with bottom the null repair and top the full normal form.

**Definition SP-D3 (fairness closure vs E2 closure).** Given $(U, t)$, say $e \notin U$ is *exposed* if some $e$-node lies strictly inside a relocated scope (strictly below some $t(u)$) and has a $U$-node strictly below it. The *fairness closure* $\mathrm{cl}_f(U)$ is the least superset of $U$ with no exposed point (recomputed as points are added; targets of added points := their lca). P1's **E2 closure** $\mathrm{cl}_{E2}(U)$ adds $e$ whenever *any* $e$-node has a $U$-node strictly below it — including $e$-nodes above the site, which are never copied. So $\mathrm{cl}_f(U) \subseteq \mathrm{cl}_{E2}(U)$, strictly in general (SP-6).

**Definition SP-D4 (survives; licensed).** A chance node $n$ *survives* the repair $r$ for the point $d$ if in $\mathrm{Rel}_r(B)$ the node carrying $d$'s component is strictly below $n$ (so the component is chosen knowing $n$'s outcome). A chance-determined event $X$ — an EC edge-event, or a union of edge-events of one coordinate — is *licensed for $d$ in $B$* if every $d$-node lies below an $X$-edge: possibilistically, every leaf below every $d$-node satisfies $X$ (P1:cf-correspondence CF-6's no-simulation hypothesis $(V_d)$ with $O_d$ replaced by $X$). "Survives" is a property of the repair; "licensed" is a property of $B$ alone.

Induct on the number of moves. $\square$

### 1.3 Interacting fibers: the locality lemma and the least fair repair

**SP-4 (locality lemma) [derived; checked — `sp1_poset.py`].** Let $r = (U, t)$ with FRec at every $u \in U$, and $e \notin U$. Then $F_e$ is strongly fair in $\mathrm{Rel}_r(B)$ (pre-enrichment sense) **iff** $F_e$ is fair in $B$ **and** $e$ is not exposed (SP-D3).

*Proof.* Members of $F_e$ outside every relocated scope are untouched. A member inside a scope with no $U$-node below it is copied verbatim into every branch of the enclosing relocated node(s); the copies differ only in pol-stamps, which the pre-enrichment sense exempts, so the copies are isomorphic to the original and to each other. Hence if no member is exposed, the output fiber's pairwise-isomorphism relation is that of $B$'s. If some member $q_e$ is exposed, its copies in different branches are resolved differently at the $U$-node below; FRec at that point makes the leaf-worlds below the two resolutions entail disjoint act-events (action-veridicality), so the copies are non-isomorphic, and the output $F_e$ is unfair. $\square$

*Spread.* The number of copies of $e$ is $\sum_{q_e} \#\{\text{branches } \alpha \text{ of the enclosing joint node in which } q_e \text{ survives resolution}\}$; on the ping-pong tree relocating $d$ alone takes $F_e$ from 3 nodes to 4 copies (the two Left members each survive in one $\hat d$-branch, the Right member in both), all four pairwise non-isomorphic [checked: `unfair_fibers` = `['e']`], and the naive iteration never terminates (P1 FR-3 reproduced: trace $d, e, \hat d, \hat e, \hat{\hat d}, \hat{\hat e}$). The three-sided gated mugging exhibits the other half: the singleton $e$ on the inner coin's third face is copied into both $\hat d_g$-branches, is fair with pol exempted and unfair without (Claim B, reproduced) [checked].

**SP-5 (LCA relocation is the least fair repair) [derived].** Assume FRec at every point of $B$. For a relocation datum $r$: $\mathrm{Rel}_r(B)$ is strongly fair (pre-enrichment sense) **iff** $U_r \supseteq \mathrm{unfair}(B)$ and $U_r$ has no exposed point. Consequently $r_{\mathrm{LCA}} = (\mathrm{cl}_f(\mathrm{unfair}(B)), \mathrm{lca})$ is fair-outputting, and it is the **least** element of the poset among fair-outputting repairs: $r$ fair-outputting $\Rightarrow r_{\mathrm{LCA}} \le r$; moreover the fair-outputting repairs form an up-set among closed data.

*Proof.* ($\Leftarrow$) Relocated nodes are singleton fibers; survivors are fair by SP-4 (they were fair in $B$ since $\mathrm{unfair}(B) \subseteq U_r$, and are unexposed). ($\Rightarrow$) An unfair $F_e$ with $e \notin U_r$ stays unfair (SP-4, verbatim copies or unchanged), and an exposed $e$ is unfair in the output (SP-4). Leastness: $r$ fair-outputting gives $U_r \supseteq \mathrm{unfair}(B)$ and closedness under exposure *at $r$'s targets*; since $r$'s targets are ancestors-or-equal of the lca's, $r$'s scopes contain the lca-scopes, so $r$ is also closed under exposure at the lca's, hence $U_r \supseteq \mathrm{cl}_f(\mathrm{unfair}(B))$, and targets are $\preceq$ by definition. Up-set: if $r \le r'$, both closed, and $r$ fair-outputting, then every $e \notin U_{r'}$ is $\notin U_r$, hence fair in $B$ (SP-4 applied to $r$) and unexposed in $r'$ (closedness), so fair in $\mathrm{Rel}_{r'}(B)$. $\square$

**The FRec hypothesis is load-bearing [checked — `sp1_poset.py`, "without recording"].** Drop recording at a point $e$ whose query is *structurally spurious* (both children identical, acts unrecorded): root coin (instance-blind, un-evented) to two $d$-nodes; the Left $d$-node's $a$-edge leads to the spurious $e$-node, the Right's $a$-edge leads directly to the same leaf. $F_d$ is unfair (shapes differ). Relocating $e$ at its own node and hoisting $\hat e$ twice to the root yields a tree whose $d$-copies are all isomorphic (pol exempted): fair, value-preserving, **with $d$ un-relocated** — a fair-outputting repair incomparable with $r_{\mathrm{LCA}}$. So without recording there is no least fair repair; the two cures are FRec (which forbids act-unrecorded queries) or the **equiv** thread's quotient eliminating spurious queries first (P1 CF-7: frames and shadows already ignore them; the bisimulation of brief B PC-2 does *not* collapse a decision node with identical children unless a rule is added — handoff).

**SP-6 ($\mathrm{cl}_f \subsetneq \mathrm{cl}_{E2}$; what E2's surplus buys) [checked — `sp1_poset.py`, "look-then-mug"].** Root decision $e$: look $\to$ the mugging $B_1$ on $d$; leave $\to$ a leaf worth $\tfrac14$ (leaf-worlds record $e$'s act). $\mathrm{unfair}(B) = \{d\}$, $\mathrm{cl}_f(\{d\}) = \{d\}$ (the $e$-node is *above* the lca = the coin, never copied), $\mathrm{cl}_{E2}(\{d\}) = \{d, e\}$. The $\mathrm{cl}_f$-repair inserts $\hat d$ between $e$ and the coin with $O_{\hat d} = \{\mathrm{act}_1 = \mathrm{look}\}$: strongly fair, value-preserving for symbolic $(q, r)$, and **records at $\hat d$** (Definition 7 — coverage holds because the ancestor's act is recorded, so FR-8's bridge and Definition 8 calibration at $\hat d$ apply). E2's joint root relocation of $\{d, e\}$ is also fair and value-preserving but strictly above it in the poset. What E2's surplus buys is *canonicity*: chance-only ancestors, $O_{\hat d}$ chance-determined, one node per entangled cluster, no sequencing choice — the **universal** thread's concern (its item 2 "least relocation that makes action-level = policy-level hold": the honest least element is $r_{\mathrm{LCA}}$ with $\mathrm{cl}_f$, and the canonical E2 form is a strictly larger element of the same poset; both are $\sim$-equivalent to $B$).

### 1.4 The refined relocation (brief B PC-3) vs the bare product

**SP-7 [derived].** PC-3's action set — "conditional policies on the CF-observable events between the LCA and the fiber" — is extensionally the bare product $\prod_{u \in U} A_u$ over the jointly relocated points, in the following exact sense. (i) Two members of one fiber are indistinguishable by definition (same $(s, O, A)$), so no event between the lca and the fiber separates *them*; an event that separates *different* relocated points $u \ne u'$ is already conditioned on by the product (one coordinate per point). (ii) A conditional policy on a chance event $X$ between the lca and the fiber that no relocated point registers either hands the agent information the description denied (illegitimate as a repair) or is inert: if a component is exercised only below $X$-edges, its $\neg X$-value never reaches a payoff, and the product already contains the exercised part (SP-11's straddle tree is the instance: the joint node's $d$-component is used only under $X$; a conditional "$a$ if $X$, $a'$ if $\neg X$" adds an inert coordinate; value preserved [checked]). Every result of this file holds for both versions; where they differ it is by inert coordinates, removed by P1 §2.1's reduction. The universal thread should nonetheless prefer the product presentation at the least element $r_{\mathrm{LCA}}$: conditional policies on *upstream* observables are what hoisting **loses**, not what relocation adds (§2).

**SP-8 (scope).** Everything in §1 is at the product-mixed grade on almost-fair $B$. For nested fibers only the pure grade transfers (FR-7(a)); the mixed-grade obstruction is the AMD's $\tfrac43$ vs $1$ (P1 FR-7(c), rechecked in SP-14). Under shared-seed run semantics (Q12) the obstruction disappears (the AMD's shared-seed value is capped at 1 — P1:synthesis item 4), so the poset extends to all finite trees exactly when run semantics is shared seed — **seeds** thread.

## 2. Licensed updates (mandate item 2)

### 2.1 The tree side

**SP-9 (the chain at ancestors of the lca) [derived; instances checked — `sp2_licensed.py`].** Let $n$ be a chance node that is an ancestor-or-equal of $\mathrm{lca}(F_d)$, with edges $b_1, \dots, b_k$ and EC edge-events $X_{b_j}$. The following are equivalent: (i) $n$ survives $r_{\mathrm{LCA}}$ for $d$ (equivalently: survives every repair whose target for $d$ is at or below $n$'s child); (ii) $n \prec \mathrm{lca}(F_d)$ strictly; (iii) all $d$-nodes lie below one edge $b$ of $n$ — $X_b$ is licensed for $d$; (iv) [with FRec at $d$] for every other edge $b' \ne b$ the leaf law below $b'$ is independent of $C(d)$ — "conditioning on $X_b$ leaves the other branches' policy-outcomes unchanged"; and (i)–(iv) imply (v) $\partial V_B / \partial C(d)$ is supported in the $b$-subtree — "$V$ factorizes across $n$ with respect to $d$" — with (v) $\Rightarrow$ (iv) only under payoff-relevance.

*Proof.* (i)$\Leftrightarrow$(ii): move (R) inserts $\hat q$ directly above the lca, so exactly the strict ancestors of the lca stay above $\hat q$. (ii)$\Leftrightarrow$(iii): the lca is the deepest node above every member; a strict ancestor has all members below one child, and conversely if all members are below one edge then $n$ is not the deepest such node. (iii)$\Rightarrow$(iv): no $d$-node below $b'$, and Definition 6's law below $b'$ consults only points there. (iv)$\Rightarrow$(iii): a $d$-node below $b'$ with FRec stamps the drawn act into every leaf-world below it, so the law below $b'$ moves with $C(d)$. (iv)$\Rightarrow$(v) trivial. Failure of (v)$\Rightarrow$(iv): the $y = 0$ mugging — the heads branch's law depends on $C(d)$ through the transfer coordinate while its payoff does not [checked: $V(q) = -q/2$; $\mathrm{Loc}_d$ not powerless outside $T$; not observable]. $\square$

At the lca itself (a chance node — the mugging's coin, TN-V2's fill, glomarization's secret coin for $d_N$) the fiber straddles $\ge 2$ edges: the node is *crossed* by $r_{\mathrm{LCA}}$, and its edge-events are not licensed — this is the coupling.

### 2.2 The Cartesian-frame side

**SP-10 [derived from P1 CF-9/CF-10; instances checked — `sp2_licensed.py`, `sp5_glomar.py`, `sp6_extra.py`].** If $X$ is licensed for $d$ in $B$ then $\mathrm{Loc}_d(B)$'s agent is powerless outside $S_X$, hence $\{S_X, W \setminus S_X\}$ is observable in $\mathrm{Loc}_d(B)$ (Eight Definitions §2 conditional-policies definition; P1's dictionary CF-1/CF-5). P1 CF-10's proof transfers verbatim with $O_d$ replaced by $X$: for a fixed column $(\pi_{-d}, \varepsilon)$ the $a_0$- and $a_1$-runs coincide until a $d$-node is met; if none is met the outcomes agree; if one is met the leaf lies below a $d$-node, hence (licensed) in $S_X$ — the powerlessness antecedent is vacuous; then CF-9. Both converses are strict, exactly as in P1 CF-11: an inert simulation gives powerlessness without licensing, and the umbrella-mugging gives observability without powerlessness (rechecked: `truth_test`: observable True, powerless False, no-simulation False).

Checked table (column-determined / powerless outside / observable at $\mathrm{Loc}_d$): gated mugging, gate $\{c{=}1\}$ for $d_g$: T/T/T — licensed; coin $\{T\}$ for $d_g$: T/F/F — coupled; $\mathrm{Loc}_{d_2}$, gate: observable. Glomarization: secret partition at $\mathrm{Loc}_{d_S}$: T/T/T; at $\mathrm{Loc}_{d_N}$: T/F/F. TN-V1 $d_E$, fill: T/T/T; TN-V2 $d_E$: F/F/F (P1 CF-16 reproduced; both TN variants are nested at $d_F$, so they sit outside the almost-fair poset at the mixed grade — the licensing verdict is possibilistic and does not need almost-fairness). Decoupled mugging, coin: observable (P1 CF-15). Global frame $\mathrm{Fr}(B)$ of the gated mugging: gate observable, coin not (a CF-12-type instance: chance-only above the gate).

**SP-11 (survives $\Rightarrow$ licensed; not conversely; the gap is inert) [checked — `sp2_licensed.py`, "straddle"].** Since (R) and (H) only move decision nodes upward, a chance node strictly above $\hat q$ in the output is strictly above every $d$-node in $B$, so *survives $r_{\mathrm{LCA}}$ $\Rightarrow$ licensed*. The converse fails: root decision $e \in \{b_1, b_2\}$ (acts recorded); below $b_i$ a chance node $n_i$ with $X$ w.p. $\tfrac12$ leading to a $d$-node ($d = (\cdot, \{x{=}1\}, \{a, a'\})$, payoffs $(2, 0)$ under $b_1$ and $(0, 1)$ under $b_2$) and $\neg X$ leading to a leaf worth 0. $F_d$ is unfair (the two subtrees differ), $\mathrm{lca}(F_d)$ = the $e$-node, $X$ is licensed (every $d$-node is below an $X$-edge; $\mathrm{Loc}_d$: powerless outside $X$, observable), yet the least fair repair is the joint root node $\hat{d \times e}$ (the $e$-node is the site, copied and resolved differently, hence exposed), above both $X$-nodes: **fairness forces the un-conditioning of a licensed observation.** The crossing costs nothing — value preserved for symbolic mixed $(q, r)$, output fair — because the $d$-component is exercised only under $X$: brief B PC-3's conditional coordinate "$a$ if $X$, $a'$ if $\neg X$" is inert (SP-7). Two morals. (a) Brief A's "an observation survives the repair iff conditioning on it leaves the other branch's outcomes alone" holds only as $\Rightarrow$; the biconditional is with *licensing*, not with survival. (b) After the merge, $X$ is **not** observable at the joint node's local frame $\mathrm{Loc}_{\hat{d \times e}}$ [checked: membership depends on the $e$-component, which chooses which coin is tossed] — the frame-side test of a component's licensing must be run at that component's own local frame before merging (or after E1-refinement); the joint node is the wrong locus, P1 CF-13's lesson one level up (handoff: **cf-frontier**).

**SP-12 (the repair depth is frame-invisible) [checked — `sp2_licensed.py`].** With chance nodes identified across copies (P1:adversary-repair D.2's convention, `identify_chance=True`) and the pol-coordinates forgotten in outcomes: $\mathrm{Fr}(B) \simeq \mathrm{Fr}(\mathrm{Rel}_{\mathrm{LCA}} B) \simeq \mathrm{Fr}(\text{hoisted})$ biextensionally (all three collapse to $4 \times 3$); without identification the relocated frame collapses to $4 \times 5$ (D.2 reproduced) and the hoisted one to $4 \times 9$. At the relocated point, the gate stays observable in $\mathrm{Loc}_{\hat d_g}$ after the LCA repair and after the hoist (pol forgotten). On the *enriched* algebra (pol kept) the hoisted agent is powerless nowhere — its disposition is stamped into every leaf-world, so the gate is *not* observable there: licensing, like fairness (Claim B), must be read on the pre-enrichment algebra. Consequence: **CF-observability says whether conditioning is *permitted*; the position in the poset says whether it is *exercised*.** The spectrum is invisible to the frame — the frame-side face of P1's finding that CF forgets $\mu$ and $\beta$, and of Eight Definitions §4.4: at a true observation the additive (updateful) and multiplicative (updateless) factorizations coincide, which is exactly why hoisting over a licensed observation is value-idle (SP-1).

### 2.3 The boxed statement

> **SP-13 (licensed update).** Let $B$ be almost fair [needed only for mixed-grade repair claims], EC-evented, with $X$ an edge-event of one chance coordinate and $d$ a queried point; read all frames and fairness on the pre-enrichment algebra. **An update on $X$ is licensed for $d$ iff every occurrence of $d$ lies below an $X$-edge** — possibilistically, every leaf below every $d$-node satisfies $X$ (no simulation of $d$ outside $X$) [definition]. Then:
> (a) licensed $\iff$ the leaf laws of the $\neg X$-branches are independent of $C(d)$ [derived; $\Leftarrow$ needs FRec at $d$]; $\Rightarrow$ $\partial V_B/\partial C(d)$ is supported under $X$, with the converse only under payoff-relevance [checked witness $y = 0$];

### 4.1 The $\pi$-mixture mugging

**SP-19 [checked — `sp4_decay.py`; Lean candidate `spectrum-mixture-threshold.lean`].** $V_\pi(q) = \tfrac{q}{2}(\pi y - x)$, affine in the pay-rate $q$. Numerics ($x = 1, y = 3$): relocated-root EDT values for pay $-\tfrac18, 0, \tfrac14$ at $\pi = \tfrac14, \tfrac13, \tfrac12$ — refuse / uniform tie / pay.

**SP-20 (what moves with $\pi$) [checked].** The relocation site is the root for every $\pi \in (0, 1]$; the calibrated root state has $P_{\hat s}(\mathrm{couple}) = \pi$; the pay value is $\tfrac12(\pi y - x)$. So the three candidate movers separate: the **LCA location does not move** (it is fixed by the *support* of the coupling hypotheses, since the fiber is the union over hypotheses); the **branch weight at the relocated node moves continuously** ($= \pi$, entering the disposition's value linearly); the **verdict moves discretely** (threshold). Brief A's "as coupling-credence decays, the LCA slides back down toward the node itself" is false under Bayesian updating: no positive credence ever moves the lca; only at $\pi = 0$ does the hypothetical query become spurious — still unfair by labelled isomorphism (`unfair_fibers = ['d']`), but frame-licensed (the coin is observable at $\mathrm{Loc}_d$, P1 CF-15) and removable by the equiv thread's spurious-query quotient. ### 4.2 Brief B's split and the "$1/k$" claim

**SP-23 (dilution re-audit) [checked — `sp4_decay.py`; Lean candidate for the linear sum].** $k$-fold mugging (P1:verify E1/E2), $x = 1, y = 3$, per-node forcing differences $\Delta G_q := G_q(\mathrm{pay}) - G_q(\mathrm{refuse})$ with reach $R_q$:
- **linear** $b_j = j/k$: $\Delta G = -x$ at the real node ($R = \tfrac12$) and $y/k$ at every simulation node ($\tfrac32$ for $k = 2$, $1$ for $k = 3$), at every pay-rate; $\sum_q R_q \Delta G_q = \tfrac12(-x) + k \cdot \tfrac12 \cdot \tfrac{y}{k} = \tfrac{y - x}{2} = 1$ — **no dilution**: the $1/k$ per-instance pivotality is exactly cancelled by the $k$-fold anthropic (SIA) sum;
- **concave** $b_j = \mathbf 1[j \ge 1]$ at $q = 1$: $\Delta G = 0$ at every simulation (the others already pay), $\sum = -x/2$; the optimum is interior and **decreases in $k$**: $q^*(k) = 1 - (x/(yk))^{1/(k-1)}$ — $1, \tfrac56, \tfrac23, 1 - 12^{-1/3}$ for $k = 1, \dots, 4$ (`max_mixed`: maxima $1, \tfrac{25}{24}, \tfrac{10}{9}, 1 + 18^{1/3}/16$), a fading pay-rate that is the *optimum*, not a bug;
- **convex** $b_j = \mathbf 1[j = k]$ at $q = 1$: $\Delta G = y$ at every simulation, $\sum = \tfrac12(ky - x)$: SIA over-incentivizes; all-pay is ratifiable-not-optimal for $y < x < ky$ (SP-15).
So brief B PC-9's "the $1/k$ credit-assignment effect of instance-level evaluation" is mis-identified: (a) instance-level evaluation *with* the anthropic sum (Theorem 1 = tremble-EDT under independent redraws, P1 item 15) shows **no** $1/k$ under linear coupling; (b) what does show it is a single-instance CDT that locates itself at one node and forces only that node — the per-simulation $y/k$ without the sum (and $-x$ at the real node: it refuses regardless of $k$); (c) event-conditioned action-level evaluation (Prop 10's myopic functional = the null repair) discounts the other branch *totally* and $k$-independently — the face-value tails state sees $-x$ against $0$. P1 item 16's moral stands and is sharpened: dilution's sign is the coupling's curvature; a uniform $1/k$ is an artifact of dropping the anthropic sum.

**SP-24 (unification of the two briefs) [derived].** Brief A's "decay = repair shrinkage" and brief B's "legitimate decay = learning the coupling credence" name **one object** — the coupling weight $\pi$ in the relocated root's prior-calibrated state — under two descriptions, of which brief A's is wrong in detail (the repair *depth* is support-determined and does not shrink under Bayes; the *weight* does, SP-20) and brief B's is right. Unified vocabulary proposed: **coupling-credence decay** (legitimate; lives in the prior's weight; threshold verdict), **support decay** (depth; non-Bayesian or $\gamma$-graded; grounding), **event-conditioning discount** (the null repair's error; not a decay).
