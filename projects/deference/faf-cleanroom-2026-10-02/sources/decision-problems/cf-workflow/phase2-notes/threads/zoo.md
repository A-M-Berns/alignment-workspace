## 0. The grid (what a signature row records)

For a problem $B$ with a designated procedure $C$ (and states where the constructor supplies them), a signature row records:

- **structural** (tree only): strongly fair (every fiber pairwise labelled-isomorphic, P1:fair-repair FR-1); almost fair (no nested fiber); one-round (no decision node below another); FRec (records at every point for every procedure); veridical (every decision node subtree-veridical, Def 7); realized (every queried $O_d$ satisfied by some leaf-world — adversary-repair A.1's added hypothesis); and for the designated $C$: covers, records.
- **calibration** (states given): strict OC (Def 8), masked OC (Def 9, local non-dogmatic, toolkit reading of vacuity), limit OC (Def 10), per-run SSC, per-occurrence SSC (Def 13) — "ok" = every queried point calibrated or vacuous.
- **Cartesian frames** (tree only, every queried point): $\{S_{O_d}, \neg S_{O_d}\}$ observable in $\mathrm{Loc}_d(B)$; observable in $\mathrm{Fr}(B)$; agent powerless outside $S_{O_d}$ in $\mathrm{Loc}_d$; membership column-determined in $\mathrm{Loc}_d$ (P1:cf CF-8/9/10).
- **normative** (for $C$): $T_{\mathrm{EDT}}(C,B)$ at the given states (Def 18); tremble-EDT-consistency (FR-11's concrete event-conditioned form, states re-fitted per $\varepsilon$ — the toolkit's `tremble_edt_consistent`); $T_{\mathrm{opt}}$; Theorem-1 ratifiability at every point; Definition-22 coherence, pure and mixed (P1:synthesis item 25's amendment).

Each catalog entry gives: the tree (compact drawing, `Problem.show()` style), algebra and points, the signature entries that matter, the separating computation, and the encoding name in `zoo.py`.

### ZO-2 [derived + checked; `zoo.py::targets` (b)] — (b) limit-OC on a strongly fair recorded tree but not per-run SSC: only with an unrealized observation

**Coincidence theorem [derived].** Let $B$ be strongly fair, FRec (records at every point for every procedure), with zero-probability chance edges pruned, and let every queried observation be satisfied by some leaf-world (realized). Then for every $C$ and every queried $d$: limit-calibrated at $d$ ⇒ strictly OC-calibrated at $d$ ⇒ per-run SSC at $d$ ⇔ per-occurrence SSC at $d$.
Proof. Fix $d$. Some leaf-world satisfies $O_d$; under full-support $C^\varepsilon$ that leaf is reached with positive probability, so some $O_d$-run exists; recording makes the $d$-node it passes subtree-veridical; strong fairness (the fiber isomorphism preserves leaf-worlds) propagates subtree-veridicality to every member of $F_d$; coverage (part of recording) gives $\{\lambda \models O_d\} \subseteq \mathrm{occ}(d)$ a.s. So $\mathrm{occ}(d) = \{\lambda \models O_d\}$ a.s. for every procedure (Prop 3's hypotheses hold for every $C$), hence the per-run SSC equation and Def 8's clause 1 are the same equation and the $V$-clauses condition on the same event; where $\nu_C(O_d) = 0$ both are vacuous ($\mu(\mathrm{occ}(d)) \le \nu(O_d) = 0$). Lemma 2 gives limit ⇒ strict. Non-nesting (FR-1(i)) gives per-run = per-occurrence. $\square$
**Witness off the realized hypothesis** (adversary-repair A.1's tree, `single_node_unrealized`): one node $d = (s, \{\mathrm{coin{=}T}\}, \{a,b\})$, leaves $a \to (a,H)$ $r{=}0$, $b \to (b,H)$ $r{=}1$; $s = \tfrac12/\tfrac12$ with $V(a) = 7$. Strongly fair, records vacuously for every procedure, limit OC *vacuous* ($\nu_{C^\varepsilon}(O_d) \equiv 0$), per-run and per-occurrence SSC **violated** for $\delta_a$ ($\mu(\cdot \mid \mathrm{occ}(d)) = \delta_{(a,H)} \ne \tfrac12/\tfrac12$) and for $\delta_b$.
**Converse direction on the realized class** (per-run SSC ⇏ limit): the fantasy tree of ZO-1 — per-run SSC vacuous at $d_2$, limit violated. SSC as defined has no tremble discipline at null observations.

### ZO-5 [derived + checked; `zoo.py::targets` (e)] — (e) relocation cannot change the frame under the one-$E$-coordinate-per-original-chance-node convention

**Claim [derived].** For any tree $B$ and any $U$ (nested fibers included), with $E$ indexed by *original* chance nodes (copies identified) and outcomes read pre-enrichment ($(\lambda|_{\mathcal E}, r)$, the pol-coordinates projected out — CF-24's coarsening), $\mathrm{Fr}(\mathrm{Rel}_U(B))$ is the same matrix as $\mathrm{Fr}(B)$ up to renaming rows. Proof: rows are $\prod_d A_d$ on both sides ($A_{\hat d} = \prod_{d \in U} A_d$ times the survivors); for a pure profile $\pi$ and a chance profile $\varepsilon$ the relocated run follows $\pi$'s $U$-tuple at the root and then the copy of the scope with $U$-nodes resolved to those answers and chance copies consulting the identified coordinates — it lands on the copy of the very leaf the $B$-run lands on (FR-7(a): pure profiles are preserved always), whose $(\lambda|_{\mathcal E}, r)$ label is the original's. $\square$
[checked] on five relocations (mugging; Claim-B witness with generic payoffs; TN-V2 joint $\{d_F, d_E\}$ (nested); ping-pong joint; AMD (nested)): identified + projected frame equals $\mathrm{Fr}(B)$ in every case. Without identification (lazy independent copies) the mugging's frame collapses to $2 \times 4 \ne 2 \times 2$ (D.2), and likewise Claim-B ($4\times4$ vs $4\times2$) and ping-pong ($4 \times 16$ vs $4 \times 2$); TN-V2 and the AMD are unchanged even lazily (their chance nodes were already per-branch, resp. absent).
Consequence: the frame cannot see the mixed-grade preservation failure on nested fibers (FR-7(b): AMD $\tfrac43 \to 1$) — a fact about $\mu$, which the functor forgets (CF-3).

### ZO-6 [checked + derived; `zoo.py::targets` (f); Lean] — (f) Theorem-1-ratifiable but not Definition-22-coherent, and the converse, with nesting

**Nested 5-node tree AMD$(2;1,5)$** (`amd_variant`): node 1: $a \to 2$, $b \to$ node 2: $a \to 1$, $b \to 5$; one point. $V(q) = 2q + (1-q)(q + 5(1-q)) = 5 - 7q + 4q^2$. ### ZO-8 [checked + derived; `zoo.py::targets` (h)] — (h) the umbrella residue from a Newcomblike story

**Policy-menu mugging, heads-honest** (`policy_menu_mugging(False)`): root coin; one point $d = (\cdot, O_T, \{P, R, P{\leftrightarrow}T, P{\leftrightarrow}H\})$ — four *policy labels* (a coordinate `pol` the leaf-worlds do not record, exactly as post 11's $C_0$); tails node: $P, P{\leftrightarrow}T$ pay ($-x$), $R, P{\leftrightarrow}H$ refuse; heads node (where $O_T$ is *false*): Omega executes and rewards the **heads clause** — $P, P{\leftrightarrow}H$ pay and receive $y$ (net $y - x = 1$), $R, P{\leftrightarrow}T$ get $0$. $\mathrm{Loc}_d = \mathrm{Fr}$:

| | $H$ | $T$ |
|---|---|---|
| $P$ | $(H, \mathrm{pay}, 1)$, $+1$ | $(T, \mathrm{pay}, 0)$, $-1$ |
| $R$ | $(H, \mathrm{refuse}, 0)$, $0$ | $(T, \mathrm{refuse}, 0)$, $0$ |
| $P{\leftrightarrow}T$ | $(H, \mathrm{refuse}, 0)$, $0$ | $(T, \mathrm{pay}, 0)$, $-1$ |
| $P{\leftrightarrow}H$ | $(H, \mathrm{pay}, 1)$, $+1$ | $(T, \mathrm{refuse}, 0)$, $0$ |

The image of (tails outcome, heads outcome) is the full product, so $\{S_T, S_H\}$ is **observable** in $\mathrm{Loc}_d$ (post 11's $C_0$ matrix), membership column-determined, $\mathrm{Loc}_d \simeq \mathrm{Hon}_d$ (CF-21's truth test passes), yet the agent is **not powerless** outside $S_T$ and the heads node is **not subtree-veridical** (no-simulation fails): (iii) without (ii) and (i). Optimum $P{\leftrightarrow}H$ ($V = \tfrac12$). The no-doubt state ($P_s(T) = 1$) is blind to the heads half of its own action set: its EDT ties $R$ and $P{\leftrightarrow}H$ at $0$.
**Theorem [derived]: cross-branch coupling kills the residue.** On a one-point tree with a chance root and a $d$-node in each branch, write $\varphi_T, \varphi_H : A_d \to$ outcomes for the two nodes' edge maps. Observability of $\{S_T, S_H\}$ in $\mathrm{Loc}_d$ ⇔ $\mathrm{image}(\varphi_T \times \varphi_H) = \mathrm{image}\,\varphi_T \times \mathrm{image}\,\varphi_H$ (splices exist); powerlessness outside $S_T$ ⇔ $\varphi_H$ constant. If the heads outcome is a *function of the tails outcome* — Omega's payout determined by the tails clause, the standard coupling — the image is the graph of a function, and a graph is a product only if the function is constant: observability ⇔ powerlessness. So the umbrella residue requires the heads outcome to vary independently of the tails behaviour, i.e. an action set richer than the tails behaviour and an Omega that responds to the *heads clause*. [checked]: `policy_menu_mugging(True)` (Omega transfers $y$ iff the tails clause pays) collapses to the $2\times2$ mugging matrix — unobservable, $\mathrm{Loc}_d \not\simeq \mathrm{Hon}_d$.

### ZO-12 [derived + checked; `zoo.py::targets` (l)] — (l) chance-determined partitions on strongly fair trees are globally observable — the $\varepsilon$-determined forward direction holds, with weaker hypotheses

Setting: $\mathrm{Fr}(B)$ with lazy chance profiles (one coordinate per chance node, CF-1); a partition $V$ of $W = \mathrm{Image}$ is *chance-determined* if membership is a function of the column $\varepsilon$ alone (adversary-cf ADV-9's reading).
**Lemma A [derived].** On any finite tree, if $V$ is chance-determined then the cell of $\pi \cdot \varepsilon$ is a function of the *prefix route* $r(\varepsilon)$: the path from the root through chance nodes up to the first decision node with $\ge 2$ actions (or a leaf). Proof: for $\varepsilon, \varepsilon'$ with the same prefix route reaching $q_1$ with children $T_a, T_b$, let $\varepsilon''$ agree with $\varepsilon$ on $T_a$'s coordinates and with $\varepsilon'$ on $T_b$'s; then $\mathrm{cell}(\varepsilon) = \mathrm{cell}(\pi_a \cdot \varepsilon'') = \mathrm{cell}(\pi_b \cdot \varepsilon'') = \mathrm{cell}(\varepsilon')$, the outer equalities because the runs coincide, the middle one by chance-determinedness. $\square$ Corollary: if the root is a decision node, every chance-determined partition is trivial — which is why the relocated mugging (CF-14(c)) is not a counterexample and why the cell is decided *before any decision*.
**Theorem [derived].** If no fiber straddles two cells — i.e. for each $d$ all members $q \in F_d$ have the same cell $\mathrm{cell}(q)$ := the cell of every $\varepsilon$ reaching $q$ (well defined by Lemma A: $q$ fixes its prefix route) — then $V$ is observable in $\mathrm{Fr}(B)$: put $\pi_f(d) := f(\mathrm{cell}(q))(d)$; along the run of $(\pi_f, \varepsilon)$ every decision node has the cell $S = \mathrm{cell}(\varepsilon)$, so $\pi_f$ answers as $f(S)$ throughout and $\pi_f \cdot \varepsilon = f(S) \cdot \varepsilon$. Strong fairness implies the hypothesis: fiber members carry identical leaf $(\lambda, r)$-labels, all leaf outcomes below $q$ lie in $\mathrm{cell}(q)$ (each is reached by some run through $q$), and cells are disjoint. **Veridicality and coverage are not needed.** $\square$
[checked] sweep over the zoo (all $2$-cell partitions of $W$ for every tree with $|W| \le 12$): chance-determined partitions exist only on trees with a chance-only prefix (mugging, parallel-fiber, S3, CX2, tickle, policy-menu, umbrella: 2 each = one partition and its complement); on the strongly fair ones (CX2, tickle) all are observable; on the unfair ones they are unobservable exactly when a fiber straddles the cells (mugging, parallel-fiber, S3) and observable when the action set is policy-rich (policy-menu, umbrella). CX2's $\{O_1, \neg O_1\}$ (adversary-verify R4: fair, veridical, coverage fails, unobservable) is *not* chance-determined (column $c{=}2$ splits the rows), so it is not in the theorem's scope — consistent. Handed to `cf-frontier` (owner of open problem 1): the conjecture is true with the hypothesis "no fiber straddles a cell", of which strong fairness is one sufficient condition.

**ZO-14 [derived + checked; `xor_tree`] — globally observable but not locally observable.** Coin $c$; $c{=}1$ → $d$-node: $a \to e$-node ($u \to w_1$, $v \to w_2$), $b \to e$-node ($u \to w_2$, $v \to w_1$) — $d$ and $e$ XOR into the outcome; $c{=}2$ → $d$-node: $a \to w_3$, $b \to w_4$; $O_d = O_e = \{c{=}1\}$.
