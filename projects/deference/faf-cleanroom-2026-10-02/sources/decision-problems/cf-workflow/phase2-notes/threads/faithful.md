## 0. Definitions introduced

**Definition F1 (faithful policy interpretation).** Fix a concrete problem $B$, a *reference procedure* $C'$ (the self-model; full support where masked prior calibration is used), and a queried point $d$. A policy interpretation $\rho$ (Definition 17: $\rho_d(a) \in \mathcal E\setminus\{\bot\}$, possibly on an enrichment $\mathcal E' \supseteq \mathcal E$ of the algebra) is
- *law-faithful for $(B, C')$ at $d$* if for every $a \in A_d$: $\nu_{B,C'}(\rho_d(a)) > 0$ and the conditional joint law of $(\lambda|_{\mathcal E}, r)$ under $\mu_{B,C'}$ given $\lambda \models \rho_d(a)$ equals the joint law of $(\lambda|_{\mathcal E}, r)$ under the deviated run $\mu_{B, C'[d\mapsto a]}$ — "the $\rho_d(a)$-worlds track where the deviated runs land" (Q10's phrase), read at the level of the extensional shadow marginalized to the original algebra;
- *value-faithful* if only $\mathbb E_{\mu_{B,C'}}[\,r \mid \lambda \models \rho_d(a)\,] = V_B(C'[d\mapsto a])$ for every $a$;
- *uniformly faithful on a class* $\mathfrak B$ if faithful for every $B \in \mathfrak B$ (each with its own calibrated $s^\circ_B$) — the form Proposition 6's abstract problem $\{B_1, B_2\}$ needs, since $\rho$ is a parameter of the procedure, not of the problem.

Under Definition 11's masked variant with self-model $C'$ ($P_{s^\circ} = \nu_{B,C'}$, $V_{s^\circ}(X) = \mathbb E_{\mu_{B,C'}}[r \mid \lambda\models X]$), value-faithfulness says exactly $V_{s^\circ}(\rho_d(a)) = V_B(C'[d\mapsto a])$ — the prior's desirability of the disposition is the value of the deviated procedure. Law-faithfulness is what makes the same $\rho$ serve every payoff labelling of the tree.

**Definition F2 (the three intrinsic referents, with their two axes).** For a queried $d$, reference procedure $C$ and act $a$, the tree supplies candidate suppositions of $a$ (each a probability-and-desirability pair, Definition 2 clause 3):
- **R1 (all-instance deviation).** Law of $\mu_{B,C[d\mapsto a]}$, in three *weightings*: unconditioned (prior level, Def 11: value $V_B(C[d\mapsto a])$), occurrence-conditioned (per-run SSC: $\mathbb E_{\mu_{C[d\mapsto a]}}[r\mid \mathrm{occ}(d)]$, Theorem 2's evaluator — ranks like the unconditioned one by Lemma 1), and observation-conditioned (strict OC: $\nu_{C[d\mapsto a]}(\cdot\mid O_d)$, $\mathbb E_{\mu_{C[d\mapsto a]}}[r\mid O_d]$). As a supposition of the *act-event* $a$ it satisfies success only where the act is recorded on the conditioning event; as a supposition of a *disposition event* $\rho_d(a)$ it satisfies success by construction.
- **R2 (single-instance forcing).** $G_q(C,a)$ for $q \in F_d$ (§8), in three weightings over the fiber: per-occurrence / SIA, $\sum_q R_q G_q(C,a)$ (Theorem 1); *real-instance*, the reach-weighted average over the **action-veridical** instances (Definition 7: nodes whose draw is entailed by the leaf-world — "the instance whose draw is my act"); *subtree-veridical-instance*, the analogous average over subtree-veridical nodes.
- **R3 (tremble-induced evidential structure, Remark 3.9's third candidate).** $\mathrm{cf}^{\mathrm{tr}}_{s_d}(X) := \lim_{\varepsilon\to0^+}\big(\nu_{B,C^\varepsilon}(\cdot\mid X\wedge O_d),\ \mathbb E_{\mu_{B,C^\varepsilon}}[r\mid \cdot\wedge X\wedge O_d]\big)$ for every $X$ with $\nu_{B,C^\varepsilon}(X\wedge O_d) \not\equiv 0$ (a rational function of $\varepsilon$; the limit exists by finiteness, Definition 10), the trembles being Definition 10's $C^\varepsilon$ at every point; under the shared-seed semantics the same formula with $\mu'$.

The two axes are: (α) the *type* — deviation / forcing / tremble-evidential — and (β) the *weighting or conditioning event* — $\top$ (prior), $\mathrm{occ}(d)$ (per-run), $O_d$ (OC), reach-weights over all instances (SIA) or over veridical instances only. Remark 3.11 discusses (α); §3 below shows the verdicts are driven by (β) and by the run semantics.

**Definition F3 (act-recording).** $B$ is *act-recording at $d$ for $C$* if $\mu_{B,C}$-a.s. every $O_d$-run passes exactly one action-veridical $d$-node and the leaf-world's act coordinate is that node's draw. Weaker than Definition 7's recording (no "exactly one $d$-node", no subtree-veridicality): the opaque Newcomb tree of §3 is act-recording (the real node) but not recording (two $d$-nodes per run).

## 1. Q10, one point

Setting: Proposition 6's $B_1$ (fair coin; $T$: real query of $d$, pay $\to (T,\mathrm{pay},0)$ $r = -x$, refuse $\to (T,\mathrm{refuse},0)$ $r=0$; $H$: hypothetical query, pay $\to (H,\bot,1)$ $r = y$, refuse $\to (H,\bot,0)$ $r=0$; $0<x<y$), self-model $C'$ with $C'(\mathrm{pay}) = q'\in(0,1)$, $s^\circ$ masked-prior-calibrated under $C'$ (Definition 11's masked variant): $P_{s^\circ} = \big(\tfrac{q'}2,\tfrac{1-q'}2,\tfrac{1-q'}2,\tfrac{q'}2\big)$ on $\big((T,\mathrm{pay},0),(T,\mathrm{refuse},0),(H,\bot,0),(H,\bot,1)\big)$, atom values $(-x,0,0,y)$. Deviated laws: $\mu_{\delta_{\mathrm{pay}}} = \tfrac12(T,\mathrm{pay},0)+\tfrac12(H,\bot,1)$, $V = \tfrac{y-x}2$; $\mu_{\delta_{\mathrm{refuse}}} = \tfrac12(T,\mathrm{refuse},0)+\tfrac12(H,\bot,0)$, $V=0$. (Script `q10_one_point.py`.)

**FA-1 [derived] — what faithfulness must say.** With one queried point, $C'[d\mapsto a] = \delta_a$ for every $C'$, so law-faithfulness at $d$ reads: $\nu_{B,C'}(\cdot\mid\rho_d(a))$ (marginalized to $\mathcal E$) $= \nu_{B,\delta_a}$ and $\mathbb E_{C'}[r\mid\rho_d(a)] = V_B(\delta_a)$. Two consequences used below: (i) the $\rho$-conditional of the *self-model's* statistics must be *self-model-independent* (it must equal the pure-$a$ law for every full-support $q'$); (ii) faithfulness is a joint condition on $\rho$ and on the tree $B$ — it is not a property of $\rho$ alone.

**FA-2 [checked; `q10_one_point.py`] — Remark 4.2's three candidates, recomputed.**
- *Act-event* $\rho_d(a) := a$. $P_{s^\circ}(\mathrm{pay}) = q'/2$; $\nu_{C'}(\cdot\mid\mathrm{pay}) = \delta_{(T,\mathrm{pay},0)}$ — all mass on tails (the heads world reads choice $=\bot$); $V_{s^\circ}(\mathrm{pay}) = -x$, $V_{s^\circ}(\mathrm{refuse}) = 0$: **refuses**, for every $q'$ and every $x,y$. Law-faithfulness fails for both acts, value-faithfulness fails for pay ($-x \ne \tfrac{y-x}2$). Verdict: fails by disjointness, exactly as v2 says.
- *Material conditional* $\rho_d(a) := \neg O_T \vee a = H\vee a$. Closed forms (new): $V_{s^\circ}(H\vee\mathrm{pay}) = \dfrac{q'(y-x)}{1+q'}$, $V_{s^\circ}(H\vee\mathrm{refuse}) = \dfrac{q'y}{2-q'}$; the conditional law puts weight $\tfrac{q'}{1+q'}$ on $(T,\mathrm{pay},0)$ instead of $\tfrac12$. Difference $= \dfrac{q'\,[\,y(1-2q') - x(2-q')\,]}{(1+q')(2-q')}$, so it **pays iff $y(1-2q') > x(2-q')$**. At $q' = \tfrac12$: $\tfrac{y-x}3$ vs $\tfrac y3$, refuses (v2's numbers, P1:toolkit-audit §3.10 agrees); at $q' = \tfrac1{10}$, $x=1$, $y=3$: $\tfrac2{11} > \tfrac3{19}$, pays. The verdict is a function of the antecedent probability $q'$, not of the stakes alone — "antecedent reweighting", quantified. Not faithful for either act.
- *Would-pay coordinate*, realized as the event $E_{\mathrm{pay}} := (T\wedge\mathrm{pay})\vee(H\wedge\mathrm{transfer}{=}1)$, $E_{\mathrm{refuse}} := (T\wedge\mathrm{refuse})\vee(H\wedge\mathrm{transfer}{=}0)$. $P_{s^\circ}(E_{\mathrm{pay}}) = q'$; $\nu_{C'}(\cdot\mid E_{\mathrm{pay}}) = \tfrac12(T,\mathrm{pay},0)+\tfrac12(H,\bot,1) = \nu_{\delta_{\mathrm{pay}}}$; $V_{s^\circ}(E_{\mathrm{pay}}) = \tfrac{y-x}2$, $V_{s^\circ}(E_{\mathrm{refuse}}) = 0$: law- and value-faithful for both acts, **pays iff $y > x$** — the $T_{\mathrm{opt}}$ verdict.

**FA-3 [checked; `q10_faithful_exists.py`] — correction to Remark 4.2's "No such event exists in that example's algebra as modelled".** Exhaustive search over all events of Prop 6's algebra (an event is determined, for faithfulness, by its realized atoms; $2^4$ candidates per problem): in $B_1$ the events faithful for pay are exactly $\{E_{\mathrm{pay}}\}$ and for refuse exactly $\{E_{\mathrm{refuse}}\}$ — so **a faithful $\rho$ does exist in the algebra as modelled**, because Omega's deterministic coupling makes the transfer coordinate record the simulated draw. Using $B_1$'s $E_{\mathrm{pay}}$ on $B_2$: $\nu_{B_2,C'}(\cdot\mid E_{\mathrm{pay}}) = (q',\,1-q')$ on $\big((T,\mathrm{pay},0),(H,\bot,1)\big)$, $V = y - q'(x+y)$ against $V(E_{\mathrm{refuse}}) = 0$: pays iff $(1-2q')y > q'x$ — the wrong answer on $B_2$ for small $q'$. **FA-5 [derived] — Theorem (one point).** Let $B$ query exactly one point $d$ (fiber $F_d$ arbitrary), let $s^\circ$ be masked-prior-calibrated (Definition 11) under a full-support self-model $C'$, and let $\rho$ be value-faithful for $(B,C')$ at $d$. Then $P_{s^\circ}(\rho_d(a)) > 0$ for every $a$ (the conditional is defined), the maximization domain of Definition 17 is all of $A_d$, and
$$\mathrm{UDT}_{s^\circ,\rho}(d) = \mathrm{Unif}\arg\max_{a\in A_d} V_{s^\circ}(\rho_d(a)) = \mathrm{Unif}\arg\max_{a\in A_d} V_B(\delta_a),$$
the uniform mixture over the best *deterministic* procedures. Consequently $T_{\mathrm{opt}}(\mathrm{UDT}_{s^\circ,\rho}, B)$ holds **iff** $\max_a V_B(\delta_a) = \max_{C''} V_B(C'')$, which is the case (i) whenever $F_d$ is non-nested — then $V_B$ is affine in $C(d)$ (Definition 21's multiaffinity) and the pure maximum is the maximum, ties included (any mixture of maximizers attains it) — and (ii) always under shared-seed semantics (a mixture over policies is an average of pure values). It **fails** under Definition 6 on nested single-point trees: on the absent-minded driver (Prop 5(c)) $\arg\max_a V(\delta_a) = \{b\}$ with value $1 < \tfrac43$. *Proof.* Value-faithfulness gives $V_{s^\circ}(\rho_d(a)) = V_B(C'[d\mapsto a]) = V_B(\delta_a)$ (one point); positivity of $P_{s^\circ}(\rho_d(a))$ is part of Definition F1; the rest is Definition 17's convention and Definition 21. $\square$ Register: Definition 11 *masked*, Definition 6 semantics, single queried point; the optimality grade is that of $T_{\mathrm{opt}}$ (Definition 18), attained at the pure grade.

**FA-6 [checked; `q10_one_point.py`] — strict prior calibration collapses the domain.** Under Definition 11 unmasked with a deterministic $C$: $P_{s^\circ}(\rho_d(a)) = \nu_{B,C}(\rho_d(a)) = \mathbf 1[a = C(d)]$ for any faithful $\rho$ (faithfulness forces $\rho_d(a)$ to carry exactly the $\delta_a$-runs), so Definition 17's domain $\{a : P_{s^\circ}(\rho_d(a))>0\} = \{C(d)\}$ and $\mathrm{UDT}_{s^\circ,\rho}(d) = C(d)$ whatever $C$ is: with $C = \delta_{\mathrm{refuse}}$, $P_{s^\circ}(E_{\mathrm{pay}}) = 0$ and UDT refuses; with $C = \delta_{\mathrm{pay}}$ it pays. This is Remark 3.9's collapse ("$T_{\mathrm{EDT}}$ approves every deterministic procedure") one level up: **strict prior calibration makes $T_{\mathrm{UDT}_{s^\circ,\rho}}$ approve every deterministic procedure.** Hence the masked variant of Definition 11 (or the cUDT route of FA-7(iv)) is not optional for Q10.

**FA-7 [checked+derived; `q10_one_point.py`] — $\mathrm{cUDT}_{s^\circ,\rho}$ and when the two coincide.**
(i) With the *evidential* counterfactual structure at the prior — $\mathrm{cf}_{s^\circ}(X) := (P_{s^\circ}(\cdot\mid X), V_{s^\circ}(\cdot\wedge X))$, Definition 20's criterion transported to $s^\circ$ — $\mathrm{cUDT}$'s values equal UDT's on every $\rho$-event of positive prior probability, for all three candidates (act: $-x$ vs $0$; material: the FA-2 closed forms; would-pay: $\tfrac{y-x}2$ vs $0$).

(iii) *Material-conditional $\rho$ with a non-evidential "rigid disposition" cf* — $P^{H\vee\mathrm{pay}} := \tfrac12\delta_{(T,\mathrm{pay},0)} + \tfrac12\delta_{(H,\bot,1)}$, $P^{H\vee\mathrm{refuse}} := \tfrac12\delta_{(T,\mathrm{refuse},0)}+\tfrac12\delta_{(H,\bot,0)}$ (success holds) — gives cUDT values $\tfrac{y-x}2$ vs $0$: pays iff $y>x$. (iv) *Deviation-calibrated cf at the strict prior.* Take $s^\circ$ strictly prior-calibrated for $C = \delta_{\mathrm{refuse}}$ and set $\mathrm{cf}_{s^\circ}(E_a) := $ the law of $\mu_{\delta_a}$ (R1 at the disposition level — "counterfactual calibration" in Remark 1.2's sense). ## 2. Q10, many points (phase-1 open problem 6)

Setting (`q10_many_points.py`): sequential points $d_1 \to d_2 (\to d_3)$, all observations $\top$, distinct states, leaf-world records every act; $\rho_{d_i}(a) := \{\mathrm{act}_i = a\}$ is law-faithful outright (each point consulted once per run, its draw recorded — FA-4's argument). **FA-9 [derived] — the best-reply theorem.** Let $s^\circ$ be masked-prior-calibrated under a full-support self-model $C'$ and $\rho$ value-faithful for $(B, C')$ at every queried point. Then for every $d$,
$$\mathrm{UDT}_{s^\circ,\rho}(d) = \mathrm{Unif}\arg\max_{a\in A_d} V_B(C'[d\mapsto a]) =: \mathrm{BR}_d(C'),$$
the uniform best reply *at $d$ against the self-model*, i.e. Theorem 2's all-instance-deviation evaluator with $C'$ in place of $C$. *Proof.* Value-faithfulness is the equation $V_{s^\circ}(\rho_d(a)) = V_B(C'[d\mapsto a])$; the domain is $A_d$ by positivity. $\square$ Two remarks. (a) Definition 11's masked variant quantifies over *procedures* $C'$, which are product across points (independent redraws at distinct points); so **within v2 the self-model is product-form by type** — "product-form priors" is the setting, not a condition to add. (b) Faithfulness is now relative to $C'$: $\rho_d(a)$ must track $C'[d\mapsto a]$, the deviation *with the other points at the self-model* — the recorded-act coordinate does this automatically.

**FA-10 [derived; checked on a $5\times5$ grid, `q10_many_points.py` §F] — the exact condition.** On an almost-fair $B$, with $s^\circ,\rho$ as in FA-9,
$$T_{\mathrm{opt}}(\mathrm{UDT}_{s^\circ,\rho}, B)\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad \prod_{d}\ \mathrm{supp}\,\mathrm{BR}_d(C') \subseteq \Pi^*,$$

## 4. Is the absence of a neutral referent the verdict?

**FA-23 [derived] — the family of intrinsic referents is the family of self-location weightings.** Every counterfactual structure definable from $(B,C,d)$ through the run semantics assigns to an act $a$ a law built from the instance-forcing laws $\{\mu(\cdot\mid\text{reach }q,\ \text{force }a\text{ at }q)\}_{q\in F_d}$ and the deviation law $\mu_{C[d\mapsto a]}$ by (i) choosing which instances count and with what weights and (ii) choosing a conditioning event ($\top$, $\mathrm{occ}(d)$, $O_d$). Success ($P^a(a) = 1$) already forces a stand on (i): "the act $a$" must be located at some instance(s). The named theories are points of this family: classical CDT $=$ forcing at the action-veridical instance with the veridical-only weighting — which is precisely the self-location an OC-calibrated state carries ($P_{s_d}(O_d) = 1$: "I am the real one"); Taylor's CDT+SIA (Theorem 1) $=$ forcing with per-occurrence weights; EDT+SSA / coherence (Theorem 2, UDT's verdicts) $=$ deviation with per-run or prior weighting; the tree-evidential R3 $=$ forcing-at-the-recorded-instance under Definition 6 and deviation under shared seed. **There is no neutral referent because neutrality would mean not choosing a self-location weighting, and success forbids that.** Constructing one fails on Newcomb: it would have to agree with two-boxing (CDT-classical) and one-boxing (EDT-classical), incompatible verdicts.

**FA-24 [derived] — the verdict, in its correct form.** Q6's last clause asks whether the absence of a neutral referent is itself the correct formal verdict on the CDT/EDT dispute. Answer, with register: *yes, in this form* — (a) at recorded points with realized observations there is nothing to dispute: all intrinsic referents coincide with conditioning at positive acts and with each other at nulled acts (FA-20); (b) at simulation-bearing points the referents differ, and they differ exactly as the calibration senses' self-locations differ — OC's "I am the real one" (classical CDT), per-occurrence SSC's SIA (Theorem 1), per-run SSC's deviation (Theorem 2) — so **the counterfactual-slot dispute is the OC/SSC dispute of P1:synthesis item 1 seen from the $\mathrm{cf}$ side**, not an independent question about causation; (c) the run semantics adds a second parameter: under Definition 6 the tree's own evidential counterfactual (R3) *is* classical CDT's in Newcomb (independent redraws screen the recorded act off from the simulation's draw — Lemma 3's mechanism), while under shared seed it is UDT's; so "EDT one-boxes in Newcomb" is, tree-side, a claim about the predictor sharing the agent's seed — the tree makes explicit what the classic argument assumes (seeds thread's Q12 territory). 1. *Strict OC + act-recording at $d$ + counterfactual calibration to any intrinsic $O_d$-conditioned referent* $\Rightarrow$ $\mathrm{cf}_{s_d}(a) = (P_{s_d}(\cdot\mid a), V_{s_d}(\cdot\wedge a))$ on $P_{s_d}$-positive acts (Definition 20's evidential criterion), and the calibrated $\mathrm{cf}$ is the same for all three referents on nulled acts (FA-20). So the direct answer to "does strict OC + recording force agreement with conditioning on positive-probability acts" is: **for a calibrated $\mathrm{cf}_s$ yes (any intrinsic referent will do); for the free $\mathrm{cf}_s$ of Definition 2, no.**
2. *Limit OC (Definition 10) + act-recording + tremble-realizable observation* $\Rightarrow$ R3 is total on $A_d$, satisfies success, is defined at nulled acts and nulled observations, and equals the $O_d$-conditioned deviation referent (FA-17, FA-20): the deviation- and tremble-referents are one referent at recorded points.
3. *Prior calibration (Definition 11) with a problem-independent disposition coordinate* $\Rightarrow$ the deviation referent on disposition events is $V_B(C[d\mapsto a])$ (FA-4, FA-9), so cUDT with R1-calibrated $\mathrm{cf}_{s^\circ}$ is the Definition-22 best reply at every point (FA-7(iv), FA-9) and optimal on almost-fair single-point trees (FA-5).
