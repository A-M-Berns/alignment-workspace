## 0. Definitions introduced (D1–D9)

**D1 (enriched algebra; P1:fair-repair R1).** $\mathcal E^{U}$ := $\mathcal E$ with fresh coordinates $\mathrm{pol}_d \in A_d \cup \{\bot\}$ for $d \in U$. A Q1-enrichment, not algebra-innocent (Remark 4.2: "whether $\mathcal E$ events the agent's dispositions is a richness condition on the algebra (Remark 6.3, Question 1)").

**D2 (stamped problem $B^{\mathrm{pol}}$).** The same tree as $B$; every leaf-world carries $\mathrm{pol}_d :=$ the action drawn at the $d$-node on its path ($\bot$ if none). Well-defined iff the draw is unique per run: $\#_d \le 1$ identically (almost fair at $d$) under Def 6, or shared seed (Q12) in general. This is P1:verify E4's enrichment — "the seed for $d$ on this run is $a$" made a world-event — realized as a tree with no relocation surgery (`common.py: stamped_mugging`).

**D3 (disposition event).** $\rho_d(a) := \{\mathrm{pol}_d = a\}$ on $\mathcal E^{U}$ — Remark 4.2's would-pay coordinate; the canonical policy interpretation (Def 17's $\rho$) once the algebra is enriched.

**D4 (per-run disposition calibration, PDC — brief B's state-side fairness condition made precise).** At queried $d$, for $C$: for every $a \in A_d$, $\mathrm{cf}_{s_d}(\rho_d(a)) = (P^a, V^a)$ with $P^a(X) = \mu_{B^{\mathrm{pol}}, C[d \mapsto a]}\big(\{\lambda \models X\} \,\big|\, \mathrm{occ}(d)\big)$ for all $X$ and $V^a(X) = \mathbb E_{\mu_{C[d \mapsto a]}}\big[r \,\big|\, \lambda \models X,\ \mathrm{occ}(d)\big]$ on non-null $X$. This is Def 20's *counterfactual calibration* (supposed statistics = deviation statistics $\nu_{B, C[d\mapsto a]}$) restricted to disposition events and conditioned on occurrence (per-run). The run semantics of $\mu$ is named at each use.

**D5 (self-locating state; masked / limit per-run SSC).** The per-run SSC state (Def 13) of $B^{\mathrm{pol}}$ at $d$: $P^{\mathrm{sl}}_{s_d} := \lambda_*\mu_C(\cdot \mid \mathrm{occ}(d))$ on $\mathcal E^{d}$, $V^{\mathrm{sl}}_{s_d}(X) := \mathbb E_{\mu_C}[r \mid \lambda \models X, \mathrm{occ}(d)]$ — Prop 2's lifted state pushed down to the enriched (not the leaf) algebra. *Masked per-run SSC* := per-run SSC under $C[d \mapsto m]$ for some full-support $m$; *limit per-run SSC* := the $\varepsilon \to 0^+$ limit under $C^\varepsilon$. These transport Defs 9/10 to Def 13; v2 defines neither (flagged to the calibration thread).

**D6 (the two procedures).** $\Pi_B(d) := \mathrm{Unif}\,\arg\max_{a \in A_d} V^{\rho_d(a)}_{s_d}(\rho_d(a))$ — Def 17's $\mathrm{cUDT}_{s^\circ,\rho}$ with $s^\circ := s_d$ (brief B). $\Pi_A$: given $(s^\circ, \rho)$ with $s^\circ$ a state on $\mathcal E^{U}$ lifted to carry occurrence events (Prop 2), at a point $d \in U$ play $\pi^*(d)$ for $\pi^* \in \mathrm{Unif}\,\arg\max_{\pi \in \Pi_U:\ P_{s^\circ}(\rho(\pi)) > 0} V_{s^\circ}(\rho(\pi))$, where $\Pi_U := \prod_{e \in U} A_e$ and $\rho(\pi) := \bigwedge_{e \in U} \rho_e(\pi(e))$ (brief A's "play the component the $s^\circ$-optimal cluster-policy assigns to your point"); at honest points act by EDT along trembles (Def 17 + Remark 3.12 in FR-11's concrete form). §6 gives the pseudocode.

**D7 (the audit).** $\mathrm{Audit}(d; s^\circ)$ passes iff $(P_{s_d}, V_{s_d}) = \big(\lambda_* P_{s^\circ}(\cdot \mid \mathrm{occ}(d)),\ V_{s^\circ}(\cdot \wedge \mathrm{occ}(d))\big)$ — the Jeffrey conditioning of the prior on the occurrence event, pushed to the point's algebra. $\varepsilon$-version: total-variation slack $\le \varepsilon$ on both clauses (App. B item 3's $\varepsilon$-calibration).

**D8 (data cost — Remark 3.8(iii) made countable).** For an implementation $I$ of a decision rule on a cluster $U$: $\mathrm{cost}(I)$ := the number of real parameters among $I$'s inputs that are not functions of the observation-calibrated components $(P_{s_d}, V_{s_d})_{d \in U}$ of the input problem (in bits: times the payoff precision $b$). Separately the *algebra cost*: bits per world of enrichment coordinates the implementation's algebra needs beyond $\mathcal E$.

**D9 (UDT1.0 / UDT1.1; [bridge-dossier](../bridge-dossier.md) row T14).** UDT1.1 := maximize $V$ over whole cluster policies — $T_{\mathrm{opt}}$ (Def 18), Remark 6.2's $\mathrm{UDT}_B$, $\mathrm{EDT}(\hat d)$ at the joint relocated root. UDT1.0 := componentwise — $\arg\max_a$ of the value of "my policy answers $a$ at $d$, the rest as it is": Def 17's $\mathrm{UDT}_{s^\circ,\rho}$ and $\mathrm{cUDT}_{s^\circ,\rho}$ have this shape.

The refuser trap: a refuser's prior is *strictly* prior-calibrated on its own statistics — $V_{s^\circ}(\rho(\mathrm{pay}))$ is undefined, the event being null — and observes likelihood ratio $1$ between "coupled" and "inert" (P1:verify G2 [checked there]); only masked/limit prior calibration forces $V_{s^\circ}(\rho(\mathrm{pay})) = \tfrac12(y - x)$, and only pay-episodes supply the data. Schedule and cost on the mugging: an exploratory pay-episode is decisive with probability $\tfrac12$ (heads: the transfer arrives iff coupled) and uninformative on tails (both hypotheses predict $-x$), so the expected number of episodes to escape at rate $\eta$ is $2/\eta$; the cost is $\eta \cdot \tfrac12(x - y)$ per episode while refusing is right, and $(1-\eta) \cdot \tfrac12(y - x)$ per episode *foregone* while paying is right and unlearned; with $\eta_n = 1/n$ the pay-samples by episode $N$ number $\sim \ln N$ — slow, unbounded. Two structural points: (a) exploration must be at the policy level — committing to "pay" before the coin — because under Def 6 an *action-level* explorer that pays with probability $\eta$ at the tails node has Omega's simulation pay with probability $\eta$ too, transfers arrive with probability $\eta/2$ per episode, and a per-state learner cannot attribute them to the tails action (slop §6's credit-assignment decay; PC-9's "bug"), so it never learns $V(\rho(\mathrm{pay}))$: the learner must record dispositions — Q1's enrichment at the level of the *data*; (b) the regress is not eliminated but relocated: "is $s^\circ$ calibrated?" becomes "does the episode frequency converge to the problem's law?", an ordinary learning-theoretic question. The Markov-chain computation (absorbing states, drift) belongs to grounding (XC-8); the general exploration characterization to dynamic.

**FP-26 [reconstructed: logical-induction facts from Garrabrant–Benson-Tilsen–Critch–Soares–Taylor 2016 as recalled, not re-read; v2's axioms quoted] — the stage-indexed residue: Q7 in logical guise.** Setting: $s^\circ_t$ := a logical inductor's stage-$t$ beliefs; per-stage algebras $\mathcal E_t$ := sentences modulo $D_t$-provable equivalence ($D_t$ the deductive state at stage $t$), with quotient maps $\mathcal E_t \to \mathcal E_{t'}$ for $t \le t'$ (more sentences identified as $t$ grows) — the sibling's coarsening maps (dossier T8), contravariant, exactly Q7's "translation maps". v2's axioms, quoted, and their fate:
- Def 1 constraint 1 ("for every event $X$, exactly one of $X, \neg X$ belongs to $\omega$"), 3 ("if $X, Y \in \omega$ then $X \wedge Y \in \omega$"), 4 (designated meets): **survive per stage** — LI's propositionally consistent worlds $\mathcal{PC}(D_t)$ satisfy them relative to $\mathcal E_t$. Constraint 2 ("if $X \in \omega$ and $X \le Y$ then $Y \in \omega$"): **survives only relative to $D_t$-entailment**; relative to the limit theory it breaks (a stage-$t$ world may hold "the digit is even" while the theory refutes it later).
- Def 2 clause 1 ("$P_s \in \Delta(\mathcal A)$", finitely additive): **breaks at finite stages** — $\mathbb P_t$ is a pricing, coherent only in the limit (limit coherence); survives for $\mathbb P_\infty$, or for a coherent projection of $\mathbb P_t$ onto $\Delta(\mathcal E_t)$.
- Def 2 clause 2 (the averaging axiom): **breaks with clause 1**, its denominators presupposing additivity; survives in the limit.
- Def 2 clause 3 ("assigns to each non-$\bot$ event a supposed probability-and-desirability pair … success"): **survives per stage, and is the payoff** — "$\bot$" is stage-relative, so a sentence refuted at $t' > t$ is a non-$\bot$ event of $\mathcal E_t$: the counterlogical mugging's spoofed digit is a legitimate supposition at the pre-computation stage. The scope decision "no counterlogicals" is respected stage-wise while the counterlogical *branch* becomes typeable one stage earlier.
- §1's standing assumption ("Throughout, all subjective states share the fixed $\mathcal A$; relaxing to per-state algebras is Question 7"): **breaks** — prior on $\mathcal E_t$, state on $\mathcal E_{t'}$. The audit (D7) is then computable only on the *earlier, coarser* algebra $\mathcal E_t$: pull the current state back along $\mathcal E_t \to \mathcal E_{t'}$ (the pulled-back state gives "even" probability $0$; $s^\circ_t(\cdot \mid \mathrm{occ})$ gives $\tfrac12$). This is email 3's "dig up the past" as an operation, and it needs precisely Q7's translation map.
- Def 11's problem-level constraint ("all queried points share one $s^\circ$"): **breaks into "one designated stage $t^*$ per problem"** — the stage before the computation; "which prior?" becomes "which stage?", and nothing in v2 selects it.
- Prop 1 / Prop 2's premise that queried states are Jeffrey conditionings of the prior: **fails structurally** for LI states — computing the digit is a deductive update, not a conditioning of $\mathbb P_t$ on an event of $\mathcal E_t$ (radical probabilism, dossier T22) — so every post-computation state fails the audit against the pre-computation prior on the computed sentence. The audit cannot distinguish "spoofed by Omega" from "learned by computing"; "trust $s^\circ_t$" then means doubting computed facts at the prior's rate, which is exactly the proposal [scrubbed] and exactly its cost. Whether that is right is normative; what is settled is the *shape*: the residue is Q7 with $\mathcal E_s := \mathcal E_{t(s)}$, translations := the deductive quotients, plus a designated stage — the list the mandate asked for.
