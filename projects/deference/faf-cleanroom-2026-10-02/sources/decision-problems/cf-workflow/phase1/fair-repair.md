All Definition/Proposition/Theorem numbers refer to `decision-problems-v2.md`. Slop-notes claims cited as "slop §n / Claim n.m" and re-verified where used, never taken on authority.

## 0. Setup and vocabulary

Fix a concrete decision problem $B$ (Definition 5): finite rooted tree, chance nodes $n$ with $\beta_n \in \Delta(\mathrm{children})$, decision nodes $q$ labelled with decision-points $d_q = (s, O, A) \in \mathcal{D}$, leaves $(\lambda(\ell), r(\ell))$. Run semantics $\mu_{B,C}$, statistics $\nu_{B,C}$, value $V_B(C) = \mathbb{E}_\mu[r]$ per Definition 6.

- **Fiber** of a queried point $d$: $F_d := \{q : d_q = d\}$.
- **Scope** of a fiber: the subtree rooted at $\mathrm{lca}(F_d)$ (least common ancestor; for $|F_d| = 1$, the node itself).
- $F_d$ is **nested** (self-succession) if some member is a proper ancestor of another; equivalently some path meets two $d$-nodes; equivalently $\#_d \geq 2$ with positive probability for some full-support procedure.
- **Strong (structural) fairness** (slop §1, adopted): for every queried $d$, all members of $F_d$ have isomorphic labelled subtrees (rooted-tree isomorphism preserving chance labels $\beta$, decision-point labels, and leaf pairs $(\lambda, r)$). A property of $B$ alone.
- Two fibers $F_d, F_e$ are **entangled** if some member of one has a member of the other strictly below it.

**FR-1 [derived].** On a strongly fair tree: (i) no fiber is nested (a proper subtree of a finite tree is not isomorphic to the whole); hence $\#_d \leq 1$ on every path, so the per-run and per-occurrence SSC equations (Definition 13) coincide identically — $\mathbb{E}_\mu[\#_d \mathbf{1}_{\lambda \models X}] = \mu(\mathrm{occ}(d) \wedge \{\lambda \models X\})$ and $\mathbb{E}_\mu[\#_d] = \mu(\mathrm{occ}(d))$ when $\#_d \in \{0,1\}$. (ii) By fiber-isomorphism, the downtree leaf-law conditional on reaching $q$ is the same for every $q \in F_d$ under **every** procedure (induction on the subtree: the isomorphism matches labels, and Definition 6's draws depend only on labels; trembled procedures included). Call it $\theta_d$ (or $\theta^C_d$ when the dependence matters). Hence the common SSC value is $\theta_d$'s world-marginal regardless of the reach-weights: the anthropic fork closes structurally, and the closure needs **only** non-nesting for (i) but isomorphism for (ii) — (ii) is what makes "the state of the point" well-defined. This verifies slop Claim 1.1 and sharpens it: per-run = per-occurrence already at non-nesting; node-independence of the value is the isomorphism's contribution.

Note on what fairness does **not** require: veridicality. A fiber of perfect-copy simulation nodes (isomorphic subtrees, observation false at some members' leaves) is strongly fair. Fairness makes indistinguishability *real* (equal continuations), not observations *true*.

## 1. Relocation, precisely (Task 1)

### 1.1 The atomic move

Two standing hypotheses on the input, used where flagged (they hold in every worked example of v2 §§6–7 after making chance evented):

- **(EC) evented chance:** every chance edge carries an event of $\mathcal{E}$; edges of one chance node pairwise disjoint; leaf-worlds entail the events on their root-path (chance edges and action edges).
- **(FRec) full recording:** $B$ records at every queried point for every procedure (Definition 7's clauses, quantified over $C$). FRec implies subtree-veridicality of every decision node and coverage; the Told-You-So tree $B_P$ (§7.1) fails it at $d_5$ (v2 change-log 15 already notes $B_P$ does not record at $d_5$).

**Definition R1 (algebra enrichment).** For a set $U$ of queried points, $\mathcal{E}^U$ is the algebra generated by $\mathcal{E}$ and fresh coordinates $\mathrm{pol}_d \in A_d \cup \{\bot\}$ for $d \in U$; worlds of $\mathcal{E}^U$ = worlds of $\mathcal{E}$ × assignments of the new coordinates. This is the disposition coordinate of Remark 4.2 ("would-pay") added by hand; it is the same *kind* of move as Proposition 2's lift to $2^{\mathrm{Leaves}}$ — both repairs of $\nu$-insufficiency by enriching $\mathcal{E}$, v2's Question 1 relativity. Relocation is **not** algebra-innocent, and this should be said plainly: if $\mathcal{E}$ already events dispositions the step is vacuous, otherwise the relocated actions do not exist as events without it. (Reusing the original act-events $a \in A_d$ instead type-checks but destroys action-veridicality at the new node on branches where the act-coordinate reads $\bot$ — the mugging's heads branch — so the calibrate step would fail; checked below.)

**Definition R2 (atomic relocation $\mathrm{Rel}_d$).** Let $F_d$ be non-nested with $\ell := \mathrm{lca}(F_d)$. $\mathrm{Rel}_d(B)$:

1. Enrich to $\mathcal{E}^{\{d\}}$; every old leaf-world gets $\mathrm{pol}_d = \bot$ by default, overwritten below.
2. Insert a new decision node $\hat q$ on the edge above $\ell$ (new root if $\ell$ is the root), labelled with the new point $\hat d := (\hat s,\ O_{\hat d},\ \{\{\mathrm{pol}_d = a\} : a \in A_d\})$.
3. $\hat q$'s $a$-child is the copy $T_a$ of the subtree at $\ell$ in which every $q \in F_d$ is deleted and replaced by the subtree under its $a$-edge ("resolved to $a$"), and every leaf-world in $T_a$ is stamped $\mathrm{pol}_d = a$.
4. $O_{\hat d}$ := the **upstream context**: the conjunction of the (EC-)events on the path from the root to $\hat q$ ($\top$ if $\hat q$ is the root). One choice per fiber: indistinguishable instances get one coordinate — the type's own constraint (Remark 2.2).
5. $\hat s$ is stipulated at the subsequent **calibrate** step (below), not by the tree surgery.

The original observation $O_d$ **is stripped**: it survives only as structure (the chance branching below $\hat q$ that used to route among the $F_d$-members). Retaining it as $O_{\hat d}$ would be wrong: $\hat q$ sits above branches where $O_d$ is false (the simulation branches), so it would fail subtree-veridicality exactly as the old nodes did. On the fair output the point determines the node, so observations are redundant (slop §1's "consequence for the type", now a by-product of the construction).

**FR-2 [checked] (worked: Counterfactual Mugging $B_1$, Proposition 6).** Original: root chance (½ H, ½ T); T-branch queries $d$ (pay → leaf $(T,\mathrm{pay},0)$, $r = -x$; refuse → $(T,\mathrm{refuse},0)$, $r = 0$); H-branch queries $d$ hypothetically (pay → $(H,\bot,1)$, $r = y$; refuse → $(H,\bot,0)$, $r = 0$). With $q := C(d)(\mathrm{pay})$: $V_{B_1} = \tfrac12(-xq) + \tfrac12(yq) = \tfrac{q(y-x)}{2}$ ✓ (matches Prop 6). Fiber = {T-node, H-node}, non-nested, lca = root. $\mathrm{Rel}_d$: new root $\hat q$, actions $\mathrm{pol} \in \{\mathrm{pay}, \mathrm{refuse}\}$, $O_{\hat d} = \top$; pay-branch: coin → $(T,\mathrm{pay},0,\mathrm{pol{=}pay})\, r{=}{-x}$ / $(H,\bot,1,\mathrm{pol{=}pay})\, r{=}y$; refuse-branch: coin → payoffs 0, 0. $V_{\tilde B}(q) = q\big(\tfrac12(-x) + \tfrac12 y\big) = \tfrac{q(y-x)}{2}$: **equal for every mixed $q$**. Output strongly fair (singleton fiber); records at $\hat d$ (pol stamped, $O = \top$, one node per run). Strict calibration at $\hat d$ pins $P_{\hat s}(\mathrm{pol{=}pay}) = q$ (forced self-knowledge, Remark 3.6), so run EDT in the masked/limit sense: $V_{\hat s}(\mathrm{pol{=}pay}) = \mathbb{E}[r \mid \mathrm{pol{=}pay}] = \tfrac{y-x}{2}$, $V_{\hat s}(\mathrm{refuse}) = 0$ — pays iff $y > x$, which is UDT's verdict via Remark 4.2's would-pay computation ($\tfrac12(y-x) > 0$). EDT = UDT on the repaired problem, no $\rho$ parameter consumed: the tree supplies $\rho_d(a) = \{\mathrm{pol}_d = a\}$ (slop Claim 5.1 verified on this instance).

### 1.2 Multiple fibers: the ping-pong obstruction and the two exits

Relocating one fiber can **unfair** another: step 3 copies every foreign decision node inside the scope; a copy in $T_a$ has its $d$-nodes-below resolved to $a$, so copies across branches have non-isomorphic subtrees whenever $d$-nodes lay below — the foreign fiber (even a previously fair one, even a previously **relocated** one) becomes unfair.

**FR-3 [checked] (ping-pong lemma).** Naive iteration of R2 ("while some fiber is unfair, relocate it at its lca, keeping one point per fiber") does not terminate on entangled fibers. Witness: $A_d = \{a_1,a_2\}$, $A_e = \{b_1,b_2\}$; root chance (½,½); Left: $d$-node, below each answer an $e$-node, leaves $f(a,b)$; Right: $e$-node, below each answer a $d$-node, leaves $g(a,b)$; generic $f,g$. Both fibers unfair and entangled. Relocate $d$ (root $\hat q_d$): the $e$-fiber's four copies split across $\hat q_d$'s branches with $a$-dependent leaves → unfair, lca $= \hat q_d$. Relocate $e$ (root $\hat q_e$ above): its branches contain copies of $\hat q_d$ whose subtrees have $e$-answers resolved differently → $\hat d$'s fiber unfair. Relocate $\hat d$: copies of $\hat q_e$ differ across the new root's branches → $\hat e$ unfair. The tree is thereafter always "one policy node at the root, two copies of the other below", and each relocation re-creates the other's unfairness: a 2-cycle, forever. Root cause: copying a decision node while keeping its point identical manufactures a fiber of indistinguishable instances that are in fact distinguishable (they sit below different answers of an *upstream, evented* choice).

**Exit E1 (observation refinement).** When step 3 copies a *relocated* node $\hat q_{d'}$ into branch $a$, refine its point per copy: $O \mapsto O \wedge \{\mathrm{pol}_d = a\}$ — a **new** point per copy (the later policy choice observes the earlier one). Then copies are singleton fibers, the cascade terminates (each original point is relocated once and disappears; refined relocated points never need relocation because their subtrees contain no unresolved occurrences of the refining point), and the output is a **sequenced** fair tree: relocated choices stacked at the top, later ones observing earlier ones, then the original chance structure. Cost: the pure-policy space grows (the $d'$-answer may now depend on the $d$-answer) — handled by the equivalence analysis in §2 (the growth is outcome-redundant: reduced normal form unchanged; sup unchanged by multiaffineness).

**Exit E2 (joint product relocation) — adopted as canonical.** Let $U$ := the **entanglement closure** of the unfair fibers: the least set containing every unfair fiber and containing $e$ whenever some $e$-node has a $U$-node strictly below it (finite, so the closure exists). Relocate all of $U$ at once: enrich to $\mathcal{E}^U$; insert one node $\hat q$ above $\mathrm{lca}\big(\bigcup_{d \in U} F_d\big)$ with action set $\prod_{d \in U} A_d$ (as pol-coordinate events, pairwise disjoint since they fix all coordinates); the $(a_d)_d$-branch resolves every $U$-node to its coordinate and stamps the pol-coordinates. (One may equally present E2 as a stack of singleton relocations with E1-refined observations — the sequenced forms; §2 shows all these outputs are equivalent, so "canonical" means: the representative with no arbitrary sequencing choice.)

**FR-4 [derived] (fairness of the output).** If no fiber in $U$ is nested, $\mathrm{Rel}_U(B)$ is strongly structurally fair. Proof. $\hat d$'s fiber is a singleton. A surviving point $e \notin U$: (a) if no $e$-node lies in the relocated scope, its fiber and subtrees are untouched, and $e \notin U$ means $F_e$ was fair; (b) $e$-nodes inside the scope are copied across branches, but by closure no $U$-node lies below any $e$-node, so resolution does not touch their subtrees: all copies are isomorphic to the original, and to each other, and to the copies of the *other* $F_e$-members (fair before, by $e \notin U$ ⊇ unfair fibers); mixed inside/outside fibers likewise. (c) Nested fibers are automatically unfair (FR-1(i)), hence already in $U$; so "no nested fiber in $U$" = "no self-succession anywhere in $B$ among unfair-or-entangled points". $\square$

**FR-5 [checked] (worked: Transparent Newcomb V2($p$), §7.2).** Tree: root queries $d_F$ (hypothetically), each answer-child queries $d_E$ (hypothetically), then chance (full w.p. $p$ iff both hypothetical answers were "large", else $1{-}p$), full branch queries $d_F$ for real, empty branch queries $d_E$ for real. Fibers: $F_{d_F}$ = {root node, 4 real full-branch nodes} — the root member is an ancestor of the real members, so $F_{d_F}$ **is nested**; likewise... no: $F_{d_E}$'s hypothetical members (depth 1) are ancestors of its real members (depth 3), so **both fibers are nested**. TN-V2 is therefore *not* almost fair in my mixed-grade sense; FR-7(a) still applies: joint relocation preserves the outcome law on **pure** profiles, which is exactly what §7.2's policy table records. Joint relocation to the root, actions $(x,y) \in \{1,2\}^2$ ($1$ = large-only, $2$ = both): branch $(x,y)$: chance full w.p. $p$ if $(x,y) = (1,1)$ else $1{-}p$; full leaf $r = L + S\,\mathbf{1}[x{=}2]$, empty leaf $r = S\,\mathbf{1}[y{=}2]$. Values: $(1,1)$: $pL$; $(1,2)$: $(1{-}p)L + pS$; $(2,1)$: $(1{-}p)(L{+}S)$; $(2,2)$: $(1{-}p)(L{+}S) + pS$ — all four **identical to v2 §7.2's V2 column**. Under $(2p{-}1)L > S$: optimum $(1,1)$, and EDT at the calibrated relocated root sees act-values equal to these four numbers and picks $(1,1)$: the Proposition 10 wedge (myopic dissatisfaction at $O_E$) cannot be stated on the fair form — there is no $O_E$-conditioned decision left to be myopic at.

### 1.3 What relocation preserves, and the self-succession obstruction

**FR-6 [derived] (run-distribution preservation).** Let $\tilde B = \mathrm{Rel}_U(B)$, no nesting in $U$. For a procedure $C$ on $B$'s points define $\tilde C$ on $\tilde B$'s: $\tilde C(\hat d) := \bigotimes_{d \in U} C(d)$ (product law), $\tilde C = C$ on survivors. Then the pushforward of $\mu_{\tilde B, \tilde C}$ along the canonical leaf map (leaf of branch $(a_d)_d$ below a resolved $U$-node ↦ the $B$-leaf below that node's chosen edges; leaves not below any $U$-node ↦ their original, summing over branches) equals $\mu_{B, C}$; hence $V_{\tilde B}(\tilde C) = V_B(C)$ and the $\mathcal{E}$-marginal of $\nu_{\tilde B, \tilde C}$ is $\nu_{B,C}$, so surviving points' calibration statuses are untouched. Proof: within a branch, chance labels and surviving decision labels are copied, and procedures are functions of points, so all non-$U$ draws are identically distributed; non-nesting makes each $U$-point consulted at most once per $B$-run, and Definition 6 samples that consultation independently of the path, which is exactly what the single up-front draw at $\hat q$ provides; leaves untouched by $U$-nodes have unchanged conditional probability in every branch, and the branch-mixture collapses. $\square$

**FR-7 [checked] (the sharp preservation statement, and the obstruction).**
(a) On **pure** profiles, product-root relocation preserves the outcome law **always**, nested fibers included: a deterministic $C$ answers every occurrence of $d$ identically anyway, which is what resolution does.
(b) On **mixed product** laws it preserves iff no relocated fiber is nested. Counterexample = the absent-minded driver (Proposition 5(c)): one point $d$ at two nested nodes; $V_B(x) = (1-x)(3x+1)$ with interior optimum $V_B(\tfrac13) = \tfrac43$; the relocated tree ($\hat q$: pol$=a$ → 0, pol$=b$ → 1) has $V_{\tilde B}(x) = 1 - x$, sup $= 1 \neq \tfrac43$. Relocation of a nested fiber is the shared-seed semantics (Q12) imposed at $d$: one draw resolves all occurrences, killing the cross terms ($x^2$) that the independent redraws generate.
(c) **No repair exists for self-succession** at the mixed grade: any strongly fair tree has each point on each path at most once, so its $V$ is multiaffine in the per-point mixtures and its sup over product laws is attained at a vertex (pure profile); the AMD's sup $\tfrac43$ is attained only at an interior mixture and exceeds every vertex value ($\max\{0,1\}$), so no fair tree matches its mixed value function under any correspondence. The perfect-recall unrolling (slop open question 2: give the driver a counter, split $d$ into $d^{(1)}, d^{(2)}$) is a genuine second repair map but is not value-preserving either: it makes all assignments available and lifts the sup to Proposition 5(a)'s assignment optimum ($4$ in the miniature). So the three candidate values bracket strictly: collapse $1 <$ original $\tfrac43 <$ unrolling $4$ [checked], and admitting unrolling does not change the fair class — it extends the pipeline's domain at the declared price of changing the problem (which is precisely the Piccione–Rubinstein dispute [reconstructed — Piccione–Rubinstein 1997 as cited by v2 §8/Remark 6.1; I have not read the paper this session]).
(d) Also checked: whether the fiber's lca lies in the fiber. For a nested fiber the lca **is** the top member (AMD: lca{top, bottom} = top ∈ $F_d$), confirming slop question 2's parenthetical; for non-nested multi-member fibers the lca is a proper ancestor of every member, hence outside the fiber.

**Definition (almost fair).** $B$ is **almost fair** if no fiber of $B$ is nested — equivalently every point is consulted at most once per run ($\#_d \le 1$ identically). By FR-4 + FR-6, exactly then $\mathrm{Rel}_U(B)$ (with $U$ the entanglement closure) is strongly fair *and* outcome-law-preserving at all grades. Strongly fair trees are the almost-fair trees with zero unfairness budget. The mugging $B_1$ is almost fair. The AMD is not. TN-V2 **as drawn is also not** (both fibers nested, hypothetical members above real ones — FR-5): its relocation is exact in the pure grade only. Mixed-grade check at $p = 1$, $L = 4$, $S = 1$, $d_E$-coordinate fixed at "large", $C(d_F)(\mathrm{large}) = m$: original tree — full branch reached iff the root's hypothetical draw is large (prob $m$); on the full branch the *real* $d_F$-node redraws: $\mathbb{E}[r \mid \mathrm{full}] = mL + (1-m)(L+S) = 5 - m$, so $V_B(m) = m(5-m) = 5m - m^2$; relocated tree — $V_{\tilde B}(m) = m \cdot pL + (1-m)(1-p)(L+S) = 4m$. At $m = \tfrac12$: $2.25 \neq 2$ [checked]. Agreement at $m \in \{0,1\}$ ✓ (pure grade). So the §7.2 table and Propositions 9–10, which are pure-profile statements, transfer exactly; mixed-strategy claims about TN-V2 do not transfer, and the nesting of a hypothetical above its real counterpart is a *mild* self-succession that the pure grade doesn't see. (A run of TN-V2 consults $d_F$ twice under independent redraws — the live agent and Omega's simulation draw independently, Remark 3.2 — which is exactly what the relocated single draw cannot mimic; the shared-seed semantics of Q12 would make TN-V2 mixed-grade relocatable. Where the thread says "the real choice has to be placed further back", the formal content is: that placement is exact for deterministic dispositions, and for stochastic ones only under shared seed.)

**FR-8 [derived] (relocate-then-calibrate lands where claimed).** Under (EC) and (FRec) on $B$, $\tilde B = \mathrm{Rel}_U(B)$ with the calibrate step — stipulate $P_{\hat s} := \nu_{\tilde B, \tilde C}(\cdot \mid O_{\hat d})$, $V_{\hat s} := \mathbb{E}[r \mid \cdot \wedge O_{\hat d}]$ (strict; or the masked/limit variants), extend surviving states to $\mathcal{E}^U$ by their calibrated conditionals — satisfies: $\hat q$ is subtree-veridical (upstream events hold below by EC-veridicality; $\top$ at root), action-veridical (pol stamped), covered (EC-disjointness routes every $O_{\hat d}$-run through $\hat q$), consulted once per run; hence $\tilde B$ records at $\hat d$, $\mathrm{occ}(\hat d) = \{\lambda \models O_{\hat d}\}$ a.s., and Proposition 3's bridge applies: strict OC at $\hat d$ = per-run SSC = per-occurrence SSC (FR-1). At the root case $O_{\hat d} = \top$ this is exactly **prior calibration** (Definition 11) — the relocated node's state is $s^\circ$ and its honesty condition is Definition 11's unconditioned clause: relocation is Definition 11 made structural (slop Claim 5.1, verified in general, not just on the mugging).

## 2. The equivalence relation (Task 2)

### 2.1 Which relation

Relocation changes the point set (TN-V2: two points → one; E1-outputs: more points), so "identical value function on the shared policy space" needs the sharing made explicit. Three grades were tried:

1. **Pointwise correspondence** (bijection of points + per-point action bijections, matching $V$ on pure profiles): too fine — it relates $B$ to $\mathrm{Rel}_{\{d\}}(B)$ but cannot relate $B$ to a joint product relocation (point counts differ), nor the two sequenced outputs $T_{d,e}, T_{e,d}$ when $|A_d| \neq |A_e|$ (profile counts $|A_d||A_e|^{|A_d|}$ vs $|A_e||A_d|^{|A_e|}$: $18 \neq 24$ at $2 \times 3$) [checked].
2. **Profile-space bijection matching outcome laws**: still too fine for E1-outputs (redundant recall coordinates inflate the profile count: $8 \neq 4$ at $2\times2$) [checked].
3. **Adopted: reduced-profile equivalence.** For a problem $B$ let $\mathrm{law}_B(\pi) \in \Delta(\Omega_{\mathcal{E}} \times \mathbb{R})$ be the $\mathcal{E}$-marginal joint world–payoff law of the pure profile $\pi$ (the extensional shadow $\widehat B$ restricted to pure profiles, marginalized to the common algebra — enrichment coordinates dropped). Reduce: $\pi \sim_{\mathrm{red}} \pi'$ iff $\mathrm{law}_B(\pi) = \mathrm{law}_B(\pi')$. Define $B \sim B'$ iff $\mathrm{law}$ has the same image set — equivalently (the reduced map being injective by construction) iff there is a bijection of reduced profile spaces commuting with $\mathrm{law}$.

**FR-9 [derived].** (i) $B \sim \mathrm{Rel}_U(B)$ for every almost-fair $B$ (FR-6/7a give law-preservation profile-by-profile; reduction removes E1's redundancy). (ii) $\sim$ is blind to states, observations, and tree shape by design; that is the feature, not a bug: states are re-stipulated by the calibrate step, so the invariant content of a problem-description is its realizable outcome-law set. (iii) $T_{\mathrm{opt}}$ (Definition 18's optimality shape) is $\sim$-invariant: $V$ is a function of the law. $\mathrm{UDT}_{s^\circ, \rho}$ with a prior-calibrated $s^\circ$ and faithful $\rho$ (Q10's ingredients) maximizes prior value over policy events = a function of the law-set, hence $\sim$-invariant; EDT and CDT read node states and are not (Proposition 6's pair $B_1, B_2$: relabel states and the verdict moves). "UDT is precisely the theory invariant under $\sim$" survives in the guarded form: the *optimality* theory is $\sim$-invariant, and the *procedure* $\mathrm{UDT}_{s^\circ,\rho}$ is $\sim$-invariant modulo its parameters being supplied $\sim$-invariantly, which prior-calibration does.
(iv) **Mixed grades.** $\sim$ as defined compares pure profiles. One can ask for matching on product laws (independent-redraw mixtures) — relocation passes iff no nesting (FR-7b) — or on all correlated laws — the relocated form realizes correlated laws the original cannot (the product-root node mixes jointly); since fair trees have multiaffine $V$, sup over correlated = sup over pure, so the extension is optimum-innocent [derived], but it is a genuine extension: the repaired problem strictly enlarges the mixed option set. Any use of the pipeline for *mixed-equilibrium* claims must say which grade it is using.

### 2.2 Slop open question 5, settled

Can two strongly fair trees be $\sim$-equivalent yet disagree about where the decision sits? **Yes — necessarily and irreparably** within $\sim$:

**FR-10 [checked].** Let $A_d = A_e = \{1,2\}$, joint payoff $v(a,b)$, worlds recording $(\mathrm{pol}_d, \mathrm{pol}_e)$. $T_{d,e}$: root chooses $a$, then a per-$a$ point chooses $b$ knowing $\mathrm{pol}_d = a$ (fair: all fibers singletons). $T_{e,d}$: mirror. Both strongly fair. Reduced profiles of each ↔ realized pairs $(a,b)$, laws $\delta_{(a,b)} \otimes \delta_{v(a,b)}$ — the same set. So $T_{d,e} \sim T_{e,d}$ (and both $\sim$ the product-root form $T_\times$), while they disagree about which choice is made first and which point observes which. No symmetry of $v$ is needed once reduction is in place; with unreduced profile-bijections a symmetric $v$ (e.g. $v(1,1){=}2, v(2,2){=}1$, off-diagonal $0$) already witnesses it at matching cardinalities. Moreover the disagreement is invisible at every grade: mixed policies realize all joint laws over $(a,b)$ in each of the three trees (mix the first choice, condition the second), so no enrichment of $\sim$ by mixtures separates them. **Dichotomy [derived]:** any relation fine enough to pin the decision's location must consult the factorization of the profile space into points — but that is exactly the data relocation changes, so such a relation rules relocation unsound. There is no middle relation under which relocation is an equivalence *and* location is an invariant. The "further principle" slop question 5 asked for cannot come from the value/law data; candidates live outside: naturalist facts about which variable is the agent's (v2 Definition 23's territory), or the CF stance that the class *is* the object (§4 below) — Garrabrant's own gloss that the additive (updateful) and multiplicative (updateless) factorizations are two presentations of one frame ("Eight Definitions", §4.4, quoted in §4).

## 3. EDT = UDT on fair problems (Task 3)

### 3.1 The theorem

**FR-11 [derived] (upgrade of slop Claim 3.2 to a proof).** Let $B$ be strongly fair and fully recorded (FRec; EC where observations are chance-determined), all chance edges of positive probability (prune the rest: fairness is preserved since isomorphisms match chance labels). Let $C$ be **tremble-EDT-consistent**: for every sufficiently small $\varepsilon > 0$ there is a state-assignment making $B$ strictly observation-calibrated for $C^\varepsilon$ (Definition 10's tremble of $C$) with $T_{\mathrm{EDT}}(C, B_\varepsilon)$ (Definition 18's support condition at every queried point; Remark 3.12's device applied to the concrete tree with states free). $C$ may be mixed. Then $V_B(C) = \max_{C'} V_B(C')$.

*Proof.* Step 1 (calibrated act-values are $Q$-values). Fix $\varepsilon$ small and a queried $d$. Full recording plus non-nesting (FR-1(i)) gives: a.s. every $O_d$-run passes exactly one $d$-node, that node subtree-veridical, its draw stamped in the leaf-world, and no other action-event stamped. Hence $\{\lambda \models a \wedge O_d\} = \{$runs reaching some $q \in F_d$ and drawing $a\}$ up to null sets, and
$$V_{s^\varepsilon_d}(a) \;=\; \mathbb{E}_{\mu_\varepsilon}\big[r \,\big|\, \lambda \models a \wedge O_d\big] \;=\; \sum_{q \in F_d} \Pr\nolimits_\varepsilon(q \mid \mathrm{occ}(d))\; \mathbb{E}_{\theta^\varepsilon_{d,a}}[r] \;=\; \mathbb{E}_{\theta^\varepsilon_{d,a}}[r] \;=:\; v_\varepsilon(d,a),$$
because by FR-1(ii) the continuation law after drawing $a$ is the same at every fiber member under $C^\varepsilon$ — the reach-weights (which are the SIA weights of Theorem 1) multiply a constant and cancel. This is where fairness works: without isomorphism the middle expression is a reach-weighted average of *different* $Q$-values — Theorem 1's functional — whose maximizer need not maximize any single continuation (TN-V2's $(2,2)$, below).
Step 2 (support condition passes to the limit). Recording under full-support $C^\varepsilon$ gives $\nu_\varepsilon(a \mid O_d) = C^\varepsilon(d)(a) > 0$ (the recording argument of Proposition 7), so $A_d^+ = A_d$ and $T_{\mathrm{EDT}}$ reads: $\mathrm{supp}\, C(d) \subseteq \arg\max_{a \in A_d} v_\varepsilon(d,a)$ for all small $\varepsilon$. Leaf probabilities are polynomials in $\varepsilon$; let $\varepsilon \to 0$: $v_\varepsilon(d,a) \to Q_C(d,a)$, the value of forcing $a$ at the (unique-per-run) $d$-consultation and following $C$ below — with no $d$-nodes below (non-nesting), this is the honest one-step deviation. Weak inequalities survive: $\mathrm{supp}\, C(d) \subseteq \arg\max_a Q_C(d,a)$... — careful, argmax can shrink in the limit; what survives is $Q_C(d, a^*) \geq Q_C(d, a)$ for every $a^* \in \mathrm{supp}\, C(d)$ and every $a$, which is all Step 3 needs.
Step 3 (backward induction). Height induction: claim $W_C(q) = M_q$ at every node, where $W_C(q)$ = continuation value of $C$ from $q$ and $M_q$ = the assignment optimum of the subtree (Proposition 5(a) applied to the subtree). Leaves: trivial. Chance $q$: $W_C(q) = \sum_c \beta(c) W_C(c) = \sum_c \beta(c) M_c = M_q$. Decision $q \in F_d$: $W_C(q) = \sum_a C(d)(a) W_C(q_a)$ where $q_a$ is the $a$-child; $W_C(q_a) = Q_C(d,a)$ evaluated at $q$ — node-independent by FR-1(ii) — and $= M_{q_a}$ by induction; Step 2 gives $W_C(q) = \max_a W_C(q_a) = \max_a M_{q_a} = M_q$. Root: $V_B(C) = M_{\mathrm{root}} = \max_\pi V_B(\pi) \geq \max_{C'} V_B(C')$ (Proposition 5(b) inequality), and $V_B(C) \le \max_{C'}$ trivially. $\square$

**Fair-tree assignment lemma [derived], en route:** on a strongly fair tree $\max_C V_B(C) = \max_\pi V_B(\pi)$ — assignments can be taken isomorphism-consistent by leaves-up optimization (subtree optima agree across a fiber by isomorphism; pick matching argmaxes), and such assignments are procedures. So Proposition 5(b)'s hypothesis (some optimal assignment constant on fibers) is a theorem under strong fairness rather than an assumption — answering the task's "does Prop 5(b) apply" pressure point. The "isomorphic copies behave as one node with summed reach probability" picture survives trembles in the only form the proof needs: node-independence of trembled continuation laws (FR-1(ii) holds for every procedure).

### 3.2 What fails without each hypothesis [checked]

- **Without trembles** (strict calibration only): the imperfect equilibrium. Two points, $O_1 = \top$: out → $0$; in → point $d_2$ ($O_2 = $ the act-event "in", evented and recorded): x → $4$, y → $1$. $C = $ (out, y): $\nu(O_2) = 0$, so $d_2$'s state is unconstrained (Definition 8's parenthetical); stipulate $P_{s_2}$ half-half on fantasy worlds with $V_{s_2}(\mathrm{y}) = 5 > 0 = V_{s_2}(\mathrm{x})$; at $d_1$ calibration gives $A^+_{d_1} = \{\mathrm{out}\}$, EDT approves out. So (out, y) is strictly-calibrated-EDT-consistent with $V = 0 < 4$. Under trembles, $\varepsilon$-calibration forces $s_2$ to the true continuation (x: 4, y: 1), EDT flips $d_2$ to x, then $d_1$ to in. The tremble is not an optional refinement here; it is load-bearing (slop question 4's suspicion — fairness constrains the tree, trembles constrain the states — is right, and the Told-You-So bullet below shows recording/veridicality is a third, independently load-bearing constraint).
- **Without fairness**: TN-V2 with $(2p{-}1)L > S$, $p < 1$. $(2,2)$ is OC-calibrated (masked; Corollary 9.1) and its EDT act-values at $d_E$ average the hypothetical instance (present on every run) with the real one under the observation-event conditioning — Proposition 10's myopic functional — and endorse two-boxing; $(2,2)$ is not optimal. Fairness is what collapses the average (Step 1).
- **Without veridicality/recording**: Told-You-So $B_P$ (§7.1) is **strongly fair** (both fibers singletons!) yet take-5 is tremble-EDT-consistent: under $C_0^\varepsilon$, $\nu_\varepsilon(\cdot \mid O_5) = \delta_{(5,5)}$ exactly (the $O_5$-leaf is reachable only via the draw m=5 at the root — selection, Remark 3.4), so the calibrated state at $d_5$ has $A^+ = \{m{=}5\}$ and EDT approves taking 5; $V(C_0) = 5 < 10$. $B_P$ fails subtree-veridicality at the root ($O_5$ false at the leaves $(10,\cdot)$ below it) and fails recording at $d_5$. So the fair class without the veridicality hypothesis still contains Löb-style traps; FRec is the third load-bearing hypothesis. [checked against §7.1's own statements]
- **Deterministic $C$ not needed**: the proof above runs on support conditions; mixed maximizers land on ties, where every mixture attains $\max_a W_C(q_a)$.

### 3.3 The converse, and "no Newcomblike problems" made precise

**FR-12 [checked] (optimal $\not\Rightarrow$ tremble-EDT).** Same two-point shape with payoffs: out → $0$; in → x → $0$, y → $-1$. $C = $ (out, y) is $V$-optimal ($V = 0 = \max$). Under $C^\varepsilon$: $v_\varepsilon(d_2, x) = 0 > -1 + O(\varepsilon)= v_\varepsilon(d_2, y)$, so $T_{\mathrm{EDT}}$ rejects y at every $\varepsilon$: optimal but not tremble-EDT-consistent. The failure is exactly trembling-hand imperfection at an off-path tie.

**FR-13 [derived] (existence).** On fair + fully-recorded trees a tremble-EDT-consistent procedure exists: backward-induct; at each fiber the functions $v_\varepsilon(d, \cdot)$ (with lower levels already fixed) are rational in $\varepsilon$, so argmax stabilizes for small $\varepsilon$ (lexicographic selection); no self-succession means $v_\varepsilon$ at a fiber depends only on strictly lower levels, so the construction is well-founded. Combined with FR-11: $\emptyset \neq \{\text{tremble-EDT-consistent}\} \subseteq \{V\text{-optimal}\}$, inclusion strict exactly at tremble-dominated ties.

**Definition (Newcomblike, three senses).** On a problem class $\mathfrak{B}$ with a calibration sense fixed: (N1) *value divergence*: some EDT-consistent (in the stated sense) procedure fails $V$-optimality; (N2) *set divergence*: the EDT-consistent set ≠ the optimal set; (N3) *updateful/updateless divergence*: the node-level verdict differs from the policy-level (relocated-root) verdict on corresponding problems.

**FR-14 [derived] ("no Newcomblike problems on the fair class", precise form).** On strongly fair, fully recorded trees, with EDT taken in the tremble-consistent sense: **N1 fails** (FR-11) — every EDT-approved procedure is optimal; **N3 fails** — the relocated form of a fair tree is $\sim$-trivial (fibers already fair; relocation is idempotent up to $\sim$ on the fair class), Kuhn-style action/policy equivalence holds because no self-succession means behavioral = shared-seed on each path [derived; the Kuhn attribution is [reconstructed] from the game-theory literature via slop §3, but the tree-level fact used — one consultation per run makes the two semantics agree — is elementary]; **N2 survives only as refinement** (FR-12): EDT is *more selective* than optimality, never *worse*. With EDT taken strictly (untrembled) instead, N1 holds even on fair trees — §3.2's imperfect-equilibrium example, (out, y) against the 4-payoff — so the tremble ingredient is not eliminable and must be named whenever the slogan is used. Conversely the almost-fair class properly contains N1-problems (TN-V2, the no-doubt mugging), and relocation carries each to a $\sim$-equivalent fair problem where N1 fails: that is the pipeline, end to end.

## 4. The two repairs, and the third (Task 4)

**Superconditioning** (Proposition 2, read as a repair): keep the tree; enrich to $2^{\mathrm{Leaves}}$; replace each queried state by occurrence-conditioned statistics; observations become $\mathrm{occ}(d)$. As stated Prop 2 *assumes* per-run SSC and exhibits OC; as a repair of an OC-but-not-SSC input it means: overwrite the states with $\mu(\cdot \mid \mathrm{occ}(d))$ — and there the anthropic fork opens (per-run or per-occurrence weights, SSA/SIA), because the input's fibers are not fair and FR-1 does not close it.

**FR-15 [derived] (comparison).** Let $B$ be almost fair, OC-calibrated for $C$.
(i) *Same map up to $\sim$*: $B \sim B^{\mathrm{ssc}} \sim \mathrm{Rel}_U(B)$ — superconditioning changes no tree and no payoffs (law-set unchanged, trivially); relocation preserves the law-set (FR-6). All three descriptions are one $\sim$-class.
(ii) *Different representatives, different guarantees*: $B^{\mathrm{ssc}}$ is still structurally unfair; what its honest states buy is Theorem 2's occurrence-conditioned evaluator, i.e. coherence/EDT+SSA ratifiability — a **necessary** condition with bad equilibria (Q4), no backward-induction leverage. $\mathrm{Rel}_U(B)$ is fair; FR-11 upgrades the guarantee to **optimality** (given trembles + recording). This is the exact content of "the two repairs point in different directions" (slop §5): one repairs the *labels* to match the structure, the other repairs the *structure* to match the labels.
(iii) *The anthropic choice*: consumed by superconditioning (which weighting), not by relocation (the relocated node is consulted once per run; its calibration is Definition 11's unconditioned prior calibration — no location credence exists to dispute). [scrubbed] On the tree side, the point that one may not even need anthropic uncertainty is a theorem-shaped point **for** relocation over superconditioning.
(iv) *Who supplies the prior*: on the tree side, the chance labels above/below the insertion point supply $P_{\hat s}$ via the calibrate step; on the agent side — an agent handed the action-level description and repairing it itself — the number must come from the agent's prior state $s^\circ$. Same map, two implementations; "which prior?" (the counterlogical worry) is a real parameter only in the agent-side implementation.
(v) *The policy interpretation $\rho$*: relocation materializes it (pol-coordinates; FR-2/FR-8); superconditioning never needs it (the decision stays at action level, and post-lift the act-event conditioning is well-defined because occ separates instances). $\rho$ is the price of the optimality-grade guarantee: you buy backward induction by paying a self-model. This matches Remark 3.8(iii)'s currying observation: relocation is the equipped design made structural.

**The third transformation** ([scrubbed]). Construal adopted here — **ATTRIBUTION-UNVETTED**: the agent-side implementation of relocate-then-calibrate, $(B, s^\circ) \mapsto \mathrm{Rel}_U(B)$ with $\hat s := s^\circ$-derived. Under that construal FR-11 is the "makes EDT and UDT agree" clause, proved; the anthropic-cost and prior-cost ledger is (iii)–(iv). Whether instead a state-side map (a superconditioning variant with the prior supplying the occurrence weights) is intended is not determined; the two differ exactly by ledger items (ii)–(iii).

## 5. Almost-fair vs observation-calibration; Cartesian frames (Tasks 1/4 residue)

**FR-16 [derived, with a counterexample bounding it].** [scrubbed] The conjectured correspondence of almost-fairness with observation-calibration: the match is real but not an equivalence. OC (Definitions 8–10) is a *statistical* property of $(B, C, \text{states})$; almost-fairness is *structural* (no nesting). The true relations: (a) OC deliberately tolerates simulation nodes — $\mathrm{occ}(d) \setminus \{\lambda \models O_d\}$ is unconstrained (Remark 3.4) — and non-isomorphic simulation members are exactly what makes a non-nested fiber unfair; so the OC-honest-but-SSC-dishonest region and the almost-fair-but-unfair region overlap on all the thread's examples (no-doubt mugging, TN-V1/V2). (b) But neither contains the other: Told-You-So is strongly fair (zero unfairness budget) yet its selection structure is the OC-pathology trembles-plus-recording must handle (FR §3.2); conversely an unfair tree can fail OC outright (stipulate wildly wrong states). The defensible sentence: **observation-calibration is the honesty standard that does not see unfairness; strong-SSC is the standard that does; almost-fairness is the structural condition under which the gap is repairable at no cost to the outcome laws.**
