# Trees ↔ Cartesian frames: the correspondence, worked

Sources: v2 = `decision-problems-v2.md` (all Definition/Proposition numbers cite it); [scrubbed]; slop = `fable-slop-notes.md` (unvetted prior notes); CF-paper = arXiv 2109.10996 text extract (`cf-paper.txt`, page markers); post 11 = local full text `11-eight-definitions-observability-full.md`. Confidence marks per the brief: [checked] / [derived] / [reconstructed] / [guess]. All numbered claims here are CF-#, disjoint from slop's numbering.

Standing CF facts used (from the local sources, not memory): frame $C=(A,E,\cdot)$ over $W$; morphism $(g,h):C\to D$ with $a\cdot h(f)=g(a)\star f$; biextensional equivalence $\simeq$; $\mathrm{Commit}^B$, $\mathrm{Assume}^F$ (CF-paper Defs 28–29, subsets of $A$ resp. $E$); additive subagent $\lhd_+$ = committing definition (Def 18), multiplicative subagent $\lhd_\times$ = externalizing definition (Def 19); observability of a finite partition $V$ of $W$: eight equivalent definitions (post 11), of which I use: conditional-policies ($\forall f:V\to A\ \exists a_f\ \forall e:\ f(v(a_f\cdot e))\cdot e=a_f\cdot e$), assuming ($C\simeq \&_i \mathrm{Assume}_{S_i}(C)$), multiplicative ($C\simeq\bigotimes_i C_i$, $C_i$ powerless outside $S_i$), internalizing–externalizing. "Powerless outside $S$": $\forall e,a_0,a_1:\ a_0\cdot e\notin S \Rightarrow a_0\cdot e=a_1\cdot e$ (post 11 §4.1). Post 11's equivalence proofs require $V$ finite and $W$ nonempty — satisfied throughout (finite trees).

Note on "Assume at a world-subset": CF's Assume takes $F\subseteq E$. Post 11 writes $\mathrm{Assume}_{S}(C)$ for $S\subseteq W$; its §3 proof shows the intended reading: when membership is column-determined, $E$ splits as $E_{S_1}\sqcup\dots\sqcup E_{S_n}$ and $\mathrm{Assume}_{S_i}:=\mathrm{Assume}^{E_{S_i}}$. I write $E^\forall_S:=\{e: \forall a,\ a\cdot e\in S\}$ and $E^\exists_S:=\{e:\exists a,\ a\cdot e\in S\}$; column-determinedness = ($E^\exists_S=E^\forall_S$ for every cell).

## 1. The dictionary

**CF-1 (Definition: induced frames).** Let $B$ be a concrete decision problem (v2 Def 5). Write $D_B$ for its set of queried decision-points, $\mathrm{Ch}(B)$ for its chance nodes, $\mathbf{A}_B:=\prod_{d\in D_B}A_d$ (*policies*: deterministic procedures restricted to $B$'s points — one answer per POINT, per v2 Def 4/Remark 2.2), $\mathbf{E}_B:=\prod_{n\in \mathrm{Ch}(B)}\mathrm{children}(n)$ (*chance profiles*: one child per chance node — the "lazy coin" convention: every chance node gets an outcome, consulted or not). For $(\pi,\varepsilon)$ the run is deterministic and reaches a unique leaf $\ell(\pi,\varepsilon)$: at a chance node $n$ follow $\varepsilon(n)$, at a decision node $q$ follow $\pi(d_q)$ (structural recursion on the finite tree; total). Three frames:

- the *fine frame* $\mathrm{Fr}^\circ(B):=(\mathbf{A}_B,\mathbf{E}_B,\ \ell(\cdot,\cdot))$ over $W^\circ:=\mathrm{Leaves}(B)$;
- the *(extensional) policy frame* $\mathrm{Fr}(B):=(\mathbf{A}_B,\mathbf{E}_B,\ \phi\circ\ell)$ over $W:=\mathrm{Image}(\phi\circ\ell)\subseteq \Omega\times\mathbb{R}$, where $\phi(\ell):=(\lambda(\ell),r(\ell))$ — the readout matching $\widehat B$'s codomain;
- the *node frame* $\mathrm{Fr}^{\mathrm{nd}}(B)$: same but with agent set $\prod_{q\ \mathrm{decision\ node}}A_{d_q}$ (v2 Prop 5's *assignments*), the run following $\sigma(q)$ at $q$.

Default frame: $\mathrm{Fr}(B)$; default $W$: the image of the extensional readout. What the frames FORGET: all chance labels $\beta_n$, all states $s_d$, all observations $O_d$, and all payoff-probability structure — the functor is possibilistic. $O_d$ re-enters only as imported data via CF-2.

**CF-2 (Definition: event partitions; transfer) [derived].** For $X\in\mathcal E$ let $S_X:=\{(\omega,r)\in W:\omega\models X\}$ and $V_X:=\{S_X,\ W\setminus S_X\}$ (cells restricted to $W=\mathrm{Image}$; empty cells dropped — post 11's partitions are of $W$, and taking $W=$ image is the convention that keeps "the rest of $\Omega$" from padding partitions with unreachable cells). Fine-to-coarse transfer: if a partition $V$ of $W$ pulls back along $\phi$ to $V^\circ$ of $W^\circ$ and $V^\circ$ is observable in $\mathrm{Fr}^\circ(B)$, then $V$ is observable in $\mathrm{Fr}(B)$: the same $a_f$ works, since leafwise outcome equality implies $\phi$-outcome equality. The converse fails in general (coarse equality is cheaper). So: positive results are strongest proved in $\mathrm{Fr}^\circ$ (mine below are — the conditional policies constructed reproduce the very leaf), negative results strongest in $\mathrm{Fr}$ (mine are computed there).

**CF-3 (mixed procedures) [derived].** The frame has no convex structure; probabilistic choice enters only as distributions over the frame's sets. A distribution over $\mathbf{A}_B=\prod_d A_d$ draws one answer per point per run and reuses it at every node carrying the point — exactly v2 Q12's *shared-seed* semantics (product distributions = independent seeds per point; general distributions = correlation devices across points, FDT territory, out of scope per v2 §8(iii)). v2's official Definition-6 semantics (independent redraws per NODE) is a distribution over $\mathrm{Agent}(\mathrm{Fr}^{\mathrm{nd}}(B))$-behavior only in the node frame; it is NOT representable as a mixture over $\mathrm{Agent}(\mathrm{Fr}(B))$ when some path carries two nodes of one point. The two agree in leaf-law whenever no path contains two nodes of one point: two runs differing only in draws at off-path nodes coincide, and per run each point is consulted at most once. Separation = Kuhn's-theorem territory [reconstructed for the name; the numbers checked]: on v2 Prop 5(c)'s absent-minded driver, behavioral value $\max_x (1-x)(3x+1)=4/3$ at $x=1/3$; mixtures over $\mathbf{A}_B=\{a,b\}$ get $\max(0,1)=1$.

**CF-4 (self-succession = a proper diagonal Commit) [checked].** The diagonal $\Delta:=\{(\pi(d_{q}))_q : \pi\in\mathbf{A}_B\}\subseteq\mathrm{Agent}(\mathrm{Fr}^{\mathrm{nd}}(B))$ gives $\mathrm{Fr}(B)\cong \mathrm{Commit}^{\Delta}(\mathrm{Fr}^{\mathrm{nd}}(B))$, hence $\mathrm{Fr}(B)\lhd_+\mathrm{Fr}^{\mathrm{nd}}(B)$ (CF-paper Claim 30(1)). The commit is proper — outcomes vanish from the image — exactly under self-succession: AMD's payoff-4 leaf (exit at the second node having continued at the first) is in $\mathrm{Image}(\mathrm{Fr}^{\mathrm{nd}})$ but not $\mathrm{Image}(\mathrm{Fr})$. So v2 Prop 5's procedures-vs-assignments gap is, frame-side, the properness of one additive-subagent relation. "One point at several nodes" is invisible in the agent set (one coordinate) and visible only through the evaluation map consulting the coordinate several times.

**CF-5 (the local frame of a point is a multiplicative subagent) [checked].** For queried $d$ define
$$\mathrm{Loc}_d(B):=\big(A_d,\ \mathbf{A}_{-d}\times\mathbf{E}_B,\ a\cdot_d(\pi_{-d},\varepsilon):=\phi\circ\ell(\pi_{-d}[d\mapsto a],\varepsilon)\big),$$
the CF rendering of v2's point-deviation $C[d\mapsto a]$: the $d$-decision with the rest of the policy externalized. Then $\mathrm{Loc}_d(B)\lhd_\times \mathrm{Fr}(B)$ on the nose, by the externalizing definition (CF-paper Def 19) with $X=A_d$, $Y=\mathbf{A}_{-d}$, $Z=\mathbf{E}_B$, $f(a,\pi_{-d},\varepsilon)=\phi\circ\ell(\pi_{-d}[d\mapsto a],\varepsilon)$: $(X,Y\times Z,\diamond)=\mathrm{Loc}_d$ and $(X\times Y,Z,\bullet)\cong\mathrm{Fr}(B)$. This is where per-point observability claims live (CF-13 shows the global frame is the wrong locus for mid-tree points).

**CF-6 (Definitions: possibilistic hypotheses).** All relative to $B$ alone (procedure-free), the possibilistic shadows of v2's statistical notions — named to keep calibration senses distinct:
- (V$_d$) *no-simulation at $d$*: every $d$-node is subtree-veridical in the possibilistic sense — every leaf below it satisfies $O_d$. (v2 Def 7's subtree-veridicality; Remark 3.3: failures = Omega-style simulations, the type's default.)
- (C$_d$) *possibilistic coverage*: every leaf with $\lambda(\ell)\models O_d$ has a $d$-node on its root path.
- *One-round*: no decision node strictly below another decision node.
- *Strong structural fairness* (imported from slop §1, the notes' proposed notion — a property of $B$): all nodes of each fiber $\{q:d_q=d\}$ have isomorphic labelled subtrees (labels: $\beta$, points, leaf $(\lambda,r)$).

**CF-7 (spurious queries are frame-invisible) [checked].** (a) Inserting a query of a fresh point $d_0$ whose answer-edges lead to identical subtrees multiplies the agent set by $A_{d_0}$ but makes rows constant in that coordinate: biextensional collapse deletes it, so the frame is $\simeq$-unchanged. (b) Inserting a spurious query of an existing point changes neither $\mathbf{A}$ nor any row: the frame is literally unchanged, while $\mathrm{occ}(d)$ and $\#_d$ move (v2's extensional-shadow discussion). So the frame sides with $\widehat B$ against SSC on spurious queries — per-run/per-occurrence anthropics are frame-invisible (they are facts about $\mu$, which the functor forgets).

**CF-8 (observables are environment-determined; Obs ∩ Ctrl = ∅) [derived].** If $\{S,W\setminus S\}$ is observable in $C=(A,E,\cdot)$ then membership is column-determined: were $a_0\cdot e\in S$, $a_1\cdot e\notin S$, take $a\in\mathrm{if}(S,a_1,a_0)$; both cases contradict. Consequently an observable $S$ with some column in $S$ and some outside is neither ensurable nor preventable: no agent-controlled event is observable. (Matches the index's Ctrl = Ensure ∩ Prevent; derived here, not cited.)

## 2. The local and one-round theorems

**CF-9 (powerlessness ⟹ observability) [derived].** In any frame, if the agent is powerless outside $S$ then $\{S,W\setminus S\}$ is observable. First, membership is column-determined: if $a_0\cdot e\notin S$ then $a_1\cdot e=a_0\cdot e\notin S$; if $a_0\cdot e\in S$ and $a_1\cdot e\notin S$, powerlessness at $a_1$ gives $a_1\cdot e=a_0\cdot e\in S$, contradiction. Then for $f$ with cells $\{S,\neg S\}$ take $a_f:=f(S)$: on $\in S$ columns the requirement is trivial; on $\notin S$ columns $a_f\cdot e=f(\neg S)\cdot e$ by powerlessness. $\square$ (The converse fails: post 11's umbrella frame $C_0$ observes rain while having power in both cells — the agent set contains the conditionals. CF-11(b) imports this into a tree.)

**CF-10 (Local Theorem) [derived].** Fix queried $d$, $S:=S_{O_d}$. Consider (i) (V$_d$): no simulation of $d$; (ii) $\mathrm{Loc}_d(B)$'s agent is powerless outside $S$; (iii) $\{S,W\setminus S\}$ is observable in $\mathrm{Loc}_d(B)$. Then (i) ⟹ (ii) ⟹ (iii), and neither converse holds (CF-11).

*Proof of (i)⟹(ii).* Fix $e=(\pi_{-d},\varepsilon)$, $a_0,a_1\in A_d$. The runs of $\pi_{-d}[d\mapsto a_0]$ and $\pi_{-d}[d\mapsto a_1]$ under $\varepsilon$ walk identical edges unless/until a $d$-node is reached (all other labels agree; chance frozen by $\varepsilon$). If the $a_0$-run reaches no $d$-node, the walks never separate: $a_0\cdot_d e=a_1\cdot_d e$. If it reaches one, its leaf lies below a $d$-node, so by (i) $a_0\cdot_d e\in S$ — the powerlessness antecedent is vacuous. (ii)⟹(iii) is CF-9; note also the proof runs verbatim in $\mathrm{Fr}^\circ$, so observability holds at the fine readout too (CF-2). $\square$

Coverage is NOT needed, and (CF-12 note) is invisible to observability altogether: a column whose run never consults $d$ but lands in an $O_d$-world satisfies the conditional-policy requirement trivially (all rows equal there, and the $a_f=f(S)$ case analysis passes). Checked on a mugging variant with a third root-chance branch to an unconsulted $(T,\bot,0)$-leaf: observable at $\mathrm{Loc}_d$ despite coverage failing. Frame-side, CF-observability tracks the $\mathrm{occ}(d)\setminus\{\lambda\models O_d\}$ half of v2 Remark 3.10's symmetric difference (simulation) and ignores the $\{\lambda\models O_d\}\setminus\mathrm{occ}(d)$ half (unconsulted observation-worlds).

**CF-11 (strictness) [checked].** (a) (ii) without (i): an inert simulation — a $d$-node off $O_d$ whose answer-edges lead to identical subtrees (a spurious query, CF-7) sims $d$ while leaving the $d$-coordinate inert outside $S$. (b) (iii) without (ii): the *umbrella-mugging*. Point $d$, $A_d=\{u,n,u{\leftrightarrow}r,u{\leftrightarrow}s\}$, $O_d=\{$rain worlds$\}$; root coin $r/s$; the $r$-node consults $d$ (edges to $ur,nr,ur,nr$), the $s$-node consults $d$ too — a genuine, outcome-relevant simulation (edges to $us,ns,ns,us$). $\mathrm{Fr}=\mathrm{Loc}_d$ is exactly post 11's $C_0$ matrix: $\{R,S\}$ observable (post 11 §2.1's table; spot-checked $f(R)=u,f(S)=n\mapsto a_f=u{\leftrightarrow}r$), yet $u\cdot s=us\neq ns=n\cdot s$: not powerless outside $S_R$, and the $s$-node is not subtree-veridical. Diagnosis: the action set already contains the conditional policies — the point is its own relocation; observability = "action set closed under conditioning on the partition, given the coupling", to which no-simulation is one sufficient route (kill the coupling) and policy-richness the other (absorb it).

**CF-12 (One-Round Global Theorem) [derived].** Let $B$ be one-round with (V$_d$) at every queried $d$ and the $O_d$ pairwise disjoint as events on realized worlds (which (C$_d$) + one-round implies: a leaf satisfying $O_d\wedge O_{d'}$ would need a $d$- and a $d'$-node on one path). Let $V_{\mathrm{obs}}:=\{S_{O_d}\}_{d\in D_B}\cup\{S_0\}$, $S_0$ the remaining image-worlds. Then $V_{\mathrm{obs}}$ is observable in $\mathrm{Fr}(B)$ (and in $\mathrm{Fr}^\circ(B)$), hence
$$\mathrm{Fr}(B)\ \simeq\ \underset{d}{\&}\ \mathrm{Assume}_{S_{O_d}}(\mathrm{Fr}(B))\ \&\ \mathrm{Assume}_{S_0}(\mathrm{Fr}(B))$$
(assuming definition) and $\mathrm{Fr}(B)\simeq\bigotimes$ of subagents each powerless outside its cell (multiplicative definition, post 11 §4.2).

*Proof.* In a one-round tree the region above any decision node is chance-only, so which decision node (if any) a run meets is a function of $\varepsilon$ alone. Given $f:V_{\mathrm{obs}}\to\mathbf{A}_B$, set $\pi_f(d):=f(S_{O_d})(d)$. Fix $\varepsilon$. If the $\varepsilon$-route meets no decision node, every policy yields the same leaf; every requirement is trivial. If it meets a $d$-node $q$: the run of $(\pi_f,\varepsilon)$ and the run of $(f(S_{O_d}),\varepsilon)$ both reach $q$ (chance-only above), draw the same answer $\pi_f(d)=f(S_{O_d})(d)$, and share $\varepsilon$ below: same leaf. Its world satisfies $O_d$ by (V$_d$) and no other $O_{d'}$ by disjointness, so the outcome's cell is $S_{O_d}$ and $f(v(\pi_f\cdot\varepsilon))\cdot\varepsilon=f(S_{O_d})\cdot\varepsilon=\pi_f\cdot\varepsilon$. $\square$

Calibration-sense tie-in, stated precisely: (V)+(C) are exactly the hypotheses of v2 Prop 3 (the veridicality bridge), under which per-run SSC and strict observation calibration become the same equation. CF-12 says the same hypotheses (indeed (V) + disjointness; coverage only feeds disjointness) are what make the observation system CF-observable. But the biconditional fails: CF-11(b)'s umbrella-mugging is one-round, coverage holds, (V) fails at the $s$-node, and $V_{\mathrm{obs}}$ is observable. So: no-simulation is sufficient, not necessary; the gap is policy-rich action sets.

**CF-13 (the sequential obstruction, and why $\mathrm{Loc}_d$ is the right locus) [checked].** *Look-decision tree*: root queries $d'$ ($O_{d'}=\top$, actions look/leave); leave → leaf $w_{\mathrm{leave}}\not\models O_d$; look → a $d$-node ($O_d=$ "opened", actions $p,q$ → leaves $w_p,w_q\models O_d$). Strongly fair (singleton fibers), (V) and (C) hold everywhere, and the tree is everyday-sequential. In $\mathrm{Fr}(B)$ ($\mathbf{E}$ trivial): rows $(\mathrm{leave},\cdot)\mapsto w_{\mathrm{leave}}$, $(\mathrm{look},p)\mapsto w_p$, $(\mathrm{look},q)\mapsto w_q$ — membership in $S_{O_d}$ is row-dependent, so by CF-8 $O_d$ is NOT observable in the global frame: "I face $d$" is a controllable of the earlier self. In $\mathrm{Loc}_d$ (agent $\{p,q\}$, environment $\{\mathrm{look},\mathrm{leave}\}$): powerless outside $S_{O_d}$ ($p,q$ agree on the leave-column), so observable by CF-9. Moral: for points below other decisions, global observability fails for reasons orthogonal to spoofing; the multiplicative-subagent localization (CF-5) — externalize the other coordinates — is forced, not decorative. (This is the task's "reach $q$ depends on the agent's own earlier draws" obstruction, discharged by $\lhd_\times$ + CF-10 rather than by the global frame. A stage-peeled global statement via iterated Commit/Assume is the Time-post's machinery; not needed for the per-point claims and left unproved here — see Unsettled (1).)

## 3. The central conjecture (slop Claim 2.2): verdict

**CF-14 (refutation of the biconditional as stated) [checked].** Slop Claim 2.2 [guess there]: veridical labelling ⟹ (strong structural fairness ⟺ every chance-determined "observation" CF-observable in the induced frame). Three separations:

(a) *Fairness is not a function of the frame at all.* Two trees $P,P'$: root coin routes to $q_1/q_2$, both carrying $d$, with identical subtrees except one interior chance label ($\beta=\tfrac12$ vs $\tfrac13$); leaf labels equal (upstream coin unrecorded in worlds). Labelled-subtree isomorphism holds in $P$, fails in $P'$: $P$ fair, $P'$ unfair. But $\mathrm{Fr}(P)=\mathrm{Fr}(P')$ identically — the functor forgets $\beta$ (CF-1). No frame property, observability included, can be equivalent to strong fairness. Fairness is (in part) a probabilistic-exchangeability condition, and CF forgets exactly the probabilities.

(b) *Observable-observations without fairness* (kills ⟸ under the "queried $O_d$'s" reading): the (S3) Smoking-Lesion tree — one point, $O_d=\top$ queried on both $\ell$-branches with different $\gamma_\ell$ below — has its (trivial) claimed partition observable while the fiber's subtrees are non-isomorphic.

(c) *Fairness without observability of chance events* (kills ⟹ under the "all chance-determined partitions" reading): the relocated mugging (root point $\hat d$, then per-branch coins) is strongly fair (singleton fiber), yet the coin partition is not observable: with lazy-coin profiles $(\varepsilon_{\mathrm{pay}},\varepsilon_{\mathrm{ref}})$, column $(T,H)$ sends pay to a $T$-world and refuse to an $H$-world — membership is not column-determined. Also note "chance-determined" is itself representation-laden: the fair representation necessarily duplicates the coin per branch (putting one coin above the fiber forces non-isomorphic subtrees whenever the coin is world-recorded), and duplicated coins are exactly what breaks column-determinedness.

**Verdict.** The true content splits as: (i) the TRUE half is CF-10 + CF-12 — *no-simulation* (possibilistic subtree-veridicality, v2's anti-simulation condition), not fairness, is what makes claimed observations CF-observable, locally always and globally in one-round trees; fairness is idle in the hypothesis (it neither implies (V) — all-sim fibers can be isomorphic — nor follows from it). (ii) The FALSE half is every biconditional reading: CF-14(a)–(c). (iii) What fairness actually contributes is tree-side, not frame-side: it closes the per-run/per-occurrence fork (slop Claim 1.1) and licenses the DAG collapse (slop Claim 1.3) — both facts about $\mu$, which the frame forgets (CF-7). Even the tempting "fair fiber ⟹ biextensionally duplicate environment coordinates" fails when routing into the fiber is agent-dependent: with $d'$ routing L→$q_1$, R→$q_2$ (isomorphic subtrees, coordinates $t,t'$), the off-diagonal columns $(t,t')$, $t\neq t'$, are genuine — the frame remembers that the agent can decorrelate the two instances, which the probabilistic DAG-quotient deliberately forgets. [checked on the 2-node example]

## 4. Worked examples (the calculations)

**CF-15 (Counterfactual Mugging, v2 Prop 6's $B_1$) [checked].** One point $d$, $O_T=\{\mathrm{coin}=T\}$; one chance node. $\mathrm{Fr}(B_1)=\mathrm{Loc}_d(B_1)$:

| | $\varepsilon=H$ | $\varepsilon=T$ |
|---|---|---|
| pay | $((H,\bot,1),\,+y)$ | $((T,\mathrm{pay},0),\,-x)$ |
| refuse | $((H,\bot,0),\,0)$ | $((T,\mathrm{ref},0),\,0)$ |

$S:=S_{O_T}$ = the $T$-column's worlds. Membership IS column-determined (the coin is not selected/controlled). Conditional policy $f(S)=\mathrm{pay}$, $f(\neg S)=\mathrm{refuse}$ needs a row equal to pay's on $T$ and refuse's on $H$: pay fails at $H$ ($(H,\bot,1)\neq(H,\bot,0)$), refuse fails at $T$. Not observable — slop Claim 2.1 re-verified inside the dictionary; and the failure is pure coupling: powerlessness outside $S$ fails at the $H$-column, i.e. exactly at the simulation node ((V$_d$) fails there), per CF-10's contrapositive. Deleting Omega's coupling (pay's $H$-entry $\to((H,\bot,0),0)$) makes the rows agree off $S$: powerless outside $S$, observable (CF-9) — and tree-side the $H$-node's answer-edges now lead to identical subtrees: the simulation has become a spurious query, frame-invisible (CF-7). "Decoupling = inerting the simulation."

**CF-16 (Transparent Newcomb V1 vs V2, v2 §7.2: the wedge IS an observability difference, at the right locus) [checked].** Points $d_F,d_E$; policies $(x,y)\in\{1,2\}^2$ ($1$=large-only, $2$=both); worlds $(\mathrm{fill},\mathrm{act})$ with payoffs $(1,1)\!=\!L$, $(1,2)\!=\!L{+}S$, $(0,1)\!=\!0$, $(0,2)\!=\!S$. Chance labels depend on the sim answers via distinct chance nodes per answer (the Def-5 encoding of "chance sends the run with probability $b(x)$").

V1: chance nodes $n_1,n_2$ (one per $x$), $\mathbf{E}=\{F,E\}^2$, evaluation $(x,y)\cdot(\varepsilon_1,\varepsilon_2)$: if $\varepsilon_x=F$ then $(1,x)$ else $(0,y)$.
V2: chance nodes $n_{xy}$, $\mathbf{E}=\{F,E\}^4$: if $\varepsilon_{xy}=F$ then $(1,x)$ else $(0,y)$.

Global frames: in BOTH variants $S_F$ is not column-determined (V1: column $(F,E)$ sends rows $(1,\cdot)$ to fill-1, rows $(2,\cdot)$ to fill-0; V2: a column with $\varepsilon_{11}=F,\varepsilon_{12}=E$ splits rows $(1,1)/(1,2)$), so no global observability difference between V1 and V2. The difference is at $\mathrm{Loc}_{d_E}$:

- V1, $\mathrm{Loc}_{d_E}$: agent $y$, environment $(x,\varepsilon_1,\varepsilon_2)$. Membership in $S_E$ is $[\varepsilon_x=E]$ — $y$-free; and on $S_F$-outcomes the leaf is $(1,x)$ — $y$-free. Powerless outside $S_E$, hence $\{S_E,S_F\}$ observable at $d_E$ (CF-9). Consistent with CF-10: in V1, $d_E$ is queried only at the real empty node, which is subtree-veridical.
- V2, $\mathrm{Loc}_{d_E}$: agent $y$, environment $(x,\vec\varepsilon)$. Take $x=1$, $\varepsilon_{11}=F$, $\varepsilon_{12}=E$: $y=1\mapsto(1,1)\in S_F$, $y=2\mapsto(0,2)\in S_E$. Membership depends on $y$: not column-determined, hence NOT observable (CF-8). Tree-side: V2 sims $d_E$ at the root, so (V$_{d_E}$) fails.

Alignment with the draft: v2 Prop 9 proves the optimal act at the empty box flips between V1 (no optimal policy one-boxes at $E$) and V2 (one-box at $E$ when $(2p{-}1)L>S$). Frame-side: where the empty-box observation is a true Observation at its own decision (V1), updating on it is optimal-compatible; where it is coupling-laden (V2), the optimum treats it at the policy level. The same computation shows $O_F$ fails at $\mathrm{Loc}_{d_F}$ in both variants (the root sim of $d_F$ couples in V1 and V2 alike): the full-box "observation" is never a proper Observation,  [scrubbed], while the V1/V2 difference lives entirely at $d_E$ — Corollary 9.1 transposed.

**CF-17 (Told-You-So, v2 §7.1: prophecies are controllables) [checked].** No chance nodes: $\mathbf{E}=\{*\}$; $\mathbf{A}=A_{d_5}\times A_{d_{10}}$. Rows: $(5,\cdot)\mapsto(5,5)$-world; $(10,10)\mapsto(10,10)$; $(10,5)\mapsto(10,5)$. Membership in $S_{O_5}=\{n{=}5\}$ is row-dependent: by CF-8, $O_5$ (and $O_{10}$) is not observable — indeed the agent can both ensure and prevent $\{n{=}5\}$: the announcement is a controllable wearing an observation's label. (V$_{d_5}$) fails at the root ($d_5$-node with $(10,\cdot)$-leaves below — v2 Remark 3.4's selection/routing node, which v2 already notes fails recording). Prop 8's trap, frame-side: a zero-respecting $C_0$ treats $P_{s_5}(m{=}10)=0$ as binding and thereby plays $\mathrm{Commit}^{\{(5,\cdot)\}}(\mathrm{Fr})$ — a voluntary additive subagent whose image has lost the $(10,10)$ outcome. The stipulated zero deletes a row of the frame, and the row was the point of the game.

**CF-18 (Smoking Lesion, v2 §7.3) [checked].** (S3): one point ($O_d=\top$), $\mathbf{A}=\{m{=}1,m{=}0\}$, $\mathbf{E}=\{0,1\}_\ell\times\{0,1\}^4$ (a $k$-draw per $(\ell,m)$-node); row $m$, column $(\ell,\vec k)\mapsto(\ell,m,k_{\ell m})$. The claimed partition ($\top$) is trivially observable — the frame endorses no complaint against the claim. The decision-RELEVANT partition $S_{\{\ell=1\}}$ is column-determined but not observable: $f(S_{\ell=1})=m_1$, $f(S_{\ell=0})=m_0$ with $m_1\neq m_0$ needs a row matching row $m_1$ on $\ell{=}1$-columns and row $m_0$ on $\ell{=}0$-columns; rows are constant in $\ell$. So in S3 the agent provably cannot condition on the lesion — the frame-side face of v2 Prop 11 (its probabilistic content, Lemma 3's independent-draw screening, is frame-invisible: the functor forgets sampling). The tickle instantiation (Prop 12; distinct points $d_1,d_0$ with $O=\{\ell{=}1\},\{\ell{=}0\}$): $\pi_f:=(\pi_1(d_1),\pi_0(d_0))$ realizes every conditional policy and membership is column-determined — the lesion partition IS observable; equally it is an instance of CF-12 ((V),(C), one-round hold). Slop §1's slogan ("the relocation repair for the lesion is the same move as for the mugging") gets frame content: dissolving Smoking Lesion = making the lesion an Observation.

**CF-19 (absent-minded driver, v2 Prop 5(c)) [checked].** One point at two nested nodes; no chance nodes. $\mathrm{Fr}$: rows $a\mapsto$ payoff-0 leaf, $b\mapsto$ payoff-1 leaf (continue at both); the payoff-4 leaf is unreachable — CF-4's proper commit, with $\mathrm{Fr}^{\mathrm{nd}}$ rows $(a,\cdot)\mapsto 0$, $(b,a)\mapsto 4$, $(b,b)\mapsto 1$. Observability has nothing to say ($O_d$ is one label at both nodes; its partition is trivial on the image): the AMD defect is not an observability defect but an agent-set defect — no row implements node-dependent play, and no mixture over rows implements Definition 6's behavioral value $4/3$ (CF-3: $\max(0,1)=1$ vs $(1-\tfrac13)(3\cdot\tfrac13+1)=\tfrac43$). Fairness excludes the tree by nesting (a finite subtree is never isomorphic to a proper subtree of itself); the frame sees the same fact as the properness of $\mathrm{Fr}(B)\lhd_+\mathrm{Fr}^{\mathrm{nd}}(B)$.

## 5. Seeming-to-observe (task 4)

**CF-20 (Definition: pseudo-observation; the honest companion).** A *pseudo-observation* at queried $d$ is the pair $(\mathrm{Loc}_d(B),\,V_d)$ with $V_d=\{S_{O_d},W\setminus S_{O_d}\}$: a labelled partition claim carried by a multiplicative subagent — the minimal frame-side structure, since the frame itself forgets $O_d$ (CF-1); the claim is exactly what the tree's decision-point adds to the frame. It is a *true Observation* iff $V_d$ is observable in $\mathrm{Loc}_d(B)$. Failure taxonomy:
- Type (a), *selection*: membership not column-determined in $\mathrm{Loc}_d$ — the "observation" is (partly) controlled: Told-You-So; TN-V2 at $d_E$; the look-decision globally (CF-13) though not locally.
- Type (b), *coupling*: column-determined but conditional policies fail: the mugging coin.
- Type (c), *approximate*: powerlessness fails by a small amount — post 11 §4.4's remark that the multiplicative view approximates observation better; the natural frame-side home of a decay claim, though quantifying it needs the probabilities CF forgets (Unsettled (3)).

When membership is column-determined, define the *honest companion*
$$\mathrm{Hon}_d:=\mathrm{Assume}^{E^\forall_S}(\mathrm{Loc}_d)\ \&\ \mathrm{Assume}^{E^\forall_{\neg S}}(\mathrm{Loc}_d),$$
agent $A_d\times A_d$ (a cell-indexed pair of answers), and the *diagonal morphism* $\mathrm{diag}:\mathrm{Loc}_d\to\mathrm{Hon}_d$: $g(a)=(a,a)$, $h$ = identity on columns; $a\cdot h(f)=g(a)\star f$ holds by construction [checked]. Equivalently $\mathrm{Loc}_d\cong\mathrm{Commit}^{\Delta}(\mathrm{Hon}_d)\lhd_+\mathrm{Hon}_d$: seeming-to-observe is being the diagonal commitment inside the frame that could genuinely condition.

**CF-21 (truth test) [derived; checked on the mugging].** With membership column-determined: $V_d$ observable in $\mathrm{Loc}_d$ $\iff$ $\mathrm{Loc}_d\simeq\mathrm{Hon}_d$. (⟸): in $\mathrm{Hon}_d$ the partition is always observable ($a_f:=(f(S)_1,f(\neg S)_2)$ works cell-by-cell), and observability is biextensional-invariant (the additive definition manifestly is). (⟹): the assuming definition gives $\mathrm{Loc}_d\simeq\mathrm{Assume}^{E^\forall_S}(\mathrm{Loc}_d)\,\&\,\mathrm{Assume}^{E^\forall_{\neg S}}(\mathrm{Loc}_d)=\mathrm{Hon}_d$. Mugging: $\mathrm{Hon}_d$ has four pairwise-distinct rows $(a_T,a_H)\mapsto(a_T\cdot T,\,a_H\cdot H)$ — $(p,p)\mapsto(-x,+y)$, $(p,r)\mapsto(-x,0)$, $(r,p)\mapsto(0,+y)$, $(r,r)\mapsto(0,0)$ — while $\mathrm{Loc}_d$ is the 2-row diagonal: not equivalent, pseudo. Decoupled mugging: rows collapse in pairs onto the diagonal: equivalent, true. The gap $\mathrm{Hon}_d\setminus\Delta$ measures the spoof: the conditional selves the claim advertises and the coupling denies.

**CF-22 (what "transforming 'observations' into Observations" does to the frame) [derived].** Three inequivalent normalizations:
- (N1) *un-commit*: replace $\mathrm{Loc}_d$ by $\mathrm{Hon}_d$ — grant the per-cell pair of answers. This is slop §5's relocation with a PRODUCT action set over distinguishable relocated points. Legitimate exactly when the cells are genuinely distinguishable to the agent — tree-side, when the fiber splits into distinct decision-points with the cell as observation. Omega-style spoofing is precisely the denial of this (v2 Remark 2.2's individuation: same point, same answer), so for the mugging N1 is a fiction: $\mathrm{Hon}_d$'s off-diagonal rows are implementable by no procedure.
- (N2) *retract the claim*: keep $\mathrm{Loc}_d$, replace $O_d$ by $\top$. The claimed partition becomes trivial, trivially observable; the decision is owned at the policy level. This is slop §5's relocation for indistinguishable/single-point fibers, and the world's "observation" is not upgraded to conditioning power; the label is corrected instead. Tree-side output: the strongly fair, $O=\top$ representative. Frame-side nothing changes but the claim (never frame data anyway) — which is WHY such decision-points cannot be represented as conditioning-decisions: representation as a conditioning-decision is what fails; the frame itself exists throughout.
- (N3) *updateful deletion* (the hygiene reading absolutized): $\mathrm{Assume}^{E^\forall_S}(\mathrm{Loc}_d)$ — restrict to the claim's columns. Well-defined only when column-determined (type-(a) failures leave contested columns belonging to no cell — the updateful move cannot even file them); where defined it discards the coupling columns rather than pricing them. Post 11 §4.4 is the CF-internal verdict: the additive (updateful) and multiplicative (updateless) factorizations coincide at true Observations and diverge at approximate ones — the crux in CF's own vocabulary, and the formal content, on the post-transformation class, of the claim that EDT and UDT coincide because there are no Newcomblike problems there.

**CF-23 (extensional shadow vs biextensional collapse: neither refines the other) [checked].** Both kill spurious queries (CF-7 = v2's shadow-invariance under spurious insertion). Separations: (i) the $\beta$-pair of CF-14(a): identical frames, different shadows (the shadow keeps probabilities). (ii) The *scrambled-consultation pair*: tree $X$ = fair coin routing to two $d$-nodes with answer-to-leaf maps swapped ($L$: $a\mapsto w_1$, $b\mapsto w_2$; $R$: $a\mapsto w_2$, $b\mapsto w_1$) vs tree $Z$ = fair coin with a spurious $d$-query per branch ($L$: both answers $\mapsto w_1$; $R$: both $\mapsto w_2$). Every procedure, mixed included, has leaf-law $\tfrac12 w_1+\tfrac12 w_2$ in both: $\widehat X=\widehat Z$ as functionals. But $\mathrm{Fr}(X)$ has two distinct rows $(w_1,w_2),(w_2,w_1)$ over columns $\{L,R\}$ and $\mathrm{Fr}(Z)$ collapses to one row: $\not\simeq$. The frame remembers pointwise (counterfactual-profile) act-relevance that every distribution washes out; the shadow remembers the measure the frame forgets. So v2's $\widehat B$ is NOT the tree-side face of biextensional collapse; they are transverse quotients agreeing on the spurious-query kernel.

**CF-24 (relocation, frame-side; the would-pay coordinate) [checked].** For the relocated mugging $\tilde B_1$ (root point $\hat d$, then per-branch lazy coins), restricting the environment to the correlated-coin diagonal, $\mathrm{Assume}^{\{(H,H),(T,T)\}}(\mathrm{Fr}(\tilde B_1))$, reproduces the unrelocated mugging matrix up to post-composition with the world-coarsening that forgets the choice coordinate on $H$-worlds ($B_1$'s $H$-leaves carry $\bot$ there, v2 Prop 6). So: unrelocated frame = (diagonal sub-environment of relocated frame) then (forget would-pay). Relocation undoes two moves at once, separately visible frame-side: un-assuming (the environment regains per-branch coin freedom — off-diagonal columns are the lazy counterfactual coins) and world-refinement (the algebra regains the disposition event — v2 Remark 4.2's richness condition for $\rho$, appearing as exactly the coarsening that separates the two frames). Also: the coin partition is observable in neither ($\mathrm{Fr}(\tilde B_1)$: CF-14(c); the diagonal restriction: CF-15) — relocation does not manufacture an Observation; it re-files the coin as ordinary post-decision chance, which is N2 again.
