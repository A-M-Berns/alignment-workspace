Synthesis of three research threads (verify / cf / repair), each with an adversarial review, against `decision-problems-v2.md` ("v2"; all Definition/Proposition/Theorem numbers cite it), [scrubbed], and `fable-slop-notes.md` ("slop", unvetted prior notes). Marks: [checked] = explicit calculation, performed in a named thread and recomputed by its adversary (I re-verified the headline arithmetic myself); [derived] = full argument in the named notes, surviving adversarial recomputation; [reconstructed] = leans on a named local source; [guess] = conjecture. Calibration senses are named at every use: strict OC (Def 8), masked (Def 9), limit (Def 10), prior calibration (Def 11), per-run / per-occurrence SSC (Def 13), and **strong (structural) fairness** = every queried point's fiber has isomorphic labelled subtrees (a property of the tree alone; slop §1, adopted by all threads).
## 1. What is now established

### A. The crux skeleton

**1. The no-doubt mugging separates the calibration senses, procedure-independently** [checked; verify A1–A2, adversary R1]. v2 Prop 6's $B_1$ with $P_{s_d}(T)=1$: masked-OC-calibrated (Def 9) for *every* procedure via the $C$-independent self-model $m(\mathrm{pay})=q_0$, interior $q_0$ required (boundary: v2 App. B); strictly OC-calibrated exactly at the fixed point $q=q_0$; and *both* SSC senses (Def 13) fail for every procedure, since both branches query $d$, so $\mathrm{occ}(d)=\mathrm{Leaves}$, $\#_d\equiv 1$, and both senses demand $P_{s_d}(T)=\nu(T)=\tfrac12\neq 1$. This OC-yes/SSC-no contrast is the formal skeleton under the whole dispute.

**2. Correction to a collapse argument** [checked; verify B1, adversary R2]. The claim that all nodes with the same subjective state have equivalent subtrees as well is false as an implication: the AMD (Prop 5(c)) at $q=0$ has one point (hence one state) at two nodes whose downtree laws coincide ($\delta_{\text{payoff-1 leaf}}$) while the subtrees are nested, hence non-isomorphic. Subtree-equivalence must be *imposed* (strong fairness), not derived from statewise calibration; weak (statistical) node-SSC admits the AMD tree and there selects the dominated $q=0$ (value $1<\tfrac43$), and its DAG merge is a stipulation that changes the extensional shadow itself, not just supra-shadow structure [checked; verify B2–B3].

**3. Strong fairness closes the anthropic fork — with a scope bound** [derived/checked; verify B1, repair FR-1, adversaries R2 + Claim E]. On a strongly fair tree: no fiber is nested (finite trees are not isomorphic to proper subtrees), so $\#_d\le 1$ and the per-run and per-occurrence SSC equations are *literally the same equation*; fiber-isomorphism additionally makes the continuation law $\theta_d$ node-independent under every procedure, trembles included; both SSC senses reduce to $P_{s_d}=\theta_d$. Scope bound (adversary-repair Claim B, [derived]): under fully evented chance + full recording + every observation realized, strong fairness forces every fiber to be a *singleton* — multi-member fair fibers (perfect-copy simulations) exist only where the algebra is instance-blind or recording fails. Fairness makes indistinguishability real; it does not make observations true (Told-You-So is strongly fair and still pathological — item 12).

### B. The Cartesian-frame dictionary and theorems

CF background used from the local sources only: frames $(A,E,\cdot)$ over $W$; the eight equivalent observability definitions (Eight-Definitions post, local full text), deployed via conditional-policies and powerless-outside ($\forall e,a_0,a_1:\ a_0\cdot e\notin S\Rightarrow a_0\cdot e=a_1\cdot e$); $\mathrm{Commit}/\mathrm{Assume}$, additive ($\lhd_+$) and multiplicative ($\lhd_\times$) subagents (paper Defs 18–19, 28–29) [reconstructed: verified verbatim against the local files by cf + adversary].

**4. The dictionary** [checked; cf CF-1/CF-5, adversary ADV-1]. $\mathrm{Fr}(B)$: agent $=\prod_d A_d$ (one answer per *point*), environment $=$ chance-outcome profiles ("lazy coins"), evaluation by the deterministic run. The functor forgets $\beta$, states, observations, $\mu$ — it is possibilistic. The local frame $\mathrm{Loc}_d(B)$ (agent $A_d$, everything else externalized — the CF rendering of the point-deviation $C[d\mapsto a]$) is a multiplicative subagent of $\mathrm{Fr}(B)$ *on the nose*. Distributions over $\mathrm{Agent}(\mathrm{Fr}(B))$ are exactly v2 Q12's shared-seed semantics, not Def 6's independent redraws; the two separate on the AMD ($\tfrac43$ behavioral vs $1$) [checked; cf CF-3].

**5. Local Theorem** [derived; cf CF-9/10/11, adversary ADV-4/5]. For queried $d$, $S:=S_{O_d}$: (i) *no simulation of $d$* (every $d$-node subtree-veridical, possibilistically) $\Rightarrow$ (ii) $\mathrm{Loc}_d$'s agent powerless outside $S$ $\Rightarrow$ (iii) $\{S,\neg S\}$ observable in $\mathrm{Loc}_d(B)$ — with both converses strict: an inert simulation gives (ii) without (i); the umbrella-mugging (post 11's $C_0$ realized as a tree) gives (iii) without (ii) — *a point whose action set already contains the conditional policies is its own relocation*. Coverage is not needed and is invisible to observability: CF-observability tracks only the simulation half of v2 Remark 3.10's symmetric difference.

**6. One-Round Global Theorem** [derived; cf CF-12, adversary ADV-6]. One-round tree + no-simulation everywhere + pairwise-disjoint realized observations: the observation partition is observable in $\mathrm{Fr}(B)$, yielding both the updateful factorization ($\&$ of Assumes) and the updateless one ($\otimes$ of powerless-outside subagents). The hypotheses are exactly v2 Prop 3's (under which per-run SSC and strict OC are the same equation): frame-observability rides the *veridicality bridge*, not fairness — and only as a sufficient condition (umbrella-mugging).

**7. Localization is forced for sequential trees** [checked; cf CF-13]. On a fair, veridical, covered look-then-decide tree, the inner observation is *not* observable in the global frame — "I face $d$" is a controllable of the earlier self — while it is observable in $\mathrm{Loc}_d$. Global observability is the wrong target mid-tree; the $\lhd_\times$ localization is mandatory.

**8. Seeming-to-observe, formalized** [derived/checked; cf CF-20/21/22, adversary ADV-12]. A pseudo-observation is the pair $(\mathrm{Loc}_d(B),\{S_{O_d},\neg S_{O_d}\})$ — a labelled partition claim on a multiplicative subagent (minimal, since the frame forgets $O_d$). Failure taxonomy: (a) selection — membership not column-determined (Told-You-So; TN-V2 at $d_E$); (b) coupling — column-determined but conditional policies fail (the mugging coin); (c) approximate — needs probabilities CF forgets. Truth test: with membership column-determined, $V_d$ is observable in $\mathrm{Loc}_d$ $\iff$ $\mathrm{Loc}_d\simeq\mathrm{Hon}_d$, the honest companion (per-cell answer pairs); $\mathrm{Loc}_d\cong\mathrm{Commit}^\Delta(\mathrm{Hon}_d)$, so seeming-to-observe = being the diagonal commitment inside the frame that could genuinely condition, and the gap $\mathrm{Hon}_d\setminus\Delta$ is the spoof. Three inequivalent normalizations of "transforming 'observations' into Observations": un-commit to $\mathrm{Hon}_d$ (legitimate only for agent-distinguishable cells); retract the claim ($O_d\leftarrow\top$, own the decision at policy level — as a reading of a proposed transformation, ATTRIBUTION-UNVETTED); updateful deletion (well-defined only when column-determined; discards the coupling instead of pricing it). Post 11 §4.4 — additive/updateful and multiplicative/updateless factorizations coincide exactly at true Observations — is the CF-internal shadow of "EDT=UDT on the post-transformation class."

**9. The shadow and the frame are transverse quotients** [checked; cf CF-23]. Both kill spurious queries and nothing more is shared: identical frames can have different shadows (the $\beta$-pair), and $\widehat X=\widehat Z$ trees can have inequivalent frames (scrambled-consultation pair). $\widehat B$ is *not* biextensional collapse.

### C. Worked examples

**10. The mugging coin is not a CF Observation; decoupling restores it** [checked; all three threads + both adversaries, computations identical]. "Pay if $T$ else refuse" needs a row equal to pay's on the $T$-column and refuse's on the $H$-column; neither row qualifies; deleting Omega's coupling ($\mathrm{pay}\cdot H:=(H,0)$) makes all four conditional policies witnessed. Observability of the coin = absence of cross-branch coupling; frame-side, decoupling turns the simulation into a spurious query, which is frame-invisible.

**11. The TN V1/V2 wedge is an observability difference at the right locus — and corrects a conjunction** [checked; cf CF-16, adversary ADV-10/11]. At $\mathrm{Loc}_{d_E}$: V1's empty box is powerless-outside-$S_E$, hence a true Observation; V2's is not (membership depends on $y$). This matches v2 Prop 9's flip of the optimal empty-box act (two data points, not a theorem). The conjunction that the box full/empty in transparent Newcomb aren't proper Observations is thereby *corrected*, not confirmed: the empty box IS a proper Observation at its own decision in V1; the conjunction holds only under the V2 reading (the variant v2 Cor 9.1 says the essay needs). The full box fails in both variants.

**12. Told-You-So: an announcement is a controllable wearing an observation's label** [checked; cf CF-17]. Type-(a) selection; $B_P$ is *strongly fair* (both fibers singletons) yet fails recording at $d_5$ — so fairness, recording, and trembles are three independent constraints (see item 19). The zero-respecting $C_0$ voluntarily plays a Commit whose deleted row was the point of the game.

**13. Smoking Lesion** [checked; cf CF-18; dialectical bound from verify B4]. In (S3) the lesion partition is column-determined but not observable — no realizable conditioning on the lesion (the frame face of Prop 11); the tickle instantiation (Prop 12) makes it observable. Dissolving the lesion = making it an Observation = the same *formal* move as relocating the mugging coin — but the announced dialectical symmetry ("the move everyone makes for the lesion") is weaker than advertised: the lesion relocation is licensed by state-ignorance ($P_s(\ell)$ interior), and in the no-doubt mugging $P_s(T)=1$, so relocation there *overrides* the state; only $C$-independent value-preservation carries over.

**14. The AMD defect is an agent-set defect, not an observability defect** [checked; cf CF-19]. No row (nor mixture of rows — shared seed caps at $1$) implements node-dependent play worth $\tfrac43$. The frame construction $\mathrm{Fr}(B)\cong\mathrm{Commit}^\Delta(\mathrm{Fr}^{\mathrm{nd}}(B))\lhd_+\mathrm{Fr}^{\mathrm{nd}}(B)$ is correct, and on the AMD the commit is proper — but the two universal identifications died (§2, item D8).

### D. Trembles, dilution, seeds

**15. Tremble-EDT under independent redraws is Theorem 1's CDT+SIA evaluator** [derived/checked; verify E1, adversary R6]. Conditioning on "my draw was $a$" equals single-instance forcing (the draw is an independent coordinate, and carries no information about which instance I am), so the per-occurrence weights reproduce $\arg\max_a\sum_q R_q G_q(C,a)$ — v2 Appendix B's conjecture, now a theorem sketch, instance-checked on the $k$-fold mugging.

**16. Dilution's sign is the coupling's curvature** [checked; verify E2, adversary R6]. $k$-fold mugging, transfer probability $b_j$: linear $b_j=j/k$ gives an affine $V$ with $k$-free slope (no dilution); concave $b_j=\mathbf 1[j\ge1]$ gives $\partial V/\partial q|_{q=1}=-x/2$ and an interior ratifiable rate that is also the optimum; convex $b_j=\mathbf 1[j=k]$ gives $\partial V/\partial q|_{q=1}=\tfrac12(ky-x)$ and, for $y<x<ky$, an all-pay policy that is Theorem-1-ratifiable but not optimal.

**17. Shared seed: the identity holds only with a hypothesis; the trade-off is real** [checked; verify E3/E5 as wounded and repaired by adversary R6]. Under shared-seed semantics, conditioning on my draw + per-run weights equals Theorem 2's evaluator ($=\arg\max_a V_B(C[d\mapsto a])$, Def-22 coherence) **only given**: the seed at $d$ has full support, and no point other than $d$ both self-succeeds and is answered stochastically. Counterexample otherwise (CX1): a root point routing into an AMD gadget on a different point flips the argmax ($\tfrac23<1<\tfrac43$). The mugging family ($d$ the only point) satisfies the hypothesis, so the 2×2's moral survives where the thread uses it: *anthropic uncertainty buys updatelessness only if my draw and the simulation's are the same draw*. The price [checked]: shared seed collapses Prop 4's realizable functions from degree-$k$ Bernstein to affine — probing dies, Remark 5.4's learnability story needs independent redraws. Both derivations of $\rho$ (shared seed; relocation) are conditional on enriching $\mathcal E$ with disposition coordinates — v2's Remark 4.2/Q1, open, not optional.

### E. The repair pipeline

**18. Relocation, made precise, works — with two corrections** [checked/derived; repair R1/R2, FR-2/3/5/6/7/8; adversary-repair Claims B, E, F]. The atomic move: enrich $\mathcal E$ with $\mathrm{pol}_d$ coordinates (Q1-type, *not* algebra-innocent), insert $\hat q$ above the fiber's lca with pol-events as actions, resolve and stamp branches, strip $O_d$ to structure, calibrate. Worked mugging: value-preserving at every mixed $q$; the output records at $\hat d$; calibrated EDT at the root pays iff $y>x$ = UDT's would-pay verdict; $\rho_d(a)=\{\mathrm{pol}_d{=}a\}$ is *derived* (given the enrichment). Ping-pong obstruction: naive per-fiber iteration does not terminate on entangled fibers; the canonical exit is joint product relocation at the *entanglement closure*. Corrections that now stand: (i) the output is strongly fair only in the **pre-enrichment-marginal sense** (pol-coordinates exempted) — the stamped coordinates break enriched-algebra fiber isomorphism whenever a surviving point sits in scope (adversary Claim B); (ii) preservation must be graded: **pure-profile law preservation holds always, nested fibers included; mixed-grade preservation holds for a class iff the class excludes nesting** (the per-problem "iff" fails on payoff-degenerate nesting).

**19. TN-V2 is not almost fair; self-succession has no mixed-grade repair** [checked; repair FR-5/FR-7, adversary Claim E]. Both TN-V2 fibers are nested (hypothetical above real); relocation reproduces §7.2's V2 table exactly on pure profiles and fails at the mixed grade ($m(5-m)\neq 4m$ at $p{=}1,L{=}4,S{=}1$). No strongly fair tree matches the AMD's mixed value function under any point/action bijection (multiaffine vs quadratic); the three candidate values bracket strictly: collapse $1<$ original $\tfrac43<$ unrolling $4$.

**20. The EDT=UDT theorem, repaired** [derived; repair FR-11/12/13, kill + repair + re-verification by adversary-repair Claim A]. Let $B$ be strongly fair (pre-enrichment sense on relocated outputs), fully recorded for every procedure, with evented chance, zero-probability edges pruned, and — the adversary's added hypothesis, without which the theorem is false — **every queried observation satisfied by some leaf-world**. If $C$ is tremble-EDT-consistent (Remark 3.12 read concretely with states freed: for all small $\varepsilon$, some state-assignment makes $B$ strictly OC-calibrated for $C^\varepsilon$ with $T_{\mathrm{EDT}}(C,\cdot)$), then $V_B(C)=\max_{C'}V_B(C')$. Mechanism: recording + non-nesting make the act-event conditioning a reach-and-draw event; fiber-isomorphism makes the continuation value node-independent, so the SIA reach-weights multiply a constant and cancel; recording under full support gives $A_d^+=A_d$; backward induction closes it, via the **fair-tree assignment lemma**: on strongly fair trees $\max_C V_B=\max_\pi V_B$ (Prop 5(b)'s hypothesis becomes a theorem). Each hypothesis has a counterexample when dropped [checked]: trembles (a fantasy state at a null observation ratifies value $0<4$); fairness (TN-V2's $(2,2)$ is masked-OC-calibrated and event-conditioned-EDT-endorsed, not optimal — Prop 10's wedge); recording (Told-You-So: strongly fair, take-5 tremble-EDT-consistent); realized observations (the adversary's single-node witness: recording vacuous, state free at every $\varepsilon$, EDT approves value $0<1$). Converse fails: optimal $\not\Rightarrow$ tremble-EDT (off-path tie, FR-12); existence holds (FR-13). So on the repaired class: $\emptyset\neq\{\text{tremble-EDT}\}\subseteq\{V\text{-optimal}\}$.

**21. "No Newcomblike problems," made precise** [derived; repair FR-14 under item 20's hypotheses]. On the repaired fair class with tremble-EDT: N1 (some EDT-consistent procedure non-optimal) fails; N3 (action-level vs relocated-policy-level divergence) fails — one consultation per run makes behavioral = shared-seed pathwise (elementary; the Kuhn *name* is [reconstructed]); N2 survives only as *refinement*: EDT is more selective than optimality, never worse — the evidentialist's scruples cost nothing and select better ties. With strict (untrembled) EDT, N1 holds even on fair trees: **the tremble ingredient is not eliminable from the slogan.** The almost-fair class properly contains N1-problems (the no-doubt mugging; TN-V2 at the pure grade), and relocation carries each to a law-equal fair problem where N1 fails. That is the pipeline, end to end.

**22. The two repairs compared; the anthropic price-tag moves** [derived; repair FR-15; verify F1(i)]. Superconditioning (Prop 2 run backwards) keeps the unfair tree and buys Theorem 2's coherence — a necessary condition with bad equilibria (Q4) — and *consumes the anthropic choice* (per-run vs per-occurrence weights). Relocation repairs the structure and buys optimality (item 20) with *no* anthropic parameter: the relocated node is consulted once per run and its calibration is Def 11's unconditioned **prior calibration** — relocation is Definition 11 made structural (verified in general, not just on the mugging). $\rho$ is materialized only by relocation and is the price of the optimality grade: backward induction is bought with a self-model. [scrubbed] is, tree-side, a theorem-shaped point *for* the relocation route — for the updateless-shaped representative, not for face-value action-level EDT.

**23. Two fair trees can be law-equal and disagree where the decision sits** [checked; repair FR-10, adversary C.3]. $T_{d,e}\sim T_{e,d}$ (same reduced-profile law sets; mixtures separate nothing) while disagreeing which choice is first and observed. Dichotomy [derived, near-tautological]: any relation fine enough to pin decision location must consult the very factorization relocation changes — no relation makes relocation sound *and* location invariant. Candidate further principles live outside the value/law data: naturalist facts (Def 23 territory), or CF's own stance that the additive/updateful and multiplicative/updateless factorizations are one object (Eight Definitions §4.4, verbatim: "You can think of the additive definition as updateful, while the multiplicative definition is updateless").

**24. Almost-fair vs observation-calibration: overlap, not equivalence** [derived, bounded; repair FR-16 minus the killed straddle example]. OC deliberately tolerates simulation members of fibers (Remark 3.4) — which is what makes non-nested fibers unfair — so the regions overlap on all the thread's examples; but Told-You-So is strongly fair yet OC-pathological (selection), and unfair trees can fail OC outright. The defensible sentence: *observation-calibration is the honesty standard that cannot see unfairness; strong-SSC is the one that can; almost-fairness (no nested fiber) is the structural condition under which the gap is repairable at no cost to the outcome laws.*

## 3. The sharpened picture

The best current statement of the conjectured pipeline, with grades.

**Almost fair.** Structural: no fiber is nested — every point consulted at most once per run ($\#_d\le1$ identically) [definition]. Its advertised match with observation-calibration is overlap, not equivalence: OC (strict/masked/limit, Defs 8–10) tolerates exactly the simulation members that make non-nested fibers unfair, so the OC-honest-but-SSC-dishonest problems of the thread (no-doubt mugging, TN-V1/V2 at the pure grade) are almost fair; but strongly fair trees can be OC-pathological (Told-You-So, selection) [derived]. TN-V2 as drawn is *not* almost fair (nested hypothetical-above-real fibers): its relocation is exact at the pure grade only [checked].

**Repair.** Enrich $\mathcal E$ with $\mathrm{pol}$-coordinates (Q1; the step is real, not bookkeeping), then joint product relocation at the entanglement closure, then calibrate — the relocated root's honesty condition is Def 11's prior calibration, and $\rho$ is derived rather than supplied [checked/derived]. Preservation grades: pure-profile laws always; all grades iff the input is almost fair; the mixed option set is strictly extended by correlated laws, harmlessly for optima on fair trees (multiaffine, vertex-attained) [derived]. The licensing relation is *not* one relation: "equivalent" means the two-part certificate "law-equal AND the input was almost fair" (§4, seam 1).

**Fair class.** Strongly fair (pre-enrichment-marginal on relocated outputs) + fully recorded + evented, pruned chance + every queried observation realized. There: per-run SSC $=$ per-occurrence SSC $=\theta_d$ [derived]; $\max_C V=\max_\pi V$ (assignment lemma) [derived]; on the fully evented/recorded/realized subclass, fibers are singletons [derived].

**Cartesian-frame correspondence.** Not an equivalence with fairness — fairness is not a function of the frame at all [checked]. What stands: the dictionary (frames forget $\mu,\beta$, states, observations; $\mathrm{Loc}_d\lhd_\times\mathrm{Fr}$) [checked]; observability in the eight-definitions sense, deployed at the *local* frame, tracks **no-simulation** (subtree-veridicality), locally always and globally in one-round trees, with strict gaps (policy-rich points are their own relocation) [derived]; the truth test $\mathrm{Loc}_d\simeq\mathrm{Hon}_d$ formalizes seeming-to-observe [derived]; the fair representative "wears the frame's structure" only under the chance-node identification convention, and what CF contributes to the dispute is the possibilistic skeleton plus the localization discipline — it cannot state the OC-vs-SSC crux, decay, or fairness-as-exchangeability, all facts about $\mu$/$\beta$ [derived; both adversaries' convergent point]. The $\varepsilon$-determined global forward direction ("on a fair veridical covered tree, every chance-determined partition is observable") is open and plausibly true [guess].

**EDT $=$ UDT.** On the fair class as bounded above, tremble-EDT-consistency $\Rightarrow$ $V$-optimality; existence holds; the converse fails, so EDT-with-trembles is a strict refinement of optimality [derived]. N1 and N3 divergence fail there; N2 survives as refinement; with untrembled EDT, N1 persists even on fair trees, so the thesis that EDT=UDT because (in some sense) there are no Newcomblike problems is true *only with the tremble ingredient named* [derived/checked]. The residue of "no Newcomblike problems" that the pipeline cannot deliver: the individuation of problems itself (§4, seam 1).

## 4. Contradictions and unreconciled seams

**Seam 1: the equivalence relation is two predicates wearing one symbol.** Repair thread adopted $\sim$ = equality of realizable pure-profile law sets and used it as the licensing concept; adversary-repair shows the coarse relation blesses the AMD collapse ($\tfrac43\to1$) while the mixed-law refinement unblesses joint relocation (which adds correlated laws). Both adversaries' closing objections converge: every "$\sim$-equivalent" conclusion in the anti-compatibilist material is really "law-equal AND input almost fair," and the almost-fairness conjunct — a structural condition on descriptions — is exactly what a description-literalist may reject. *Settling calculation:* none purely mathematical; the candidate fix is a grade-indexed family of relations ($\sim_{\text{pure}}$, $\sim_{\text{mixed}}$, $\sim_{\text{corr}}$) with relocation proved sound per grade per class, and an explicit statement of which grade each downstream theorem consumes. FR-10's dichotomy [checked] says no refinement will also pin decision location.

**Seam 2: does relocation preserve the frame?** Repair said yes [derived]; adversary-repair killed it (coin copies decorrelate); cf's CF-24 [checked] says the frames differ by exactly un-assuming + world-coarsening. Reconciled: CF-24 is the true statement; the repair claim holds only under the one-$E$-coordinate-per-original-chance-node convention — a shared-seed convention *for chance*. *Settling calculation:* restate the frame functor with $E$ indexed by original chance variables, recompute CF-15/16/17/18, and check whether CF-12's proof survives (it quantifies over lazy profiles).

**Seam 3: fairness's role — central tree-side, idle frame-side.** The repair thread's pipeline makes strong fairness the load-bearing hypothesis (fork closure, assignment lemma, backward induction); the cf adversary proves no $\beta$-sensitive property can be frame-functional and that no-simulation, not fairness, drives observability. Not a contradiction once layered — fairness contributes $\mu$-facts the frame forgets — but the conjecture bound them into one arrow ("fair problems … correspond to Cartesian Frames"), and that arrow survives only as: the fair form is the natural tree presentation of the frame *given* the identification convention, with the honesty content (recording, realized observations, trembles) invisible to CF. *Settling calculation:* prove or refute ADV-9's $\varepsilon$-determined forward direction; a proof would give fairness a genuine (if partial) frame-side shadow, a refutation would complete the divorce.

**Seam 4: which fairness on relocated outputs, and the vacuity worry.** Enriched-algebra fairness dies on pol-stamping; pre-enrichment fairness saves FR-11, but adversary-repair's singleton-collapse shows that on the fully evented/recorded/realized class the fiber machinery is vacuous (all fibers singletons) — while the anthropically interesting multi-member fibers (perfect copies) live only on instance-blind algebras, which sit in tension with EC. *Settling calculation:* restate FR-11 with EC weakened to "evented except within fibers" (instance-blind inside a fiber) and check Step 1's fiber summation does real work; this is where the anthropic content either re-enters the theorem or is shown to be priced out by honesty.

**Seam 5: the 2×2's off-diagonal cells and CX1's boundary.** Verify's table (independent/shared × SIA/SSA) has proved diagonal cells and unanalyzed "mixed" cells, and CX1 shows the shared-seed/per-run cell's identity needs its hypothesis. *Settling calculation:* characterize exactly when shared-seed per-run conditioning equals Theorem 2's evaluator (conjecture: iff no point other than $d$ is both nested and stochastically answered — one direction is CX1, the other is E3's argument), and compute both off-diagonal cells on the $k$-fold mugging.
