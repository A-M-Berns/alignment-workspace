## 0. Plan and the one idea that organizes the grid

The whole grid turns on one structural fact about the tree type: at a decision-point $d$ with $O_d=\top$, **evidential conditioning conditions on the world-event $\{m{=}1\}$, and whether that event is written only by $d$'s own draw is Definition 7's recording (coverage + action-veridicality)**. Lemma 3 screens the lesion off exactly when it is; the textbook contrast (calibrated EDT refrains, calibrated CDT smokes) needs the act-event to be written on runs that do not consult $d$ — a *reference class* writing "$m{=}1$" — which is precisely a coverage failure, i.e. the other half of Remark 3.10's symmetric difference $\{\lambda\models O_d\}\setminus\mathrm{occ}(d)$ (P2:zoo ZO-17's doppelgänger). Every encoding below is sorted by where its lesion correlation lives: in the stipulated state only (inconsistent for every procedure — Prop 11), in a reference class (consistent under observation calibration, dishonest at both SSC grades), or in observations (the tickle instantiation — Prop 12, EDT = CDT).

Sections: §1 encodings E1–E12 with toolkit constructors; §2 the grid; §3 Lemma 3, its failure modes, and the state-side lesion; §4 the headline theorem; §5 updatelessness and relocation; §6 new propositions for §7.3; §7 fairness / CF observability; then the fixed closing sections.

## 1. Encodings (all in `C1-scratch/sl_zoo.py`; every tree passes `Problem.validate()`)

Common algebra unless said otherwise: coordinates lesion $\ell\in\{0,1\}$, act $m\in\{0,1\}$ ($m{=}1$ smoke), outcome $k\in\{0,1\}$ (cancer / killed); leaf payoff $r=\alpha m-\beta k$ with $\alpha,\beta>0$ (v2 §7.3). "Textbook parameters" (TB) are the FDT paper's: $\gamma_1=\tfrac{99}{100}$, $\gamma_0=\tfrac1{100}$, $\alpha=1000$, $\beta=10^6$ (`yudkowsky-soares-2017-fdt.txt:168–181`, READ), $\rho=\tfrac12$. All $\mathrm{CDT}$ verdicts below are computed under the four tree-intrinsic referents of P2:faithful Def F2 (READ, `repair/faithful.md:38`): R1-state $=\mathbb E_{\mu_{B,C[d\mapsto a]}}[r\mid O_d]$; R2-real $=$ reach-weighted forcing $G_q$ at the node-action-veridical $d$-nodes (FA-19′, classical CDT's referent in the act-recording algebra); R2-SIA $=\sum_q R_qG_q$ (Theorem 1); Def 22 $=V_B(C[d\mapsto a])$ (Theorem 2's evaluator). No `do()` exists in v2, so "CDT" bare is never written.

**E1 — classic S1–S3 (v2 §7.3), two states.** Tree: chance $\ell\sim\mathrm{Bern}(\rho)$; query $d=(s,\top,\{m{=}1,m{=}0\})$ on both branches; chance $k\sim\mathrm{Bern}(\gamma_\ell)$; leaf $(\ell,m,k)$. Constructor `ex.smoking_lesion_S3` (toolkit, TRUSTED per audit §2), which supplies the (S2)-stipulated state $P_s(\ell)=\rho$, $P_s(m{=}1)=\sigma$, $P_s(k\mid m{=}1)=\kappa_1>\kappa_0=P_s(k\mid m{=}0)$ (numbers used: $\rho=\tfrac12,\gamma=(\tfrac14,\tfrac34),\alpha=1,\beta=3,\sigma=\tfrac12,\kappa=(\tfrac14,\tfrac34)$ — P2:faithful's Q6 instantiation). E1′ = the same tree with the state pinned by `dp.calibrated_state` for the row's $C$ (i.e. $\Sigma_{S1,S3}$ without (S2)). E1-tickle = `ex.smoking_lesion_tickle` (Prop 12: distinct points $d_1,d_0$ with $O=\{\ell{=}1\},\{\ell{=}0\}$).

**E2 — "known base rates, no test" = the reference-class family (new).** Reading adopted (stated, since the mandate's phrase is not a quotation from any source read here): the agent knows the population statistics of $(\ell,m,k)$ from a study of *other* agents, has no lesion test ($O_d=\top$), and its *own* act-conditionals are the population's — Lewis's "c(K|A)" filled in from base rates. Tree: chance $\ell$; chance "who" — with probability $\pi$ the run consults my point $d$ ($O_d=\top$), with $1-\pi$ it draws a study member's behaviour; chance $k\sim\mathrm{Bern}(\gamma_\ell)$; leaves $(\ell,m,k)$ — **the algebra does not record who wrote $m$** (enriching it with a "who" coordinate is Q1's relativity and dissolves the family, exactly as Remark 6.3's enrichment dissolves Prop 6). Three sub-encodings: **E2a** the study members are lesion-driven *mechanisms* (chance $m\sim\mathrm{Bern}(\sigma_\ell)$, $\sigma_1=\tfrac9{10},\sigma_0=\tfrac1{10}$); **E2b** they are *agents*: tickle-type points $p_1,p_0$ with $O=\{\ell{=}1\},\{\ell{=}0\}$ whose procedure is stipulated ($C(p_1)=$ smoke, $C(p_0)=$ refrain); **E2c** they are *calibrated EDT agents whose desirability depends on the lesion* — payoff $r=\alpha_\ell m-\beta k$ with $\alpha_1=1000>0>\alpha_0=-1$ (the FDT paper's $U_S/U_R$, `:1224–1226`), so that at their strictly calibrated states EDT itself smokes at $p_1$ and refrains at $p_0$; my point $d$ has $O_d=\top$ and the same lesion-dependent payoff. E2c is the honest formal home of "the lesion causes love of smoking": the population correlation is *produced by calibrated agents*, nothing in the tree is stipulated against calibration.

**E3 — P01's smoking robots** (`posts/01-smoking-lesion-steelman.md:73–113`, READ). ## 3. Lemma 3 generalized, where it fails, and the state-side lesion

### 3.1 Lemma 3′ (screening at recorded points) — [derived; sweep-checked on every recorded roster row, `sl_zoo.py::targets` "C1-4-sweep"]

**Setting.** $B$ a concrete problem, $d$ a queried point, $C$ a procedure, Definition 6 run semantics. Call an event $X$ *decided above* a $d$-node $q$ if every leaf below $q$ satisfies $X$ or none does; write $\chi_X(q)\in\{0,1\}$. Call $X$ *pre-query* (relative to $d$) if it is decided above every $d$-node met on an $O_d$-run.

**Lemma 3′.** Suppose $B$ records at $d$ for $C$ (Definition 7: every $O_d$-run passes exactly one $d$-node, subtree-veridical, action-veridical, and the leaf-world satisfies an action of $A_d$ only if it was drawn there). Then for every $a\in A_d$ with $\nu_{B,C}(a\wedge O_d)>0$ and every pre-query $X$:
$$\nu_{B,C}(X\mid a\wedge O_d)\;=\;\frac{\sum_q R_q(C)\,\chi_X(q)}{\sum_q R_q(C)}\;=\;\nu_{B,C}(X\mid O_d),$$
the sums over the $d$-nodes met on $O_d$-runs. ### 3.2 Where Lemma 3′ fails — the four routes, and which ones Smoking Lesion can take [derived; each route witnessed in the grid]

### 3.3 The state-side lesion: two points on two branches — Proposition 15 [checked symbolically, `sl_zoo.py::targets` "C1-pooled-identity"; Lean candidate `lean/C1-pooled-correlation.lean`, UNCHECKED]

$$\nu(k{=}1\mid m{=}1)-\nu(k{=}1\mid m{=}0)\;=\;\frac{(\gamma_1-\gamma_0)(q_1-q_0)\,\rho(1-\rho)}{\nu(m{=}1)\,\nu(m{=}0)},\qquad \nu(m{=}1)=\rho q_1+(1-\rho)q_0 .$$

(ii) *Under strict (limit) OC with $O_{d_1}=O_{d_0}=\top$*, both states are pinned to $\nu(\cdot\mid\top)$, so they agree in $(P,V)$ (Remark 4.4) and can differ only in $\mathrm{cf}$; every $\mathrm{cf}$-blind procedure ($\mathrm{EDT}$, $\mathrm{UDT}_{s^\circ,\rho}$) then has $q_1=q_0$ and the correlation vanishes: **P01's "it is assumed that those with a smoking lesion are more likely to smoke, but this is inconsistent with their being EDT agents" is a theorem** (E3 two-point row at $C=(\tfrac12,\tfrac12)$: `corr = 0`). A $\mathrm{cf}$-sensitive procedure can sustain $q_1\ne q_0$ — then the pooled state shows the correlation and $\mathrm{EDT}$ at that pooled state is type-blind (E3 at $C=(\text{smoke},\text{refrain})$: `EDT@st = ref` at *both* points, so that $C$ is not $T_{\mathrm{EDT}}$-approved). *Proof.* (i) Definition 6 leaf weights: $\nu(k{=}1\wedge m{=}1)=\rho q_1\gamma_1+(1-\rho)q_0\gamma_0$ etc.; clear denominators (sympy identity in `targets`). ## 4. The headline question — Proposition 13 and Proposition 14

**Question (mandate task 3).** Is there any consistent formulation (some calibration sense, some procedure) at which a calibrated EDT refrains and a calibrated CDT smokes, or is the contrast an artefact of descriptions inconsistency-making for every procedure (Prop 11)?

**Answer, with scope.** *Neither disjunct as posed.* The contrast is impossible at every recorded point under every sense (Proposition 13), so it is not sustained by any instantiation of v2's $\Sigma_{SL}$ (S1–S3 with the act written only at $d$). It **is** sustained — exactly, with textbook numbers, under strict, masked and limit observation calibration, with the EDT procedure itself calibrated-and-approved — by the *reference-class* instantiations, in which the act-event $\{m{=}1\}$ is also realized by other agents (E2a/E2c), and by the *compulsion* instantiation (E13), in which the environment realizes it. In both the calibrated CDT of every tree-intrinsic referent smokes. The same trees under per-run / per-occurrence SSC screen the correlation off (E2), or (E13) are dissolved by adding the decision coordinate to the algebra. So the textbook contrast is neither an inconsistency artefact nor a fact about calibrated evidential choice at one's own decision: it is a fact about **world-event conditioning at a point that Definition 7 does not record** — the other half of Remark 3.10's symmetric difference, $\{\lambda\models O_d\}\setminus\mathrm{occ}(d)$.

### Proposition 13 (recording kills the contrast) — [derived; instances checked in every `records = T` row]
