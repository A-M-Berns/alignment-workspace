### 1.2 The headline question and its verdict

**Is the textbook contrast — evidential conditioning refrains, the causal referents smoke — ever well-defined at a calibrated state?** [checked on every row above; derived in general — C1-8′ (Proposition 13), L1-29′, L5-25′/33′]
