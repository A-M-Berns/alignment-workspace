## Setting (the book as a v2 object; fixed for every claim below)

Base problem $B$ with a queried point $d = (s_d, O_d, A_d)$; an act $a \in A_d^+$ (positive $P_{s_d}$-probability); $e := V_{s_d}(a)$ (at a strictly calibrated point $= \mathbb E_\mu[r \mid a \wedge O_d]$), $c := V^a_{s_d}(a)$ (the supposed value, $\mathrm{cf}_{s_d}$ free except for success), $s := \mathrm{sgn}(c - e)$, $\Delta := P_{s_d}(a)\,|c - e|$; $\delta$ is the post's $d$. **Extension $B'$:** a bet point $d_B = (s_{d_B}, O_d, \{\mathrm{buy}, \mathrm{decline}\})$ inserted immediately above the $d$-node met on $O_d$-runs; $d$ replaced by $d' = (s_{d'}, O_d, A_d \times \{\mathrm{keep}, \mathrm{sell}\})$; simulation nodes carrying $d$ carry $d'$ and route on the base act; $r' = r - 2\delta[\mathrm{buy}] + [\mathrm{buy} \wedge \mathrm{base}{=}a \wedge \mathrm{keep}]\,(c - r)\,s + [\mathrm{buy} \wedge \mathrm{sell}]\,\delta$. **Label honesty** (the post's A2): $C'(d')(x\text{-keep}) + C'(d')(x\text{-sell}) = C(d)(x)$. **Bet-locality** (the post's A3): the supposition of the bet event leaves the supposed law of (act, side, world) at the state's actual law. **Reverse bet $R$** (P09's device, "we offer the second transaction whether the agent agrees to the first or not", `threads/P09.md:25, :39` as located by the reviewer): receive $\delta$, owe $(c - r)s$ on base-$a$ runs, offered at $d'$ regardless of what happened at $d_B$. **Myopic $d_B$ state**: one whose law of the side coordinate on the $a$-branch is "keep"; **sophisticated**: one whose law is the true continuation (sell w.p. 1) — which is what strict OC (Definition 8) and local masking (Definition 9, which frees only $d_B$'s own act) both deliver.
