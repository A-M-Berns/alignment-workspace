### §0 Standing definitions (all [derived]; v2 Definitions 6–8, 17–18; P2:faithful F2, F3′)

- **Node-action-veridical** $d$-node $q$: for every $a\in A_d$ every leaf below $q$'s $a$-edge satisfies $a$ (P2:faithful F3′). Such nodes cannot nest (a node below $q$'s $a$-edge whose $b$-edge leaves satisfy $b$ would put $a\wedge b$ on a leaf). $N_d$ := the set of them.
- **F3′ for a procedure $C$** (veridically act-recording): every $q\in N_d$ is subtree-veridical, and $\mu_{B,C}$-a.s. every $O_d$-run passes exactly one node of $N_d$. **F3′ structural** := F3′ for every full-support procedure = every chance-positive $O_d$-path passes exactly one node of $N_d$ (this is what Lemma C2-L3 and FA-20′(i) use; equivalent to F3′ for the trembles $C^\varepsilon$).
- **Definition 7 recording for $C$** (v2 `:75`, procedure-relative): $\mu_{B,C}$-a.s. exactly one $d$-node of *any kind* on each $O_d$-run, subtree-veridical, action-veridical, and the leaf satisfies only the drawn act. **Definition 7 recording for every $C[d\mapsto m]$** ($m\in\Delta(A_d)$) := the same a.s. under every point-deviation at $d$; since a full-support $m$ reaches every path $C$'s other labels allow, this is "exactly one $d$-node on every $O_d$-path positive under $C$'s other labels" — CA-12′'s "for every procedure" restricted to the fiber of $d$.
- $R_q(C):=\mu_{B,C}(\text{reach }q)$; $\theta_{q,a}$ := law of the leaf below $q$'s $a$-child with every node below drawing from $C$; $G_q(C,a)=\theta_{q,a}(r)$. **$v_d(a;C):=\sum_{q\in N_d}R_q(C)\,G_q(C,a)\big/\sum_{q\in N_d}R_q(C)$** — reach-weighted forcing at the veridical nodes $=$ **R2-real** by definition.
- **MSR** (mixed-strategy ratifiability, limit form; P2:calibration's device D4): $\mathrm{supp}\,C(d)\subseteq\arg\max_{a\in A_d}v_d(a;C)$, with $v_d$ read as the tremble-pinned evidential act value at the strictly calibrated state (R3). **MSR$^{17}$**: the same on Definition 17's domain $A_d^+$. **FF**: Remark 3.12's fixed-form tremble-consistency. **TS**: the test-sequence form (P2:dynamic DY-3). **εFP**: P05's literal device — weight above the floor $\varepsilon$ only on maximizers, all acts at least $\varepsilon$ (P2:seeds SE-18′(a)). **DE**: Skyrms's deliberational equilibria (TARK `:120–121`).

### §1 Self-knowledge and the two theorems

**C2-1 [derived] — the object of self-knowledge is the mixture, never the draw.** Strict observation calibration (Definition 8) at a node-action-veridical, subtree-veridical $d$-node that is a.s. the only such node on $O_d$-runs, under Definition 6, gives $P_{s_d}(a)=\nu_{B,C}(a\mid O_d)=C(d)(a)$ even when further $d$-nodes (a predictor's simulations) lie *above* it and move its reach weight — the draw at that node is the only act-specific factor of $\nu(a\wedge O_d)$ (Lemma C2-L2, first identity; [checked] on Newcomb, XOR, Death in Damascus by the reviewer, `adv_selftransparency.out`, and by me on Newcomb and tree J). Masking (Definition 9) deletes this (the fixed point dissolves, P2:calibration CA-10′); limit calibration (Definition 10) keeps it. *Takeaway:* P05's "knows its mixed strategy" is strict OC at the label under Definition 6 at an F3′ node — the state knows $C(d)$, not the act.

**Lemma C2-L1 [derived] (factorization).** For subtree-veridical $q\in N_d$: $\mu_{B,C}(\text{reach }q,\ \text{draw }a\text{ at }q,\ X)=R_q(C)\,C(d)(a)\,\theta_{q,a}(X)$, by Definition 6's independent draw. **Lemma C2-L2 [derived] (F3′ for $C$, $\nu_C(O_d)>0$):** $\nu(a\mid O_d)=C(d)(a)$ and $\mathbb E_\mu[r\mid a\wedge O_d]=v_d(a;C)$ for every $a$ with $C(d)(a)>0$ — the factor $C(d)(a)$ cancels. **Lemma C2-L3 [derived] (F3′ structural, $\nu_C(O_d)>0$):** $\mathrm{R3}(a)=\lim_{\varepsilon\to0}\mathbb E_{\mu_{C^\varepsilon}}[r\mid a\wedge O_d]$ exists and equals $v_d(a;C)$ for every $a\in A_d$ (the quotient of polynomials in $\varepsilon$ has positive denominator at $0$).

### §2 Existence, and which device *is* mixed-strategy ratifiability

**C2-6 [derived] — Skyrms's DE $=$ MSR when the informational feedback is the calibrate step.** Read Skyrms's "states of nature" as the act-independent pre-act history $k$ (upstream chance and simulation draws; post-act chance keyed to the act folds into $\mathbb E[r\mid a,k]$) — Lemma C2-L1 licenses this at F3′ points, where the state's act-conditionals are the $\theta$-laws — and take $I(x,\cdot):=$ the strictly calibrated statistics at label $x$, $y(x)=\nu_{C[d\mapsto x]}(\cdot\mid O_d)$ on the non-act coordinates.
