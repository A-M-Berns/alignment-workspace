## 2. P05-5 — "exploration is empty on the miniature" is a mistranslation (KILLED)

**What the thread claims.** "Exploration as P05 uses it — one fixed procedure satisfying the constraints at every small $\epsilon$ — is Remark 3.12's fixed-form device, **empty** on Remark 4.3's miniature: $v_\varepsilon(a)-v_\varepsilon(b) = 2-3q_\varepsilon$ … so no mixed strategy satisfies P05's constraints 1–3 there" and the verdict table: "the fixed-form constraint is empty on the miniature (DY-2/CA-14′); keep only in test-sequence form."

Remark 3.12's device (read, v2 `:148`) is precisely that other thing: "for every sufficiently small $\varepsilon$ some instantiation $B_\varepsilon$ is strictly calibrated for $C^\varepsilon$ and $T(C, B_\varepsilon)$ holds" — the *untrembled* $C$ approved against the state of $C^\varepsilon$. DY-2's emptiness (read, `dynamic.md:38`: "$q_\varepsilon = (1-\varepsilon)q + \varepsilon/2$ … a mixed support needs a tie at every small $\varepsilon$") is about that device, with the "at every small $\varepsilon$" quantifier that P05 does not have and the $q$/$q_\varepsilon$ split that P05's single label does not have.

**Recomputation [checked, `adv_p05.out` §E].** On the miniature with label $q \in [\epsilon, 1-\epsilon]$ the strictly calibrated act values are $(2(1-q),\, q)$ — as CA-13′ and the thread agree. The corners: at $q = \epsilon$, $a$ is the unique maximizer ($2(1-\epsilon) > \epsilon$) but $P(b) = 1-\epsilon > \epsilon$ — rejected; at $q = 1-\epsilon$, $b$ maximizes but $P(a) = 1-\epsilon > \epsilon$ — rejected. Grid scan over 301 rationals plus $\{\tfrac23, \epsilon, 1-\epsilon\}$ at $\epsilon = \tfrac1{10}$ and $\tfrac1{100}$: the P05-ratifiable set is $\{\tfrac23\}$ — non-empty, unique, $\epsilon$-independent for $\epsilon \le \tfrac13$. **What survives of P05-5.** (i) The $\epsilon$-floor puts mass exactly $\epsilon$ on non-maximizers, Definition 10 puts $\varepsilon/|A_d|$ on every action — they coincide up to reparametrization only for two actions: correct and worth keeping. **Verdict: KILLED** (located error: the antecedent "one fixed procedure … at every small $\epsilon$" is not in P05; the emptiness belongs to Remark 3.12's device).
