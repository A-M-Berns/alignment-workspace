## 2. The decisive objection: Proposition 11 (and every L1 verdict built on it) needs *recording*, which (S1)–(S3) do not stipulate

**2.1 The gap in the proof [derived].** Lemma 3 concludes "$m\perp(\ell,k)$ under $\nu_{B,C}$" for *the draw* at $d$; Proposition 11 then reads $P_s(k\mid m{=}1)=P_s(k\mid m{=}0)$ at a masked state $\nu_{B,C'}(\cdot\mid\top)$. The step from the draw to the world-coordinate $m$ uses two facts about v2's *enumerated* instantiation shape ("chance $\ell$; query $d$; act $m$; chance $k$; leaf $(\ell,m,k)$"): every run consults $d$ (coverage), and the leaf's $m$ is the draw (action-veridicality) — together Definition 7 recording. Neither is in (S1) ("cancer drawn from the lesion only"), (S2), or (S3) ("a single queried point with $O_d=\top$"). Any instantiation in which the coordinate $m$ is also written by something other than $d$'s draw satisfies (S1)–(S3) literally and escapes the argument. L1 found one such instantiation itself (L1-20(ii), action-veridicality fails) and then asserted the opposite in amendment 2 ("Neither revives (S3)'s single uninformative point; (S3) stays inconsistent for everyone") — the tremble-(ii) tree *is* a single queried point with $O_d=\top$. It missed the other family entirely.

**2.2 Counterexample A — Yudkowsky–Soares's third population as a tree [checked; `adv_l1_counterexamples.py` §A].** Chance who $\in\{E,A\}$ with $\pi=\tfrac12$. $E$-branch: query $d=(s,\top,\{m{=}1,m{=}0\})$, *then* chance $\ell\sim\mathrm{Bern}(\tfrac12)$, then $k\sim\mathrm{Bern}(\gamma_\ell)$. $A$-branch (a lesion-following automaton — Y–S's "agents that use a different decision procedure", modelled as chance): chance $\ell$, $m:=\ell$, chance $k$. Leaves $(\text{who},\ell,m,k)$, $r=\alpha m-\beta k$, FDT numbers $\gamma=(.99,.01)$, $\alpha=1000$, $\beta=10^6$. Toolkit: `validate` [], **one $d$-node**, `is_strongly_fair` True, `is_almost_fair` True, every $d$-node subtree-veridical; `covers` False, `records` False ("passes 0 d-nodes" on automaton runs). For $C=$ refrain: strictly calibrated state has $P_s(k\mid m{=}1)-P_s(k\mid m{=}0)=\tfrac{49}{75}>0$ — **(S2) holds**; `strict_oc` calibrated, `limit_oc` calibrated (`masked_oc` reports violated *for the strict state*, as at every self-certain node — an interior-self-model state is masked-calibrated, exactly L1-12(i)'s rider); evidential act values $(-989{,}000,\ -336{,}667)$ → refrain, and `T_EDT(refrain)` **True** — calibrated *and approved*; Theorem-1 forcing sums $(-249{,}500,-250{,}000)$ and Theorem-2 deviation values $(-499{,}250,-499{,}750)$ → smoke; classical CDT (cf holding $P_s(\text{who},\ell,k)$ fixed) $(-499{,}000,-500{,}000)$ → smoke; $V_B(\text{smoke})-V_B(\text{refrain})=\pi\alpha=500$, optimum smoke. Same pattern for $C=$ smoke and mixed $q$ (gap $\tfrac{49}{75}$ at every $q$ — the automata write it). At the per-run and per-occurrence SSC states the conditional on the unplayed act is undefined for deterministic $C$ and the strict state is `violated`: **SSC conditions on being consulted and screens the reference class off** (this is v2's own Remark 3.10: at $O_d=\top$ strict OC collapses to $\nu$, SSC retains content through occurrence).
