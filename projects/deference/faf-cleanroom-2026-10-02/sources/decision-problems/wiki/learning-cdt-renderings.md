# Defining CDT for a learner: the renderings, the collapse theorem, the fork

Part of `two-lesions-index` · Part III, page 11 · Previous: `channels-and-agent-boundary` · Next: [non-responsiveness](non-responsiveness.md). Status per `two-lesions-provenance`. Source: [defining-cdt-in-the-learning-setting](../directions/defining-cdt-in-the-learning-setting.md) (Theorem 3, Corollary 3.1, Definitions 24–26, all numbers); [two-lesions-doc-2026-09-18](../two-lesions-doc-2026-09-18.md) §6 (Definition 4, Proposition 7); [two-lesions-exchange-2026-09-19](../two-lesions-exchange-2026-09-19.md); [sl-defensible-claims](../sl-workflow/notes/final/sl-defensible-claims.md) S9, S12, S25; `sl-critique` C1.

**Headline.** Every way of defining CDT for a learning agent is, at a recorded point under Definition 6, one of two things: conditioning on the act or the draw, or an interventional supposition that provably equals conditioning on positive-probability acts; so classical CDT and EDT are one evaluator there and the contest has no content [derived; Theorem 3; the double-lesion case checked over all 25 DAGs]. The exact condition for the collapse is an iff — the act independent of its graph-parents under the state — which proves the session note's Claim 9.1 in scope and refutes it as stated for "every graph prior": latent confounders whose effects cancel and reversed structures with real downstream effects are observationally consistent counterexamples [checked]. Non-responsiveness is the *form* of every CDT, not its content; the content is the choice of the algebra a supposition holds fixed [derived]. And v2's counterfactual component is typed as a belief but normed as a rule; the Dutch book with a doubled action set forces the belief reading, which is what the author's constraint denies the choice-rule reading [derived].

## Why it is hard

The author, in the exchange: "It seems really tricky to define cdt in this setting, and I don't know how I want to do it." The Docs Claude's diagnosis: CDT's *target* is not the problem — $E[U \mid do(a)]$ is well-defined in the double lesion and the effect of smoking on cancer is zero. What is hard is *estimation*: to compute that quantity from its own record an agent needs variation in the act it knows to be independent of the state, and "which variation is exogenous" is both the contested question and the boundary question. "Every rendering of learning-CDT has to stipulate exogeneity somewhere; the design choice is where." The author's later objection is the question-begging worry: a learner that treats every act as an experiment assumes the act has no unobserved parents, which forces the lesion to explain the act–cancer correlation from the cancer side and "begs the question on CDT = EDT" — "if we're representing CDT, we can't do it with a learning theory that inevitably makes it agree with EDT." His two proposals: a CDT whose causal model may be any one consistent with the raw statistics (prior-selected), or one given interventional access to all variables in a lab.

## The menu

| rendering | what it is | v2 representation | collapses to conditioning… |
|---|---|---|---|
| **(i) act-level** (the doc's Definition 4) | treats each performed act as an intervention; estimates effects by outcome frequency among episodes with that act | Definition 20's evidential criterion — no new object | everywhere the conditional is defined, by definition |
| **(ii) intention-level / ITT** | condition on the sampled intention; compliance-adjust for the realized act | needs the draw as a coordinate; $A_d$ = draw-events; an exclusion restriction as a structural stipulation | everywhere on the enriched algebra; the realized act's effect is derived, not conditioned |
| **(iii) prior over structures** (the author's first proposal) | $\mathrm{cf}_s = \int \mathrm{cf}^G_s\, d\pi(G)$ over causal models Markov-compatible with $P_s$ | Definitions 24–25 below; an epistemology (Definition 19), not counterfactual calibration | at recorded points for the temporal part of $\pi$ (Theorem 3); prior-relative elsewhere |
| **(iv) lab CDT** (the author's second proposal) | $\pi = \delta_{G^\ast}$, the tree's mechanism graph, learned by interventional access, then act in the field | Definition 26; a parameter of the point in Definition 11's pattern | at recorded points; parts from EDT at the run's two homes and under the shared seed |
| **(v) non-responsiveness** | the causal model, once fixed, is not revised by information about the agent's own current act | a property of $\mathrm{cf}_s$ relative to a subalgebra | not a rendering: separates nothing at recorded points; individuates CDT only through the fixed algebra |

The doc's Proposition 7 — the act-level causal learner and the evidential learner compute identical conditionals from identical records in each tagging regime — is a correct theorem about rendering (i), on which the coincidence is trivial. The Docs Claude later called the collapse "manufactured"; better: (i) is the rendering on which it is trivial, and the interesting CDTs are (iii)–(iv), for which the coincidence is Theorem 3 with hypotheses [derived].

## Theorem 3

**Setting.** $\mathcal{E}$ finite, generated by coordinates $V = V_0 \sqcup \{m\} \sqcup V_1$: pre-query chance $V_0$, the act $m$, post-query chance $V_1$. A latent-free *causal structure* for a state $s$ is a DAG $G$ on $V$ with $P_s$ Markov to $G$; its interventional law at $m = a$ is the truncated factorization $P^{G,a}$. "Observationally consistent with $P_s$" is Markov compatibility — the only thing a record can test.

> **Theorem 3 (collapse of interventional supposition at recorded points).** [derived] Let $B$ record at $d$ under Definition 6 with $\nu(O_d) > 0$ and $O_d$ pre-query or $\top$, $P_{s_d}$ strictly or limit observation-calibrated, and $G$ a latent-free causal structure for $s_d$. For every positive-probability act $a$:
> (i) $P^{G,a} = P_{s_d}(\cdot \mid a)$ on all of $\mathcal{E}$ **iff** $P_{s_d}(a \mid \mathrm{pa}_G(m)) = P_{s_d}(a)$ almost surely;
> (ii) the identity holds whenever $\mathrm{pa}_G(m) \subseteq V_0$;
> (iii) if moreover $m \perp \langle V_1 \rangle$ under $P_{s_d}$ — post-query chance does not read the act, as under (S1) — the identity holds for **every** compatible $G$;
> (iv) consequently CDT with $\mathrm{cf}^G$ and EDT approve the same labels at $d$ whenever the causal argmax meets the positive-probability acts.

*Proof.* Compatibility gives $P_s(v) = P_s(m \mid \mathrm{pa}_m) \prod_{i \neq m} P_s(v_i \mid \mathrm{pa}_i)$, so the truncated product is $P^{G,a}(v_{-m}) = P_s(v_{-m}, a)/P_s(a \mid \mathrm{pa}_m(v))$ while $P_s(v_{-m} \mid a) = P_s(v_{-m}, a)/P_s(a)$; they agree at every $v$ iff $P_s(a \mid \mathrm{pa}_m(v)) = P_s(a)$ — (i). Recording makes $m$ a function of the draw alone and Definition 6 makes the draw independent of everything upstream, so $m \perp \langle V_0\rangle$ (Lemma 3) — (ii). (iii) and (iv) follow. Limit calibration changes nothing. $\square$

**Checks** [checked; script `claim91_dags.py`]. On the (S1) recorded tree, 25 DAGs on $(\ell, m, k)$, 16 Markov-compatible, and the identity holds for all 16 — reversed structures included (clause iii). With a direct effect $m \to k$ added (still recorded): 7 compatible; the identity holds for the two temporal ones and the two reversed ones in which $m$ is a root, and **fails for the three in which $k$ is a parent of $m$** — $P(k \mid do\, m) = 0.205$ against $P(k \mid m{=}1) = 0.305$, $P(k\mid m{=}0) = 0.105$. Latent cancellation: $U \to m$, $U \to k$, $m \to k$ with $P(k\mid m{=}1) = P(k\mid m{=}0) = 0.38$ and $P(k \mid do\,m) = (0.500, 0.575)$ — an effect of $-0.075$ that no observational record removes.

So the session note's Claim 9.1 — "for every graph prior" — was an overstatement. **Corollary 3.1 (where supposition parts from conditioning)**: (a) at *null acts*, where the truncated factorization needs probability-zero cells and the parameter prior fills them; (b) at *non-recorded points* — coverage failure and action-veridicality failure both make $m$ a function of the forcing as well as the draw, and the compatible temporal structure gives the population rate while conditioning gives the run's statistics; (c) under the *shared seed* (Definition 6′), where Lemma 3 fails and the Newcomb correlation appears at a recorded point; (d) under *latent confounders*, always, if latents on the act are admitted — which is exactly the author's "not treating its own action … as an experimental intervention a priori," so not a corner case of his first proposal but its defining feature.

**The doc's model-based paragraph, reconciled.** The doc says the untagged record cannot discriminate lesion-causes-both from smoking-causes-cancer "while the lesion is unobserved." In v2's algebra the lesion is a coordinate, so at the reference-class point the compatible *temporal* structure is unique and the prior-over-structures CDT restricted to temporal graphs is determinate (smokes); the doc's indeterminacy needs its Definition 3's "neither kind of agent observes the state directly." Both right in their algebras; together they locate the prior's influence: through backward structures when the confounder is observed, through temporal ones too when latent, always at null acts, always with latents on the act.

**The draw as a coordinate is rendering (iii) plus one axiom.** On the overwrite tree with the draw recorded, Theorem 3 applies to the draw: $P(k\mid a{=}1) = P(k\mid a{=}0) = 0.105$ at every label [checked]. The realized act is then a post-decision coordinate with parents $\{a, \ell\}$, and its effect on $k$ is identified by the IV adjustment (`iv-reading-of-decision-problems`) *under the exclusion restriction* that $a$ reaches $k$ only through $m$ — a structural stipulation that observational consistency does not supply. The compliance adjustment is where that axiom is spent.

## Non-responsiveness: form, not content

The exchange's thesis: what every CDT shares, and "the whole content of 'CDT' as a distinct theory," is that the causal model, once fixed, is not revised by information about the agent's own current act. In v2 terms $\mathrm{cf}_s$ is *non-responsive on* $\mathcal{E}_0$ if $P^a_s \restriction \mathcal{E}_0 = P_s \restriction \mathcal{E}_0$ for all $a$. Every interventional supposition is non-responsive on the algebra of the act's non-descendants. But so is the evidential conditional on the pre-query algebra at every recorded point — that is Lemma 3′ (S4). So at recorded points non-responsiveness is shared and separates nothing; it separates CDT from conditioning exactly where conditioning on the act moves a fixed-algebra event — the run's two homes and the shared seed. The thesis is right about the *form* of every CDT and silent about its *content*, which is the choice of $\mathcal{E}_0$: classical CDT fixes the physical non-descendants (Omega's prediction included), the doc's $\mathfrak{B}$ fixes the logical state but not the prediction, EDT fixes nothing. The exchange's own trio — responsiveness gets Newcomb right and Troll Bridge wrong, non-responsiveness the reverse — is two settings of $\mathcal{E}_0$, resolved by a third: hold $\Box\bot$ fixed, let the prediction move ([non-responsiveness](non-responsiveness.md), [policy-responsiveness](policy-responsiveness.md)). ## The order-δ miss

The exchange said the lab CDT's $do(\text{smoke})$ "misses the actual cancer rate among its own smoking episodes by an amount of order δ." Right for the act it takes, wrong for the act it forgoes [checked; session note's parameters]:

| label $p$ | $P(k\mid m{=}1)$ | $P(k\mid m{=}0)$ | miss on smoke | miss on abstain |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | 0.600 | 0.1025 | $+0.495$ | $-0.0025$ |
| 0.5 | 0.1078 | 0.1023 | $+0.0028$ | $-0.0028$ |
| 1 | 0.10528 | 0.050 | $+0.00028$ | $-0.055$ |

At its own fixed point ($p = 1$) the lab CDT's miss on smoking is $\varepsilon_L\varepsilon_A\delta(\gamma_1-\gamma_0)/(1-\varepsilon_A\delta) \approx 2.8 \times 10^{-4}$, linear in $\delta$; its miss on abstaining is $\varepsilon_L(\gamma_1 - \gamma_0) = 0.055$, an $O(1)$ gap on an $O(\delta)$-probability act. The Dutch-book exposure, which prices $P(a)\,|\mathrm{cdt}(a) - \mathrm{edt}(a)|$, is $O(\delta)$ on both. **Which clause of Definition 8 fails? Neither.** The lab CDT's $P_{s_d}(k \mid m{=}1)$ is correct; what says $0.105$ is the *supposed* probability $P^{m=1}_{s_d}(k)$, which Definition 8 never inspects and Remark 3.11 exempts. The miss is registered only by Definition 20's evidential criterion — a counterfactual theory — so its normative force depends entirely on the fork.

## The fork: belief or choice rule

v2's Definition 2 types $\mathrm{cf}_s$ as a belief — a single probability–desirability pair under a supposition, Joyce's format — and norms it as a choice rule — success only, no calibration (Remark 3.11), agreement with conditioning optional. The two answers to "what happens if I smoke," $P_s(\cdot \mid \text{smoke})$ and $P^{\text{smoke}}_s$, coexist in every v2 state.

**Why the choice-rule reading does not survive the doubled action set** [derived]. The revised Dutch book (`12-dutch-booking-cdt-revised-argument` ll. 41–60) folds a bet into the act: the action set becomes $\{a, a'\}$ with $a'$ = "$a$ and sell $B$." In v2 that is a decision point whose actions are compound events, and CDT evaluates the supposed desirability of the whole compound act under the single measure $P^{a'}_s$. A choice-rule $\mathrm{cf}$ that priced the bet by $P_s(\text{fill} \mid a')$ and the box by $P^{a'}_s(\text{fill})$ would assign one event two probabilities inside one supposition — not a probability on $\mathcal{E}$, hence not a value in Definition 2's codomain. Once bets can be bundled with acts, the type forces one measure per supposition: the belief reading. The choice-rule reading is coherent only for an agent that never faces a compound act — immune to the book by not being a theory of what compound acts bring about.

**The author's constraint, read.** "To avoid Dutch books, counterfactual expectations must equal conditional probabilities whenever the latter are well-defined" is then coherence for the one measure that both chooses and prices, binding on positive-probability acts and silent elsewhere — the author's own scope at `12-dutch-booking-cdt-revised-argument` ll. 124–138. The exchange's suggestion that the constraint "is best read as denying that the second thing counts as a theory of counterfactuals at all" is right, and the doubled action set is the reason. Two cautions from the run (S12): the book has force at mixed labels and only an $o(\varepsilon)$ residue at deterministic fixed points (AlexMennen's point), and a strictly calibrated bet point knows the continuation and declines the book — so the constraint constrains *evaluators*, not any calibrated agent's purse.

**$\mathfrak{B}$ sits exactly on the fork.** The doc's $\mathfrak{B}$ carries a T-respecting credence $P$ — under the hypothesis $\Box(\mathrm{Cross}_\mathfrak{B} \to \Box\bot)$ its $P(\Box\bot \mid \mathrm{Cross}_\mathfrak{B})$ would be $1$ — and crosses iff $10 - 20 P_0(\Box\bot) > 0$ with $P_0$ "formed without regard to its own act." It violates the author's constraint *dispositionally*, in a hypothetical that its violation keeps from obtaining (Proposition 10: $T \nvdash \mathrm{Cross}_\mathfrak{B} \to \Box\bot$). That is why no bookie can price $\mathfrak{B}$'s gap: the act at which it would be priced is one whose conditional is never actually $1$ (`lobian-lesion`, [non-responsiveness](non-responsiveness.md)).

## Determinism, inheritance, and the author's correction

The exchange: a deterministic agent has no exogenous variation, so the ideal CDT's causal knowledge "has to be inherited from its bounded predecessors' randomization," and this "fails exactly where the environment responds to the current procedure rather than to the act, which is Newcomb." The author: backwards — see [newcomb-correction](newcomb-correction.md). Checked on the tree [checked; $L = 10$, $S = 1$, $p = 0.9$, tagged exploration $\eta = 0.05$]: under Definition 6 (Omega samples the label) the tagged interventionist learns "no effect" — true as physics — two-boxes, and the policy value falls from $8.825$ to $2.175$; under Definition 6′ (Omega reads the live draw) it reads its exploration as an intervention that moved the box — false as physics — one-boxes, and wins. Its verdict is a function of whether the environment reads the draw, not of anything about the agent: the "shadow of predecessors' exploration" is a *run semantics* (Q12), not a tremble rate, and Definition 10 must carry that choice explicitly (`next-coherent-care`). The inheritance story fails not because predecessors could not vary the right thing — policy-level variation moves a label-reading Omega — but because what they varied is read differently by the two semantics.

## The doc's two proposed replacements

**Definition 4 → lab CDT.** Replacing the act-level learner by the lab CDT makes the doc's §6 sentence "on no construal does the causal learner outperform the evidential learner in the calibrated problem, except by being handed in advance what the calibration requirement forbids it to be handed" false as stated — the lab CDT smokes at the untagged fixed point where untagged EDT abstains, and is right — unless "handed" covers "learned in a lab the field record did not contain," which is the exchange's restatement: non-responsiveness buys nothing that structural knowledge does not buy the responsive agent (who identifies the forcing from policy variation, `epsilon-versus-voi`). Moral three survives as a claim about *knowledge states*: the lab CDT versus untagged EDT is unfair in the calibration requirement's own sense, the lab being a situation the evidential agent was not placed in. What it costs is Proposition 7, restated as: the act-level learner coincides with EDT; the lab CDT differs by $O(\delta)$ on the act it takes and $O(1)$ on the act it forgoes.

**Proposition 10 → non-responsiveness.** Already the doc's text: $P_0$ "formed without regard to its own act"; "remove the responsiveness and the proof fails." One precision: $\mathfrak{B}$'s *credences* are T-respecting by stipulation; what it lacks is the (β)-type responsiveness of its *rule*. The cost is dispositional non-compliance with the author's constraint, not with T-respect. And $\mathfrak{B}$'s rule on TB($\theta$) is exactly $\mathrm{CDT}_G$ with the temporal structure $\mathrm{bot} \to m$ — cross iff $\theta < \tfrac12$ — so the doc's "causal agent" is the lab CDT with $\mathcal{E}_0 = \langle \Box\bot \rangle$, and the session note's "FDT learner" is the same object under a name chosen for its Newcomb behaviour, which the bridge does not test ([marginal-formula-learner](marginal-formula-learner.md)).

## A definition for the next version

**Definition 24 (causal structure; interventional supposition).** A causal structure for $s$ is a DAG $G$ on the coordinates, optionally with latents, whose observable marginal is $P_s$; its interventional supposition of $a$ is $\mathrm{cf}^G_s(a) := (P^{G,a}, V^{G,a})$ by truncated factorization. Its *fixed algebra* is $\mathcal{E}_0^G := \langle \mathrm{nondesc}_G(m)\rangle$, on which $\mathrm{cf}^G_s$ is non-responsive.

**Definition 25 (observational causal consistency).** $T_{\mathrm{OCC}}(C, B)$ holds iff at every reachable queried $d$ there is a probability $\pi_d$ over causal structures for $s_d$ with $\mathrm{cf}_{s_d} = \int \mathrm{cf}^G_{s_d}\, d\pi_d(G)$. It consults reachable states only, so it is an *epistemology* (Definition 19), not counterfactual calibration; it does not beg Q6, since the tree's own mechanism graph is compatible with every calibrated state at pre-query observations, so classical CDT's supposition is always admissible.

**Definition 26 (CDT in the learning setting).** $\mathrm{CDT}_\pi(d) := \arg\max_a V^{\pi, a}_{s_d}(a)$. Named cases: the *lab CDT* ($\pi_d = \delta_{G^\ast}$) and the *prior-over-structures CDT* (posterior conditioned on $P_{s_d}$). A rendering is *temporal* if supported on latent-free $G$ with $\mathrm{pa}_G(m) \subseteq V_0$.

**Corollary 3.1 restated:** CDT$_\pi$ and EDT approve the same labels on positive-probability acts at every point that records under Definition 6, for every temporal $\pi$; they part at null acts, non-recorded points, the shared seed, and non-temporal or latent-bearing $\pi$.

**What the contest reduces to.** Four modelling parameters, each in v2 or one step from it: which events are the actions ($A_d$: act, draw or disposition — the exchange's "which variable do you condition on" and the trichotomy of `tags-and-draw-coordinate` are one question in two vocabularies); the run semantics (Definition 6 or 6′, Q12); the fixed algebra $\mathcal{E}_0$ (the content of non-responsiveness, needing the pre-action subalgebra that v2.1 excised, back in a targeted role); and the null-act fill (prior cells or tremble limits). On the recorded-point class under Definition 6 the contest is *dissolved* — Theorem 3 leaves it nothing to be about, which is the run's S1 and the 2017 "basically one notion of counterfactual" with its parameters named. Off that class it is *relocated*: to a choice of $A_d$ that Definition 3 leaves to the problem, a semantics Definition 6 fixes and Q12 questions, and an $\mathcal{E}_0$ that no calibration pins. CDT versus EDT is then a specification-shaped dispute, decidable per problem once the four parameters are written down, and the framework's contribution is to make writing them down compulsory. [derived]

**Two things not to do.** Do not define learning-CDT as rendering (i) and report its coincidence with EDT as a finding. Do not make Theorem 3's collapse a *calibration* condition on $\mathrm{cf}_s$ — that is the question-begging Remark 3.11 warns of; Definition 25 keeps it subjective, and the collapse is then a theorem about what observational consistency already forces.

**Open items** (diagnosed, not settled): finite-sample Occam between an unconfounded and a latent-cancellation structure (the confounded model is singular; rate uncomputed); Theorem 3 at the subjective-state grades (expected to transfer under the run's $H^\ast$, unproved); a shared-seed version (conjecture: collapse iff the act is independent of its parents *given the seed*); non-supervenient payoffs.
