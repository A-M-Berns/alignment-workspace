## 1. Verdict

**pass.**

The gate reproduces (`scripts/wp-audit` on all fourteen modules: PASS, 782 declarations, axioms `propext`/`Classical.choice`/`Quot.sound` only, no `sorry`; `-open.txt` lists nothing and nothing rests on an open statement). No headline is vacuous, a squeeze, or trivially true on the range the source intends; every witness inhabits its package; no junk value reaches a headline's conclusion on that range. What the probes found is a set of boundaries where a headline is true *for a junk reason* on a measure-zero parameter the mandate excludes but the statement admits (`D = 0`, `π(L) = 0`, `π_b = 1`), one theorem stronger than shipped (positivity unneeded), two witnesses whose advertised strength exceeds what they exercise, one kind overstated, and one source check that is a tautology of its own fixture.
