## F5 — "No fair tree matches the AMD's mixed value function under any correspondence" needs the correspondence to be a point/action map (imprecision, `fair-repair.md` FR-7(c); C.1)

As literally stated ("any correspondence"), the claim is false. Explicit counter-construction (audit r1): a one-point almost-fair tree with three actions worth `0`, `1`, `4/3` realises every value in `[0, 4/3]` by mixing, so a map of the AMD's procedures into its procedures that sends `procQ 1 ↦ δ_x` (value `0`), `procQ 0 ↦ δ_y` (value `1`) and `procQ q` to a mixture worth `(1−q)(3q+1)` agrees with the AMD's value *everywhere*; nothing forces such a map to act through a point and its actions. What is true, and proved (`amd_no_almostFair_match`): for every almost-fair `B'`, point `d'`, actions `x, y` playing `a, b`, and `C'`, if the two pure act values match the AMD's then the value at the `(⅓, ⅔)` mixture at `d'` is `2/3 ≠ 4/3` — because on almost-fair trees `V` is affine in each coordinate (`AlmostFair.value_mix2`) and the AMD's `(1−q)(3q+1)` is not. The mandate's phrasing "every map of the AMD's procedures into `B'`'s that agrees on the two pure profiles" inherits the imprecision; the Lean states the point/action version.
