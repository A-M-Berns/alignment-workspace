## 3. Non-blocking issues

- **N1. `Twin.twin_kills_good` — the name holds on the regime `c ≤ b(1-δ)` and is empty on `c > b(1-δ)`.** The statement is `∀ P : Params, ∃ ρ ∈ [0,1)`, with `s := 1`: the trap is a twin fixed point, the good point is not, `w_island = (1-δ)(1-ρ) < c/b`, `TB` at the trap, the trap a floored fixed point. It never says the good point *was* a fixed point before the twin. By `good_isPlainFP_iff` at `s = 1` the good point is a plain fixed point of `sos ⟨b,c,δ,1⟩` iff `c ≤ b(1-δ)`; when `c > b(1-δ)` it is dead already, and the proof's second case (`c > 2b(1-δ)`) takes `ρ = 0`, i.e. no twin at all. So on that regime the theorem restates `good_isPlainFP_iff` and kills nothing. On the live regime the proof's `ρ = 1 - c/(2b(1-δ)) ≥ 1/2` genuinely does the killing. Probe `TwinKillsLive.lean`: `twin_kills_live (P) (hlive : c ≤ b(1-δ))`: the good point is a plain fixed point of `sos (atOne P)` and there is `ρ ≥ 1/2` with the trap a twin fixed point and the good point not; `good_dead_without_twin (P) (c > b(1-δ))`: `¬ (sos (atOne P)).IsPlainFP good`. Fix I'd accept: add `twin_kills_live` (or the two conjuncts `(sos ⟨b,c,δ,1⟩).IsPlainFP good` and `1/2 ≤ ρ` under the hypothesis `c ≤ b(1-δ)`) beside `twin_kills_good`, and one clause in its docstring and ledger row saying the killing is the live regime and the dead regime is vacuous. Not blocking because the ledger's Claim cell states exactly the Lean's content, the mandate's (iii) is matched verbatim (it also did not require the good point to be alive), and the N+ witness (`b = 1, c = 1/2, δ = 1/10`: `c = 1/2 ≤ 9/10 = b(1-δ)`) is in the live regime.
