### 3.1 `family_threshold` (extension): the first clause is the cellwise-P3 verdict, and the family's *other* cell is not mentioned

The theorem's first clause is `argmax ((d2Aσ σ).restrict C1 _).P3 (W ≡ λ*) 1 (S1 u) = {0}` — the T2 verdict at `C₁` of P3 with `W ≡ λ*`, `λ*` the `sup'` of record. More substantively for an "adversarial" reading: the mandate's extension asks for "the exact revelation threshold `σ̂` at which the verdicts first part", and the family has *two* cells; the headline, finding 52 and the report speak of `C₁` only. If the verdicts parted at `C₀` ("says `i₂`") below `7/9`, "first part at `7/9`" would be false for the family. So at `C₀` the two verdicts agree for every `σ ∈ [1/2, 1]`, and the family-wide first parting is indeed at `σ̂ = 7/9`, at `C₁`. (By hand: `λ̂_{C₀} = 41/90` as well for `σ < 1`, and `λ_{C₀}(σ) = max((5 − 2σ)/10, (9 + 21σ)/(10 + 90σ)) < 41/90` on the whole range; at `σ = 1` the masses coincide and both verdicts tie.)
