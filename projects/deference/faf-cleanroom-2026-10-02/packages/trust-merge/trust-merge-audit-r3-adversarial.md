## 2. Blocking issues

### B1 — `li_aumann_failure_open` (T9(3), `Hop2.lean`): the typed OPEN is not the LI Aumann failure — its disagreement conjunct is decoupled from its tracking clauses, and the bundle reading that the clauses support is refuted by `thm:con`

**The statement.**

```lean
theorem li_aumann_failure_open :
    ∃ (B : MergeBundle) (φ : Sentence),
      (B.X = fun _ => literalIndicator φ) ∧
      (∀ n, ∃ v : PCWorld, v.ConsistentWith (B.DPH.D n) ∧ v.Holds φ) ∧
      (∀ n, ∃ v : PCWorld, v.ConsistentWith (B.DPH.D n) ∧ ¬ v.Holds φ) ∧
      ((fun n => B.est n) ≈ₙ (fun n => B.μ n)) ∧
      ((fun n => (B.β n).expect B.H n) ≈ₙ (fun n => B.est n)) ∧
      ¬ ((fun n => B.H n φ) ≈ₙ (fun n => B.A n φ))
```

with `B.est n = 𝔼^A_n(α_{0,n})` (`A`'s same-day expectation of the ledger LUV naming `𝔼^H_{f n}(X n)`), `B.μ n = 𝔼^H_{f n}(X n) = H_{f n}(φ)` on the constant family, and `β` an e.c. quote in `H`'s language reflected at `B.est n`.

**What the source and the mandate ask for.** `trust-lab-2-inventory` 2-022: "there are logical inductors `P¹, P²` with mutual good feedback such that for some `X` not decidable by `Γ`, `𝔼¹_n(X) ≂_n`-knows `𝔼²_n(X)` and vice versa (each's estimate of the other's estimate tracks it), yet `𝔼¹_n(X) ≁_n 𝔼²_n(X)`" — the agents' *own* estimates of `X`, each known to the other, over a *common* undecided `X`. The ideate (ll. 195–197): "two logical inductors with mutual good feedback can have persistently `≂ₙ`-distinct estimates of an unobservable `X` even where each `≂ₙ`-knows the other's estimate". The mandate's T9(3): "each's expectation of the other's estimate `≈ₙ` it, yet their estimates do not `≈ₙ` agree ... if it cannot be typed without the bundle, that is the finding (an OPEN not statable over FAF is not a deliverable — then record it, no `sorry`)".

**What is wrong, in three parts.**

(i) *The disagreement conjunct is decoupled.* `A n φ` — `A`'s own price of `φ` — occurs in the final conjunct and nowhere else. The two tracking clauses say that `A`'s merge estimate tracks `H`'s deferred price (`B.est ≈ₙ B.μ`) and that `H`'s read of the merge estimate tracks it (`𝔼^H(β) ≈ₙ B.est`); nothing in the bundle quotes `A`'s own price of `φ` into `H`'s language, so `H` "knows" `A`'s forecast of `H`, never `A`'s estimate of `φ`. The source's "each knows the other's estimate" is therefore absent on the `H`-reads-`A` side, and the conjunct that is supposed to be the Aumann failure can be discharged by any mechanism that moves `A`'s price of `φ` away from `H`'s. Probe `AumannOpenDisagreementDecoupled.lean`, `disagreement_of_disproved_elsewhere`: for any bundle `B` and sentence `φ`, from the OPEN's own second conjunct (`φ` positively undecided in `H`'s process at every stage — FAF's `lic_nonDogmatism` keeps `H n φ ≥ ε` eventually) and `∃ k, (∼φ) ∈ (mirrorProcess …).D k` (`A`'s process disproves `φ` at some stage — FAF's `lic_disprovable_tendsto_zero` gives `A n φ → 0`), the final conjunct `¬ ((fun n => B.H n φ) ≈ₙ (fun n => B.A n φ))` follows. That is two inductors with different theories, which the source rules out ("`X` not decidable by `Γ`"; the OPEN requires undecidedness only in `DPH`, not in `A`'s process). A witness of the typed statement would witness nothing about agreement under mutual knowledge.

(ii) *The reading the clauses support is refuted.* Over the bundle, "`A`'s estimate of `φ`" is the merge estimate `B.est n` (`merging-inductors-model.md` §0: `B_t(φ) := 𝔼^A_t(⌜ℙ^H_{f(t)}(φ)⌝)`; the tracking clauses are written for it), so the mandate's "their estimates do not `≈ₙ` agree" reads `¬ ((fun n => B.H n φ) ≈ₙ (fun n => B.est n))`. Probe `AumannOpenMergeEstimateAgrees.lean`, `mergeEstimate_reading_agrees`: for any bundle on a constant family, `B.est ≈ₙ B.μ` implies `(fun n => B.H n φ) ≈ₙ (fun n => B.est n)` — because `B.μ n = H_{f n}(φ)` (`literalIndicator_expect`), `H n φ` converges (`lic_price_convergesTo`, `thm:con`, with `B.H_hworld`), `f n → ∞` (`DeferralFunction.lt`), so `H n φ − H_{f n}(φ) → 0`, and the tracking clause does the rest. So the LI Aumann failure *as a statement over the bundle for a fixed sentence* is false: mutual per-day tracking plus convergence forces `H`'s price to agree with the merge estimate. By the mandate's own rule this is a finding to record without a `sorry`, not an OPEN to leave. The typed `A n φ` is the only non-refuted choice of conclusion over the bundle — and (i) says why it is non-refuted.

(iii) *The docstring, open-list entry, ledger row and F-T9' describe the source's statement, not the typed one.* Docstring: "each's expectation of the other's estimate tracking it per day ..., yet their prices of `φ` not `≈ₙ`-agreeing"; open list: "each's expectation of the other's estimate tracking it, yet their prices of it not agreeing"; ledger Claim: "two inductors with mutual good feedback, an undecided sentence, mutual per-day tracking, prices not agreeing"; F-T9': "per-day tracking of each agent's read of the other's estimate". "Mutual" is one-directional about `H`'s price; "the other's estimate" on `H`'s side is `A`'s forecast of `H`, not `A`'s estimate. The docstring's "the per-day tracking clauses are per-day readability statements that cost (L) each" is also wrong for the `A`-side clause: under `σ_late` the ledger literal is absent before day `f n` (`ledgerLuv_absent_before_payout`), so `B.est ≈ₙ B.μ` is per-day *forecast* accuracy — the per-day grade of Prop A, which the package proves only at the averaged grade and which `thm:wub` does not deliver — not readability of a published number.

**Why it is blocking.** [STANDARDS](../../STANDARDS.md) §2: an open statement is "a precise Lean statement of a conjecture"; §3: names and docstrings don't oversell; §6: Status must match. The row's Status `open` and the name `li_aumann_failure_open` certify that *the LI Aumann failure* is what is open. As typed, the statement is a different conjecture — one whose hard part is the bundle's inhabitation plus a per-day tracking clause, and whose "disagreement" is free once two inductors with different limits on `φ` are in hand — and the reading faithful to the bundle is refuted. The grade of this OPEN is the mirror of audit r2 N5 on `li_nontransitivity_open`, but sharper: there the typed form at least contained the source's conjecture as an instance; here neither typed-form witnesses nor source-form witnesses need be the other (a source-form witness need not satisfy `𝔼^H(β) ≈ₙ B.est`; a typed-form witness need not have `H` know `A n φ`).

**Fix I would accept** (either; no proof changes). (a) *Retype to the source.* Keep the bundle for the `A`-reads-`H` side (by probe 1, `B.est ≈ₙ B.μ` is as good as tracking `H n φ`), add on the `H`-reads-`A` side an e.c. quote `γ : ℕ → LUV` in `H`'s language reflected at `A n φ` in every `DPH`-world (`li-quote-lane`'s `OneWayPair` shape, `paperOneWayPair`'s data) with the per-day clause `(fun n => (γ n).expect B.H n) ≈ₙ (fun n => B.A n φ)`, require `φ` undecided in both polarities in `A`'s process as well, and keep the conclusion `¬ (H n φ ≈ₙ A n φ)`; drop the `β` clause, which plays no role; say in the docstring that the merge estimate is not the object of the disagreement and that the typed form is now the source's symmetric statement. (b) *Withdraw the `sorry`.* Port probe 1 as a package theorem (`liAumann_mergeEstimate_agrees`, Kind C: tracking plus `thm:con` force `H n φ ≈ₙ B.est n` on a constant family), record in F-T9' and the ledger that the bundle reading of 2-022 is refuted and that the source's reading needs a same-day quote of `A`'s own price in `H`'s language (the F-API 2 shape the bundle does not carry), remove the entry from `trust-merge-open.txt`, and mark T9(3) as "finding, no OPEN" in the report's disposition. In both cases: fix the docstring's "readability" sentence, the open-list reason, the ledger Claim ("mutual per-day tracking") and F-T9'; and note that the `A`-side clause is the per-day grade of Prop A, stronger than any theorem of this package.
