## 3. Non-blocking issues

**N1 — the provenance label `(b)` on `hmarket` and `compiler` is wrong (presentation).** Ledger rows `overlay_isLogicalInductor_of_computableMarket`, `OverlayBoundedEvaluatorCompiler.toComputableMarket` / `overlay_isLogicalInductor_of_compiler`, and the `Hyps:` lines of the three docstrings say "(b) `hmarket : ComputableMarket (overlayHistory DP ov)`". STANDARDS §3 (b) is "a published theorem taken as stated because FAF lacks it". This hypothesis is not a theorem for general `ov`: it is *false* for most overlays. Sketch (prose, not machine-checked): by `overlay_half_differs_from`'s route, for every `n ≥ N₀` the cell `(n, largeAtom n)` is unmentioned, so an overlay constant in its past arguments with `ov n _ _ (largeAtom n) = f n` realises every `f : ℕ → ℚ ∩ [0,1]` on those cells — uncountably many distinct markets against countably many `ComputableMarket`s. The hypothesis is the antecedent of a conditional whose status the ledger correctly gives as `partial: computability open`; the right label is "assumed; L6(b), open; provable only under a computability condition on `ov` (F-15)". Fix: relabel the two Hyps cells and the three docstring lines. Nothing else changes.

**N2 — the numerals `676` and `1096` are prose, not Lean (presentation).** `firm_mentioned_small_from` exposes `N₀ = (C+16)^2` as a function of `C`, but bli-found's `tokenSize_le_of_structured` is `∃ C, …` with `C = 10` only inside its proof term (`refine ⟨10, ?_⟩`, `Cleanroom/Bli/BliFound/Structured.lean:601–605`), so no Lean statement in the run pins `676` (or attempt A's `1096`). Fix (cheap, bli-found side or here): a `tokenSize_le_of_structured_ten` with `C = 10` explicit, then `firm_mentioned_small_ten : ∀ n ≥ 676, …`. Until then the ledger's "`N₀ = (C+16)^2 = 676` at `C = 10`" should say the `10` is bli-found's proof-internal choice, not a stated constant.

**N3 — T3(a)'s `W ≠ ∅` branch was not exercised on an instance where `W = ∅` fails (weak-but-disclosed witness; closed by probe).** F-12 says so honestly. `audit-r1-probes/WaivedExercise.lean` supplies the mandate's "optional N+ for (a) on its own": `spike` (one share of `⌜a₀⌝` on day 0, nothing after) on the all-`0` history has day-0 value `1 > marketMakerError 0 = 1/2` in the all-true world, so the `W = ∅` hypothesis is provably false (`example : ¬ (∀ n ∉ ∅, ∀ v, …)`), and `not_exploits_of_dayValue_le_off_finite … {0} …` proves `¬ spike.Exploits zeroHist DP` for every `DP`. Probe (b) in `Vacuity.lean` adds that the half and zero overlays are different markets for **every** process, not only `paperDP 𝗜𝚺₁`.

**N5 — a D3 edge worth one sentence (presentation; probe (c)).** On day `n` a sentence the firm **mentions but does not trade** (a rank-`n` price leaf of an untraded sentence, F-4(b); or a past-day leaf) is priced `0` by the overlaid market on that day: `overlayQuote_of_mem` reads the core quote, the core state's support is inside the firm's support (`coreState_accepts.1`), so the "core quote" is FAF's junk `0` off the support.
