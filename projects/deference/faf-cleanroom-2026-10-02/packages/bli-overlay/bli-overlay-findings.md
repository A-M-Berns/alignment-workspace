Severity: **blocking** (the note's conclusion fails) / **local error** / **imprecision** / **presentation**. Pointers are to `bli-program` (`run/survey/bli-program.md`), `bli-overlay-mandate`, and the Lean under `Cleanroom/Bli/BliOverlay/`. Where the two attempts reached the same finding independently, that is said; where only one did, it is attributed.

## The mandate's Known issues (both attempts confirmed all five)

**F-1 (Known issue 1; local error in `bli-program` §2.1's sketch — confirmed by both, with proofs).** The program compares the firm's *token count* (`≈ n·n(n+1)^n`) with `sizeBound n = 2^{2^n}` and concludes the inclusion "from `n = 4` on". The token count is the wrong quantity: the size of a sentence a `P`-digit stream can name is exponential in `P` (bli-found's `bridgeBound C P ≤ 2^((C+6)(P+1))`, the Gödel escape), and the index-`j` clock `progClock j n = a (n+1)^k + a` has degree `k = progDeg j = (Nat.unpair j).2`, bounded for `j ≤ n` by `√n` and not better. The honest comparison is `2^((C+6)(progClock j n + 1))` against `2^{2^n}`, i.e. `(C+6)(√n (n+1)^{√n} + √n + 1) ≤ 2^n`. It holds eventually — from `N₀ = (C+16)^2 = 676` (attempt B, `AttemptB.firmClock_arith`) or `N₀ = 2^10 + 4C + 32 = 1096` (attempt A, `AttemptA.eventually_firm_clock_le`) at the structured constant `C = 10` — not from `n = 4`. The mandate's warning that the crude `Nat.unpair_left_le`/`unpair_right_le` bounds fail is confirmed by both (they give `progClock j n ≤ n(n+1)^n + n`, and `(n+1)^n > 2^n`). `sizeBound` needed no change; the target `firm_mentioned_small` stands.

**F-2 (Known issue 2; imprecision — confirmed by both).** The plan's F2 wording (`support … ⊆ smallSet n`, traded sentences only) is weaker than what §3.3 (i) uses (the mentioned set, price leaves included). The headline is the `MentionedBy` form `firm_mentioned_small`; `firm_support_subset_smallSet` is its corollary through `support_subset_mentionedSet`, marked `Fidelity: weaker`.

**F-3 (Known issue 3; presentation — confirmed by both, sharpened by attempt B).** The plan's angle-B wording names `liaTrader_not_exploited` as the lemma that "applies unchanged"; the trader-generic lemma is `marketMaker_not_exploited (Tr) (DP)` (`MarketMaker.lean:1547`), and it *is* what applies on angle B: `AttemptB.overlay_firm_not_exploited` is `marketMaker_not_exploited (pvTrader DP ov) DP` transported along an equality of plausible-assessment sets. Both angles define the states before any trader (attempt A's `firmStrat` and attempt B's `pvTrader` are definitions *after* their recursions); what makes the definition-after-the-fact harmless is that FAF's `marketMakerStates` reads `Tr.strat n` only at day `n`, so `coreState_eq_marketMakerStates` closes by the same strong induction as `liaStates_eq_marketMakerStates`. On angle A neither lemma is used (T4 goes through `MarketMaker_accepts` → T3(b) → T3(a) with `W = ∅` → `trading_firm_dominance`).

**F-4 (Known issue 4; imprecision in §3.3 (i) — confirmed by both, extended by attempt A).** §3.3 (i) says the market-maker argument "is indifferent to what the history says *off that support*, on that day and on earlier days". (a) *Earlier days*: the firm's coefficients carry price leaves at earlier days (the enumerated traders' features are arbitrary `EF`s of rank `≤ n`), so the indifference is off the strategy's *mentioned set*, not its support — T1 is exactly the right congruence; the budgeter reads earlier days as *data* (`rawPriorWorthRat`, `priorBudgetBreach`, `budgetAtoms` sit inside `EF.const` and the `if` of `BudgeterAt`), never as leaves, and the strategy *itself* depends on the earlier days as data — which is why the firm must be built from the overlaid table (`firmStrat_eq`). (b) *That day* (attempt A): an enumerated trader may read `price ψ n` for a `ψ` it does not trade, so a day-`n` overlay must stay off the *mentioned* set, not merely off the support; the budgeter's own day-`n` leaves (`worldValueFeature` carries `price φ n` for traded `φ`) are inside the support. Nothing breaks: D3 overlays outside `mentionedSet (firmStrat n)`; the prose overstates.

**F-5 (Known issue 5; presentation — confirmed by both with proofs).** `mentionedSet (TradingFirmAt DP Q n)` does not depend on `DP` or `Q`: attempt A's `mentionedSet_TradingFirmAt_eq` (the union over `j ≤ n` of the enumerated traders' day-`n` mentioned sets) and attempt B's `mentionedSet_TradingFirmAt_eq DP DP' Q Q' n` (the equality form; `mentionedSet_TradingFirmAt_indep` in the record). The program's `∀ Q` in F2 is vacuous for the *set*; it still matters for the *strategy* (whose coefficients depend on `Q` as data), so the statement over all `Q` is the right one and costs nothing.

## Findings the attempts added

**F-6 (mandate T2 step 3; presentation — attempt A).** The mandate asks for square-root bounds on *both* `Nat.unpair` components; only the degree's is load-bearing (`progCoeff j ≤ j ≤ n` suffices for attempt A's estimate). Attempt B used both, which is how it reaches the smaller constant: with `progCoeff j ≤ √n` the clock is `≤ √n (n+1)^{√n} + √n` and `(C+6)(2√n+1) ≤ 4^{√n}`, giving `(C+16)^2`; attempt A's coefficient-crude route pays `≈ log₂ n` extra in the exponent and lands at `2^10 + 4C + 32`. Neither decomposition is the mandate's suggested "`(n+1)^{√n} ≤ 2^{n/2}` from `n + 1 ≤ 2^{√n/2}`" (attempt A, F-7): A goes exponent-first through `4(l+1)^2 ≤ 2^l` for `l = log₂ n ≥ 10`, B through `4(s+1)^2 ≤ 2^s` for `s = √n ≥ 9`.

**F-7 (FAF API request; presentation — attempt A).** `LogicalInduction.quoteFromEntries_map_eq` (`MarketMaker.lean`, lookup in `l.map (ψ ↦ (ψ, f ψ))`) is `private`; attempt A re-proves it (`AttemptA.quoteFromEntries_map_eq`) for `restrictState_quote_of_mem`. Making it public would remove the duplicate.

**F-8 (mandate T5 route; presentation — attempt A).** The witness day `n := max N₀ 1` is unnecessary: bli-found's `largeOn_witness n` holds for every `n`, so `n := N₀` suffices. Both attempts note that the mandate's optional `overlayQuote_eq_lia_on_mentioned` (agreement with the LIA on the mentioned set for a *nonzero* overlay) is false in general — the firm reads the overlaid past as data, so its day-`n` strategy and mentioned set differ from the LIA's — and neither stated it.

**F-9 (the T2 witness's day; presentation — both).** `firm_mentions_something` exhibits the day as the enumeration index of FAF's `buyAtomDaily` from `exists_enumeratedTrader_eq` (through complexitylib's `exists_desc_computesInTime_clock`); the index is existential, and no concrete day was computed (that would mean evaluating a TM compilation — the `decide`-on-FAF cost the mandate forbids in practice; FAF's `MachineTraderProgram.index` would be the route). The witness is N+ on the strength of the construction (a real e.c. trader, a real day-varying sentence, every `DP` and `Q`); an auditor wanting a numeric day should not expect one from this route.

**F-10 (T3(a)'s quantifier; presentation, positive — attempt A).** The waived-days lemma is stated over **every** `PCWorld`, not only worlds consistent with `DP.D n`, and its proof needs no process — the unconditional shape of FAF's `marketMaker_day_value_le`. Consequently it holds for a `DP` all of whose stages are unsatisfiable, where `Exploits` is empty anyway; the N+ at `paperDP 𝗜𝚺₁` (`overlay_half_paperDP_hworld`, `overlay_plausibleAssessments_nonempty`) is what shows the theorem is not empty at its use.

**F-11 (program §3.3 (iii) "bounded day value"; imprecision — attempt B).** The bound is `∑_{n ∉ W} 2^{-(n+1)} + ∑_{n ∈ W} absBound_n`; the second sum is finite only because `W` is, and the program's "(bounded day value)" needs the range clause on the history (`hP`) for `Strategy.abs_value_le` — a history outside `[0,1]` has no uniform day bound, and the lemma is false for it. `not_exploits_of_dayValue_le_off_finite` carries `hP`.

**F-12 (mandate T3(c) witness; resolved — both).** T3(a)/(c) with `W ≠ ∅` is exercised by the waived recursion (T7): `waived_firm_not_exploited` applies T3(a) with an arbitrary finite `W`, and `waivedQuote_empty` / `AttemptB.waived_empty_eq` show the `W = ∅` case is the unwaived recursion. What remains unexhibited is a *numeric* `W ≠ ∅` day, which nothing needs. The mandate's remark that on angle A T7 is "immediate" is confirmed (one `if n ∈ W` in the day step); its remark that on angle B "the waived days [are] handled by T3(a) after all" is also confirmed — on both angles T7's no-exploitation is T3(a)/(c) with the given `W`; the past-view trick is not what carries it on either.

**F-13 (§3.3 (ii) "the market maker checks against the overlaid past"; imprecision in the documentation of the guard — attempt B).** On angle B the acceptance test is run on the substituted strategy against the **core** past; `marketMakerAccepts_substPast_iff` shows this is the same test as the bare strategy against a past whose rational history agrees with the substituted constants on the mentioned past cells. So the phrase is literally false for the core past FAF's `MarketMaker` is handed, and true only after substitution — which is exactly what an auditor should check at a past-day leaf (trap (i)). On angle A (the record) the phrase is literally true: the past handed to `MarketMaker` is the restricted *overlaid* past (`rationalHistory_overlayRestrictedPast`). Not an error in the mathematics.

**F-14 (T4(v) needs both halves of the induction; presentation — attempt B).** `overlayQuote_zero_eq_liaQuote` needs states and quotes at once: the day-`n` firms coincide only because the tables below `n` coincide, and the tables coincide off the mentioned set only because the LIA state's support lies inside the firm's support (`MarketMaker_accepts.1`). The program's sketch treats "the overlay may set every unmentioned sentence" and "FAF quotes `0` elsewhere" as independent facts; they are linked through `support ⊆ mentionedSet`, which is where an over-narrow D1 (traded sentences only) would have broken the identity.

**F-15 (T6, the shape of the open item; presentation, for `bli-coherent-mm` — both).** With both `Computation.lean`s the only unproved link between `overlay_no_ec_trader_exploits` and `IsLogicalInductor (overlayHistory DP ov) DP` is the inhabitation of the compiler boundary — `Computable₂` of the natural-coded bounded evaluator. It is a genuine open item of size L–XL (FAF's certificate for the LIA is `LIACompiler.lean`, 4041 lines), and it depends on `ov`: for an `ov` that is not a computable function of the encoded records the certificate is false, so any claim of L6(b) for the BLI must name the overlay's computability (the Tier-B tent values of `bli-superbelief`'s `tentExpr` are computable terms; B3's coherent world measure is a search). The two boundaries differ in one design choice — attempt A decodes stages inside the evaluator as FAF does (`overlayPrefixAtFuelP` over a `DeductiveProcessComputation`), attempt B takes the stage table as a parameter with a `stages : ∀ m, D m = DP.D m` field — and a continuation would need (i) the stage decoding, (ii) the compilation of the mentioned-set scan and the restricted past / the substitution, (iii) `ov` as a computable function of `(n, encoded past, encoded state, encoded sentence)`. Not stated as a Lean `sorry` by either attempt, because the honest statement needs a definition of "`ov` computable on encoded finite data" that no customer has fixed.

## The reconciliation's findings

**R-1 (the two recursions are the same object; positive — proved).** The mandate predicted the two attempts' recursions "should" produce the same core states because `MarketMaker` depends on its inputs only through the mentioned cells. Proved: `coreState_quote_eq_attemptB` (`CrossCheck.lean`) — for every `DP`, `ov`, `n`, attempt A's core state and overlaid table equal attempt B's. The day step is `AttemptB.MarketMaker_congr`; the two acceptance predicates agree because the past-substituted firm's exact value on a candidate history reads the substituted constants at every mentioned past cell and those are what the restricted past lists (`rationalHistory_overlayRestrictedPast`), and on day `n` both read the candidate. There is **no disagreement** between the attempts on any target: every headline is proved by both, over definitions now proved equal. The identity also gives the record both readings of the object — FAF's `MarketMaker` on the firm against the overlaid past (by definition) and FAF's `marketMakerStates` of the past-view trader (`coreState_eq_marketMakerStates`) — and a second, independent derivation of the firm's non-exploitation (`overlay_firm_not_exploited_via_marketMaker`, through `marketMaker_not_exploited` rather than T3(a)).

**R-2 (which angle is of record, and why; presentation).** The mandate's tie-break — "whichever reaches T4 with fewer `(c)` clauses (definitional stand-ins counted)" — does not separate the attempts: both have zero. The record takes attempt A's recursion because its day step hands FAF's fixed point the firm's own strategy and a past carrying the overlaid values (the literal reading of §3.3 (ii)), with no local transformation of the strategy (`substPast` is a new `EF → EF` map, exact by `denoteWith_substPast`, but a device FAF does not have); attempt A's only local device, `restrictState`, builds an FAF `RationalBeliefState` from entries. Attempt B's angle is kept in full as evidence and its signature identity is transported (R-1). For T2 the record takes attempt B's proof (the smaller constant) and keeps attempt A's as the independent derivation.

**R-3 (the customer's entry point has two natural shapes; presentation, for `bli-coherent-mm`).** Attempt A's `dayValue_le_of_accepted_state` takes an arbitrary rational table with the acceptance-style bound (no `RationalBeliefState`, no `MarketMaker`); attempt B's takes a past and an accepted state with the agreement split into past cells and day-`n` cells, plus a form for the past-substituted strategy. The record keeps all three (`dayValue_le_of_accepted_state`, `_split`, `_subst`); the arbitrary-table form is the most general, and M2's coherent market maker only has to produce a table with the bound.

**R-4 (what was not cross-checked; scope).** The waived recursions (T7) of the two attempts were not proved equal (only their `W = ∅` instances are, through each attempt's `_empty` lemma and R-1); the two T6 evaluators were not related (each is sound for its own recursion, and the recursions are equal by R-1, so their successes are equal prefixes — a corollary not stated in Lean). Neither is a gap in the package's claims; both are noted so an auditor does not look for them.
