## Known issues of the mandate, as met

**KI-1 (program §2.1's numeric claim; local error — confirmed).** The program compares the firm's *token count* (`≈ n·n(n+1)^n`, "below `2^{2^n}` from `n = 4` on") with `sizeBound`. The size of a sentence a `P`-digit stream can name is exponential in `P` (bli-found's `bridgeBound C P ≤ 2^((C+6)(P+1))`, from the Gödel escape), and the clock at index `j ≤ n` has coefficient and degree up to `√n` (`unpair_fst_le_sqrt`, `unpair_snd_le_sqrt`, `progClock_le`), so the honest comparison is `2^((C+6)(√n·(n+1)^{√n} + √n + 1))` against `2^{2^n}`. The inclusion holds from `N₀ = (C+16)^2` on (`firmClock_arith`; `676` at `C = 10`), not from `n = 4`. The mandate's warning that the crude `Nat.unpair_left_le`/`unpair_right_le` bounds do not suffice is correct: they give `progClock j n ≤ n(n+1)^n + n` and `(n+1)^n > 2^n`. `sizeBound` needed no change. Severity: local error in the program's sketch; the target stands.

**KI-2 (the plan's F2 wording; imprecision — confirmed).** `support (…) ⊆ smallSet n` (traded sentences) is weaker than what §3.3 (i) uses (the mentioned set, price leaves included). Both are proved: `firm_mentionedSet_subset_smallSet` is the headline, `firm_support_subset_smallSet` the corollary (`support_subset_mentionedSet`). Severity: imprecision.

**KI-3 (the plan's angle-B wording; presentation — confirmed, with a sharpening).** "`liaTrader_not_exploited` applies unchanged" names the wrong lemma: `liaTrader_not_exploited` is specific to `liaTrader DP`; the trader-generic lemma is `marketMaker_not_exploited (Tr) (DP)`, and it *is* what applies unchanged here (`overlay_firm_not_exploited` is `marketMaker_not_exploited (pvTrader DP ov) DP` transported along `overlay_plausibleAssessments_eq`). The past-view trader is defined **after** the states (`pvTrader` in `Recursion.lean` comes after `overlayDay`): its day-`n` strategy substitutes the overlaid days `< n`, which are functions of the states. What makes the definition-after-the-fact harmless is that FAF's `marketMakerStates` recursion only ever reads `Tr.strat n` at day `n`, so `coreState_eq_marketMakerStates` closes by the same strong induction as `liaStates_eq_marketMakerStates`. Severity: presentation.

**KI-4 (§3.3 (i)'s "indifferent … on earlier days"; imprecision — confirmed).** The firm's coefficients carry day-`n` price leaves of traded sentences (`worldValueFeature`: `mem_priceQueries_worldValueFeature`) and the budgeter reads earlier days as **data** (`rawPriorWorthRat`, `priorBudgetBreach`, `budgetAtoms` — none contributes a price leaf: `mem_priceQueries_budgetScaleFeature`). So indifference holds off the strategy's *mentioned cells* (T1, `Strategy.value_eq_of_eqOn_mentioned`), not off its support, and only for the value of the day strategy — the strategy *itself* depends on the earlier days as data, which is why the firm must be built from the overlaid table (`firmStrat_eq`). Severity: imprecision.

**KI-5 (independence of `DP`, `Q`; presentation — confirmed and proved as an equality).** `mentionedSet_TradingFirmAt_eq DP DP' Q Q' n : mentionedSet (TradingFirmAt DP Q n) = mentionedSet (TradingFirmAt DP' Q' n)` — the firm's mentioned set is the union over the open indices `j ≤ n` of the enumerated raw traders' day-`n` mentioned sets (`mentionedBy_TradingFirmAt_iff`, both directions). The program's `∀ Q` in F2 is therefore vacuous as a quantifier over the *set*; it still matters for the *strategy* (whose coefficients depend on `Q` as data), so F2's statement over `TradingFirmAt DP Q n` for all `Q` is the right one and costs nothing. Severity: presentation.

## New findings

**F-B1 (the substitution is invisible to the market maker exactly when it equals the past; imprecision in §3.3 (ii)).** §3.3 (ii) says the overlaid table on days `< n` "is already defined" and the day-`n` values "enter only through the strategy's day-`n` price leaves". On angle B the *acceptance test* is run on the substituted strategy against the **core** past, and `marketMakerAccepts_substPast_iff` shows this is the same test as the bare strategy against a past whose rational history agrees with the substituted constants on the mentioned past cells. So the phrase "the market maker checks against the overlaid past" is literally false for the core past FAF's `MarketMaker` is handed, and true only after substitution — which is precisely what an auditor should check at a past-day leaf (the mandate's trap (i)). Not an error in the mathematics; a statement about what the fake version would look like. Severity: imprecision (documentation of the guard).

**F-B2 (the zero overlay is FAF's LIA — a proved fact, not an assumption; presentation).** `overlayQuote_zero_eq_liaQuote` needs *both* halves of the induction (states and quotes) at once: the day-`n` firms coincide only because the tables below `n` coincide, and the tables coincide off the mentioned set only because the LIA state's support lies inside the firm's support (`MarketMaker_accepts.1`). The program's sketch treats "the overlay may set every unmentioned sentence" and "FAF quotes `0` elsewhere" as two independent facts; they are linked through `support ⊆ mentionedSet` (`support_subset_mentionedSet`), which is where an over-narrow D1 (say, traded sentences only) would have broken the identity. Severity: presentation.

**F-B3 (`ComputableMarket` for the overlay needs `ov` on encoded finite data — the boundary's exact shape; open).** The compiler boundary `OverlayBoundedEvaluatorCompiler DP ov D` asks for `Computable₂ (overlayQuoteNatAtFuel D ov)` plus `D = DP.D`. Compared with FAF's `LIABoundedEvaluatorCompiler`, which takes a `DeductiveProcessComputation` and decodes stages inside the evaluator (`processStagePrefixAtFuel`), mine takes the stage table as a parameter: a customer instantiating it must (i) decode stages as FAF does, (ii) compile `prefixTable` (a `mentionedSet` scan over the firm's trades plus `Strategy.substPast`) and (iii) supply `ov` as a computable function of `(n, encoded past, encoded state, encoded sentence)`. None of this is in Lean; T6's honest status is `partial: computability open`, as the program predicts. Severity: none (an open target, stated).

**F-B4 (program §3.3 (iii) "bounded day value"; imprecision).** The waived-days bound is `∑_{n ∉ W} 2^{-(n+1)} + ∑_{n ∈ W} absBound_n`, and the second sum is finite only because `W` is; the program's "(bounded day value)" needs the range clause on the history (`hP`) for `Strategy.abs_value_le` to apply — a history outside `[0,1]` has no uniform day bound, and the lemma is false for it. `not_exploits_of_dayValue_le_off_finite` carries `hP`. Severity: imprecision.

**F-B5 (the T2 witness day is non-constructive; presentation).** `firm_mentions_something`'s index `j` comes from `exists_enumeratedTrader_eq` and is not computed; the mandate anticipated this ("say honestly which `n` your witness needs"). The witness is N+ nonetheless: a real e.c. trader, a day-varying sentence, every process and every table. A numeral day would need FAF's `MachineTraderProgram.index` evaluated on `buyAtomDaily`'s machine description, which the mandate forbids in practice (`decide` on FAF machinery). Severity: presentation.
