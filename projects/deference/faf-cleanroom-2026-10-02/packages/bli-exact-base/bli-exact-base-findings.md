## F1 — `bli-found`'s `D_PC` is coherence on the whole algebra over the small atoms, which no finite-support perturbation of FAF's LIA has on any day; the program's "coherent on the small sentences" is the small-sentence form (blocking for the mandate's literal K7b statement; local error in `bli-found`'s definition of record as a rendering of `bli-program` §2.6; a sharpening of bli-slides-044)

**Pointer.** `bli-found` `Constraints.D_PC` (`CoherentOn (DP.D n) ((smallSet n).biUnion sentenceAtomCodes) (Q n)` on every day), `Constraints.CoherentOn` (quantifies over *every* sentence whose atoms lie in `A`); `bli-program` §2.6 ("the base is propositionally coherent on the small sentences"); mandate § 2 (K7b: "exact `D_PC` … on every day `< H`") and § Definitions (`D_PC_on`, `¬ D_PC (spliceHistory …)` "days `≥ H` are the LIA"). Lean: `Tables.not_coherentOn_of_finiteSupportPerturbation` (P), `not_D_PC_on_splice`, `not_D_PC_splice`, `not_D_PC_lia`; the forms of record `Defs.CoherentOnSmall`, `D_PCsmall_on`, `Tables.segment_D_PCsmall_on`.

**What is true.** The algebra over the day-`n` small atoms contains the atom-free tautologies `⊤ ⋎ ⊥ ⋎ … ⋎ ⊥` of every length (`bli-linkage`'s `tautChain`, injective). A stage mixture prices every one of them at `1`. FAF's LIA prices every sentence outside its finite day-`n` support at `0`, and a finite-support perturbation changes finitely many coordinates, so on every day some chain is priced `0`. Hence for every deductive process `DP`, every `P` with `FiniteSupportPerturbation (liaHistory DP) P`, every day `n`, every finite `D` and every atom set `A`: `¬ CoherentOn D A (P n)`. In particular the mandate's `D_PC_on H (spliceHistory H t) (paperDP 𝗜𝚺₁)` is **false for every `H ≥ 1` and every table** — not because days `≥ H` are the LIA but because day `0` already fails — and `D_PC (liaHistory DP) DP` is false for every `DP`.

**What the sources mean.** `bli-program` §2.6's words are "on the small sentences"; the property `bli-linkage`'s package forces on a base (`LiaPackage.package_forces_*`: `E1x` carries the mixture's values to the base on `smallSet n` only) is agreement with a stage mixture on the small sentences; and bli-slides-044's gap is about exact coherence "on all sentences, or at least on the small ones". The statement of record for the splice is therefore `CoherentOnSmall (DP.D n) n (Q n)` — *some* stage mixture agrees with `Q n` on `smallSet n` — which is `coherentOnSmall_iff_exists_mixture_agree`: exactly the base clause of `PCPσ ∧ E1x` on one day. `bli-found`'s full-algebra `CoherentOn` remains right for the *superbelief* `P` (which prices every sentence), which is how `bli-linkage` uses it; it is wrong as a predicate on a base that is a finite perturbation of the LIA.

**Consequences.** (i) K7b's headline is `segment_D_PCsmall_on` with `Fidelity: weaker: small-sentence agreement`, and `D_PC_on` is kept as the mandate's literal with its emptiness proved. (ii) Every OPEN row of `Open.lean` is stated with `D_PCsmall`. (iii) bli-slides-044 sharpened: FAF's LIA is not exactly coherent on the small algebra on *any* day over *any* process, not merely not on all days. (iv) **API note for `bli-found`**: a small-sentence form of `D_PC` (this package's `D_PCsmall`) should sit next to the full-algebra one, with the docstring saying which bases can have which. (v) Stated sharply (audit r1 fidelity N10): the mechanism is finite per-day support alone — the Lean states it for finite-support perturbations of FAF's LIA, and any market that prices all but finitely many sentences at `0` on a day fails the same way — so as a base condition `bli-found`'s `D_PC` is wrong for every FAF-style construction, not only for perturbations of the LIA.

## F6 — The mandate's contrast lemma names a non-acceptable point; the acceptable point on the unconstrained simplex is the point mass at a cell of *minimal* representative (local error in the mandate's § 7)

**Pointer.** Mandate § 7 ("the point mass on the world `(φ true, lit r_max true)` with `rep r_max = 1`"). Lean: `Obstruction.contrast_acceptable` (P), `tradeValue_bundle`.

## F7 — bli-soto-b-026's displayed derivation has the wrong mixture weights; the conclusion survives (presentation; inventory flag confirmed and machine-checked)

**Pointer.** `bli-soto-b-inventory` bli-soto-b-026 (`Q_k(φ | Q_{k+n}(φ) = p) = ∑_B P(B)·B(φ | …)`). Lean: `Convex.wrong_weights_counterexample` (P), `Convex.mixture_cond_identity` (L), `Convex.selfTrustSet_convex` (P).

**What is true.** A mixture's conditional is the mixture of conditionals with weights `P(B)·B(E)/P(E)`; with weights `(1/2, 1/2)`, `B(E) = (1, 1/2)`, `B(φ∧E) = (1, 0)` the displayed formula gives `1/2` and the conditional is `2/3`. ## F16 — The splice's own quote code, twice: K7c (d)'s "by construction" hides a self-reference (dissolved here), and K7c (b)'s segment `[N₀, H)` is circular as written, with no pinned day before the horizon known (blocking for the non-vacuity of judged item 3 as the mandate designed it; local error in the mandate § 3 (b)/(d) and in round 0's ledger row for `pinned_eventually_splice`; continuation 1; the OPEN re-stated over the cell quote code in repair round 2 after audit r2 fidelity B1)

*(a) The self-reference.* The mandate's K7c (d) defines the day-`n` prices of the day-`(n+1)` cell literals "as the superbelief's cell masses" and calls the identity "by construction". But the splice's cell literal is the quotation atom `Formula.atom (litIdx code (n+1) c r)` (`Kernel.spliceCellSentence_eq_atom`), whose index nests the program code `code = (spliceCellQuote H t …).code` of the cell truth predicate of the table `t` itself — a number chosen by `Classical.choice` from a `Computable` proof. A table defined as the marginal of a mixture that sets *its own* literal atoms is therefore defined in terms of its own program code. The only general way to close such a loop is Kleene's recursion theorem (`Nat.Partrec.Code.fixed_point₂`) with an explicit `BooleanQuoteCode` (FAF's structure does expose `code : ℕ`), which needs the table to be **computable uniformly in the code** — sentence evaluation in finite worlds, rational sums over the family, the smallness test — and the mandate's own trap ("the tables are data you exhibit by the lemma's choice, not by computation") forbids exactly that. FAF's LIA never meets this: its process decides every program's quotation atoms uniformly and the market never prices its own literals exactly. *Dissolution (this package):* the kernel overrides, inside every world, **every** stage-fresh atom of the day-`(n+1)` cell-literal shape for the three segment coordinates — for every code at once (`Kernel.LitShape`, `Kernel.slice`). The table is then a function of the stage, the day and the index only; it never reads its code; the base's own literals are among the overridden atoms because every atom of that shape is fresh at stage `n` (`Kernel.litIdx_fresh`, by F18). The cost is dogmatism about *other* programs' literals of that shape (small sentences the stage has not decided), which F17 shows the package already has at the base's own uncharged literal. This is a `variant` of the mandate's Route W, disclosed in `Kernel.slice`'s docstring.

*(b) The segment.* `pinned C index n` asks the day-`(n+1)` literals to be small on day `n`; their atom index nests the code of the splice *at horizon `H`*, so `pinned_eventually_splice`'s `N₀` is `N₀(H, t)`. The mandate's "choose `H := N₀ + 2`" and round 0's `exists_segment_day N₀ : ∃ n, N₀ ≤ n ∧ n < N₀ + 3 ∧ 2 ≤ n` fix `N₀` before `H`: they are arithmetic in an abstract `N₀` and do not produce a horizon and a day `n < H` on which anything is pinned. Round 0's ledger row cited `exists_segment_day` as the N+ of `pinned_eventually_splice`; corrected (continuation 1). What the Lean fixes (corrected in repair round 1 after audit r1, both lenses): **no code at all**. `linkedCode H` is `(BooleanQuoteCode.ofComputable …).code`, and `ofComputable` takes its code by `Classical.choice` from `Nat.Partrec.Code.exists_code` (FAF `Quotation/Packages.lean:313`), so the chosen code is any program with that behaviour, short or long, and nothing in Lean bounds it in either direction: the pinned set before `H` is neither provably empty nor provably non-empty. Size estimate for what a *constructive* proof would yield: `tokenSize (Formula.atom a) ≈ log₄ a + O(1)`; the index is a six-deep `Nat.pair` nest, so `log a ≈ 16 · log code`; the table-embedding code (`spliceValue_computable` goes through `Splice.computable_lookupOr` with the constant list `segmentEntries H t`, which has more than `sizeBound n = 2^(2^n)` entries on day `n`, `bli-found` `sizeBound_lt_card_smallSet`) has `log code ≳ 2^(2^(H−1))`, so its literal is small (`≤ 2^(2^n)`) on no day `n < H`. A short code exists mathematically (the linked table is the brute-force average over the stage-consistent assignments; `O(log H)` bits plus constants), but exhibiting it needs the uniform computability of (a) *and* an explicit `Nat.Partrec.Code` with a size bound — and, since `spliceValue` is the LIA's `paperQuote` off the segment, an explicit code for the LIA's own quotes, which FAF also takes by `ofComputable`. **The consequence, drawn in repair round 2 (audit r2 fidelity B1):** a row stated at `linkedCF H` — at the code `Classical.choice` returned — is not a conjecture about the kernel but about which program the choice returned, closable by no construction (an explicit short code is a different number from `linkedCode H`) and refutable by no length argument. So the cell quote code is now a parameter: `QuoteLane.spliceCFq` / `Segment.linkedCFq H q` for an arbitrary `q : BooleanQuoteCode 𝗜𝚺₁ (spliceCellTruth H linkedPrice halfRound)`, with `linkedCF H = linkedCFq H (linkedQuote H)` (`rfl`); every K7c (d)/K7d statement is proved for every `q` (`segment_package_inhabited_q`, `linked_nnu_day_q`, `segment_D_NNUcell_on_q`, `kernel_identity_q`, `linked_literal_dogmatic_q`, `linked_not_D_ND_on_q`, `linked_actual_state_charged_q`, `linked_boundary_q`, `trilemma_sharp_segment_q`, `linked_pinned_eventually_q`; every `Kernel` lemma was already generic through `LitAtoms`, and `hf_any` is freshness for every code), the `linkedCF H` forms being their instances; and the OPEN row is `linked_segment_day_exists : ∃ H n (q : BooleanQuoteCode …), 2 ≤ n ∧ n < H ∧ ⌜⊤⌝ ∈ pinned (linkedCFq H q) segmentIndex n` — "some horizon admits a cell quote code of the linked splice short enough that a segment day is pinned before it", a genuine conjecture, plausibly true, closable by the explicit code above (`bli-short-code`).
