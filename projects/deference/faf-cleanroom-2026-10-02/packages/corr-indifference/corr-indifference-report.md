## Conventions and departures from the mandate

- **Utilities are parameters, `Press` is a field.** `SoaresModel O A₁ A₂` has fields `Press : Finset O` and `p : A₁ → Distr O` only; `best`, `IsBest`, `EU`, `branchSum`, `vN`, `vS`, `mixU`, `indiffU` take the utility as an argument, as the mandate's D-1 asks. `A₂` is a type parameter with no field, so that the derived objects are stated over one model.
- **`[Nonempty A₂]`** is an instance argument of the derived objects, not a field ("`A₂` finite nonempty").
- **D-2 is stated over an arbitrary finite `O`, `Press`** (`mixU` puts `if b ∈ Sh then chigh else clow` on every `o ∈ Press`), not only over `O = Obs`. The source's `O = {Pr, ¬Pr}` is the instance `twoObs` (`Obs.lean`), and `twoObs_vN` shows the quotient `vN` reduces there to the source's eq. (7) `max_{a₂} U_N(a₁, ¬Pr, a₂)`. Theorems 1–2 hold verbatim over the general `O` (Fidelity: stronger); the witness (`Witnesses.lean`) is on `twoObs` with `A₁ = Fin 2`.
- **`Fintype Obs`** did not exist in `corr-three-step` (it never sums over `Obs`); `Obs.lean` supplies the instance in this package's namespace. This is a new instance on another package's type, disclosed here; nothing of theirs is redefined.
- **The junk convention is carried explicitly.** `vN` and `vS` are the quotients of record; the product-form lemmas `branchSum_compl_eq_mul_vN` and `branchSum_press_eq_mul_vS` hold *unconditionally* (at mass 0 both sides are 0), which is why the §3 identity needs no nondegeneracy hypothesis as an equation of reals. Every headline that *reads* `vN` or `vS` as the conditional value it names carries the corresponding `pressMass < 1` / `0 < pressMass` hypothesis, and its docstring says whether the proof uses it (usually it does not).
