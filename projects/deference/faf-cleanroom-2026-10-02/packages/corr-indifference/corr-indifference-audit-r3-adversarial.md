## 1. Verdict

**blocking** — one issue, in the findings text and three docstrings/ledger cells around the package's headline finding F2, introduced by repair round 2: F2 now says that at a certain-press action "in the source's own terms `v_N(a₁)` is `0/0`", and on that basis presents `Witnesses.strict_cause_steering_twoObs` as a strict failure of the source's sentence "on the source's carrier". But §2.1 of the source — where eq. (10) and the refuted sentence live — defines `v_N(a₁) := U_N(a₁, ¬Pr, A₂(a₁, ¬Pr))`, a cell value with no division (l. 165); the conditional expectation `E[U_N | O ∉ Press ; a₁]` is §3's *redefinition* (l. 207). Under §2.1's own `v_N` the certain-press action of the witness has `v_N(sure) = 12`, attains (10), and *is* the `v_N`-maximiser, and the weak global reading of the sentence holds on `O = {Pr, ¬Pr}` with no qualifier at all (probe `CellMaxGlobalOptimum`, a 25-line theorem from the package's own lemmas). So the clause is false, and the "strict failure on the source's carrier" is a failure under §3's reachability-aware reading only — which is a sharper and correct finding, but not the one F2 states (B-1). Everything else holds: gate PASS re-run by me (`1739 declaration(s); axioms used: ['propext', 'Classical.choice', 'Quot.sound']`, the report's line reproduced); open list empty, no `sorry`; every one of the 168 declaration names in the ledger's first column exists (script over the modules); every repair-round-2 change named in the report is present in the Lean and was audited afresh (the ten new declarations: none is a squeeze, none vacuous, none an undisclosed (c); their arithmetic re-derived by hand); no headline is trivially true on its stated domain (the junk guards `ha₀`, `hq1`, `hnd` are real domain conditions, probes `VnmaxJunkGuard` and r2's `SilentMaxUnreachable`); no definition with junk values affects a headline's truth. Seven non-blocking items, one of them a weak N+ with a better witness supplied.

- **Where.** [corr-indifference-findings](corr-indifference-findings.md) F2, "Global reading" paragraph: "a certain-press action's silent cell is unreachable (its `vN` is the package's junk `0`; **in the source's own terms `v_N(a₁)` is `0/0`**).
