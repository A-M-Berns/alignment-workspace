## Modelling choices (disclosed once; each is on its declaration too)

1. **Popper axiom form is (b)**: `IsPopper` is P07 I2′'s reconstruction (Popper 1959 appendix *iv / van Fraassen 1976 as quoted by Hájek 2003 pp. 316–317, the multiplication axiom confirmed from Hájek). Additivity is required only on normal conditions (`¬ Abnormal p B`, i.e. `p (· | B) ≢ 1`).
2. **The `≡ 1` convention** lives only in `popperLimit`; `limitCond` keeps `dp-calibration`'s junk `0`.
3. **Rays are polynomial weight families** (`Ray`): no ordered field `ℝ(ε)`. A ray "starts at `C`" when its constant coefficients are `C`'s weights (`IsRayOf`).
4. **The ray limits are algebraic** like `dp-calibration`'s: quotient of the coefficients at the order of the condition polynomial.
5. **Nonstandard labels in polynomial coordinates**: `NonnegNear0 p := p = 0 ∨ 0 < trailingCoeff p`; the bridge lemma (`Nonstandard.lean`) proves it equivalent to non-negativity on some `(0, ε₀)`.
6. **MSR is Definition 18′ verbatim** (`MSRAt`): realizability of every supported act is demanded unconditionally; the relation to D4's escape clauses is a theorem, not a redefinition.
