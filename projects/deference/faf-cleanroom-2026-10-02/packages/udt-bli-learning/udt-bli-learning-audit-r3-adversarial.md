### N1 — The refuting family `xPrior` and the N+ family `wPrior` vary the *utility* with `n`; the report's "not an artifact of the encoding" check does not say so, and the finding is robust to fixing it (probe `FixedUtilityRefutation.lean`)

**Declarations.** `EpsCorrWitness.vPrior g b` (`handPrior` over `tfMass`, `twoState`, `tfPP` and the utility `vU g b`), `wPrior n := vPrior 1 (10/(n+1))`, `xPrior n := vPrior (1/(2(n+1))) (1/(n+1))`; headlines `not_exactAsymptoticClause`, `x_value_eventually_optimal_choice_never`, `w_inhabits_eventually_eps_updateful`, `w_inhabits_eventually_updateful_of_gap`, `w_oneStep_flips`; ledger rows for all of these; F14; report T9 and `## Repair round 2` ("Not an artifact of the encoding: the family's points are positive, its branch probabilities action-blind, its utility bounded by `1`, and the gap that defeats the clause is the model's own home-value difference").

**What I checked.** In `vPrior g b` the base law `tfMass`, the state map and the points are constant in `(g, b)`; **only `U = vU g b` changes**. So along `xPrior n` the agent's beliefs about the world (`μ`, `state`, `pp`) never move and its utility function does — the opposite of a *belief*-state sequence (`Defs.lean`'s `beliefState`, by contrast, keeps `U` and moves `μ` through `conditionOn`). The theorems `eventually_eps_updateful`, `eventually_updateful_of_gap` and the proposition `ExactAsymptoticClause` quantify over arbitrary `Q : ℕ → FiniteBLIPrior`, so the refutation is a correct refutation of the clause *as stated*, and the N+ inhabits the headlines' packages *as stated*. But the source's clause is about one agent in one environment whose beliefs update (Notion l. 368: "for each environment … the agent's belief in acausal correlations is below epsilon … the performance of UDT will eventually be optimal in that environment"), and F14's headline says the exact clause "is false" and "the source's defensible content is the approximate statement". If the clause restricted to fixed-utility sequences were *true*, that headline would be an artifact of the encoding freedom, which is exactly what STANDARDS §3's artifact check is for — and the report's check lists positivity, action-blind branch probabilities, boundedness and the home-value gap, never the moving utility.

**Evidence that the finding stands.** Probe `audit-r3-probes/FixedUtilityRefutation.lean` (elaborates with `scripts/lean-check`, exit 0, no errors): worlds `Ω4 = (state, pp·T1, pp·T2, hidden)`, base law `yMass p = (1/8)·(if hidden then p else 1 − p)`, points `yPP` as `tfPP`, and **one fixed utility**

`yU ω = [state = T1] · (if pp·T1 then 0 else [hidden]) + [state = T2] · (if pp·T1 then 2·[hidden] else 0)`.

Computed for every `p ∈ [0, 1]` (`y_ppMass = 1/2`, `y_jointMass = 1/4`, `y_branchProb = 1/2`, `y_condEU_T2_T1 = (2p, 0)`, `y_condEU_T1_T2 = p/2`, `y_homeEU_T1 = (0, p)`, `y_homeEU_T2 = p`, `y_EU_T1 = (p, p/2)`): `CorrBounded (yPrior p) T (2p)` at both tables (`y_corrBounded`), `|yU| ≤ 2`, and for `p > 0` `pay` is a one-step choice at `T1` and not an updateful one (`y_pay_oneStep_not_updateful`). The family `zPrior n := yPrior (1/(n+2))` has `(zPrior n).U = (zPrior n').U` by `rfl` (`zPrior_U_fixed`; likewise `state` and `pp`, `zPrior_state_pp_fixed`), satisfies every hypothesis of the clause with `ε_n = 2/(n+2) → 0`, `M = 2`, `ρ = 1/2` (`z_package`), and **`fixedU_not_exactAsymptoticClause : ¬ ExactAsymptoticClause witIndex 1 twoTables Bool 2`** closes. So the exact clause is false even when only the prior over a hidden coordinate moves — a genuine sequence of updated beliefs of one agent with one utility. The package's diagnosis ("the slack bounds the loss, not the choice") is the mechanism in both families; the moving utility was a convenience, not the cause.

**Fix I would accept.** (i) One clause in the docstrings of `vPrior`, `wPrior`, `xPrior` and in the rows for `not_exactAsymptoticClause` / `x_*` / `w_*`: "the base law, state map and points are `tfPrior`'s and constant along the family; the stage-`n` object differs in its utility `vU g_n b_n`" — and the same words in F14 and in the report's artifact sentence, together with the fixed-utility check. (ii) Either cite the probe as the artifact check, or adopt `zPrior`/`yU` into `EpsCorrWitness.lean` as a second refuting family (16 worlds; the probe's lemmas are the computation). The same construction with `2` replaced by `20` and the hidden mass `1/(n+2)` gives a fixed-utility N+ for the two asymptotic headlines (gap `1`, correlation `20/(n+2)`, verdict flipping at `n = 19`); I did not machine-check that variant (cost; the refutation was the check STANDARDS asks for).
