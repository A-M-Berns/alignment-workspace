## 3. Non-blocking issues

### N1. `FSADependence` (Defs) drops the source's second-situation clause; the refutation survives it (probe A)

[formal-single-agent](../../sources/udt-representation-theorem/formal-single-agent.md) lines 63–69 read, in full: "there exists a policy π with π(s) = a **and π(s') = a₁**, and another policy π' with π'(s) = a **and π'(s') = a₂**, such that: E[U | π] ≠ E[U | π'] despite agreeing on action at s", after "there exist situations s ≠ s' and actions a, a' such that U(s, a) depends on the action chosen at s'". The Lean predicate `FSADependence U := ∃ s π π', π s = π' s ∧ U π ≠ U π'` has no `s'`, `a₁`, `a₂`; the docstring's quotation elides exactly those words with "…", and the ledger row's Fidelity cell says `exact`. Under the weakest literal reading (`a₁`, `a₂` unconstrained) the two predicates are equivalent as soon as `|S| ≥ 2`, which is the refutation's standing hypothesis; under the reading the gloss suggests (`a₁ ≠ a₂`, "depends on the action chosen at s'") the source's predicate is strictly stronger. Probe A defines the stronger predicate `FSADependence'` (`s ≠ s'`, `π s = π' s`, `π s' ≠ π' s'`, `U π ≠ U π'`) and proves both that `sepIndicator` satisfies it (`fsaDependence'_sepIndicator`: `(1,0)` and `(1,1)` agree at `0`, disagree at `1`, score `1 ≠ 2`) and that on `|S| ≥ 2` it is still exactly non-constancy (`fsaDependence'_of_nonconstant`, by walking from `π` to `π'` one coordinate at a time and taking the first value-changing step). So the finding "the definition is non-constancy" holds under every reading, and only the Fidelity cell and the elided quotation are off. Fix I would accept: either state the predicate with the clause (the probe's form, plus `fsaDependence_of_fsaDependence'`) or keep `FSADependence` and change Fidelity to "variant: drops the `s'` clause; equivalent on `|S| ≥ 2` for `a₁, a₂` unconstrained, and the refutation holds for the `a₁ ≠ a₂` reading too (audit probe)". Quote the sentence without the ellipses.

### N2. `SelfSealing.statDecay_stationary` admits `ρ > 1`, where nothing is a distribution (probe B)

The hypotheses are `0 < ε < 1` and `0 < ρ`, with no `ρ ≤ 1`. At `ε = 9/10`, `ρ = 10` — admitted — `stepDecay` has the entry `P(1B → 1B) = 1 − ρ = −9` (`stepDecay_junk`) and the "stationary distribution" has `π(2B) = 100/29 > 1`, `π(1B) < 0` (`statDecay_junk`), while `statDecay_stationary` still applies (`statDecay_stationary_junk_instance`): the theorem is an algebraic fixed-point identity, and on that range it says a non-distribution is a fixed point of a non-stochastic matrix. The intended range is inside the hypotheses and the identity is right there, so the headline is not wrong, but "exact stationary law" (ledger, docstring) needs the bound. Probe B supplies it: `statDecay_nonneg` and `statDecay_le_one` under `0 < ε < 1`, `0 < ρ ≤ 1` (`2ρ + ε − 2ερ − ρ = ρ(1−ε) + ε(1−ρ) ≥ 0`). Fix: add `ρ ≤ 1` to `statDecay_stationary` and `perEpisodeValue_eq` (or state the two bounds beside them) and say "distribution" only then.

### N3. The EDT verdict in `edt_ne_udt_transparent` is the payoff table; the prior enters only through non-nullness (probe C)

`edtScore_eq_of_pos` already says it: on a positive event `{O = o, A = a}` the payoff is the constant `u o a`, so the EDT score *is* the cell. Probe C makes the consequence explicit: for any two full-support priors and `0 < ε < 1`, `edtScore μ ε o a = edtScore μ' ε o a` at every `(o, a)` (`edtScore_prior_independent`), and the EDT verdict is the dominance verdict cell by cell (`u_dominance : u o one < u o two`). This is the standard fact that EDT, having conditioned on the observation, two-boxes in transparent Newcomb, and it is what the source claims; the theorem is not trivially true for an unintended reason (its content is on the UDT side, T9's threshold, and in the *comparison*). But the headline's docstring "for every full-support prior over the agent's own policy" reads as if the prior shaped the EDT verdict. Say in the docstring and the ledger row that the prior is load-bearing only through positivity of the four events (which is exactly what T10(b) then removes), and that on this model the EDT rule coincides with CDT's dominance verdict — the separation from UDT is the point, not a difference from CDT. Worth adding in the same breath: the separation on the full branch holds against *both* readings of "UDT" the corpus uses — UDT1.1 (`oneTwo_strictArgmax`, `ε < 999/1999`) and the corpus's own rule `argmax_a E[U | π(o) = a]` = `ctScore` ([when-udt-edt-diverge](../../sources/udt-representation-theorem/when-udt-edt-diverge.md) line 27, [notation](../../sources/udt-representation-theorem/notation.md) §3.2) (`ctRule_oneBoxes_on_full_of_lt`, `ε < 999/2000`) — so the headline's choice of UDT1.1 is not load-bearing for the separation, only for the *empty*-branch disagreement of T10(c).
