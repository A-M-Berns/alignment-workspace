## 3. Non-blocking issues

1. **`Transparent.edt_ne_udt_transparent` — ledger Fidelity `exact` against a source it partly refutes.** The ledger row cites [when-udt-edt-diverge](../../sources/udt-representation-theorem/when-udt-edt-diverge.md) lines 84–88 as the source. Line 86 of that note says: seeing the empty box, EDT two-boxes, while UDT "one-boxes even facing the empty box — which is what makes the box full for agents with that policy" — i.e. the source locates the separation on the *empty* branch with UDT one-boxing there. In the model of record (gap1 §8, `Transparent.V`) the policy optimum strictly two-boxes on `empty` for every `ε > 0` (margin `1000·ε`: probe `probe_udt_twoBoxes_on_empty`) and ties at `ε = 0` (`probe_tie_at_zero`); the Lean's separation is on the *full* branch. Relative to the mandate's T10 target the theorem is exact; relative to the cited source it is a variant that proves the separation the source wanted and refutes the route the source gave. The docstring says "the corpus's prose gives the empty-branch story, on which both two-box" and findings §1 says "the UDT policy also two-boxes there", so the fact is disclosed — but the ledger's `exact` cell is the one place a reader checks, and the findings file never names the source's sentence as an error. Fix I'd accept: Fidelity cell `variant: separation on the full branch; the source's empty-branch story is refuted (the optimum strictly two-boxes on empty for ε > 0, ties at ε = 0)`, and one sentence in findings §1 quoting line 86 with severity *local error* in [when-udt-edt-diverge](../../sources/udt-representation-theorem/when-udt-edt-diverge.md) (and the parallel sentence in [daniel-h-challenge](../../sources/udt-representation-theorem/daniel-h-challenge.md) "Transparent Newcomb").

2. **T13's reading of "simplifies to" — name the tautological reading too.** The refutation `Simplification.rhs_argmax_ne_lhs_argmax` reads the right side of [core-argument-draft](../../sources/udt-representation-theorem/core-argument-draft.md) Step 4 as C&T's prior-conditioned rule `E_μ[U | π(s) = a]` ([notation](../../sources/udt-representation-theorem/notation.md) §3.2), which the source's next sentence ("condition on 'my policy maps this situation to action a'") supports; the docstring, ledger and findings §6 label this ATTRIBUTION-UNVETTED. There is a third reading under which the sentence is a tautology — "for an agent with a single policy `π*`" both sides denote `U(π*[s ↦ a])` and "simplifies" is notational — and it is not mentioned. The point-mass reading *is* covered (`rhs_delta_junk`: under `δ_{π*}` the right side is `−1` at every `a ≠ π* s`, so the "simplified" rule cannot compare alternatives). One sentence in findings §6 naming the tautological reading and saying why the refutation targets the C&T reading would close this. Presentation.
