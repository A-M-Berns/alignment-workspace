## 2. Blocking issues

### B1 — `CommClone.naive_cloning_refuted` (T10(e), load-bearing 4): the refutation is of a weaker hypothesis than the one the ledger, F-9 and the report say it refutes

**The declaration and what it proves.** `naive_cloning_refuted : CommunicativeCloning Λ S X1 3 4 followed ∧ prior.PolicyFair Λ.toProcLayer ∧ modProb Λ X1 4 < modProb Λ X1 3 ∧ Λ.chosenEU X1 4 < Λ.chosenEU X1 3 ∧ …` — Communicative Cloning at the single action `a₁ = pill` (clone `say10`, event "both rooms press 10"), Policy Fairness, and a strict preference for the pill (`10 > 5`) over the minimal-modification action `say10`. True, kernel-clean, and the numbers are right (I recomputed the three-world model: `chosenEU pre pill = 10`, `chosenEU pre say10 = (0 + 10)/2 = 5`, `𝔼[U | say10 ∧ followed] = 10`, `modProb pill = 1`, `modProb say10 = 0`).

**What is wrong.** The source's assumption is universal. [udt-tiling-working-notes-2025-06-30](../../sources/references/bli/understanding-trust/udt-tiling-working-notes-2025-06-30.md) line 13, "Assumptions", verbatim: "Communicative Cloning: For every action `a₁ ∈ A_o`, there is a communicative clone `cc(A)` which achieves the minimum modification probability while achieving the same expected utility, provided communicated advice is followed: `m(o, cc(a₁)) = min_{a₂} m(o, a₂)`; `E(u | π*(o) = a₁) = E(u | π*(o) = cc(a₁) ∧ [π† ≡ μ†]_{−o})`." The inventory (bli-paper-075) renders it the same way ("for every `a₁ ∈ A_o` there is `cc(a₁)` with …"). The package's `CommunicativeCloning Λ S o a₁ cc followed` is the instance at one `(a₁, cc)`, and the refutation establishes it at the pill only. On the model the universal form **fails**: at `a₁ = say10` the only positive point of minimal modification probability is `say10` itself (`modProb pill = 1 > 0`), so `cc(say10) = say10` is forced, and CC there reads `chosenEU pre say10 = 𝔼[U | say10 ∧ followed]`, i.e. `5 = 10`. Probe `CloningUniversal.lean`: `not_cc_say10 : ¬ ∃ cc, CommunicativeCloning Λ S X1 4 cc followed` and `cc_universal_fails : ¬ ∀ a₁ ∈ S.Aof X1, ∃ cc, CommunicativeCloning Λ S X1 a₁ cc followed`, both proved from the package's own `numbers`. So the model does not inhabit 075's hypothesis; it is not a countermodel to "CC + PF ⟹ no strict preference for an action of non-minimal modification probability" as the notes state CC.

It is worse than a scope slip, because under the universal form the intended conclusion is nearly immediate. Probe theorem `cc_chain`: for any paper layer, structure, observation and event `f`, `CommunicativeCloning Λ S o a₁ cc f → CommunicativeCloning Λ S o cc cc f → Λ.chosenEU o a₁ = Λ.chosenEU o cc` — two rewrites (`𝔼[U | a₁] = 𝔼[U | cc ∧ F] = 𝔼[U | cc]`). CC at a minimal-modification action with itself as clone says "conditioning on the advice being followed does not change its value", which is "advice is followed, in value" in disguise — exactly what the Third Button denies (the rooms best-respond to "press 10" by pressing 20). `cc_at_say10_kills_refutation` makes it concrete on the model: `CommunicativeCloning Λ S X1 4 4 followed → Λ.chosenEU X1 3 = Λ.chosenEU X1 4`. So under the literal universal CC the Third Button is *excluded by the hypothesis* and 075's intended theorem holds (trivially, question-beggingly) on every model whose clone map stabilises; what the Third Button refutes is the proof *plan* — that from CC at the non-minimal `a₁` alone (step 2) plus fairness-style steps the "advice followed" conjunct could be dropped (steps 3–5). That is a sharper and different finding from the one the package records.

**Where the misstatement lives.** (i) Ledger row `modProb`, `CommunicativeCloning`, `CommClone.naive_cloning_refuted`: Claim "Communicative Cloning and Policy Fairness hold, yet UDT 1.0 strictly prefers the pill"; Status "**refuted** (the naive form: CC + PF alone …)"; Hyps "on the refutation model it is the natural event, both rooms pressing "10", so the refutation is of 075's hypothesis and not of a weaker one". (ii) F-9's title "Naive communicative cloning is refuted even under Policy Fairness · blocking for the naive form of 075's theorem (CC + PF alone)" and its sentence "so the refutation is of 075's hypothesis and not of a weaker one". (iii) The report's "State of the package" Refutations bullet: "bli-paper-075's naive theorem is false even under Policy Fairness"; and the ledger's "Rows recorded in the findings" line "F-9 refutes its naive form". None of these says that CC is established at the pill only, that the notes' CC is universal, or that the universal form fails on the model. The mandate's T10(e) itself prescribed the single-instance form ("075: for `a₁ = pill`, `cc(pill) = say10` …"), so the formalizer followed the mandate; but the "not of a weaker one" sentence was added in repair round 2 (against fidelity N-3) and is a claim about the source, and [STANDARDS](../../STANDARDS.md) §3 (names and docstrings don't oversell) and §5 (findings must describe the source fairly) govern it. The mandate's rendering of 075 should be recorded as a deviation from the source.

**Why blocking.** The audit rules name "a false claim in the findings file" and "a ledger row that misstates status"; this is both, on a load-bearing item (mandate §1 item 4: "the refutation of the naive communicative-cloning theorem"). A reader of the ledger or F-9 would believe 075's assumption, as the notes state it, has a Third-Button countermodel; it does not, and the honest status of 075 is the opposite in an important respect (its literal hypothesis assumes away the counterexample).

**Fix I would accept.** (a) Restate the claim everywhere as the single-instance form: `naive_cloning_refuted` refutes "CC at the action in question (with its clone) + PF ⟹ that action is not strictly preferred", which is the hypothesis the plan's step 2 actually uses; ledger Claim, Status (`refuted (the single-instance form …; the notes' universal CC fails on the model, not_cc_say10)`), the Hyps sentence, F-9's title and body, the report's Refutations bullet and the "not headlines" line. (b) Lift `not_cc_say10` and `cc_universal_fails` into `ThirdButtonPrior.lean` (N−, the universal form's failure on the Third Button) with provenance, and `cc_chain` into the library as the two-line theorem it is (Kind L; Fidelity: the notes' universal CC at a self-cloning minimal action). (c) Record the finding (F-9 rewritten or a new F-19): bli-paper-075's Communicative Cloning, read literally for every `a₁`, is a "listeners follow" assumption at the minimal-modification actions, under which the intended theorem is two rewrites and the Third Button is excluded; read as the plan uses it (at the non-minimal `a₁`), it does not suffice, and the Third Button shows steps 3–5 cannot be filled from it and Policy Fairness. Which reading the author intends is ATTRIBUTION-UNVETTED. (d) Note the mandate deviation in the report's list.
