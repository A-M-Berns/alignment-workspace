## 2. Blocking issues

### B1 — The `CausalStructure` docstring (`Structure.lean` 163–169) still claims the three hypotheses make the paper's limit exist and be unique; the package's own `Chain.limit_not_unique` refutes it

**The text** (`Structure.lean`, the docstring of `structure CausalStructure`):

> "**A causal structure** on a paper structure: a rank on observations under which every modification an available action issues targets a strictly later observation (`rank_lt`), no two modifications disagree on a point (`no_disagree`), forced actions are non-modifying (`mod_nonMod`) and well-typed (`mod_typed`). These are the three hypotheses under which the paper's "limit of repeated `elim`" exists and is unique (bli-paper-062's proposal; bli-slides-036's assumption turned into theorems); each of Paul's race conditions (bli-slides-009) violates one of them (`StructureWitness.lean`)."

**What is wrong.** The second sentence is the claim the round-2 adversarial audit blocked on (its B1) and the round-2 repair withdrew everywhere else: `Chain.limit_not_unique` (`StructureWitness.lean`, lifted from the auditors' probes) exhibits a *valid* `CausalStructure` (all four fields proved) with Limited Self-Modification and a well-typed `π = (m₁, m₂, a)` for which two complete, repeat-free eliminations end at different non-modifying policies (`(a,a,a)` in rank order, `(a,a,b)` in reverse). I traced both from `elim`'s definition: in rank order `m₁` fires first and overwrites `X2` with `a`, so `m₂` never fires; in reverse order `m₂` fires first and forces `b` at `X3`, which `m₁`'s later overwrite of `X2` cannot undo. So under exactly these hypotheses the paper's *unordered* limit does **not** exist uniquely; what exists is `effCausal`, the rank-ordered result, and `elimSeq_eq_effCausal` shows it is the same along every rank-sorted order. The `ElimOrder` module docstring, the `elimSeq_eq_effCausal` docstring, the root module, the ledger rows (`elimSeq_eq_effCausal`: Fidelity `variant: … the paper's unordered limit is not unique even under the causal structure`; `Chain.*`: "the three hypotheses do not make the paper's unordered limit unique"), F-1's correction paragraph and the `StructureWitness` module docstring all now say this. The docstring of the structure that *carries* the three hypotheses says the opposite, and it is the first thing a reader of `Structure.lean` meets. The third sentence ("each of Paul's race conditions violates one of them") is true of Paul's three but, read with the second, implies the hypotheses exclude every race condition; the `StructureWitness` docstring corrects this ("A fourth order-dependence … survives all three hypotheses"), this one does not.

**Why blocking.** [STANDARDS](../../STANDARDS.md) §3: "Names and docstrings don't oversell." audit.md §2 lists "names and docstrings that oversell" under the fidelity lens and "a headline … weaker than its name/docstring/ledger says" as blocking; the round-2 adversarial audit treated this exact claim as blocking when it stood in the theorem docstring and the findings. It is now machine-refuted inside the package that asserts it. A grep (`exists and is unique|exactly what the race|well-defined limit|order-independence of (iterated|repeated)|violates one of them`) over the Lean, the report, the ledger and the findings finds no other residual: the only other hits are the corrected passages and quotations of the paper.

**Fix I would accept.** Replace the second and third sentences with, e.g.: "Under these three hypotheses `effCausal` is well-defined (`effCausal_of_forced`: the forced action is unique), non-modifying, idempotent and type-preserving, and is reached by one pass of `elim` along any rank-sorted enumeration (`elimSeq_eq_effCausal`, `effCausal_reached`). They do **not** make the paper's unordered 'limit of repeated `elim`' unique: `Chain.limit_not_unique` (`StructureWitness.lean`) is a valid causal structure on which two complete eliminations differ, so the elimination order is a clause of the definition of `eff`, which `effCausal` supplies. Each of Paul's three race conditions (bli-slides-009) violates one of the hypotheses; the fourth (a chain) survives them." Also change the `Fidelity:` line of the same docstring from "the paper assumes the limit; here the order is explicit data" to say the order is part of the definition, if the repairer wants the two sentences to match the ledger's `variant: explicit order in place of the assumed limit` exactly (that line is already acceptable).
