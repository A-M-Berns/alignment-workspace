## Representation of record, and why

- **Observations and actions.** Observations are `udt-bli-core`'s tables `↥𝒟`; actions are one finite type `Act`. The paper's typing is data on `PaperStructure 𝒟 Act` (`Structure.lean`): `Aof o` (`𝒜_o`), `selfMod` (`𝒜^m`), `twin` (`â`, with `twin_nonMod`, `twin_id` and `twin_typed : a ∈ Aof o → twin a ∈ Aof o` — one direction only; the mandate's biconditional is false on its own suggested T5 witness, where `twin m = x` is available at `ō` while `m` is not, and the paper never states the converse, which follows from its disjoint action sets), and `mod : Act → ↥𝒟 → Option Act` — the paper's set `mod(a) ⊆ 𝒪 × 𝒜` of forced policy-points represented as a partial function (functional by representation; a non-modifying action forces nothing, `mod_nonMod_none`). `WellTyped`, `NonMod` are predicates; `Policy 𝒟 Act` is `udt-bli-core`'s total-function type (`variant`).
- **Chosen versus effective.** `PaperLayer P` (`Rules.lean`) is a chosen-policy coordinate `chosen : Ω → Policy` and a map `eff` with `pp = eff ∘ chosen`; `PaperLayer.toProcLayer` is the `udt-bli-core` `ProcLayer` whose procedures are policies, so the run's one fairness predicate `PolicyFair` applies unchanged. The paper's `E_p(u | π* = ⌜π⌝)` is `Λ.procEU π` (an `abbrev` for `Λ.toProcLayer.procEU π`), its `E_p(u | π*(o) = a)` is `Λ.chosenEU o a := 𝔼_μ[U | chosen · o = a]`. On the trivial layer (`chosen = pp`, `eff = id`) `chosenEU` is `EU` (`chosenEU_trivial`, `rfl`) and `toProcLayer` is `udt-bli-core`'s `trivialLayer` (`rfl`). Theorems 3 and 4 live on the trivial layer (`pp` is the chosen policy; every docstring of `Vingean.lean` says so).
- **`eff`: two objects, one of record.** `EffData S` (grade (c): the paper's assumption, `main.tex:97`, as four fields — exists, non-modifying, idempotent, type-preserving) and the construction of record `CausalStructure.effCausal` under a rank with modifications targeting strictly later observations, no disagreement, forced actions non-modifying and well-typed; its three properties are theorems (`effCausal_nonMod`, `effCausal_idem`, `effCausal_wellTyped`) and `CausalStructure.toEffData` packages them, so every theorem stated over an `EffData` carries those hypotheses at grade (a) when the data comes from a causal structure. Theorems 1 and 2 take the `eff` properties as explicit hypotheses (`hidem`, `hnm`, `hwt`), so one statement covers both objects. `fires` is defined by well-founded recursion on the rank; `effCausal` uses `Classical.choose` on `Forced` with a *unique* witness (`effCausal_of_forced` proves the choice is determined by `no_disagree`), so no junk value enters.
- **Argmax is a predicate.** `IsUDT10 S Λ πstar` is the fixed-point condition on a distinguished policy (each `πstar o` available and at least as good as every available action of positive point mass); `IsUDT11` is prior optimality among well-typed positive policies; on the trivial layer with every policy well-typed it is `udt-bli-core`'s `IsPriorOptimalOnSupport` (`isUDT11_trivial_iff`). The circularity of `π*` is resolved as the mandate says: `πstar` is the agent's actual choice, `chosen` the prior's uncertainty about it, tied together only by assumptions (KDP).
- **Maxima over action sets** are never `Finset.sup'`: "≤ the best available non-modifying action" is `∃ a ∈ Aof o, a ∉ selfMod ∧ 0 < ppMass o a ∧ value ≤ EU o a`; "max ≥ max" (Faith in Joint Argmax) is "every positive cell on the right is dominated by some positive cell on the left". Theorem 3's maximum ranges over `nonModAt o = 𝒜^{-m} ∩ 𝒜_o` (bli-paper-013(ii)'s fix).
- **Null-event lemmas** (`Rules.lean`): `condExp_congr_null` (a.s.-equal events have the same conditional expectation, with no positivity: numerator and denominator both agree), `condExp_and_eq_of_ae` / `massOf_and_eq_of_ae` (drop a probability-one conjunct), `massOf_eq_one_iff` (mass one ⟺ null complement). These are the only tools that move conditioning events; nothing divides. Junk `0` at null events is inherited from `udt-bli-core`; every headline names the positivity it needs.
- **The computation-output model** (`ArgmaxVars P`, grade (c), disclosed in every headline of `Vingean.lean`): `αM o'`, `αJ A' o o'`, `αC o' a' o` are coordinates of the prior standing for the outputs of the paper's self-referential argmax computations, constrained only by the assumptions. The extensional reading is the same predicates at constant variables (`Collapse.lean`), not separate definitions. ATTRIBUTION-UNVETTED that this is the authors' intent (bli-paper-017's own label).
- **Names.** Nothing here is called `PolicyFair`, `Fair`, `DecisionDetermination` or `TotalTrust`; the coordination predicates carry the paper's names. `udt-bli-tiling` imports `PolicyCoordination` and `thm2_udt10_tiling` / `thm2_no_strict_selfMod` for bli-paper-006/007 rather than redefining them.
