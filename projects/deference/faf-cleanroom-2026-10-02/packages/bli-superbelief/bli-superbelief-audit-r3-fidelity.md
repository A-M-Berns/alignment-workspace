### 3.3 Digits, bits, and the strategy-level identity are prose steps, not theorems in the package

What the Lean bounds is `(digitize (escExpand …)).length`, a digit count. The class meters `List Bool`: `bitsToDigits w` reads three bits per digit (`Criterion.lean:2061`, `(List.range (l.length / 3)).map (digitAt l)`), so the machine's output is three times the digit count — said in the docstrings ("three bits per digit") and interface note 1 ("bit-length"), but no statement in the package carries the factor, and FAF's `Criterion.lean` defines no `digitsToBits` to cite for the forward direction (I grepped). Likewise the step from `unRpn (escExpand (serializeTrades [(tentExpr …, ψ)])) = serializeTrades [...]` to "FAF's decoder reads it as the strategy" is FAF's `strategyOfTokens_unRpn_escExpand` (`RpnSentence.lean:1911`, unconditional: `strategyOfTokens n (unRpn (escExpand ts)) = strategyOfTokens n ts`), cited in three docstrings and never instantiated here. Both are one-liners; neither is a trust gap (the cited lemma is unconditional and the bit factor is definitional), and the mandate assigned the `FP` composition to `bli-assemble`. Fix I would accept, optional: a local `3 * (digitize …).length` corollary for the bit count, and `strategyOfTokens n (unRpn (escExpand (serializeTrades [(tentExpr 𝓜 m h Q, ψ)]))) = strategyOfTokens n (serializeTrades [(tentExpr 𝓜 m h Q, ψ)])` by `strategyOfTokens_unRpn_escExpand`, so the chain from the digit bound to the strategy identity is closed inside the package.

### 3.4 F-12's exact one-coordinate constant is derived in prose, not in Lean

F-12 says "the exact one-coordinate constant is `4d/(d+1) ∈ [2, 4)`, so `2` is right only at `d = 1`". I re-derived it: for `x, x'` on one side of `1/2` the law difference is `2(x'−x)·(δ₀ − U_d)` (or with `δ₁`), and `‖δ₀ − U_d‖₁ = (1 − 1/(d+1)) + d/(d+1) = 2d/(d+1)`, so the ratio is `4d/(d+1)`; across `1/2` it is at most that. The Lean has the upper bound `4` (`tent1_l1_le`, `tentLaw_l1_le`) and the `d = 2` instance (`tentLaw_l1_two_witness`: `4/3` at sup-distance `1/2`, ratio `8/3 = 4·2/3`, consistent). True, but F-12 should say "derived in prose; Lean has the bound `4` and the `d = 2` instance" so a reader does not take the exact constant as machine-checked.
