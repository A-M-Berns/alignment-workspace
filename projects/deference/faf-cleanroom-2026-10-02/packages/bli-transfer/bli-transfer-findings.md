## The disagreement between the attempts, resolved

**R-1 — T3 is false: the clamped market is never a logical inductor (blocking for `bli-program` §3.2(d) and §4 L5; corrects the mandate's T3 and the plan's "extension of record"; resolves the attempts' disagreement in attempt B's favour).**

*Claim under test.* `IsLogicalInductor Q DP → IsLogicalInductor (clamp Q) DP`, `clamp Q k ψ = max (min (Q k ψ) (1 − ε_k)) ε_k` on every sentence, `ε_k = 2^{-2^k}` (program §3.2(d); bli-paper-033/039, bli-slides-006/008, bli-soto-a-002 reading C; mandate T3).

*The two attempts.* Attempt A proved the accounting through the rewrite (`clampSpec`, `Strategy.clamp_value_sub_le`: day value gap `≤ magnitude · ε_n`; `Trader.clamp_netWorth_sub_le`) and left the headline conditional (`AttemptA.clamp_isLogicalInductor_of`) on the clamp certificate `hec`, bounded partial sums of `magnitude · ε` for every e.c. trader `hbound`, and the clamped table's computability `hcomp` — status `partial`, with the stall points recorded (the `ℚ`-code bound in `magnitude_le_of_ec`, the day-aware oracle, the rational `Primrec` kit). Attempt B refuted the claim: `sellBottom`, which on day `n` sells `2^{2^{n+1}}` shares of `⊥` (coefficient `n+1` nested `letE`-squarings of `const 2`, cost `O(n)`), is efficiently computable (`sellBottom_ec`, through FAF's `MachineTokenStream.concatVar`), and on any market pricing `⊥` at `≥ ε_n` it banks `≥ 2^{2^{n+1}} · 2^{-2^n} = 2^{2^n}` per day with no downside (`⊥` pays `0` in every world). The clamp forces exactly that floor, so `clamp Q` is exploited for **every** history `Q` over every process with consistent worlds.

*Resolution, in Lean.* `clamp_not_isLogicalInductor` (B's, over the same `clamp`: `Defs.attemptB_clamp_eq` is `rfl`) is the package's T3 row, status **refuted**, with N+ `clamp_lia_not_isLogicalInductor` (the LIA over `paperDP 𝗜𝚺₁` is an inductor, its clamp is not). The reconciler adds `clamp_bounded_partial_sums_false`: attempt A's `hbound` is false on every `Q` (the `⊥`-seller's day-`i` term is `2^{2^i} ≥ 1`, so the partial sums are unbounded), and `clamp_conditional_antecedent_false`: A's three hypotheses are jointly unsatisfiable over any process with consistent worlds, proved by composing the two attempts' theorems as they stand. So the attempts do not contradict each other — A's conditional is vacuous — and A's accounting lemmas locate exactly where the program's argument breaks: the rewrite leg is fine, the settlement residual `∑_n magnitude_n · ε_n` is not summable because e.c. magnitude is doubly exponential, not `2^{poly}` (R-2). Ledger: A's conditional is `flagged: antecedent unsatisfiable`, never dropped.

*Analysis (attempt B's F-B1, not formalized).* An *expressible* floor of polynomial cost is `≥ 2^{-2^{p(n)}}` for some polynomial `p`, and a trader with `p + 1` squarings beats it; so no clamp with an expressible rate rescues the variant. The Lean covers the definition of record and, more generally, any market with `P n ⊥ ≥ 2^{-2^n}` (`sellBottom_exploits`, `floor_not_isLogicalInductor`). Natural next question (not attempted by either attempt or the reconciler; not an OPEN row since no Lean statement is written): the family of `⊥`-sellers with `p(n)+1` squarings for a polynomial `p`, certified e.c. at a polynomial ruler, refuting every floor `≥ 2^{-2^{p(n)}}`.

*Surviving neighbour.* T1 with exact constraint 1 (`overlay_isLogicalInductor'`): an expressible re-pricing of large sentences that never touches `⊥` (small on every day) has no settlement residual at all. Non-dogmatism cannot be imposed by a floor on refutable sentences; the LI paper's non-dogmatism is about unrefutable ones. For `bli-assemble`: `clamp` and `E1c` remain definitions of record, but the "clamped B1 is non-dogmatic everywhere" plan is dead as an LI route — the clamp destroys the criterion.

**R-2 — "Trade magnitude `≤ 2^{p(n)}`" is false for FAF's traders (blocking for `bli-program` §4 L5's citation; mandate Known issue 7, refuted).** `not_magnitude_le_two_pow_poly` (B's): for every market and every polynomial `p`, `sellBottom`'s day-`n` magnitude `2^{2^{n+1}}` exceeds `2^{p(n)}` for some `n`. The program cited the bound as available; the mandate asked for it as the lemma `magnitude_le_of_ec`; attempt A's stall on it was a metering exercise that could not have succeeded. The true bound is doubly exponential in the day (`letE` sharing lets a feature of cost `c` with `b`-bit constants denote up to `2^{b · 2^c}`; without sharing the bound would be `M^{cost}`). The positive doubly-exponential bound is proved by neither attempt (a natural next question, no Lean statement written).

## Known issues 1–8 of the mandate, as met by both attempts

**KI-1 (bli-paper-031 footnote / bli-slides-002 — the condition is stated on the *number* of small sentences).** Confirmed by both attempts: nothing in the transfer theorem uses a count; what `overlay` and `ExprMap` use is *which* sentences are small (`SmallOn k ψ`, `bli-found`), and the theorem additionally needs expressibility (`ExprMap.fires`/`leaves`) with Tier B (`ExprMap.silent`) — an `ov` that sets un-fired large sentences to `1/2` is not an expression map and the theorem is false for it (a trader reading such a leaf is not rewritten). The sharp refutation L3 is `bli-leak`'s, not re-proved. Severity: local error in the source's stated condition; the surviving statement is T1.

**KI-2 (bli-slides-002's two readings).** Confirmed by both: "traders never read large prices" is exactly `RestrictedEC` and gives `restrictedEC_not_exploits_overlay`, a theorem about a proper subclass (`restrictedEC_separation`) for *every* re-pricing in `[0,1]` — it proves too little (a `(c)`) and too much (no expressibility needed), so it is not the criterion; "large = unread" is circular ([bli-found-findings](../bli-found/bli-found-findings.md) F-5). The theorem true under neither reading is T1. Severity: imprecision.

**KI-3 (bli-slides-030's "modify them however we like").** Confirmed false as a transfer by both, in the sense made precise by `ExprMap.silent`'s docstring; the surviving form is T1 ("modify the large prices by any efficiently expressible function of the day's or earlier days' small prices, with Tier B elsewhere"). Attribution of the corrected form to any person: ATTRIBUTION-UNVETTED (the transfer theorem as proved is this run's, program §7.14). Severity: local error in the source's phrasing.

**KI-4 (bli-paper-033/039, bli-slides-006/008, bli-soto-a-002(C) — the rounding rate).** Confirmed by both that no rate is stated; the rounded form (Route A, T4) has no theorem in either direction (attempt record below); and — this is where the attempts differed — the clamped form is **refuted** (R-1), not a theorem. **KI-5 (program §3.1 (iii): "`(k, ψ) ↦ expr k ψ` is in `FP`" without saying how `ψ` is presented).** Confirmed by both and made precise: the certificate of record (`SpliceOracle`, `SpliceCertificate`, `RunAgrees`) is stated on the **run** the machine holds — every spelling `parseRpn` accepts (canonical run, Gödel escape `[1, c]`, structured escape `[1, 0, …]`) — and the emitted body's leaves are canonical runs (`EF.rawSerialize`), never Gödel codes, because a small sentence's code can be doubly exponential in its `tokenSize`. ## T4 — Route A attempt record (merged; no OPEN row, plan §0.3)

**Claim under test** (bli-paper-033/034, bli-slides-006/008; program §3.2(c)): `Q` an LI, `P_k = D_k ∘ Q_k` on small sentences with `‖D_k − id‖_∞ ≤ 2^{-2^k}` and `D_k` a rounding to a finite grid — is `P` an LI?

**Proof attempt (both attempts, same stall).** The only route is the bounded-difference accounting through a rewrite that reads the perturbed price exactly, and a rounding is not expressible (discontinuous; `EF.denote` is continuous, FAF's `EF.continuous_denote`), so no `SpliceSpec` exists for it. Without a rewrite, a day-`n` leaf reading `price φ 0` sees a fixed error (up to `1/2`) multiplied by an e.c. coefficient of magnitude up to `2^{2^{poly(n)}}` (R-2 sharpens the mandate's `2^{n²}`) — unbounded for every rate; the finite-perturbation device (`EfficientPrefixPatch`, freezing days `< K`) only moves the problem to day `K`. Stall point: no per-trader constant exists for the bounded-difference route, and no other route to `noExploit` is available in FAF or this package.

**Refutation attempt (attempt B succeeds for one half).** The `⊥`-seller refutes any rounding that maps a positive price of a refuted sentence **up** to a grid point `≥ 2^{-2^n}`: `floor_not_isLogicalInductor` applies verbatim to any market with `P n ⊥ ≥ 2^{-2^n}` for all `n`. So a rounding with a *ceiling* on refuted sentences is dead whenever `Q` prices them positively. A rounding that rounds *down* (floor to the grid, `0` stays `0`) escapes this attack; attempt A's candidate (the LIA at `paperDP 𝗜𝚺₁`, the grid `2^{-2^k} ℤ ∩ [0,1]`, the amplifier trader `mul (const 2^{n²}) (price φ 0)`) does not obviously profit — its amplified position is on a sentence the rounded market also prices sensibly — and a persistent-sign artefact would need a trader of magnitude `2^{2^k}` on day `k` reading a midpoint sentence, whose coefficient is expressible (R-2!) but whose *sentence selection* is not obviously e.c. Stall point: no candidate with a persistent sign under floor rounding.

**Positive partials.** Rounding restricted to days `≥ K` with all day-`< K` leaves frozen is T1 plus FAF's finite-support freeze (`FreezeOracle.lic_iff_of_finiteSupport`) when the moved cells are finitely many — not assembled (a rounding moves infinitely many cells). Rounding that is itself expressible: none of the source's roundings is.

Severity for bli-paper-033/039's stated form: blocking (the rate is undischarged in both directions, and the clamped reading is false); surviving neighbour: T1 with exact constraint 1.
