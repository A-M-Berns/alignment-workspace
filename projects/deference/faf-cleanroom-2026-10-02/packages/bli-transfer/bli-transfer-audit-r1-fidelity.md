## 3. Non-blocking issues

**NB-1 (findings, scope of the T3 refutation; R-1, R-2, KI-4).** `clamp_not_isLogicalInductor` and `not_magnitude_le_two_pow_poly` are correct over FAF's `IsLogicalInductor`, and the findings name the mechanism (`letE` sharing) — but they never draw the consequence for the *sources*. FAF's `EF` docstring (`Framework/Criterion.lean:86–98`) says the `var`/`letE` constructors are "a **disclosed extension** of the paper's feature grammar … the extension enlarges `EfficientlyComputable`", licensed by the LI paper's footnote reading expressible features as "a generalization of arithmetic circuits", and that "a literal *tree* reading of tex:786-788's grammar would not be" polynomial for the same features. Under that literal tree reading a day-`n` feature has polynomially many nodes with constants of polynomially many bits, so its magnitude *is* `≤ 2^{p(n)}` (the program's §3.2(c) reasoning, "constants are rationals of polynomially many bits, `mul` nests polynomially"), the settlement residual `∑ 2^{p(n)} 2^{-2^n}` is summable, and the clamp is neither proved nor refuted. So the honest scope is: *T3 is refuted over FAF's e.c. class (sharing allowed, per FAF's paper-licensed reading); the program's L5 route is closed because it was argued over FAF; whether the clamped market satisfies the paper's criterion under a sharing-free grammar is open, and whether the paper's authors intend sharing is ATTRIBUTION-UNVETTED.* R-2's "without sharing the bound would be `M^{cost}`" is the fact; KI-4's "the clamped reading of 033 is false" and R-1's headline should carry the scope in the same sentence (research/CLAUDE.md rule 1). Fix I'd accept: one scoping sentence in R-1, R-2 and KI-4 citing the FAF docstring, and the same clause in the `clamp_not_isLogicalInductor` docstring's "Scope" line.
