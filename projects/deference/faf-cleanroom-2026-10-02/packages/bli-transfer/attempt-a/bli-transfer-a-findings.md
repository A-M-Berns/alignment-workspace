## Known issues 1–8 of the mandate, as met

**KI-1 (bli-paper-031 footnote / bli-slides-002 — the condition is stated on the *number* of small sentences).** Confirmed by formalization in the following sense: nothing in the transfer theorem uses a count of small sentences; what `Defs.overlay` and `ExprMap` use is *which* sentences are small (`SmallOn k ψ`, size `≤ 2^{2^k}`), and the theorem additionally needs expressibility (`ExprMap.fires`/`leaves`) — a re-pricing that is not an efficiently expressible function of the day's small prices is not covered (the sharp refutation is `bli-leak`'s L3, not re-proved here). Severity: local error in the source's stated condition; the surviving statement is T1.

**KI-2 (bli-slides-002's two readings).** The first reading, "traders never read large prices", is exactly `RestrictedEC` and gives `restrictedEC_not_exploits_overlay`, a theorem about a proper subclass (`restrictedEC_separation`: an e.c. trader may read the day-`0` price of a sentence large on day `0`), hence a `(c)` and not the criterion. The second reading is circular ([bli-found-findings](../../bli-found/bli-found-findings.md) F-5; not re-examined). The theorem true under the criterion's own quantifier is T1. Severity: imprecision.

**KI-3 (bli-slides-030's "modify them however we like").** Confirmed false as a transfer in the sense made precise by `ExprMap.silent`'s docstring: the theorem is proved for re-pricings that are efficiently expressible functions of the day's small prices (with un-fired large sentences keeping `Q`'s value); the proof visibly uses `fires` at every fired leaf and `silent` at every un-fired one (`EF.spliceOn_denoteWith`), and a leaf reading an un-fired large sentence whose value was changed is *not* rewritten, so nothing bounds its trader. Attribution of the corrected form to any person: ATTRIBUTION-UNVETTED (the transfer theorem as proved here is this run's, per program §7.14). Severity: local error in the source's phrasing.

**KI-4 (bli-paper-033/039, bli-slides-006/008, bli-soto-a-002(C) — the rounding rate).** Confirmed: no rate is stated in the sources, and the formalization gives the rounded form no theorem (T4's attempt record below records where both attempts stall). The clamped form is T3 (pending in this attempt). Severity for bli-paper-039 as printed: blocking (its argument is 031 + 033 and 033's rate is undischarged); surviving neighbour: T1 with exact constraint 1 (`overlay_isLogicalInductor'`).

**KI-5 (program §3.1 (iii): "`(k, ψ) ↦ expr k ψ` is in `FP`" without saying how `ψ` is presented).** Confirmed as an imprecision, and made precise: the certificate of record (`Certificate.SpliceOracle`, `Contraction.RunAgrees`) is stated on the **run** the machine holds — every spelling `parseRpn` accepts, canonical, Gödel-escaped or structured — and the emitted body's leaves are canonical runs (`EF.rawSerialize`), never Gödel codes, because a small sentence's code can be doubly exponential in its `tokenSize`. Severity: local; the theorem stands under the corrected hypothesis.

**KI-6 (program §3.1's guard `¬ SmallOn k ψ` on the map).** Confirmed: the guard would make the certificate decide smallness from a raw spelling in polynomial time, which the theorem does not need. The definition of record drops it (`ExprMap` fires wherever the value is reproduced) and the guarded form is the corollary `ExprMap.ofLargeOnly`. Severity: imprecision.

**KI-7 (program §4 L5 cites "trade magnitude `≤ 2^{p(n)}`" as available).** Not a citation anywhere in FAF; it is a lemma of this package (`magnitude_le_of_ec`, T3, pending). Severity: presentation.

**KI-8 (de Bruijn shifting under the administrative `letE`).** Confirmed and discharged exactly as the mandate predicts: `EF.Closed` on bodies (`ExprMap.closed`) and `EF.denoteWith_congr_env` (agreement below `freeBound` suffices) make the shift harmless; the more general `SpliceSpec` allows a body to read `var 0` — the bound price — which is what T3's `clampE k` does. The dead leaf is kept (`EF.spliceOn`), as FAF's freeze does; it is what makes the rank law `EF.spliceOn_rank` an equality and the flat rewrite parser-transparent.

## New findings

**F-A1 (imprecision; `bli-program` §3.1, `ExprMap` field `ov_range`).** The program's hypothesis list does not say that the re-pricing stays in `[0,1]`. It does not follow from (i)–(ii): a body may denote any real, and `expr` may be `none` on a small sentence (where `overlay` ignores `ov`). Without it the overlay is not a `ComputableMarket` (whose first conjunct is the range) and the magnitude bound on the pre-settlement days fails. Added as the field `ExprMap.ov_range`. Pointer: `Defs.lean`, `Transfer.overlay_range`.

**F-A2 (presentation; the mandate's `RunOracle`-shaped certificate).** FAF's `RunOracle.R_spec` quantifies over *every* buffered word; the splice pass only ever calls the oracle on a block-well-formed buffer (the fold invariant `bufWF_condStepR`), so `SpliceOracle.R_spec` could be weakened to `BlockWF bufW` with no change to the theorem. Attempt A keeps the universal form for parity with FAF unless the N+ instance needs the weaker one (then it is changed and this entry updated). Pointer: `Certificate.lean`.

**F-A3 (presentation; mandate § Definitions of record, `clampE`).** The mandate describes the clamp body as "`k` nested `letE`-squarings" with "the bound price `var j` inside `j` nested `letE`s". One outer `letE` binding `ε_k` (itself `k` nested squarings, closed) is simpler: inside it the price is `var 1` and `ε_k` is `var 0`, and `clampE_freeBound = 1` states exactly what is free. No content difference. Pointer: `Defs.clampE`.

**F-A4 (imprecision; the mandate's `RestrictedEC` witness).** "Day-`n` trade `(price (atom n) n, atom n)` for `n ≥ 1`; use `⊥` on day `0`" cannot be realized as one `ofTradeBlocksBig` family with `⊥` on day `0` and atoms after without a sentence-family combinator FAF lacks; trading *nothing* on day `0` (count `0`) is what the machine constructor supports and is equally in the class. Pointer: `Witnesses.restrictedWitness`.

**F-A5 (the mandate's T1.5 oracle shape; presentation).** The mandate describes the N+ oracle as "tag test + length comparison on an atom's payload run". A map that fires on day `0` only needs no length comparison — every atom is large on day `0` (`Size.three_le_tokenSize_atom` against `sizeBound 0 = 2`) — so the attempt's oracle is the family test alone, under both atom spellings; the day-`k ≥ 1` extension (a capped double-exponential comparison of the run's length with `2^{2^k}`, in unary, from FAF's `uMul`/`leFlag` kit) is what `bli-assemble`'s oracle adds. Recorded so the reconciler does not read the day-`0` restriction as an accident. Pointer: `WitnessLia.lean` header.

**F-A6 (Foundation's decoder is not tight; imprecision in the mandate's "computable smallness test on codes").** `Formula.ofNat c = some φ` does not determine `c = encode φ` (`⊥` has infinitely many codes, as FAF's `Construction/Freeze/Oracle.lean` header notes). Nothing in T1.4 or T1.5 needs tightness: the smallness test and the witness map are read off the code's constructor tag by cases (`Computable.tsCode`, `WitnessLia.wExpr_decode`). Pointer: `WitnessLia.wExpr_decode`.

## T3 — what resisted (KI-7's lemma and the clamp certificate)

T3 lands as the conditional theorem `Clamp.clamp_isLogicalInductor_of` (kind C, `partial`), with the accounting through the rewrite fully proved (`clampSpec`, `Strategy.clamp_value_sub_le`, `Trader.clamp_netWorth_sub_le`). Three hypotheses are named and not discharged in this attempt:

1. **`magnitude_le_of_ec` (KI-7).** Plan: `Cobham.output_length_poly_of_mem_FP` bounds the machine's word by `p(n)`; the decoded coefficients' constants are rationals decoded from tokens `c < 4^{|ds|}`; a structural bound `|e.denote V| ≤ (B + 2)^{cost e}` for `V` in `[0,1]` with `B` a bound on the constants' magnitudes; `cost ≤` token count. The step that stalled is the bound `|q| ≤ f(c)` for `Encodable.decode (α := ℚ) c = some q` under the `Encodable ℚ` instance `EF.streamStep` uses — it requires unfolding Mathlib's `Denumerable ℚ` (through `ℤ × ℕ+` and `Nat.unpair`), which was not done. Nothing here is evidence against the lemma; it is a metering exercise of the bridge lemma's kind.
2. **The clamp certificate.** `Certificate.lean`'s pass is reusable as-is (the parameter block `pair (F x) x` carries the unary day), but the emitted body `rawSerialize (clampE k)` has length `Θ(k)` and `k` is a day *token*, so a word-level emitter must emit at `min k n` and the token model must be shown to agree with the true one on rank-valid streams (a leaf with `k > n` fails `strategyOfTokens`' rank validation in both models). The word-level repetition of the `letE`-squaring block `min k n` times is `FP` (`Cobham.iterate_mem_FP`), but the congruence lemma and the clamped `SpliceOracle` were not written.
3. **`ComputableMarket (clamp Q)`**: rational `max`/`min`/`pow` on `Q`'s table are primitive recursive in FAF (`Construction/Primcodable.lean`: `ratMax_prim`, `ratMin_prim`, `ratPow_prim`), not imported here.

Severity: none against the sources — T3's statement is the program's own corrected form; the gap is in this attempt's coverage.

## T4 — Route A attempt record

**Claim under test** (bli-paper-033/034, bli-slides-006/008; program §3.2(c)): `Q` an LI, `P_k = D_k ∘ Q_k` on small sentences with `‖D_k − id‖_∞ ≤ 2^{-2^k}` and `D_k` a rounding to a finite grid — is `P` an LI? Ruled by plan §0.3 to end as an attempt, not an OPEN row.

**Proof attempt.** The only route the package has is the bounded-difference accounting (`Exploits.of_boundedDifference`) through a rewrite that reads the perturbed price exactly. A rounding is not expressible in `EF` (discontinuous; `EF.denote` is continuous, FAF's `EF.continuous_denote`), so no `SpliceSpec` exists for it: `fires` would need a body denoting `D_k (Q k ψ)` from `Q k ψ`, impossible for a step function. Without a rewrite, the day-`n` gap of a trader whose day-`n` coefficient reads `price φ 0` is `|e.denote P − e.denote Q|`, and `e` may be `mul (const 2^{n²}) (price φ 0)` (an e.c. trader: the constant's token has `n²` bits, polynomial), giving a gap `2^{n²} · |D_0 − id|` — unbounded in `n` for any rate on `D_k` unless `D_0 = id`. The finite-perturbation device (`EfficientPrefixPatch`, freezing days `< K`) moves the problem to day `K`. **Stall point**: no per-trader constant exists for the bounded-difference route, and no other route to `noExploit` is available in FAF or this package.

**Refutation attempt.** Candidate: `Q := liaHistory (paperDP 𝗜𝚺₁)`, `D_k` rounding to the grid `2^{-2^k} ℤ ∩ [0,1]`, and the amplifier trader `Tr_n = (mul (const 2^{n²}) (price φ 0), φ)`. Its position on day `n` is `2^{n²} · P_0(φ)` shares of `φ` bought at `P_n(φ)`; the amplified *coefficient* is a price `P` also rounds sensibly, so the trader's profit is governed by `P_n(φ)`'s own drift, not by the rounding artefact at day `0` — no persistent sign. A refutation needs a rounding artefact with a persistent sign on a sentence the trader can settle (e.g. a sentence whose `Q`-price sits at a grid midpoint infinitely often, so the rounding is one-sided by `2^{-2^k}` each day, and a trader of magnitude `2^{2^k}`), but such a trader is not efficiently computable (its coefficient has `2^k` bits on day `k`). **Stall point**: the artefact is too small for an e.c. trader to amplify, and the amplifier's gain is not persistently signed.

**Honest partial that is cheap and proved elsewhere**: rounding restricted to days `≥ K` with all leaves of days `< K` frozen is T1 plus FAF's finite-support freeze (`FreezeOracle.lic_iff_of_finiteSupport`) when the moved cells are finitely many — not formalized here, as the moved cells of a rounding are not finitely many. Severity for bli-paper-033/039's stated form: blocking (the rate is undischarged in both directions); the clamped variant T3 is the surviving neighbour (rule 3's surviving-neighbour clause).
