### The disagreement

T3 is the one place the attempts differ, and it is a real finding: the mandate's T3 (program §3.2(d), "the clamped re-pricing is a logical inductor", with the plan calling it the package's extension of record) is **false**, and so is the program's §4 L5 citation "trade magnitude `≤ 2^{p(n)}`" that its proof route needed. Attempt A followed the mandate's route faithfully — rewrite reading the clamp exactly, residual `≤ magnitude · ε_n`, summability from the magnitude bound — proved every step it could, and stalled honestly on `magnitude_le_of_ec` (at a `ℚ`-code bound it could not have closed, since the lemma is false). Attempt B noticed that FAF's `letE` sharing lets a feature of cost `O(n)` denote `2^{2^{n+1}}`, built the `⊥`-seller, and refuted both. The reconciler's `clamp_bounded_partial_sums_false` shows A's `hbound` fails on every `Q`, and `clamp_conditional_antecedent_false` composes A's conditional with B's refutation to show the antecedent is unsatisfiable; so the attempts are consistent, A's conditional is vacuous, and A's accounting lemmas (`clampSpec`, `Strategy.clamp_value_sub_le`, `Trader.clamp_netWorth_sub_le`) remain correct and useful — they say exactly which leg of the program's argument breaks (the settlement residual is not summable). Full record: [bli-transfer-findings](bli-transfer-findings.md) R-1, R-2.
