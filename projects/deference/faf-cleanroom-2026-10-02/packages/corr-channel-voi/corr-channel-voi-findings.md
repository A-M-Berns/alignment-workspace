## The mandate's known issues, confirmed or sharpened

**F-1 (presentation; mandate Known issue 1).** The plan's parenthetical for 2-086 ("`joint`'s open problem 1 as a finite tower-property theorem") mismatches the inventory: 2-086 is the confidence ball. **F-2 (local imprecision, sharpened to a local error; Known issue 2).** `substitution.md` R7 item 29 says a compromised self-check "reports `R` with probability `ρ` regardless of `W`"; the `[checked]` closed form comes from `scan_test.py` l. 97–100, whose model reports `R` *always* when the world is right. Both readings are defined (`selfCheckI`, `selfCheckII`) and both closed forms proved (`selfCheckI_voiGiven_closed`, `selfCheckII_voiGiven_closed`; reading (II) carries the factor `(1 − e + eρ)` on the `αc` term). **Sharpening:** under the prose's reading the sentence "after 'scan says `Wr`' the agent shuts down" (item 30) is *false in general*: a compromised check says `Wr` in the right world with mass `(1 − ε)e(1 − ρ)`, and at `(ε, e, ρ, c, h) = (1/100, 1/2, 1/2, 1, 1)` the `Wr` signal's gain is `6/25 > 0` — the agent continues after its own check says it is wrong (`w_readingII_continues_on_Wr`). In the script's reading the sentence holds unconditionally (`selfCheckI_stops_on_Wr`). So the prose and the script differ not only in the closed form's factor but in a qualitative claim. **Repair round 2:** the two readings also differ in *value*, and the difference is a theorem: wherever both closed forms hold, reading (II) values the button at least as much as reading (I) (`selfCheckI_le_selfCheckII`), and in reading (I)'s positive part by exactly `(1−ε)·e(1−ρ)·αc` (`selfCheck_readings_gap`) — zero iff `α = 0`, `e = 0`, `ρ = 1`, `ε = 1` or `c = 0`, the complete list of where the prose's and the script's values agree; at `(ε, e, ρ, α, β, c, h) = (1/5, 1/2, 1/2, 1/10, 1/2, 1, 5)` the values are `9/200` and `13/200` (`w_readings_differ`, `w_readings_gap`). Severity: local error in the prose (the script's model is the one whose claims are true). Pointer: `substitution.md` l. 98–100.

**F-3 (imprecision; Known issue 3; severity lowered from "local error" in repair round 1).** `miri.md` I10.6's "occurs iff `β < α`" is an implication plus an existence: `Δ₋ < 0 ∧ Δ₊ < 0 → β < α` (`beta_lt_alpha_of_inverted`, with `c, h ≥ 0` only) and for every `β < α` some `(ε, c, h)` realises the inverted case (`inverted_exists`: `ε = 1/2`, `c = h = 1`). The converse "`β < α` ⟹ inverted at the given `(ε, c, h)`" is false: with `β < α`, *small* `ε` puts `Δ₊ > 0` (at `(α, β, c, h) = (9/10, 1/10, 1, 1)`, `ε = 1/20` gives `Δ₊ = 1/20`) and *large* `ε` puts `Δ₋ > 0` — the press is heeded — with `Δ₊ = −17/20 < 0` there (`ε = 19/20`); the round-1 text had the two cases swapped (audit r1 adversarial N4). Read as "the inverted case *can* occur iff `β < α`" (the iff over the existence of `(ε, c, h)`), the source's sentence is exactly what is proved; the imprecision is that the quantifier over `(ε, c, h)` is silent. Pointer: `positive/miri.md` l. 222.

**F-4 (ill-posed, fixed by a reading; Known issue 4).** "Accuracy-only `L`" is undefined wherever used (2-078 (3), `substitution.md` R9 item 37, `byrnes-herd.md` l. 203–207, `legitimacy.md` R3 item 1). D5 fixes the reading *Blackwell-monotone predicate on experiments at a fixed prior* (`AccuracyOnly`; ATTRIBUTION-UNVETTED). Under it, the sources' two examples are of this kind because they hold of *every* experiment (`reflects_all`, `expBrier_le_priorBrier`), and T4(d) is a theorem (`AccuracyOnly.cannot_separate`, `brainReader_separation`). Those two examples are constant-true, so they exercise neither clause of the theorem; the non-constant instance the theorem is checked on is the value threshold `t ≤ 𝒱(k)` (`thresholdL`, Blackwell-monotone by T8(a), rejecting `trivialExp` and admitting the button at the package's cell — repair round 1, both audits' B1). **The reading matters, and the two candidate readings split the theorem exactly along the mandate's two sentences** (repair round 1, from audit r1 adversarial N6). The sources' words — "a property of the transition's Bayesian structure", "determined by the `ω`-algebra and the belief trajectory alone" — read at least as naturally as *invariance under Blackwell equivalence* (`AccuracyInvariant`, D5′, implied by D5) as they do as upward-closure. Under D5′, clause (ii) survives unchanged — `L ⟨k₁, perfect⟩ ↔ L ⟨k₂, perfect⟩`, read-only and disabling scans still indistinguishable (`AccuracyInvariant.prod_perfect_iff`) — and clause (i) **fails**: the exact-value criterion "worth exactly `v`" (`valueIs`) is invariant, admits the button at `v = 𝒱(button)`, and rejects `⟨button, perfect⟩` whenever the button is worth less than perfect information (`valueIs_admits_rejects_scan`; cell `w_invariant_rejects_scan`, `71/100` vs `9/10`). So "no accuracy-only `L` separates reading from disabling" holds under both readings; "admitting the button admits the read-only scan" needs the monotone one. A predicate that is not even invariant (say, one that inspects the signal alphabet's size) could separate read-only from disabling scans; whether such a predicate deserves the name "accuracy-only" is what the sources leave unsaid. ATTRIBUTION-UNVETTED throughout. Pointer: `legitimacy.md` l. 65; `byrnes-herd.md` l. 203.

**F-5 (local imprecision; Known issue 5; title corrected in repair round 1).** The "iff" that overclaims is C10's reading of C9 ("above it, the agent … works against it") and the inventory's corr-wf13-018 — C9's own derivation is exact ("necessary … and when full suppression is cheap … also sufficient"), i.e. a necessary condition plus a limit (audit r1 fidelity N9). Proved: deception pays iff `qm + δ < E[X 1_Pr; a⁰]` (`deception_pays_iff`); hence deception paying implies the below-threshold inequality fails (`pos_of_deception_pays`), and the inequality gives honesty for *all* `q, δ ≥ 0` (`honest_dominates_of_nonpos`); the converse holds for the family (`honest_dominates_forall_iff`) and not at fixed `(q, δ)`: `E[X 1_Pr] = 2/25 > 0` yet honesty dominates at `q = δ = 1/2` (`w_holtman_fixed_fails`). The inventory's "non-interference iff the thesis's inequality" (corr-wf13-018) should read "iff, over all suppression costs". Pointer: `critique/holtman.md` l. 89–99.

**F-6 (ill-posed, finite content stated; Known issue 6).** 2-072's "no assumption on `P` or `V` makes `F` monotone" quantifies over an undefined class of assumptions. The finite content proved: for every prior, stakes, concealment family along a garbling chain and cost with `τ > VOI(button₀)`, `F` is not antitone (`concealF_not_antitone`), because `F 1 ≥ F 0 ↔ τ ≥ VOI(button₀)` (`concealF_one_ge_zero_iff`) under the one structural fact that the fully concealed sensor is constant. Whether this is "no assumption" depends on what assumptions are allowed; the universality over `(ε, c, h, α, β, τ)` is what is established (ATTRIBUTION-UNVETTED as a reading of Christiano's conjecture). Also: the "delegitimizing to degree `κ`" clause of T1 adds nothing to `F` in this model — `F` depends on `κ` only through `(α κ, β κ)` and `τ(κ)`. Scope (audit r2 fidelity N7): that is a fact about the *correct Bayesian's* `F`, whose second act uses the true posterior under `a₁^κ`; for an imposed-trust agent (the package's `imposedValue`, T4(e)) the clause would enter, and that `F` is not modelled here. Repair round 2 adds the strict endpoint form `F 0 < F 1 ↔ VOI(button₀) < τ` (`concealF_zero_lt_one_iff`, from the identity `F 1 − F 0 = τ − VOI(button₀)`), of which `concealF_not_antitone` is the corollary. Pointer: `critique/christiano.md` l. 151.

**F-7 (imprecision; Known issue 7).** 040's general form `εh ≤ q·d` is `[conjectured]` in the source and is a *sufficient* condition only: `never_scans_of_le` proves it for every channel `k'` (via T3), and the exact condition for the perfect scan is `q·d < min(εh, (1−ε)c) − VOI(button)` (`scans_perfect_iff`), strictly weaker than `εh ≤ q·d` whenever `VOI(button) > 0`. ## The package's own findings

**F-10 (local error in the source's universal claim).** `substitution.md` R5 item 25: "with `α_N = 0` (the humans never object without reason) it asks and obeys at every `q > 0` [checked]". The "obeys" half is a theorem (`askModel_alphaN_zero_heeds`: D1 at the ask model for every `q > 0`, `β_N > 0`, `VOI < d`). The "asks" half is **false as stated**: with `α_N = 0` the ask has positive value iff silence would make the agent scan, `q(1−β_N)(d − VOI) < (1 − q)·VOI` (`askModel_alphaN_zero_asks_iff`, regime-free via Good's equality clause); at `q = 99/100`, `β_N = 9/10`, `VOI = 3/2`, `d = 5` the agent heeds but `VOI(ask) = 0` (`w_ask_alphaN_zero_fails`) — no signal could make it scan, so asking is worthless. The script's grid stops at `q = 1/2` (`scan_test.py` l. 121), where the claim holds (`w_ask_alphaN_zero_half`: `VOI(ask) = 23/40`). The claim is true in full when `β_N = 1` (`askModel_perfect_objection_asks`). **Scope (repair round 1, audits' N3/N5):** R5 item 24 sets the paragraph up on the *scan region* `q·d < VOI` ("then it scans"), and there the sentence is true for every `q > 0` as a corollary of the exact criterion (`askModel_alphaN_zero_asks_of_scan_region`; cell `w_ask_alphaN_zero_scan_region` at `q = 1/10`). The refutation lives only where the agent would not scan anyway (`q·d ≥ VOI`); at `q = 1/2` the agent is outside the scan region (`q·d = 5/2 > 3/2`) yet still asks, so the region is sufficient, not necessary. Severity: local error in the sentence as written (its universal quantifier ranges over a region where it fails); a missing scope qualifier, not an error in the mechanism — asking is available exactly when the overseers' verdict could flip the decision, which is what the exact criterion says. Pointer: `substitution.md` l. 83–85.

**F-11 (shape of a mandate target; not a source error).** The mandate's T2 asks for `voiGiven = εh` "under `Δ₋ ≤ 0 ∧ E[X] ≥ 0`". That statement is false without `Δ₊ ≥ 0`: the inverted sensor (`Δ₋ ≤ 0 ∧ Δ₊ < 0`) has `VOI(button) = −Δ₊ > 0` and hence `VOI(scan | button) = min(εh, (1−ε)c) − (−Δ₊) < εh`. Proved as `twoState_voiGiven_discounted` under `Δ₋ ≤ 0 ∧ Δ₊ ≥ 0` (`E[X] ≥ 0` then follows and is not needed), and under the source's standing `α ≤ β` with `E[X] ≥ 0` (`twoState_voiGiven_discounted_of_le`). The source itself (R2 item 6) assumes `β > α` throughout, so the source is right and the mandate's hypothesis list was one clause short. Pointer: `corr-channel-voi-mandate` T2.

**F-12 (imprecision in the mandate's E1 phrasing).** "No imperfect binary sensor attains" the bound is true, but the class of binary sensors that attain it is `{(0, 1), (1, 0)}`: the perfectly *inverted* button attains `𝒱 = (1−ε)c` too (`twoButton_attains_iff`), since Blackwell value is label-blind. Harmless for the source (which assumes `β > α`), worth saying because it is the same label-blindness that makes I10.6's inverted sensor "look corrigible on four desiderata of five". Pointer: `corr-channel-voi-mandate` E1.

**F-13 (a generalisation the mandate's S4 invites that is false).** T4(b) — after the scan, `VOI(button | scan) = 0` — holds for the *perfect* scan (`voiGiven_after_perfect`) and fails for a merely Blackwell-dominating `k'`: a second, conditionally independent reading of the same imperfect button is worth `9/1000 > 0` at `(1/10, 1/10, 9/10, 1, 10)` (`w_second_button_worth`). So S4's "(a)–(d) with `ofMap id` replaced by any `k'` with `BlackwellLE button k'`" goes through for (a) and (d) and not for (b): the source's `σ(ω) ⊆ σ(y)` (miri I10.8) is exactly the perfect case, and "the agent preserves the button only while it's the cheapest channel" (corr-core-025) should be read as "only while it is the cheapest channel *not already dominated in the sense of conditional independence*" — an imperfect scan leaves the button with residual value. Pointer: `positive/miri.md` l. 226; `corr-channel-voi-mandate` S4.

**F-14 (presentation; anthropic-davis §2.2).** The "theorem to prove or refute" at items 6–7 — on `B_δ` the thesis-agent continues whatever the press — is the contrapositive of the cellwise compliance condition: a cell where the press expectation is positive is a cell where continuing is press-optimal (`cell_fails_of_pos`). Its content is the *reachability* of such a cell by the agent's information (a hypothesis about the scan, `k`), not an impossibility about the prior; with a perfect scan the confident cell is always reachable unless `α = 0` (`pointwiseTrust_perfect_iff`). Pointer: `critique/anthropic-davis.md` l. 189 ("One theorem (CONST, formal)"); the set-up is items 6–7 at l. 73–77.

**F-15 (attribution, ATTRIBUTION-UNVETTED).** 2-078's clause (3) ("any `L` separating read-only from disabling is a method list / a default policy / an idealised human") is a classification, not a theorem; D5 makes half of it one: no *accuracy-only* `L` separates them (`AccuracyOnly.cannot_separate`), and the influence defect — a reference-point criterion, the "default policy" of the clause — does (`brainReader_separation`). The other two members of the list are not modelled. Pointer: `corr-wf13-2-inventory` 2-078.

**F-16 (scope of a source theorem, confirmed exact).** Wentworth §2.4's closed form `VOI = (εβh − (1−ε)αc)⁺` holds under the continue-by-default regime in product form and fails off it (`w_regime_form_fails`: `321/400 ≠ 341/400` at `h = 20`); the regime-free statement is the three-max form `twoState_voiSensor_button`. This confirms `corr-three-step`'s finding rather than adding to it; recorded because T1's trap names it.
