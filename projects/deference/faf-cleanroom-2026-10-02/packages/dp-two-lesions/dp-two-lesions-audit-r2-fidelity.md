## 3. Non-blocking issues

**N1 — F3's answer to the general-`γ` factor question is misleading; the wiki's guess is exactly right** (findings F3 last paragraph; report §T20 "The general-γ factor question is answered in F3"; mandate T20 "say whether the factor is right"). F3 says: "the wiki's guessed factor `(γ₁ − γ₀)` appears in `qE` … i.e. the endpoint values … carry the factor exactly, **but the quadratic's middle coefficient does not factor through it**." Each clause is true of the coefficients, but the dichotomy "endpoints yes, middle no" is an artifact of reading the `β`-part of `qE` and the whole of `qB`: `qE` does not factor either (its `−αρδL(ρAδA + κ)` term), and the reason is the same for both — `Q = (Δ − α)·D` carries `−α·D`. The question the wiki asked (`double-lesion-fixed-points.md` line 53: "the general-γ version [of the `p̌`, `1−p̂` asymptotics] presumably carries a factor `(γ₁ − γ₀)` — guess, not re-derived") is about `Δ` and its crossings, and there the answer is exact on all of `[0, 1]`: probe `GammaFactor.lean` proves `Delta_eq_gamma_factor : P.Delta p = (P.γ₁ − P.γ₀) * P.docGamma.Delta p` for every `p ∈ [0, 1]` and every `P : DlParams K`, where `docGamma P` is `P` with `γ = (1, 0)` and nothing else changed (by hand: `condSmoke = γ₀ + (γ₁−γ₀)·ρ(δL + (1−δL)p)/(ρδL + κp)` and `condAbstain = γ₀ + (γ₁−γ₀)·ρ(1−δL)(1−p)/(ρAδA + κ(1−p))`, using `kcC = κγ₀ + ρ(1−δL)(γ₁−γ₀)`), and `crossing_iff_gamma_factor : Δ_P(p) = α ↔ Δ_{P'}(p) = α/(γ₁ − γ₀)` when `γ₀ < γ₁`. So the general-`γ` Proposition 3 is the doc's Proposition 3 with `C ↦ C(γ₁ − γ₀)`: the fixed points, `m(δ)`, `δ*`, `p̌`, `p̂` and the first-order formulas all carry the factor on `C`, which is precisely the wiki's guess. **Fix:** rewrite F3's last paragraph and the report's §T20 sentence to say the factor is exact (`Δ_γ = (γ₁ − γ₀)·Δ_{(1,0)}` on `[0, 1]`, the quadratic not factoring only because of `−α·D`), and promote the probe's two theorems into `Laws.lean` or `Interior.lean` (ledger row: Kind P, Source wiki line 53 / mandate T20, Fidelity exact).
