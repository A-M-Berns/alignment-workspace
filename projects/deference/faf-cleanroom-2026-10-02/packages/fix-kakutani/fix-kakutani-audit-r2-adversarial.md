## 3. Non-blocking issues

1. **F3's wording around the R2-A fixed point (presentation).** The repaired F3 says "The corpus does contain such an equilibrium [an isolated irrational one]: **the** exact mixed fixed point of the §6 instance … On the inventory's description that instance has both features — three situations mixing independently, and conditional expectations that are rational functions of `σ`". Three small inaccuracies, none false about the number or its source: (i) `uea-2-inventory` 170 records two *further* mixed fixed points of the same instance found by Newton, so it is *an* exact mixed fixed point, not *the* one; (ii) the exact fixed point `σ = (q a + (1 − q) b, a, a)` mixes at `s₀` only (the other two situations are pure), so "three situations mixing independently" describes the game's shape, not this equilibrium; (iii) its irrationality therefore comes from the second feature alone — the conditional `F_s(a; σ) = ((1 − δ) q U_a + δ p_a V_a) / ((1 − δ) q + δ p_a)` (`uea-2-inventory` 170, claim (1)) is a rational function of `q`, so the one-unknown indifference equation is polynomial of degree 2 — not from multilinearity across several mixing players. F3 already hedges ("reading of the inventory entry; the polynomial system was not re-derived here"), and its substantive claims (arbitrary correspondences: false; bimatrix isolated equilibria: rational; this instance: a non-linear payoff) are right. Fix: "an exact mixed fixed point", and "the instance's conditionals are rational functions of `σ`, and this fixed point mixes at one situation, so its indifference equation is a quadratic" in place of "both features".
2. **The bridge lemmas have no ledger rows although the mandate asked for them (presentation, ledger).** Target 3 says "Also ship, as `L` rows, the two directions of the hemicontinuity bridge"; the ledger lists `HasClosedGraphOn.upperHemicontinuousOn` and `HasClosedGraphOn.of_upperHemicontinuousOn` under "Supporting lemmas (no rows)". Round 1's fidelity audit (N6) read this as a mandate-internal inconsistency with the "Load-bearing" paragraph ("everything else … must not be a headline row"), and [STANDARDS](../../STANDARDS.md) §6 makes supporting rows optional, so nothing is hidden — both lemmas are fully docstring'd, audited in both rounds, and exercised by probes 4a–4c. But they answer a numbered mandate target, which is §6's definition of a headline. Fix (cheap): two `L` rows, Fidelity `n/a`, Witness `probe 4a/4b` on `wedgeF` and `probe 4c` on `sharpGraphF`.
3. **Two report/ledger nits (presentation).** (i) [fix-kakutani-report](fix-kakutani-report.md) "Discharge recipe": "The closed graph is usually proved by `hasClosedGraphOn_iff_seq_of_isClosed` …, as `brouwer_findim_of_kakutani` and `mpBR_hasClosedGraphOn` do (the latter by writing the graph as an intersection of closed sets …)" — `mpBR_hasClosedGraphOn` does not use the sequential form (Witness.lean 275–295 is the closed-set intersection), so "as … do" and "the latter by" contradict each other; say "as `brouwer_findim_of_kakutani` does; `mpBR_hasClosedGraphOn` instead writes the graph as an intersection of closed sets". (ii) The ledger's Witness supporting-lemma list omits `bestResponse_subset_Icc` (Witness.lean 189); harmless.
4. **The empty-index instance of the pi forms is a tautology (observation; inherent, mandate-sanctioned).** Probe 3b proves that over `ι = Fin 0` the conclusion of `kakutani_pi_stdSimplex` follows from `hF_nonempty` alone (the domain is a point, probe 3a). The mandate says not to require `Nonempty ι` ("an empty product is a point, and the theorem holds trivially"), the `d = 0` case of target 1 is handled the same way, and the N+ witnesses are on `ι = Fin 2`, so nothing is wrong. Fix (optional): one clause in the `kakutani_pi_stdSimplex`/`kakutani_pi_Icc` docstrings, so that nobody later reads an empty-index application as a check.
5. **ClosedGraph.lean's module docstring overstates the forward bridge's hypotheses (presentation).** It says "with compact `K` and values in a compact set it implies Mathlib's `UpperHemicontinuousOn F K`"; the lemma `HasClosedGraphOn.upperHemicontinuousOn` (line 68) needs no compactness of `K` — only first-countability and values in a compact `L` — and its own docstring says so correctly. Claiming *more* hypotheses than the lemma has is the harmless direction, but the module docstring is what a consumer reads first. Fix: drop "compact `K` and".
6. **"Values off `K` are irrelevant" is a docstring claim, not a lemma (optional).** Probe 5 proves `(∀ x ∈ K, F x = F' x) → (HasClosedGraphOn F K ↔ HasClosedGraphOn F' K)` in four lines; consumers whose correspondences are defined by cases may want it in `Defs.lean` as an `L` lemma (`HasClosedGraphOn.congr`).
