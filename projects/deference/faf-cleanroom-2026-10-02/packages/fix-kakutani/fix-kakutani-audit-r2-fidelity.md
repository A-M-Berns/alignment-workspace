## 3. Non-blocking issues

- **N1 — F3's new mechanism sentence names an inert feature (imprecision, findings file).** The repaired F3 says the §6 instance "has both features — three situations mixing independently, and conditional expectations that are rational functions of `σ` under the continuous extension — so a quadratic irrational is unsurprising (reading of the inventory entry; the polynomial system was not re-derived here)". The witness is `σ = (q·a + (1−q)·b, a, a)` (`updateless_existence.py:521`, `uea-inventory` 029's Source line): it mixes at `s₀` **only**; `s₁, s₂` are pure. With `s₁, s₂` pure, `U_a = U(aaa) = 1` and `U_b = U(baa) = 3/8` are constants, and the `s₀` indifference condition `c0['a'] = c0['b']` (asserted at `:529`) is an equation between two ratios `((1−δ)q·U_a + δp_a V_a)/((1−δ)q + δp_a) = ((1−δ)(1−q)·U_b + δp_b V_b)/((1−δ)(1−q) + δp_b)`, each affine over affine in `q`; cross-multiplying gives a quadratic in `q`. So the quadratic irrational comes entirely from the self-posterior ratio (the second feature); the "three situations mixing independently" feature is inert for this witness — a `|S| = 1` self-game produces the same degree by the same mechanism. The hedge and the conclusion are fine; the fix is one clause: "the witness mixes at one situation only, and the degree-2 comes from the self-posterior ratio at `s₀` (affine over affine in `q`, cross-multiplied), not from several situations mixing". Not blocking: F3's substantive claims (the constant-correspondence counterexample; bimatrix isolated equilibria are rational; the corpus's irrational fixed point is uea-029's, not uea-028's threshold) are all correct and verified.
