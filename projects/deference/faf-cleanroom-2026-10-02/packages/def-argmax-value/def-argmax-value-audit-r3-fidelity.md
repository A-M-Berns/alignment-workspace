## 3. Non-blocking issues

**N1 — Push the agenda (probe): the package already constrains the truth-teller's price more than the OPEN's register says — its limit points lie in `{0, s, 1}` — so the OPEN is exactly "`s` is a cluster point of `P_{f n}(τ_n)`".** `Open.lean`, the open list and the ledger row register the OPEN as "truth value unknown: `p_n → 1` (`τ_n` eventually always true) and `p_n → 0` are each consistent with everything proved". True, but incomplete in a way that matters for whoever attacks it: `concentrates_self` on `truthMenu` with `selectionPackage_self` — the same lemma that settled the punishing menu's masses in round 1 (`punishing_quotes_tie`) — says, with `p_n := P_{f n}(τ_n)` and the threshold `s_{f n} := probeThreshold`, for every `ε > 0`:

- at `j = 1` (`E*(I^1) ≈ₙ 1 − p_n`, `m^1 = s_{f n}`, `M_n = max(p_n, s_{f n})`): `(1 − p_n)·1[s_{f n} + ε ≤ p_n] → 0` — **along the days on which the price is `ε` above the threshold, the price tends to `1`**;
- at `j = 0` (`E*(I^0) ≈ₙ p_n`, `m^0 = p_n`): `p_n·1[p_n ≤ s_{f n} − ε] → 0` — **along the days on which it is `ε` below, it tends to `0`**.

Hence (with `s_{f n} → s`) every limit point of `p_n` lies in `{0, s, 1}`, and the OPEN's statement is equivalent to `∀ ε > 0, ∃ᶠ n, |p_n − s| < ε`. The truth-teller is the positive-feedback fixed point whose only interior rest point is the unstable one at `s`; "the price stays interior" means "the price keeps returning to `s`", not "the price wanders in `(0,1)`". Machine-checked: `audit-r3-probes/TruthPriceCluster.lean` — `truthPrice_above_tendsto_one`, `truthPrice_below_tendsto_zero`, and `truthPrice_interior_iff_cluster_at_s : (∃ c > 0, ∃ᶠ n, c ≤ p_n ∧ p_n ≤ 1 − c) ↔ (∀ ε > 0, ∃ᶠ n, |p_n − s| < ε)` for `0 < s < 1`, instance line at `𝗣𝗔`, `succDeferral`, `½`; all three `[propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound]`. Nothing here decides the OPEN, which stays open. **Fix I'd accept:** adopt the three theorems into `TruthTeller.lean` (as the earlier rounds' probes were adopted) and restate the register of the OPEN in `Open.lean`, the open list and the ledger row: "limit points of `P_{f n}(τ_n)` lie in `{0, s, 1}` (`truthPrice_above_tendsto_one`, `_below_tendsto_zero`); the question is whether `s` is a cluster point (`truthPrice_interior_iff_cluster_at_s`); `p_n → 0`, `p_n → 1`, `p_n → s` and oscillation among the three are each consistent with everything proved". A remark for the attacker, **heuristic and not machine-checked**: a trader that buys `τ_n` at day `f n` with the ramp weight `Ind_δ(P_{f n}(τ_n) − s_{f n} > 0)` never loses (wherever the weight is positive, `τ_n` holds) and banks `(1 − p_n)·w_n`, so the criterion should also forbid a non-summable approach to `s` from above (symmetrically from below, selling), which would cut the cluster-at-`s` case down to a summable one — the route to settling the OPEN is a trader argument through FAF's criterion, not the fold; recorded only as a suggestion.
