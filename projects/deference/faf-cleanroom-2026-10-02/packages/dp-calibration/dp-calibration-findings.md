## F17 — T3(d)'s "two-leaf single-point tree" with payoffs "`10` and `10 − ε`" cannot exist as stated; the delegated two-point reading realizes it (mandate slip, T3(d); dp-core-2-053 read ATTRIBUTION-UNVETTED)

On that tree D2 approves exactly `(δ_a; δ_{a'})` (`limitTie_eventTremble_iff`) and D1 approves every `(m; δ_{a'})` (`limitTie_limitStateEdt`), which is the claimed divergence (`limitTie_routes_differ`). Severity: mandate slip (the two-point construction is the natural reading of 2-053's "values `10` and `10 − ε`"; the attribution of that reading to the chat is ATTRIBUTION-UNVETTED — the mandate itself flags it so). Consequence for the research: DY-3's route and D1 are different devices even where both are defined; a limit tie is where they part, and they part in the direction of the act whose value does not degrade under the tremble.
