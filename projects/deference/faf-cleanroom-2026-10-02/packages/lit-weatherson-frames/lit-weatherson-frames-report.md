## Deviations from the mandate (each checked, not trusted)

1. **`ValueBdd` is over uniformly bounded menus.** The mandate's convention `ValueBdd π F := ∀ ι o, (∀ i, Bdd (o i)) → …` (per-option bounds) is refuted by Coin itself: `Coin.O_bdd` shows every `O i` is bounded and `Coin.not_valueInt`'s strategy `s` has a summable return, so per-option `ValueBdd` fails on Coin and T8(c) as worded is false. The corrected predicate uses `BddFam o := ∃ M, ∀ i w, |o i w| ≤ M`, which is what "bounded utilities" means (one utility function, bounded); `ray_valueBdd`, `Bentham.valueBdd`, `value_imp_totalTrust` and T12's pointer are all stated with it, and `Coin.O_not_bddFam` records that Coin's menu is not uniformly bounded. Held up: yes, with the correction.
2. **Zhang's proof is a single extremal step, not a strong induction.** The mandate's proof (Claim(`v`) by downward strong induction over the attained values) is replaced by `no_top_disagreement`: maximize `max (A w) (B w)` over the *disagreeing* supported worlds only, and contradict deference at that maximum. The induction was needed only to keep the agreeing worlds above `v` from interfering, and they do not interfere because a supported agreeing world in `[A = M] ∩ [B = b]` has `b = M`, which the upper half of constraint 4 allows (`c ≤ M`). Held up: yes; the same step proves S3 verbatim.
3. **Lumping bridge on `Fin (N+1)` with the lump at index `N`**, instead of `Fin N` with `N ≥ 1` and the lump at `N − 1`, to avoid the side condition and `Nat` subtraction. Held up: yes.
4. **Bentham's `ValueBdd` (any menu) is proved**, beyond the mandate's `ValueFinInt`; the mandate's remark that the restricted menu is a `Finset.image` of a finite menu generalizes to the image of the strategy's choices on `W_N`, which is finite for any menu. Held up: yes.
5. **`ValueInt` carries a summability guard on the strategy's return** (the mandate: "never state a Value claim whose strategy return is not shown summable"): the guard makes the predicate itself honest rather than only the refutation. Held up: yes; `Coin.not_valueInt` supplies the guard.
6. **T4's `twoStrat_recommended` takes no integrability hypothesis** (recommendation compares the row's value of `Eℕ … X`, whatever it is, with `t`); integrability is supplied by the headline so that the value is a genuine expectation. Disclosed in the docstring.
7. **R-literal is guarded by positive cell mass** (`0 < mass C (lev₂ A B a b) → …`); the unguarded existential form would be unsatisfiable on every cell, a strawman. Disclosed in the docstring.
8. **E1's `q`, `c`, `v` are written with `2^k` (zpow) and division** (`q k = (2^k)^2/4`, `2/(2^k)^2`; `c k = (10/27) 2^k`, `1 − (10/27)/2^k`; `v k = 2^k/3`, `1 − (2/3)/2^k`) rather than `4^{k−1}` and `2^{−k}`, so that every identity is a rational-function identity in `y = 2^k > 0` closed by `field_simp; ring` after `2^{k±1} = 2^k · 2^{±1}`. The numbers are the mandate's; all four claims (1)–(4) held with the mandate's arithmetic (`identity` in the three cases `j ≤ 0`, `j = 1`, `j ≥ 2`; `HasSum q 1` as `11/12 + 1/12`).
