## 3. Non-blocking issues

1. **Ledger witness cell for `gallow_condExp` (S1) misattributes `G5`.** The row says Witness `G5 (finite instance)`, but `G5` is a `Fin 5` weight with a null world, not an instance of the measure-theoretic statement (no `MeasureSpace`, no `condExp` identities compiled from it). Nothing inhabits `gallow_condExp`'s package in Lean. Its hypotheses are trivially satisfiable (`A = B = Y` constant, or any bounded `gen A`-measurable `A` with `Y = A = B`), so the STANDARDS §3 witness duty is met by "trivially satisfiable", which the cell should say (grade N−), or compile the constant inhabitant. Fix: reword the cell; optionally add a one-line constant witness in `GallowCondExp.lean` (kept out of the root, as now).

2. **The abstract's literal clause ("when the option set is finite") is refuted one step removed.** F-T4 rests on `value_imp_totalTrust : ValueBdd → TotalTrustC` (all menus) and `valueFinInt_imp_totalTrustInt`; the predicate that matches the abstract's clause word for word — Value for *finite* menus of *bounded* options, `ValueFinBdd` of `Composite.lean` — is not shown to imply `TotalTrustC`. The two-option proof gives it verbatim: probe `ValueFinBddImpTotalTrust.lean`, `valueFinBdd_imp_totalTrustC`. Fix: add it beside `value_imp_totalTrust` (kind L) and point F-T4 at it.

3. **The countable ratio-form equivalence is cited, not compiled.** Every `TotalTrustC`/`TotalTrustInt` row carries Fidelity "exact (product form, positive-mass convention)", supported by the dependency's finite `totalTrust_iff_cond`; on the countable side the identification "`∑' π (X − t) 𝟙 ≥ 0` ⟺ (mass of the threshold event `> 0` → `t ≤ E_π(X | event)`)" is asserted in docstrings but not proved. Probe `TotalTrustCIffCond.lean` proves it for bounded `X` and any `IsDist π` (`totalTrustC_iff_cond`; the mass-zero case is exactly the positive-mass convention: a nonnegative series summing to zero vanishes termwise). Fix: add it to `CFrame.lean` (kind L). Also: the textual anchor for the convention is DDB's own footnote 10 ("Here and throughout we leave implicit the restriction that the conditional probability is well-defined"), which is more direct than Lemma 7.1; F-T4 and F-T10 should cite it — it strengthens the finding's claim that the convention is DDB's, not this package's.

4. **`R8` is shown to fail `Between4`, not the row of record's `BetweenUpper`.** `zhang_finite_upper` assumes only the upper half; the compiled evidence that `R8` fails *its* hypothesis package is `R8.not_between4`, one step weaker. It does fail the upper half, at the agreement cell `(⅘, ⅘)` where the posterior is `17/20 > ⅘`: probe `R8NotBetweenUpper.lean`, `R8_not_betweenUpper`. Fix: add it to `PoolingWitnesses.lean` and cite it in the `zhang_finite_upper` ledger row.

5. **"Non-geometric prior" is the wrong phrase for `Ray23`.** The report (§C, "a non-geometric prior") and the mandate call `π n = (2/3)(1/3)^n` non-geometric; it is a geometric distribution with ratio `1/3`. The ray lemma is stated for every full-support `π`, so its proof cannot use Coin's weights and the second witness only shows the hypothesis `IsRayPrior` is inhabited twice. Fix: say "a second, different geometric prior (not Coin's weights)"; if a witness that is not geometric is wanted, `π n = 6/(π² (n+1)²)` is what the mandate itself suggested and declined.

6. **Bentham's Value rows assume recommendation at the null world but never use it.** `RecommendedC` requires optimality at world `0` (row `π`, `π 0 = 0`); the report says correctly that `tr_recommended` never uses it. So `ValueFinInt π frame` and `ValueBdd π frame` hold for the strictly weaker hypothesis "recommended at every world of positive prior mass", for free. Since the whole point of T9/T10 is that the null world is immaterial, that stronger form is the natural statement (and is what a dependent quantifying over a.e.-strategies will want). Fix: state an a.e.-recommended variant of `Bentham.valueFinInt` (and note that the two-option witness of T4 is recommended everywhere, so nothing else changes). Non-vacuity of the finite-menu predicates as stated is confirmed by probe `RecommendedExists.lean` (an argmax chosen by the row is recommended on every `CFrame`, Bentham included).

7. **F-T2a's framing.** "The proof gloss of l. 88 is not a proof" is true, but the sentence at l. 88 explains why the *Gaussian* construction needs infinitely many values; the paper does not purport to prove Zhang's theorem (it is Zhang's, "Forthcoming"). Suggested wording: "the paper's one-sentence explanation of why finitely many values block the construction (l. 88) names the extremal value but not the argument; the proof of record is `no_top_disagreement`". Severity stays presentation.

8. **The countable predicates are vacuous without `IsDist π`.** `TotalTrustC π F`, `TotalTrustInt`, `ValueBdd`, … do not carry `IsDist π`; for a non-summable `π` every `tsum` in them is Lean's `0` and `TotalTrustC` holds trivially. Every headline in this package supplies `IsDist`/`IsRayPrior` or an explicit prior, so nothing here is affected — but `corr-legit-general` imports these predicates. Fix: either bake `IsDist π` into the predicates or put the warning in the `CFrame.lean` module docstring (it is currently only in `Eℕ`'s).

9. **Ledger witness cell for `zhang_finite_upper` says "`G5` inhabits it".** `G5.witness` gives `Pools` for every `λ`; `BetweenUpper` follows by `Pools.between4` (any `0 < λ < 1`) and `Between4.upper`, but that instance is not compiled. Fix: one line in `PoolingWitnesses.lean` (`G5.betweenUpper`), or reword the cell "inhabits it via `Pools.between4`".
