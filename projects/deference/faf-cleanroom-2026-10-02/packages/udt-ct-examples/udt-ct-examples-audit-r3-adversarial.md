## 2. Blocking issues

### B1 · `MemI.not_isCA_pill`, `MemI.not_hasCA`, `MemI.report_law_differs` (and F-15, F-13, report §T2(e), the `MemoryInternal.lean` module docstring): the CA failure "for the paper's reason … not a representation artifact" is produced by the dummy external action `Ä(light) = r`; with a harmless dummy the pill *has* a communicative alternative under these weights

**What the Lean says.** `IsCA o e a c` (`Concrete.lean:242`) compares `P(Π̈ = π ∣ Π*(ȯ, ö) = a)` with `P(Π̈ = π ∣ Π*(ȯ, ö) = c, Π̈ = R)`. On `MemI`, `polOf' c' r (o, e) = (c', r)` at a light input (`MemoryInternal.lean:102`), because `aE ω = MemH.actOf d r (obs ω)` is `r` at the light instance (the "dummy" the module docstring names, line 25) — so the pill action is `(1, r)` and the conditioning event `evS o e (1, r)` **fixes `r`**. `report_law_differs` is then `P(report = r ∣ c' = 1, r) = 5/8` against `P(report = r ∣ c' = 0, r) = 7/8` (`report_key`: `80 · 128 ≠ 112 · 128`), and `ca_key` is the same separation at the profile `(r, r, r)`. The dummy is a device: `E_fac` (`Structure.lean:123`, every `(Ä, D_E)` pair attained) forbids a trivial external action at the light instance when the asked instance's is two-valued (the mandate's own trap, audit r1 adversarial N6), so *some* two-valued `Ä(light)` is forced; the package chose the report policy `r`, which puts `r` into `Π̈(light)` and into the action at every light input.

**Probe 1** `MemIReportMarginal.lean` (passes), on `MemI.S` itself: with the pill action alone conditioned on — `{c' = 1}`, which `pill_eq_union` shows is the union over `r` of the two pill events `evS o e (1, r)` at every light input — the report's law is the same fair coin as under `{c' = 0}`: `report_marginal_agrees : P(Π̈(asked) = x ∣ c' = 1) = P(Π̈(asked) = x ∣ c' = 0)` for both `x` (`values`: `128/256` each side). This is `Mem.asked_law_agrees` again, on the carrier the package calls the paper's picture. The joint law of `Π̈ = (r, r, report)` does differ (`joint_differs`, `40 · 256 ≠ 56 · 256` at the profile `(0, 0, 0)`), but only through the coordinate `Π̈(light) = r` — the dummy's.

**Probe 2** `MemNoiseDummyCA.lean` (passes): the 96 worlds `(((c', d), (r, r')), (ö, ℓ))` with `MemI`'s `tie`, memory-wipe and `7 : 1` weights unchanged and `r'` a uniform boundary coordinate that is the light instance's external action (read by nothing); `Π̈ = (r', r', report(d, r))`. In the count form `IsCA` unfolds to on a message-free carrier (`evFollowsR = univ`): `ca_identity` — for every external policy `π` and both `x`, `P(Π̈ = π ∣ pill, x) = P(Π̈ = π ∣ nothing, x)`; `minMod_nothing` — `m(nothing, x) = 64/256 = 1/4 ≤ 3/4 = m(pill, x)`; `minMod_asked` — the two attained actions at the asked input tie at `m = 1/2`, so each is its own communicative alternative by `isCA_self_of_minMod`; `dd_fails` — clause (2) still fails at `MemI`'s atom (`21 · 256 ≠ 88 · 48`, `dd_values`). So on this carrier **"nothing" is a communicative alternative of the pill at every light input, `HasCA` holds, and decision-determination fails** — the retracted diagnosis of [examples-revisited](../../sources/udt-representation-theorem/examples-revisited.md), not F-15's "both". (The `AbstractDS` fields of this carrier are not re-discharged in the probe — cost, `96²` pairs — they are `MemI`'s with one more boundary coordinate that nothing reads, and none of the four statements uses them; I checked `E_fac`, `B_fac`, `A_fac`, `I_fac`, `IB_det` and the two common-information equations by hand.)

**Why it is the dummy and not the paper.** Hand computation, room for the repairer to check: with `q_r = P(r = 0)` and the colour marginally uniform, `P(report = 0 ∣ pill) = 2/8 · q_r + 3/8` and `P(report = 0 ∣ nothing) = 6/8 · q_r + 1/8`; they agree iff `q_r = 1/2`. `MemI`'s weights are symmetric in `r`, so the report's own law is the same coin under both actions and the CA identity can fail only through something that reveals `r` — which is exactly what `Ä(light) = r` does. Under an asymmetric report-policy prior (say `r` weighted `3 : 1`, giving `9/16` against `11/16`) the laws differ *marginally*, the CA identity fails whatever the dummy, and the failure is the paper's own reason (the pill makes the report track the light, "nothing" makes it track the unforced policy). So the honest statement is: **on the paper's picture the CA verdict is prior-dependent and the DD verdict is not** (the DD mechanism needs only F-15's two facts, the memory wipe and the pill working, and probe 2 shows it survives the dummy change).

**Where the claim is made.** `MemI.not_isCA_pill`'s docstring and ledger row ("for the paper's reason", "Fidelity: exact (on this representation): the CA identity fails at the coordinate the pill acts on", "not the F-1 artifact"); `MemI.report_law_differs`'s docstring and row ("the pill decouples the report from the unforced report policy, which is the paper's reason"); the `MemoryInternal.lean` module docstring ("neither is a representation artifact here"); F-15's headline and its "Both questions at once" paragraph ("not at a coordinate that reveals the choice"); F-13 ("on the faithful one the Memory Problem breaks both hypotheses"); report §T2(e) and the "Pushed further" paragraph of "Repair round 2". The ledger header's package-wide (c) list has no entry for the dummy external action (`CBm`, `MemH`, `MemI`, `CBh` all use one); audit r1 fidelity N1 already flagged that `CBm.release_valve`'s properness runs through it.

**Fix I would accept** (either; (i) is the stronger package). (i) Add a second prior to `MemoryInternal.lean` with the unforced report policy asymmetric (`r` weighted `3 : 1` is enough; 48 worlds, ≈45 s), and prove `report_law_differs`-style separation **with `r` averaged** — `P(Π̈(asked) = 0 ∣ c' = 1) ≠ P(Π̈(asked) = 0 ∣ c' = 0)` (`9/16 ≠ 11/16`) — plus `not_isCA_pill`/`not_hasCA` on it; keep `MemI.not_decisionDetermined` (prior-independent mechanism); rewrite the three docstrings, the two ledger rows, F-13, F-15 and §T2(e) to say that on the paper's picture DD fails for every prior of the family while the pill lacks a communicative alternative iff the prior's unforced report is not calibrated to the pill's colour (`q_r ≠ 1/2`), cite probe 2 for the calibrated case, and add the dummy external action to the ledger header's (c) list. (ii) Keep the symmetric prior, regrade `MemI.not_isCA_pill`/`not_hasCA`/`report_law_differs` to Fidelity `weaker: conditional on `r`, which the dummy `Ä(light) = r` reveals` with Status `flagged: representation-dependent (dummy)`, and rewrite F-15's headline to "fails decision-determination; the CA verdict depends on the prior and on the dummy" with the same text changes. In both cases strike "neither is a representation artifact here" from the module docstring.
