**Package-wide (c), disclosed once.** (1) Inherited from `udt-comm-trust` §3: every decision structure takes `Ω` to be the support of the prior; "a.e." reads "everywhere"; every conditioning event is empty or of positive mass. Junk conventions: `−1` for conditional expectations (the paper's), `0` for conditional probabilities (`udt-comm-trust`'s). (2) **The pre-instance's choice is its external action** (`CB`, `Mem`, `TB`; report §0.1, findings F-1): forced by `I_fac`/`B_fac`/`IB_det`; `Π̈(pre)` reveals the choice, so no two distinct pre-actions can be communicative alternatives on these carriers — and on `Mem` it is also *why* decision-determination holds (`MemH`, the same carrier with the choice hidden, fails it; F-15). `CBm` uses the paper's own probabilistic-effect picture instead (a realized internal action, effect with probability `3/4`; its pre-action is the dummy `Ä(pre) = k`, the agent's own room policy). (3) **Copies are a free environment coordinate correlated in law** (`udt-comm-trust` T8′; report §0.2, findings F-2). Every number is an integer count lifted once (`CountExt`).
