## 0. Representation decisions (settled before the first witness; they differ from the mandate's sketch)

Two decisions were forced by the abstract structure's fields, and both are disclosed as modelling substitutions `(c)` on every witness in addition to the inherited support convention of `udt-comm-trust` §3.

**0.1 The pill/message choice is the pre-instance's *external* action, not an internal one.** The mandate's sketch has `AI` carrying the choice at `pre` (`nothing`/`pill`/`message`) while the pill is the side channel `Ǒ` at the rooms. That is not representable at the support level: `I_fac` (`I` factors as `(Ȧ, D_I)`) makes the internal action `Ȧ` support-independent of every subvariable of `D_I` — proved once as `aI_indep_of_dI` (`CountExt.lean`) — and the rooms' side channel lives in `D_I` (`Ǒ ⊑ Ȯ ⊑ (Ȧ, D_I)`, with `Ȧ` constant at the rooms), so the choice at `pre` cannot determine the pill at the rooms. The paper's own remark that "there is merely some probabilistic relationship between internal actions and (potentially self-modifying) internal observations" (C&T LaTeX draft, Modification) is exactly this constraint. Two escapes were examined and rejected: (i) an internal action at `pre` correlated with the pill in law only (the paper's picture) needs a second free dynamics coordinate and pushes every carrier to 48–108 worlds, and makes "nothing" modifying with positive probability; (ii) `Ȧ = Ǒ` at every instance as a function of `(D_I, D_B)` with cycle-shaped level sets is consistent with `I_fac`, `B_fac` and `IB_common` only from 4 × 4 dynamics values (not built). The chosen representation: the pre-instance's external action `Ä(pre)` is the channel state `s`; `Ȧ ≡ 0`; `D_I = s`; nothing in `E` reads `Ä(pre)` (`U = 0` at `pre`); `Π† = Π*` at every unforced realized input (`hlink`, proved on each witness). Consequence recorded in the findings (F-1): `Π̈(pre)` reveals the choice, so *at the pre input no two distinct pre-actions can be communicative alternatives* in this representation — the CA identity compares the laws of the whole `Π̈`. T1(d) and T3(c) therefore use a different carrier (see §T1(d)).

**0.2 Copies are a free environment coordinate, correlated in law.** As `udt-comm-trust`'s `W2` (its finding T8′): the other room's action `a` is a `D_E` coordinate whose law is tied by the prior's weights to `Π̈` at the other room. Two consequences that the corpus's prose does not have: the pill's effect on the copy is whatever the prior says it is (`W5`/`W10` put weight `7 : 1` on the copy pressing `$10` under the pill); and the pre-instance's external observation `Ö = pre` must be realized alongside every internal observation the rooms realize (`O_fac`), so `Ȯ(pre) = s` — the pre-instance "observes" the channel state.

**0.3 Every number is an integer count.** Utilities are `u/2` (`CB`), `u/1` (`Mem`), `u/4` (`TB`) for natural-valued `u`; `CountExt.lean` turns every conditional expectation and modification probability into a ratio of counts and every comparison into a cross-multiplied natural-number inequality settled by `decide +kernel`. Each structure `S W` takes its `IntWeights` as a parameter where the factorization fields are prior-independent (`CB`); decision-determination clause (2) is proved per prior.
