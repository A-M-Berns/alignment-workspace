## Representation decisions, and why

- **Pattern A (enlarged latents) via `kernelProd μ k : Distr (W × T)`** (`Basic`), with the parent's event-split machinery (`pressExpectOn`, `pressMassOn`) on `Finset` cells. Conditioning on a coordinate is a cell restriction; "pointwise on `y`" is `∀ y₀, pressExpectOn a (cell yOf y₀) X ≤ 0`. No headline rests on `x / 0 = 0`: every strict claim carries `0 < pressMassOn …`.
- **Pattern B (the programmers' rule)** is `ruleVal P hOf X h₀ := if cellSumX P X hOf h₀ < 0 then 1 else 0` on the *product-form* cell sum, so a null cell is not pressed — which is what the rule on the conditional would also do under Lean's convention, but only by accident (F-7). `ruleInstance μA P hOf X` carries two priors (the agent's and the programmers'); `commonPriorRule P hOf X := ruleInstance P P hOf X`.
- **Pattern C (sequences of two-state instances)**: `Nat.iterate` of `erode` with the interval invariant carried by `erode_iter_mem_Icc`, so `twoState (erode^[n] ε) α β c h …` is built for every `n`; the crossing index is the predicate `εβh(1 − β)^n < (1 − ε)αc(1 − α)^n` with `IsLeast` on the instance.
- **The stop-hypothesis setting is `Option Ωin`** (`none = ω⊥`), general `A₁`, `A₂`, `Sh`; hypotheses name the sensor values and the stop-world mass rather than building a new structure, so the theorems apply to any instance (the witness `stopWorld` on `Option (Fin 2)` instantiates them with `rfl`).
- **Good's theorem is stated on `Distr W` with a `kOf : W → K` partition** (`Partition`), with `cellSum`, `partValue = ∑_k sup'_b cellSum`, `priorBest = sup'_b expect`; the parent's `voiButton` is the case `K = Obs` on `jointDistr S a : Distr (Ω × Obs)` (`voiButton_eq_partValue_sub`), re-derived to show the bridge. T9 uses the two-option specialisation `partValue2 = ∑_k max (cellSumX k) 0`.
- **The `causal` toy and the live channel** are built from `kernelProd` on `Bool × Bool` and `World × Bool`; the noisy channel absorbs its coin into the press probability rather than a third coordinate.
- **Brier accuracy** is stated on `World` with `brier2 p ω = 2 (p − 1_W(ω))²` and the silence posterior identified with `erode α β ε` (the same expression), making T17 and T4 share one object.
