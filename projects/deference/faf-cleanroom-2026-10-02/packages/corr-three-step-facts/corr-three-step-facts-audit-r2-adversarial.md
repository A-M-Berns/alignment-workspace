## 2. Blocking issues

What I tried, and why it did not break anything:

- **Vacuity of `HullCondition`.** A condition of the form "`∃ a b ≥ 0` with a linear identity" invites the worry that some choice of weights always works. It does not: by the sufficiency theorem's contrapositive, any frame on which a `Q`-measurable payoff is pressed with a positive `y`-cell sum fails it — the T8 straddle frame with `qOf = id` (`not_hull_straddle`). On the trivial question `Q = Unit` every frame satisfies it (`a h₀ = P(h₀ ∩ y₀)/P(h₀)`), but so is question-relative deference itself; that is the object, not an artifact.
- **Squeeze in `commonPriorRule_pressExpectOn_nonpos_of_hull`.** The hypothesis (cone membership) is conjecturally *equivalent* to the conclusion (the OPEN iff), so I checked the proof is not a one-step unfold: it is the `y`-cell decomposition, the two dot-product lemmas, an interchange of sums and a termwise sign argument — the easy half of Farkas, real but short (Kind nit, N6).
- **Degenerate models for the load-bearing five.** T8: `Y = Unit` makes `RefinesModNull` trivial and the iff reduces to "the sum over pressed cells is `≤ 0`", true and not vacuous; a Dirac prior makes every cell but one null and both deference and the hull condition hold trivially, consistently. T3: `stopHyp_necessity` with `α = 0` or `M < 0` stays true (checked the `nlinarith` inputs: `εβ X(none) ≤ 0` with `X(none) ≥ −M > 0` forces `εβ = 0`).
