## 3. Non-blocking issues

1. **`readLocal_exact_iff` (`SealedDefense.lean:592`) proves its "only if" for score equality; the narrowed A5 claim is about choices.** The statement is `(∀ Q g, AtomProfile ℓ atom Q g → ∀ c, P1 (liftV (readLocal atom cw Q g)) c = W Q c) ↔ ∀ j, a_j cw_j = b_j`. The `⟸` direction is what "escapes" needs and `argmax_readLocal_eq_argmax_W_of_exact` carries it to choices. The `⟹` direction says: if the reading reproduces `W` *pointwise* on every atom-profile standard then the weights are exact. VERIFY's "a reading escapes A3 for every correlation structure iff …" reads the left side as *choices* agree, and the docstring's "i.e. it escapes A1–A3 for every harm profile" invites that reading. The choice-level "only if" is not in the package except for D2′ on the selection model (`readD2'_escapes_iff`). It is true, and short: `probe1_choice_level_necessity` proves, on any sealed `Problem S (Fin 2)` with `π(L) > 0`, that a single atom with `a_j cw_j ≠ b_j` yields a standard (atom-profile `¬L` part, an `L`-offset `−t` on one option with `t = (a_j cw_j + b_j)/(2π(L))`) on which the reading's argmax and `argmax W` are different singletons. **Fix I'd accept:** add the probe's theorem (or its `Nonempty`-two-options generalisation) as `readLocal_escapes_iff` and let the ledger row say "iff at the level of choices"; or leave the Lean and change the Fidelity cell to "iff at the level of scores; the choice-level 'only if' is proved for D2′ (`readD2'_escapes_iff`) and for two-option menus (audit r2 probe 1)".

2. **Finding 13 and the report scope the gated plain-EU inversion to "void-heavy priors under action-dependent legitimacy"; it also happens under sealing.** `voidHeavy` is B's toy (`a₁` voids in `b`), so the witness `ruleScore_EU_gated_inverts` is action-dependent, and the finding's general sentence ("for any score that accrues value on void worlds, gating fails on void-heavy priors under action-dependent legitimacy") carries the qualifier along. `probe3_sealed_gated_EU_inverts` gives a *sealed* instance — `π(b) = 9/10`, `L = {g}`, `Q(g, ·) = (0, 0)`, `Q(b, ·) = (0, 1)`, `0 ≤ Q ≤ 1`, `n = (0, 1)`, `ϖ = 2` — where the ungated EU score excludes the violator (`9/10 − 2 < 0`) and the `[leg]`-gated one picks it strictly (`9/10 − 2/10 = 7/10 > 0`). Since cluster A *is* the sealed regime, the sealed witness is the relevant one for the escape NEGATIVES A4 describes. Not a false claim (the qualifier is sufficient, not necessary), but the honest general statement is: gating fails for any score that accrues value on void worlds whenever the violator's `¬L` gain exceeds `ϖ·π(L)` net of its `L`-branch loss, sealed or not. **Fix:** drop "under action-dependent legitimacy" from the finding's last sentence and the report's §Target 15 / "Stretch S3" wording (keep it for the description of `voidHeavy` itself), optionally adding the sealed instance.

3. **A3b (3)'s middle clause in the sources is imprecise and the findings file does not say so.** NEGATIVES A3b (3): "exact for every `g` iff `ρ` is constant, i.e. iff `L ⊥ 𝒢`"; item 010 and mandate target 13: "iff `b_j/a_j` is constant across supported atoms iff `L ⊥ atom`". With an unsupported atom (`a_j = 0 < b_j`) `ρ` can be constant on the supported atoms while the unweighted estimator is inexact and independence fails: `probe4_rho_const_not_exact` (`a = (1/2, 0)`, `b = (1/4, 1/4)`, `π(L) = π(¬L) = 1/2`, `c ≡ 1`).
