**N1. Target 15's "(or P2)" clause is not in Lean, and the naive P2 clause is false under the exclusion convention.** Mandate target 15: "no option with `n ≥ 1` is in the argmax of the rule-scored P1 (**or P2**, or plain EU)". The package has `ruleScore_ungated_excludes` (P1) and `ruleScore_EU_excludes` (plain EU); no P2 form is defined, the report §Target 15 does not mention the omission, and "State of the package" says every core target is proved. Probe 1 defines `P2rule` (`P1 V a / P(L | a) − ϖ n a`, `none` at `P(L | a) = 0`) and shows three things. (a) `probe1_P2_gated_eq_ungated`: `P2 (ruleScore ϖ n V) = P2rule` wherever defined — for P2 the gated and ungated terms *coincide* (the `[leg]`-gated penalty `ϖ n a · P(L | a)` is divided by the same `P(L | a)`), so finding 13's P1-vs-EU dichotomy has a third case that it should record: P2 cannot see the gate at all. (b) `probe1_P2_exclusion_picks_violator`: on `nullCompliant` (compliant `a₀` void everywhere, violator `a₁` legitimate everywhere, `u ≡ 0`, `ϖ = 2 > D = 0`) the ungated P1 form excludes `a₁` while `argmaxOpt (P2rule …) = {a₁}` — under the exclusion convention the violator is the *only candidate*, so the P2 clause needs a defined compliant option. (c) `probe1_P2rule_excludes_of_sealed`: under `SealedBy ℓ` with `0 < π(L)` the P2 clause holds, twelve lines from the package's own lemmas. Fix I'd accept: add `P2rule`, (c) as the theorem, (b) as its N− boundary (the exclusion-convention edge is a small finding of its own: the rule term cannot save P2 when every compliant option is null), and a sentence in the report scoping the target.
