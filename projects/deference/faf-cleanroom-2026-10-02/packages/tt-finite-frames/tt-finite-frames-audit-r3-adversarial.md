**N1 — "join" names two dual objects in one package; the source's own notation sides with `Join.lean`, against the newer use.** Repair round 2 added two things called the join. (i) `Join.lean` (F4): the pair map `w ↦ (f₁ w, f₂ w)`, whose cells are the *intersections* of the cells of `f₁` and `f₂` (`Corr.ofMap_pair`) — the finest common refinement, "the join in the refinement preorder" (module docstring l. 9), i.e. the least upper bound in the *information* order where a finer partition is greater; ledger §F and report §"Repair round 2" say the same. (ii) `Witnesses.lean` `Thm51Inhabitant.e6` (l. 265: "The join of `c` and `d`: cells `{0,1,2,3}, {4,5}` (the components of the overlap graph …)"), `quote_eq_join` (l. 272: "the join expert's quote"), `Squares.lean` ll. 228 and 294–296 ("for instance the join of the two, the components of their overlap graph"; "The join of `c` and `d` is the finest such `e`"), the ledger's §E row ("`c` and `d` refine the join `e = {{0,1,2,3},{4,5}}`"), report §E ("on the inhabitant `e` is the join") and "next steps" item 4 ("construct the join of two partitions (components of the overlap graph)"): the components of the overlap graph, whose cells are *unions* — the coarsest common coarsening, the greatest lower bound in the information order. These are the two dual lattice operations. F-S1 itself calls the second one the *meet* in three places ("the cells of the *meet* of the two partitions (the components of their overlap graph)", "the meet partition's expert", "meet-measurable") and then the "join" once ("on the `Fin 6` inhabitant the join is `{{0,1,2,3},{4,5}}`"), and `Squares.lean` l. 291/310 has "meet-measurable" in the docstrings of the very theorems whose section header (l. 228) says "join". The source settles which word is which: `route-transitivity` §5.1's Diagnosis (l. 176) writes `𝔼[X ∣ 𝔄] = 𝔼[𝔼[X ∣ 𝔄 ∨ 𝔐] ∣ 𝔄]`, a tower identity that needs `𝔄 ⊆ 𝔄 ∨ 𝔐`, so its `∨` is the *generated* σ-algebra — the intersection partition, `Join.lean`'s object. Under the source's notation the overlap-components partition is `𝔄 ∧ 𝔐`, the meet, as F-S1 (mostly) says. Fix I would accept: call the overlap-components partition the *meet* (or, to sidestep lattice conventions altogether, "the coarsest common coarsening") in `Witnesses.lean` (`e6`'s docstring, `quote_eq_join`'s docstring and name — `quote_eq_meet`; nothing but the docs references it), `Squares.lean` ll. 228 and 294–296, the ledger §E row, report §E and next-steps item 4, and the one stray "join" in F-S1; leave `Join.lean` as is.
