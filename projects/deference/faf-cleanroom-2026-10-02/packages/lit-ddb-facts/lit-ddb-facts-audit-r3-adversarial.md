## 3. Non-blocking issues

- **N1. Nestedness is necessary, not only sufficient; the ledger cell overstates in the wrong direction.** The Fidelity cell of `PriorFrame.totalTrust_of_nested_of_simpleTrust` says "restricted to nested evidence, which is exactly where the unrestricted claim fails". The package proves nested ⟹ (Simple Trust ⟹ Total Trust) and exhibits one non-nested failure; it proves nothing about *where* the claim fails, and the report's E1 honestly lists "whether Total Trust of the prior forces nested evidence" as not attempted. It does. (i) *Transitivity from Total Trust, any prior frame* — probe Q1, `priorFrame_Ev_subset_of_mem_of_totalTrust`: `totalTrust_cands` makes every row totally trust the frame, `TotalTrust.simpleTrust` + `positiveAccess_of_simpleTrust` give the row at `w` positive access, and positive access at `q = E_w` (`mass_row_Ev`) says every world `v ∈ E_w` has a row certain of `E_w`, i.e. `E_v ⊆ E_w` by regularity. With F14 this is `priorFrame_factive_trans_of_totalTrust`. (ii) *Nestedness from Total Trust, on paper.* By Theorem 4.1 (`TotalTrust.hullAndModestlyInformed`) `μ ∈ hull C_μ` and every row is modestly informed. For a row `P_u = μ(· | E_u)`, factivity gives `cell(P_u) = S_u := {v : E_v = E_u} ⊆ E_u`, so `P̂_u = μ(· | S_u)`, and `C_u⁻ = {μ(· | E) : E ∈ 𝓔_u}` with `𝓔_u := {E_v : v ∈ E_u, E_v ≠ E_u}`, every member of which is `⊊ E_u` by transitivity. Dividing the coordinate `y ∈ E_u` of a modest-informedness representation by `μ(y)/μ(E_u) > 0` gives nonnegative `d` with `1 = d_S·[y ∈ S_u] + ∑_{E ∈ 𝓔_u, y ∈ E} d_E` for every `y ∈ E_u`; the hull condition for `μ` gives likewise `1 = ∑_{E ∋ y} d_E` over all realized sets, for every `y ∈ W`. Suppose `E_a ∩ E_b ∋ x` with `E_a, E_b` incomparable. If no realized set contains `E_a ∪ E_b`, take realized `E_a' ⊇ E_a`, `E_b' ⊇ E_b` maximal under inclusion: they are distinct, meet at `x`, and at `y = a'` the only realized set containing `a'` is `E_a'` (any such set contains `E_{a'} = E_a'` by transitivity, hence equals it by maximality), so `d_{E_a'} = 1`, likewise `d_{E_b'} = 1`, and at `y = x` the hull equation reads `1 ≥ 2`. Otherwise take `E_u` minimal among realized sets containing `E_a ∪ E_b`; then `E_a, E_b ∈ 𝓔_u`, their maximal supersets `E_a', E_b'` in `𝓔_u` are distinct (a common one would be a smaller realized set containing the union), meet at `x`, and the same computation in the equations of `MI(P_u)` — `a' ∉ S_u`, the members of `𝓔_u` containing `a'` are exactly `E_a'`, `x ∉ S_u` — gives `1 ≥ 2`. So `TotalTrust μ → Nested`, and with the package's theorem, on every prior frame `Value μ ⟺ TotalTrust μ ⟺ Nested ∧ SimpleTrust μ ⟺ Nested ∧ factive ∧ transitive`. (iii) *Search.* `search4_nested.py` (b): all 112 factive transitive non-nested four-world maps × 15 priors — none has Value; (c): over all 50,625 maps × 2 priors, all 486 pairs with Value are nested, factive and transitive. **Fix I'd accept:** copy Q1 into `Prior.lean`; either formalize (ii) (a finite maximality argument, no new infrastructure) or list `TotalTrust Q.μ Q.toFrame → Q.Nested` as an OPEN row with the argument above; rewrite the Fidelity cell to "restricted to nested evidence, which is necessary as well (…)" and add the equivalence to F15 and the report's E1. Note that Q1 also sharpens the ledger row of `PriorFrame.Ev_subset_of_mem_of_nested`: with Total Trust in place of Simple Trust, transitivity needs no nestedness.
