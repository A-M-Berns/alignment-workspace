**`pass`.** Every headline's Lean statement, unfolded to frames' definitions of record, says what its name, docstring and ledger row say and matches the DDB sentence it cites; there is no squeeze, no undisclosed (c), no over-selling name, no core target skipped; every ledger row's declaration exists with the stated Kind, Fidelity and Status; the single OPEN row carries nothing; and the round-2 repair is correct in substance and correctly described — in particular the new Target 17 material, which no audit had yet checked, holds up: `ExamplesPrior.cxQ` is a prior frame exactly as fn 25 defines one, its prior trusts (frames' `Trust`, all `q, p` and all real `t`) and does not value the induced frame (I re-derived both by hand: `X = (−6, 3, 0)`, `s = 1` gives the product sum `−3/5`; equivalently the candidate `P_2 = (0, ⅓, ⅔)` is not modestly informed, since `C_2⁻ = {(¼, ¾, 0)}` puts mass on world 0), so DDB's composite claim — l. 157's "in a natural subclass of frames, Value also follows from Trust (Theorem 7.4)" with fn 25's "the special subclass is the class of prior frames" — is false as DDB states it, and the refutation is robust to the `Trust` convention (the frame is factive, so no world of any conditioning event `p` is excluded under either reading). ## 3. Non-blocking issues

### NB1 — `nullSelf_validates_all`'s docstring calls New Reflection a hereditary principle

`Examples.lean` l. 1444: "`nullSelf` validates all four hereditary principles (Reflection, New Reflection, Total Trust, Value)". New Reflection is precisely the principle that is *not* hereditary — fn 56, findings F8, and the package's own `nonHered4_not_hereditary` (Target 14(ii)) — while Reflection, Value and Total Trust are (`reflects_cands`, `value_cands`, `totalTrust_cands`). The theorem statement is fine (it says `Validates` for the four, which is true); only the word "hereditary" is wrong. Fix: "validates all four principles" (or "the three hereditary principles and New Reflection").

### NB2 — `informedReflects_iff_simplex_and_junk` cites the wrong probe

`Reflection.lean` l. 535: "Source: … `lit-ddb-facts-audit-r2-adversarial` Q2 (copied)". That theorem is the adversarial audit's **Q1** (its N8); Q2 is the lexicographic-complement instance. The report's repair bullet ("N8 — Q1 copied") has it right. Wording only.

### NB3 — The "might as well not be included" pointer is l. 343, not l. 351

`Examples.lean` l. 1413 (`nullSelf_validates_reflects_modest`): "(DDB §4 l. 351: modest candidates 'might as well not be included')". In the transcription that sentence is at **l. 343** ("…so modest candidates might as well not be included in the frame"); l. 351 is Figure 4's right-hand caption ("The process iterates as new extreme points are pulled towards the others…"). Inherited from the round-2 adversarial audit's N3, which gave l. 351. Same species as rounds 1–2's pointer clusters; one number.

### NB4 — "Exhaustive search" oversells the round-2 search in two docstrings

`Prior.lean` module docstring: "the round-2 audit's exhaustive search on three worlds found no failing nested evidence map"; docstring of `prior_trust_value_all_nested_open`: "the round-2 audit's exhaustive search on three worlds found no counterexample". The search is exhaustive over evidence maps (all 343) but samples the prior (25 regular distributions) and the deferrer (37 grid points), so it is not exhaustive over three-world prior frames, let alone over deferrers. `lit-ddb-facts-open.txt` states the grid sizes correctly and is the record that matters; the two docstrings should say "search over all three-world evidence maps (grids of priors and deferrers)". The numbers themselves I reproduced (header above).

### NB5 — F15's title drops the scope of "true on nested prior frames"

The heading "'On prior frames Trust ⟹ Value' (§2 l. 157, fn 25) is false as stated; true on nested prior frames" is true only for the deferrer-is-the-prior reading (`prior_value_of_trust_of_nested`); the separate-deferrer reading on nested frames is the package's OPEN row, and the body of F15 says so under "What remains open". `research/CLAUDE`'s epistemic-discipline rule 1 asks headline sentences to carry their scope; the report's "State of the package" and the `Prior.lean` docstring do ("Simple Trust *of the prior*"). Fix: "…; true on nested prior frames for the prior as deferrer (separate deferrer: open)".

### NB6 — The `diracQ` witnesses are N−, not N+

The ledger grades `diracQ_trust_and_value` (witness cell of `prior_value_of_trust_of_nested` / `tfae_nested`) and `diracQ_open_all` (witness cell of `prior_trust_value_all_nested_open`) N+. `diracQ` has `E_w = {w}`, so the induced frame is immodest (`diracQ_immodest`) and every one of these facts is decided by the immodest collapse (`tfae_immodestFrame`) — the proofs use exactly that — with no role for nestedness or for the closed-set induction. Relative to the theorems they guard they are degenerate: N−, "immodest frame; the collapse decides it". This costs nothing, because the non-degenerate instances are already in the package: `chainQ_nested_trust_value` (modest rows, nestedness load-bearing) for the proved theorem, and the same declaration at `π = μ` is an instance of the OPEN statement's shape on a modest nested frame (the OPEN row quantifies over all `π ∈ Δ`, and `μ ∈ Δ`). Cite it there; keep `diracQ_open_all` as the "for every deferrer" instance, graded N−.

### NB7 — The ledger header's blanket hypothesis sentence no longer covers Target 17

"Every theorem's only hypothesis beyond its antecedent is `hπ : π ∈ stdSimplex ℝ W` or its nonnegativity half (a)". Since round 2, `totalTrust_of_nested_of_simpleTrust`, `prior_value_of_trust_of_nested`, `tfae_nested` and the OPEN row take `hn : Q.Nested`, and `totalTrust_of_factive_of_trans_of_nested` takes factivity and transitivity of the evidence. None is (b) or (c) — nestedness restricts the class of frames and is shown necessary by `cxQ`; factivity and transitivity are derived from Simple Trust in the headline — and every row discloses it in its Fidelity cell, so the "no (b) or (c)" half of the sentence stands. Only the "only hypothesis" half needs a clause: "…or a disclosed structural hypothesis on the frame (immodesty of the candidates, biconvexity, nestedness)".

### NB8 — F10's last clause about `cxQ` describes the exclusion as "exercised" where it cannot matter

F10: "the counterexample `ExamplesPrior.cxQ_trust` is checked in the record form, and its `p` with excluded worlds (`p ∌ 0` at row 0) are where the exclusion is exercised, all covered by the brute force". A world that `Frame.condProbEvent` drops can only change Trust's conditioning event `p ∩ [P(q | p) ≥ t]` if it lies in `p`; row 0 (`δ_0`) has `P_0(p) = 0` exactly when `0 ∉ p`, i.e. when world 0 is not in `p` anyway. So on `cxQ`, as on every factive prior frame, the exclusion never changes the event — which is F10's own point one clause earlier. The sentence should stop at "checked in the record form, and the exclusion reading is immaterial on it by the factivity argument above". Wording; the conclusion of F10 is right.

## 4. What I checked and how, per headline

Method: for each ledger row I unfolded the Lean statement to plain words (frames' definitions of record from `Defs.lean`, package definitions from their bodies), compared it with the DDB sentence the row cites (re-read at the line my `grep -n` on the transcription found), and checked Kind / Fidelity / Hyps / Status against what the statement is. "✓" means statement = ledger row = source, with only `hπ` (or nonnegativity) plus the theorem's antecedent as hypotheses. Definitions of record used throughout: `Reflects π F` = for every candidate `ρ ∈ C_π` (`C_π = image P (supp π)`), `π w · 𝟙[P_w = ρ] = π(P = ρ) · ρ w` at every `w`; `NewReflects` (NR-str) = every candidate has `0 < ρ(P = ρ)` and `π w · 𝟙[P_w = ρ] · ρ(P = ρ) = π(P = ρ) · ρ w · 𝟙[P_w = ρ]`; `NewReflectsVac` = the identity only where `0 < ρ(P = ρ)`; `SimpleTrust` = `t · π[P(q) ≥ t] ≤ π(q ∩ [P(q) ≥ t])` under a (redundant) positivity guard; `Trust` = the same on `p ∩ [P(q | p) ≥ t]`, where `[P(q | p) ≥ t]` keeps only worlds with `P_w(p) > 0` and `t · P_w(p) ≤ P_w(q ∩ p)`; `TotalTrust` = `∀ X s, 0 ≤ ∑ π w (X w − s) 𝟙[s ≤ E_w X]`; `Value` = every recommended strategy (cell constraint included) for every nonempty menu beats every option; `Frame.Validates Φ` = `∀ i, Φ (P i) F`; `Frame.ModestlyInformed` guarded by `0 < ρ(P = ρ)`, `ModestlyInformedU` unguarded with the junk `P̂ = 0`; `PriorFrame` = regular `μ`, nonempty `Ev`, rows `μ(· | E_w)` as a ratio (legitimate: `μ(E_w) > 0`).
