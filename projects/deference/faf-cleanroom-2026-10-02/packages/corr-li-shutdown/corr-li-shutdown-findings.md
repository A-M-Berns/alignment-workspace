## F11 — Statement 1(c′)'s proof establishes "unbounded", not "`sup = +∞ ∧ inf = −∞`" (local error in a proof; conclusion weakened; the two-sided form is **refuted** under the Lean's upward-ramp clause — machine-checked — and under both ramp clauses jointly the trichotomy's first two cases are realized by the machine-checked drift instance; open only over Def. 4.4.1 proper)

`li-final.md` §Proofs 1: "Suppose `sup_N T_N < ∞`. If `inf_N T_N = −∞` we are done for the inf; so suppose also `inf_N T_N > −∞`, and let `M := limsup_N T_N`, finite." The finiteness of `limsup T` uses boundedness on *both* sides, and the case "`T` bounded above, `T → −∞`" is never closed; the mirror has the same gap. So the argument proves `¬(BddAbove ∧ BddBelow)` — the realized signed error is unbounded — and that is the theorem (`SignedError.lean`, `signedError_core`, P; `signedError_unbounded` over FAF, `partial` on F12 and vacuous, F17).

**Pushed one step (repair round 2).** The argument needs only `limsup T` finite, which holds as soon as `T` is bounded above and does *not* tend to `−∞` (Mathlib: a bounded-above sequence that is frequently above some level is cobounded, and its `limsup` is then a real it approaches from below infinitely often). So under the upward-ramp clause a bounded-above running sum **tends to `−∞`** (`signedError_bddAbove_tendsto_atBot`, P), under the downward-ramp clause a bounded-below one tends to `+∞` (`signedError_bddBelow_tendsto_atTop`, by the symmetry `price ↦ 1 − price`, `truth ↦ 1 − truth`), and under both: **`T → −∞`, or `T → +∞`, or unbounded on both sides** (`signedError_trichotomy`). The case the sources never close is the *only* way a bounded-above `T` can satisfy their clause.

**The two-sided form is false over the Lean's clause** (found by the round-2 fidelity auditor, B1; lifted as `signedError_two_sided_refuted`, P). Round 0 had listed `sup = +∞ ∧ inf = −∞` under `signedError_core`'s hypotheses as OPEN with `Fidelity: exact`; both were wrong. The clause quantifies over the **upward** ramps `Ind_{η'}(T_N > M')` only, so on `price ≡ 1/2`, `truth ≡ 1`, `p = 1/2` — `T_N = −(N+1)/2` — every ramp is eventually `0`, no ramp diverges, the clause holds vacuously, and `T` is bounded above. The instance is excluded by Def. 4.4.1 (the constant weighting `1` averages `truth ≡ 1` to `1 ≠ 1/2`), so the sources' statement over *their* hypothesis is untouched by it; what is refuted is the identification of the Lean's clause with Def. 4.4.1. **No clause over functions of the running sum can recover the two-sided form** (the barrier instance **machine-checked** in repair round 3, `Drift.lean`; the powers-of-two variant argued only): a Boolean stream with `#ones(≤ N) = (N+1)/2 + d_N`, `d_N → ∞`, `d_N = o(N)` — e.g. `truth_i = 1` at the powers of two and alternating `0, 1` elsewhere, or the barrier recursion `truth_N := 1[T_{N−1} > −√N]` — has, at `price ≡ 1/2`, `T → −∞` bounded above while *every* weighting that is a function of the running sum (upward ramps: eventually `0`; downward ramps: eventually `1`) and the constant weighting average it to `1/2`. Def. 4.4.1 excludes these too, but only through weightings that read more than the running sum (the indicator of the powers of two; a ramp on the window increment `T_N − T_{N−1}`, which reads `truth_N` itself and pairs it with `truth_{N+1}`), i.e. through the full strength of "pseudorandom against all P-generable weightings". **Repair round 3 (audit r3 adversarial B1, fidelity N1):** the barrier recursion is built as `driftTruth` — the walk `driftExcess` on the excess `D = −2T` (`D_0 = 1`, `D_{N+1} = D_N ± 1`, up iff `D_N < ⌊√(N+1)⌋`), `⌊√N⌋ − 1 ≤ D_N ≤ ⌊√N⌋ + 1`, density `1/2` — and `signedError_trichotomy_atBot_realized` / `signedError_trichotomy_atTop_realized` inhabit the trichotomy's **two-sided** package in its first two cases (N+: the clause on the side the running sum drifts away from is vacuous, as the conclusion forces; the other is exercised with content — its ramps are eventually `1` and average the stream to `1/2`). The round-2 docstring of the trichotomy had cited `signedError_two_sided_refuted`'s instance for this realization; the auditor's probe showed that instance violates the downward clause (its downward ramps are eventually `1` and average `truth ≡ 1` to `1`), so that citation was wrong and is withdrawn — the one-sided instances realize the respective *dichotomies*, the drift instance the trichotomy. Case 3 under both clauses (unbounded on both sides) remains argued: i.i.d. Bernoulli(`1/2`) priced at `1/2`, almost surely (recurrence of the simple random walk); the block-alternating stream (`truth = 1` on `[k², (k+1)²)` for even `k`), deterministically — its ramp-weighted averages need a within-block count that was not attempted. Whether the two-sided form holds over Def. 4.4.1 — a recurrence statement about a pseudorandom walk — is therefore a genuine question, with no abstract real-sequence statement (the class of weightings is market-relative) and no non-vacuous FAF statement at `succ` (F17). It is recorded here, not listed OPEN. The sources' J2 consequence ("no finite total of realized errors is available") survives with "unbounded", and is sharpened by the trichotomy.

## F17 — Statement 1(c′)'s two hypotheses collide at every deferral with `f(k+1) ≤ poly(f(k))`: good feedback at `succ` is never pseudorandom (**blocking the source's Statement 1(c′) as stated at such `f`**; the Lean refutation is `Collision.lean`)

If `truth = 1` infinitely often, `W` is divergent and `succ`-patient and its `truth`-average tends to `1 ≠ p`; if `truth` is eventually `0`, the constant weighting `1` averages it to `0 ≠ p`.
