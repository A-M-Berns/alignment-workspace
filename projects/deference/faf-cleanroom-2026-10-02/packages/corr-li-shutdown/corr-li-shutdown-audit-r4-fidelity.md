## 2. Blocking issues

### B1. F19 — the recorded obstruction to the `loe` route is not FAF's (also in `open.txt`, `Legitimacy.lean` §(b′), the ledger row, the report)

**The claim** (F19; verbatim in `open.txt` and the `Legitimacy.lean` §(b′) header): "FAF's `loe` takes the coefficient as an expressible feature `a n`, but its `DeterminedViaTheory` hypothesis asks the day-`n` deductive state `D_n` to decide the value of the combination `Z_n − a_n·X_n`, i.e. the quote literals of the day-`n` value of `a_n` — and no deductive process decides the literals of its own day-`n` prices at day `n`, because the day-`n` prices are computed *from* `D_n`. So `loe` factors out a coefficient the *theory* has decided, not one the *market* has computed … the route is the paper's introspection family (4.11 `introspection`, 4.12 `self-knowledge`)". The ledger row: "**What resists:** FAF's `loe` needs `DeterminedViaTheory` at day `n`, and no deductive process decides the literals of its own day-`n` prices at day `n`". Report §T2 and § Repair round 3 repeat it.

**What FAF says.**

1. `PCWorld.ConsistentWithTheory (v) (DP) : Prop := ∀ n, v.ConsistentWith (DP.D n)` (`Framework/Criterion.lean:864`, docstring: "the propositional rendering of the paper's `cworlds(Θ)`"). `LUVCombination.DeterminedViaTheory As P DP truth := ∀ n (v : PCWorld) (ν : LUV → ℝ), v.ConsistentWithTheory DP → (As n).ValuesAt v ν → (As n).value P ν = truth n` (`Properties/ExpectationProperties.lean:1675`). The quantifier is over worlds consistent with the **completed** theory; the day-`n` stage `D_n` appears nowhere. The coefficient `(a n).denote P` is read at the actual market (`linearityLUVComb_expect`, `Endpoints.lean:269`) — the theory is not asked to decide it.
2. `RationalQuoteCode.reflected (Q : QuotationTheoryPresentation DP T) (q : RationalQuoteCode T value) (n) (v) (hv : v.ConsistentWithTheory DP) : v.ValuesAt (q.luv n) (value n)` (`Quotation/Packages.lean:391`; "Every completed-theory world values the threshold family at the represented rational"), for **any** `RationalQuoteCode` — its `pos_complete`/`neg_complete` fields are `T`-provability of the quote literals. The package's `paperCurrentWeightQuoteCode T w hw hmem := RationalQuoteCode.ofComputable T (hw.computable (paperMarketComputation T)) hmem` is a `RationalQuoteCode T w`; FAF's `deferredWeightQuoteCode` is the same constructor at `(hw.computable market).comp f.computable` (`MarketQuoteCodes.lean:804–810`). So every completed-theory world of `paperDP T` values the current-day quote at `w n`, with `paperQuotationPresentation T` (`Paper/TheoremDP.lean:435`) as the presentation — no deferral is involved on either side.
3. `meshProductLUV_valuesAt` (`MarketQuoteCodes.lean:529`): in every completed-theory world valuing `X n` at `x`, the mesh product with any `RationalQuoteCode` is valued within `1/(n+1)` of `x · value n`.

Probe `audit-r4-probes/CurrentQuoteReflected.lean` states 1 (as `Iff.rfl`), 2 and 3 at `paperCurrentWeightQuoteCode` and elaborates them by direct application (`probe_consistentWithTheory_unfold`, `probe_currentWeight_reflected`, `probe_meshProduct_current_near`; axioms in `CurrentQuoteReflected.out`, §5).

**What actually stands between the OPEN and a proof.** The exact `hdet0 : DeterminedViaTheory (linearityLUVComb a b X Y Z) P DP (fun _ => 0)` of `lic_linearity_of_expectation_seq` fails for the mesh product by the slack `1/(n+1)` of item 3, not by any stage-`n` issue. FAF's own `lic_expect_combination_provind_le` / `_ge` (`Endpoints.lean:113–160`) reduce their `hval` to an eventual bound `hmesh : ∀ ε > 0, ∀ᶠ n in atTop, ∀ v, v.ConsistentWithTheory DP → ((As n).meshAffine (n + 1)).value P v.payout ≤ c + ε` (via `meshAffine_eventually_near`) before calling `affine_provind_theory_le_const`. So the route the mandate named is: `a n :=` the feature presenting `w` (`hw : PGenerableRat`, i.e. `∃ feature, GeneratedRatFeature P w feature`, `Framework/Expectations.lean:119`), `b := 0`, `Z n := meshProductLUV (paperCurrentWeightQuoteCode …) X n`; `BoundedSequence` (poly certificate from the feature's `polyTok`, `hX` and `meshProductLUV_machineThresholdCodeSeq`; `l1Norm ≤ 2`) and `WorldValued` (from `hval` and item 3) for the combination; approximate determination within `1/(n+1)` from items 2–3; and the two eventual-`ε` provind steps transcribed from FAF (FAF is read-only; a re-proof here is [STANDARDS](../../STANDARDS.md) §1's pattern). I did not attempt it (an auditor's budget); it is the repair's job to attempt or to record honestly.

**Also wrong in F19.** (i) "for a deferred weight `w_{f(n)}` this is why `thm:ccee` (reading (b)) is a theorem of its own and not a corollary of `loe`": `ccee`'s content is the quoted *deferred expectation* `⌜E_{f n}(X_n)·w_{f n}⌝` (the `Z'` side of `ConditionalExpectationQuote`), not the weight factoring; the weight quote is reflected in every completed-theory world on both sides by the same `reflected`. (ii) "the route is the paper's introspection family (4.11/4.12)": the paper's 4.11 concerns the market's price of a quote literal *of its own current price* (a continuity argument, needed because the e.c. family of *true* literals is not computable); the factoring statement here is an expectation identity over worlds that decide the quote, which is `thm:loe`'s territory — the paper's `loe` (`main.tex:1700`) allows exactly "bounded P-generable sequences of rational numbers" as coefficients. The wiki's item 4 asks "verify whether the paper's 4.11–4.12 family covers this or whether it is a lemma to prove"; the honest answer the package could give is "it is `loe` with P-generable coefficients, plus the mesh slack".

**Fix I would accept.** (a) Prove `decidedWeight_factors_open` along the route above and remove it from the open list (F19 then becomes "the mandate's route works; the wiki's item 4 is `loe`"); or (b) if that resists within budget, rewrite the attempt record in all five places to the true state — "the exact `DeterminedViaTheory` of `lic_linearity_of_expectation_seq` fails only by the mesh slack `1/(n+1)` (`meshProductLUV_valuesAt`); the completed-theory worlds decide the current-day quote (`RationalQuoteCode.reflected`, probe); what is missing is an eventual-slack transcription of FAF's `lic_expect_combination_provind_le/ge` plus the `BoundedSequence`/`WorldValued` plumbing of the combination; not attempted" — and drop the sentences about `D_n` deciding its own prices, about `ccee`-versus-`loe`, and about 4.11/4.12 being the route. Under (b) F19's severity line reads "the mandate's route is open, not dead", and the `open.txt` entry's reason changes accordingly. Nothing rests on the OPEN, so no Lean beyond the record needs to change under (b).
