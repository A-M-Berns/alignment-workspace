**Package-wide (c), by reference:** layer B (the FAF bridge and everything over `AbstractDS`) inherits `udt-comm-trust` §3 (c) — the carrier `Ω` is the support of the prior, so "a.e." and "everywhere" coincide (`distMeasure_ae_iff_forall_of_pos`). **The bridge itself is an equality**: `IsSubvariable ↔ AEFunctionOf`, `DecisionDetermined ↔ (U ⊑ E ∧ CondIndepFun E D_{I,B} Π̈) ↔ (U ⊑ E ∧ I[E : D_{I,B} | Π̈] = 0)`. Layer A (`Access`, `Approx`, `Witness`) has no (c) and no choice-based junk value in any definition (the axiom `Classical.choice` is used, through Mathlib's reals and in `extensional_of_factors`'s extractor; the gate permits it — repair r1, both audits' NB1). The latent-model encoding (`Latent.lean`) codes the three ranges into one sum type by injections; every headline is stated for the uncoded variables (not a (c): an injective relabelling, disclosed in the docstrings).
