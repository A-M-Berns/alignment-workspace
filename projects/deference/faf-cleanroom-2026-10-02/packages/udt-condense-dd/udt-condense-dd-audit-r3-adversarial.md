## 3. Non-blocking issues

1. **The T4(b) witness's payoff makes the law-shift term invisible.** `Witness.Varying.u o a b = [a = b]` (`R = 1`). For that payoff `varyingUtility Dof u p pol m = 1 − mass (Dof (p m)) (errSet p pol m)` for *every* `Dof`, `p`, `pol`, `m` (probe `VaryingPayoff.lean`, `indicator_utility`), so on `Varying` the gap `|U 0 − U 1| = 7/32` is bounded by `δ = 7/32` from `accurate` alone (`gap_le_delta_from_accuracy`): neither `lipschitz` nor `support` is needed to get below the theorem's `1/2`. The r1 criterion (bound below the trivial range bound `R`, r1 adversarial B2) is met, the mandate's N+ criteria (`L > 0`, `Dof` non-constant, utilities differ) are met, and the instance is not degenerate — so the N+ grade stands — but the prediction-dependence of the law, which is the whole point of the regime, contributes nothing to this instance's gap. Drop-in strengthening, elaborated in the probe: `u' o a b := if o = 0 then 0 else [a = b]` on the same data gives `U 0 = 3/4`, `U 1 = 1/2` (`utilities'`), gap `1/4 > 7/32 = δ` (`gap_exceeds_delta`: the gap now exceeds any own-accuracy bound — `7/32` from the error at observation `3` plus `1/32` from the mass the verdict moves from observation `3` to observation `0`, where `u'` is `0`), and `approxDD_varying` still applies with bound `1/2 < 1 = R` (`bound_instance'`). Recommend shipping `u'` as the payoff of record (or as a second instance) so that both terms of `R(2δ + L·2δ/γ)` are load-bearing on the witness.
2. **`AbstractDS.polS` (the chosen policy `Π*`) is unconstrained, and the T9 witnesses fill it with a constant that disagrees with `Π̈`.** On `Xor` (and likewise `Three`, `XorSkew`), `polS ω = fun _ => (0, polTable k 0)` ignores the observation, while the realized external policy `Π̈` is the identity or the flip; probe `XorStrict.lean`, `polS_ne_polE`: at world `(0,0,0)` and observation `1`, `Π*` says `0` and `Π̈` says `1`. `udt-comm-trust`'s structure imposes no link between the two fields (`decomposition` takes `IsSubvariable S.DIB S.polS` as a hypothesis), and nothing in T9's headlines reads `polS`, so no claim is affected; but a reader of "two non-constant policies" should know the sentence is about `Π̈`, not `Π*`. One sentence in `WitnessLatent.lean`'s module docstring. (The structural looseness itself is an upstream matter.)
3. **`udt_formula_condExp`'s Witness column names only half its hypothesis package.** The row says "`udt-comm-trust`'s `W2` inhabits DD"; the theorem also needs `hopt : IsOptimal S.policyUtility (S.polE ω)`. That is satisfiable on every structure — an unrealized policy has the junk utility `−1`, a realized one has utility in `[0,1]`, so the argmax over realized policies is globally optimal — but no instantiation is shipped. Say "DD on `W2`; `hopt` by an argmax world, not instantiated", or instantiate (cheap on `W2`).
4. **`Varying.bound_instance_ref` carries `γ = 7/32` under `D₀ = unif4`, whose minimum is `1/4`.** With `γ = 1/4` the reference-law bound is `9/16 + 1/16 = 5/8` rather than `9/16 + 1/14 ≈ 0.634`. Valid either way (the hypothesis `γ ≤ D₀(o)` only needs a lower bound) and below `R`; cosmetic, presumably kept to share `γ` with the own-law instance. Say so, or use `1/4`.
5. **Positive checks worth recording.** (i) `Xor`'s latent is a proper coarsening of *both* given variables: `¬ IsSubvariable Π̈ E` and `¬ IsSubvariable Π̈ D_{I,B}` (probe `XorStrict.lean`), plus `¬ IsSubvariable D_I Π̈` and `¬ IsSubvariable D_{I,B} E` (the environment has its own randomness `j`, so DD's Markov clause is not "E is determined by the dynamics"); `Xor.nondegenerate` states the `D_{I,B}` half and `E` non-injective, not the `E` half — the probe supplies it. (iii) Every one of the 160 backticked names in the ledger that is a package declaration exists in the stated module (scripted check; the non-hits are FAF/PFR/Mathlib names, hypothesis names and prose).
