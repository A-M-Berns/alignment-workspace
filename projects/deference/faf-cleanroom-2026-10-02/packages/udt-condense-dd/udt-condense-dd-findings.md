4. **The prediction-dependent constant** — *presentation* ([gap1-reframing-predictor-access](../../sources/udt-representation-theorem/gap1-reframing-predictor-access.md) §4 Corollary, prediction-dependent regime; udt-rep-033). The note asserts `|U(M₁) − U(M₂)| ≤ R (2δ + L · 2δ/γ)` with accuracy `δ` under an unsubscripted `D`. Under the reading of record — accuracy under each mechanism's own observation law (ATTRIBUTION-UNVETTED as to the reading) — **the note's constant is correct** and is now derived: `|U₁ − U₂| ≤ R (2δ + tv(D₁, D₂))` with the total-variation distance entering once, by a pointwise bound on `D₁ g₁ − D₂ g₂` (`varying_sub_le`, `approxDD_varying_tv`), and `tv ≤ L · |disSet| ≤ L · 2δ/γ` (`approxDD_varying`); with the note's own single-step Lipschitz hypothesis verbatim, `approxDD_varying_single_step`. What the note lacks is the derivation, which it asserts ("the bound becomes …") and does not carry out. *Correction of record (repair round 1):* the package's first version derived `R (2δ + 4Lδ/γ)` by a proof that split the difference into a fixed-law part and a change-of-law part and paid `tv` twice, and this entry then put the note's constant under suspicion ("the note counts it once"; OPEN). Both r1 audits proved the note's constant from the package's own lemmas (probe `NoteConstant.lean`); the slack was the proof's, not the note's.

5. **"`Π̈` is a perfect condensation variable"** — *local error*, ATTRIBUTION-UNVETTED reading (`topics/decision-determination` line 99; udt-rep-045). Quoted: "**`Π̈` is a perfect condensation variable for the agent's influence on the environment**". In Eisenstat's sense (`LatentModel.PerfectlyCondenses` of `(D_{I,B}, E)` by the latent model with pair latent `Π̈`, `polLatent`) this is false on decision-determined structures: perfect condensation forces `Π̈` to be a function of `E` (`aeFunctionOf_polE_of_perfectlyCondenses`, FAF Corollary 4.6), and `Three` is decision-determined with `Π̈` not a function of `E` (`Three.dd_not_perfectlyCondenses`). What DD *does* give is exactly the ordered-Markov clause of Theorem 4.9(B) (`orderedMarkov_polLatent_iff_dd`, the full `OrderedMarkov`); under DD, perfect condensation reduces to `Π̈ ⊑ E` (`perfectlyCondenses_polLatent_iff_isSubvariable_of_dd`). If the note means only the Markov clause, its sentence is true and its vocabulary wrong. The refutation is robust to the choice of reading: in *every* reading where `Π̈` is a latent `Y_A` with `E`'s index in `A`, Corollary 4.6 forces `Π̈ ⊑ E`, so any such reading is refuted by `Three` (r1 fidelity audit NB11). It is not an encoding artifact: the same `polLatent` perfectly condenses on `Xor` (`Xor.perfectlyCondenses`, repair round 2) — eight worlds, two non-constant policies (identity and flip), `E = (Π̈_k(j), j)`, decision-determined, `Π̈ ⊑ E` because the policy is recovered from the pair as `k = [Ä ≠ Ö]`, with `E` not injective and `D_{I,B}` strictly finer than `Π̈` (`Xor.nondegenerate`). The r1 check on `udt-comm-trust`'s `NoSub` (`NoSubPerfect.perfectlyCondenses`) is only a satisfiability check: there `E ω = ω` is injective, so every variable is `⊑ E` and the verdict has nothing to do with the policy (regraded N− in repair round 2; r2 adversarial audit B1). The note's next two paragraphs (amalgamation, recovery, lines 103–109) make no checkable claim beyond DD: the amalgamation paragraph restates that DD gives `P(E | Π̈)` and invokes Lemma 4.13 for consistency of two *models* where only one model is present; the recovery paragraph's "the correspondence theorem's recovery terms vanish for `Π̈`" is not a statement about any latent model that has been shown to condense anything.

6. **"Correspondence theorem for agency"** — *local error* ([condensation-connection-attempt](../../sources/udt-representation-theorem/condensation-connection-attempt.md) lines 92–107, quoted at 105: "Then Π₁ = Π₂ — they must agree on the policy"; udt-rep-040). Under the note's two formal conditions (determination `H(A_i | Π, O_i) = 0`, recoverability `H(Π | behaviour) = 0`) the conclusion is false: `agency_correspondence_fails` on four uniform worlds with `Pol₂ = (Pol₁, one more bit of behaviour)`. Eisenstat's Theorem 4.15 needs perfect condensation of both models, which the corpus never verifies for agents and which DD does not supply (item 5). The mandate's own sketch (`O` constant) would not have worked: with a constant observation, determination forces the policy to be injective on the support and the two attributions *do* determine each other; the observation must carry the extra bit. That is the only shape a single-situation refutation can take — any recoverable refinement of a sufficient `Pol₁` that is non-constant given `Pol₁` must vary with `O`, since determination already makes `B` a function of `(Pol₁, O)` — so the refutation's content is: *recoverability lets the latent absorb observation information*, which a minimality premise would have to exclude (r1 fidelity audit NB3). A "coverage" premise in its natural reading would not: `AgencyFail`'s observation `O = snd` takes both values with mass `1/2` each, so every observation is realized and the sentence still fails (r2 fidelity audit NB7). The refutation of the two formal conditions stands as stated.

7. **The "representation theorem"** — *local error, repeated* ([agency-as-condensation](../../sources/udt-representation-theorem/agency-as-condensation.md) lines 228–239; [condensation-connection-attempt](../../sources/udt-representation-theorem/condensation-connection-attempt.md) lines 140–153; [unified-formal-framework](../../sources/udt-representation-theorem/unified-formal-framework.md) Layer 5; [formal-theorem-attempt](../../sources/udt-representation-theorem/formal-theorem-attempt.md) Axioms 1′–3′; udt-rep-038). Over `udt-comm-trust`, "conditions 1–4 ⟹ `Π(o) = argmax_a E[U | Π(o) = a]`" is `udt-policy-calc`'s global ⟹ local optimality with DD as an unused hypothesis when the conclusion is stated over policy utilities (`udt_formula_of_optimal`, `S`). Of [condensation-connection-attempt](../../sources/udt-representation-theorem/condensation-connection-attempt.md)'s conditions 1–3 — *its own numbering*: 1 determination, 2 recoverability, 3 decision-determination (rationality is 4) — decision-determination does work, once, when the conclusion is written as conditional expectations given `D_{I,B}`: it turns `E[U | D_{I,B} = ·]` into a policy utility (`udt_formula_condExp`, `C`; `condExp_DIB_eq_policyUtility`), which is exactly the note's "once we have policy-based utility" (line 153). Determination (`Ä = Π̈(Ö)`) holds by construction of `Π̈` in `udt-comm-trust` (`polE_apply_of_DIB_eq`) — baked into the definition of the policy variable, not absent; recoverability across situations has no counterpart in the single-situation structure. So the note's sentence "The content is in conditions 1–3" is right about DD and wrong about determination and recoverability, which are inert; beyond DD's one rewriting, the theorem's residual content is global ⟹ local. [agency-as-condensation](../../sources/udt-representation-theorem/agency-as-condensation.md)'s version numbers determination, recoverability, *unity* as 1–3 (DD is its 4), and there the three are indeed absent from the proof. *Correction of record (repair round 1):* the first version of this entry read the CCA sentence under the AAC numbering and called it "false of its own theorem" (r1 fidelity audit B3); withdrawn. The corpus's own audit notes the conditions invert Eisenstat's scoring desideratum (a single global latent scores badly); this package adds that determination and recoverability are inert and DD is used once. Also: under global optimality the modification hypothesis of the note's local display is inert — `udt_formula_condExp` holds for every `ω'`, the realized-modification form is the corollary `udt_formula_condExp_modify` (r1 adversarial audit §3.4).

## Further findings

13. **The mandate's T9 witness cannot exist** — *finding about the mandate* (mandate T9(a), "a decision-determined AbstractDS whose environment ignores the agent (`E` independent of `D_{I,B}`) and whose `Π̈` is non-constant"). `polE_const_of_indep_E_DIB`: in any `AbstractDS`, `E ⊥ D_{I,B}` forces `Π̈` constant, because `Ä = Π̈(Ö)` and `Ö` are functions of `E` — in C&T's terms, `E = (Ä, D_E)` *contains the external action*, so "`E` independent of `D_{I,B}`" forces the action to be the same at every realized observation across all dynamics, i.e. `Π̈` constant. The refutation witness must have `E` depending on `Π̈` while `Π̈` is not a function of `E` (`Three`). Neither `Two` nor `W2m`/`NoSub` of `udt-comm-trust` fits (their `E` determines `D_B`).

14. **T5's shape** — *finding about the mandate* (mandate T5: `U M = F (p M|_{supp}) (pol M)`). With the payoff allowed to read the *whole* policy, "utility depends on `M` only through the on-support policy" is false (take `F _ π := [π(o₀) = a]` at an off-support `o₀`). The theorem holds when the payoff's policy dependence is also on-support (`utility_of_onSupport`), which is what the note's "the environment's response uses only on-support predictions" means once the payoff is included.

15. **The T4(b) witness and `TransparentNewcomb`** — *presentation*. `udt-policy-calc`'s `Transparent` data is not of the form `∑ o, (Dof (p M)) o · u o (p M o) (pol M o)` over a mechanism type (its utility is a coin-average over `(policy, coin)` pairs with the observation computed from the prediction), so the T4(b) witness is a purpose-built four-observation instance (`Varying`, `O = Fin 4`, skew vs uniform; since repair round 1 — the first version was two-observation); `Transparent.obs`/`coinW` are used for T10(b)'s marginals instead.

## Verdicts the mandate asked for (§7)

Nothing is open in the package.
