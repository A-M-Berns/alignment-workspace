## F5 — `faithful.md` FA-5: "`T_opt` holds iff the pure optimum is the optimum" (imprecision; tie caveat, not instantiated)

FA-5 says `T_opt(UDT_{s°,ρ})` holds **iff** the pure optimum is the optimum. On non-nested (almost-fair) one-point trees it holds (`udtProc_onePoint_isOptimal_of_almostFair`), and on the AMD it fails (`amd_inclusion_not_optimal`), so the two directions the note uses are right. The "iff" hides a tie caveat under nesting: `UDT_{s°,pol}(d) = Unif argmax_a V_B(δ_a)` on every one-point tree (`udtProc_onePoint`), so when two pure acts tie at the pure optimum and the value is not affine in the mixture (nested fiber), the uniform mixture over the tied optimal acts can be strictly below the pure optimum even though "the pure optimum is the optimum" over pure procedures. No instance was built here (a two-act nested tree with a tie at the top and a strictly convex value in the mixing weight would do); recorded as an imprecision with the mechanism named. Status: note.

## F8 — `faithful.md` FA-8: the pay-faithful set on the 2-fold concave mugging (presentation; new detail)

FA-8 reports "faithful sets for pay" on the 2-fold concave mugging without exhibiting one. The natural guess — the `T`-pay leaf plus all transfer leaves — is **not** faithful: under the concave coupling `(refuse, pay)` also transfers, so the `H`-side `C'`-mass is `½q'(2−q')` against the `T`-side's `½q'`. A faithful set is the `T`-pay leaf plus the transfer leaves below `(pay, pay)` and `(pay, refuse)` (`kfold2_concave_pay_faithful`, with `concavePayE` excluding `(refuse, pay)`), and excluding `(pay, refuse)` instead works equally: the faithful set is **not unique** under nesting, in contrast with FA-3's uniqueness on `B₁`. The impossibility for refuse is exactly as the note says (`kfold2_concave_no_refuse_faithful`: masses `(1−q')/2` vs `(1−q')²/2`). Under the linear coupling the faithful sets are simply the payoff classes (`linearPayE`, `linearRefuseE`). The mandate's own guess ("the `T`-pay leaf plus the `H`-leaves below two pays") has `H`-mass `½q'²`, not `½q'`, and is not faithful either. Status: proved; detail added to the note's claim.

## F14 — `faithful.md` Open 1: "forces the coupling to be affine" is false without normalization, and the per-act answer is a finite locus, not "affine" (local error in the conjecture as stated; refutation proved, `k = 2` characterization proved) — repair round 1

Open 1 conjectures that on the `k`-fold mugging "a faithful set exists for `a` iff the deviation law `μ_{δ_a}` is a conditional of `μ_{C'}` on a union of leaves for all `q'`, which on nested fibers forces the coupling to be affine in the number of paying draws". Round 0 stated this as the OPEN row `kfold_faithful_iff_affine_open` (for both acts, every coupling `b` with `0 ≤ b_j ≤ 1`); the round-1 audits refuted it in both directions at `k = 2`, and the refutation is now in the package (`KfoldAffine.lean`, `kfold_faithful_iff_affine_refuted`): `halfB = (0, ½, ½)` is not affine yet has faithful sets for both acts for every full-support self-model (`halfB_faithful_both_not_affine`), and `b4 = (0, ¼, ½)` is affine yet has no pay-faithful leaf-set at `q' = ¼` (`b4_affine_not_faithful`, `no_pay_faithful_quarter`). Both counterexamples have `b_2 ≠ 1`. For **normalized** couplings (`b_0 = 0`, `b_k = 1`, which every coupling the note considers is) the conjecture survives at `k = 2` and is sharpened: pay-faithful sets exist for every `q'` iff `b_1 ∈ {½, 1}` (`kfold2_norm_pay_faithful_iff`), refuse-faithful iff `b_1 ∈ {0, ½}` (`kfold2_norm_refuse_faithful_iff`), both iff `b_1 = ½`, i.e. affine (`kfold2_norm_faithful_both_iff_affine`). The two mechanisms are the *first-draw set* (faithful iff the coupling ignores the second draw: `b_1 = b_2` for pay, `b_1 = b_0` for refuse — for any `b`, any `x, y`) and the *payoff-class set* (faithful iff affine); nothing else works, already at `q' = ½`. For general `k` the hand derivation (not machine-checked; docstring of `kfold_faithful_iff_affine_normalized_open`) gives the per-act locus `i_j b_j = C(k−1, j−1)` for some integer `C(k−1, j−1) ≤ i_j ≤ C(k, j)` at each level `j` (`kfold_pay_faithful_iff_locus_open`), whose intersection with its refuse mirror is exactly `b_j = j/k` — so the note's "affine" is right for both acts on normalized couplings and wrong per act. What the note needs: the normalization hypothesis, and "affine" replaced by the locus in the per-act form. Severity: local error (the conjecture as phrased is false; the intended claim, normalized and for both acts, stands and is proved at `k = 2`). Status: refuted (as stated), proved (`k = 2`, normalized), OPEN (general `k`).
