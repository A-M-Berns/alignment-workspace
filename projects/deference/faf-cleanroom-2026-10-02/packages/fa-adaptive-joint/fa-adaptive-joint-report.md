**Why it is OPEN, and what a proof needs** (the mandate asked for the budget accounting in detail). The dependency's `hSideBridge` runs FAF's `feedbackTrader`: round trip `k` opens at `f k` and closes at `f (k+1)` along the *single* chain `interleave f d`, so trips never overlap and the budget is `feedbackWealth`, a product over closed trips whose logarithm telescopes (`sub_two_mul_sq_le_log_one_add`). An adaptive weighting opens a piece on any day `n` and closes it at `f n`; pieces nest (`f n = 2n+2`: up to `n/2` open at once; `2^n`: every `m` with `log₂ n < m < n`, i.e. `n − O(log n)` — the round-0 text said `O(log n)`, corrected in repair round 1). FAF's own corrected 4.8.16, `luv_wubexp_ofComputation`, likewise takes `WeightingSupportedOnDeferralImage W P f` on a strictly increasing deferral — it is the same single-chain trader. So no FAF theorem covers the overlapping case and the trader is new. The design I would build, and the two places it is hard:

1. *Trader.* `adaptiveTrader`: day-`n` strategy = `Strategy.join` over `k ≤ n` of "open piece `k` at day `k` of size `c_k`, close at `f k`" (FAF's `AffineCombination.roundTrip` on `bundle X k`, as `feedbackRoundTrip` does), with `c_k := λ · fire_k · free_k` an `EF`, where `free_k` is the free cash — itself an `EF` by the same `letE` chain as T1 (`free_{k+1} = free_k − c_k · price_k(bundle) + ∑_{m : f m = k+1} c_m · price_{k+1}(bundle X m)`), so the whole trader is one straight-line program per day and its `MachineSpliceStream` certificate is T1's construction once more, in the shape of `FeedbackTraderEmission` / `MachineSpliceStream.ec`. Size: `O(n)` bindings each of bounded size plus the open sums; polynomial.
2. *Bounded below.* Long-only: `free_{k+1} ≥ free_k (1 − λ fire_k) > 0` at an opening (prices `≤ 1`), `free` only grows at a closing, holdings are nonnegative bundles with world values in `[0,1]` (`bundle_magnitude_le_one`), so `netWorth_n(v) ≥ free_n − 1 ≥ −1` in every world. This half is routine given (1).
3. *Unbounded above — the crux.* With a single chain, `W_{k+1} = W_k (1 + λ w_k r_k)` and `log W_N ≥ (λγ − 2λ²) ∑ w_k → ∞` when returns are frequently `≥ γ` (`lic_not_frequently_positive_feedback_return`). With overlapping pieces the mark-to-purchase wealth `W` changes only at closings by `W ↦ W + c_m (sell_m − buy_m) = W (1 + λ (free_{open(m)}/W) fire_m ret_m)`, and `free_{open(m)}/W` is not `1`: it is `1 − (committed capital)/W`, where committed `≤ λ ∑_{open} fire_m · free ≤ λ · W` by `OnePosition` (this is exactly where `∑_{open} fire ≤ 1` enters).
