## F4 — ZO-12 / CFF-12's column-determinedness hypothesis is not needed for the no-straddle theorem in run form (imprecision; a strengthening here — regraded in repair round 1)

Both state the no-straddle theorem *for column-determined (chance-determined) partitions*, and both need the hypothesis to state their own notion of straddling: CFF-B′ (line 50) defines it through `S`-exits and `¬S`-exits, which exist only for a pre-decision `S`, and CFF-12's proof routes through the exits' realizable classes `⊆ S`. With straddling taken in *run form* (`Straddles`: some pure run consulting `d` lands in `S`, another outside — `Straddle.lean`), the theorem holds for every two-cell partition: if no point with two or more actions straddles `S`, then `{S, Sᶜ}` is observable in the lazy frame (`observable2_fr_of_not_straddles`, proved directly by `runLeaf_congr`), and column-determinedness then *follows* by CF-8 (`columnDetermined_fr_of_not_straddles`). The sources' statements are correct as stated; the package's is stronger because its definition of straddling does not presuppose the hypothesis. Pointer: ZO-12 line 108 ("If no fiber straddles two cells, the partition is observable"), CFF-12 line 80 ("if no point straddles the pre-decision `S`"). Nothing downstream is affected; the surviving-direction statement CFF-13(a) does need column-determinedness (for "strongly fair ⟹ no straddle", through Lemma A).

## F7 — The mugging's non-observability and the payoffs: CF-15 exact, Claim 2.1 silently needs `x ≠ 0`, `y ≠ 0` (imprecision in Claim 2.1; corrected in repair round 1)

CF-15 (cf-correspondence line 75) and Claim 2.1 (fable-slop-notes line 39) parametrize the mugging symbolically in `x`, `y`; neither states a positivity clause (the "for `x, y > 0`" was the mandate's wording, T11(a) and §6 item 6, imported into the first version of this entry as if it were the sources'). Claim 2.1's frame records only `(coin, payoff)` — `pay·H = (H, +y)`, `refuse·H = (H, 0)` — so "pay if `T` else refuse" is realized by `pay` when `y = 0` (and "refuse if `T` else pay" by `pay` when `x = 0`): its "[checked]" argument needs `y ≠ 0` (and `x ≠ 0` for the other order) and does not state it. Pointer: slop line 39.

## F8 — CF-4's `Fr(B) ≅ Commit^Δ(Fr^nd(B))` is an isomorphism only over the queried points (imprecision)

CF-1 defines `𝐀_B` as the product over the *queried* points `D_B`. With the agent over all points of `ι` (this package's `Fr`, the natural choice for a functor from trees), two policies differing at an unqueried point have the same diagonal assignment, so the relation is a biextensional equivalence, not an isomorphism (`fr_biextEquiv_commit_diag`, fidelity `variant`). `Fr B ◁₊ Fr^nd B` holds as stated. Pointer: cf-correspondence line 19.

## F15 — CFF-13's second "non-vacuous instance" is vacuous for the theorem's conclusion (local error under the natural reading, imprecision under the charitable one; found by audit r1, proved in repair round 1, severity split in repair round 2)

CFF-13 (cf-frontier line 82) names two non-vacuous instances of its forward direction: tree `M` and "the β-pair's `P′` (realizable-outcome-fair, not strongly fair)". The second cannot serve: on `Fr (pairTree β)` for *every* inner weight `β` — so for both members of the pair — every column-determined two-cell partition is trivial on the image, because the root coin is unrecorded in the worlds (CF-14(a)) and Lemma A makes the cell a function of the root branch (`pairTree_columnDetermined_trivial`, `pairTree_no_nontrivial_columnDetermined`, `TreeM.lean`). Observability then holds for the trivial reason (`pairTree_observable2_trivially`), so the β-pair exercises nothing of CFF-13(a) or (b). Tree `M` is the only instance the source names that works; it is shipped as `treeM` with the full package and both cells realized (`treeM_observable2_lazy`, `treeM_conditional_forced`).

Two readings of the source's sentence (audit r2, the two lenses disagree): **(A)** "non-vacuous instance" names a tree on which the *conclusion* has content — then the `P′` entry is a local error, since no column-determined partition of `Fr (pairTree β)` has both cells realized (proved). **(B)** the entry lists `P′` with its *fairness status* ("realizable-outcome-fair, not strongly fair") as a tree inhabiting (b)'s hypothesis strictly beyond (a)'s — which it does; then the sentence is an imprecision (it does not claim an observable non-trivial partition). The source should say which. Under either reading the β-pair cannot serve as a witness for CFF-13(a) or (b)'s conclusion, and under either it is N− for the backward direction of dp-core-063 as well (F19).

## F16 — The mandate's "(and at `Fr B`)" in T11(d) is false (local error in the mandate; refuted in Lean)

ZO-15 (zoo line 120) says that on a pruned tree recording at `d` for every procedure, "the first `d`-node a column reaches decides membership in `S_{O_d}` independently of the `d`-answer" — a statement about varying the `d`-answer, i.e. at `Loc d` (`columnDetermined_loc_of_pruned_recordsForAll`, `Records.lean`). That is false: `lookRecTree` (look-decide with the `d`-action recorded in the world) is pruned and records at `d` for every procedure, yet `S_{O_d}` is not column-determined in `Fr` — membership depends on the *other* point's answer (`lookRecTree_records_not_columnDetermined_fr`). The parenthetical is not ZO-15's sentence.

## F18 — Mandate T11(b) elides the locus of CF-16's full-box clause (local error in the mandate; found by audit r2, both lenses)

## F20 — ZO-5(b)/(c) and the mandate's T7(c): which "nested", and which diagonal (local error in the mandate's hypothesis; imprecision in the sources' "identified frame"; found in repair round 3, after audit r3 fidelity 1 / adversarial N1)

Two things, both proved in Lean (`Diag.lean`, `DiagStruct.lean`, `DiagWitnesses.lean`).

**(i) The hypothesis.** The mandate words T7(c) as ZO-5(b)'s "if" "under `∀ d ∈ U, ¬ Nested B d`", with `dp-core-tree`'s `Nested` — which carries a *positive-mass* clause (`∃ ℓ, Positive B ℓ ∧ 2 ≤ count d B ℓ`, `seeds.md` SE-2). The sources' argument (zoo line 71: "a path meets each `U`-point at most once"; cf-frontier line 62: "automatic when no fiber in `U` is nested") is possibilistic — it needs every `U`-point to be *minimal* in its fiber, `dp-fairness-reloc`'s `¬ NestedFiber B d`. The two differ on unpruned trees, and the difference bites: the 2-fold linear mugging with a root coin that puts all its mass on tails (`zmTree coinTails`) satisfies `∀ d ∈ U, ¬ Nested B d`, has a nested `d`-fiber, and loses its transfer coin under relocation, so its structural diagonal is not injective and the identified relocated frame is not equal to `Fr B` as a matrix (`not_nested_insufficient`). So the "if" half holds under `¬ NestedFiber` (`diagStruct_injective_of_not_nestedFiber`, `frLit_iso_fr_of_not_nestedFiber`), which is the stronger hypothesis (`NestedFiber.of_nested`), and not under the mandate's `¬ Nested` unless the tree is pruned (`NestedFiber.nested_of_pruned`). Severity: local error in the mandate's phrasing; the sources say "nested fiber" and are right. Fix in the mandate: "under `∀ d ∈ U, ¬ NestedFiber B d` (or `¬ Nested` on pruned trees)".

**(ii) The object.** ZO-5(b)'s "equal as matrices under the row bijection and the column identity iff `E' = E_B`" is a statement about the *structural* identification of copies — each copied chance node reads its original's coordinate whether or not any run consults it — so that the identified relocated frame's columns are indexed by `∏_{E'}`. Any rendering of the identified frame that fixes the diagonal profile only on run-consulted coordinates (this package's `diag`, a `Classical.choose` of `exists_diag`'s run specification; and, by the same token, any definition of `Fr_κ` through "profiles that give the same runs") has a kernel that is `U`-independent — `diag U B ε = diag U B ε' ↔ ∀ π, runLeaf π B ε = runLeaf π B ε'` (`diag_eq_iff`) — and cannot see `E' ≠ E_B` at all: it is already non-injective on a chance-only tree with `U = ∅` (`not_injective_diag_twoCoins`). Audit r3 adversarial N1's proposed rendering `(∀ d ∈ U, ¬ Nested B d) → Function.Injective (diag U B)` is therefore false (that probe refutes it), and the statement the sources make needs the structural diagonal (`diagS`, `diagStruct`, `FrIdentS`). Severity for the sources: imprecision — cf-frontier CFF-A's "copies inherit their original's variable" is the structural reading and is the one under which (b) is true; UN-7 (`≃ᵇ`) holds for both (`mapWorlds_frIdent_biextEquiv_fr`, `mapWorlds_frIdentS_biextEquiv_fr`).
