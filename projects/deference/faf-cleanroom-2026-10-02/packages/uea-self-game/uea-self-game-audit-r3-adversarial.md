### B1. `inclusion_open` is false: a game with a pure Herrmann fixed point and no pure extension fixed point

**Declaration.** `Cleanroom.Uea.UeaSelfGame.Game.inclusion_open` (`Open.lean:44`):

```
theorem inclusion_open (h : ∃ π, G.IsPureFP π) : ∃ π, G.IsFPext (pureMix π) := by sorry
```

Carried as open in: ledger row 41 (Kind OPEN, Status `open (content |S| ≥ 2 …; the r2 auditor's exhaustive 2 × 2 grid search found no counterexample)`); `uea-self-game-open.txt` line 1; `Open.lean`'s header and the docstring; the root module's `Open` bullet; `FlooredBridge.lean`'s header and `exists_isFPext_pureMix_fin1`'s docstring ("`inclusion_open`'s content is `|S| ≥ 2`"); report target 8(e), target 13 line, "What was not done", repair-round-2 row A-N4, and the continuation's "What remains open"; findings F18's last sentence. The mandate's 8(e) asked: *"attempt; state OPEN with the exact `Prop` if it resists"*.

**What's wrong.** The statement is refutable, not open. Probe `InclusionRefuted.lean` (elaborates) builds the game `G` on `S = Fin 2`, `A = Fin 3` (actions `a, b, c`): `U(ab) = U(bc) = 1`, `U(cc) = 1/2`, every other `U = 0`, `π* = ab`, `Po = (1/4) δ_{ab} + (1/2) δ_{bb} + (1/4) δ_{bc}`, `δ = 3/4`, and proves:

- `isPureFP_ac : G.IsPureFP ![0, 2]` — the policy `ac` is a pure Herrmann fixed point, strict at both situations (`strict_ac`). Under `μ_{ac}` the own action's conditional is `3/7` at `s₀` and at `s₁` (`cond_ac_00`, `cond_ac_12`: the fibre is `{ac, ab}` resp. `{ac, bc}`, weights `1/4` and `3/16`, utilities `0` and `1`); the available deviation `b` has the pure `Po`-conditional `1/3` at both (`cond_ac_01`, `cond_ac_11`); the third action is **unavailable** at both — `c` at `s₀`, `a` at `s₁` — because no `Po`-policy plays it there (`pa_02 = 0`, `pa_10 = 0`; `not_avail_ac_02`, `not_avail_ac_10`). And `U(ac) = 0` (`U_ac`): the only pure Herrmann fixed point of the game is its worst policy.
- `no_pure_ext_fp : ∀ π, ¬ G.IsFPext (pureMix π)` — all nine pure policies, by a failing `(s, b)` each. `ac` fails only through the unavailable `c` at `s₀`, whose extended conditional is `Ucoord (δ_{ac}) s₀ c = U(cc) = 1/2 > 3/7` (`not_ext_unavail`, via `Fext_eq_Ucoord_of_pa_eq_zero` and `Ucoord_pureMix`). Every other policy loses to an *available* action whose `Po`-conditional is `1` — `a` at `s₀` for the six policies with `π(s₀) ≠ a` (their own conditional is at most `7/13`), `c` at `s₁` for `ab` (`1 > 7/13`) — or `1/3 > 0` (`b` at `s₁` for `aa`); these are Herrmann failures, which the extension inherits (`not_ext_avail`, via `Fext_pureMix`).
- `inclusion_conjecture_refuted : ∃ G : Game (Fin 2) (Fin 3), G.δ = 3/4 ∧ (∃ π, G.IsPureFP π) ∧ ∀ π, ¬ G.IsFPext (pureMix π)`, and `not_inclusion`, the negation of `inclusion_open`'s universal form over `Type`.

The data are the definitions of record only (`Game`, `muSelf`, `cond`, `avail`, `IsArgmaxH`, `IsPureFP`, `Fext`, `IsFPext`, `pureMix`), evaluated through the package's own `condDen_fin2`/`condNum_fin2`/`condDen_muSelf`/`condNum_muSelf`; no convention is bent. The instance has unavailable actions by necessity: where every action is available the two fixed-point notions coincide at a pure policy (`Fext_pureMix`), so a counterexample must use the one place they differ, and `|A| = 3` supplies it on two situations (`|S| = 3`, `|A| = 2` also does — see below).

**How it was found, and how rare it is.** An exact-integer random search over the definitions of `Defs.lean` (pure policies only; scratch script, not committed; `U` on a `1/8` grid, `Po` on one to four random policies with small integer weights, `δ` from a list): at `|S| = |A| = 2`, **2 200 000** instances and no counterexample (consistent with the round-2 auditor's exhaustive `24 960`-instance grid); at `|S| = 2, |A| = 3` three counterexamples in `7 720` instances; at `|S| = 3, |A| = 2` — the sources' sampled shape — three in `27 678`; at `|S| = |A| = 3` three in `5 121`; at `|S| = 2, |A| = 4` three in `4 232` (each run stopped at its third find). So under random rational data the event has probability of order `10⁻³`–`10⁻⁴`, which the sources' `1 500` and `4 × 2 000` samples (`uea-2-inventory` 2-014, 2-015) miss with high probability; the conjecture was never more than sampled evidence, as 2-014's own "Source status" line says ("the (2) inclusion is proved in prose in uea-029 and here only sampled" — the prose proof is of `FP_ext ⊆ FP_Herr`, the direction that holds, `IsFPext.isFPherr`). The probe's instance was hand-simplified from a search hit and re-verified exactly before being written in Lean; for these data `δ` may be anything in `[2/3, 4/5)` (at `δ = 2/3` the fixed point `ac` is held by ties, at `3/4` it is strict), and the search finds counterexamples with `δ` as small as `1/4` in other shapes.

**Why it is blocking.** [STANDARDS](../../STANDARDS.md) §2 makes the open list a record of precise statements that are conjectures or unproved steps, and §5 says a false claim is to be refuted in Lean; `run/prompts/audit.md` lists "a ledger row that misstates status" as blocking, and round 2's B1 applied exactly that to `sup_limit_open`. **Fix I'd accept.** (i) Remove `inclusion_open` from `Open.lean` and the open list; land the probe's instance as a library module (e.g. `Inclusion.lean`, or beside the `|S| = 1` convention witness in `Herrmann.lean`) with the refutation theorem under a name that says what it is (`inclusion_conjecture_refuted`), Kind P / N+, Fidelity exact, Source 2-014/2-015 and mandate 8(e).
