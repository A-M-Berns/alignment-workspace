## F2. The §5.5 "obstruction" paragraph is wrong (local error; §5.5)

*"The obstruction to reaching δ/(1−δ): that needs pV_o → 1, i.e. P_o concentrated on optimal policies agreeing with π at s₀; but at any situation where π deviates from those policies their action must be made unattractive by polluting P_o-mass, which costs either V_o … or p …; the second option gives T1."* The verdict stands — the `T3` instances exceed `δ/(1−δ+δ²)` (F1) — but the error is in the clause "the second option gives T1", not in the dichotomy. In `T3(n, θ, g)` the deviation `b` at `s_i` (`i ≥ 1`) has conditional `w/(w+θ)`: its fibre is the optimal `ρ_i` (weight `w = (1−θ)/(n−1)`, utility `1`) and the polluter `bⁿ` (weight `θ`, utility `0`); at `θ = 0` it equals `1 > cond(a)` and `aⁿ` is *not* a fixed point. So the deviation is made unattractive precisely by pollution, and the polluter `bⁿ` disagrees with `π` at `s₀` — it *is* the note's "second option", costing `p` (`p = 1 − θ`). The Lean's `T3.cond_11 = 9/11 = (9/20)/(9/20 + 1/10)` is this ratio at `n = 3`, `θ = 1/10`. The note's actual error is to assume the polluter's weight must be of order `δ` (as in T1, where `θ = δ/(1−δ+δ²)`): one polluter of weight `θ → 0` serves all `n − 1` deviations at once, because each deviation's own `Po`-weight `w` also vanishes as `n` grows, so `w/(w+θ)` is driven below `cond(a)` at a cost `p V_o = 1 − θ → 1` in the own-situation conditional. Lean: the `T3` iff-conditions of F1. (Repair round 1 replaced this entry's mechanism sentences, which had wrongly credited the self-mass on `aⁿ` rather than pollution — the round-1 fidelity audit, B3.)

## F19.
