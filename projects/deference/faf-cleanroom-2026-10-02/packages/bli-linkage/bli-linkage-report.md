### Disagreements, and how they were settled

There is **no case where one attempt proves what the other refutes**. The material differences are definitional, and three of them matter:

1. **Which faith predicate.** A states K2 over `E2xσIdx` because it proved `E2xσ` unsatisfiable over every list-table B2 system (F-A1); B states it over `E2xσ` and inhabits that with a system whose `val` is a conditional probability, not a list table (`wS`). Both are right about their own objects. The reconciliation re-proved A's refutation over B's coherence (`InstanceB2.e2xσ_unsat_of_unlisted_tautology`) and applied it to B's B2 instance: **B's `d_nnucell_B2` is vacuous for every `rep`**, which is stronger than B's own flag (FB-4: `witnessRep` only, and "no two-state inhabitant" for `rep01`). Settled: `E2xσIdx` is the faith predicate of record for list-table instances; the abstract theorem of record is stated over it; the `E2xσ` form is a corollary.

2. **Which coherence predicate.** A's `PCPσ` adds a partition clause to the mixture worlds (because the mandate's K1 sketch uses `C.excl` inside a stage mixture, where it is unavailable); B keeps bli-found's `CoherentOn` and adds the grid condition `SpuriousEntails`. The two K1/K2 theorems are incomparable (FR-5). Settled in B's favour for the record — the mandate's `PCPσ` is kept verbatim, the condition sits on instance data (discharged for `fixedStates`, true of every full product grid), and the conversion is definitional only in B's direction — with A's theorem restated through the bridge as `determination_partition` and its partition clause counted as the package's one definitional `(c)`, confined to that row.

3. **The B2 witness.** A left the two-state witness over `paperDP 𝗜𝚺₁` unbuilt (the stage check); B proved it impossible for `witnessRep` and said, in prose, that with `rep01` only `tbl 0 1` can be charged. The reconciliation proved the latter (`charged_eq_tbl01`) and the `witnessRep` version at `E2xσIdx` (`fixedSystem_witnessRep_unsat_idx`). Settled: on `[⌜⊥⌝, ⌜⊤⌝]` the B2 package admits at most a point mass, so judged item 1's "N+" is met as N− on this index, with the N+ living in B's abstract witness over an uncertain coordinate; this is written in the ledger's Witness column.

Two further differences are matters of packaging, not substance: K3's conclusion (both diagnosed the metering gap; B repaired it — B's is of record, A's flagged), and K4a's hypothesis shape (`Tabular`/`E2xσIdxFull` versus `b2StateSystem`/one-day faith — both in).
