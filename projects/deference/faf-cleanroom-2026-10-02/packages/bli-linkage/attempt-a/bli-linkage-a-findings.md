### F-A1. Full-scope faith `E2xσ` is unsatisfiable over any list-table B2 system under coherence (local error in the mandate's K2/K4c plan; a structural fact about `b2StateSystem`)

**Pointer.** `bli-found` `Constraints.E2xσ` (scope `Sminus m m`), `StateSentence.b2StateSystem` (`val m q φ = 0` for unlisted `φ`); mandate § K2 ("`E2xσ (stateOf C) S P`"), § K4c ("a `P` over the B2 `fixedSystem` satisfying `PCPσTheory ∧ E5σ ∧ E2xσ`"). Lean: `B2.e2xσ_unsat_of_unlisted_tautology` (P), `B2.fixedSystem01_e2xσ_unsat` (P).

**What is true.** The scope `Sminus m m` contains, from `m = 2`, the tautology `⊤ ⋏ ⊤` (size `10 ≤ sizeBound 2 = 16`, no atoms), which no finite table over `[⌜⊥⌝, ⌜⊤⌝]` lists, so its value in every candidate is the junk `0`. Faith at it reads `P n (⊤ ⋏ ⊤ ⋏ σ_q) = 0 · P n σ_q = 0`, while any partition-respecting mixture gives `P n (⊤ ⋏ ⊤ ⋏ σ_q) = P n σ_q`; with partition mass one some `σ_q` is charged — contradiction. So over `fixedSystem01` (and over every `b2StateSystem` with a finite index), `PCPσ ∧ E5σ ∧ E2xσ` is false from day `n = 1`, and the mandate's K2 determination theorem *as stated with `E2xσ`* has an empty hypothesis package at B2 (it is still a theorem: `determination_e2xσ`). The same holds for the `Sminus`-scoped K4c witness the mandate asks for. **Surviving neighbour:** faith on the written-out coordinates, `E2xσIdx` (`Defs.lean`), which is what K2 reads and what the witnesses inhabit (`Trilemma.sharp`, `T3_…`). This is the B2 face of bli-found F-2/F-12 (constraint 2 conditions a written-out table on sentences the table does not price): a table that is a finite list cannot be faithful on a scope larger than its index; a table over *all* of `smallSet m` with coherent values could (bli-trajectory's `cohSystem` has `val` a world mixture), but a *rounded* table is not coherent (bli-finite `roundTo_not_coherent`), so exact faith over the whole scope is out of reach for B2 tables anyway. Severity: **local error in the mandate's plan** (K2's hypothesis shape at B2, K4c's scoped witness); the program's claim survives at the index-restricted scope.

### F-A2. `bli-found`'s `witnessRep = (1/4, 3/4)` cannot carry faith even on the index (local error in the mandate's K1/K4c witness plan)

**Pointer.** `bli-found` `StateSentence.witnessRep`, `Grid.fixedSystem`; mandate § K1 (witness: `Grid.fixedSystem`), § K4c ("faith at `⊥`/`⊤` is `0`/`1` and consistent"). Lean: `B2.witnessRep_faith_unsat` (P).

**What is true.** `⊥ ∈ Sminus m m` always and `⊥` is listed, with `val q ⊥ ∈ {1/4, 3/4}` for every fixed table; coherence gives `P n (⊥ ⋏ σ_q) = 0`, so faith at `⊥` forces `P n σ_q = 0` for every `q`, against mass one. The mandate's K4c sentence presumes the representatives `0`, `1` at the decided coordinates; `bli-found`'s representatives were chosen to lie strictly inside the open cells `(-1, 1/2)`, `(1/4, 2)` for `LNKcell`, which `0` and `1` also do (`b2StateSystem_val_mem_cellOfTable` at `rep01`). **Surviving neighbour:** `fixedSystem01` with `rep01 = (0, 1)` (`InstanceB2.lean`), which is `Tabular` and carries K1/K2. Severity: local error in the mandate's witness plan (K1's witness itself, which needs no faith, is unaffected).

### F-A3. The mandate's K3 instance plan has a gap of its own: the move-sentence family is price-dependent, so `MachineSentenceCodes` is not "from `χ`'s" (imprecision in the mandate § K3)

**Pointer.** mandate § K3 ("`ψ n := ∼ lit (n+1) (χ n) (todayIdx n)` … a single negated atom, so `MachineSentenceCodes` follows from `χ`'s"); `Degenerate.moveSentence`, `no_degenerate_linked_bli` (hypothesis `hψ`).

**What is true.** `todayIdx n = round n (Q n (χ n))` reads the day-`n` price. FAF's `MachineSentenceCodes` is a *syntactic* emitter class (the sentence codes must be machine-written from the unary day; prices enter a trader only through feature coefficients), so the family `n ↦ ∼ cellSentence (n+1) ⌜χ n⌝ (todayIdx n)` is not certified by the atom lemma — its index is not a machine-written function of `n`. Two honest routes: (i) take `hψ` as a hypothesis (done: `no_degenerate_linked_bli`); (ii) a trader on the *fixed-cell* move sentences `∼ lit (n+1) c r₀` for each `r₀` with the continuous coefficient `1 − price_n(∼lit)` (certifiable as bli-leak's `leakTrader_ec` is), which under the degenerate package loses nothing (the coefficient vanishes on the days today's cell is not `r₀`) and gains one on each day the price leaves cell `r₀`; by pigeonhole over the finitely many cells some `r₀` is left infinitely often when the price moves infinitely often. Route (ii) was not built. Severity: imprecision in the mandate (the theorem's shape survives with `hψ` explicit; the instance needs the two-literal trader).

### F-A4. The trilemma's "drop coherence" horn is about the base's coherence, and the mandate's T2 shape is inconsistent with its own non-vacuity requirement (imprecision in `bli-program` §3.6(ii) / mandate § K2)

**Pointer.** mandate § K2 (T2: "the σ-parametric degenerate `P` defined by cases … small `ψ ↦ Q n ψ`"; "Each witness: ≥ 2 states charged"). Lean: `Trilemma.no_T2_over_coherent_base` (C), `T2_agreeing_faithful_not_coherent` (N+).

**What is true.** (i) A point-mass `P` charges one state, so the mandate's T2 cannot meet its "≥ 2 states charged" clause. (ii) When the base `Q` is itself a partition-respecting world mixture and the day-`(n+1)` linked states are small on day `n` — true on every pinned day of a two-cell instance over a short index — `E1x` transfers `Q`'s values to the state algebra and `E1x ∧ E2xσIdx ∧ E5σ` already force `D_NNUcell` without any coherence assumption on `P` (`no_T2_over_coherent_base`). So the horn "`P` incoherent" over a coherent base is empty where it matters; what the trilemma's third horn really says is that the *base* must be incoherent on its own linked next-state beliefs. The honest T2 is over an incoherent base (`incoherentP` is its own base). Severity: imprecision in the program's statement of the trilemma (the trilemma stands with the horn relocated).

### F-A5. `PCPσ` needs the cell partition in its worlds, and completed-theory coherence collapses the superbelief (imprecision in the mandate § Definitions)

**Pointer.** mandate § Definitions (`PCPσ`: "`CoherentOn (DP.D n) … (P n)`"), § K1 ("`C.excl` forbids any other literal"); `Defs.CellFamily.Partitions`, `CoherentOnCell`, `Forced.theory_coherent_point_mass`.

**What is true.** The stage `D n` of `paperDP T` does not contain the exclusivity/exhaustiveness of day-`(n+1)` cell literals (they enter a stage only after the day-`(n+1)` prices exist), so a mixture of bare stage-consistent worlds can hold a state `σ_q` and a second literal of the same coordinate, and K1 fails for a reason unrelated to linkage. The mandate's proof sketch uses `C.excl`, which is a completed-theory fact; the faithful definition adds the partition clause to the mixture worlds (`CoherentOnCell`). At the other end, completed-theory coherence (`PCPσTheory`) with `E5σ` forces `P n σ_actual = 1` — the superbelief is the point mass on the realized next state — so K1/K2 would be vacuous there; the mandate anticipates this for the witness and it holds generally. Severity: imprecision (filled by deviation 2 of `Defs.lean`).
