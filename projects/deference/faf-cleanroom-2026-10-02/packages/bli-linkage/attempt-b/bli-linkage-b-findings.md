## FB-1. The trilemma's survival is a property of what the state sentence entails, not of the linkage predicate (imprecision in the mandate's framing of angle B; the decisive question answered)

**Pointer.** Mandate § Attempt angles (B): "look for the weaker linkage under which the trilemma dissolves"; `bli-program` §3.6(ii); `bli-program-desiderata` I6. Lean: `Determination.determination_stage`, `d_nnucell_of_package`; `Dissolve.dissolution`.

**What is true.** For the B2 state sentence (the conjunction of its cell literals, `stateOf`), the exact determination `P n (lit_r) = cellMass_r` is *propositional*: it follows from stage-level coherence and the partition constraint alone, over any grid closed under single-entry changes (`SpuriousEntails`), with no linkage predicate, no interval semantics and no fact about the stage. Imposing the interval linkage `LNKcell` on top changes nothing: its brackets (`Bracket.bracket_lower_day`/`upper_day`) are consequences of the same conjuncts. So no choice of linkage predicate dissolves the trilemma *for B2*. What does dissolve it is a different **state sentence**: the interval state `intervalState` (the conjunction of the open-cell quotes), linked to the quotes propositionally and to the cell literals not at all — then the full package `LNKcell ∧ PCPσTheory ∧ PCPσLinked ∧ E5σ ∧ E1x ∧ E2xσ` is satisfiable with `D_NNUcell` false (`Dissolve.dissolution`, N+). The mandate's phrase "the trilemma dissolves under interval linkage into an LP-feasibility condition" is therefore right about an *interval-state* BLI design and wrong as a statement about B2 under `LNKcell`. **Severity:** imprecision in the mandate's angle text (the question was well-posed; its answer splits by state sentence). The program (`bli-program` §3.6) does not make the error: it ties linkage to the cell literals.

## FB-2. The mandate's K1 route uses completed-theory exclusivity inside a stage mixture (local error in the mandate's proof sketch; the theorem stands by another route)

**Pointer.** Mandate K1: "in every charged world exactly one state holds … that state's conjunct gives the literal with the state's own index, and `C.excl` forbids any other literal; sum", with `PCPσ` the **stage** form and `C.excl` quantified over `v.ConsistentWithTheory DP`. **What is true.** A world of a stage mixture is consistent with `DP.D n`, not with the completed theory, and for `n` below the literals' decision day the day-`(n+1)` cell literals are free atoms in it; `C.excl` does not apply. The sketch therefore proves K1 only at the theory level — where `E1x` makes it degenerate (FB-3). At the stage level the missing step is supplied not by exclusivity of literals but by the **grid**: a charged world holding a candidate `q` and a spurious literal `lit_{c,r}` with `r ≠ q(c)` holds, by `SpuriousEntails`, a second candidate, contradicting exclusivity of *states* (which `E5σ` does give). Full product grids have the property; `Grid.fixedStates` has it (`InstanceB2.fixedStates_spuriousEntails`). Over a grid *without* it (a candidate missing), the stage-level determination can fail by a spurious literal — not formalized; the shape is in the handoff. **Severity:** local error in the mandate's sketch; no source claim affected; the theorem of record is stronger than sketched (no theory fact).

## FB-3. Theory-level coherence plus small agreement forces the base to have already decided tomorrow's pinned quotes (finding about the formulation; the theory-level package is degenerate)

**Pointer.** Mandate § Definitions (`PCPσTheory`), K4 ("coherence must be relative to the completed theory"); Lean `Bracket.theory_coherent_decides`, `theory_coherent_e1x_decides`.

**What is true.** For any `IntervalFamily` (any quote family reflected to a fixed price in every completed-theory world — FAF's `quoteAt T` is one), a mixture of completed-theory worlds prices every quote at `1` (price strictly inside) or `0` (strictly outside). With `E1x`, so does the base, on every pinned next-day quote. An inductor uncertain about any small next-day quote cannot satisfy `PCPσTheory ∧ E1x`. The same holds for the B2 cell literals (reflected by `cellSentence_reflected`), and `Grid.fixedStates_decided` is the state-level form: at the theory level exactly one candidate is charged. So every theory-level theorem of the package (`determination_theory`, `FaithB2.liar_full_faith_inconsistent_B2`) is about a point-mass superbelief over a base that knows tomorrow's rounded prices. The stage-level forms (`PCPσ`, `PCPσLinked`) are the ones with content. **Severity:** imprecision in the mandate's K4 (which prescribes the theory level without saying it is degenerate) and in the plan's use of `PCPσTheory`; no source claim affected.

## FB-4. `bli-found`'s witness representatives admit no faithful coherent partition: faith at `⊥` pins charged values at `⊥` to `0` (local error in the mandate's N+ prescription; a design finding about the witness grid)

**Pointer.** Mandate K1 ("Witness (N+): `Grid.fixedSystem` at `𝗜𝚺₁` … with a `P` that is a mixture of ≥ 2 … worlds charging ≥ 2 distinct tables"), K4c ("a `P` over the B2 `fixedSystem` satisfying `PCPσTheory ∧ E5σ ∧ E2xσ`"); `bli-found` `witnessRep` (`1/4`, `3/4`), `fixedSystem`; `Constraints.E2xσ` (scope `Sminus m m ∋ ⊥`). Lean: `Faith.faith_at_falsum`, `faith_at_verum`, `package_unsat_of_val_falsum_ne_zero`, `InstanceB2.fixedSystem_witnessRep_unsat`.

**What is true.** `⊥ ∈ Sminus m m` for every `m` and holds in no world, so under *any* coherence (stage or theory) faith at `⊥` gives `0 = val_q(⊥) · P n σ_q`: every charged candidate values `⊥` at `0`. The B2 system values a listed `⊥` at the representative of the cell the table assigns it; with `witnessRep` that is `1/4` or `3/4`, never `0`, so no candidate can be charged and `E5σ`'s mass-one fails: **`PCPσ ∧ E5σ ∧ E2xσ` is unsatisfiable over `fixedSystem`, for every `P`**. With representatives `0`/`1` the same mechanism at `⊤` (`⊤ ∈ Sminus m m` for `m ≥ 1`, holds everywhere) pins the charged candidate's entry at `⊤` to the cell with representative `1`, so on the index `[⌜⊥⌝, ⌜⊤⌝]` the charged candidate is the single table `tbl 0 1`: no two-state superbelief exists on this index at all. Consequences: (i) `InstanceB2.d_nnucell_B2` is vacuous at `witnessRep` (stated in its docstring; it is kept, over a general `rep`, as the instantiation of record); (ii) the mandate's K1/K4c witnesses cannot be met as prescribed; (iii) a non-degenerate witness needs an *uncertain* coordinate in the index — which is what `Witness.lean` and `Dissolve.lean` use (`atom 0`). The deeper point for the program: decided coordinates (`⊥`, `⊤`, any theorem or refutation of the theory) are not merely harmless entries of a written-out table — faith *pins* the superbelief's charged states at them, and a grid that lists them with the wrong representative is empty of solutions. **Severity:** local error in the mandate (the N+ prescription); design finding about `bli-found`'s T7 witness (its `LNKcell_witness`/`fixedStates_decided` are unaffected — they are about linkage and decision, not about faith).

## FB-6. Under interval linkage the "moves" clause of K3 strengthens to "clears the open neighbourhood" (imprecision in the mandate's angle text, resolved)

**Pointer.** Mandate § Attempt angles (B): "'moves' must now clear the neighbourhood, which is a weaker hypothesis to discharge — say whether `hmove` changes". Lean: `Degenerate.degenerate_quote_zero_day`, `no_degenerate_linked_bli_interval`.

**What is true.** With the degenerate superbelief, every charged world holds the state of `deg n` and hence the quote of the *open* cell it assigns `χ n`; so a quote of an interval `J` whose closed interval misses that closed cell is priced `0`, and the trader buys it. The move that pays is "the next-day price lands in `J`", i.e. **clears the open neighbourhood** of today's cell — a *stronger* requirement on the price than crossing the rounding boundary (the neighbourhood strictly contains the cell), hence a **weaker theorem** for the same `hmove` wording; the mandate's "weaker hypothesis to discharge" has the direction backwards: it is a weaker *conclusion-side* condition (fewer days count as moves), so harder to discharge, not easier. `hmove` changes accordingly in `no_degenerate_linked_bli_interval`. The literal form (`no_degenerate_linked_bli`) keeps the mandate's "crosses the rounding boundary". No partition hypothesis is needed in the interval form (the point mass alone forces the quote). **Severity:** imprecision (direction of a comparison) in the mandate's angle text.

## FB-7. K4a survives interval linkage at `halfRound`; the straddling of `p` is irrelevant (mandate's angle question answered; imprecision in the question's premise)

**Pointer.** Mandate § Attempt angles (B): "the open cell around ½ straddles `p = ½`, so the liar's contradiction needs the forced neighbourhood on one side of `p` — determine whether K4a survives for `halfRound`". Lean: `Faith.charged_cell_contains_truth`, `faith_full_scope_inconsistent`, `straddle`, `halfRound_cell_excludes_truth`, `FaithB2.liar_full_faith_inconsistent_B2`.

**What is true.** The contradiction of K4a is driven by the liar's *price-reflection* in completed-theory worlds (`L m` holds iff `𝑸_m(L m) < p`), which pins the charged candidate's value at `L m` to the truth value; the value also lies in the open cell the candidate assigns `L m` (the representative). The forced interval *quote* plays no role, so whether it straddles `p` is immaterial. What matters is whether the cell of the actual price excludes the truth value: for `halfRound` at `p = 1/2`, a price below `1/2` rounds to `(-1, 1/2) ∌ 1` and a price at or above rounds to `(1/4, 2) ∌ 0` — always excluded, so K4a holds (`liar_full_faith_inconsistent_B2`). The straddle (`straddle`: the cell of `p` always contains `p`, for any legitimate rounding) only shows that the *quote* cannot decide the liar — true, and irrelevant.
