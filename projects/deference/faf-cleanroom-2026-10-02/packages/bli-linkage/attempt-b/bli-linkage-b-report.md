## The answer to angle B's decisive question (headline, with its register)

**Proved, at the stage level, over grids closed under single-entry changes:** the trilemma survives interval linkage *for the B2 state sentence*, and it does so for a reason stronger than the mandate's sketch — the exact determination of the marginals is **propositional**. `Determination.determination_stage` derives `P n (lit_{n+1,φ,r}) = cellMass_r` on every pinned coordinate from stage-relative coherence (`PCPσ`) and the partition constraint (`E5σ`) alone, with no linkage hypothesis, no interval semantics and no fact about what the stage has decided. The only grid property it needs is `SpuriousEntails` (a candidate together with a cell literal it does not list entails another candidate), which every full product grid has. With `E1x` and `E2xσ` this gives `D_NNUcell` (`d_nnucell_of_package`) and the trilemma's horn (0) (`trilemma_T0`). The mandate's K1 route (theory-level `C.excl` inside a *stage* mixture) does not type-check as sketched — `C.excl` speaks of completed-theory worlds, the mixture's worlds are stage worlds — and `determination_theory` is the theory-level version it does support.

**Proved, as an explicit N+ witness:** where interval linkage is the *only* link — the state sentence is the conjunction of the open-cell interval quotes (`intervalState`) and the cell literals are atoms it does not entail — the full linked package `LNKcell ∧ PCPσTheory ∧ PCPσLinked ∧ E5σ ∧ E1x ∧ E2xσ` is satisfiable with the pinned set eventually non-empty and `D_NNUcell` false (`Dissolve.dissolution`). Two candidates with distinct tables, each of mass `1/2`, two cells, the base at `1/2` on the coordinate.

**So (register: proved in Lean, abstract objects):** whether the exact-D-NNU trilemma survives depends on whether the state sentence entails the cell literals, not on which linkage is imposed. Under B2 the interval brackets of `Bracket.lean` are *consequences* of the cell-literal conjuncts, not an alternative linkage; under an interval-only design the forced finite-time condition is the bracket LP (`bracket_lower_day`/`bracket_upper_day`/`bracket_balance`), an inequality system in place of the identity.

**A finding about the formulation (proved):** `theory_coherent_e1x_decides` — theory-level coherence (`PCPσTheory`) together with `E1x` forces the base to price every pinned next-day quote at `0` or `1` according to tomorrow's price, for any `IntervalFamily` (any quote family reflected to a fixed price, as FAF's is). So the theory-level package is degenerate over any base uncertain about a small next-day quote, and the stage-level forms (`PCPσ`, `PCPσLinked`) are the ones of record. The dissolution witness escapes this only because its quote atoms are not reflected to a price — which is what "interval linkage alone" means.
