## 3. Non-blocking issues

1. **`CrossCoherentVia`'s anticipation clause does not see `Xj`, and the docstring's "cross-ontology counterpart of `CoherentVia`" oversells by that much.** `CrossCoherentVia ci Xi Xj as := (∃ m : CondModel Xi Xj, Compatible m ci) ∧ BridgeCalibrated as ci`, and `BridgeCalibrated as ci` is a statement about `Xi`, `as`, `ci` only (its type does not mention `Xj`). Probe A (`crossCoherentVia_indep_Xj`, elaborated): a witness `as` for `(Xi, Xj)` is a witness for every `Xj′` satisfying clause (1). This is faithful to SC Def 12.2's *words* — clause (2) says "an anticipation structure on `X̂ᵢ` targeting `X̂ⱼ`", the *space*, and never mentions the measure `Xⱼ` — but not to SC's gloss one line later ("Condition (2) ensures `dᵢ` anticipated `dⱼ`'s beliefs") nor to §14's "one's beliefs are a calibrated update of the other's". (a) This is a further imprecision in SC §12.2 that F14 does not name: fixing `(Ā, κ)` removes the existential collapse but still leaves `Xⱼ` unrelated to `κ`; file it (F15, imprecision, §12.2).
