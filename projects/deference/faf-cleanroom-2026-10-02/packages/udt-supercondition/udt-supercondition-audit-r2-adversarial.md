### 3.1 SC Def 12.2's anticipation clause never mentions `Xⱼ`; `CrossCoherentVia` is not "the cross-ontology counterpart of `CoherentVia`" (a sharpening of F14; imprecision in SC, and an overselling docstring)

So the clause SC glosses as "ensures `dᵢ` anticipated `dⱼ`'s beliefs" cannot do that even with `(Ā, κ)` fixed: probe `crossCoherentVia_iff_of_bridgeCalibrated` — for `Ĉ`-calibrated `as`, `CrossCoherentVia ci Xi Xj as ↔ ∃ m, Compatible m ci` for **every** `Xj` — shows the fixed-structure variant still depends on `Xⱼ` only through Thm 2.4's condition. By contrast the same-ontology `CoherentVia as Xj` pins `Xⱼ` to an anticipated kernel: probe `coherentVia_eq_kappa : CoherentVia as Xj → ∃ ā₀, Xj = as.κ ā₀`. And `CrossCoherentVia` is strictly weaker than "a compatible model *calibrated w.r.t. `as`* exists": probe `crossCoherentVia_not_calibratedModel` — `CrossCoherentVia ciU unif2 δ₁ asU` holds while no compatible model calibrated w.r.t. `asU` exists (`Q₀ = δ₀`, `δ₁ ≪ δ₀` fails).
- What the package says: `CrossCoherentVia`'s docstring calls it "The cross-ontology counterpart of `CoherentVia`, and the version with content"; F14 says "What survives is the fixed-structure variant `CrossCoherentVia` … (the cross-ontology `CoherentVia`) … a genuine constraint … This is what `corr-reflect-frames` / `udt-endorse-policy` should import". - Fix I would accept: (i) new finding **F15** — Def 12.2 clause (2) does not mention `Xⱼ`, so no reading of Def 12.2 (existential or fixed-structure) says `dⱼ`'s beliefs were anticipated; severity imprecision, blocking for §12.3's intended use of "epistemically coherent" cross-ontology; (ii) ship the Def 12.1-style analogue as a definition of record, e.g. `CrossCoherentAt ci Xi as ā₀ Xj := BridgeCalibrated as ci ∧ 0 < (Xi.map as.a) ā₀ ∧ Xj = as.κ ā₀`, with probe `compatible_of_bridgeCalibrated_kappa` (bridge-calibration plus `Xⱼ = κ_{ā₀}` at a positive atom already yields a compatible model, bound `1/Xᵢ(ā₀)`) showing clause (1) is then a theorem, not a clause; (iii) reword the `CrossCoherentVia` docstring and F14 ("the fixed-structure rendering of SC's words; not the analogue of `CoherentVia`, which is `CrossCoherentAt`").
