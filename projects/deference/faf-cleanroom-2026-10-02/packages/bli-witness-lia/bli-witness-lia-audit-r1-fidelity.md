## 3. Non-blocking issues

### 3.1 "Derived, not typed": the `1/2` is the kernel's stipulated mixing weight, and the forcing that would make it a derivation is not stated

**Where.** `Prior.lean` `stateMass_ask_rec` docstring ("derived from the inductor's kernel … No number is typed in"); report § State ("**derived and not typed**: `μ(Ask) = μ(Rec) = 1/2` … which is the kernel's own decomposition `linkedPrice n = ½·sliceT n + ½·sliceF n`"); the module docstring of `Prior.lean`.

**What the Lean does.** `stateMass_ask_rec` is `stateMass_sistSkel_h0` (the `trajLaw` fiber at horizon one is `(sk.κ n).law t T`) → `segmentSkeleton_law_linked` (on a segment day the skeleton's law at `linkedTable n` is `twoPointLaw n`) → `twoPointLaw_charged`. The last step is `unfold twoPointLaw; rw [if_pos rfl, if_neg hne, …]; norm_num`: it reads `1/2` off the **definition** `twoPointLaw n Q := (if Q = sliceT n then 1/2 else 0) + (if Q = sliceF n then 1/2 else 0)` (`bli-exact-base` `Skeleton.lean:169`), and `segKernel`'s law is **defined** as `if t = linkedTable n then twoPointLaw n else tentLaw 𝓜 n t` (`Skeleton.lean:221`). So the masses are a stipulation of the dependency's kernel. The decomposition theorem `linkedPrice_eq_avg` (the kernel market `kMix` is the `1/(2k)`-uniform mixture over `Fin k × Bool`, `Kernel.lean:527`, so its `Bool` marginal is `1/2` by construction) does not enter the mass derivation at all; it enters T3's conjunct 12 (`Balanced`) through `twoPointLaw_balanced`. The inductor's coin price `1/2` (`coin_price`, from `kMix_fresh`) is likewise the mixture's `Bool` weight.

**Why it matters for U15.** The program's risk column for U15 reads "va: branch beliefs assumed rather than derived". Here they are neither assumed as a hypothesis (good — no headline takes a mass) nor derived from the inductor: both the inductor's price and the skeleton's law carry the same `1/2` because both were built from the same kernel, and the Lean proves the two **coincide** (`coin_price`, `Balanced`) rather than that one **determines** the other. The determination U15 means — §3.6 (ii)'s "branch beliefs from `E2x` and the inductor's small beliefs" — is, on this witness, one line: a probability on the grid supported on the two candidates, balanced at `linkedTable n`, has `μ(sliceT)·1 + μ(sliceF)·0 = linkedTable n (freshCoord)` and `μ(sliceT) + μ(sliceF) = 1`, so `μ(sliceT) = Q_n(freshCoord)`. That lemma is absent, so "the masses are the inductor's price" is currently an identity between two definitions, not a theorem that the inductor's belief forces the masses.

**Fix I would accept.** (i) A lemma in `Prior.lean`, e.g. `masses_forced_by_balance (F : Superbelief smallIndex (n+1)) (hP : IsProb (segmentMesh K).d F) (hsupp : ∀ Q ∈ grid …, Q ≠ sliceT n → Q ≠ sliceF n → F Q = 0) (hB : Balanced (segmentMesh K).d F (linkedTable n)) : F (sliceT n) = linkedTable n ⟨freshCoord, _⟩ ∧ F (sliceF n) = 1 − linkedTable n ⟨freshCoord, _⟩`, instantiated at `((segmentSkeleton H K hK).κ n).law (linkedTable n)` with `.prob`, `stateMass_other_zero` and `.balanced` — then `stateMass_ask_rec` can honestly say "the masses are the unique ones a balanced two-candidate law at the inductor's table can have, and they equal its coin price". (ii) Reword the docstring and report: "no number is typed in *this package*; the `1/2` is the kernel's `Bool` mixing weight (`kMix`, `twoPointLaw`, `bli-exact-base`), which the inductor is re-priced to quote". The ledger row for `stateMass_ask_rec` already describes the proof route accurately and needs no change.
