## 3. Non-blocking issues

1. **F3's realizable-scenario sketch is stated at the wrong level (processes, not traders), and repeats the Lemma-3 conflation that F1 corrects.** F3 (and the docstring of `frozen_divergent_pair_open`, and its `fa-delay-bsi-open.txt` reason, and the T3 (ii) ledger Status cell) say a realizable refutation needs "a family whose truth `H` cannot anticipate at the freeze … while `A`'s process decides it *before* `H`'s does, so that `A` quotes `≈ 1` on exactly the true blocks". Over FAF a process's decisions do not move a market's prices — traders read prices, not `D` — so "`DPA` decides `φ_k` early ⟹ `A` quotes high" does not follow; provability induction lifts `ℙ^A_n(φ_n)` only along an *e.c.* sequence of theorems, and "the true blocks" of a pseudorandom family is not one. The honest necessary conditions are about what *e.c. traders* can compute: (i) the family's truth is not efficiently computable by `H`'s traders at `T_k` (so the frozen credence is pinned low), (ii) it *is* efficiently computable by `A`'s traders at `d_k` (so `A` can advertise), and (iii) `H`'s realized credence rises by the horizon on the advertised blocks — which `A`'s own unbiasedness on the quote gate forces on average, and which `H`'s traders can deliver only through a cheap handle (its own computation becoming affordable within the block, which with BSI's constant block length `4` and polynomial traders is not available, or the ledger quote itself). The mandate's T3 (iii) says exactly this ("on an unpredictable family the forcing is the very thing T1 says the criterion does not supply without a cheap handle") and F3 contradicts it without engaging it. The *status* F3 records (open; sources' scenario not LI-realizable; not attempted) is right and is unaffected; the sketch should be reworded in the four places, and the mandate's warning cited. (I did not grade this blocking because it is a sketch of an unattempted construction, explicitly hedged, and the OPEN statement itself does not depend on it.)

2. **`frozen_divergent_pair_open` Fidelity "exact" overstates in two directions, both already visible in the cell's own parenthesis.** (a) It is stated for *every* admissible triple, where BSI's display (for fixed `t, ε, δ`) is denied by one; (b) its pair is a `OneWayPair`, in which `A` sees none of `H`'s prices — a subclass of BSI's block-stale pairs. A witness of the OPEN refutes BSI's display (the direction F3 needs), but the OPEN's *failure* would not establish the display. Honest cell: `stronger: all admissible triples; A-side staleness stricter than BSI's` — "exact" is the wrong word.

3. **The inclusive schedule clause is vacuous at lookahead `1`** (`ScheduleSat.lean`, `closesByBoundary_succ`): `ClosesByBoundary S succDeferral d` holds for every freeze schedule `S` and every deferral `d`, because `n + 1 ≤ T (blockOf n + 1)` is `lt_T_blockOf_succ`. So for one-day lookaheads `deficit_bound_open` and `frozen_divergent_pair_open` are stated for all window-disjoint schedules of all freeze schedules, and the ledger's "schedule closing by the boundary" restricts nothing in that regime. This is not a defect (BSI's horizon `T_{k+1}` is reached from `T_{k+1} − 1` by exactly this lookahead, and the trader argument does not need the clause when the window is a single day), but the rows should say the clause bites only for lookaheads over a day. The same probe closes round 1's "by inspection" item: the clauses `hclose ∧ hwd` are jointly satisfiable for every `S` (`schedule_clauses_satisfiable`, with `f := succDeferral` and `d k := 2k + 2`, a `DeferralFunction` built with the `UnaryRuler` certificate pattern of `fa-forcing-trader`'s `linearSchedule 0`), and at BSI's schedule the same `d` also satisfies the *strict* clause (`evenSchedule_closesWithinBlocks_bsi`): both readings of BSI's conflicting clauses are inhabited at BSI's constants by one schedule.

4. **E2 is vacuous on every finite day-set** (`GateClosesVacuous.lean`): `gate_closes_of_realized_tendsto_zero` has no clause that `E` fires infinitely often; on `E ≡ EF.const 0` every hypothesis holds (generable, `{0,1}`-valued, and the along-`E` antecedent `hY` for free, since the filter is `⊥`) and the conclusion is a statement in `⊥`. Inherited from `fa-theorem-a`'s `lemmaP`, which has the same shape; recorded so the ledger's "half the composition" is read with "on a day-set that fires infinitely often" understood. A one-line clause `∃ᶠ n, (E n).denote A = 1` in the docstring's Scope line would do.

5. **Deck-TT is falsifiable, and `DeckTrustQuote` does not smuggle the conclusion** (`DeckTTBites.lean`, the dual of round 1's `ExpertZero.lean`): over the paper market, the expert `≡ 1` on the refutable family `V ≡ 𝟙⊥` has the reflecting pair `(𝟙⊥, 𝟙⊤)` at `(v, δ) = (½, ¼)` — every field of `DeckTrustQuote` is met — and `DeckTT` fails for it (`deckTT_overadvertising_false`: `𝔼_n(𝟙⊥) → 0`, `𝔼_n(𝟙⊤) → 1`, by the package's own `expect_tendsto_of_determinedVia_tendsto`). So the definition of record is neither free for every expert nor made true by its own fields; its content is exactly "the expert's advertising is honoured by `H`'s own expectations". Positive finding; worth a sentence in the `DeckTT` row next to the silent-expert remark.

6. **`deckTT_self_luv_open` should say whether it is believed true.** It asks for an *exact* product LUV `V_n · Ind_δ(…)` for every e.c. valued `V`; the docstring records that FAF's `dd:mesh` machinery realizes products only within a vanishing slack. If FAF's LUV syntax cannot express the exact product for some `V`, the universal-over-`V` statement is false and the OPEN is a conjecture, not a gap in plumbing. The Open-list convention elsewhere in the run ("OPEN, possibly false as stated") fits; or state the slack version the deck's inequality actually needs (an approximate product, which `expect_asympEq_of_eventually_values_close` already tolerates) and mark the exact one as the stronger form.
