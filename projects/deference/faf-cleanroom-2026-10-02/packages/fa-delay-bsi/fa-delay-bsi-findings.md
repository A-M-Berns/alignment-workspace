## F1 (T1) — BSI's Theorem B: Lemma 3's generability claim over FAF — the price route is circular (proved); the constant route, which is BSI's actual move, rests on the sources' unformalized hardness claim. Severity: **blocking** for Theorem B's proof *as the sources argue it*; over FAF the refutation's status is "price route closed, FP-hardness step not formalized". Ledger: a `variant` row (plan §0.4 rule 3), not `refuted`. **Rewritten in repair round 1** (audit r1 fidelity B1): an earlier version called the locality lemma "the formal core" of the refutation and said "the generator has no channel" to the deductive state — false over FAF, which admits the constant route below.

**Quoted (BSI §4, lines 71–75).** "**Theorem B.** In `𝓔`, with `(t, ε, δ) = (3/16, 1/32, 1/32)`: both agents satisfy the logical induction criterion; and `∑_n w_n = ∞`. Moreover the divergence is carried by the explicitly stated gate `g_n := Ind_δ(quote_n > 3/16)·Ind_δ(ℙ^H_n(X_{k(n)}) < 5/32)·𝟙[n > m_{k(n)}]`, which is computable from quantities the human sees (`g` is `𝒞^H`-generable), and is not `𝒞^A`-generable". Lemma 3 (line 83): the human-side target `p^H` (`¼` pre-mid, `σ_{k(n)}` post-mid) is generable because "the post-mid branch reads decided atoms, a hard constant for the poly-time generator since the atoms sit in `D_H`'s day-`m_k` output, which the generator at indices `≥ m_k` can afford to read in this construction's bookkeeping".

**The reading formalized.** The class in which Lemma 3 places `p^H` is FAF's `PGenerableRat` (LI Def. 4.3.5, `def:ece`): a rational sequence denoted day by day by an expressible-feature progression (`GeneratedRatFeature`). An expressible feature (`EF`, `Framework/Criterion.lean:99`) is built from price features, rational constants, `+`, `×`, `max`, safe reciprocal and shared `let`; `EF.denote : EF → History → ℝ` takes no deductive process. A generable target therefore has exactly two kinds of input: the market's prices through the day, and the **emitted syntax itself** — in particular constant leaves, which FAF admits for every machine-writable stream: `PGenerableRat.ofMachineRatCodes (hq : MachineRatCodes q) (P) : PGenerableRat P q` through `ratCodeFeature q n := EF.const (q n)` (`Framework/Expectations.lean:157`; restated as `constantRoute_generable`).

**Two routes; what is proved and what is not.**

- *Price route — closed, (a).* `EF.denote_eq_of_agree`: a feature's denotation depends only on prices on days `≤ rank`; `pgenerable_price_local`: a `PGenerableWeighting`'s day-`n` value depends only on prices on days `≤ n`; `generatedRat_price_local`: if `feature` generates `q` at `P`, its day-`n` value on *any* history agreeing with `P` through day `n` is `q n`. So if the feature generating `p^H_n := σ_{k(n)}` reads `σ` off `H`'s prices, then `H`'s prices through day `n` already carry `σ_{k(n)}` — by a margin, when read through a ramp (a ramp of a price denotes `σ` exactly only when the price is beyond a threshold by `δ`; audit r1 adversarial non-blocking 10) — and Theorem 4.4.5 with that target concludes what it assumes. Instance on a real object: `quoteRampAbove_price_local`.
- *Constant route — BSI's actual move, not touched by the lemmas.* Lemma 3 says the post-mid branch emits "a hard constant for the poly-time generator": over FAF that is `ratCodeFeature (σ ∘ k)`, admitted by `PGenerableRat.ofMachineRatCodes`. The locality lemmas are **vacuous** on it — the constant leaf denotes its payload on every history with no agreement hypothesis (`constantRoute_locality_vacuous`, `rfl`; audit probe `audit-r1-probes/ConstantRoute.lean`). The route is open exactly when `n ↦ σ_{k(n)}` is machine-writable: `MachineRatCodes` is three `MachineDigits`, each a `MachineTokenStream`, i.e. emitted by some `F ∈ Complexity.FP` of the unary day (`Framework/Machine/WriteOutMachine.lean:345`). So FAF *does* have the object for the question "can the poly-time generator afford to compute `σ_k`" — and that question is exactly the sources' **hardness claim** (`delay-program` §3, `delay-and-visibility` §3: "there is no primitive for the deductive state … to produce the numeral `σ_k` the generator must compute it, in time poly(n), and `σ_k` is Ackermann-hard"). **Not formalized; ATTRIBUTION-UNVETTED.** Why it is not stated as an OPEN either: BSI's environment `𝓔` is not built (mandate T1 "Traps": "XL, not worth building"), so there is no named `σ` about which to state `¬ MachineRatCodes (σ ∘ k)`; a statement over an arbitrary `σ : ℕ → Bool` is trivially true by cardinality (countably many FP emitters) and trivially false for a constant `σ` — a fake OPEN either way. The only honest form is over a built `𝓔` with its Ackermann-hard atoms, which the mandate ruled out.

**Consequence for Theorem B.** Over FAF, Theorem B's Lemma 3 is **not refuted by anything in the package**: what is proved is that reading `σ` from prices is circular. The refutation's whole force rests on `¬ MachineRatCodes (σ ∘ k)` for BSI's family, which is the sources' argument, and Lemma 4's hypotheses ("the human's price lies within `1/64` of `0` on post-mid refuted days") stand or fall with it (they are supported by Lemma 3 only through 4.4.5 on a generable target). The mandate's T1 paragraph framed the two-horned dichotomy and the formalizer followed it; [STANDARDS](../../STANDARDS.md) wins (mandate Known issue 7).

## F2 (T2) — BSI's case split is at the wrong threshold; the clean split is on ramp values. Severity: **local error** in the proof; result unaffected.

BSI §5 line 98: "either the frozen credence was already below `t − ε/2` — then `w^{frozen} ≥` a fixed fraction of `w` (ramp arithmetic, absorbing `δ`) — or …". **False as a case analysis**: `bsi_split_wrong_threshold` — for every `t`, `ε > 0`, `δ > 0` with `t + δ ≤ 1`, `0 ≤ t − ε − δ`, and every `K ≥ 1`, at `a = 1`, `c = 0`, `F = t − ε/2 − δ/K` the frozen credence is below `t − ε/2`, `w = 1` and `w^{frozen} = 1/K`; at BSI's constants and `K = 100` the ratio is `1/100` (`bsi_split_wrong_threshold_bsi`). `delay-program` T6 and `delay-and-visibility` §5 diagnose this ("it needs the ramp *saturated*, at `t − ε/2 − δ`, not `t − ε/2`") and separately verify the displayed inequality (by hand and on a grid); they do **not** claim that a case split at `t − ε/2 − δ` yields the constant `2/ε`. **Formalizer's observation, not a claim of the sources** (wording corrected repair r1, audit r1 fidelity non-blocking 5): a split at `t − ε/2 − δ` would give only `|c − F| ≥ ε/2 − δ` in the other case, hence `2/(ε − 2δ)` and a `δ < ε/2` requirement. **The split that gives the displayed constant is on the ramp values** `θ := Ind_δ(c < t − ε)` against `η := Ind_δ(F < t − ε/2)`: `violWeight_le_frozen_add_displacement` holds with constant `2/ε` and **no relation between `δ` and `ε`** (the mandate's route, verified: in the case `θ > η`, comparing the ramp arguments gives `F − c > ε/2`). **Strengthening.** `violWeight_le_frozen_mul_live_add_displacement`: the frozen term is needed only on *current-violation* days (`c < t − ε`); this is the quantity the v3 trader can bound, and the shape the OPEN summed bound takes.
