## F5 — The layer-cake bound needs no sign condition on the deferrer (imprecision, in the mandate)

Mandate T3(a) asks for `π ≥ 0`; `layer_cake_bound` is proved without it (the elaborator reported the hypothesis unused): `TotalTrustOn U π F` is a statement about the product sums, and the integral argument never uses the sign of `π`. ## F6 — "`α̂_t` tracks the true `α`" needs identifiability; reflection buys only the martingale (imprecision; Known issues 6)

`christiano` T2 l. 153 ("as conservation of expected evidence says it must for a reflective agent"). Conservation of expected evidence is `alphaHat_martingale`: over one round the expected posterior mean equals the prior mean — a statement about the agent's own future estimate, true for every prior. Tracking the *true* `α` is consistency, which needs the outcome `ω` observed (branch (ii): with the update rule Bayes over the run's `twoPress` likelihood, `likeRound`/`likeHist`, the odds after `n` self-assessed false presses are geometric, `posterior_odds_geometric`, and the estimate crosses the two-state threshold after a least finite `n` iff the sharp sensor does, `alphaHat_crossing`, `exists_crosses_iff`, bridged to `twoState`'s `belowThresholdIneq` by `crosses_iff_not_belowThreshold`); from the compliance record alone two sensors on one fibre of `record` are indistinguishable (`fibre_same_likelihood`; `record_nonidentification`). `anthropic-davis` 18's dichotomy is the right statement.

## F10 — The retention form's gain is construal-dependent: a step for the state mixture, linear for the randomized install (construal-dependent; corr-wf14b-2-016 unaffected)

Mandate T14, second form: "interpolate the installed state between `Q_ow` and `Q_add` by a retention `r ∈ [0, 1]` (a mixture — a modelling choice, disclose (c)) … prove the gain is continuous in `r`, vanishes at `r = 1`, is positive at `r = 0`". Under the construal the mandate prescribed — the install a *mixture of the two states* with hard decisions (continue iff `E[X] > 0`) — the mixture's expectation at the `σ_A = r` cell is `−3/2 + 2r`, so its decision flips at `r = 3/4` (`retention_step`) and the gain is `9/200` for `r ≤ 3/4`, `0` above (`retention_gain`): positive at `0`, zero at `1`, with a jump at `3/4`. Under the other natural construal — a *randomized install*, the additive state with probability `r` and the overwriting one with `1 − r`, decisions still hard (arguably corr-wf14b-2-016's "the fraction of the agent's decision-relevant information the installed state conditions on") — the gain is `(1 − r) · 9/200` (`randomized_gain`, one line from `y1_values`): continuous, positive at `0`, zero at `1`, exactly as the mandate says. So the mandate's sentence is true under one reading of its own "(c)" and false under the other; the first version of this finding called it a local error in the mandate and added that "a continuous gain would need a soft (randomized) decision rule", which is wrong — a randomized *install* suffices (audit round 1, fidelity N2). corr-wf14b-2-016 itself asks for a model of `T` with a retention measure; both constructions are (c)-disclosed stand-ins, and the record should say which it means.
