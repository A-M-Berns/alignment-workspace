LUVs". The corpus's Mart quantifies over e.d. LUVs, which name a unique real (`setting-and-notation` l. 24), i.e. valued sources; the Lean quantifies over more. The column should say `variant: no source-valuedness clause (FAF's cee shape); stronger than Mart over e.d. LUVs`.
