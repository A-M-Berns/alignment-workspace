## F1 — The two clocks: FAF's deferral forces `f n > n`, the corpus's same-day publication is not representable (imprecision; resolved toward FAF)

- **Pointer:** root-deference-001's flag ("v6 §0.4 has two clocks (form-day `n` vs publication `e(n)`)"); `deference-in-logical-induction-v6` §0.4 ("the day index `n` is *when `A` forms the forecast*; the publication stage `e(n)` is *when `H` may read it*", schedules ordered `n ≤ e(n) < F(n) < σ(n)`); FAF `Properties/SelfTrust.lean` `DeferralFunction.lt : ∀ n, n < f n`.
- **What the package does:** `Expert` carries a `DeferralFunction`, and the expert's estimate of `X n` is `(X n).expect E.A (E.f n)` — the *deferred-day* estimate. So the AI instance of every notion reads the expert's day-`f(n)` estimate at the novice's day `n`; the corpus's `e(n) = n` (publish the same day) is excluded by `DeferralFunction.lt`. The self-instance (`Expert.self P DP f`) is exactly FAF's `thm:cee`/`thm:ccee`/`thm:st` shape, so nothing is lost there.
- **Severity:** imprecision. The corpus's Mart display is written at day `n` with `a_n` "the published forecast" although `H` can only read it from `e(n) > n`; this package makes the time shift explicit and forces it strictly. A dependent that needs `e(n) = n` (a same-day quote) must either take `f` with `f n = n` (not a `DeferralFunction`) or reindex, which is `li-quote-lane`'s pull-back lemma (its target (5)), not a definition here.

## F3 — Exact coherence `E*(const s) = s` fails over FAF's grid expectation; the two-option argmax is at the expert's *quote* of the constant, not at `s` (local error at the LUV level, in the mandate's T1 gloss and the corpus's F1/coherence reading; the corpus's own ⚠ 2026-07-25 caveat already flags exact coherence as an idealization — this is its two-option instance)

- **Pointer:** `two-option-value-iff-total-trust` §Setting: "Coherence is used only through `E*(const s) = s`, so that 'the expert takes `X` iff `E*(X) ≥ s`' *is* the argmax rule on this menu"; `expert-conditions` §Coherent and its ⚠ (2026-07-25): "exact coherence is an idealization the instances cannot satisfy"; mandate T1: "coherent = `A` is a single history, so `E*(const s) = s` and argmax is well-defined — no separate coherence predicate is needed: `LUV.expect` is linear in the prices by construction"; FAF `Framework/Expectations.lean` `LUV.expect P n X := X.expectApprox (P n) (n + 1)`, `expectApprox V k X := k⁻¹ · ∑_{i<k} V (X.gt (i/k))`.
- **The fact (checked by reading FAF's definition):** `LUV.expect` is linear in the *prices*, as the mandate says, but the expert's estimate of a LUV `C n` valued `s` in every consistent world is the grid average `(f n + 1)⁻¹ · ∑_{i ≤ f n} A (f n) (C n).gt (i/(f n + 1))` of the expert's prices on the threshold sentences. Even for an inductor whose prices on decided sentences were exactly `0`/`1`, this is `#{i : i/(f n+1) < s} / (f n + 1)`, which is not `s` unless the grid happens to contain `s` (e.g. `s = 1/3` at grid `1/(f n + 1)` gives `⌈(f n+1)/3⌉/(f n+1)`). So `E*(const s) = s` is false at finite days, and true only asymptotically (the grid error `≤ 1/(f n + 1)` and `thm:expprovind`/`thm:ec` for the prices of decided sentences; for a *fixed* LUV `C₀` valued `s`, `LUV.expect_converges` gives `(C₀).expect A m → s` along `m`; for a day-varying `C n` read at `f n` it is a pull-back question, `li-quote-lane`).
- **Consequence for the two-option identity:** on the menu `{X, C}` with `C n` valued `s`, `Menu.argmax` selects `X` iff `E*(C n) ≤ E*(X n)` (`Menu.argmax_two`, `Menu.lean`), i.e. the cut is at the expert's quote `q_n := E*(C n) ≈ s`, not at `s`. The LUV-level bridge from `Value` (T6d, `TwoOptionLUV.lean` `value_twoOption_hardAbove`) is therefore proved **at the threshold `q_n`**: `E^H_n(X_n·1[q_n ≤ E*(X_n)]) − s·E^H_n(1[q_n ≤ E*(X_n)]) ≳ₙ 0`. The fixed-`s` form the corpus and the mandate state (`Value → HardTotalTrustAbove … s` per instance) is reached only under the surrogate hypothesis `∀ n, E*(C n) = s`, disclosed as `(c)` in `value_twoOption_hardAbove_of_weightQuote`. The **finite-exact** identity (T6a, `TwoOptionFinite.lean`) is unaffected: there the expert's estimates `e : W → ℝ` are data and `const s` is valued exactly.
- **Severity:** **mandate** — a local error in the mandate's T1 gloss ("no separate coherence predicate is needed") as far as constants are concerned; **not a correction to a wiki page**: for the corpus it is the two-option instance of an already-flagged imprecision (`expert-conditions`'s ⚠ 2026-07-25). Nothing downstream is blocked: `def-argmax-value` owns the scoped Value predicate and F1's asymptotic form, and the soft form (`SoftTotalTrustAbove`) is untouched because its weight ramps on `E*(X n)` alone.
- **Nearest well-posed versions:** (i) the day-varying-threshold statement, proved (`value_twoOption_hardAbove/Below`); (ii) the fixed-`s` statement under the surrogate reading, proved with `(c)` disclosed; (iii) the asymptotic surrogate: for an *inductor* expert and a *fixed* `C₀` valued `s`, `LUV.expect_converges` gives `E*(C₀) → s`, and for day-varying `C n` the asymptotic form `E*(C n) ≈ₙ s` should follow from uniform provability induction on the grid thresholds — so `hsur` is `(c)` only at the *exact* grade, and its asymptotic form is a (b)/(a) target for `def-argmax-value` or `li-quote-lane`; even with it, an asymptotic fixed-`s` *inequality* needs a band around `s` (the hard indicators at `q_n` and at `s` differ when `E*(X n)` falls between), which is the soft/band machinery — not attempted here (a definitions package). (Audit round 1, fidelity item 10.)

## F4 — FAF's `thm:st` ramps on the future *price* `P_{f(n)}(φ_n)`; the corpus's soft Total Trust ramps on the estimate `E*(X_n)`; for FAF's `LUV.indicatorOf` these differ exactly (imprecision in the FAF-corpus alignment; resolved by the literal indicator)

- **Severity:** imprecision (alignment), resolved. It matters for `def-self-trust`: "the self-case is free" for soft Total Trust holds exactly for sentence sources presented by their literal threshold, and asymptotically (via `thm:ei`) for `indicatorOf`.
