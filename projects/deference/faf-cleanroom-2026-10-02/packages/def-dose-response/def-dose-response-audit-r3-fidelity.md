## 2. Blocking issues

### B1 — F6 is a false finding: the "mesh rounding" is the package's `0/1` table, not the design's; and `hmesh` on every steering day is `v ∈ {0, 1}`

**What F6 claims** (findings §2, F6; repeated in `Advisor.lean`'s header, the docstrings of `steeredAdvisor_quote_exact` / `quoteStream_steeredAdvisor_exact`, and the ledger rows for `steeredAdvisor_quote_exact` and `steeredAdvisor_isLogicalInductor`): "The day-`n` expectation is the `1/(n+1)` mesh (FAF's `def:e`)"; the steered quote is the ceiling `⌈v(n+1)⌉/(n+1)`; "a dyadic `v = ½` is not exact at `n = 2`"; "**For the committed stream to carry `a_n(u) = v` on every steering day, `v`'s denominator must divide `lcm(1, …, N*)`; otherwise (∗) carries a rounding term**"; severity "imprecision" against the note's "steering targets are taken dyadic" (§2.1) and §6.2's "`A_v` … whose prices on the day-`n` quote LUVs for `u`, `n < N*`, are `v` — so the committed stream has `a_n(u) = v` for `n < N*`".

**What is wrong.** FAF's `def:e` mesh is in the *thresholds*, not in the attainable values: `LUV.expect P n X = (n+1)⁻¹ ∑_{i<n+1} P n (X.gt (i/(n+1)))` (`Expectations.lean:313`, `:323`), and `MarketComputation.expectQuoteAt` is the same sum over the market program's exact rational quotes (`MarketQuoteCodes.lean:187`). If every threshold sentence is priced at `v`, the expectation is `v` — on every day, for every rational `v ∈ [0,1]`, day `0` (precision `1`) included. Lemma 4.3's finite-support reading, `li-projection`'s `prescribe_finiteSupport`, accepts any `[0,1]`-valued table (`ht : ∀ p ∈ S, 0 ≤ t p.1 p.2 ∧ t p.1 p.2 ≤ 1`). So the note's §6.2 sentence is achievable *exactly*, by the same finite patch at the same coordinates (`steeredSet N`) with the constant table. The rounding the package reports comes from its own choice of `steeredTable v` — `1` at a threshold below `v`, `0` otherwise (`Advisor.lean:154`), the *coherent* prescription "price the thresholds as if the LUV were worth `v`" — whose expectation is the count of mesh points below `v`. That is a property of the package's construction, not of the mesh, the note, or the design. (ATTRIBUTION-UNVETTED which prescription the note's author had in mind; "whose prices on the day-`n` quote LUVs … are `v` — so … `a_n(u) = v`" reads as the expectation, and under either reading the note's claim is achievable.)

The quantitative sentence is false on its own terms as well. Mesh-exactness on every day `< N` (`hmesh : ∀ n < N, ∃ k ≤ n + 1, v = k/(n+1)`) at `n = 0` is `v = k/1`, `k ≤ 1`: for every `N ≥ 1` it forces **`v ∈ {0, 1}`**. "Denominator dividing `lcm(1, …, N)`" is neither necessary nor sufficient for anything here — `v = ½` has denominator dividing `lcm(1, 2) = 2` and is not mesh-exact on day `0`. So `steeredAdvisor_quote_exact`'s docstring example class ("any `v` whose denominator divides `lcm(1, …, N)`") is wrong, the mandate's D4 instruction that seeded it ("take `v` with denominator dividing `lcm(1, …, N*)` so the rounding is exact on every steering day") is wrong, and the three `_steered` rows and the OPEN `twoWay_closure_exists`, which carry `hmesh` together with `0 < N`, are stated — without saying so — for `v ∈ {0, 1}` only (destinations `½ ± γ p̂/2`). They are true and non-vacuous there (the witnesses take `v = 1`), but "`v` mesh-exact on every steering day" in the ledger's Claim/Fidelity cells and the OPEN's docstring reads as a mild restriction, and the OPEN as stated is a strict special case of the note's T2(b)/(c) (any dyadic `v ≠ ½`).

**Evidence.** Probe `audit-r3-probes/FidelityMeshCollapse.lean`: `hmesh_forces_bool` (`0 < N → hmesh → v = 0 ∨ v = 1`) and `half_not_mesh_exact_at_two` (the docstring's example class fails at `v = ½`, `N = 2`). Probe `audit-r3-probes/ConstantTableExact.lean`: `constTable v`, `steeredAdvisorConst A v N := patch A (steeredSet N) (constTable v)`, `steeredAdvisorConst_isLogicalInductor` (an inductor exactly, for every `v ∈ [0,1]`, by `prescribe_finiteSupport`), `steeredAdvisorConst_quote` (`quoteStreamR (steeredAdvisorConst A v N) n = v` for every `n < N` and every rational `v`, no mesh hypothesis — the proof is `steeredAdvisor_quote`'s with `patch_mem` and `Finset.sum_const`), and `steeredAdvisorConst_half_day_two` (`½` on day `2`, where `steeredAdvisor_half_day_two` gives `2/3`). Both elaborate clean.

**What it touches.** F6 (its heading, its "must divide `lcm`" sentence, its severity against the note); `Advisor.lean`'s header line 28 and the docstrings of `steeredTable` ("Fidelity: exact" — it is the indicator reading), `steeredAdvisor_quote_exact` (the `lcm` example class; "the note's 'dyadic' is not enough"), `quoteStream_steeredAdvisor_exact`, `steeredAdvisor_half_day_two` ("a dyadic target is not exact at a non-dyadic mesh" — true of this table only); the ledger rows `steeredAdvisor_quote_exact` (Fidelity: "the note's 'dyadic' is not enough — F6"), `steeredAdvisor_isLogicalInductor` (Claim: "the exact-prescription reading needs mesh-exactness"), `steeredSet`/`steeredTable`/`steeredAdvisor`, the `_steered` row and the OPEN row ("`v` mesh-exact on every steering day" without the `{0, 1}` consequence); the OPEN's docstring and open-list entry; report § T2 (c) ("exactness needs `v` exact at every mesh `≤ N` (F6)"); the mandate's D4 trap (a mandate error to flag, as F8 flags another).
