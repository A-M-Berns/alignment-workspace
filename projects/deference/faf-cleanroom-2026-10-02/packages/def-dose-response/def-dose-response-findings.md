### F13 — the thinning step of `faithful-acceleration.md` §5 fails outright at a doubling lookahead, and is available at bounded lag. Severity: presentation (confirms the note's own remark with a witness, and locates the obstacle: lookahead speed, not sparsity). Pointer: `dose-response.md` §5 Remark; `faithful-acceleration` §5.

Three facts (repair round 1 rewrote this finding; the first version's inference was a non sequitur, both audits' N2). (i) `harmonic_summable_on_dyadic_cover`: `∑ 1/(n+1)` diverges, yet along every schedule `j ↦ 2^j + k` of the countable strictly increasing cover `{2^j + k}` of `ℕ⁺` it is summable — this defeats the *pigeonhole-over-a-cover* justification of "thin to a sparse schedule, still with divergent sum" (a cover's members can all be summable), but not the step itself, since the thinning needs only *some* sparse schedule with divergent mass. (ii) `harmonic_summable_on_every_doubling_schedule` / `thinning_step_fails_at_doubling`: for a lookahead `f` with `f n ≥ 2n + 1`, window-disjointness forces `2 d_k < d_{k+1}`, hence `d_k + 1 ≥ 2^k`, so **every** `f`-window-disjoint schedule carries summable harmonic mass — the step has no instance at such a lookahead; and such a lookahead is one FAF's `DeferralFunction` admits, `fa-forcing-trader`'s `linearSchedule 0` (`f n = 2n + 2`: `linearSchedule_zero_doubling`, `thinning_step_fails_at_linearSchedule_zero` — adversarial audit r2 N3, so (ii) is not vacuous). (iii) `harmonic_not_summable_on_linearSchedule_zero`: at the successor lookahead the even days are window-disjoint and carry divergent harmonic mass — the step *is* available at bounded lag (in general, for `f n − n ≤ L`, by pigeonhole over the residue classes mod `L+1`, which is the pigeonhole that does work). So the unrestricted display stays OPEN (`unrestricted_thinned_forcing`) and the obstacle to deriving it from the scheduled form is the speed of the lookahead.
