## T3 — Front-running (`FrontRun.lean`, `Instances.lean`): predictable landings are already priced — asymptotically, with no claim about any single push

- **The trader.** `frontRunner φ q δ`: day `0` trades `c_0` shares of `φ 0`; day `n+1` trades `c_{n+1}` shares of `φ (n+1)` and `−c_n` shares of `φ n` (closing yesterday's position; rank `n ≤ n+1`), where `c_n := rampBelow − rampAbove ∈ [−1, 1]`, `rampBelow = clip₀₁((q_n − δ − P_n(φ_n))/δ)`, `rampAbove = clip₀₁((P_n(φ_n) − q_n − δ)/δ)` (FAF's `clip01`; `|c_n| = 1` at distance `≥ 2δ`, `c_n = 0` within `δ`). Two-sided, so one trader covers both failure directions. `roundTrip_value_eq` (L, shared with T7): the round trip is world-independent, `e(w − p₀) − e(w − p₁) = e(p₁ − p₀)`. `frontRunner_netWorth` (L): `netWorth_n = ∑_{i<n} c_i (P_{i+1}(φ_i) − P_i(φ_i)) + c_n (w(φ_n) − P_n(φ_n))`.
