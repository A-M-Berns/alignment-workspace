## 3. Non-blocking issues

### N1 — `genLic_boundedBelow_pointwise` (OPEN): its bounded-loss half is a corollary of T2.3; the open content is the unbounded-loss regime only

The OPEN (`Open.lean`) says: for an e.c. `T` whose plausible assessments against `exoHistory DP H` are bounded below, `∃ c, ∀ n v plausible, T.netWorth ≤ c + exoLoss DP H v n`, for *every* demand `H`. For every demand of bounded plausible loss — T2.3's regime — this follows in a few lines from what the package already proves: `noEcExploit_of_boundedLoss` gives `¬ T.Exploits`, so bounded below forces bounded above by some `c₀`; and in every plausible world `exoLoss ≥ −3` (T2.1's `firm ≤ 1 + Loss` with FAF's firm floor `tradingFirmTrader_netWorth_floor`, `−2 ≤ firm`), so `T.netWorth ≤ c₀ ≤ (c₀ + 3) + exoLoss`. Probe `audit-r3-probes/BoundedBelowFromNoExploit.lean` (`scripts/lean-check` exit 0, imports `GenLic` only): `exoLoss_ge_neg_three` and `genLic_boundedBelow_pointwise_of_boundedLoss`, with exactly the OPEN's conclusion under the added bounded-loss premise. So the OPEN's content sits entirely in the unbounded-loss regime (the landed persistent push, `persistent_push_unbounded_loss`), where `Loss` grows and the question is whether `T`'s upside is controlled by it with coefficient one. The docstring's "At `H = 0` this is the criterion itself" is true but understates the discharge: it is the criterion for every bounded-loss `H`. Not blocking: the statement is universally quantified over `H` and is not trivially true, the Status is `open`, and the "possibly false" caveat concerns the regime that matters. Fix I'd accept: adopt the probe's two lemmas, and let the OPEN's docstring, F2's third paragraph and the open-list reason say that the bounded-loss case is proved and the unbounded-loss case is what is open (or restate the OPEN with the persistent push's regime as its hypothesis).

### N3 — `valueBeta`'s form deviates from the mandate's and F9 does not record it

Mandate T5.1: `valueBeta P n a φ := P n φ * P n (actionAt n a)` (product form), with the two-term display `c · P n φ_u · P n (actionAt n a) + d · P n (ψ_world ⋏ actionAt n a)`. The package's `valueBeta P n a c φu d ψ := c * P n φu + d * (P n (ψ ⋏ actionAt n a) / P n (actionAt n a))` divides the world term by the action cell and leaves the `u`-term unweighted. I think the package's form is the more faithful rendering of the source's "(β) Evaluate actions with current `u`-beliefs and future world-beliefs" (line 81): in the mandate's product form the `u`-term `c · P_n(φ_u) · P_n(a)` is action-*dependent* through `P_n(a)`, so `beta_no_manipulation_incentive` would be false as stated there whenever the action cells have unequal mass, which is the opposite of what (β) means. The ledger's Fidelity cell says "variant (two-term utility, conditional by division)" and the division junk at `P_n(a) = 0` is disclosed in the docstring, so nothing is hidden; but F9 (deviations from the mandate's signatures) lists seven deviations and not this one. Fix: a one-line F9.8 with the reason above, so the mandate-writer's product form is recorded as the imprecision.

### N4 — `HcRealized` is uninhabited for a sharper reason than "not attempted": `hcWitness`'s numbers cannot be read as prices by any coherent history or state

Carried from rounds 1 and 2, disclosed in the ledger ("no inhabitant shown … could be unsatisfiable"). The round-2 adversarial audit judged it "satisfiable in principle, as `pushHist` does". Reading the definition (`Reflect.lean:456`): clause 3 sets `S.val (n+1) (code s) (uSent ⋏ actionAt n a) = M.implVal s a` and clause 4 sets `I.algo n (uSent ⋏ (stateAtom ⋏ actionAt)) = M.algoVal s a · I.algo n (stateAtom (code s))`. For `hcWitness`, `implVal true true = 2` and `algoVal true true = −1`: the state's "price" of a sentence would be `2` and the algorithmic history's price of a conjunction would be `−(its price of the state atom)` — negative whenever the state atom has positive price, which clause 2 forces (`w true true + w true false = ½` if the state atom's price is the cell sum). `StateSystem.val` and `History` are unconstrained reals, so `HcRealized … hcWitness` is satisfiable, but only by a state and a history that are not belief states even in `pushHist`'s weak sense (`pushHist` is incoherent on contradictions but its prices are in `[0, 1]`). The honest atoms-level reading needs the value tables normalised into `[0, 1]` first (a common positive affine rescaling of `algoVal` and of `implVal` leaves every clause of `HcCommitment` invariant, since clauses (a)–(b) compare weighted sums and clause (c) survives generically), or `HcRealized` should carry a separate utility scale. Not blocking: `HcRealized` is a definition of record the mandate asked to "state, prove nothing non-trivial about", and the ledger says it is uninstantiated; but the ledger's reason should be this one, so that a continuation does not try to realise the witness's raw numbers.
