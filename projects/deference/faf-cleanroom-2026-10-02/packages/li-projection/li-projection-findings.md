## F1 — The efficiency certificate of the mirror traders is not a flat-stream rewrite of FAF's freeze kind; it needs an `FP` formula-code transformer FAF does not have (severity: **blocking for the mandate's T1.5 specification** — not for the mathematics, which is fully proved; an FAF API request)

What was found while sizing the template. FAF's criterion quantifies over `EfficientlyComputable` traders: `∃ F ∈ Complexity.FP` with `strategyOfOutput n (F (unaryDay n)) = Tr.strat n`, the strategy decoded from a *flat* RPN token stream (`Framework/Criterion.lean`). The mirror `T^b` must (1) rewrite every **trade frame** `[6, ⌜φ⌝]` to `[6, ⌜φ⟦u:=b⟧⌝]` — FAF's freeze never touches frames (it replaces price leaves by constants), and FAF's conditioning frame pass rewrites `φ ↦ φ ⋏ ψ` as a *block* operation that never looks inside `φ`; (2) look **inside** sentence blocks, where a block may be an **escape leaf** `[1, c]` with `c` a Gödel code (`parseRpn`: `1 :: c :: tail ↦ decode c`), so the machine must compute `encode ((decode c)⟦u:=b⟧)` — a polynomial-time recursive descent over Foundation's `Nat.pair`-nested formula codes (`toNat`), for which FAF has the arithmetic (`DigitFP.unpairFstW`, `unpairSndW`, `mulW` in `FP`) but no assembled transducer (its own escape-leaf handling, `FiberTest`, only *tests* a code against finitely many fixed targets); (3) emit the constants `q m` for the days `m ≤ n` named in binary inside the stream, from an emitter (`MachineRatCodes`) efficient on the *unary* day — polynomial by running the emitter at every day `≤ n`, but another automaton. Each step is polynomial time; none is a mathematical obstruction; together they are a compiler project on the scale of FAF's own `Construction/Freeze/*` (9 247 lines at the pin; corrected in repair round 1 from "≈ 12 000") and `Construction/Conditioning/*` (13 953). `bli-transfer`'s `SpliceCertificate` (its L1) covers price leaves through a run-level oracle but neither frames nor the inside of blocks; it is the sibling obligation the mandate names, and it does not discharge this one.

What the package does instead. The certificate is *reduced*, in the shape FAF uses for its own seam (`FreezeStreamRewriter`, "the seam between the economic argument and the compiler"), to two named `Complexity.FP` facts stated at the decoded-strategy level, uniformly in the machine, with hypothesis (i) of Lemma A explicit (`MachineRatCodes q`). The two facts are OPEN and are the T1.5 headline; the derivations `EfficientlyComputable.mirror`/`.affineCombo` are plumbing (each hypothesis is equivalent to its conclusion, `mirrorStreamRewriter_iff` — audit r1 adversarial B1). The analogy with FAF holds for the shape of the isolation, **not for its status: FAF discharges its `FreezeStreamRewriter` (`FreezeStep.freezeStreamRewriter_of_runOracle`; it is not a hypothesis of anything public), and this package does not discharge its pair.** Every row whose conclusion is "the projection *is* an inductor" rests on them and is listed as such, and nothing else does. Hypothesis (i) is *discharged* for the eventually-constant weights the note proves Lemma A for (`MachineRatCodes.ofFiniteTable`). A dependent that wants the criterion for a projected market inherits the OPEN; `li-splice-condition` in particular.

Nearest well-posed version FAF could supply: an `FP` map `c ↦ encode (f (decode c))` for structural `f` on `Formula ℕ` (a stack-machine descent with `unpairW`/`mulW`, iterated polynomially many times), plus an emitter-generic frame pass. With those, `mirrorStreamRewriter` is routine.

## F3 — "A trader confined to an undecided sentence cannot exploit an exactly coherent pricing" is false; the mandate's T4.2(i) rendering is refuted (severity: local error in the mandate's rendering; sharpens lean-deference-076's own flag and lean-deference-2-032(b))

Pointer: mandate T4.2(i) ("for *any* history `P` (no criterion assumed) with `P n φ + P n (∼φ) = 1` for all `n`, both `φ`-worlds consistent with every stage, and a trader all of whose trades are on `φ` or `∼φ` (coefficients arbitrary), `¬ T.Exploits P DP`: the net worths in the two worlds sum to `0` on every day"); lean-deference-076 (origin chat msg 10 and the inventory's flag); lean-deference-2-032(b). Lean: `Fragments.lean` (`confined_exploits_oscillating`, `confined_not_exploits_const`).

The claimed identity "net worths in the two worlds sum to `0`" is false: with `p_n := P_n(φ)`, the day-`n` values `a_n` (in a `φ`-world) and `b_n` (in a `¬φ`-world) of a trade of `c_n` shares of `φ` satisfy `p_n a_n + (1 − p_n) b_n = 0` — the *price-weighted* sum vanishes, with day-dependent weights — so cumulative net worths do not combine with fixed weights when `p_n` varies. Counterexample (proved): `oscP` prices `atom 0` at `9/10` on even days and `1/10` on odd days (and `∼atom 0` complementarily, exactly coherent); the trader `oscT` sells `10/9` shares on even days and buys `10` on odd days. In the `atom 0`-world its net worth after day `2m+1` is `(m+1)·80/9 → ∞`; in the `¬atom 0`-world it is `1` or `0`; so it is bounded below by `−1/9` in every world and unbounded above: it exploits. What survives without the criterion is the **time-constant** form (`confined_not_exploits_const`: `P n φ = p ∈ (0,1)` for all `n`, exact coherence, both verdicts plausible), and `p ∈ (0,1)` is necessary too (a dogmatic constant price `p = 0` on an undecided sentence is exploited by buying daily). With the criterion the statement is `noExploit` (kind T, no row). What the *source* (lean-deference-076) gets wrong is its two-world step — "bounded-below-everywhere forces bounded-above", the fixed-weight combination of the two worlds' net worths — which is exactly what `oscT` breaks when the price varies; its remark that limit coherence "is what blocks the buy-both arbitrage" is about the buy-both trader and is correct (`buyBoth_exploits` is its converse). So the sharpening is: even *exact* coherence at every day does not make a confined trader harmless; only the criterion (or price stability) does. (Reworded in repair round 1, audit r1 fidelity N4.) The flagged converse — the buy-both trader exploits a pricing with `P φ + P ∼φ ≤ 1 − ε` — is proved (`buyBoth_exploits`, with its efficiency certificate).

What the package proves. `u1_u2_inductor`: for an inductor `H` over `DP`, a fresh atom `u` and a `u`-free conditioning family `ψ`, the projection `H' := project H u (fun _ => c)` is an inductor (modulo (A)), agrees with `H` on the conditioned record **restricted to `u`-free sentences**, and has a different limit **on `u`**. Why that is weaker than the source.
