## 3. Non-blocking issues

- **N1 — The prose OPEN is closed, negatively, for every family with a persistent cell inside `[0, 1)`; the report's diagnosis of what the refutation needs is wrong.** Report § Open, findings § Open and `open.txt` ask whether a finite mixture of completed-theory worlds of `paperDP T` satisfies `ExactSelfTrustXInterval (quoteAt T) cells P` with a cell of positive mass, expect "no", and say "the refutation needs a day-`m` incoherence of the LIA that FAF does not provide". It does not: FAF's `thm:lp`, already in the package as `lia_liar_asymptotic`, suffices. Machine-checked (`LiarIntervalMixture.lean`, section `Asymp`, `[𝗜𝚺₁ ⪯ T] [Consistent T]`):

  ```lean
  theorem no_mixture_exactReflectionInterval (cells) {lo hi : ℚ} (hlt : lo < hi) (hlo : 0 ≤ lo) (hhi : hi < 1)
      (hcells : ∀ m, (lo, hi) ∈ cells m) {k} (W : Fin k → PCWorld) (w : Fin k → ℝ)
      (hW : ∀ i, (W i).ConsistentWithTheory (paperDP T)) (hpos : 0 < ∑ i, w i) :
      ¬ ExactReflectionInterval (quoteAt T) cells (mixtureHistory W w)
  theorem no_pointWorld_exactReflectionInterval … (v) (hv : v.ConsistentWithTheory (paperDP T)) :
      ¬ ExactReflectionInterval (quoteAt T) cells (pointWorldHistory v)
  theorem no_mixture_exactReflectionInterval_x3Cells … :      -- the family {(0, ½], (½, 1]}
      ¬ ExactReflectionInterval (quoteAt T) (fun _ => {(0, 1/2), (1/2, 1)}) (mixtureHistory W w)
  ```

  Argument: take the liar at threshold `p := (lo + hi)/2 ∈ (0, 1)`. By `thm:lp` its quote tends to `p`, so (`Metric.tendsto_atTop` at `ε = (hi − lo)/2`) some day `m > 0` has `lo < 𝑸_m(L_m) < hi` strictly (`exists_liar_quote_interior`). Every completed-theory world then holds the cell `(lo, hi]` (`holds_quoteAt_of_lt_of_lt`), so the mixture gives it its total mass `> 0` (`mixture_cell_mass`); the mixture respects the entailments on the pair (`mixture_respectsEntailment`); the liar is decided (`liar_decided`), so its conditional is `0` or `1` (`decided_conditional`), and a cell with `0 ≤ lo`, `hi < 1` contains neither (`decided_fails_interval`, the interval analogue of `decided_fails_midpoint`). No incoherence of the LIA is used — only that it prices a decided sentence strictly inside `(0, 1)` at some day, which is what `thm:lp` says about the liar. The point mass of a completed-theory world is the `k = 1` case; this is the interval analogue of the package's `pointWorld_not_exactReflection`, which the ledger's `ExactReflectionInterval` row currently lacks ("admits ⊤-coherent inhabitants in principle … no coherent inhabitant exhibited — OPEN").

  *What remains open after this*: (i) families whose every cell reaches `1` or below `0` (e.g. the single cell `(0, 1]`): there the liar at `p = ½` is interval-exact when `q < ½`, and the refutation would need a *false* decided sentence priced in `(0, 1)` or the liar priced `≥ p`, neither of which `thm:lp` gives; (ii) growing-resolution families — Soto's actual `(m/2^{k+1}, (m+1)/2^{k+1}]` cells — where a fixed-threshold liar's quote must land strictly inside *some* sub-`1` cell, which only fails if it sits exactly on a dyadic boundary at every day `> n`; `≈ₙ` does not exclude that; (iii) bli-soto-b-027's "day-`m` support" restriction: whether the liar is in the LIA's day-`m` support is not in FAF, so that question stays open as the report says.

  *Fix I'd accept*: adopt `decided_fails_interval` into `Defs.lean` beside `decided_fails_midpoint` and the five `Liar.lean`-level lemmas into `Liar.lean` (Kind L / C; `no_mixture_exactReflectionInterval` is a small Composition of `thm:lp` with the decided-sentence lemma); rewrite report § Open, findings § Open and `open.txt`'s fourth item as "**refuted** for every family with a persistent cell `(lo, hi]`, `0 ≤ lo < hi < 1` (`x3Cells`, every fixed-resolution dyadic partition of `(0, 1]` at resolution `≥ 1`), by `thm:lp` alone; open for (i)–(iii) above"; and change the `ExactReflectionInterval` row's Witness cell to say that no completed-theory point mass or positive mixture inhabits it on such families, so the predicate's inhabitants at `quoteAt T` are, like the midpoint form's, outside the theory-respecting class — the difference between the two forms is `⊤` alone (`top_exact_interval` vs `coherentTop_forces_zero`), not the existence of a coherent inhabitant.

- **N2 — "The interval claim does not extend to straddling cells" is false for straddling cells with `0 ≤ lo`, `hi < 1`.** The sentence appears in findings F6 ("and the *interval* claim does **not** extend to straddling cells (at `(lo, 1]` with `lo < q < p` the liar's conditional `1` lies inside the cell)"), in report § Design decision 2 ("the interval claim does *not* extend to straddling cells, so the slide's caveat stands for the interval reading"), in the docstring of `liar_midpoint_fails_any_cell` (`Liar.lean:325–326`) and, for the predicate, in that of `not_exactReflection_of_liar` ("the predicate's refutation at a *straddling* cell … would go through `liar_midpoint_fails_any_cell` and is the F5 artifact"). Machine-checked counter-instance (`LiarIntervalMixture.lean`, section `Cells`, `[𝗣𝗔⁻ ⪯ T] [𝗥₀ ⪯ T]`):

  ```lean
  theorem liar_fails_interval_any_cell (p) (P) (n m) {lo hi} (hlo : 0 ≤ lo) (hhi : hi < 1)
      (hresp : RespectsEntailment (TheoryWorlds (paperDP T)) (P n) (liar T p m) (liarCell T p m lo hi))
      (hpos : 0 < P n (liarCell T p m lo hi)) :
      ¬ ((lo : ℝ) * P n (liarCell …) < P n (liar T p m ⋏ liarCell …) ∧ P n (liar T p m ⋏ liarCell …) ≤ (hi : ℝ) * P n (liarCell …))
  theorem liar_fails_interval_straddling …   -- p = ½, cell (¼, ¾]: lo < p ≤ hi, and the conclusion holds
  ```

It fails to extend only at cells reaching `1` (where a *true* liar, `q < p`, is interval-exact — F6's parenthetical example, which is correct) or below `0` (a false one). The accurate sentence is: "the interval failure extends to every straddling cell with `0 ≤ lo`, `hi < 1`, but as the decided-sentence artifact shared with `⊤`; straddling cells carry no liar-*specific* information, which is the sense in which the slide's caveat stands".
