## Design

**File discipline** (mandate Known issue 8, plan §0.5). FAF-free files, importable by `def-*` packages without `Construction.*`: `Defs` (definitions of record), `Abstract` (X1 (a), X2 (i), bli-soto-b-024), `PCLIC` (X5; Mathlib only), `Smoothed` (X6 (v)), `Accuracy` (X7-exact over `bli-finite`). Each construction-facing file imports `Construction.Paper.Market` or `Construction.Freeze.Oracle` and nothing of the sibling packages under repair (Known issue 10: `bli-linkage`'s `liar`, `bli-exact-base`'s `lookupOr`/`computable_lookupOr` recipe are copied with the precedent cited in the docstring, never imported).

**The three decisions that shaped the statements.**

1. *Theory-respect on two sentences, not an algebra* (Known issue 3). `RespectsEntailment 𝒲 p φ ψ` asks only `ψ ⊨ φ → p(φ ⋏ ψ) = p(ψ)` and `ψ ⊨ ∼φ → p(φ ⋏ ψ) = 0`, with `⊨` relative to a class of worlds `𝒲` (`TheoryWorlds (paperDP T)`: completed-theory worlds). Every finite linear combination of payouts of worlds in `𝒲` satisfies it for every pair (`mixture_respectsEntailment`, Kind L) — this is the generator of all witnesses. `bli-found`'s `CoherentOn` was not used: it quantifies over every sentence of an algebra and no finite perturbation of the LIA satisfies it (`bli-exact-base` `Tables.not_coherentOn_of_finiteSupportPerturbation`). The LIA's own finite-time status under `RespectsEntailment` is **not claimed** (not studied).
2. *The completed theory decides the liar* (`liar_decided`) — **and what that does and does not buy** (revised in repair round 1). Over FAF the liar's truth value is the fixed rational fact `𝑸_m(L) < p` about the paper market's price, shared by all completed-theory worlds. The formalizer's first draft read this as voiding the slide's straddling caveat ("every cell with interior midpoint fails"). Audit round 1 showed that reading is the F5 artifact: under theory-respect *every* decided sentence — `⊤` included — has conditional `0` or `1` on every cell (`decided_conditional`), so *midpoint*-exactness fails for it at every interior-midpoint cell (`decided_fails_midpoint`, `top_fails_midpoint`); nothing self-referential is involved. The liar theorems are therefore stated in the **interval form** "the conditional lies outside the cell" (`exact_reflection_fails_liar`), which `⊤` does not satisfy at a cell reaching `1` (`top_exact_interval`) and which the liar fails at every one-sided cell by its anti-correlation with its own price (`liar_cell_conditional`); the interval claim does *not* extend to straddling cells, so the slide's caveat stands for the interval reading. The hypothesis is honestly disclosed as respect for the **completed** theory, which knows the day-`m` price already on day `n < m`; the stage-relative variant (coherence with `D n` only) is not claimed and, as far as I can see, would not refute anything at interval resolution — the stage does not yet decide the liar.
3. *The exact predicates are unguarded* (Known issue 4). `ExactNNUAt` is `D_NNU`'s body at `ε = 0` without the `SmallOn` guard; `not_smallOn_one_quoteAt` shows the guard fails on day 1 for every interval quote, so `D_NNU` says nothing there (`d_nnu_day1_vacuous`). `ExactNNU` implies `D_NNU` at `ε = 0` (`ExactNNU.d_nnu`).

**Conventions** (Known issue 6). Cells are `quoteAt`'s `(lo, hi]`; the representative of a cell is its midpoint `mid I` (bli-soto-b-025's convention, findings F5); `ExactIntro` uses `lo < Q n φ ≤ hi`. No theorem depends on an end point except as findings F6 records: with `L := ℙ_m(L) < p`, the point `p` lies in no one-sided `(lo, hi]` cell, which is why the one-sided witness carries `q ≠ p`; and inside `[0, 1]` no `(lo, hi]` cell contains `0`, so a false decided sentence fails interval-exact reflection at every cell of a family in `[0, 1]` (the liar's one-sided failure is not of this kind — `top_exact_interval` is the encoding check).

**The definition of record is family-relative** (repair round 1). `ExactReflection quoteAt cells P` quantifies over the cells `I ∈ cells m` of a per-day finite family (Soto's dyadic partition at one resolution), as `ExactNNUAt` always did. The mandate's shape — every rational cell `(lo, hi)` — is kept as `ExactReflectionAllCells` with its collapse lemma `exactReflectionAllCells_zero_on_negative_mid` (findings F14) and no headline is stated over it.

**The refuted object of record is the interval form** (repair round 2; audit r2 adversarial B1/B2 (iii) (α)). Round 1 had already moved X1 to the interval form "the conditional lies outside the cell" because the midpoint identity fails for every decided sentence (F5); round 2 applied the same test to X3 and to the definition of record. `ExactReflectionInterval quoteAt cells P` asks, at every cell of the family of positive mass, `lo · P_n(χ_I) < P_n(φ ⋏ χ_I) ≤ hi · P_n(χ_I)`; `ExactNNUAtInterval` asks `∑ lo_I · P_n(χ_I) ≤ P_n(φ) ≤ ∑ hi_I · P_n(χ_I)`. The midpoint forms imply them on non-degenerate cells (`ExactReflection.interval`, `ExactNNUAt.interval`), so a violation of the interval form is a violation of the midpoint form that cannot be the artifact of the representative. X3's headlines are now the interval violations, with the midpoint ones as corollaries. The midpoint forms are kept as what they are: the identities Soto's bundle market imposes by definition and the body of `bli-found`'s `D_NNU`. **They fail STANDARDS §3's encoding check**: the midpoint predicate over every sentence has no inhabitant that is coherent at `⊤` with positive mass on a cell of midpoint `≠ 1` (`coherentTop_forces_zero` — the identity at `φ := ⊤` reads `P(χ) = mid · P(χ)`), i.e. on any family inside `[0, 1]` every positive-mass inhabitant is `⊤`-incoherent. The interval form passes it: `⊤` is interval-exact at a cell reaching `1` (`top_exact_interval`) and a `⊤`-coherent inhabitant need only give `⊤`'s cells with `hi < 1` mass `0` (`coherentTop_interval_below_cell`, `top_fails_below_cell`) — a consistent belief. Whether a coherent history satisfies the interval form for *every* `φ` at once at `bli-found`'s `quoteAt T` is open (§ Open); the obstacle is that all completed-theory worlds agree on every quote sentence, so a world mixture would have to put `P_n(φ)` in the cell of `𝑸_m(φ)` for every `φ`, and the paper market's day-`m` prices are not coherent.

**Theory parameters** (Known issue 7). `Liar.lean` and `Backbone.lean` are generic in `(T) [T.Δ₁] [𝗣𝗔⁻ ⪯ T]`, adding `[𝗥₀ ⪯ T]` where `quoteAt` is used, `[LO.Entailment.Consistent T]` only where a completed-theory world must exist (`exists_theoryWorld`, the two witnesses) and `[𝗜𝚺₁ ⪯ T]` only for the asymptotic artifact check. `Perturb.lean` and `Open.lean` work at `T := 𝗜𝚺₁` (as `bli-found`'s `Grid.lean` and `bli-exact-base` do), where instance search discharges everything.
