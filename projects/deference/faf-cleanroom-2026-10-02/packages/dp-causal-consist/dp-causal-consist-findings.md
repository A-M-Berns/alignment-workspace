## F1 — Theorem 3(i)'s "almost surely" is harmless for positive acts (mandate §6.1) — imprecision, **resolved**

Source: [learning-cdt-renderings](../../sources/decision-problems/wiki/learning-cdt-renderings.md) Theorem 3(i) ("iff `P_{s_d}(a | pa_G(m)) = P_{s_d}(a)` almost surely"), proof ("they agree at every `v` iff …").

The proof's equality is pointwise on the truncated support, and the mandate expected that support to reach `P`-null parent cells where the CPD is free, making the printed iff wrong unless "a.s." is read on the truncated support. **It cannot**: `prob_parentCell_pos_of_prod_pos` (`Truncate.lean`) shows that a point with `∏_{v ≠ m} φ_v(x) > 0` has `P(pa_m = pa_m(x)) ≥ ∏_{v ∈ nondesc(m)} φ_v(x) > 0`. So for a positive-probability act the two readings of "a.s." coincide, `thm3_ii`/`thm3_iii` need no positivity of the parent cells, and the freedom of the CPD on null cells reaches the truncated law only at *null acts* — which is exactly Corollary 3.1(a) as printed (`nullAct_truncate_ne`, `Dags.lean`). The note is consistent with itself. Residual imprecision: the statement should say "at every parent configuration of positive probability", which is what `thm3_i_of_strictlyPositive` renders and what `thm3_i`'s right side is equivalent to.

A side finding on the mandate (not the source): §4 T2(i)'s sketch of the null-cell witness ("`pa_m = {ℓ}`, `P(ℓ = 1) = 0`, `m`'s CPD free at `ℓ = 1`") does not work — the truncated laws of two such CPDs coincide because `φ_ℓ(1) = 0` is pinned by `P` and truncation cuts `φ_m`; the freedom that shows in the truncated law is at a null act.

## F4 — Axiom NR does not determine NR-cf(cross) at `TB(θ)`'s stay label (mandate §6.4) — **local error** in the five-evaluation table's provenance

Source: [non-responsiveness](../../sources/decision-problems/wiki/non-responsiveness.md) "Axiom NR" l. 69 ("Hence `P^a_s = ∑_e P_s(e) P_s(· | a, e)`") and "Consistency" ("on TB(θ) at the stay label … NR-cf(cross) `= 8`"); [non-responsiveness-learnability](../../sources/decision-problems/directions/non-responsiveness-learnability.md) §8 D.

What is proved: `nr_kpart_iff` (`NR.lean`) shows the K-partition sum is the act-conditional iff every cell is independent of the act, and its `→` direction shows a *bad cell* (`P(e) > 0 = P(a ∧ e)`) makes the printed sum fall short of a probability (`kPart(A) < 1`); `nr_forces_kpart` forces `P^a = ∑_e P(e) P(· | a, e)` only under "every positive cell meets `a` positively"; `tb_stay_guard_fails` (`TbTheta.lean`) shows the `¬bot` cell of `TB(θ)` at `q = 0` is exactly such a cell (`P(¬bot) = 1 − θ > 0 = P(cross ∧ ¬bot)`). On this cell clause (ii) of the axiom is vacuous and clause (i) pins only `P^a(¬bot) = 1 − θ`. On the four-point world space, success then forces `P^a = θ δ_{(bot,cross)} + (1−θ) δ_{(¬bot,cross)}`, so the *probability* component is in fact determined; what the axiom leaves free is the `V`-component at `(¬bot, cross)` — clause (iii) is silent there because the event is `P_s`-null — and NR-cf(cross) `= −10θ + (1−θ)·v` for any `v`. The note's `10 − 20θ` (its `8` at `θ = 1/10`) is `v = 10`, the deviation's fill; `v = 0` gives `−10θ`. The family `cfNR v` (`TbThetaNR.lean`) realises every `v`: `cfNR_nrAt` (NR for every `v`), `cfNR_value` (NR-cf(cross) `= −10θ + (1−θ)v`), and `cfNR_ne_kPart` (its `cf(cross)` gives `{(¬bot, cross)}` probability `1 − θ` where `kPart` gives `0` — the N− companion of `nr_forces_kpart`). At every *mixed* label the number is forced — for the probability component by `nr_forces_kpart`, and (repair round 1, `NRValue.lean`) for the desirability by `nr_forces_kpart_V`: every `NRAt` component has `V^{cross}(cross) = 10 − 20θ` (`tb_nrAt_V_cross`) — and equals the deviation (`nrcf_eq_deviation`, now stated for an arbitrary `NRAt` component), which is the note's actual claim; only the stay-label row of the table mis-attributes its value to the axiom. The finding does not depend on the package's guard variant of the `V`-clause: the wiki guards clause (iii) by `P_s(a ∧ e) > 0`, the Lean by `P_s(X ∧ a ∧ e) > 0`, and at the stay label both are silent on the `¬bot` cell (`P_s(cross ∧ ¬bot) = 0`) and both bind at the `bot` cell on `{(bot, cross)}`, so `cfNR v` satisfies the wiki's clause as printed (audit r1 fid N11). Register: the printed "hence `P^a_s = ∑_e …`" is *ill-defined* on the bad cell (its summand's conditional has no value) rather than false; what is refuted is that the axiom supplies the table's `8`. Severity: local error in provenance; the identity NR-cf = deviation holds wherever the axiom speaks.

## F9 — The mixture of `State`s needs the desirability read on supports — imprecision in v2's Definition 2 as a Lean object (new)

## F15 — T3(C)'s "unique compatible temporal DAG" is false as stated under Markov compatibility (new, repair round 2) — imprecision in the source

Source: [defining-cdt-in-the-learning-setting](../../sources/decision-problems/directions/defining-cdt-in-the-learning-setting.md) l. 46 ("the compatible temporal structure — with `ℓ` in the algebra it is unique") and l. 131 (script (C): "7 compatible (1 temporal)"); mandate T3(C) ("exactly one is compatible with the bypass state").

Markov compatibility as Theorem 3 defines it (`FactorizesOverDAG`: some CPD on the DAG factorizes the law — "the only thing a record can test") is monotone in the edge set: a superset DAG with CPDs that ignore the extra parent factorizes whenever the subset does. So on the bypass state `{ℓ → m, ℓ → k, m → k}` is compatible alongside `{ℓ → m, ℓ → k}` (take `k`'s CPD constant in `m`), and both are temporal under every reading of F10 (`pa(m) = {ℓ} ⊆ V₀`; `pa(k) ⊆ {ℓ, m}`). "Unique" is therefore false as stated; the script's count "1 temporal" presupposes minimality (no edge removable without losing compatibility) or faithfulness, neither of which Theorem 3 or Definition 26 states. The correct statement is "the unique *minimal* compatible temporal DAG" — and that is the version the continuation should prove (handoff T3(C): enumerate the eight DAGs with `pa(ℓ) = ∅`, `pa(m) ⊆ {ℓ}`, `pa(k) ⊆ {ℓ, m}`, show every one without both `ℓ → m` and `ℓ → k` fails compatibility at a mixed `q`). Not formalized; recorded from the handoff's observation at audit r2 fid N4 / adv N10's request. Severity: imprecision (the uniqueness clause needs a minimality qualifier; nothing downstream in the note uses it beyond the count).

## F14 — T3(D)'s non-identifiability claim needs checking before it is stated (mandate, not source) — note for the continuation
