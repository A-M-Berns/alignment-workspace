## New findings

- **F-1 (presentation; `weak-endorsement-deference-model` §2.1, trust-lab-023).** The deferred liar `χ_n ↔ (ℙ_{f(n)}(χ_n) < ½)` is not a new fixed point: it is FAF's same-day Kleene diagonal reindexed by `f` (`defDiag ψ f n := ψ (f n)`; `defDiag_reflected`). - **F-2 (presentation; vq-wiki-059/060, `route-negative-introspective` §5.1 Lemma F; rewritten after audit r1 B1).** Lemma F has two forms with *incomparable* fidelity, and the lab's note does not separate them (it states one lemma with the summable conclusion and "in particular both terms `→ 0`"; it does not claim the corollary needs the stronger hypothesis). (a) The source's own statement — hypothesis "`D^n_H` decides `X_n`", conclusion `∑_n λ_n(1 − P_n(X_n)) < ∞`, "`→ 0`" as its corollary — is now formalized as `gated_forcing_summable` (and its mirror), by the lab's own trader through FAF's exploitation engine; the stage-decidedness hypothesis is genuinely used there (the fired share must pay `1` in every world consistent with `D_n`). (b) The `→ 0` corollary holds under the *weaker* hypothesis of completed-theory truth only (`gated_forcing`, `AffineCombination.TheoryTruth`, affine provability induction): the inventory's FAF-objects line "requires `D_H` to decide `X_n` by day `n`" (vq-wiki-059, the inventory's gloss, not the note's text) is not needed for `→ 0`. So neither form is "strictly stronger" than the other (the earlier version of this finding said so, wrongly): (a) has the stronger conclusion, (b) the weaker hypothesis. A corollary of (a) worth noting: for a sentence in stage `k` of the process, `∑_n (1 − P_n(φ)) < ∞` (`price_summable_of_mem`) — summable provability induction, a strengthening of `thm:provind` on constant families that the LI paper does not state.
- **F-3 (imprecision; `li-diagonal-mandate` T2a).** The mandate's engine link has margin `0` (`t < 𝔼_n(Y_n) → y_n ≤ t − c`). On the real diagonal the link is only available with a vanishing margin, because `𝔼_n(𝟙χ_n) = P_n(χ_n ⋏ ∼∼χ_n)` equals `P_n(χ_n)` only in the limit (`thm:ei`). The engine was therefore proved in the (stronger) vanishing-margin form (`pinning_engine`); the margin-`0` form is its corollary (`pinning_engine_sharp`). - **F-4 (ill-posed; trust-lab-026).** "Maximal among all schemata" has no order on schemata. So the only surviving schema is the one-sided soft inequality, and it survives with slack (`soft_self_trust_nondegenerate`), not degenerately. The source's `t = ½` is a special case of the hard rows.

- **F-11 (presentation; `route-negative-introspective` §5.2, vq-wiki-060(b) Lemma S; from audit r1 adversarial N1; last two sentences corrected in repair round 2 after audit r2 fidelity B1 / adversarial N10).** Lemma S's boxed statement — `liminf_j P_j(π_{κ j}) = 0`, `limsup = 1` for a revisiting enumeration of a proved and a refuted sentence — needs neither Preemptive Learning (`thm:tbo`) nor an e.c. certificate of the enumeration `κ`: on the days with `κ j = k₁` the diagonal price *is* `P_j(π_{k₁}) → 0` (`thm:provind` on the constant family), and those days are frequent, so `liminf ≤ ε` for every `ε`; dually for `k₀` (`lemmaS`, certificate-free, Kind L). The note's proof establishes the sup-form `liminf_j sup_{m ≥ j} P_m(ψ_j) = 0` (and the inf-form `limsup_j inf_{m ≥ j} P_m(ψ_j) = 1`) first, by Convergence and Limit Coherence, and then invokes 4.2.4 for the passage to the boxed form — the trivial direction (`P_j(ψ_j) ≤ sup_{m ≥ j} P_m(ψ_j)`). Over FAF **both forms** follow from `thm:provind` on the constant families under the revisiting hypotheses: the sup/inf-form is `lemmaS_supForm` (`LemmaS.lean`, repair round 2; audit r2 probe `LemmaSSupForm.lean`), the same three lines with the future supremum in place of the day-`j` price. `thm:tbo`'s hard direction (`liminf_j P_j(ψ_j) ≥ liminf_j sup_{m ≥ j} P_m(ψ_j)` for an e.c. enumeration) is what makes the two forms *equivalent* in general, and that is how the note's "revisiting is necessary" argument (stated for the sup-form, via the §5.5 impossibility) reaches the boxed statement for e.c. enumerations; `thm:tbo` would matter here only for sentences not decided in the process. Nothing in this package bears on that necessity claim, which stands as the note grades it ("proved modulo the §5.5 impossibility"); the earlier version of this finding said the sup-form "would need `thm:tbo` and cheapness of `κ`" and that the necessity argument did not reach the boxed statement — both withdrawn.
