**What the note's mechanism is, and why the Lean is not it.** The note *keeps the honest quote* `⌜ℙ^H_{f(t)}(φ)⌝` and removes `A`'s feedback about it: the quote atom is **undecided** by `A`'s theory, so `A`'s price of it is *free* ("anything `A` likes") and the exploiter must be "a trader that knows `A`'s bias". `botQuotes` puts in the quote's place a sentence `A`'s theory **refutes**, so the merge's bias is *forced* to `0` by `thm:provind` and the dumbest trader collects it. **The note's own instance, stated OPEN** (repair round 1): `gen_honestQuote_noFeedback_w1 : ¬ IsLogicalInductor (mergeMarket w1H ledgerQuotes) (paperDP 𝗜𝚺₁)` (`Open.lean`) — the paper LIA over the bare `paperDP 𝗜𝚺₁` with the honest quote atoms kept but recorded by no ledger (never decided), in the note's direction; market half proved (`honestQuote_noFeedback_w1_computable`). **Direction unknown**: nothing available pins the LIA's prices of never-decided atoms either way (`fa-theorem-a` F15), so the note's "trader that knows `A`'s bias" is exactly the object a refutation still needs. So the status of (Gen) as the note means it is **conjecture, unchanged**; what changed is that the all-vacuous instance is a precise FAF statement.

**Still sharper than the note, within the ∃-over-`Q` reading:** the exploitation is against *every* deductive process with consistent stages, not just `𝒫𝒞(Γ)` for a shared `Γ` (`gen_refuted_notInductor`).

## F6 (Known issue 2) — which process `𝔼^∗` is judged against is unpinned in every source · severity: imprecision — **corrected in repair round 1** (audit r1 fidelity N2 / adversarial N5)

## F18 — the note's fallback "at best an inductor on the good-feedback subsequence" had no FAF object; it now has one, and its status is: refuted off the subsequence, open on it · severity: imprecision in the source (an undefined phrase); the first version of this package's claim that it was *refuted* was false (audit r1 fidelity B1 / adversarial B2)

The sources define no "criterion along a subsequence". Repair round 1 gives the phrase a FAF object: `TraderSupportedOn Tr S` (every coefficient of `Tr`'s strategy denotes `0` off the day-set `S`) and `InductorOn P DP S` (no e.c. trader supported on `S` exploits `P` against `DP`; `GenRefuted.lean` § E). It is FAF's `noExploit` clause at `S = univ` (`inductorOn_univ`), antitone in `S` (`InductorOn.mono`), satisfied by every inductor on every `S` (`InductorOn.of_inductor`) — a weakening, as "at best" requires — and its quantifier has content: `buyTopEven` / `buyTopOdd` are certified e.c. traders supported on the even / odd days (`parityTrader_efficientlyComputable`: FAF's `EfficientlyComputable.ofSingleTradeBlocksBig` with `MachineTokenStream.ifZero` on the parity ruler). This is one reading of the phrase (the trader *trades* only on `S`; its coefficients may read every day's prices); the reindexed-market reading (`n ↦ B_{s(n)}` as a market over `n ↦ DP.D (s n)`, `def-tracking-pin`'s relabelling) is a second, not formalized here.

**Status of the fallback under it, at the mixed merge (honest on the even days, `⊥` on the odd days):** (i) **unrestricted**: refuted (S1, `gen_refuted_mixed_notInductor`) — which the note itself asserts ("unconstrained off it"); (ii) **along the odd days** (the `⊥`-quoted days): refuted (`gen_refuted_mixed_oddDays`, by `buyTopOdd`; at W1 `gen_refuted_mixed_oddDays_w1`); (iii) **along the even days** — the fallback proper: **OPEN** (`fallback_evenDays_noExploit`, inhabited at W1 by `fallback_evenDays_w1`). On the even days the mixed merge is the honest merge (`mergeMarket_mixedQuotes_even`), so (iii) is T3 restricted to even-day traders; it does not follow from `inductor_half_noExploit` (the markets differ off the even days) and a refutation at W1 meets `fa-theorem-a` F15. The *behavioural* reading of the fallback (ideate Idea 1's (1a)) is a theorem: Lemma P along the even days (`mergeMarket_provind_evenDays`). So the note's conclusion "inductor-like on the good-feedback subsequence, unconstrained off it" is *right* for the behavioural reading, *consistent with* everything proved for the criterion reading, and *open* on the subsequence for the criterion reading. **What the note needs:** a definition of "inductor on a subsequence" (either of the two above), and the note's `w` is a weighting, not a day-set — for a `{0,1}`-valued generable `w` the two coincide (the even days are `fa-theorem-a`'s `evenDays_pgenerable`), for a general `w` the day-set reading needs `supp w`. **Also recorded (the general (Gen) scenario):** the note's "unobservable class" is a *subclass of sentences* with the honest quote on every sentence and feedback only on the complement; `li-quote-lane`'s `ledgerProcess` records every item (each with a schedule), so a partial ledger process — recording `ledgerLuv ⌜φ⌝ n` only for `φ` in a decidable class — is the object the general form needs; `gen_honestQuote_noFeedback_w1` is its all-vacuous end. Pointer: `GenRefuted.lean` § E, `Open.lean` § C, `Witnesses.lean` § C.
