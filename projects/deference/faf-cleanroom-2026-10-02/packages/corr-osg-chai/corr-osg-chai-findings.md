## F-1 — Hadfield-Menell Theorem 1's strictness clause mixes `Pr(U_a ≤ 0)` and `Pr(U_a < 0)` (KI-1). Imprecision.

Pointer: `04-chai/hadfield-menell-2017-the-off-switch-game.md` l. 88 (Eq. 3 writes `E[−U_a | U_a < 0] Pr(U_a ≤ 0)`), l. 100 (clause 2: "non-zero support on `U_a > 0` and `U_a < 0`"). With the convention `π^H(0) = 1` the mass at `U_a = 0` contributes to neither term of Eq. 3, so the mismatch is harmless for the value; the *iff* form of clause 2 needs strictly positive mass on `{U > 0}` and on `{U < 0}`. `osgDelta_rational_pos_iff` is that iff; the paper states only `⇐`.

## F-2 — Corollary 1's Eq. 4 associates the branches the wrong way round in the paper itself. Local error (in the paper's display).

Pointer: l. 166 of the extraction, and the paper's own PDF (`04-chai/hadfield-menell-2017-the-off-switch-game.pdf`, p. 3: `pdftotext -layout` output l. 246–248 shows `−Ua(1 − π^H(Ua))` on the `Ua < 0` row and `Ua π^H(Ua)` on the `Ua ≥ 0` row — re-read in repair round 1 after audit r1 N1; the earlier hedge "possibly a pdftotext layout artefact" is withdrawn). Computing Eq. 1 on a Dirac belief gives `Δ = U π^H` when `U < 0` and `Δ = −U(1 − π^H)` when `U ≥ 0` (`osgDelta_delta`). The printed association is false, not a matter of convention: at `U < 0`, `π^H = 0` it gives `Δ = −U > 0`, but `w(a)` and `s` both pay `0`; and it contradicts the paper's next sentence, since under the printed association `Δ = −Ua(1 − π) ≥ 0` for *every* `π` when `Ua < 0`, so `Δ` would not be "only non-negative if π^H is the rational policy". Downstream users should take the checked form. The corollary's per-point content (`osgDelta_delta_nonneg_iff`) is unaffected. The mandate's T3(a) copied the printed form (report deviation 2).

## F-15 — The mandate's chance-node iff is wrong. Local error (in the mandate, not a source).

Also T3(b) needs no `[0,1]` bound on `π^H` (only the function-level (c) does).
