## Deviations from the mandate's shapes (loud)

1. **T3(d), the chance node's equality case.** The mandate asks for `osgDelta μ U (fun _ => p) = 0 ↔ p = 0 ∨ p = 1 ∨ E[U] = 0`. That is false: at `p = 1` with `E[U] < 0`, `Δ = 1·E[U] − max(E[U], 0) = E[U] < 0` (always allowing a bad action is worse than `s`). Proved instead: `osgDelta_const_eq_zero_iff : Δ = 0 ↔ (0 < E[U] → p = 1) ∧ (E[U] < 0 → p = 0)`, with no `[0,1]` bound needed. The paper only asserts the boundary cases, so this is a correction of the mandate, not of the source.
2. **T3(a), Eq. 4's branches.** The mandate copies the printed Eq. 4 (`Δ = −U(1 − π)` when `U < 0`, `Δ = Uπ` when `U ≥ 0`). Computing from Eq. 1 with a Dirac belief gives the *opposite* association: `Δ = Uπ` when `U < 0` and `−U(1 − π)` when `U ≥ 0` (`osgDelta_delta`). The printed association is false, not merely a typo of the mandate: at `U < 0, π = 0` it gives `Δ = −U > 0`, but `w(a)` and `s` both pay `0`. Recorded as finding F-2 (the pdftotext layout of the two-case display may be the cause; either way the Lean is the checked form).
3. **T3(b) needs no `[0,1]` bound** on `π^H` (the mandate says "under `πH ∈ [0,1]`"); only the function-level (c) needs it.
4. **Cor. A.6 is stated as `IsGreatest`**, not as `optValueStoch = optValue`: the stochastic pairs form an infinite set, and an `sSup`-style `optValueStoch` would carry a junk value; `optValue_isGreatest_stoch` says exactly that the deterministic optimum is the greatest stochastic payoff. Its uniqueness clause is a separate declaration, `stoch_opt_dirac_of_unique` (repair round 1).
5. **Prop. 4.3's H side** transfers with an arbitrary H-policy (`fun _ => default`): in the diagonal game where H sees A's observation, H's policy lives on `ΩA`, and since the transferred A never waits, H's policy in the original game is immaterial.
6. **Mandate D2's product-weights lemma** (`payoffStoch = ∑ (product weights) · payoff`) was not the original route to Cor. A.6's bound, which went through the fibrewise `exists_det_ge` (audit r1 fidelity N9, an unlisted deviation). It is now `payoffStoch_eq_sum_det` (repair round 1) and carries Lemma A.5 and the uniqueness clause; the bound keeps its fibrewise proof.
7. **`RedundantA`, `NoPrivateA` (and the H sides) were first stated at every state**; Garber's Def. 4.2 and Def. A.11 are almost-sure statements under `P0 ⊗ obs`. Restated on the support of `P0` in repair round 1 (audit r1 N2); Prop. 4.3's proof first copies a positive-mass state's row onto the null states (no payoff, optimum or OPP changes: `isOPP_withObs_iff_of_eq_on_support`), then runs the paper's argument (`exists_opp_alwaysWait_of_factor`).
