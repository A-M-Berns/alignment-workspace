## 3. Non-blocking issues

**N1. `xt_rule_thm79_refuted` / F1: the side-condition check covers only the attained true values, and the hypothesis's reading should be stated.** Theorem 7.9's `I_X` is a function on `[v₀, vₙ] × [v₀, vₙ]` and its monotonicity hypothesis ("strictly increasing as `|x − y|` increases") is asked for every `y ∈ [v₀, vₙ]`; `xtRule_valueDirectedOn : ValueDirectedOn Xxt xtRule` checks it only at `y = X w ∈ {0, 2}`. The docstring and ledger say "on the range", which reads as the whole `[0, 2]`. The gap is real but closes in two lines: probe §2 proves `xtRule_monotone_all_k` — per-side strict monotonicity for every `k ∈ [0, 2]` — from the factorisation `I(x₂, k) − I(x₁, k) = (x₂ − x₁)·((x₂ + x₁)/2 + (x₂² + x₁x₂ + x₁²)/6 − k − k²/2)` (`xtRule_sub`). Second, the hypothesis must be read *per side*: read across sides, it fails for `xtRule` at `k = 1` (`|3/2 − 1| < |49/100 − 1|` yet `I(3/2, 1) = 13/48 > I(49/100, 1)`, `xtRule_not_cross_side`) — and it fails equally for DDB's own `(t − k)` family with the same `λ = (1 + t) dt` (`tkRule_not_cross_side`, `J(3/2, 1) = 7/24 > J(49/100, 1)`), a member the probe proves gsp on the range (`tkRule_isGspOn`, by the identity `E_ρ(J(s)) − E_ρ(J(2p)) = (s − 2p)²(s/3 + p/3 + 1/2)`; a non-step member, so not covered by `isGsp_stepRule`). So the cross-side reading would make Theorem 7.9 false for its own family, the per-side reading (value-directedness) is the only tenable one, and under it the refutation stands with every `k`. Fix: cite the probe (or move `xtRule_monotone_all_k` into `Representation.lean`) and add one sentence to F1 and the docstring: "the monotonicity hypothesis is read per side (as value-directedness); the cross-side reading excludes the theorem's own family for non-uniform `λ`".
