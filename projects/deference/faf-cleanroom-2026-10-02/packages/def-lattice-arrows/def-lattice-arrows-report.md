## The design in one paragraph

Every arrow is: (i) a `LUVCombination` built from the quote LUVs by `listComb` with constant coefficients (the `loe` step is definitional, `listComb_expect`); (ii) a world-value computation from the reflection clauses of def-lattice's packages (`Reflects`, `WeightQuote`, `CondQuote`, `GapQuote`, `Follows`), giving a bound that holds *eventually* or *within a vanishing slack*; (iii) the one ε-outside lemma (T0), which patches the finitely many early days by replacing the combination's constant below the day `N` by a large rational (machine-metered by `MachineSpliceStream.ifZero` on the ruler `n + 1 − N`), so that FAF's `lic_expect_combination_provind_*` — which wants its world bound on every day — applies at `c − ε`; (iv) a chain of `≈ₙ`/`≲ₙ` with the deference instance. The expert's side is always asymptotic (`ExpertFoldAt`, `ExpertFoldCond`, `ExpertPin`, `hSE`): no exact expert identity appears in the package (findings F9). Existence of quotes is never assumed inside a hypothesis-notion; each predicate-level arrow names its existence clause (`QuotesAvailable`, `ProductQuotesAvailable`, `CondQuotesReflected`, `RampQuotesAvailable`, `BandQuotesAvailable`, `GapPackagesAvailable`, `ProbeMenusAvailable`), and `OneWay.lean` discharges the first three for computable experts over `paperDP T`.
