## F1 — `G` is decidable but not efficiently (anson-016; anson-020 flag (i))

**Severity: imprecision.** v6 §6: "Membership is decidable (run `D` and `H^{[n]}` to the finite stage `F(n)` and check)." True, and rendered twice: `Decidable (Timely S ε n)` by `inferInstance` (`Defs.lean`), and — since repair round 1 — `timely_computable` (`Computable.lean`): for a shared process computable in FAF's sense and computable contracts, horizon, diagonal and tolerance, `n ↦ decide (Timely S ε n)` is a computable function, exactly the quoted procedure (the sibling run that produces `Y n` enters as the hypothesis `hY`). But the on-`G` theorems at grade (a) need `G` as an e.c. *sentence-family gate* — FAF's `UnaryRuler` (an FP indicator on the unary day) — and membership costs a sibling run to `F n`, which is not FP in general. So every on-`G` theorem is stated along an e.c. sub-fragment `G' = {n | t n = 0}` (`AgreeAlong`, D3) and the whole-`G` forms carry an FP certificate `hcert : ∀ n, t n = 0 ↔ Timely S ε n` as a (c) (`engineA_ofCert`, `condTower_onG_ofCert`). This is anson-020's flag (i) made precise: the "provably small" bound sequence is e.c. exactly when the sub-fragment is.

## F3 — non-vacuity of `G` is a diagonal property (anson-023's quantifier-order flag, moved to the definition)

**Severity: imprecision, potentially blocking the construction's reach (self-description corrected in repair round 1, audit r1 adversarial B3 (ii)).** v6 §6: "On `G`, `Y_n → truth`." What the package has: `onGSystem` (`OnG.lean`), a one-way inhabitant timely at every day at every non-negative tolerance — but its siblings are FAF's LIA *pinned* at the horizon to the decided value by a one-coordinate perturbation (`perturbAt`, an inductor by `thm:ifp`), not converged; it inhabits the on-`G` theorems' hypothesis packages (every `hz`-row has its N+ there, `OnG.lean` §E) without touching the diagonal question. `offGSystem` is off `G` at every day at every tolerance (`offG_not_timely_any`); `antiSystem` (`OnG.lean` §F) is decided at every day and timely at no tolerance below `1` — at tolerance `≥ 1` every day of it is timely, as at any decided-everywhere system (`anti_timely_iff`, audit r2 adversarial N2).
