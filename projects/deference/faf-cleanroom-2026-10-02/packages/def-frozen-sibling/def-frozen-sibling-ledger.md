Hyps cells cite the checklist rows of `def-frozen-sibling-hypotheses` they rest on; `hz` = checklist row 6, the (c) of record. **Witness regime** (audit r1 N5/N7; r2 adversarial N4): every on-`G` witness lives at `onGSystem`, whose polarity pattern (even/odd) is e.c., so by findings F4 the quote is redundant there — machine-checked in repair round 2 (`hpat₁_onG`, `hpat₀_onG`, `engineA_truth_onG_noHz`: the pattern route reaches `engineA_truth_onG`'s conclusion with no `hz`); the witnesses inhabit the statements' full hypothesis packages (N+) but not the regime where the quote is load-bearing, and whether an explicit computable witness can exhibit that regime is **open** (no theorem on disk excludes it; the round-1 "no explicit computable witness can exhibit" was a conjecture stated as a fact); "along every e.c. sub-fragment" means along every *infinite* one (`AgreeAlong` is vacuous on a finite `G'`, audit r1 fidelity N8).
