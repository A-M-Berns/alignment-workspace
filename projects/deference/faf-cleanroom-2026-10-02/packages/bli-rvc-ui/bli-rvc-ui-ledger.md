Every declaration is in namespace `Cleanroom.Bli.BliRvcUi`. "Hyps (b)/(c)" lists non-(a) hypotheses; "—" means all hypotheses are (a) or there are none. Rows marked "repair round 1" were added or regraded after audit round 1 (`bli-rvc-ui-audit-r1-fidelity`, `bli-rvc-ui-audit-r1-adversarial`); the report's § Repair round 1 lists each blocking issue and what changed. Sources cite inventory items (`bli-paper-inventory` etc.), the program (`bli-program`, `bli-program-desiderata`, `bli-program-construction`) and the mandate (`bli-rvc-ui-mandate`).
