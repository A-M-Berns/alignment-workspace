Not blocking: attempted in two forms, one proved and disclosed as N−, one OPEN with a correct statement; but the orchestrator should decide whether `ui_strict_paper_open` is scheduled as follow-up work, since as things stand T3.5's content (bli-soto-a-082, P8 (i)) has no N+ anywhere in the run.
- **N3 — F-6's wording "through the sibling prime rather than through quantification"** misdescribes the mechanism it correctly exhibits. Soto's sentence (PDF 07 p. 2, verbatim in `pdftotext`): "the latter could only fail if a finite and `Ax`-consistent `C ∧ ¬∀mφ(m)` propositionally entailed infinitely many `φ(i)`. This isn't possible with our `Ax`, because the only way to have this is through quantification." The sibling `u' = ∀m(φ(m) ∧ φ(m))` entails every `φ(i)` *through quantification* — schema 1 applied to `u'` gives `φ(i) ∧ φ(i)`, propositional logic gives `φ(i)` — and `¬u ∧ u'` is `Ax`-consistent because neither schema relates the two primes (schema 2 is about two-variable universals). So what is refuted is "this isn't possible with our `Ax`" together with the implicit step "the only universal that instantiates to the `φ(i)` is `∀mφ(m)` itself", not the clause "through quantification", which is true of the sibling too. bli-soto-a-2-006 (i)–(ii) says exactly this with `∀m¬¬φ(m)`; F-6 and `secondLimit_fails_free`'s docstring ("that Soto's 'only through quantification' rules out") should say "a different quantification, which Soto's reason overlooks". The finding's substance (the inequality fails; the stated reason is wrong) is correct and fairly attributed.
- **N4 — The refutation of Soto's inequality itself is one Gaifman step away and is not stated.** `secondLimit_fails_free` refutes the *lemma* Soto uses (`lim_m P(¬∀mφ ∧ ⋀_{i≤m} φ(i)) = 0`); his second inequality is `P(∀mφ) ≥ lim_m P(⋀_{i≤m} φ(i))`. Probe `SecondIneqItself.lean` (elaborates) derives from the package's bound `∃ ε > 0, ∀ m, P∞(u) + ε ≤ P∞(⋀_{i≤m} inst i)` over `freeDP2 DP` — `P∞(⋀) = P∞(u ⋏ ⋀) + P∞(∼u ⋏ ⋀)` by `disjoint_add`/`congr`, and `P∞(u) ≤ P∞(u ⋏ ⋀)` because the schema makes `u` entail every instance in every completed world. Suggest adding it as a library corollary so the ledger's "**refuted** (PDF 07's second inequality …)" names a theorem about the inequality, not only about its premise. Presentation; the current Claim column describes exactly what the Lean proves.
- **N5 — Fidelity label of `secondLimit_fails_free` / `_paper` / `_paper_lia` and `secondLimit_fails_generic`: "exact (criterion level, limiting belief)" should be "variant: criterion-level analogue".** Soto's claim is about his extrapolation `P` (PDF 07 Step 2, a chained extension of a finite `Q`, `bli-extrapolation`'s object); the Lean is about every logical inductor over the schema-augmented process, as the mandate asked ("at the criterion level rather than the extrapolation's"). Same mechanism, different object; the parenthesis already says so, the label should be `variant` so that a dependent does not take it for a theorem about Soto's `P`.
- **N6 — Registry payload convention.** `Tags.lean` asks every family to put the *day* first in its payload (`Nat.pair day rest`, so `Size.atomDay` can read it). The package's family-`9` atoms use the first component as a slot (`⟨0, ·⟩` universal-role atoms, `⟨1, ·⟩` the free `u`/`u'`, `⟨2, c⟩` free instances), and family `8` uses day `0` with slot `j` in `rest` — the latter follows the convention, the former does not. No theorem here puts these atoms through `smallSet`/`atomDay`, so nothing logical follows, but F-15 and interface note 5 should tell the orchestrator that the registry row for family `9` reads "slot first, not day", or the payloads should be moved to `⟨0, ⟨slot, c⟩⟩`.
- **N7 — `tableUI`'s Fidelity says "variant: unlinked universal-role atom" while `twoSided_ui_unsat_paper`'s says "exact".** Both are defensible (the refutation is stronger with an unlinked `u`), but one label should propagate; say "exact (unlinked `u`, harmless for a refutation of `UITwoSided` — see `tableUI`)" on the theorem.
- **N8 — `ui_free_nontrivial`'s `hfree` is stronger than needed and the docstring could say so.** It forbids the base from mentioning *any* family-`9` atom, which is what `paperDP_free9` gives and is fine for the paper base, but it also means the free witness cannot be stated over `paperAx T` or `paperAx2 T` (which mention family `9`).
