## 5. The judgment the repair asked for: `ui_strict_fresh` is N−

The report's T3.5 paragraph records that the two round-1 lenses disagreed (fidelity: N+ for T3.5 proper, N− only as T3.3's guard; adversarial: N− outright), that the repair followed the adversarial grading, and asks the next round to judge. Judgment: **N− is right.** What `ui_strict_fresh` exhibits, unfolded, is: a fresh atom that no stage decides has limiting belief `< 1` (`lic_nonDogmatism_dual`), while a family of theorems of the base has limiting belief `1` (`lic_provind_true`), and the schema `u 🡒 inst c` contributes nothing to either fact — `paperAx T` and `paperDP T` have the same completed-theory worlds at this family, and `UILimit` there is `P∞(u) ≤ 1` (`uiLimit_of_inst_limit_one`, verified). [STANDARDS](../../STANDARDS.md) §3's N+ standard is "exercises the content"; the content of bli-soto-a-082 is that a *universal* whose instances are believed stays improbable, and here the universal role is a label on an unconstrained atom (the same object as T3.3's "unlinked atom" failure mode, with the link present but idle). The fidelity lens's reading — that the mandate asked for exactly this construction and the five conjuncts are computed — is true but is about fidelity to the mandate, and the mandate's pre-grading is what F-19 shows to be impossible. STANDARDS wins over the mandate (STANDARDS §0); the conflict is flagged here as required. Consequence for dependents is stated correctly in F-19 and interface note 2: do not cite UI over a family whose instances are theorems of the base.
