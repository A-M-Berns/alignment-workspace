## T1 — the diagonal theorem (core, P) — **proved**

**Claim.** For `p : ℕ → [0,1]` and any `R : ℕ → CausalRule`, `x := diag R p` satisfies: for every `k` with `Tendsto (prefixSum ((R k).w x)) atTop atTop`, `weightedAverage ((R k).w x) (fun j => truthR x j − p j) ≈ₙ 0`.

**Lean.** `diag_pseudorandom` (P; day-varying target, as the mandate recommends for T9); `diag_pseudorandom_const` (C; constant `p`: `weightedAverage ((R k).w x) (truthR x) ≈ₙ p`); `diag_pseudorandom_of_surjective` (L; `Countable ι` via a surjection). The analytic core is `weightedAverage_tendsto_of_mart_le` (P), stated for any stream whose running products are bounded by `2/mass i`.

**Proof structure (all in `Diagonal.lean`).** `pot_congr`: the potential through day `n−1` reads days `< n` only. `pot_update_avg`: `p n · pot(x[n:=true]) + (1 − p n) · pot(x[n:=false]) = pot(x) + 2·mass n`, for **every** real `p n` (the identity is `p(1−p) + (1−p)(0−p) = 0`; F1). `pot_diag_succ_le`, `pot_diag_le`: along the diagonal `pot ≤ 2·∑ mass ≤ 2` (needs `p n ∈ [0,1]` for "min ≤ average"). `mart_diag_le`: each signed running product `≤ 2/mass i`. `sum_log_bound`: with FAF's `sub_two_mul_sq_le_log_one_add` and `Real.log_prod`, `∑_{[i,n)} (nudge − 2·nudge²) ≤ log(2/mass i)` where `nudge = ±tilt·w·(t − p)` and `|nudge| ≤ tilt ≤ ½`. `window_bound`: both signs give `|∑_{[i,n)} w (t − p)| ≤ log(2/mass i)/tilt i + 2·tilt i·∑_{[i,n)} w`. The limit: for `ε`, pick `m` with `2/(m+2) < ε/2`, `i := pair k m`; `|D_n|/S_n ≤ B_i/S_n + 2·tilt i` with `B_i/S_n → 0` by `Tendsto.div_atTop`.

**Hypotheses.** `hp : ∀ j, 0 ≤ p j ∧ p j ≤ 1`, `hdiv` — both (a) (conditions on the parameters; the divergence hypothesis is the content: a rule with convergent realized sum is excluded by hypothesis, not by `weightedAverage`'s junk value). Traps checked: causality is strict (`j < n`); weights are in `[0,1]` for every stream (fields of `CausalRule`); `diag` is a `def`; `p ∈ {0,1}` allowed.

**Witness (N+, `Witnesses.lean`).** `diag_not_eventuallyConst`: with `R 0 = constRule` and `p ∈ (0,1)`, `diag R p` is not eventually constant (through `not_eventuallyConst_of_average`: a `{0,1}` stream with plain density in `(0,1)` is not eventually constant). `diag_even_density`: with `R 1 = evenRule`, the density on even days is `p`. **Adaptive witness (repair round 1, from the adversarial audit's probe):** `constRule` and `evenRule` are functions of the day alone; `prevRule` (weight `1` on day `n ≥ 1` iff `x (n−1)`) reads the stream, and `diag_prev_density` shows that with `R 0 = constRule`, `R 1 = prevRule`, `p ∈ (0,1)`, its realized sum on the diagonal diverges and the truth frequency on the days following a `true` day tends to `p` — so the adaptive clause of `CausalRule` (the content of strict causality) is exercised. Numerical sanity at `p = ½` over constant rules: `diag_const_half_zero` (day `0` is a tie, `false`) and `diag_const_half_one` (day `1` compensates: `true`), by unfolding the finite potential and `nlinarith` on `mass 0 · tilt 0² > 0` — no `native_decide`, no `decide` on reals (kind `L`: sanity computations, not non-vacuity witnesses).
