## K1 — "for every inductor `P`" is false; the relative form is what is provable (imprecision in the plan; confirmed)

**Pointer.** `plan` `### li-pseudorandom` ("so that `PseudorandomFrequency truth p f P` holds for every inductor `P` at a chosen `p`"); mandate § Context 1.

**Finding.** `PseudorandomFrequency truth p f P` is a property of the pair `(truth, P)`: the weightings are evaluated on `P`'s prices. For a fixed non-trivial *computable* `truth` (with infinitely many true members), a market that prices each undecided member at its truth value defeats the claim, by the following unformalized argument: the P-generable weighting `W i := price φ_i i` — member `i` weighted by its own omniscient price, a continuous feature (a hard indicator "`1` if the price exceeds `½`" is not an `EF`; repair round 1 corrected the example) — puts positive weight exactly on the true members, so its weighted truth frequency is `1` and it diverges. Not refuted in Lean: an explicit `IsLogicalInductor` instance for that omniscient market needs a computability certificate (the criterion bundles `ComputableMarket`, which is why the argument needs `truth` computable) and a no-exploitation proof (the plausible values of any trader are bounded by the number of simultaneously undecided members, which is one for a sparse decision schedule), which is a package of its own. What this package proves instead is the **relative** form, in two shapes: T4 (`pseudorandomFrequency_of_history`: for each fixed `P` a family exists, depending on `P`) and T5/T6 (`pseudorandomFrequency_of_causal`, `truthStar_pseudorandom`: a family pseudorandom relative to the market *it itself induces*, for any causal builder, in particular the LIA over the process deciding it). The mandate's reading that the plan meant "for the inductor of record" is ATTRIBUTION-UNVETTED; T6 delivers that reading. A cleaner, FAF-shaped route to the counterexample (round-2 adversarial audit, item 8; still unformalized): the LIA over `atomDP a x id` — the non-strict profile `g = id` is precisely the omniscient configuration `omniscient_causalBuilder_id` (`Witnesses.lean`), and for primitive recursive `a`, `x` the delay-one analogue is a certified inductor (`atomDP_succ_computable`, `Market.lean`); provability induction along the e.c. family would give `P n (atom (a n)) ≈ₙ truth n`, and the weighting `price (atom (a i)) i` then selects the true members. **Severity: imprecision in the plan; the package is unaffected.**

## F1 — The mandate's suggested activation indexing has a redundant factor; the package uses one index (presentation; new)

**Pointer.** Mandate T1 ("Index the rules by pairs `(k, m) := Nat.unpair` with … mass `c_{k,m} := 2^{-(k+m+2)}`; rule `(k,m)` is activated at day `N := k+m`").

**Finding.** With a single index `i` running over `Nat.pair k m`, mass `2^{-(i+1)}` and activation at day `i`, the potential is a finite sum at each day and the total mass is `≤ 1`; the pair-indexed mass is unnecessary. The formalized potential is `pot R p x n = ∑_{i<n} mass i · (mart(+) + mart(−))` with `mart` over the window `[i, n)`, and the mandate's one-line identity holds for **every** real `p n` (`pot_update_avg`), the range restriction `p n ∈ [0,1]` entering only at the "min ≤ weighted average" step (`pot_diag_succ_le`). **Severity: presentation.**

## F2 — The `[0,1]` clamp is realized by restricting the rule to the truth prefix, making causality automatic (presentation; new)

**Pointer.** Mandate T1 trap (ii), T5 ("clamp ((W_k n).denote (liaHistory (atomDP a x g)))").

**Finding.** The builder rule is defined as `clamp ((W n).denote (B (restrict x n)))` — the feature is evaluated on the market built from the truth *prefix* of days `< n` — so the rule is strictly causal for **any** builder `B`, with no proof obligation in the definition; the causality hypothesis `CausalBuilder B g` enters only in the theorem, where it identifies the realized weights with `(W n).denote (B x)` through T3 (`builderRule_w_eq`). This removes the circularity the mandate warns about ("`truth⋆` must be a `def` built from `diag`") at the definitional level: `truthStar` is `diag` of explicitly defined rules, with no hypotheses baked into the definition. **Severity: presentation (a cleaner rendering than the mandate's).**

## F3 — `IsLogicalInductor` is not needed for the pseudorandomness of the family, only for `thm:benford` (presentation; new)

**Pointer.** Mandate T6.3 ("its `IsLogicalInductor` instance is T7's").

**Finding.** `truthStar_pseudorandom` (T6.3) holds for the LIA's prices as a `History`, with no inductor certificate: causality of the LIA over `atomDP` (`liaHistory_atomDP_causal`) is a property of the recursion `liaStates`, not of the criterion. The certificate is needed only to *apply* `lic_learning_pseudorandom_frequency` (`truthStar_learned`), i.e. to conclude that the LIA's prices on the family tend to `p`. The (a)-grade deliverable is therefore `truthStar_pseudorandom` — FAF's predicate relative to the LIA's prices, needing no certificate. But the word *computable* belongs to T7 in every reading, and both named dependents need it: `bli-program` §3.2(b) (L3) says each trade pays ≈ ½ "by `lic_learning_pseudorandom_frequency`" over a *computable* family pseudorandom relative to the LIA's own generable weightings, and §3.6(iii) (K3) reads "Moves" off `lic_learning_pseudorandom_frequency` on day `n`; both go through `thm:benford`, whose Lean form takes `[IsLogicalInductor P DP]` — for the process of record that is `truthStar_isLogicalInductor`, OPEN over T7. So L3, K3, and every use of `lic_learning_pseudorandom_frequency` on the process of record carry `partial: over OPEN computability (li-pseudorandom T7)`. Only a dependent that consumes `PseudorandomFrequency` itself as a hypothesis-discharger, with no inductor instance in play, reads `proved`; no such dependent is named. (Repair round 1: the previous version of this paragraph said L3/K3 "get it at grade (a) now" — false, round-1 fidelity audit B2.) **Severity: presentation (a finer split than the mandate's row rule).**

## F4 — The T9 two-family form is the reindexing exercise the mandate said it was; the mandate's construction (the disjoint union of the rule families) is the right one (presentation; rewritten in repair round 2)

**Pointer.** Mandate T9 ("`diag` over the disjoint union of the rule families … each is pseudorandom against weightings that read the *other* families' truth values"); round-2 fidelity audit B1 and its probe `audit-r2-probes/EvenLift.lean`.

**Finding.** As first written (rounds 0–1) this finding claimed that, at the level of FAF's `PseudorandomFrequency`, the subfamily statement needed the reindexed *feature progression* `W' j := if Even j then W (j/2) else const 0` to be P-generable — a `MachineSpliceStream` closure FAF lacks — or a subfamily-relative predicate, and filed the difficulty as "definitional" and as an imprecision in the mandate's expectation. That was wrong, and the package, not the mandate, was at fault. T1 (`diag_pseudorandom`) quantifies over *arbitrary* strictly causal rules and never sees a feature progression: the day-reindexed **rule** (weight `clamp ((W m).denote (B (restrict x (2m))))` on day `2m`, `0` on odd days) is a `CausalRule` whether or not any reindexed progression is P-generable. What the subfamily statement needs is a diagonal whose rule family *contains* these lifted rules — exactly the mandate's "`diag` over the disjoint union of the rule families" — and the round-1 `truthStar₂` diagonalized over the builder rules only, so it had not been given the rules it needed. Repair round 2: `truthStar₂` is redefined over the disjoint union of the builder rules, their even lifts and their odd lifts (`Lift.lean`, `Joint.lean`), and `truthStar₂_even_pseudorandom` / `truthStar₂_odd_pseudorandom` are proved verbatim (FAF's predicate, every `f`, weightings free to read the other family's decided atoms); the countable-family form `truthStarω_pseudorandom` (family `r` on days `Nat.pair r m`) is proved the same way. No FAF closure property and no new predicate are involved (K5(v) withdrawn). What is true in the old finding: the *feature progression* `W'` need not be P-generable, and the package does not claim it is — the lifted rule is diagonalized against directly. **Severity: presentation (a defect in this package's earlier diagnosis, now repaired; nothing against the sources).**
