## 3. Non-blocking issues

**N1 — The D4 docstring of `interiorCoherentMarketMaker` and the report's headline sentence oversell non-dogmatism by one qualifier.** Record `Maker.lean` lines 244–246: "non-dogmatic on every sentence of `S` undecided by `D`"; [bli-coherent-mm-report](bli-coherent-mm-report.md) §State: "non-dogmatic on every undecided sentence of `S`". The theorems are correct and carry the qualifier — `interior_nonDogmatic` takes "undecided" over **finite** worlds `WD D B`, and `interior_D_ND_day_on` carries `hBS`. But the maker is stated for arbitrary `S` with no atom bound, and for a sentence of `S` outside the bound it is dogmatic although the sentence is undecided by `D` in the `PCWorld` sense. Probe `MakerOutsideBound.lean`: `atom_bound_undecided B` (a `PCWorld` consistent with `∅` holds `atom B`, another refutes it) and `interior_quote_atom_bound_eq_zero` (`(interiorCoherentMarketMaker T past ∅ B S hS (hW_empty B) hε).quote (Formula.atom B) = 0` whenever `atom B ∈ S`), through `marginal_atom_bound_eq_zero` (every weight vector over `B` atoms prices `atom B` at `0`, because the atom reads `false` in every finite world). In every record use (the recursion's `pcAtoms_bound`, `interior_D_ND_day`'s `hB`, the witnesses' `atomBoundOf`) the bound covers `S`, so nothing downstream is affected. Fix I'd accept: add "within the atom bound" to the D4 docstring and the report sentence (and to the `interiorCoherentMarketMaker` ledger row's claim column, which currently says "coherent on `S`; decided sentences at their truth" — accurate — but the row for `interior_nonDogmatic` should say "held by some and refuted by some `D`-consistent **finite** world").

**N2 — Findings numbers drifted: the Lean docstrings and the ledger cite attempt A's numbering, the reconciled findings file renumbered.** Reconciled: F10 = `IsWorldMarginal`-of-a-quote unsatisfiable, F11 = stage-conditional coherence, F12 = no atom bound in T1, F13 = the normaliser bound, F16 = classical enumeration order. **N4 — The paper-process witnesses are N+ for the hypothesis package, not for the output; the ledger and two docstrings blur the two.** `interiorMakerPaperDP` uses `B := atomBoundOf (mentionedSet T ∪ D n)`, and attempt B's `atomBoundOf A = A.sup atomBound` (no `+ 1`), so no atom is guaranteed free: `interiorMakerPaperDP_fullSupport` (ledger: N+) is "full support on `WD`" over a simplex not shown to have two worlds, and nothing at the paper process shows an undecided mentioned sentence. `pair_paperDP_sum_one` (`AttemptA/Witness.lean` line 72 and the record docstring: "the output is non-degenerate") takes an arbitrary `a` with `B = paperDP_atoms n` computed from the stage alone; for `a ≥ paperDP_atoms n` the quotes are the dogmatic pair `(0, 1)` — the sum is still `1` and the contrast with FAF's `≥ 2 − ε` stands, which is the only sense in which that output is non-degenerate. Fix: in the T4(d) ledger row say "N+ for the package (every hypothesis discharged over a real process); output non-degeneracy is the `D = ∅` witnesses'", and drop "non-degenerate" from `pair_paperDP_sum_one`'s docstring or restrict it to `a < paperDP_atoms n`.

**N5 — T4(c) is stated for a hypothetical accepted vector, not for the maker.** `responsive_near_half` and `responsive_atom_accepted_near_half` take `hacc` as a hypothesis; the mandate's wording is "whose only accepted coherent prices are near `1/2`", and nothing in the record applies it to `coherentWeights`. Probe `ResponsiveMaker.lean` does both in a few lines: `coherentWeights_responsive_near_half` (the record maker on the responsive strategy, empty stage, `a < B`, prices `atom a` within `ε` of `1/2`) and `coherentAccepts_responsive_zero_eq_half` (at `ε = 0` the only coherently accepted price is exactly `1/2`, so T1's fixed point on this strategy is unique on the mentioned atom and is not the Dirac on any world). Fix: promote both into `Contrast.lean` as the maker-level N+ for T1/T2 (Kind N+, no new hypotheses).

**N6 — The OPEN statement's hypotheses may be too weak for the natural proof; say so, or state the ℓ¹-norm variant beside it.** `omni_fixed_point` assumes `Continuous G` into the **product** topology on `Sentence → ℝ` plus a uniform ℓ¹ bound. With those, the pairing `μ ↦ ∑' φ, G μ φ · ν{φ}` need not be continuous (mass can escape to the tail: `G μ_k = e_{φ_k}` converges to `0` coordinatewise with ℓ¹ norm `1`), so neither Ky Fan's minimax inequality nor a Schauder-type argument applies as stated. If `G` is continuous into ℓ¹ (norm topology), the statement follows from Ky Fan's inequality on the compact convex `ProbabilityMeasure (ConsistentWorlds D)` with `f(ν, μ) := ⟨G μ, ν − μ⟩` (affine in `ν`, continuous in `μ`, `f(μ, μ) = 0`), which Mathlib lacks. This is my analysis, not machine-checked, and does not change the OPEN status; it is a pointer for the continuation the findings file (R8) invites. The OPEN statement is otherwise what it says: the `tsum` is summable under `hsum` (payouts and prices in `[0,1]`), `omniPrice` is a genuine measure value, and the hypothesis package is satisfiable (`G ≡ 0`).

**N7 — `coherent_fixed_point` dropped the mandate's `hB`; the headline docstring should point at `WD`'s caveat.** The record headline is stronger than the mandate's shape (fewer hypotheses, same conclusion over finite worlds) and its `PCWorld` form carries `hB`, so this is correct. But "supported on the `D`-consistent worlds" in the headline docstring (`FixedPoint.lean` line 123) is the `worldOf` reading, under which a stage sentence outside the bound constrains nothing or everything. Probe `WDOutsideBound.lean`: `WD_neg_atom_bound_eq_univ` (`WD {∼atom B} B = univ`) and `not_mem_WD_atom_bound` (`WD {atom B} B` is empty, so `hW` fails although `atom B` is satisfiable). The caveat is on `WD`'s docstring ("meaningful under `hB`"); one clause in the headline docstring would close the gap.

Also checked and found in order (no issue): the T6 coherence clause is empty on a day whose priced set is empty — probe `CoherentQuoteOnEmpty.lean`, `coherentQuoteOn_empty_iff : CoherentQuoteOn V ∅ D B ↔ ∃ w, IsWorldMeasure w D` — and the package guards it with `pcOverlay_paperDP_mentioned_nonempty` (nonempty from some day on at the witness), which is the right guard.
