### Disagreements

**None on any claim.** Every target both attempts stated is proved by both over objects proved identical or equivalent (`Worlds.lean`, `coherentAccepts_iff_attemptB`), and the five load-bearing items are each reached by both routes. The differences are of **strength and shape**, all recorded: attempt A's T1 (and T2(a)/T3(a)) need no atom bound where attempt B's carry the mandate's `hB` (F12); attempt B's abstract lemma has the weaker continuity hypothesis (R2); the two D3 predicates bundle different side conditions (R1); the two makers choose different candidates (so the two recursions are different markets, R3); attempt B's T4(a)/(c) are general in the sentence where attempt A's fix an atom, and attempt A alone has the contrast at FAF's recursive market (R5).
