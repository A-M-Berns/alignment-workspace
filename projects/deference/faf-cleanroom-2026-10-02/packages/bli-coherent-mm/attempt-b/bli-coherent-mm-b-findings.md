## F-B1 — The mandate's angle-B normaliser bound is false; positivity is what the argument needs (local error in the mandate; harmless)

Pointer: `bli-coherent-mm-mandate` §Attempt angles (B): "the denominator is `≥ ∑ (p + G p) = 1` by the identity, so `g` is continuous and simplex-valued".

The identity is `∑ u, p u · G p u = 0`, not `∑ u, G p u = 0`, so `∑ (p + G p) = 1 + ∑ G p` need not be `1`, and the clipped normaliser `Z(p) = ∑ max 0 (p u + G p u)` need not be `≥ 1`: on two worlds with `p = (0.9, 0.1)` and `G p = (0.05, −0.45)` (identity: `0.045 − 0.045 = 0`), `p + G p = (0.95, −0.35)` and `Z = 0.95 < 1`. What the fixed-point argument needs is only `Z(p) > 0` on the simplex, which **is** true: `∑ u, p u (p u + G p u) = ∑ p u² > 0` by the identity, and each summand `p u (p u + G p u) ≤ max 0 (p u + G p u)` because `0 ≤ p u ≤ 1` (`clipNorm_pos`, `FixedPoint.lean`). The rest of the sketch (on the support `G p u = p u (Z − 1)`, off it `G p u ≤ 0`, `(Z − 1) ∑ p u² = 0`) is correct and is what `clipMap_fixed_le` proves. Severity: local error in the mandate's sketch; no effect on the result.

## F-B2 — "`IsWorldMarginal (coherentMarketMaker …).quote S D B`" is false as written; the marginal statement must be about the extended table (imprecision in the mandate; fixed in the statement of record)

Pointer: `bli-coherent-mm-mandate` T2(b) (`coherentMarketMaker_coherent`), T6 (`pcOverlay_coherent_mentioned : ∀ n, IsWorldMarginal (pcQuote DP ov n) …`).

bli-finite's `IsWorldMarginal V A D B` demands `V φ = ∑ u, w u · payout_u φ` for **every `φ` generated by `A`** (`GenBy A`: the Boolean closure), while a `RationalBeliefState` quotes `0` off its entry list — so the maker's quote, which is `0` off `S`, is not a world marginal on `GenBy S` whenever `GenBy S ⊋ S` contains a sentence of positive marginal (e.g. `⊤ = ⊥ 🡒 ⊥`: generated by anything, quote `0`, marginal `1`). The literal statement is therefore unprovable and, for compound sentences, false. The statements of record are: (i) `IsWorldMarginal (piTable w) S D B` for the maker's **extended** table `piTable w` (true on all of `GenBy S`), together with `∀ φ ∈ S, quote φ = piTable w φ`; and (ii) bli-finite's `CoherentOn` of the table restricted to `S` (`coherentMarketMaker_coherentOn`, `pcOverlay_coherentOn`), which is what "exactly coherent on `S`" means. The ledger marks the marginal form `variant` for this reason. Dependents (`bli-measure`, M5) that cite "`pcOverlay_coherent_mentioned` in the `IsWorldMarginal`/`CoherentOn` form" should cite the conjunction `pcOverlay_coherent_mentioned` (marginal of the extended table + agreement on the mentioned set) or `pcOverlay_coherentOn`. Severity: imprecision in the mandate; the intended content is proved.

## F-B3 — FAF's `MarketMaker` is provably incoherent at every finite time (bli-slides-044's gap and Soto's PDF 05 fn. 1, as theorems) — confirmed, Known issue 6 (finding of record)

Pointer: `bli-coherent-mm-mandate` T4(a); bli-slides-044; Soto PDF 05 fn. 1 ("we cannot just ensure that the trader only buys at the price of 1 or sells at the price of 0 … Consider a trader that buys a share of `W₁ⁿ` and one of `W₂ⁿ`. Then we can't put price 1 in both").

`marketMakerAccepts_pair_sum_ge` (`Contrast.lean`): every state FAF's `MarketMakerAccepts` accepts against `T_pair` (one share of `φ`, one of `∼φ`, constant coefficients) at tolerance `ε` has `quote φ + quote (∼φ) ≥ 2 − ε`, because the acceptance test ranges over **all** Boolean tables on the support, including the propositionally impossible one in which `φ` and `∼φ` both pay. Hence `marketMaker_incoherent_on_pair`: for `ε < 1` (always, at FAF's `marketMakerError n`), `MarketMaker T_pair past ε` prices `φ` and `∼φ` to a sum above `1`, and `marketMaker_not_worldMarginal_on_pair`: it is not a world marginal on `{φ, ∼φ}` relative to any stage. Soto's footnote reads the same obstruction off FAF's style of maker (ATTRIBUTION-UNVETTED: the footnote is about his own PC-market maker, written before FAF existed; the identification with FAF's `MarketMaker` is this run's). The remedy is the one both Soto and the program describe: price worlds, not Boolean tables — `CoherentAccepts` checks only `D`-consistent worlds, and on the same `T_pair` every world measure gives value exactly `0` (`pair_coherent_value_eq_zero`). Severity: the gap bli-slides-044 names; FAF's construction is correct for the LIC (which is a limit statement) and simply not exactly coherent at finite times.

## F-B4 — Soto's Theorem 2 holds; of his two maps, the clip-and-normalise map closes the gap (confirms Known issue 1; diagnosis is this run's)

Pointer: Soto PDF 05 p. 3, Theorem 2 and its sketch; `bli-coherent-mm-mandate` Known issue 1.

Soto's first map `fix(P)(W) := P(W) + B(W)·P(W)·δ(P)` keeps the simplex but, as he says, breaks at `P_fix(W) = 0`: a fixed point says nothing about `B(W)` where `P(W) = 0`, and nothing forces `B(W) ≤ 0` there. (It cannot be repaired by choosing `δ`: multiplying the step by `P(W)` is what loses the off-support information.) His second map subtracts `min_{W'} B(W')` and normalises; at a fixed point with `P_fix(W) = 0` at the minimiser he reports being unable to derive a contradiction — and with that map there is in general no contradiction to derive: the minimiser's coordinate can stay at `0` while some `B(W') > 0` survives at a world whose coordinate is kept positive by the normalisation (this run's reading; ATTRIBUTION-UNVETTED as to why his attempt stalled). Angle B's map `g(P)(W) := max 0 (P(W) + B(W)) / ∑ max 0 (·)` (FAF's clip `max 0 (min 1 (x + shares))`, renormalised instead of clipped at `1`) does not multiply by `P(W)` and does not subtract the minimum: at a fixed point an off-support world has `max 0 (B(W)) = 0`, i.e. `B(W) ≤ 0` **directly**, and the on-support worlds have `B(W) = P(W)(Z − 1)` with `Z = 1` forced by the identity. `coherent_fixed_point_abstract` is Theorem 2 in its "necessary and sufficient" form, with the identity derived for the instance (`sum_worldValue_eq_zero`). The inventory's diagnosis of the stall ("clipping at `0`" rather than "subtracting the minimum") is confirmed in the sense that the clip is exactly the device that makes the off-support case trivial. Severity: Soto's claim is correct and now proved; the sketch's gap was real.

## F-B5 — "Respects `D̄`" and program §3.8's restriction are automatic for measures on `D`-consistent worlds (confirms Known issue 3; imprecision in the program)

Pointer: Soto PDF 05 Def 3 ("respects the deductive process iff `P_n(φ) = 1` for each `φ ∈ D_n`. Equivalently, if all its worlds with non-zero probability satisfy `φ`"); `bli-program` §3.8 ("restrict the domain to measures giving decided sentences their truth value").

`coherentMarketMaker_respects` / `_respects_false` and `piTable_eq_one_of_decided`: a world measure supported on `D`-consistent worlds prices every sentence true in all of them at `1` and every sentence false in all of them at `0` — with no atom bound and no extra constraint, for any sentence (not only those of `D`). Severity: imprecision in the program's wording.

## F-B6 — Coherence on all of `smallSet n` is the same existence theorem; only its cost is open (confirms Known issue 4; imprecision in program §2.7)

Pointer: `bli-program` §2.7; `bli-coherent-mm-mandate` T2(e), T5.

T2 and T3 are parametric in `S ⊇ mentionedSet T`; `coherentMarketMakerSmall` is the `S = smallSet n` instance (one abbrev). What is genuinely open is complexity: the maker enumerates `W_D ⊆ FiniteWorld B` with `B` the atom bound of `S ∪ D`, i.e. up to `2^B` worlds, and by T5 (`pos_iff_satisfiable`, `interior_pos_iff_satisfiable`) its full-support prices decide `D`-relative satisfiability of every sentence of `S` over those atoms — so a day-`n` price table on `smallSet n` solves SAT instances with as many variables as `smallSet n` has atoms. That is why the record prices `mentionedSet T` (the maker's own enumeration) and why the `smallSet` variant is a cost question, not an existence one. The program should say "coherence on `smallSet n` exists; its computability within the LIA's resource bound is open". Severity: imprecision.

## F-B9 — The recursion needs a fallback on uninhabited stages, and the honest coherence statement is stage-conditional (new; imprecision in the mandate's T6 statement)

Pointer: `bli-coherent-mm-mandate` T6 (`pcOverlay_coherent_mentioned : ∀ n, …`); D5.

FAF's `DeductiveProcess` has no consistency field, so some stage `DP.D n` may have no consistent world at all, and then no world measure exists on `W_D`: the recursion uses the empty state there (`pcCore`'s `dite`). On such a day every stage-relative statement is vacuous (`not_stageInhabited_vacuous`), the day bound and non-exploitation are unaffected, but the coherence headline must carry `(∃ v : PCWorld, v.ConsistentWith (DP.D n))` as a hypothesis — which is exactly FAF's `hworld` and holds at every stage of `paperDP 𝗜𝚺₁` (`pcOverlay_paperDP_coherent_mentioned`, unconditional). `StageInhabited DP ov n` (a finite world over the day's atoms) is equivalent to it (`stageInhabited_iff`). The mandate's unconditional `∀ n` is therefore true only for processes with a consistent world at every stage; `PCInductor.coh` is stated with that hypothesis. Severity: imprecision; no mathematical loss.

## F-B12 — PDF 04's payoff scheme: the running total is a uniform conditional probability, and it fails exactly on undecided primes (confirms Known issue 7 and bli-soto-a-2-003; local error / hidden assumption, conceded by the source)

Pointer: Soto PDF 04 pp. 1–2 ("intermediate payoffs that end up adding up as necessary"; "we need all prime formulas to be eventually decided for this idea to work"); bli-soto-a-2-003 (i), (iii).

`Payoffs.lean` makes the scheme exact as finite arithmetic (`variant`: not FAF's market). (i) `payoff_eq_uniform`: the running total a share of `W` has received is `1/|completions s|` if `W` completes the decision state `s` and `0` otherwise — the probability of `W` under the **uniform** distribution on assignments conditioned on the decisions — and Soto's three increment rules are exactly the differences of that total (`increment_confirm`, `increment_refute`, `increment_of_refuted`, `sum_increments`). (ii) `payoff_full`/`bundle_full`: once every prime is decided every world and every bundle `φ` pays its truth, which is the only case in which "the expected reward works out" as the source says. (iii) On a never-decided prime the total of an unrefuted world stays at `1/2^(n−k)`, i.e. the scheme silently assigns probability `1/2` to each undecided prime, independently; `half_forcing` shows the per-step consequence the inventory noted: splitting an unrefuted world on a fresh prime and buying the cheaper child at any `θ < 1/2` yields an immediate payoff `2^{−(m+1)}` exceeding the cost `θ·P(W)` whenever `P(W) ≤ 2^{−m}`. The scheme therefore forces every undecided prime toward `1/2` regardless of evidence — a hidden assumption, not a theorem of the construction; the source concedes the undecided case. ATTRIBUTION-UNVETTED: the uniform-average reading and the `½`-forcing are this run's observations about the arithmetic, not claims about Soto's intent. Severity: local error / hidden assumption in PDF 04, already conceded.
