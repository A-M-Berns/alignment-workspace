## T1 — the coherent fixed point (core, Kind P, headline M1) — proved

**Abstract lemma** `coherent_fixed_point_abstract {ι} [Fintype ι] [Nonempty ι] (G) (hG : ContinuousOn G (stdSimplex ℝ ι)) (hid : ∀ p ∈ stdSimplex ℝ ι, ∑ u, p u * G p u = 0) : ∃ p ∈ stdSimplex ℝ ι, ∀ u, G p u ≤ 0`. Kind P (infrastructure), Fidelity exact (Soto's Theorem 2 in its "necessary and sufficient" form).

*Which map, and what its fixed-point argument needed.* Angle B's map, `clipMap G p u := max 0 (p u + G p u) / clipNorm G p` with `clipNorm G p := ∑ u', max 0 (p u' + G p u')` — the simplex form of FAF's `priceAdjustment` (`max 0 (min 1 (x + shares))`), renormalised. The argument needed exactly three facts:

1. `clipNorm_pos`: the normaliser is **positive** on the simplex: `∑ p u (p u + G p u) = ∑ p u² > 0` by the identity, and `p u (p u + G p u) ≤ p u · max 0 (·) ≤ max 0 (·)` since `0 ≤ p u ≤ 1`. The mandate's angle-B sketch claims the normaliser is `≥ ∑ (p + G p) = 1` "by the identity"; that is **false** in general (`p = (0.9, 0.1)`, `G p = (0.05, −0.45)` satisfies the identity and gives normaliser `0.95`), but positivity is all that is needed (finding F-B1). Continuity (`clipMap_continuousOn`) and self-mapping (`clipMap_mapsTo`) follow.
2. `clipMap_fixed_le`: at a fixed point with normaliser `Z`, coordinatewise `p u · Z = max 0 (p u + G p u)`; on the support `G p u = p u (Z − 1)`, off it `G p u ≤ 0`; the identity gives `(Z − 1) ∑ p u² = 0`, so `Z = 1` and `G p ≤ 0` everywhere.
3. Brouwer: FAF's `BrouwerProof.stdSimplex_hasFPP` (Sperner; `fixed_of_hasFPP` gives the `ContinuousOn`/`MapsTo` form on `Fin d → ℝ`), grade (a); transported from `Fin (card ι)` to `ι` by `Fintype.equivFin` (`coherent_fixed_point_abstract_fin` is the `Fin d` form).

No Kakutani was needed (the correspondence route of angle B was not used; `Cleanroom.Found.FixKakutani` is not imported).

**Headline** `coherent_fixed_point (T : Strategy n) (past) (D) (B) (hB : ∀ φ ∈ mentionedSet T ∪ D, atomBound φ ≤ B) (hW : ∃ u : FiniteWorld B, (worldOf u).ConsistentWith D) : ∃ p : FiniteWorld B → ℝ, IsRealWorldMeasure D B p ∧ (∀ u ∈ worldsOf D B, T.value V_p (worldOf u).payout ≤ 0) ∧ ∀ v : PCWorld, v.ConsistentWith D → T.value V_p v.payout ≤ 0`, where `V_p := Function.update (beliefHistory past) n (fun φ => ∑ u, p u * (worldOf u).payout φ)` literally (the mandate's shape) and `IsRealWorldMeasure D B p := (∀ u, 0 ≤ p u) ∧ ∑ u, p u = 1 ∧ ∀ u, p u ≠ 0 → (worldOf u).ConsistentWith D`.

*Derived, not assumed.* The identity for the instance is `sum_worldValue_eq_zero` (uses only `∑ p = 1`: `∑ u, p u * value_u = ∑_{φ ∈ support} shares φ · (∑ u p u payout_u φ − V_p n φ) = 0` through FAF's `Strategy.value_eq_sum_support`); continuity of `p ↦ T.value V_p (payout u)` is `continuous_worldValue`, from `EF.continuous_denote` termwise over the trade list (`continuous_tradeList_value`) composed with the continuous affine map `p ↦ V_p` (`continuous_fixedHistory`, `continuous_priceOf`); nonemptiness of the simplex on `ι := ↥(worldsOf D B)` from `hW`. The `PCWorld` form restricts `v` to `B` atoms (`FiniteWorld.restrict`, bli-finite's `holds_worldOf_restrict` under `hB`) and uses FAF's `value_eq_of_world_eqOn_support` (`value_restrict_eq`).

## T8 — Soto's intermediate payoffs (extension, Kind P + findings) — proved, `variant`

`Payoffs.lean` (namespace `….AttemptB.Payoffs`, imports only Mathlib: PDF 04's scheme is not FAF's market, which pays by world at the end, so it is formalized as finite arithmetic over a decision state and disclosed `variant`). `World n := Fin n → Bool`, `Decision n := Fin n → Option Bool`, `Refuted W s := ∃ i, s i = some (!W i)`, `confirmed W s` (Soto's `k`), `decided s`, the running total `payoff W s := if Refuted W s then 0 else 1 / 2^(n − confirmed W s)`, `decideAt`, `increment W s i b := payoff W (decideAt s i b) − payoff W s`.

* **(a)** `payoff_eq_uniform : payoff W s = if W ∈ completions s then 1 / |completions s| else 0` with `card_completions : |completions s| = 2^(n − decided s)` — the running total **is** the uniform-completion probability of `W` given the decisions (bli-soto-a-2-003 (i), this run's reading; ATTRIBUTION-UNVETTED). Soto's three increment rules are the differences of `payoff`: `increment_confirm` (`1/2^(n−k−1) − 1/2^(n−k)` on a fresh agreeing decision), `increment_refute` (`−1/2^(n−k)`), `increment_of_refuted` (`0` afterwards); hence any refinement path telescopes, `sum_increments : (pathIncrements W s path).sum = payoff W (applyPath s path) − payoff W s`.
* **(b)** `payoff_full : payoff W s = if W = ofFull s hfull then 1 else 0` once every prime is decided, and `bundle_full : ∑ W ∈ univ.filter φ, payoff W s = if φ (ofFull s hfull) then 1 else 0` — a share of `φ` (the bundle of `φ`-worlds, Soto's Def 1) totals `1[φ]` (the identity item 031 claims, over a finite refinement sequence; `φ` a decidable predicate on assignments).
* **(c)** `half_forcing (m) (hθ0 : 0 ≤ θ) (hθ : θ < 1/2) (hP : P ≤ 1/2^m) : θ * P < 1 / 2^(m+1)` with `child_payoff : 1/2^(m+1) = (1/2)·(1/2^m)` — the cheaper child's immediate payoff exceeds its cost; a per-step inequality, not FAF's `Exploits` (2-003 (iii)).

What the arithmetic says about the source (finding F-B12): the scheme is consistent exactly when every prime is eventually decided — then (b) makes every world and every bundle pay its truth — and on an undecided prime the running total stays at the conditional probability `1/2^(n−k)`, which is the "chances of undecidability" the source says must be taken into account; the uniform `1/2` per undecided prime is a modelling choice the source does not justify, and (c) shows a trader can profit from it at any sub-`½` price. Hypotheses: (a) throughout.
