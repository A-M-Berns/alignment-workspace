## Angle A, as built

**The fixed point** (`FixedPoint.lean`). Nash's map `nashMap G p u := (p u + max (G p u) 0) / (1 + ∑ u', max (G p u') 0)` is continuous on all of `ι → ℝ` (`continuous_nashMap`; the denominator is `≥ 1`) and simplex-preserving (`nashMap_mem_stdSimplex`). At a fixed point `p u · M = max (G p u) 0` with `M := ∑ max(G p, 0)` (`nashMap_fixed_le`): if some `G p u₀ > 0` then `M > 0`, every `u` with `p u > 0` has `G p u > 0`, and `∑ u, p u · G p u > 0` (`Finset.sum_pos'`: nonnegative terms, one positive because `∑ p = 1`), against the identity. Brouwer is FAF's `BrouwerProof.stdSimplex_hasFPP` on `stdSimplex ℝ (Fin n)` (from Sperner), transported to `stdSimplex ℝ ι` by reindexing along `Fintype.equivFin` (`exists_fixed_stdSimplex`: `toFin`/`ofFin` preserve the simplex by `Equiv.sum_comp`; `fixed_of_hasFPP` on `toFin ∘ f ∘ ofFin`). No homeomorphism of subtypes was needed. **What the fixed-point argument needed**: the identity on the simplex, continuity per coordinate, nonemptiness — nothing else; no `ContinuousOn`/`MapsTo` bookkeeping since the map is globally continuous.

**The instance**: `ι := ↥(WD D B)`, the `D`-consistent finite worlds; a simplex point `p` is extended by zero to `FiniteWorld B` (`extR`); the day-`n` table is `worldTable p φ := ∑ u, p u · payoutRat u φ`, the history `worldHistory prior n p := Function.update prior n (worldTable p)`, and the demand `worldValue T prior D B p x := T.value (worldHistory prior n (extR p)) (payoutReal x)`. The identity `∑ u, p u · T.value (worldHistory prior n p) (payoutReal u) = 0` for `∑ p = 1` is proved by regrouping over the support (`Strategy.value_eq_sum_support`, `Finset.sum_comm`): each traded `φ` contributes `shares · (∑ p u · payout u φ − (∑ p) · worldTable p φ) = 0`. Continuity: `EF.continuous_denote` composed with the affine map `p ↦ worldHistory prior n (extR p)` (continuous into the product topology on `History`, one cell at a time), summed over the trade list (`continuous_list_sum`).

**The search** (`Maker.lean`). `decodeWeights B k` decodes a `List ℚ` (Mathlib's `Encodable`) and reads coordinate `u` at position `Fintype.equivFin u`; `encodeWeights` is its section (`decodeWeights_encodeWeights`, so the enumeration is total). The generic `firstAccepted P hex` is `Nat.find` on the decidable `CandidateAccepts P k := ∃ w, decodeWeights B k = some w ∧ P w` (`instDecidableCandidateAccepts`: decode, then test), with `searchIndex_min` ("no earlier candidate is accepted"). `coherentWeights := firstAccepted (CoherentAccepts T past D B S ε) (exists_coherentAccepts …)`, `interiorWeights := firstAccepted (CoherentAcceptsFull …) (exists_coherentAccepts_fullSupport …)`; the makers are `ofWeights S` of these (`restrictState` of `bli-overlay`, the range a proof). Both are `noncomputable` for two reasons: the `Nat.find` (as FAF's `MarketMaker`) and the `Trunc`-chosen enumeration order (findings F10). `MarketMaker_eq_of_eqOn_mentioned`-style congruences carry over in principle (the acceptance predicate reads only mentioned cells of the past); not stated.

**The rational density** (`Accept.lean`). `exists_rat_simplex_near`: for `p` in the simplex and `δ > 0`, a rational simplex point within `δ` in every coordinate, positive wherever `p` is — round every coordinate but one *down* to a rational in `(max (p x − δ/N) (p x / 2), p x)` (or `0` where `p x = 0`) and return the deficit to the remaining coordinate, which then exceeds `p x₀` by less than `(N−1)·δ/N < δ`. T2(a): the accepted region `{p | ∀ x, worldValue … p x < ε}` is open (`isOpen_acceptedRegion`), contains the fixed point, hence a sup-norm ball (`Metric.isOpen_iff`, `dist_pi_lt_iff`), hence a rational simplex point. T3(a): first mix `t · uniform` into the fixed point (`t := min (δ/2) 1`; every coordinate becomes `≥ t/N > 0`, the move is `≤ t ≤ δ/2`), then round within `δ/2` keeping positivity.

**The recursion** (`Recursion.lean`). `bli-overlay` attempt A's pattern with a richer record (`PCDayRecord`: core, atoms, weights, quote, range). The priced set is parametrised: `pcDaySet DP X n prev := mentionedSet (pcDayStrat DP n prev) ∪ X n`, record `X = fun _ => ∅`, `smallSet` variant `X = smallSet` (T2(e); `pcOverlaySmall_coherent`). `pcDayCore` is a `dite` on `hW : ∃ u : FiniteWorld (pcDayAtoms …), (worldOf u).ConsistentWith (DP.D n)` — the interior maker and its weights, or `(emptyState, 0)`. The dependent types (`weights : FiniteWorld atoms → ℚ`) made `pcCore_spec` need a "generalize the record first" step (`key : ∀ R, R = pcDayStep … → …`) rather than a rewrite with the recursion equation.

### T8 — Soto's intermediate payoffs (`Payoffs.lean`, extension; Kind P/L, `variant`)

Formalized as finite arithmetic, disclosed `variant` (PDF 04's scheme is not FAF's market, which pays by world at the end; nothing about `Exploits` is claimed): `intermediateTotal n k := 1 / 2^(n − k)`; **(a)** `intermediateTotal_eq_completionProb` (the total received is the uniform-completion probability `(1/2)^(n−k)`, 2-003 (i)) and `increments_telescope` (the increments of `j` successive confirmations sum to the difference of totals — the bookkeeping is consistent); **(b)** `intermediateTotal_decided` (`k = n` gives `1`) and `bundle_total_decided` (in a decided state with true world `u₀`, `∑_{u ⊨ φ} 1[u = u₀] = payoutRat u₀ φ`: a share of `φ`, the bundle of `φ`-worlds, totals `1[φ]`, 031's identity over `FiniteWorld B`); **(c)** `split_cash_inequality` (`θ < 1/2`, `P ≤ 2^{−m}` ⟹ `θ·P < (1/2)·2^{−m}`, the per-step inequality of 2-003 (iii)). The undecided case — the scheme's failure, conceded by the source — is findings F9, not a theorem. In prose, for the record: (a) a world share with `n` prime assignments, `k` confirmed, none refuted, has received `1/2^{n−k}` in total — the uniform-completion probability of the world (2-003 (i)); (b) once every assignment is decided the total is `1` if the world is true and `0` otherwise, so a share of `φ` (the bundle of `φ`-worlds) totals `1[φ]` — the telescoping identity 031 claims, over a finite refinement sequence; (c) the per-step cash inequality behind 2-003 (iii): splitting an unrefuted `W` on a never-decided prime at a price `θ < 1/2` gives the cheaper child's share an immediate payoff `(1/2)·2^{−m}` exceeding its cost `θ·P(W)` when `P(W) ≤ 2^{−m}` — a per-step inequality, not FAF's `Exploits`. Status: proved (as `variant`).
