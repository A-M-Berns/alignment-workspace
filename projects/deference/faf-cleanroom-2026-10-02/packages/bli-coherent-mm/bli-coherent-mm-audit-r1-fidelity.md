3. **The pair-strategy witnesses for the non-interior maker are N+ for the hypothesis package but N− for the search.** `coherentMarketMaker_pair_sum_one`, `gridCoherentMarketMaker_pair_sum_one`, `coherent_pair_accepts`, `pair_paperDP_sum_one`, `pair_paperDP_accepts` hold for *every* world measure (`pair_value_eq_zero`: `T_pair` is worth exactly `0` in every world; `marginal_neg_add`), so they show the package is inhabited with `2^B ≥ 2` worlds and the output is a coherent table, but not that the acceptance test ever rejects a candidate — on `T_pair` the record maker may well return a point mass.
