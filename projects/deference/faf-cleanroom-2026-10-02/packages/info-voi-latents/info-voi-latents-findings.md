## F1 — "Natural-latent coverage" names one of Wentworth–Lorell's two redundancy errors; naturality needs all three (imprecision, already corrected in the final; now Lean-checked)

[generalization-final](../../sources/corrigibility/workflow-2026-09-14/dynamics/generalization-final.md) D10 l. 45 and S1 l. 59 already rename the develop file's "natural-latent coverage" to the expected information gain `EIG = I[Λ : X_k | X_{≤t}]`. The Lean makes the correction exact: `Eig.EIG`, `Eig.medErr`, `Eig.redErr'` are the three conditional mutual informations and `Eig.NaturalOver Λ X_{≤t} X_k μ` is their joint vanishing (D9). The two-bit witness `Eig.twoBits_not_natural` (`Ω = Bool × Bool` uniform, `Λ = id`, `X₁ = fst`, `X₂ = snd`) has `medErr = 0` and `EIG = redErr' = log 2 > 0`: mediation holds, neither redundancy direction does, and `¬ NaturalOver`. So `EIG` small is a redundancy statement in one direction, not naturality, exactly as the final says; the theorems of the natural-latents post are not available from `EIG` alone. Register: the final's correction is confirmed, not extended.

## F2 — Fano applied backwards in the develop file's P5(ii) (local error in the develop file; repaired in the final); P5(ii)'s entropy identity holds in the designer's expectation only (imprecision)

[generalization-adversary](../../sources/corrigibility/workflow-2026-09-14/dynamics/generalization-adversary.md) A5.2 l. 58 is right: Fano's inequality is `H[Λ | Y] ≤ h₂(p_e) + p_e log(|Λ| − 1)`, an upper bound on conditional entropy, and yields nothing from `H[Λ | Y] ≤ δ`. The repaired step is the min-entropy bound `1 − sup π ≤ −log(sup π) ≤ H(π)` (Target 6(i)); [generalization-final](../../sources/corrigibility/workflow-2026-09-14/dynamics/generalization-final.md) P5(ii) l. 125 uses it. Lean: `Mediation.neg_log_sup_le_entropy` (carrier (i)) and `Mediation.expMiss_le_condEntropy` (`E[1 − P(Λ | Y)] ≤ H[Λ | Y ; μ]` over FAF), both proved (updated in repair round 1; this finding previously said "not yet formalized"). A second, smaller point (audit r1): P5(ii) l. 125's "`E_y[−ln π*_y] = H(Λ_C | X_{≤t})` when `y` is drawn from the true joint" is an identity only when the truth `λ^H` is averaged *together with* `y` over the joint — the designer's expectation, which is what the Lean's `expMiss` takes; for a fixed `λ^H` and `y ~ P^H(· | λ^H)` the left side is `−E[log P(λ^H | y)]`, not the conditional entropy. Severity: local error in the develop file, repaired in the final; imprecision in P5(ii)'s gloss of the expectation.

## F4 — "Phase change" attribution withdrawn; what survives is coordinate non-identifiability (attribution error, already withdrawn)

[generalization-adversary](../../sources/corrigibility/workflow-2026-09-14/dynamics/generalization-adversary.md) A8.5 l. 91: P8(d)'s phenomenon is one fixed distribution and two hypotheses agreeing on five coordinates, i.e. ordinary non-identifiability of a coordinate from data that do not vary it — not Wentworth–Lorell's phase change (a change in the distribution changing which functions are natural latents). The final withdraws the attribution. Severity: attribution error, withdrawn in the source; the Lean records the true statement.

## F5 — Wentworth's third diagram is automatic in the deterministic-redundancy form of the two-variable theorem (presentation; ATTRIBUTION-UNVETTED)

Confirmed in Lean: the statement of `NaturalLatents.aeFunctionOf_of_mediates_detRedundant` carries no third-diagram hypothesis. In the deterministic-redundancy reading (`Λ'` a.e. a function of each `X_i` alone — the post's gas example, ll. 33–35 and 67 of `wentworth-2023-natural-latents-the-math`), the conclusion `AEFunctionOf Λ Λ'` follows from mediation and redundancy alone: `Λ' = f ∘ X₁ = g ∘ X₂` a.e., conditional data processing gives `I[Λ' : Λ' | Λ] ≤ I[X₁ : X₂ | Λ] = 0`, and `I[Λ' : Λ' | Λ] = H[Λ' | Λ]`, so Condensation's Prop 2.5 applies. The right word is *automatic*, not *unused*: deterministic redundancy makes `Λ'` `X`-measurable, so the third diagram `Λ ⊥ Λ' | X` holds by construction and adds nothing. The post's remark that the third diagram rules out xor-type interactions therefore applies to the stochastic form only, where it is a genuine hypothesis. Both halves are now theorems (repair round 2, `NaturalLatentsStochastic.lean`): `thirdDiagram_of_detRedundant` (deterministic redundancy ⇒ the third diagram, for every `Λ`) and `stochRedundant_of_detRedundant` (⇒ stochastic redundancy), so the deterministic theorem is a corollary of the post's stochastic one (`condMutualInfo_latent_eq_zero'`), and the stochastic theorem itself — with the third diagram as a hypothesis — is proved (`natural_latents_stochastic`, formerly OPEN; audit r2 adversarial item 2). ATTRIBUTION-UNVETTED that the post intends the deterministic form anywhere. Severity: presentation.

## F6 — S2's "direct bound `VOI ≤ M·m_t(A)` (harm mass, D5)" is false under D5's own definition; the regret-mass bound is the repair (local error; ATTRIBUTION-UNVETTED on the repair)

[generalization-final](../../sources/corrigibility/workflow-2026-09-14/dynamics/generalization-final.md) D5 l. 35 *does* define the harm mass (this finding previously said it did not; corrected in repair round 1 after audit r1): "the *harm mass* `m_t(A) := sup_{a∈A} P_t(a is harmful)`", with harm relative to the null action (D11 l. 47). S2 l. 61 ends "the direct bound `VOI ≤ M·m_t(A)` (harm mass, D5) is the one to quote". Under D5's definition that bound is **false**: two equiprobable states, null action `≡ 0`, actions `(2, 0)` and `(0, 2)` — neither action is ever below the null action, so `m_t = 0` (read pointwise, or in D11's expectation reading), while the perfect signal gives `VOI = 1`; no `M` rescues it. Lean: `Voi.harmMass_zero_voi_pos`, with `Voi.harmMass` D5's definition of record (pointwise reading of "`a` is harmful"; ATTRIBUTION-UNVETTED that this is the note's event — the counterexample is unchanged under the expectation reading). The bound that is true and carries the rare-state instance is `Voi.voi_le_mul_regretMass`: for a prior-optimal `a⋆` and `B = {w | ∃ a, u a⋆ w < u a w}` (`Voi.regretMass`, now a definition of record), `voi ≤ M · μ(B)` with `M` the cross-action range at each state. At the rare-state instance `B = {wrong}`, `μ(B) = ε`, and the bound *applied* gives `voi ≤ 1/10` (`Voi.rareState_voi_le_regretMass`; `voi = 801/10000`). So `voi_le_mul_regretMass` is a *repair* of S2's last sentence, not a precisification of it; ATTRIBUTION-UNVETTED that the regret set is what the note *meant* by "harmful". Two scope points from audit r2 (both Lean, `VoiHarmMass.lean`): (i) the counterexample's menu omits D11's null action, which is immaterial — with the null action in the menu (`(2,0)`, `(0,2)`, `(0,0)`) still `harmMass = 0` and `voi ≥ 1` (`nullMenu_no_M`), and in the *rare-state* regime S2 names (`(1−ε, ε)`, `0 < ε ≤ 1/3`, menu `(1,0)`, `(0,2)`, `(0,0)`) still `harmMass = 0 < 2ε ≤ voi` (`rareNull_no_M`), so the sentence is false even in the regime it is about; (ii) **where the sentence is true**: for the binary execute-or-null menu `{a, ∅}` with `a` prior-optimal, D5's harm mass of the menu *is* the regret mass of `a` (`harmMass_binary_eq_regretMass`) and `voi ≤ M·harmMass` holds (`voi_le_mul_harmMass_binary`) — which is exactly the case the rare-state instance illustrates (`stopMenu`, `continue` prior-optimal, harm set `{wrong}`), and why the note's `εh = 0.10` is right there. D11's decision set for J3 is the future option set `A_{>t}`, not `{a, ∅}`, which is what makes the general sentence wrong. Severity: local error (S2's last sentence is false as written under D5, for any menu with more than one non-null action); the corrected statement is proved, and the binary case is recorded as the true special case.

## F7 — The rare-state VOI formula holds under two orderings the note leaves implicit (imprecision)

[generalization-final](../../sources/corrigibility/workflow-2026-09-14/dynamics/generalization-final.md) P2 l. 107 states "exact VOI = εβh − (1−ε)αc" for the rare-state setting. `Voi.rareState_voi` proves `voi = max 0 (εβh − (1−ε)αc)` under (O1) `εh ≤ (1−ε)c` (continue is prior-optimal) and (O2) `ε(1−β)h ≤ (1−ε)(1−α)c` (continue stays optimal after no press). Without O1 the prior-optimal action is stop and the formula changes; without O2 the no-press branch contributes. Both hold at the instance. Severity: imprecision (a case distinction omitted).

## F9 — `voi_nonneg` and the easy direction of Blackwell need no hypothesis on the prior (presentation)

Good's theorem `0 ≤ voi` (`Voi.voi_nonneg`) and "a garbling cannot raise the Bayes value" (`Split.bayesValue_le_of_blackwellLE`) hold for every vector `μ : W → ℝ`, not only for priors in the simplex: the first because constant rules are candidates and the rows of `k` sum to one, the second because a garbled rule's value is a `g`-mixture of posterior scores of the finer experiment. The note states both for posteriors; the Lean records the weaker hypothesis package. Severity: presentation.

## F10 — Pinsker for the finite KL is false without absolute continuity; every KL statement in the corpus silently assumes it (imprecision in the sources; an encoding hazard)

With Lean's `x / 0 = 0` and `log 0 = 0`, the finite sum `∑ p log(p/q)` is `0` for disjoint point masses while `TV = 1` (`pinsker_fails_without_absCont`). The four uses of Pinsker across the corpus (S2, S5(i), the hazard chain, Target 9(e)) are all on posteriors against a prior or on fibres of one joint, where absolute continuity holds automatically (a posterior vanishes where the prior does: `Bridge.voi_le_mul_sqrt_mutualInfo` discharges it from the product form). No source states the hypothesis. Severity: imprecision (harmless in every use found), recorded because it is the trap the encoding sets.

## F11 — `FiniteRange`/`FiniteEntropyOf` instance search is stuck on bare `Prod.fst`/`Prod.snd` (toolchain hazard; FAF API note)

Verified 2026-09-30 in a scratch file against this run's toolchain (Lean v4.31.0, FAF `159ec3f`), and replicated by both r1 auditors — the committed evidence is `packages/info-voi-latents/audit-r1-probes/FiniteRangeStuck.lean` and `audit-r1-probes/F11Check.lean` (each fails to build on exactly its bare-projection `example`, by design; neither is imported by the library): `example : FiniteRange (@Prod.snd Bool Bool) := inferInstance` fails with "typeclass instance problem is stuck, it is often due to metavariables", while `example : FiniteRange (fun p : Bool × Bool => p.2) := inferInstance` and the same with a named `def` succeed. Consequently every PFR/FAF lemma with a `[FiniteRange X]`/`[FiniteEntropyOf X μ]` instance argument fails to elaborate when `X` is written as the bare projection, even with full type ascriptions. Workaround used here: named projections (`Eig.bit1`, `Eig.bit2`, `Turner.polH`, `Turner.polAI`), with `Turner.mutualInfo_pol` connecting to `Bridge.lean`'s `I[Prod.fst : Prod.snd ; ·]` by `rfl`. FAF's own `APITests/Condensation.lean` uses `Prod.fst`/`Prod.snd` only in instance-free positions (`IsUniform`, preimages), which is consistent. Severity: none for the mathematics; a hazard for future clients, worth a line in `faf-map-other`'s hazards list.

## F12 — Neither reading of S3(a)'s "value part" that the note offers makes both of P3(a)'s sentences true: the Λ-TV bound and the composed MI bound are false for P3(a)'s own definition, `voiθ ≥ 0` fails for the gloss's reading (with which the profile-menu reading coincides), and only under H_val is the split coherent — where it is trivial (local error, blocking S3(a)'s conclusion as stated; found by audit r1, proved in repair rounds 1–2)

[generalization-final](../../sources/corrigibility/workflow-2026-09-14/dynamics/generalization-final.md) S3 l. 65 glosses the value part as "the VOI `E_a` would have if it were informative about `Λ` only"; P3(a) l. 113 defines it as the VOI of the garbling `E^Λ_a` that reveals only `P_t(Λ_A | E_a)` (the Λ-posterior), says `VOI^θ := VOI − VOI^Λ ≥ 0` by Blackwell, and then claims "the value part obeys P2 with `Λ_A` in place of `ω_A`": `VOI^Λ ≤ M·E[TV(P_t(Λ_A | E_a), P_t(Λ_A))] ≤ M√(½ I(Λ_A ; E_a))`. S3(a) marks this **HOLDS**. Lean (`SplitValuePart.lean`):

- **P3(a)'s definition** (`Split.voiΛ`, the Λ-posterior garbling): `voiΛ ≤ voi` holds (`Split.voiΛ_le_voi`), but the Λ-TV bound is **false**. `Split.valuePart_not_bounded_by_lambdaTV`: `Λ = Bool`, `θ = Fin 2`, uniform prior, signals `(true, 0) ↦ 0`, `(false, 0) ↦` fair coin, `(·, 1) ↦ 1`, menu "guess `θ`, prize `M`" (`Λ` irrelevant to the payoff; `HVal` fails). The two signals have distinct Λ-posteriors (`(2/3, 1/3)` and `(2/5, 3/5)`), so the garbling is a relabelling and `voiΛ = voi = 3M/8` (`Split.bayesValue_le_pushforward_of_injective`, `voi_pushforward_eq_of_injective`), while `M·E[tv(postΛ s, μΛ)] = M/8`. The mechanism is general: whenever `s ↦ P(Λ | s)` is injective — the generic case — the Λ-posterior garbling is the identity up to relabelling and `voiΛ = voi`, whatever the signal says about `θ`. That refutes the *first link* of Target 4(v). The *composed* bound `VOI^Λ ≤ M√(½ I(Λ_A ; E_a))` is weaker (Pinsker goes the other way), so its failure does not follow; it fails at the same instance for a different reason — PFR's `I[Λ : S] ≤ 1/15` there (`≈ 0.034` nats), so `M√(I/2) ≤ M√(1/30) < 3M/8` (`SplitComposed.valuePart_not_bounded_by_lambdaMI`, on the joint of `(μΛ, leakΛ)` with `leakΛ` the Λ-lift of `leakθ`, `leakΛ_eq_lift`; audit r2 B1, the auditors' probe promoted). So the mandate's Target 4(v) is **refuted** for this definition, in both its links.
- **The scope of "no definition".** The two readings above are the ones the note offers; trivial definitions (`VOI^Λ := 0`) satisfy both sentences vacuously, and the natural third candidate — the Λ-posterior garbling's VOI on the *profile* menu `V̄ ∘ fst` — coincides with the lift's VOI (its posterior score at `s` is `P(s)·G(postΛ s, a)` with no `HVal` needed, the computation of `bayesValue_kΛ_eq_of_hval`), so it fails the same way as the lift. The header's claim is scoped accordingly (audit r2, fidelity item 1).
- **The gloss's reading** ("informative about `Λ` only"): the Λ-lift `k^Λ((λ,ϑ), s) := ∑_ϑ' P(ϑ' | λ)·k((λ,ϑ'), s)`, the signal's Λ-likelihood read back as a kernel constant in `ϑ` (`Split.meanField2`, proved equal to the lift by `meanField2_eq_lift`). It satisfies `HVal` by construction, so the Λ-TV bound *does* hold for it (`voi_le_mul_sum_tvΛ_of_hval`) — but it is not a garbling of `k`, and `voiθ ≥ 0` **fails**: `Split.meanField_voi_gt_voi` — `Λ = θ = Fin 2`, `P(λ) = ½`, `P(ϑ = λ | λ) = 9/10`, signal `s = ϑ`, `V(a, λ, ϑ) = 𝟙[a = λ] − ½·𝟙[a = ϑ]`: prior value `¼`, `voi = 3/20` for the experiment and `6/25` for its lift.
- **Where the claim is true.** Under `HVal` the value problem is its Λ-marginal — `voi μ k (menu V) = voi μΛ (margΛ k) V̄` (`Split.voi_eq_voi_margΛ_of_hval`) — the two readings coincide (`voi = voiΛ`, `Split.voi_eq_voiΛ_of_hval`), the split is trivial (`voiθ = 0`), and Target 4(v) holds with `M` the within-action range of the *profile* `V̄` over the support of `μΛ`: `Split.voi_le_mul_sum_tvΛ_of_hval` (`voi ≤ M·∑_s P(s)·tv(postΛ s, μΛ)`) and `Split.voi_le_mul_sqrt_mutualInfoΛ_of_hval` (`voi ≤ M√(I[Λ : S]/2)` over PFR's `mutualInfo` on the joint of `(μΛ, margΛ k)`). The TV bound is *attained* at `meanField2` (`voi = 6/25 = (3/5)·(2/5)`, `SplitComposed.meanField2_tv_bound_attained`). `HVal` itself is the kernel identity on *every* `ϑ`, which is strictly stronger than S3(c)'s `I(θ; E_a | Λ) = 0`: it also constrains null-mass coordinates (`SplitComposed.nullθ_not_hval`, `nullθ_voi_eq_voiΛ`); the implication kernel form ⇒ information form is proved on the joint measure (`SplitInfoForm.condMutualInfo_theta_signal_eq_zero_of_hval`, repair round 2).

What the note needs: S3(a)'s "**HOLDS**" should read "HOLDS UNDER H_val", where by S3(c) the split is trivial; without H_val the sentence must name a definition of `VOI^Λ`, and neither candidate satisfies both of P3(a)'s claims. The corrected S3(a) therefore folds into S3(c), and the bound on "the value part" has no content beyond S2 applied to the Λ-marginal problem. The θ-hole (S3(b)) is unaffected: `hole_voiΛ = 0` under both readings. Severity: local error, blocking S3(a)'s conclusion as stated (and P3(a)'s inequality); the repaired statement is proved.
