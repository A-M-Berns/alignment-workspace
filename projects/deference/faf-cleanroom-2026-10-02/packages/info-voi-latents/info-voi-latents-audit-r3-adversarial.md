## 3. Non-blocking issues

1. **`MediationAssembly.predictive_tv_assembly`: the gloss on `hmed` overstates.** The docstring says the mediation gap `∑_y ∑_λ P(y)·PH(λ|y)·klFin (KH y λ) (K λ) ≤ ε_med` is what "Target 1's twin identity renders as `I[X_k : X_{≤t} | Λ]`", and the ledger's Fidelity column repeats it ("the mediation gap enters as its KL form, which Target 1 identifies with `I[X_k : X_{≤t} ∣ Λ]` on carrier (ii)"). The twin identity is `I[X_k : X_{≤t} | Λ] = E_{(λ,y)} KL(P^H[X_k | λ, y] ‖ P^H[X_k | λ])` — against the *true marginal kernel* `P^H[X_k | λ]`. The theorem's `hmed` is against the *agent's* kernel `K λ`, which nothing ties to the truth (H3 enters only as the mixture shape of the agent's predictive). By the compensation identity the left-hand side of `hmed` is `I[X_k : X_{≤t} | Λ] + ∑_λ P(λ)·KL(P^H[X_k | λ] ‖ K λ)`, so `hmed` is *stronger* than "the mediation error is at most `ε_med`" unless `K` is the true kernel. Probe `AssemblyKernel.lean`: `Y' = Λ' = Unit` (mediation error `0`), `KH = (1, 0)`, `K = (½, ½)`: the left-hand side is `log 2`. Not a defect of the theorem — its hypothesis is exactly what it says, and it is *more* general than the note's (it holds for an agent whose kernel is wrong, charging the mismatch to `ε_med`); a defect of the gloss. Fix I'd accept: in the docstring and the ledger's Fidelity/Hyps, "when `K λ = P^H[X_k | λ]` (H3 as the note states it: the shared kernel is the true one), `hmed` is `I[X_k : X_{≤t} | Λ] ≤ ε_med` by Target 1; in general it bounds the mediation error plus the kernel mismatch", and optionally the compensation identity as a lemma. Two smaller points on the same theorem, harmless: `hacK : ∀ y l, AbsCont (KH y l) (K l)` and `hacP : ∀ y, AbsCont (PH y) (Pt y)` quantify over null-mass `(y, l)` and `y` too (the consumer `sum_mul_tv_le_sqrt` needs them only on the support, as `expected_miss_assembly`'s `hac` correctly does); stating them support-only would be the sharper form.
