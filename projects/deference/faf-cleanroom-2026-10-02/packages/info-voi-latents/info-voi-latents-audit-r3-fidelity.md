## 3. Non-blocking issues

1. **`Mediation.predictive_tv_assembly` takes H3 in a form stronger than S5 states it; the Fidelity label ("exact") should say so, and it is a small finding about the source.** [generalization-final](../../sources/corrigibility/workflow-2026-09-14/dynamics/generalization-final.md) S5 l. 73 states (H3) as "`P_t[X_k | Λ=λ] = P^H[X_k | Λ=λ]` *on the effective version space* and `X_k ⊥ X_{≤t} | Λ` in the agent's model". The Lean's H3 is that the agent's predictive is `mix K (Pt y) = ∑_λ K(λ)·Pt(λ | y)` with the *same* `K` that the mediation gap `∑_{y,λ} P(y)·PH(λ|y)·klFin(KH y λ ‖ K λ) ≤ ε_med` is measured against — i.e. the shared kernel on the whole support of the agent's posterior (which, under H4's absolute continuity, contains the truth's). That is exactly what P5(i) l. 125 uses ("`P_t[X_k | X_{≤t}] = ∑_λ P^H[X_k | λ] P_t[λ | X_{≤t}]` by (H3)") and what the mandate's Target 6(iii) writes, but it is more than S5's sentence: with H3 only on `V^η`, the agent's predictive differs from the `K`-mixture by up to `P_t(Λ ∉ V^η | y)` in total variation, and S5(i)'s bound `√(ε_med/2) + √(ε₄/2)` would need an extra `E_y ρ_t(y)` term. The docstring describes the hypothesis accurately ("H3: the agent's predictive is `∑_λ K(λ)·Pt(λ | y)`"), so this is a label, not a substitution: write `Fidelity: exact for P5(i)'s form of H3 (the shared kernel on the support of the agent's posterior), stronger than S5's "on the effective version space"`, and add one line to the findings (imprecision in S5's statement of H3 relative to its own proof). The same applies to `expected_err_assembly`, which does not use H3 at all and is unaffected.
