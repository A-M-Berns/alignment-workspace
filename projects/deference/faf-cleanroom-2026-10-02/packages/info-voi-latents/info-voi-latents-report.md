### Target 2 — Pinsker's inequality, finite (`Finite.lean`) — `core`, load-bearing 1

**Claim.** For `p q ∈ stdSimplex ℝ W` with `p ≪ q`: `0 ≤ KL(p‖q)` and `TV(p,q) ≤ √(KL(p‖q)/2)`.

**Lean.** `tv p q := (1/2) ∑ |p − q|`; `klFin p q := ∑ w, p w · log (p w / q w)`; `AbsCont p q := ∀ w, q w = 0 → p w = 0`.
- `tv_eq_sum_filter` (P): `tv p q = ∑ w ∈ {q < p}, (p w − q w)` — the set form used by the Lipschitz step and the Pinsker reduction.
- `sum_mul_log_div_ge` (P): the log-sum inequality over any finset, junk-safe (`∑_A q = 0` forces both sides `0` under `hac`), from `Real.one_sub_inv_le_log_of_pos` per coordinate (`mul_log_div_ge_aux`).
- `klFin_nonneg` (P, Gibbs) and `klFin_ge_grouped` (P): grouping by a predicate dominates the binary divergence of the two group masses.
- `binary_pinsker` (P): `2(a − b)² ≤ a log(a/b) + (1 − a) log((1−a)/(1−b))` for `b ∈ (0,1)`, `a ∈ [0,1]`. Proof by calculus exactly as the mandate suggests: `binPhi b a := KL(a‖b) − 2(a−b)²` written with `negMulLog` so it is continuous on `ℝ` (`continuous_binPhi`), `hasDerivAt_binPhi` gives `φ' = g(a) − g(b)` with `g(a) = log a − log(1−a) − 4a`, `logitAux_monotoneOn` (derivative `(1−2a)²/(a(1−a)) ≥ 0`) gives the sign of `φ'`, and `antitoneOn_of_deriv_nonpos`/`monotoneOn_of_deriv_nonneg` on `[0,b]`/`[b,1]` with `φ(b) = 0` give `φ ≥ 0` (`binPhi_nonneg`), boundary `a ∈ {0,1}` included by continuity on the closed intervals.
- **`pinsker`** (P, headline): `tv p q ≤ √(klFin p q / 2)`. Boundary cases `q(A) ∈ {0,1}` (`A = {q < p}`) handled explicitly: both force `tv = 0` through `hac`.
- `pinsker_fails_without_absCont` (N−, the encoding test): on `Fin 2`, `p = δ₀`, `q = δ₁`: `tv = 1`, junk `klFin = 0`, and `¬ tv ≤ √(klFin/2)`. Shows `hac` is load-bearing.
- `klFin_eq_sum_klFun`, `klFin_eq_zero_iff` (P): `klFin p q = ∑ q w · klFun (p w / q w)` and `klFin p q = 0 ↔ p = q` under `hac` (Mathlib's `InformationTheory.klFun`).
- `sum_mul_sqrt_le` (P): Jensen for `√` from `Real.strictConcaveOn_sqrt`.
