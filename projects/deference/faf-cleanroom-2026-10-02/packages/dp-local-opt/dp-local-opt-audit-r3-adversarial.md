## 3. Non-blocking issues

Ordered by how much I would want them fixed before the package is cited.

**N1 — The general theorem's witness never exercises strong fairness: `stronglyFair_strictLocalMax_inhabited` lives on `twoPoint 2 4 1`, where `pt` is injective, every fiber is a singleton, `StronglyFair` holds by `LabIso.refl`, and the theorem reduces to the already-proved `twoPoint_strictLocalMax_isOptimal`.** The ledger row for `stronglyFair_strictLocalMax_isOptimal` grades this witness N+ and says, honestly, "the hypothesis package `StronglyFair ∧ IsStrictLocalMax` is inhabited on `twoPoint 2 4 1` … the conclusion is exercised on the `twoPoint` family". It does inhabit the full package, so STANDARDS §3's non-vacuity requirement is met and I do not grade this blocking. But what the general theorem adds to the `twoPoint` theorem is precisely the multi-node fiber: `siaSum_eq_fiberMass_mul_of_iso` summing over two `LabIso` nodes, and the induction's minimality step (`exists_node_lt_of_mem_queried_child`) choosing among nodes of one fiber. Nothing in the package inhabits `IsStrictLocalMax` on any strongly fair tree with a fiber of size `≥ 2`, so had `StronglyFair` been (say) over-strong in a way that made such trees never carry a strict local maximum, the theorem would be vacuous exactly where it is new, and nothing would have noticed. It is not: probe `FiberTwo.lean` builds `gatedTwo` (`p1: out → 1; in → fair coin over two copies of decision p2 [x → 4, y → 1/2]`; `V(p, q) = p + (1 − p)(4q + (1 − q)/2)`, the value function of `fairDepth2`, but with a two-node `p2`-fiber) and proves `gatedTwo_package : StronglyFair gatedTwo ∧ ¬ Function.Injective (pt gatedTwo) ∧ IsStrictLocalMax inX gatedTwo ∧ IsOptimal inX gatedTwo ∧ value inX gatedTwo = 4 ∧ value outY gatedTwo = 1`, with `IsOptimal` obtained once from the general theorem (`gatedTwo_inX_isOptimal`) and once directly from the closed form (`gatedTwo_inX_isOptimal'`: `4 − V(r, s) = (7/2)(1 − r)(1 − s) + 3r ≥ 0`), and the two `p2`-nodes shown distinct (`p2node_ne`, via an index function, no `decide` on trees). Grade of the shipped witness, by me: N+ for joint satisfiability of the predicate with non-constant `V`, N− with respect to the strong-fairness clause (satisfied reflexively). Fix I'd accept: adopt `gatedTwo`, `gatedTwo_stronglyFair`, `gatedTwo_pt_not_injective`, `gatedTwo_inX_isStrictLocalMax` and `gatedTwo_package` into `StrongFair.lean` or a small new witness module, and name them in the row's Witness column beside the `twoPoint` witness.

**N2 — The theorem's own proof gives a strictly stronger conclusion the package does not state: on a strongly fair tree a strict local maximum is pure at every queried point.** The step `hf` of the proof shows, from strictness in the `d`-direction alone, that `∑_a m(a) V_{c₀ a}(C) ≤ ∑_a C(d)(a) V_{c₀ a}(C)` for every `m`; the same move with `m ≠ C(d)` and the strict inequality kept gives `<`, and an affine function on a simplex cannot be strictly below its value at `C(d)` at every other point unless `C(d)` is a vertex (its value at `C(d)` is the average of its values at the support's vertices, each of which is strictly smaller — the `Finset.sum_lt_sum` argument of `thm1At_of_coherentAt`). Probe `PureCorollary.lean` proves `stronglyFair_strictLocalMax_pure : StronglyFair B → IsStrictLocalMax C B → ∀ d ∈ queried B, ∃ a, C d = FinDistr.pure a` over the package's `value_deviate_eq_gated` (general `Ω ι acts K`), and packages `stronglyFair_strictLocalMax_isOptimal_pure : … → IsOptimal C B ∧ ∀ d ∈ queried B, ∃ a, C d = δ_a`. Two consequences worth a sentence each in the docstring, the ledger's Claim column and F10: (i) the headline's hypothesis on strongly fair trees is "a strict deterministic best response at every reached point", far more restrictive than "local maximum", and the reader should know that `IsStrictLocalMax` on this class is satisfiable *only* at pure profiles (so a strongly fair tree whose optimum is mixed at a reached point — a tie between two actions — has no strict local maximum at all, extending `twoPoint541_no_strictLocalMax`'s boundary); (ii) F10's Q4/Q5 consequence sharpens to "on the strongly fair class a refinement satisfied only at strict local maxima can select only pure global optima, and selects nothing on trees with a mixed optimum at a reached point". Purity is not the mechanism of the theorem — `twoStag`'s `(H,H)` is pure too — so this does not weaken the result; it is STANDARDS §5's "obvious strengthening", attempted and found to hold. Fix I'd accept: adopt the probe's two theorems (Kind P / C) and record the strengthening in F10.
