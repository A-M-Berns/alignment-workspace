# tt-ladder findings — errors, ill-posed claims and gaps in the sources

*Formalizer: Claude Fable 5.1 ([scrubbed]), 2026-09-30. Mandate: `tt-ladder-mandate`. Companions: [tt-ladder-report](tt-ladder-report.md), [tt-ladder-ledger](tt-ladder-ledger.md). Every Lean name is in `Cleanroom.Li.TtLadder` (`Cleanroom/Li/TtLadder/`). Severity scale per [STANDARDS](../../STANDARDS.md) §5: blocking the note's conclusion / local error / imprecision / presentation. Readings of what a note *meant* are ATTRIBUTION-UNVETTED; the Lean refutes the reading stated, not the author's intent.*

Everything below is at the sequence level: the refuted sentences are claims about how two real sequences (a quote `a_n` and a credence `e_n`) relate under the notes' own definitions. None of it says which rung an inductor reaches; that is `def-self-trust`'s business, not this package's.

## F1. The printed ladder of `faithful-acceleration.md` §5: the middle arrow is false and the middle rungs are incomparable (local error, refuted in Lean)

*Title corrected in repair round 2 (audit r2 fidelity N1, adversarial N5): an earlier title said the ladder is "inverted", a word inherited from lean-deference-046/2-002 and the mandate. Nothing is inverted — the first `⇓` (product reading) and the third stand; the second is false in both directions.*

**Pointer.** `faithful-acceleration` l.124–134: "bounded-violation ⇓ limit ⇓ bounded ε-violation ⇓ averaged, each ⇓ a strict weakening".

**Reading.** At fixed `t, δ` with `ε > 0`: `BV ⇒ L_prod ⇒ T(t,ε) ⇒ Avg` over `[0,1]`-valued sequences, each strict (ATTRIBUTION-UNVETTED: the display at l.128 prints the product form `g_n(E^H_n(X) − t) ≳_n 0`; the prose at l.122 ("on the flagged days, H's credence converges to at least `t`") and l.132 ("actually reach `t`") reads conditional, `liminf_{a_n > t} e_n ≥ t`; both readings are checked).

**Verdict.** The second `⇓` is false: `middle_rung_false` refutes `∀ a e ∈ [0,1], L_prod(3/4, 1/8) → T(3/4, 1/8, 1/8)` with the trust lab's harmonic gate `a_n = 3/4 + (1/8)/(n+1)`, `e ≡ 0` (`witness_gate`: the gate is exactly `1/(n+1)`; the product `−(3/4)/(n+1) → 0`; the weight is `1/(n+1)`, harmonic). The same configuration fails the averaged rung too (`witness_not_averaged`, `lProdSeq_not_avgSeq`): the "limit" configuration sits *below* both rungs the ladder places under it. Under the conditional reading the *first* `⇓` is false as well (`tAllEpsSeq_bvSeq_not_lCondSeq`, W2: `BV ∧ ¬L_cond`); under the product reading it is sound (`lProdSeq_of_bvSeq`, trust-lab-059's `top_rung_sound` re-proved over FAF's `ctsInd`, with `e ≥ 0`).

**Corrected fixed-`t` order** (all proved): `BV ⇒ T_∀ε ⇒ T(t,ε) ⇒ Avg` (`tAllEpsSeq_of_bvSeq`, `tSeq_of_tAllEpsSeq`, `avgSeq_of_tSeq`), with `L_cond ⇒ T_∀ε` (`tAllEpsSeq_of_lCondSeq`), `L_cond ⇒ L_prod` (`lProdSeq_of_lCondSeq`), Prop A `T_∀ε ⇒ L_prod` (`lProdSeq_of_tAllEpsSeq`, needs `e ≥ 0`); and the non-arrows `L_prod ⇏ T(t,ε)` (W1 `lProdSeq_not_tSeq_w1`, W4 `lProdSeq_not_tSeq`), `T(t,ε) ⇏ L_prod` (park′ `tSeq_not_lProdSeq`), `L_prod ⇏ Avg` (W4), `Avg ⇏ T` (alt `avgSeq_not_tSeq_alt`, gap), `T_∀ε ⇏ BV` and `L_cond ⇏ BV` (creep `lProdSeq_lCondSeq_tAllEpsSeq_not_bvSeq`, W3), `T_∀ε ⇏ L_cond` and `BV ⇏ L_cond` (W2). So `L_prod` and single-instance `T(t,ε)` are incomparable, and `BV` and `L_cond` are incomparable. The second `⇓` is thus false in *both* directions (`middle_rung_false`, `tSeq_not_lProdSeq`): the middle rungs are incomparable, not reversed.

**Survivor.** The repaired arrow: under `SupportNondegenerate` (the gate bounded away from `0` where positive — hard or effectively hard gates), `L_prod ⇒ T_∀ε`, and in fact `L_prod ⟺ L_cond ⟺ T_∀ε` (`middle_rung_repaired_iff`, no bounds on `e`). W4's gate visibly violates the proviso (`witness_gate_no_gap`). This is the precise content of root-fa-007's flag ("the first arrow's direction depends on δ … the formalizer should fix one convention"): with the soft ramp it is the proviso that restores the printed direction, and the hard-indicator claim ("with a hard indicator the printed direction is right") is *read through* the proviso — no hard-gate predicate exists in the package; a hard gate is a gate with values in `{0, 1}`, which satisfies `SupportNondegenerate` with `c = 1`, so `middle_rung_repaired_iff` is the Lean content behind that sentence.

## F3. `faithful-acceleration.md` l.138: the parked-credence example is a correct strictness witness; the slack it illustrates is single-margin, and the "costs nothing" gloss on it is the FA chat's, not the note's (imprecision — in the gloss)

**Verdict.** The example is offered to show that $\sum_n w_n < \infty$ does *not* imply the limit — the strictness half of the sentence's "weaker than" — and it shows exactly that: `park_witness`, `tSeq_not_lProdSeq` (`T(t,ε) ∧ ¬L_prod`, at `t = ½, ε = ⅕, δ = 1/10`). Severity: imprecision in the gloss (lean-deference-046 / the chat), presentation in the note (it would help to say "at this margin").

## F4. `faithful-acceleration.md` l.171: "the per-day limit statement is not forced here" is false under the theorem's own quantification (local error)

**Pointer.** `faithful-acceleration` l.171: "The *per-day limit* statement ($E^H_n(X\mid a_n>t)\gtrsim_n t$ for each $n$) is **not** forced here: it would need the per-day tower".

**Reading.** `LCondSeq a e t` at every rational `t` — the conditional limit along gate-touched days — is strictly stronger than the theorem family `T_full` (ATTRIBUTION-UNVETTED; the FA chat reads `E^H_n(X ∣ a_n > t)` this way, "the alternative per-day ratio reading makes no sense per-day").

**Verdict.** At the theorem's quantification (every rational `t`, `ε`), the two are the *same* statement: `tFullSeq_iff_lCondSeq_all` (`T_full(δ) ⟺ ∀ t, L_cond(t)` for `[0,1]`-valued `a`, any `δ > 0`), both equivalent to `Dominates e a`, i.e. `0 ≤ liminf (e − a)` (`tFullSeq_iff_liminf`). The honest slogan from the chat: the credence is eventually never undercut by the flag at any margin, per sentence — no rates, no cross-sentence uniformity. The sentence is right only at a *fixed* `t` (F5). The finding does not depend on the reading of "per-day limit": under the other natural reading — the ladder's own limit rung `L_prod(t)` at every `t` — it survives too, since `T_full ⇒ T_∀ε(t) ⇒ L_prod(t)` for `e ≥ 0` (`lProdSeq_of_tAllEpsSeq`, Prop A), and `L_cond(t)` with an infinitely-touched gate is literally `t ≤ liminf_{n : a_n > t} e_n` (`lCondSeq_iff_liminf`). Commentary, not a theorem (ATTRIBUTION-UNVETTED): this does not contradict acceleration (per fixed `X` the lag days are finitely many) and does not touch the 2a diagonal (one-sided, compares `e` to `a`, not `a` to the realized `Y_n`).

**Related imprecision.** l.136 ("Strength, stated honestly" / "averaged in, averaged out", the "modest-expert ceiling"): the slack is transient per threshold, not a permanent averaged residue — above `T_full` the only residue is summability/rates (W7, `tFullSeq_not_bvSeq`: `T_full` at every width with `BV(½)` failing). Severity: imprecision.

## F10. Bookkeeping choices, recorded (presentation)

- Compactness constants (target 3): the chat's `ε ≤ 3c/16` and lean-deference-044's `ε, δ ≤ c/8` are replaced by `t ∈ (q − c/2, q − c/4)`, `ε ∈ (c/16, c/8)`, `δ` **free**; on the tail `a − t > 3c/16` and `(t − ε) − e > 5c/16` (`dominates_of_tFullSeq`). For `∀t·L_cond`: `t ∈ (q − 5c/8, q − c/8)`, margin `c/4` (`dominates_of_lCondSeq_all`).
- Indexing: every logarithmic witness runs through `n ↦ n + 2` (`log (n+2) ≥ log 2 ≥ 1/2`, from `1 − 2⁻¹ ≤ log 2`, no numeric `log 2` bound needed), creep through `n + 2` (so `e ≥ 0` on day 0), the harmonic gates through `n + 1`. root-fa-015's profile is capped at `1` (`min 1 (1/log (n+2))`, which is W1's gate) to be `[0,1]`-valued as the item states; the uncapped `1/log (n+2)` exceeds `1` at `n = 0`.
- Divergence of `∑ 1/log (n+2)`: there is no such lemma in this Mathlib (checked `Log/Summable.lean`, `InvLog.lean`, `PSeries.lean`); every divergence is a comparison with the harmonic series through `log (n+2) ≤ n + 1`.
- The averaged lemma is stated with `e ≥ 0` only (no `e ≤ 1`), as the sources' proof uses; `Avg` is vacuous for `t ≤ ε + δ` (`avgSeq_of_nonpos`).
- `LCondSeq` is the `∀ c > 0, ∀ᶠ n, t < a n → t − c < e n` form; it is *true* when the gate is touched finitely often (the sources' convention, `lCondSeq_of_finite_gate`). The restricted-filter form (`lCondSeq_iff_eventually_inf_principal`) and the `liminf` bridge for an infinite gate set under `e ∈ [0,1]` (`lCondSeq_iff_liminf`: `L_cond(t) ⟺ t ≤ liminf e (atTop ⊓ 𝓟 {n ∣ t < a n})`) were written in repair round 1; the infinite gate set makes the filter `NeBot`, and the bounds keep the `liminf` from being a junk `sSup`.
- Repair round 1 also put on record the two trivial ends of the fixed-`t` semantics: a *silent* quote (`a ≡ 0`) satisfies every fixed-`t` predicate and the proviso for every credence (`silent_quote_floor`), and the averaged predicates hold against every credence on a *summable* gate (`avgSeq_gatedMeanSeq_vacuous_sqQuote`) — both inherent to the sources' definitions, both now kernel-visible rather than only disclosed.
- The `1/√(n+1)` profile is used for the `L²` rung's divergent side (`straddle_sqrt_witness`) so that both sides of `bvSeq_straddle_iff` are rational-index; W7 itself keeps the chat's `1/√log (n+2)`.
