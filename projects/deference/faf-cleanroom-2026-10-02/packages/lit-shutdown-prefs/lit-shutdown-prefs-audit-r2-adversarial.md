## 3. Non-blocking issues

- **N1 — Two docstrings still grade the Thorstad refutation `N−`.** `SISWitnesses.lean` l. 546 (`thorstad_monotonicity_restatement_refuted`) and l. 562 (`thorstad_refuted_f_zero`) say `Kind: N−`; the ledger row says "N+ (refutation; regraded from N− at adversarial N4)" and the report says the regrade was done. - **N2 — `Drest.theorem_5_1_neutrality_false_joint` lacks the existence half.** It proves every joint maximiser of `F 8 (1/4) 2` over `stdSimplex × [0,1]^2` is non-uniform, which refutes Theorem 5.1's neutrality clause under its own quantifier only together with the existence of a joint maximiser; the slice theorem `theorem_5_1_neutrality_false` has existence, the joint one does not state it, and the ledger row does not say so. Probe `JointMax.lean`: `F_le_F_one` (`F` is monotone in `ρ` on `[0,1]^k`, for `0 ≤ lam < 1`, `0 ≤ mu`), `joint_maximiser_exists` (the slice maximiser with `ρ ≡ 1` is a joint maximiser) and `theorem_5_1_neutrality_false_joint_full` (both halves). Fix: lift the two lemmas (or note in the ledger row that existence is the slice theorem's plus monotonicity in `ρ`).
- **N3 — The report's record of attempts on the open question is flawed, and the constraint the flaw reveals belongs in the OPEN docstring.** Report § Repair round 1, item 4 and "What would settle it" propose proving "the ILPACS-closure of `[10] ≻ [9]` is asymmetric" as the route to a model. Two problems. (i) In framework S, a lottery with no sweetening and no souring ("isolated") is behaviourally indifferent to every other isolated lottery, whatever their lengths — every clause of `Strict.indiff` is vacuous (`Isolated.lean`, `indiff_of_isolated`). ILPACS clause 2b accepts that indifference as weak preference, so from any strict pair `A ≻ B` and any two isolated `I₁, I₂`, ILPACS gives `½A + ½I₁ ≻ ½B + ½I₂` and POSL forces `A.lengths ∪ I₁.lengths = A.lengths ∪ I₂.lengths` (`isolated_lengths_eq`, kernel-checked). In every relation whose winners contain `[10]` and whose losers contain `[9]` — which is what the report proves about its closure — `[0,0]` and `[0,0,0]` are both isolated, so POSL ∧ ILPACS fails (`not_both_isolated`). Asymmetry of that closure is therefore irrelevant: it is not a model. (ii) "The least relation containing the seed and closed under the display" is not a well-defined object: `lacks` and behavioural `indiff` are negative in `lt`, so the ILPACS rule is not a Horn clause and need not have a least fixed point. What the probe establishes positively: any model of POSL ∧ ILPACS with a strict preference must be *rich* — all its isolated lotteries share the same lengths outside `A.lengths` — so the existence direction needs a relation in which (nearly) every lottery has a sweetening or souring, and every such cardinal or ordinal rule the repairer tried is then exposed to the reweighting mechanism of F7. Fix: record (i)–(ii) in the OPEN docstring and the report (replacing the closure route), and add the isolation lemma to `PostConsistency.lean` as a constraint on models. The vacuous cross-length indifference of isolated lotteries is also a candidate findings entry about the sources (imprecision, 2024 fn `x09fuv8skd` / 2025 §6): the behavioural definition of indifference makes ILPACS force cross-length preferences for any agent with two "unrelated-to-everything" lotteries of different lengths, which the text does not anticipate.
- **N4 — F7's register slightly outruns the Lean, and misses the source that supports it most.** The Lean `hEU` is expected sum-total on single-length-1 lotteries (the 2024 footnote's principle, `u₁ := sumTotal`, three trajectories used). F7's sentences "any agent with EU-like within-length preferences" and "(or comparably monotone)" are hand generalisations; they are true — for any VNM utility `u_l` with three levels `t_a ≻ t_b ≻ t_c` at some length, the same two decompositions with weights `(ε, 1−ε)`/`(1−ε, ε)` and `ε` small give the two-cycle — but not mechanized. Meanwhile 2025 §12 (l. 253 ff.) *assumes* "for each `l`, the agent's preferences over length-`l` lotteries satisfy the four von Neumann–Morgenstern axioms" for the Neutrality+ derivation, so `hEU` is an instance of the paper's own §12 setup, which F7 does not cite. Suggested register: "inconsistent with expected-sum-total single-length preferences (Lean); by the same construction with any within-length VNM utility having three levels at some length (hand), i.e. with 2025 §12's own assumption." Likewise the ledger's refuted row quotes no source sentence; §0.4 rule 3 would have it quote 2025 §6 ("a condition that we can expect competent agents to satisfy", l. 139/149) and §12's VNM sentence, and say the refuted proposition is their conjunction with POSL.

- **N6 — `Dominated.induced` is a finsupp and `DominatedRep` does not require total mass one.** The headline's `L`, `L'` are probability mass functions (each summand is a lottery, weights `r, s, 1−r−s` sum to one) but this is nowhere stated; `DominatedRep` now has the well-formedness clauses (`dᵢ ∈ [0,1]`, `dᵢ + eᵢ ≤ 1`, `Z' ≥ 0`) but not `∑ cᵢ + mass Z' = 1`. Harmless for the headline (the paper's display does not state it either); a one-line lemma `induced` sums to one would make "policy lottery" literal. Presentation.
