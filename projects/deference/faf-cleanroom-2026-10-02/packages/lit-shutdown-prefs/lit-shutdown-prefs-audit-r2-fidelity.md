## 3. Non-blocking issues

1. **F7 generalises from sum-total to every within-length expected-utility agent with a strict 3-chain — which is the 2025 paper's own assumption (push further).** `posl_ilpacs_two_cycle` takes `hEU` = "single-length-1 lotteries are ranked by expected sum-total". Thornley 2025 §12 (l. 253) assumes "for each `l`, the agent's preferences over length-`l` lotteries satisfy the four von Neumann–Morgenstern axioms", and its worked agent (l. 269) "prefers length-`l` lotteries in line with the user's expected bank balance". The two ILPACS moves need only three same-length trajectories `t₁ ≻ t₂ ≻ t₃` ranked by *some* EU utility `u` and one trajectory `s` of another length: with `D = ½[t₃] + ½[s]`, `X = α[t₁] + (1−α)D`, `Y = β[t₂] + (1−β)D`, the first instance gives `X ≻ Y` for every `α, β ∈ (0,1)`, and the length decompositions give `Y ≻ X` as soon as `E_u(Y₁) > E_u(X₁)` where `X₁ = (2α/(1+α))[t₁] + ((1−α)/(1+α))[t₃] → [t₃]` as `α → 0` and `Y₁ = (2β/(1+β))[t₂] + ((1−β)/(1+β))[t₃] → [t₂]` as `β → 1`; since `u t₂ > u t₃` some `(α, β)` works. So the antecedent of `neutrality_of_posl_ilpacs` is empty for **every** agent satisfying 2025 §12's within-length VNM assumption with a strict 3-chain at some length (POST clause (1)'s "many pairs" all but guarantees one), not only for sum-total agents. Probe `audit-r2-probes/VnmTwoCycle.lean` (kernel-checked, `lean-check` clean): with `u [10] = 100`, `u [9] = 1`, `u [0] = 0` the package's weights fail (`package_weights_fail`: `E_u X₁ = 200/11 > 18/19 = E_u Y₁`) but `α = 1/1000` restores the cycle (`two_cycle_vnm`, `inconsistent_vnm`), reusing the package's `Y`, `Y₁`, `D`. Suggested: state the general version (`∀ u`, three length-1 trajectories with `u t₁ > u t₂ > u t₃`, `∃ α β`, two-cycle) as a headline, upgrade F7's register from "expected-sum-total agents" to "every within-length-EU agent with a strict 3-chain (2025 §12's own assumption)", and sharpen the OPEN docstring's item (i) accordingly — a model, if one exists, must fail VNM within lengths or have no length with three strictly ranked trajectories.
