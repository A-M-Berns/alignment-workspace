# udt-comm-trust — ledger

Ledger for work package `udt-comm-trust` ([STANDARDS](../../STANDARDS.md) §6). One row per headline; supporting lemmas have no row. All declarations live in `Cleanroom.Udt.UdtCommTrust` (sub-namespaces `AbstractDS`, `ConcreteDS`, `W2`, `W2m`, `Two`, `NoSub`, `Det`). Sources: T = [communication-trust-translated](../../sources/udt-representation-theorem/communication-trust-translated.md) (line numbers), O = the original `references/communication&trust.md`, topic notes as named. Status values: `proved` / `refuted` / `open` / `partial: <what>` / `flagged: <issue>`. Rows marked **(r1)** were changed or added in repair round 1 (see [udt-comm-trust-report](udt-comm-trust-report.md) §Repair round 1).

**Package-wide (c), disclosed once:** every decision structure takes `Ω` to be the support of the prior (`AbstractDS.pos`); "a.e." reads "everywhere", every conditioning event is empty or of positive mass, "exists `ω`" and "positive probability" coincide. Restricting a finite space to its support loses nothing the paper states (not proved). Junk conventions: `−1` for conditional expectations (the paper's), `0` for conditional probabilities (this package's; the paper states none). **Quantifier caveat (audit r1):** `Stable`, `StableR`, `InternallyDriven`, `HasCA`, `Follows`/`{Π̈ = R}` quantify over the value types `OI`, `OE`, not over realized values; at an unrealized `ȯ`/`ö` the projections and `Π̈` read junk (`Classical.choice`). Every witness realizes every value, so no headline is affected on the witnesses; on a structure with an unrealized recommended instance the theorems that take these as hypotheses are weaker than the paper's, and `selfTrust_repaired`'s `∀ ȯ ö` conclusion is stronger.
