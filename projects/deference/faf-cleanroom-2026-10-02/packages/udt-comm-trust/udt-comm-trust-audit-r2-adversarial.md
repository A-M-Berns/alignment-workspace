## 2. Blocking issues

None. The candidates I considered and rejected, so the pass is legible:

- **`polE_follows_R` vacuous or a squeeze?** Not vacuous: `Det.polE_follows_R` discharges every hypothesis (I re-read each discharge; `Det` is a legitimate `AbstractDS`, see §4). Not a squeeze: no single hypothesis yields the conclusion; but the *conjunction* of the four advice hypotheses forces every chosen policy in the support to follow the recommendation at every realized recommended input (probe, §3 N3), so every model has a prior that is degenerate in the external component there. That is exactly what N− with the package's explanation means, and the ledger and docstring say it. Disclosed; non-blocking (the evidence pointer is wrong, N3).
- **`aE_follows_R_of_udtRuleAt` N+ really N−?** The rule is assumed at one world; the proof needs AF-R's strict gap and the argmax; on `W2` at `followWorld` the gap is the computed `1/2 > 7/16` (`score_lt`, count identities) and `deviating_world` shows a world of positive mass at the same input choosing the other action — so the rule at the world, not the structure, forces the conclusion. `aE_follows_at_follow_world` is proved *by applying the theorem* with every hypothesis discharged on `W2`, not by `rfl`. N+ stands, with the caveat in N4 (the modification hypotheses are trivial on `W2`).
- **T4(b) `P` hollow?** (i) `polSC_sub` is `IsSubvariable.of_comp` — by construction; (ii) `polD_eq_polSC_of_oC` is `neutralize_spec` + `rfl` — at a world already at the neutral value; (ii) proper is `SideChannelInert`, an assumption, and `polD_eq_polSC_iff_inert` is the honest `L` saying so; (iii) chains junk-`0` and inertness. No `P` content survives, and the module docstring, finding 4′ and the report say precisely this. The Kind labels on two rows still say `P` (N1); the situation is disclosed, so not blocking.
- **`followsR_of_hasCA_const` a squeeze?** No: the CA identity at one input plus the constancy of `R` yields `{Π̈ = R} = Ω` by a real argument (the right conditioning event meets `{Π̈ = π̈}` in nothing for a non-following `π̈`, so the left conditional probability is `0`, contradicting `ω`'s membership). The hypotheses are satisfiable (a two-world design in N5). No witness is shipped; the cell says "none needed" (N5).
- **A core target skipped without attempt?** T13(d) (`Faithful` failure witness) has never been attempted in three sessions. Both round-1 lenses graded this non-blocking (`§3.8` fidelity), and the repair round's reason is recorded. I keep that calibration (N7) rather than raise the bar in round 2, but note it is now the one core item with no attempt record.
- **False claim in the findings?** I built and machine-checked the T15 eight-world design (N6) and checked item 9's account of `W2.not_udtRule` / the per-world witness; item 7's claim that `Det` satisfies the whole package; T14's `udtRule_of_const`.
