## F1 — The table-state B3 of the mandate is not a probability measure on worlds (blocking for the mandate's target 1 as stated; repaired by a design change)

**Where.** `bli-measure-mandate` target 0 (`canonWeights`, `b3History` at the least horizon) and target 1(a) ("for every finite atom set `A`, `CoherentOn ∅ A (b3History n)`"); the horizon-consistency sketch ("`trajLaw_marginal` + the junk-`false` convention agreeing with the unmentioned atoms").

**What.** With the state a coherent table on `smallSet m` and the world weights a canonical selection `canonWeights m Q` (a `Classical.choose` from the fiber of weight vectors with marginal `Q`), the horizon-`h` price of a *large* base sentence `r` (one with atoms within the bound but outside `smallSet (n+h)`) is the selection's marginal of `r` at the last table, and the horizon-`(h+1)` price is the kernel's mixture of the *next* selections' marginals of `r`. A kernel balanced on `smallSet (n+h)` constrains the next tables' entries on `smallSet (n+h)`, not their selections' marginals of `r`, and the selection at day `m+1` is independent of the selection at day `m`. So `𝐏_n(r) ≠ ∑_q 𝐏_n(r ⋏ ⌜𝑸_{n+1} = q⌝)` in general: additivity fails on the algebra generated by one large base atom and the day-`(n+1)` state atoms, and `CoherentOn ∅ A (b3History n)` is false for that `A`. Concretely: `smallSet m = {p}`, `B = 2`, `d = 2`, `t = (p ↦ ½)`, `canonWeights m t = ½δ_{(p,r)} + ½δ_{(¬p,¬r)}`; `𝐏(r) = ½` at horizon `0`, while at horizon `1` it is the face average's mean at `r`, unconstrained by balance on `{p}`. The sketch overlooks that the base world's type (`FiniteWorld (B (n+h))`) changes with the horizon.

**Repair (B3 of record).** Make the state the rounded world measure itself (`Grid.lean`: `wIndex B`, `cgrid`, `vecOf`), so that the table determines its vector, balance on the index is balance on worlds, and horizon consistency holds (`Measure.lean`: `b3Mass_succ`). Every target of the mandate is then provable (targets 1–4 proved). Cost: richer states (each candidate carries a full world distribution over `B m` atoms; `val m q φ` is its marginal on every sentence, a disclosed `variant` of Appendix B's `Q̂[φ]`), and the bound must be unbounded (`AtomBounds.B_unbounded`). The two-vectors-one-marginal fact the mandate wanted recorded is exactly what breaks the table-state design; no `canonWeights` is defined, so the refutation of the mandate's 1(a) is **by hand** (the two-atom example above), not in Lean — there is no mandate object to refute formally.

**Severity.** Blocking for target 1(a) as the mandate states it over the mandate's object; not blocking for the program (`bli-program-construction` §2.7's "every propositional identity holds" is true of B3 of record, `b3History_coherentOn`).

## F2 — Past state atoms in the scopes of `E1r`/`E1x`/`E2x`/`E3`/`E4`: `bli-found`'s full scopes are **refuted** for B3 of record, and the mandate's "definitional `E1r` on `smallSet n`" is false for the object it specifies (local error in the mandate; the state-free scope is the honest desideratum)

**Where.** `bli-measure-mandate` target 2 ("**E1r** `P n φ = roundedTable n φ` on `smallSet n` (definitional, `L`)"; "`E1x (ratHistory Q) P`" on the denominator mesh; "state bli-found's `E2x S P` as the corollary"; `IsBLI_AppB S P` as the conjunction); `bli-found`'s `E2x`/`E3` (scope `Sminus m m`: day-`m` small sentences whose atoms refer to days `< m`) and `E4` (scope `smallSet n`).

**What.** A candidate code `q ∈ states m` is large on day `m` (`stateAtom_large`), but `⌜𝑸_m = q⌝` becomes small on later days, so a day-`n` small sentence may mention a *past* system state atom (`m < n`; `Constraints.lean`: `sysState_day_lt_of_small`), and `Sminus m m` admits state atoms of days `< m`, including days in `(n, m)` and days `≤ n`. On such an atom the B3 world reads the **realized** state (`b3Bits_past`), which is what exact `TB` requires on day `n+1`'s own atom (`Bayes.lean`: `b3Bits_cons_actual`); the base's world measure and a *candidate's* world vector read their own bits. The base of record (`recBase`: the recursion run over `DP`, **not** over `bliDP`) never hears about realized states and has full support on the stage's consistent worlds (`recBase_fullSupport`), and the paper stage is state-tag-free (`paperDP_tagFree`); so on the day the realized day-`0` state's atom `φ := ⌜𝑸_0 = actual 0⌝` becomes small, B3 prices it `1` and the base prices it `≠ 1`. Consequences, all **proved in Lean** (`Refuted.lean`, over `paperDP 𝗜𝚺₁` on the denominator mesh — the package's own N+ setting; repair round 1, carrying audit r1 adversarial B1's probe into the package):

(a) `bli-found`'s `E1x (ratHistory Q) P` is refuted on the denominator mesh (`paper_E1x_refuted`), `E1r` likewise (`paper_E1r_refuted`), and `E4` on all of `smallSet n` likewise (`paper_E4_refuted`: the right-hand side of `E4` is the rounded measure's marginal of `φ`, `sum_superbelief_val_eq`, i.e. the base's price). Only "if" is proved; "only if" would yield `RespectsPast` only at the `(m, q)` whose atom is small on day `n`, not as `RespectsPast` is defined.

(b) `bli-found`'s `E2x` at `Sminus m m` scope is refuted (`paper_E2x_refuted`) on a **past** state atom: at `m := n + 1`, the point-mass candidate at the extension of a consistent world reading `φ` as `false` is charged by the face kernel (`candidate_charged_of_support`), its value of `φ` is `0`, while `𝐏_n(φ ⋏ σ) = 𝐏_n(σ) > 0` since every charged B3 world holds the realized past literal. The case of a *future* atom (`n < k < m`: a candidate's fixed opinion about `⌜𝑸_k = q'⌝` versus the chain's backward conditional, which varies with the start table when the candidate is reached from several predecessors) is **argued, not proved**, and so is the claim that no kernel choice repairs it (a kernel relative to the realized past would break the counterfactual branches instead — argued, not proved; audit r2 adversarial N8). `E3` at `Sminus m m` scope is refuted the same way (`paper_E3_refuted`, added 2026-10-02 by the repair's continuation agent): `m := n + 1`, `o := n + 2`, `q₁` the realized day-`(n+1)` state (charged, `paper_realized_charged`), `q₂` the point-mass candidate of day `n + 2` reading `φ` as `false` (charged from the realized day-`(n+1)` table, `candidate_charged_of_support`); the two-step mass `𝐏_n(σ₁ ⋏ σ₂) = 𝐏_{n+1}(σ₂) · 𝐏_n(σ₁) > 0` is exact `TB` (`b3_TB`) with the conjuncts swapped (`b3History_and_comm`), `𝐏_n(φ ⋏ (σ₁ ⋏ σ₂)) = 𝐏_n(σ₁ ⋏ σ₂)` since every charged B3 world holds the realized past literal (`b3History_paperPhi0_and`), and `val (n+2) q₂ φ = 0`. The future-atom case is open for `E3` as for `E2x`.

(c) Hence `bli-found`'s `IsBLI_AppB S P` is **refuted** for B3 of record (`paper_isBLI_AppB_refuted`), not merely unclaimed. The rows of record are the **state-free** forms with the atom bound (`E1rSF`, `E2xSF`, `E3SF`, `E4SF`, `IsBLI_B3`), stronger in the bound and narrower in the state atoms; "state-free" is `TagFreeSentence stateTag` — no state-**tagged** atom, candidate or not (slightly narrower than "no system state atom"; `E1r` in fact holds on non-candidate state-tagged atoms, which B3 reads from the base world — `sysState`'s disclosed convention).

**Severity.** Local error in the mandate: its "definitional `E1r` on `smallSet n`" and its `E1x` on the denominator mesh are false for the object it specifies, because the base of record is run over `DP`, not over `bliDP`; `bli-found`'s `Sminus` scope is the wrong desideratum for a Markov superbelief: the scope `bli-found` gives `E2x`/`E3` (`Sminus n m`, the day-`n` small sentences quoting no market day `≥ m` — `bli-program` §2.1's length split) should read "state-free" for such a superbelief. ## F3 — The design note's tension is weaker than stated: full-stage `PCP` holds for the stage-free kernel (local correction to the mandate's expectation)

**Where.** `bli-measure-mandate` target 0 design note ("the stage-free kernel can charge worlds refuting **large** theorems of `DP.D n`, so `PCP` relative to the whole stage is not expected") and target 1(b) ("the full-stage `PCP` is stated OPEN or refuted").

**What.** Pinning along the chain (`Kernel.lean`: `ctraj_zero_persists`): a world conjunction `φ_u` with zero weight in the rounded start measure keeps zero weight along every charged trajectory, so every charged last-day world restricts to a world charged by the rounded base measure, which is `DP.D n`-supported — and a `DP.D n` sentence lies within the day's atom bound (`B_stage`, as `pcAtoms` provides). Hence every charged B3 world satisfies **all** of `DP.D n`, large theorems included (`Coherence.lean`: `b3World_consistent`), and `PCP hh S (bliDP DP states actual) P` is a theorem (`b3History_PCP_bliDP`). The tension the design note identifies is real only for the *candidates' opinions about the realized past* (F2), not for stage-relative coherence of `𝐏_n` itself. The choice "grid relative to `∅`, kernel stage-free" is kept, and it loses nothing on the base stage.

**Severity.** Local correction (the mandate's expected outcome was pessimistic); a positive result.

## F5 — B3 of record is noncomputable as defined, independently of the base (local; a limit on target 7)

**Where.** `bli-measure-mandate` target 7 ((i) "`ComputableMarket (pcHistory …)` — open upstream"); `Lic.lean` (`ObstructionB3Computable`).

**What.** Even over a computable base, `b3History` is built from two classical choices inherited from `bli-finite`/`bli-superbelief`: `remainderRound` is `Classical.choose (exists_gridRound …)` and `faceAverage` is the uniform average of `Classical.choose`d balance solutions (`faceWitness`). So `ComputableMarket (b3History base 𝓜)` — FAF's *extensional* predicate on the values of `𝐏` (`Framework/Criterion.lean:1067`: some partial-recursive table coincides with them) — is open in a stronger sense than (i): the definition gives no computable presentation, so the predicate is **not established and not refuted** here — nothing in the definition decides it either way (a `Classical.choose` coincides with a computable selection whenever the witness set is a singleton, for instance). This is a statement of what the package has, not an independence result (repair round 2 wording, audit r2 fidelity N9; repair round 1 said "undetermined as defined"; the formalizer's "false-as-defined" asserted a refutation the package does not have and could not have). A computable B3 needs a computable largest-remainder rounding (straightforward) and a computable full-support face solution (the hard part: `faceGen` is an existential filter; `bli-superbelief`'s `Expr.lean` gives expressible balance solutions of a fixed form, which is also what obstruction (iii) needs). The mandate's obstruction list (i)–(iii) should include this (i′).

**Obstruction (ii), corrected (repair round 1).** The mandate's (ii) ("off the denominator mesh B3 satisfies `E1r`, not `E1x`") locates the gap to `bli-transfer`'s L1 in the mesh. It is not the mesh: exact small agreement with the base holds only on the state-free part of `smallSet n` (and there only on the denominator mesh), and **fails on past state atoms on every mesh** — `paper_obstructionE1x_refuted` refutes `ObstructionE1x` on the denominator mesh itself (F2). The coarse-mesh rounding example the first report described as the N− for (ii) only exhibits the state-free rounding error (`b3_E1r_err`); the N− of record is the refutation on the real construction.

**Severity.** Local (target 7 is stretch and expected OPEN); worth recording because it moves obstruction (i′) from "upstream" to "this construction's own definition", and obstruction (ii) from the mesh to the realized-past convention.
