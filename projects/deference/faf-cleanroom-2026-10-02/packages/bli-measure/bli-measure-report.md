## Design: what B3 of record is, and why it is not the mandate's table-state object

The one design decision the mandate flags (target 0's design note) is the stage the grid and kernel are relative to; the package keeps the mandate's answer (grid relative to `∅`, kernel stage-free). A second decision, not anticipated by the mandate, turned out to decide whether target 1 is provable at all (finding F1 in [bli-measure-findings](bli-measure-findings.md)):

**The mandate's grid of record cannot be a world measure.** The mandate's state is a coherent table on `smallSet m` together with a canonical world-weight selection `canonWeights m Q` (a `Classical.choose` from the fiber of weight vectors with marginal `Q`), and `𝐏_n ψ` is the horizon-`hor n ψ` sum over trajectories and last-day worlds. The selection at day `m+1` is independent of the selection at day `m`: a kernel balanced on `smallSet m` says nothing about how the day-`(m+1)` selections' marginals of a *large* base sentence `r` relate to the day-`m` selection's marginal of `r`. So the horizon-`0` and horizon-`1` prices of `r` differ in general, and `𝐏_n(r) ≠ ∑_q 𝐏_n(r ⋏ ⌜𝑸_{n+1} = q⌝)`: additivity fails on the algebra generated by one large base atom and the day-`(n+1)` state atoms, and `CoherentOn ∅ A (b3History n)` (target 1(a)) is **false** for that `A`. The mandate's horizon-consistency sketch ("`trajLaw_marginal` + the junk-`false` convention") overlooks that the base world's type changes with the horizon. Concretely: `smallSet m = {p}`, `B = 2`, `d = 2`, `t = (p ↦ ½)`, `canonWeights m t = ½δ_{(p,r)} + ½δ_{(¬p,¬r)}` gives `𝐏(r) = ½` at horizon `0`, while at horizon `1` it is the face average's mean at `r`, an unconstrained number.

**B3 of record takes the rounded world measure itself as the state.** The day-`m` state is a weight vector in `(1/d m)·ℕ` on `FiniteWorld (B m)`; it is coded as a table over the index `wIndex B` whose day-`m` sentences are the world conjunctions `φ_u` (Soto's `φ_W`) for `u : FiniteWorld (B k)`, `k ≤ m` (`Grid.lean`). Then `bli-finite`'s `coherentGrid` and `bli-superbelief`'s face machinery apply verbatim, the table determines its vector (`vecOf`, `cgrid_apply_eq_wMarginal` — no selection), the kernel's balance on `wIndex B` *is* balance on worlds, and horizon consistency holds (`b3Mass_succ`: the day-`(n+h+1)` vectors, averaged under the kernel, restrict to the day-`(n+h)` vector). The cost: the states are richer than Appendix B's price lists (every candidate carries a full world distribution over `B m` atoms, and `val m q φ` is its marginal on every sentence), and the atom bound must be unbounded (`AtomBounds.B_unbounded`) so that every sentence has a covering horizon. This is disclosed as `variant` on every definition of record and in the ledger. The mandate's `canonWeights` is not defined, so no "two weight vectors share a marginal" row is needed; the fact is still true (two vectors on two atoms with the same marginal on one of them) and is what breaks the table-state design.

**The second scope decision (finding F2).** A day-`n` small sentence may mention a *past* system state atom `⌜𝑸_m = q⌝` (large on day `m`, small later). The B3 world reads such an atom by the realized-past convention (`q = actual m`), which is what exact `TB` needs on day `n+1`'s own atom; the base's world measure and a candidate's world vector read their own bits. So `bli-found`'s `E1r`/`E1x`/`E4` on all of `smallSet n` need the base to respect the realized past (`RespectsPast`) — which the base of record does **not**: it is the recursion run over `DP`, not over `bliDP`, it never hears about realized states, and it has full support on the stage's consistent worlds; so `RespectsPast`, `E1r`, `E1x` (on the denominator mesh), `E4`, `E2x` and `E3` at `Sminus` scope and `IsBLI_AppB` are **refuted** for B3 of record on the real construction (`Refuted.lean`, repair round 1, at the realized day-`0` state's atom once it is small). A base respecting its realized past is the fixpoint base over `bliDP` (`bli-assemble`'s `Fixpoint.lean`), a different object, not constructed. The rows of record are the state-free forms with the atom bound (stronger in the bound, narrower in excluding state-tagged atoms), bundled as `IsBLI_B3`.
