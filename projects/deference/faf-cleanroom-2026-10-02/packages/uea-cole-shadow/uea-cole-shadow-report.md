## Target 10 — tightness class (`extension`): resolved in repair round 1 (`Strict.lean`)

**Claim.** Is `gap = O_h > 0` attained by some model, plain fixed point and trust-bound node with `0 < w_h < 1`? The tight trap gives `gap/O_h = 1 - δ` for every `δ`, so the supremum `1` is approached.

**Lean.** `theoremB_strict` (P, (a)): every model, every plain fixed point, every decision node with `0 < w_h < 1` and `TB_h`: `V^*_ξ(h) - V^π_ξ(h) < O_h`. `gap_eq_odds_not_attained` (C): no model, plain fixed point and node with `0 < w_h < 1`, `TB` and `gap = O_h` exists (`Type`-universe existential). `theoremB_second_eq_iff` (L — one algebraic identity in `p̄`, `w`, `V^*`, no policy or fixed-point hypothesis): the second step of Theorem B is tight iff `p̄_h = 1`. So the answer is **never attained** — but each step of the two-step bound is attained *separately*: the first at the tight trap (`SinkOrSwim.tight_trap_pbar`: `gap = (1-w)V^* = δ` with `p̄ = 0`, second step strict), the second exactly when `p̄_h = 1`, e.g. at the informative mixed witness (`SinkOrSwim.mixed_witness_second_step_tight`: `(1/4)(4/3) = 1/3 = O_h`, first step strict, `gap = 1/60`); `theoremB_strict` says they are never tight together. The supremum `1` of `gap/O_h` is approached by the trap with `p̄ = 0` (`SinkOrSwim.sup_approached_with_pbar_zero`), not through `p̄ → 1`. (Repair round 1 had written here "tight in its first step and never in its second" and, in `theoremB_second_eq_iff`'s docstring, "the only way to approach `O_h` is through `p̄_h → 1`"; both were false — round-2 fidelity audit B1 — and are corrected in repair round 2.)

**Route** (the round-1 auditors' derivation, machine-checked). Suppose `gap = O_h`. Then both inequalities of `theoremB` are tight. The second forces `p̄_h = 1` (`(1-p̄)(w V^* - 1) = 0` and `w V^* ≤ w < 1`). The first is a sum of per-action inequalities over the support, so each is tight (`Finset.sum_eq_sum_iff_of_le`); `p̄_h = 1` supplies a supported `a` with `π̄(a|h) > 0`. At such an `a`, the tight per-action inequality combined with Lemma A (`xia_mul_Qxi`) gives the identity `ξ(a|h)(Q_ξ(h,a) - w V^*) = (1-w) π̄(a|h) (Q̄(h,a) - 1)` with left side `≥ 0` and right side `≤ 0`, hence `Q̄(h,a) = 1` (`Qmix_eq_one_of_per_action_eq`); so `Q^*(h,a) = 1 = V^*(h)` and the non-self mixture is optimal at every child `hae` of positive percept probability (`Vbar_eq_Vstar_of_Qbar_eq`). The depth induction `Vpi_eq_Vstar_of_Vbar_eq_Vstar` — at a node reached by both the self and the non-self at which `π̄` is optimal, every plain fixed point is optimal: a residual-supported `b` is `π̄`-optimal; if the self never plays it, `w_{hb} = 0` and `Q_ξ(h,b) = Q̄(h,b) = V^*`; otherwise the induction at the children gives `Q^π(h,b) = Q^*(h,b) = V^*`; so `M(h) = V^*`, so every self-supported `c` has `Q_ξ(h,c) = V^*` with `w_{hc} > 0`, forcing `Q^π(h,c) = V^*` — then gives `Q^π(h,a) = Q^*(h,a) = V^*(h)`. But the tight per-action inequality at `a` reads `π(a|h)(V^* - Q^π(h,a)) = (1-w)[π(a|h) V^* + π̄(a|h)(1/w - V^*)] > 0`. Contradiction.

**The `w_h = 1` corner** (repair round 2, round-2 adversarial audit item 6). `theoremB_strict` excludes `w_h = 1`, where Theorem B reads `gap ≤ 0 = O_h`; that this exclusion is necessary is now machine-checked: `InstW.w_eq_one_corner` (`InstanceW.lean`) — a two-step model whose residual never enters the node `N` after root action `1` (`δ = 1/10`, the residual plays `0` surely), with the plain fixed point `polW` (`1` at the root, `0` at `N`): `w_N = 1`, `TB_N` with equality, `gap(N) = 0 = O_N`, and `0 < w_root = 9/10 < 1`. `gap_eq_odds_attained_at_w_one` is the existential `gap_eq_odds_not_attained` denies, with `w_h < 1` replaced by `w_h = 1`, proved. So Theorem B's bound is attained exactly at `w_h = 1`.

**Kind/Fidelity.** P/C; `stronger` than the note's Theorem B (strict). **Witness.** `SinkOrSwim.tight_trap` (the supremum is approached); non-vacuity of the hypothesis package is Theorem B's. The necessary conditions asked for by the mandate (`p̄_h = 1`, `V^* = 1`, `Q̄ = 1` on the support with `π̄ > 0`) appear as the intermediate facts of the proof, not as a separate theorem, since the class they describe is empty.
