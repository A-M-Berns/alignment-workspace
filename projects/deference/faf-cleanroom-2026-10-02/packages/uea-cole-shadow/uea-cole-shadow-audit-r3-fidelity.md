## 3. Non-blocking issues

### 3.1 The "(1/4, 1/2) only from `m = 4`" gloss is not what `Chain.dyadic_ratio_bracket` proves

The docstring of `Chain.dyadic_ratio_bracket` ("So the ratio `gap/bound` lies in `(1/4, 1/2)` only from `m = 4` on … and tends to `1/2`"), the ledger's witness cell of `theoremC_depth` ("`gap/bound ∈ (m/(4(m+2)), m/(2(m+2)))` for `m ≥ 2` — `Chain.dyadic_ratio_bracket`, so inside `(1/4, 1/2)` only from `m = 4`") and the report's Target 7 ("which is inside `(1/4, 1/2)` only from `m = 4` on") present the range claim as a consequence of the bracket. It is not: the bracket's lower end `m/(4(m+2))` is below `1/4` for every `m` (probe `probe_bracket_lower_lt_quarter`), so the bracket never places the ratio above `1/4`; read literally, "the interval `(m/(4(m+2)), m/(2(m+2)))` is inside `(1/4, 1/2)`" is false for every `m`. What the library proves is: `gap/bound < m/(2(m+2)) < 1/2` for every `m ≥ 2` (the bracket's upper end), hence `< 1/4` at `m = 2` (upper end exactly `1/4`); `< 1/4` at `m = 3` (`dyadic3_ratio_lt_quarter`); and at `m = 4` the two evaluation lemmas give `gap = 25845/237728 > 1/10 = bound/4` (probe `probe_dyadic4_ratio_gt_quarter`, one `norm_num` from `dyadic4_gap` and `dyadic4_bound`), i.e. `≈ 0.272 > 1/4` — a fact the library does not state. For `m ≥ 5` the "inside `(1/4, 1/2)`" claim is prose arithmetic (true: each term of the chain sum tends to `1` for `2^k ≪ 2^m`, so `S_m > (m+2)/2` from `m = 4` on), as is "tends to `1/2`". The N+ grade of Theorem C's chain witness is unaffected — it rests on `dyadic4_informative` (`0 < gap < bound < 1`, machine-checked), which is exactly right. Fix I'd accept: reword the three places to "the bracket gives `gap/bound < 1/2` for every `m ≥ 2` and `< 1/4` at `m = 2`; `< 1/4` at `m = 3` (`dyadic3_ratio_lt_quarter`); `> 1/4` at `m = 4` (from `dyadic4_gap`, `dyadic4_bound`); the ratio tends to `1/2` (prose)", optionally landing `probe_dyadic4_ratio_gt_quarter` as `Chain.dyadic4_ratio_gt_quarter`. Presentation: the sentence is false as a statement about the bracket, true as a statement about the ratio, and sits in a witness annotation and a support lemma's docstring rather than in a headline's statement or the findings file — which is why it is not blocking, unlike round 2's B1, which misdescribed the tightness structure of a headline in its own docstring and in the findings.
