## Interface notes for dependents (read these first)

1. **Everything is over an abstract `UnivStructure`** (design decision 1): `univ : ℕ → ℕ` injective, `pol : Bool`, `inst : ℕ → ℕ → Sentence`, `split : Set (ℕ × ℕ × ℕ)`; `S.univSentence u` is `atom (univ u)` or its negation by `pol`; `Ax S : Set Sentence` is schema 1 (`univSentence u 🡒 inst u i`) plus schema 2 as two implications per split triple. Nothing in `Syntax`–`Refutations` is about arithmetic; the first-order instance is target 8 (not landed; see §8).
2. **Entailment is semantic**: `AxEntails S Γ φ := ∀ v : PCWorld, AxHolds S v → v.ConsistentWith Γ → v.Holds φ`, `AxConsistent S Γ := ∃ v, AxHolds S v ∧ v.ConsistentWith Γ`. Soto's rule takes a `[∀ Γ φ, Decidable (AxEntails S Γ φ)]` instance explicitly; `axEntails_decidable (L : S.Local)` provides one from a locality structure, and `mkAtomicLocal` builds a (computable) `Local` for any `mkStructure (fun i => atom (g i)) (fun _ => ⊤)` whose instance atoms avoid the universal atoms (`halvingS_local`, `enumS_local`); the witness statements nevertheless use `open Classical`, so a dependent that wants the computable instance must supply `halvingS_decidable` itself.
3. **Enumerated coordinates**: `e : ℕ ≃ ℕ`; `conj e (u : FiniteWorld k)` is `⋀_{j<k} lit (e j) (u j)`; `enumWorld e u : BoolPCWorld` reads `u` at `e j` and `false` elsewhere; `level e φ` is one above the largest `e.symm`-index of an atom of `φ`. The workhorse lemmas are `holds_congr_of_holds_conj_pc` (a world holding `conj e u` agrees with `enumWorld e u` on every sentence of level `≤ k`) and `chainValAt_eq_of_le` (summing at any level `≥ level e φ` gives `chainVal e p φ`).
4. **Two objects for the extrapolation**: `extrapolateVal S e q : Sentence → ℚ` (rational, the `chainVal` of `sotoRule S e q`) and `extrapolate S e q hq0 : Measure BoolPCWorld` (FAF's `gaifmanMeasure` of that valuation; `hq0 : ∀ w, 0 ≤ q w`). `extrapolate_sentence` links them: `extrapolate … {v | v.toPCWorld.Holds φ} = ENNReal.ofReal (extrapolateVal … φ)`. `univEvent S u`/`instEvent S u i` are the events used in the Gaifman statements.
5. **Standing hypotheses** on the base `q : FiniteWorld B → ℚ`: `hq0 : ∀ w, 0 ≤ q w`, `hq1 : ∑ w, q w = 1`, `hbase : BaseAxConsistent S e q` (`∀ u, q u ≠ 0 → AxConsistent S {conj e u}`). `extrapolate` needs only `hq0`; `extrapolate_extends`/`extrapolateVal_eq_base` need `hq0 hq1`; everything `Ax`-a.e. (3a, 4a, 4b, 4d) needs all three. A base that is the chain of a rule (`chainPMF r B`) gives `extrapolateVal_of_chainBase`: small sentences are priced by `chainVal e r`.
6. **`HalvingCondition S e B u`** does not mention `q` (it is about the rule's `Ax` regime and entailment); `HiddenUniversal S e q u` does (positive `sotoPMF` mass). `FreshInstances S e B u` is the syntactic sufficient condition (4e).
