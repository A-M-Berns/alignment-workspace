## 3. Non-blocking issues

1. **F-17's scope: the wireheading identity does follow from constraint 2 alone when the next state settles the current action.** F-17 and `wirehead_identity`'s fidelity line say the identity "fails under the `faith` field alone" and that "the source's derivation silently uses more than constraint 2", on the strength of `wfPrior` (`wf_identity_fails`). That is true as stated, but `wfPrior` is a prior whose single table cannot express the action (its small index is `{p, q}`), so the action cell `state = T ∧ act = a` is a proper sub-event of `state = T` on which the field says nothing. On the natural reading of bli-soto-b-058 — "an action `a` chosen at time `n`" and a written-out day-`(n+1)` state, which records the day-`n` action (`Q'[A_n = a] ∈ {0, 1}`) — constraint 2 alone suffices. So the strengthening `wirehead_identity` takes is needed exactly when the next state does *not* settle the action, which is what `wfPrior` exhibits. Fix: one sentence in F-17 and in the fidelity line ("needed when the next state does not settle the current action; under the field alone when it does"), replacing "silently uses more than constraint 2" by "uses constraint 2 plus the unstated fact that the next state records the action"; optionally promote the lemma (Kind L).
