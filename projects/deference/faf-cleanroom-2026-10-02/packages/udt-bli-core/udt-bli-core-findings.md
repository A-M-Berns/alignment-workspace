## Mandate §5 items (expected; confirmed or refined here)

**F-1 (presentation; `bli-program-construction` §3.10, §7.7).** The construction draft's `UDTFrame` takes conditional laws and values as *data* with faith axioms; its Good's theorem is then a max-of-sums over an array. Not adopted: here every conditional is `condExp` of one measure `μ` (`Defs.lean`), and Good's theorem (`Good.lean`, `good`) is about `exAnteValue π = 𝔼_μ[U | pp = π]` of actual policies; the array formula `sepValue` is *derived* from `μ` under the policy-level predicates (`exAnteValue_eq_sepValue`), and is shown to differ from `exAnteValue` in general (F-4).

**F-2 (U4's gloss refuted, tie-free, under its stated point-level hypotheses with full positivity; its displayed inequality is a true identity the gloss misidentifies with it; `bli-program` §3.9 U4; revised in repair rounds 1 and 2).** The program states Good's theorem under the *point-level* `Reflective ∧ NoCrossBranch`, displays the max-of-sums inequality `∑_T μ(T) max_a 𝔼[U | T, a] ≥ max_a ∑_T μ(T) 𝔼[U | T, a]`, and glosses it as "deferring to the one-step (= updateful) rule weakly dominates every precommitted action". The gloss, read about the ex-ante value `𝔼[U | pp = π]` of the rule's choice — the reading the program itself commits to when it writes that without `NoCrossBranch` the inequality "can fail — the mugging" (the array inequality cannot fail on the mugging; only the ex-ante gloss can) — is **false under U4's stated hypotheses, even with full positivity, and for every tie-break**: the tie-free prior (`WitnessTieFree.lean`, `tfPrior` — two tables of mass `1/2`, independent fair points, `U` on `T1` = `f(pp·T1, pp·T2)` with `f = [[4,1],[0,3]]`, on `T2` = `g(pp·T2) = (1, 2)`) is `Reflective ∧ NoCrossBranch ∧ NDPOL ∧ NDHOME ∧ NDPOLICY`, `ab` is the **unique** updateful and the **unique** one-step policy there, and `exAnteValue ab = 3/2 < 5/2 = exAnteValue (const c)` for **both** constants (`TieFree.good_fails_pointLevel`); the rule has one choice and every precommitment strictly beats it. The displayed inequality holds there (`sepValue ab = 9/4 ≥ 7/4 = sepValue (const c)`), and `exAnteValue ≠ sepValue` on every one of these positive policies (`TieFree.exAnteValue_ne_sepValue`): what fails is the identification of the array's entries with `exAnteValue`, exactly the step `good` takes through `LocalUtility`, which fails on `tfPrior` (`TieFree.not_localUtility`). The honest statement of the gloss is `good` under the *policy-level* `ReflectivePolicy ∧ LocalUtility` (`Good.lean`, `cellEU_eq_homeEU`, `exAnteValue_eq_sepValue`). Against the program, not against the sources. *Correction of the second version of this finding (audit r2 fidelity B1 = adversarial B1):* it cited the XOR prior (`WitnessXor.lean`, `xorPrior`) as the witness and called U4 "false as stated"; on `xorPrior` both rules are totally indifferent (every home value `1/2`/`0`, every one-step value `1/4`, `Xor.every_policy_updateful`, `Xor.every_policy_oneStep`), so the beaten policy `ab` is updateful by tie only and the tied maximizers `aa`, `bb` attain the best constant value — the XOR prior refutes the ∀-form ("every updateful policy weakly dominates every constant policy", `good`'s conclusion) under the point-level package, not the gloss for the rule's choice; `Xor.good_fails_pointLevel` is regraded N− as a refutation of U4, and `Xor.exAnteValue_ne_sepValue` (tie-independent) stands. *Correction of the first version (audit r1 fidelity B1):* it cited the correlated-points prior `corrPrior`; that was a non sequitur — `corrPrior` is not `NoCrossBranch`, so the point-level package does not hold there, and on its positive policies `exAnteValue = sepValue` does hold. `corrPrior` witnesses the *converse* gap (`ReflectivePolicy ∧ LocalUtility` does not give `NoCrossBranch`, F-11 (i)).

**F-3 (presentation; `bli-program` §3.9 U4).** "Strict iff the argmax varies across positive-mass tables" is imprecise when maximizers are not unique: a maximizer *set* can vary while a common maximizer exists (then equality holds). ## This run's findings

**F-11 (local error in the mandate's T10 formulation, refined; mandate §4 T10).** The mandate asks for `CoordinationFree → NoCrossBranch ∧ Reflective` from a single dependency map `dep` whose soundness clause says "`U` and the branch mass restricted to `state = T` are functions of `pp|_{dep T ∪ {T}}`". Two corrections. (i) `NoCrossBranch` does not follow from any syntactic coordination-freeness alone: the correlated-points prior has `U` on cell `T` a function of `π T` and branch masses constant across positive policies (it is `CoordinationFree` with empty dependency maps, `WitnessLayers.lean`, `corrEntangled`) and still fails `NoCrossBranch` — the points must be independent given the state (`noCrossBranch_of_coordinationFree` takes `IndependentPointsGivenState`; `coordinationFree_not_noCrossBranch` is the gap). (ii) `Reflective` follows from the mandate's single-map clause **only with `NDPOLICY`**: proved (`reflectivePolicy_of_mandateCoordinationFree`, the sum-to-one argument — if every branch's probability depends only on its own point and they sum to one for every policy, each is constant), and refuted without it (`CorrTN.mandate_gap`: two perfectly correlated policies with branch probabilities `9/10` vs `1/10` satisfy the clause vacuously and are not `Reflective`). The formulation of record (`Entangled`) splits the map into a utility dependency `dep` and a probability dependency `depP`; `CoordinationFree := ∀ T, dep T ⊆ {T} ∧ depP T = ∅` gives `ReflectivePolicy` (hence `Reflective`) and `LocalUtility` with no positivity, and `NoCrossBranch` with independence given the state. An earlier version of this finding claimed the Transparent-Newcomb prior refutes the mandate's clause with `dep = ∅`; that was wrong — the point at the full box moves the *other* branch's probability too, so `dep T2 ∋ T1` and the prior is not mandate-coordination-free.

**F-12 (imprecision; `bli-program` §2.8 / mandate T2, `t.InUnit`).** `ofSkeleton` needs no hypothesis on the prior-day table `t`: with horizon `h + 1` every outcome's last table is a grid table (`mem_trajGrid_succ`), and `trajLaw` is a probability for every `t` (`Kernel.prob` is unconditional). The mandate's `t.InUnit` is only needed if one wanted the horizon-`0` case (`state = t` itself), which is excluded by construction.

**F-13 (presentation; mandate §3.7 / bli-slides-034).** The mugging's residual branch: the mandate's "`EU Ask pay = 441/10 + (2/100)·r pay`" is consistent only if the residual utility reads the *Ask* point (`U = r (pp · Ask)` on `Other`), which is what `muggingPrior` does; if the residual read its own point the residual term would average over `r`. **F-14 (scope of the constructions; `Product.lean`).** Every prior built by `IndepData.toPrior` — `ofSkeleton`, `ofSkeletonWith`, `muggingPrior` — is `Reflective` and `ReflectivePolicy` by construction (the policy coordinate is independent of the state). Cross-branch *probability* influence (branch re-weighting) is not representable by this constructor; it is hand-built in `WitnessCorr.lean` (the Transparent-Newcomb-shaped prior) to check `Reflective` false where the two rules separate through re-weighting alone. Dependents that need a skeleton-derived prior with re-weighting must build a different constructor.

**F-15 (ill-posed in the finite model; bli-soto-a-012, Notion 236–253).** The "sensible cross-branch consequences" and "sensible branch re-weighting" desiderata say the large conditionals should reflect what the *small beliefs* say about policy points of other branches. A `FiniteBLIPrior` carries no small beliefs about large policy points (the source itself asks "what would a small belief *about* the correlations of the large branches even look like?"), so the finite rendering can only be stated **relative to a supplied specification** (`SensibleCrossBranch P cross`, `SensibleReweighing P w`, `Desiderata.lean`). Their invariance readings — the specification says the point does not matter — are exactly `NoCrossBranch` and `Reflective` (`noCrossBranch_of_sensibleCrossBranch`, `reflective_iff_sensibleReweighing`). The countable-tree definitions (Def 1–2 with entanglements through an LI's `conditionalQuote`) are recorded, not formalized: `conditionalQuote` at a refuted condition is junk (`bli-program` §3.9 L8), which is the same ill-posedness at the LI level.

**F-16 (imprecision; `bli-program` §3.9 U4 and mandate T5).** The program's `Reflective ∧ NoCrossBranch` package for the updateful-behaviour lemma (T5, `oneStep_iff_updateful`) is exactly right, but its two halves are checked false on *different* priors: `NoCrossBranch` on the mugging (`Reflective` holds there), `Reflective` on the Transparent-Newcomb-shaped prior (`NoCrossBranch` holds there, `TN.noCrossBranch`). Neither predicate is redundant. The mandate's checklist ("each predicate checked false on a committed prior where the conclusion fails") is met by the two priors together, not by the mugging alone. A third data point (repair round 1, corrected in repair round 2): on the tie-free prior `tfPrior` both point-level predicates hold and the rules agree on a **unique** policy `ab`, as T5 says (`TieFree.oneStep_unique`, `TieFree.updateful_unique`) — and Good's conclusion (T6) still fails for that policy against both constants (F-2). (On the XOR prior the rules also "agree", but vacuously: both are totally indifferent there, so it is not a data point about the rules' agreement.) So the point-level package is the right package for T5 and the wrong one for T6; the program's U4 borrowed T5's hypotheses for T6's conclusion.

**F-17 (imprecision; bli-soto-b-058, "Consequence 3"; found by audit r1 adversarial N1).** The source derives the wireheading identity `𝔼_n[u | a] = ∑_{Q'} P_n(Q_{n+1} = Q' | a) Q'[u]` "with constraint 2 in force", i.e. from unconditional reflection `P_n(φ | Q_{n+1} = Q') = Q'[φ]`. That is not enough: `wfPrior` (`WitnessLayers.lean`) has one table `Q₁ = (p ↦ 1/2, q ↦ 0)`, `Ω = Bool` as the current action, `p` true iff the action is `true`, `U = [p]`; the `faith` field holds (`μ(p | state) = 1/2 = Q₁ p`), yet `𝔼[U | act = true] = 1 ≠ 1/2 = ∑_T μ(T | act) · believedValue T` (`wf_identity_fails`). The identity needs reflection *inside each action cell*, `P_n(φ | Q_{n+1} = Q', a) = Q'[φ]` (`FaithGivenAct`), which is what `wirehead_identity` takes and what the source's derivation silently uses. The docstring's fidelity line now says so (variant, needed), and the package's earlier "Fidelity: exact" was an oversell.

**F-18 (local error in this package's rendering of Soto's entanglements, and a limit of the mandate's checklist; `Desiderata.lean`, `Entangled`; bli-soto-a-020 Def 1, bli-soto-a-021 Def 3; mandate T10 and §6; found while answering audit r2 adversarial N2).** The package's `Entangled` structure renders "which other nodes' policy points branch `T`'s utility mentions" by a *pointwise* soundness clause: `U ω = U ω'` whenever the states agree and the points in `dep T ∪ {T}` agree. With full agreement of the points this says `U` is a function of `(state, pp)` (`U_eq_of_entangled`). So **no `Entangled` structure exists on any prior whose utility carries world randomness beyond the policy** — in particular none on the tent prior, whose bet is judged in the world (`Tent.no_entangled`, `WitnessLayers.lean`), and the mandate's checklist item "`CoordinationFree` inhabited (N+) on a prior built from the tent skeleton" is unmeetable at the pointwise level; the only inhabitant the package had, `corrEntangled`, is on a hand-built prior with `U` a function of `(state, pp)` and null policies. The bridges never use the pointwise clause, only the *expected* utility of a cell (`condExp_eq_of_const_on` in `localUtility_of_coordinationFree`). Repair round 2 adds the expectation-level variant `EntangledExp` (utility clause: `cellEU T π = cellEU T π'` on positive cells whose policies agree on `dep T ∪ {T}`; probability clause unchanged): `Entangled` implies it (`entangledToExp`); coordination-free in expectation gives `ReflectivePolicy` and `LocalUtility` (squeezes, as at the pointwise level), `NoCrossBranch` with independence given the state, and Desideratum 3 (`d3_finite_exp`); conversely every `ReflectivePolicy ∧ LocalUtility` prior carries a coordination-free `EntangledExp` with empty maps (`entangledExpOfPolicyLevel`), so at one level **coordination-free in expectation ⟺ the policy-level predicates**. The tent prior carries one (`Tent.tentEntangledExp`), and Desideratum 3 holds there with a varying maximizer (`Tent.d3_on_tent`, N+); the negatives hold at both levels (`Mugging.not_coordinationFree`, `Xor.noCrossBranch_without_coordinationFree`). For the sources: Soto's Def 1 is syntactic ("mentions", at the LI level); a semantic finite rendering has to choose between the pointwise and the expectation reading, and only the latter admits utilities that depend on the world. The package's definition of record `Entangled` is unchanged (its docstring and the ledger now disclose the limitation); dependents that want the tent skeleton should use `EntangledExp`.
