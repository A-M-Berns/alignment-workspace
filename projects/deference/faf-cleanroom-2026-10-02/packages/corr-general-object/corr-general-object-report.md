The simplex carrier of `lit-ddb-frames`/`corr-reflect-frames` is reached by `Distr.mass_mem_stdSimplex` and back by `Distr.ofSimplex` (`Defs`).
- **Kernels.** `Push Ω` is the bundled structure the mandate asks for; theorems are stated on the bare kernel `k : Ω → ℝ` with `IsKernel k` (`∀ ω, 0 ≤ k ω ∧ k ω ≤ 1`) where the bounds matter (deviation 2: kernels built on the fly — `ind E`, the density kernel — need no packaging). `postPush P k hk h` carries both the nonnegativity of `k` and `0 < pushMass P k` as arguments, so it has no junk value.
- **Product form everywhere.** `pushMass`, `pushExpect`, `offExpect`, `Endorsed`, `ValueLegit`, `FunLegit` divide nowhere; the divided forms (`expect_postPush`, the odds form, `pushExpect / pushMass`) carry their guards.
- **The bridge.** `toThreeStep P k hk V Sh h1 h2 : ThreeStep Ω Unit A` with `μ _ := P`, `press _ := k`, `V _ _ a ω := V a ω`.
