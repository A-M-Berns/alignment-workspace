## F-22 (new; settled in repair round 1). T19(c)'s Blackwell clause is false as stated and true for label-preserving garblings (imprecision in the mandate's phrasing: the unlabelled Blackwell order is the wrong order for a labelled push)

"`procureValue` is monotone in the Blackwell order for fixed `Q`". The first version of this finding guessed the obstruction was `cellRegret`'s kernel-dependence; it is the labelling. `resistanceValue` is monotone in neither direction (two-world toy in `Blackwell.lean`'s docstring: the garblings `(q, p)` of the perfect push have `R = 5(p + q)`), not formalized.
