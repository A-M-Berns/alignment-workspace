## 3. Non-blocking issues

**N1 — Target 7(a) is target 3 before its repair: the Kinds invert the logical dependence.** The ledger has `exploring_lesion` (`⊡(Hexp x d) 🡒 (cross 🡒 bad)`) as `P` and `exploring_deliberate_stays` (`⊡(Hexp x d) 🡒 ∼d`) as `C`, and the Lean proves them in that order (`lob_under_boxdot`, then a derivation). P1 (`exploring_deliberate_stays_by_axiomL`, `boxdotHexp_iff_boxdot_neg`, and an `example`): `⊡(Hexp x d) 🡒 ∼d` is axiom L + K + propositional logic with no `lob_rule` — `Hexp` weakens propositionally to `□L 🡒 L` for `L := cross 🡒 bad`, because under `∼d` the crossing is `x` and `bad` holds — exactly the route of the repaired `stays_schema`; `exploring_lesion` is then a `cl_prover` corollary; and `⊡(Hexp x d) 🡘 ⊡(∼d)` for every `x`, `d`. So the content of target 7(a) is one axiom-L step establishing that the boxed exploring schema is boxed deliberate staying, and the lesion is its propositional face. Round 1 (fidelity N2, adversarial N1) found this for target 3 and the repair regraded there; the same regrade belongs here: `exploring_deliberate_stays` `P`, `exploring_lesion` `L`, and one sentence in the docstrings. Round 1's adversarial audit already noted "same structure as target 3 (the content is `∼d`)" in its per-headline line; it did not become a repair item.

**N2 — The binding-coin schema does not collapse, and that is the sharp contrast the package's two target-7 headlines are circling.** P2 (`coin_schema_not_boxdot_neg`): `GL ⊬ ⊡(Hcoin x d) 🡒 ∼d` for `x ≠ d`, on the three-world chain with `d` at the root and `x` at the middle world (at the root `□((x ⋎ d) 🡒 □⊥)` fails because the middle world crosses and is not a dead end, so `Hcoin` holds there vacuously while `d` holds). With N1 this gives a clean two-line statement of dp-core-112(a) versus (b): against the exploring-agent troll the boxed schema *is* boxed deliberate staying (`⊡Hexp 🡘 ⊡∼d`), against the original troll with a pooled coin it is not (`⊬ ⊡Hcoin 🡒 ∼d`), and the package's `binding_coin_lob_stalls` (`⊬ ⊡Hcoin 🡒 (cross 🡒 □⊥)`) is the weaker consequence. Suggest adding P2's statement to `Countermodels.lean` as the target-7(b) headline or recording it in the findings. Separately, the `binding_coin_lob_stalls` row's Kind `N+` is the category stretch round 1 (fidelity N14) named for `causal_not_afflicted` and `tiling_not_afflicted`: it is a `⊬` headline with a countermodel, not a witness for another theorem; `P` or `L` as for its siblings.

**N3 — `unprovable_box_bot` is not Gödel's second theorem; five places say it is.** FAF's `ModalAgents/GL.lean` header: "`unprovable_box_bot : GL ⊬ □⊥`" with no gloss, and "`unprovable_neg_box_bot : GL ⊬ ∼□⊥ (Gödel's second incompleteness theorem)`"; the proof of `unprovable_box_bot` is `unnecessitation!` plus the consistency of GL, i.e. the modal image of "T proves no false Σ₁ sentence of the form □φ" — which is the Σ₁-soundness the doc's Proposition 10 invokes, so the package's *mechanism* label is right. The mislabel originates in round 1's fidelity B1 ("It uses Gödel's second theorem") and was copied. Fix: replace "Gödel 2" by "GL ⊬ □⊥ (FAF's `unprovable_box_bot`, from unnecessitation and the consistency of GL — the GL form of Σ₁-soundness)"; `Fallible.lean`'s docstring already has the right form.
