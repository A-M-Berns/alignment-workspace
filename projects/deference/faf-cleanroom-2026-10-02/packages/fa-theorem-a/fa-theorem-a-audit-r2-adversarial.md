### N2. `lemmaP`'s Fidelity line says `hE01` forces a price-independent day-set; it does not — the Lean theorem is *stronger* than the note (probe `HE01PriceDependent.lean`)

`lemmaP`'s docstring (Fidelity line, adopted from fidelity r1 N7 in round 1) and the ledger row: "an `EF` denotes a continuous function of the day's prices, so a `{0,1}`-valued one is constant in the prices on each day — `hE01` forces `E` to be a price-independent day-set, which is precisely the note's e.c. `E ⊆ ℕ⁺`". So the Lean `lemmaP` admits any generable gate whose *realized* weights are `{0,1}` — e.g. `A`'s own quote gate `Ind_δ(a_n > t)` at an instance where `a_n` never lands in the soft zone `(t, t+δ)` — a genuine strengthening of "efficiently computable `E ⊆ ℕ⁺`", and nothing in the proof (`Engine.lean`) uses price-independence. Fix: Fidelity `stronger: E is any PGenerableWeighting with realized weights in {0,1}, which includes every price-independent e.c. day-set and also gates of A's own prices that happen to be hard at A's realized prices`; delete the continuity sentence from the docstring and the ledger row.
