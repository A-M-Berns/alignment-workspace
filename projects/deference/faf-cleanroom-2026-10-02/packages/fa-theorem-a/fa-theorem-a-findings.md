## F6 — OP8's statement is ambiguous (imprecision)

**Pointer:** `open-problems` item 8; `faithful-acceleration-result` §4.2 Extension ("assumes `𝔼^H_n(X_{k(n)}) → p_∞` for a single limit across blocks, and then runs verbatim"); vq-wiki-032. **Lean:** `theoremA_convergentFamily` (`Extensions.lean`).

## F7 — OP16: 4.8.12/4.8.13 as stated do not replace convergence (wrong direction, proved); the no-surprise inclusion does, and `A`'s side needs nothing (local error in the open problem's premise; new result; the `H`-side residue is conjecture)

**Pointer:** `open-problems` item 16 ("Does 4.8.12 (Persistence of Expectation Knowledge) replace convergence in Theorem A? … stated for an arbitrary `BLCS` sequence with no fixedness hypothesis, which is exactly what the varying-question extension needs"); `unbiasedness-theorem-families` §4.3. **Lean:** `limsup_quote_le_limsup_realized`, `liminf_realized_le_liminf_quote`, `theoremA_limitRange` (`Extensions.lean`); FAF `BoundedSequence.exppolymax`, `BoundedSequence.perexpkno` (statements read, not used).

Proved: for **any** family `X`, arbitrary delay, zero visibility, `liminf Y_n ≤ liminf a_n` and `limsup a_n ≤ limsup Y_n` — the quote's limit range sits inside the realized values' limit range, with no convergence hypothesis and no new FAF endpoint (Route A at `E ≡ 1`). So on `A`'s side nothing at all replaces convergence: convergence was never needed there. What Theorem A's comparison with the diagonal needs is the **no-surprise inclusion** `Y`-range ⊆ `e`-range (`hlo`, `hhi` of `theoremA_limitRange`): `liminf 𝔼^H_n(X n) ≤ liminf Y_n` and `limsup Y_n ≤ limsup 𝔼^H_n(X n)`. Convergence of both to one limit gives it trivially. FAF's 4.8.13 (`exppolymax`: `liminf 𝔼_n(B_n) = liminf futureHigh`, `limsup 𝔼_n(B_n) = limsup futureLow`) and 4.8.12 (`perexpkno`: `liminf futureLow = liminf Val`, `limsup futureHigh = limsup Val`) give, with `futureLow n ≤ Y_n ≤ futureHigh n`, the **reverse** inclusion `e`-range ⊆ `Y`-range: the diagonal cannot be more extreme than the future, but the future may be more extreme than the diagonal — that is what "learning after a delay" means. So the route OP16 proposes does not go through: 4.8.12/4.8.13 do not replace convergence *as stated* (proved: wrong direction, from FAF's own statements); the sharpened statement is `theoremA_limitRange`, and on the proved side the dividing line is surprise (an `H` whose delayed expectations of `X_n` systematically exceed its day-`n` ones), not variation. The per-day `Dominates` form is not implied by the limit-range inclusion alone: `limsup a ≤ limsup e` does not give `a_n − c < e_n` eventually (two sequences oscillating out of phase). **Open residue, in prose, with its register** (repaired in round 1 per audit r1 N1/N3): whether a natural `H`-side hypothesis weaker than convergence implies the no-surprise inclusion is *conjectured no* (the expected obstruction is an `H` that learns `X_n` between day `n` and day `f n`), and whether the per-day `Dominates` conclusion can actually *fail* for a non-convergent inductor `H` is *conjectured yes*; neither is proved or refuted here — a Lean refutation needs LIA price pinning, out of budget ([STANDARDS](../../STANDARDS.md) §5: failure to prove is not evidence of falsity). `eisenstat-lookahead-construction` §3.2 names 4.8.11/4.8.12 as "the natural tool" for the bridge between the two convergences; this finding answers that remark negatively for 4.8.12/4.8.13 as stated. Severity: local error for the proved part (the open problem's premise points at a theorem that gives the reverse inclusion); the residue is conjecture, not a finding.

## F10 — vq-wiki-043's calibration route has a junk-value trap (local error in the note)

**Pointer:** vq-wiki-043 (b) (the Recurring-Calibration route to Half 1, untried). **Lean:** `thresholdTruth_theoryTruth`, `calibration_route` (`Extensions.lean`).
