## F1 — Lemma 3 as printed is false: coverage failure (local error, v2 §7.3 line 256)

The tree is not covered (`cexA_not_covers`). `dp-core-tree`'s F1 already holds the action-veridicality witness (`overwrite_m_not_indep_lesion`); this is the coverage side, and the two are the two homes of T4. Reading: refuted under the explicit-hypothesis reading; under the enumerated-shape reading the tree is out of scope and the lemma is under-hypothesised, not false — the shape line ("Instantiations: …") is load-bearing (ATTRIBUTION-UNVETTED whether it is a stipulation, C1 Open 2). The run-level first clause (the draw independent of the lesion on the consulting runs) holds on the same tree (`cexA_draw_indep_lesion`), so the printed lemma's error is again the draw/coordinate conflation of `dp-core-tree` F1.

**Repair.** `postQuery_screening_recorded` (`General.lean`): under `RecordsFor` at `O_d = ⊤` and `PostQueryIndep`, `ν(X ∧ a)·ν(b) = ν(X ∧ b)·ν(a)`; with `X = evK` the `k`-clause, with `dp-core-tree`'s `screening_recorded` at `X = evL` the `ℓ`-clause. The weakest post-query hypothesis found is `PostQueryIndep` (the cancer mass below a `d`-node's edges is proportional to the edge mass with one constant per node), strictly weaker than the lesion-only clause: counterexample A's `d`-node satisfies it with the lesion drawn afterwards. Lemma 3's true content is therefore: **recording, plus post-query independence of the draw**; (S1)'s lesion-only clause is one sufficient condition for the latter.

## F12 — The mandate's T15 reading of dp-sl-2-008 ("Bayes posterior of the type given the label") is satisfiable, so its "(c) the conjunction fails for every `C`" is false as written (imprecision, mandate T15; repair round 1)

So the item's sentence survives under the certainty reading and not under the mandate's; the formalizer's first version disclosed the switch as `variant` without saying why it was needed (audit r1 fidelity §3.5, adversarial N4).
