## F1 — Lemma 3 as printed conflates the run-level draw with the world-level act (local error, v2 §7.3)

**Source.** `decision-problems-v2` §7.3 Lemma 3: "In any instantiation where each run realizes at most one $d$-node and post-query chance depends on the path only through pre-query chance and the drawn action: conditional on the realized node, the draw is independent of pre-query events; under (S1)+(S3), $m \perp (\ell, k)$ under $\nu_{B,C}$ for every $C$. *Proof:* Definition 6 samples the draw independently of the path."

**Two readings of the first clause** (revised in repair round 1 after the adversarial audit's B1). Read literally — *conditional on the realized node* $q$ — the clause is empty **in a tree**: a decision node of Definition 5's tree fixes its whole path prefix, so a pre-query event is decided (constant) below $q$, and the identity $\mu(X \cap \text{edge } a \cap \text{below } q)\cdot\mu(\text{below } q) = \mu(X \cap \text{below } q)\cdot\mu(\text{edge } a \cap \text{below } q)$ holds for **every** leaf weighting, including ones that bias the draw at $q$ by the path (`Tree.decidedAt_edge_identity`, kind T; round 1 shipped its $\mu_{B,C}$ instance as `screening_at_node`, kind P, which was wrong of it). (In the POMDP packaging that v2 §3.1 mentions in passing, where one node can be reached along several histories, the per-node clause is not empty; the emptiness is a fact about trees.) The per-node content of the proof's sentence "Definition 6 samples the draw independently of the path" is `Tree.mass_edge`: the mass of the $a$-edge below $q$ is $C(d_q)(a)$ times the mass below $q$ — the conditional law of the draw given the node is $C(d_q)$, whatever the path. The clause has content only when the node is *not* conditioned on: **aggregated over the $d$-nodes**, the draw is independent of every event decided at each $d$-node, because that conditional law is the same $C(d)$ at every node. That is `Tree.screening_draw` (`Screening.lean`): if every positive run meets $d$ at most once and every $d$-node met on a positive run decides $X$, then $\mu(X \cap \mathrm{drew}_a)\,\mu(\mathrm{occ}\,d) = \mu(X \cap \mathrm{occ}\,d)\,\mu(\mathrm{drew}_a)$ for every $a$ and every procedure — no recording, and no hypothesis on post-query chance. So the printed first clause is true under the printed hypotheses *if* "conditional on the realized node" means "on the runs that realize a node"; as worded it is vacuous (imprecision).

**What is proved and what fails.** `screening_draw` is a statement about the **draw** at $d$ (a run-level event), not about the **action event** $a \in \mathcal E$ (a world-level event). The second clause, "$m \perp (\ell,k)$ under $\nu$", is world-level: $m$ there is the coordinate of the leaf-world. The passage from "the draw at $q$ is $a$" to "$\lambda(\ell) \models a$" is Definition 7's clauses 3–4 (action-veridicality and its converse) — i.e. recording — which the printed hypothesis does not state. The **overwrite tree** (dp-core-083: a post-draw chance node that re-samples the act coordinate, lesion-dependently) is now in Lean (`Overwrite.lean`, adopted from the fidelity audit's probe and extended): every run meets $d$ once (`overwrite_count`), $\{\ell = 1\}$ is decided at every $d$-node (`overwrite_decided_lesion`), and both post-query coins are functions of $\ell$ and the draw only, so the printed hypothesis shape holds; the run-level clause holds there (`overwrite_screening_draw`: $\tfrac14 \cdot 1 = \tfrac12 \cdot \tfrac12$, `overwrite_draw_values`), while the world-level second clause **fails** — $\nu(m{=}1 \wedge \ell{=}1)\,\nu(\top) = \tfrac38 \ne \tfrac5{16} = \nu(m{=}1)\,\nu(\ell{=}1)$ (`overwrite_m_not_indep_lesion`) — and the tree is not Definition-7 recorded (`overwrite_not_recordsFor`: clause 3 fails on the run lesion / draw $0$ / coin fires). The SL run's own diagnosis ("false without (S4)") is confirmed: (S4) "the act coordinate is written only by $d$'s draw" is recording's clause 3–4 in the tickle instantiation.

**Nearest true statements.** Run level: `screening_draw` above. World level: Lemma 3′ (`Tree.screening_recorded`): under Definition 7 recording at $d$ for $C$ and $X$ pre-query, $\nu(X \cap a \cap O_d)\,\nu(O_d) = \nu(X \cap O_d)\,\nu(a \cap O_d)$ for every $a$ and every procedure, with no hypothesis on post-query chance. The (S1)+(S3) instance follows by taking $X = \{\ell = 1\}$ (pre-query) at a recorded point.

**Severity.** Local error in v2 §7.3 (past the author-vetted line ≈ Definition 8) for the second clause; imprecision for the first (vacuous as worded, true on the aggregate reading). Proposition 11's use of Lemma 3 is unaffected *when the instantiation records* (which "chance $\ell$; query $d$; act $m$; chance $k$; leaf $(\ell, m, k)$" does); the lemma's stated generality is what fails.

## F2 — Definition 7's "a.s." as "not identically zero on symbolic weights" (imprecision, cf toolkit; dp-cf-012)

**Source.** The cf toolkit reads "$\mu_{B,C}$-a.s." as "not identically zero as a polynomial in the symbolic weights", which makes `records` vacuously true at $\nu(O_d) = 0$ and let phase 1's FR-11 be false without a realized-observations hypothesis (dp-cf-012's flag).

**Definition of record here.** `Tree.RecordsFor` quantifies over leaves with $0 < \mu_{B,C}(\ell)$ for the *given* procedure $C$ — the per-leaf reading. The two readings agree on full-support procedures with all chance weights positive and disagree exactly where a leaf has zero mass under $C$ but positive mass under some other procedure: e.g. Told-You-So's $d_{10}$ under $C_0$ (never reached) is recorded per-leaf vacuously here too, but a leaf that is unreachable *because of $C$'s own zeros* is ignored here and counted by the symbolic reading. Every recording theorem in this package therefore carries its positivity through the witness (the N+ trees realise $O_d$ with $\nu(O_d) > 0$).

**Severity.** Imprecision (a reading choice with consequences), recorded so auditors of `dp-calibration` know which reading the tree package fixes.

## F3 — SE-1(a) is definitional under the product-mixture rendering of 6′ (presentation, `seeds.md`)

**Source.** `seeds.md` Definition 6′ is stated operationally ("draws once … a seed $a_d \sim C(d)$, then walks … following the edge $a_{d_q}$ at every decision node"), and SE-1(a) "$\mu'_{B,C} = \sum_\pi (\prod_d C(d)(\pi(d)))\,\mu_{B,\pi}$" is marked *derived*.

**Status here.** The package renders 6′ operationally (`Tree.leafLawSeed`, a memoised walk), so SE-1(a) is a genuine theorem (`Tree.leafLaw'_eq_product_mixture`, kind P), proved by generalising over the seed environment. A package that took the product mixture as the definition would have to label SE-1(a) `D`, not `P` (mandate §6 item 3). No error in the source; recorded to fix the register.

## F4 — "Recording = F3′ + exactly-one" for full-support $C$ (local error, dp-cf-2-001) — refuted as stated, repaired

**Source.** `cf-workflow/phase2-notes/repair/faithful.md` line 40 states the identity verbatim — "for full-support $C$, Def 7 recording $=$ F3′ $+$ 'no other $d$-node on an $O_d$-run'" — and dp-cf-2-001 restates it as "Lemma (stated) … ", flagged *suspicious* (asserted without proof), with F3′ restated as "every node-action-veridical $d$-node is subtree-veridical (Def 7) and $\mu_{B,C}$-a.s. every $O_d$-run passes exactly one node-action-veridical $d$-node".

**Refuted as stated** (`twoBranch_recordsFor_not_actRecording`, `RecordingFull.lean`). F3′'s first clause quantifies over *all* $d$-nodes, including simulation nodes that no $O_d$-run meets, about which Definition 7 says nothing. The two-branch tree — a fair coin, then in each branch a $d$-node whose leaves record the act, with $O_d = \{o = 1\}$ true in one branch only — is Definition-7 recorded at $d$ for **every** procedure (so for every full-support one) and meets $d$ exactly once on every run, yet the off-$O_d$ node is node-action-veridical and not subtree-veridical, so F3′ fails. The direction "recording $\Rightarrow$ F3′ $+$ exactly one" is false; the identity cannot hold for full-support $C$ since the counterexample is procedure-independent. (Reading formalised: F3′ with clause (i) over all $d$-nodes. That is the literal wording — "(i) every node-action-veridical $d$-node is subtree-veridical" quantifies over all $d$-nodes, simulation nodes off the $O_d$-runs included — so the refutation stands on the text as written and makes no claim about what the author intended; the round-1 hedge is dropped for that reason. Round 1's fidelity audit §3.5 had offered a second reason, that line 40's own classification of the routing root applies the all-nodes reading; round 2's fidelity audit §3.1 shows that classification is consistent with both readings, because the routing root *is* on the $O_d$-runs, so that reason is withdrawn. The restricted reading, clause (i) over the nodes met on positive $O_d$-runs, is the repaired identity below, which holds.)

**Repaired** (`recordsFor_iff_actRecordingOn`). With clause (i) restricted to the $d$-nodes met on positive $O_d$-runs (`ActRecordingOn`), node-action-veridicality read a.s. (on positive leaves), and Definition 3's pairwise disjointness of the action events as a hypothesis: for every procedure (full support not needed), Definition 7 recording at $d$ $\iff$ `ActRecordingOn` $\wedge$ exactly one $d$-node on every positive $O_d$-run. Disjointness is what turns clause 3 (the drawn action's event holds) into clause 4 (no other action's event holds). Under full support and all chance edges positive the a.s. reading is the plain one, which is presumably the source's intended setting (FR-11 prunes zero edges).

**Severity.** Local error in dp-cf-2-001 (AI-derived text); the surviving identity is the repaired one.

## F5 — Remark 4.3's miniature is payoff-degenerate at the value level under 6′ (imprecision, mandate §6 item 7; dp-sl-006)

The miniature's two semantics differ at the leaf-law level (`miniature_leaf_separation`: the leaf (sample $a$, live $b$) has mass $\tfrac14$ under Definition 6 and $0$ under 6′ at $q = \tfrac12$) and at the value level too ($V = 3q(1-q)$ vs $V' = 0$) — so it is *not* value-degenerate in the sense of SE-2 caveat c1 unless $q \in \{0, 1\}$. The mandate's phrase "$r \equiv 0$ under 6′" is right ($V' \equiv 0$) but the miniature is a fine witness at both levels; the c1 caveat needs a tree where the nested draws lead to equal payoffs. Presentation only.

## F6 — The positivity hypothesis in FA-4 / T11 is unnecessary (presentation, `faithful.md` FA-4)

`Tree.disposition_faithful` proves $\mu_{B,C}(S \cap \mathrm{drew}_a)\,\mu_{B,C[d\mapsto a]}(\mathrm{occ}) = \mu_{B,C[d\mapsto a]}(S \cap \mathrm{occ})\,\mu_{B,C}(\mathrm{drew}_a)$ with only $\#_d \le 1$: when $C(d)(a) = 0$ both sides vanish. FA-4's "$\nu(\rho_d(a)) > 0$" is needed only to *divide*. Presentation.

## F7 — SE-2's "(⇒)" needs the re-routing construction, which the source states in one clause (presentation, `seeds.md` SE-2)

The source's proof of (⇒) says "the path itself if its two $d$-answers differ; else deviate to $b \ne a$ at $q_2$ and follow positive chance edges". Formalised as `exists_positive_inconsistent` via `exists_positive_with_other_draw`: the re-routed continuation must be chosen chance-positive *and* the second node's new edge must lead to a positive leaf (every subtree has one, `exists_positive_leaf`). No gap; recorded because the "follow positive chance edges" clause is doing real work (SE-2 caveat c2).

## F8 — The mandate's SSC statement and the refutation (imprecision, mandate T6)

The mandate writes the SSC form as "$\mu(X \cap \mathrm{actEv}\,a \mid \mathrm{occ}\,d) = \mu(X \mid \mathrm{occ}\,d)$", which as written is not a screening identity (it drops the conditioning on $a$). The form formalised is the occurrence-conditioned independence $\mu(X \cap a \cap \mathrm{occ})\,\mu(\mathrm{occ}) = \mu(X \cap \mathrm{occ})\,\mu(a \cap \mathrm{occ})$ (`screening_ssc_of_hStar`), which is Lemma 3′ with $\mathrm{occ}(d)$ for the $O_d$-runs and reduces to it under $H^*$. Under `RecordsFor` alone it fails on $B_1$ at $C(d) = \tfrac12$ with $X = O_T$: $\tfrac14 \cdot 1 \ne \tfrac12 \cdot \tfrac14$ (`ScreeningWitness.lean`).
