## 4. What I checked and how

Method. (i) Gate re-run before reading any Lean. (ii) Every ledger row: the declaration exists in the named file; its statement unfolded to `leafLaw` / `Finset` sums and compared with Kind, Fidelity, Hyps, Status and the cited source sentence. (iii) For every headline, an attempt to make it say nothing: degenerate instantiations of its hypotheses (`O_d = ∅` or `⊤`, `acts d` a singleton, no decision nodes, zero-weight chance edges, deterministic and uniform procedures, `k = 0`), and for the theorem whose content is a carried hypothesis (`recordsFor_iff_actRecordingOn`'s disjointness) a counter-model in Lean with the hypothesis dropped. (iv) Every witness checked to inhabit the *full* hypothesis package of the theorem it guards and graded by whether the theorem's own content is exercised on it; every number in the ledger recomputed by hand from the catalogue tree. (v) Junk hunt: every `/` (`gNode`, `bernCoeff`, `screening_recorded_div`, `screening_draw_div`, `tickle_identity_div` — each guarded or confined), every `Nat` subtraction (`leafLaw_twoPoint`, `prod_twoPoint_draws`: guarded by `countDraw_le_count` / `List.count_le_length`), every `Classical` use (`Proc.assignOf`, `extendAssign`, `infiniteFamilyFunctional`: existential or classical `if`, none reaching a headline's value), every cast (`edgeOf` only), every `if` default in a definition of record (none).
