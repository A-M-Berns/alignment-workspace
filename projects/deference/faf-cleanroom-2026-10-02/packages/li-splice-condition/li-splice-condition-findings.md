## F5 — The summable-disagreement conjecture: proved for the parity-trader family, open in general, with the diagnosis (severity: n/a; agenda)

Pointer: R1.5 ("a *summable* disagreement along every e.c. sequence is the plausible sufficient condition [conjectured, ~0.6]"); mandate Extension. `parityTrader_exploits_left_of_summable`: the parity trader's net worth against the splice and against `P₁` differ by at most `Σ_n |P₁_n(φ) − P₂_n(φ)|`, so exploitation of the splice transfers to `P₁`; hence `parityTrader_not_exploits_paritySplice_of_summable` for `P₁` an inductor — nothing is assumed of `P₂`. The conjecture as stated is therefore plausibly false, or needs the disagreement weighted by the trader's magnitude (or decaying faster than every polynomial); stated OPEN (B9). Credence in the unweighted conjecture: below the source's `0.6`; the parity-trader result is evidence only for traders with bounded quantities.
