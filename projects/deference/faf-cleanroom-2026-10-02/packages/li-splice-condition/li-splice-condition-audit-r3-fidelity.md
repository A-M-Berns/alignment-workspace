## 3. Non-blocking issues

**N1 — The candidate theorem's floor is uninhabitable by any inductor on an e.c. family every member of which the completed theory refutes; the docstring's "may be … refuted by later stages" and the header's "refuted by the next stage" need that limit stated, because it is the source's exploration made precise.** `conditioned_candidate_not_gatedExploits` takes `hfloor : ∀ n, ε ≤ P n (ψ n)` with `hcode : MachineSentenceCodes ψ` and `[IsLogicalInductor P DP]`. FAF's provability induction has a disprovable half, `lic_provind_false` (`AffineCoherence.lean:792`: `hdis : ∀ n v, v.ConsistentWithTheory DP → v.Holds (∼ψ n)` gives `P n (ψ n) ≈ₙ 0`). Probe `audit-r3-probes/CandidateFloorRefuted.lean` (exit 0): `candidate_floor_impossible_of_all_refuted` — under the theorem's own hypotheses plus "every `ψ n` is refuted by the completed theory", `False`; and its contrapositive `candidate_floor_realizable` — the floor forces `∃ n v, v.ConsistentWithTheory DP ∧ v.Holds (ψ n)`. So in the regime the `Candidate.lean` header describes ("unchosen candidate … refuted by the next stage") taken for *every* day, the hypothesis package is empty, and the theorem says nothing; it has content exactly when exploration realizes the candidate in the completed theory (and, morally, infinitely often — the cofinite version needs an e.c. certificate for the prefix-to-`⊥` family, not built). This is not a defect of the statement — the source's escape (ii) is "exploration that keeps `ℙ(a) ≥ ε`", and the probe says what that costs — but the docstring, the ledger's Claim column ("they may be mutually exclusive across days and refuted later") and F14's bullet 4 ("kept unrefuted by exploration") should carry it: *the floor is inhabitable only if the candidates are realized in the completed theory; an always-unchosen e.c. candidate has price `→ 0`.* Two consequences for the grading: (i) the witness `paperCandidate_not_gatedExploits` uses a family (`spliceAtom 3`, `spliceAtom 4`, fresh, never decided) in which nothing is ever refuted, so it is N+ for the hypothesis package as written but exercises none of the "refuted when unchosen" structure the reading is about — say "N+ for the package; no inhabitant in the source's refuted-candidate regime is shown, and by `candidate_floor_realizable` any such inhabitant has realizable candidates"; (ii) F14's "the only reading in which the source's floor does work" is right, and sharper than stated: the floor is not only a hypothesis but a hypothesis with a derivable consequence (realizability). Fix: adopt the two probe theorems as lemmas beside `conditioned_candidate_not_gatedExploits` (they import only `Candidate` and FAF's `AffineCoherence`), one clause in the docstring, the row and F14.

The headline `conditioned_candidate_not_gatedExploits` is rightly `C`.
