## 2. Blocking issues

### B1 — The candidate reading (C) is presented on a domain where its floor hypothesis is uninhabitable by any inductor; the theorem is vacuous there, its witness avoids the domain by never letting the process learn the candidates, and F14 tells the author the opposite

**Where.** `Candidate.lean` header l. 11–15 ("Such conditions are mutually exclusive across a day's menu and refuted by the next stage, so no monotone `DeductiveProcess` holds them consistently") and l. 29–30 ("The floor is a genuine hypothesis here (the candidates are not in the process, so nothing makes their prices tend to `1`)"); the docstring of `conditioned_candidate_not_gatedExploits` l. 127–128 ("they may be mutually exclusive across days and refuted by later stages") and l. 134–135; the ledger row (l. 60: "Nothing is assumed of the conditions beyond the floor (they may be mutually exclusive across days and refuted later; no process holds them): the conditional-contract guarantee a decision sequence needs"; "The floor is a genuine hypothesis here"); findings F14 candidate bullet (l. 82: "the day's conditions are unchosen candidate actions `a_n` kept unrefuted by exploration, `P_n(a_n) ≥ ε`; mutually exclusive across a day's menu, refuted by the next stage … **Here the floor is a genuine hypothesis** … this is the only reading in which the source's floor does work") and closing paragraph (l. 84: "the well-posed guarantee is gated non-exploitation under the floor, proved here with a uniform `ε`"); report l. 7 and §Repair round 2 (l. 239); `DayIndexedWitness.lean:136–157` (`paperCandidate_not_gatedExploits`, `Kind: N+`).

**The statement** (`Candidate.lean:141`, quoted):

```
theorem conditioned_candidate_not_gatedExploits (P : History) (DP : DeductiveProcess)
    [hLI : IsLogicalInductor P DP] (ψ : ℕ → Sentence) (hcode : MachineSentenceCodes ψ)
    (ε : ℚ) (hε : 0 < (ε : ℝ)) (hfloor : ∀ n, (ε : ℝ) ≤ P n (ψ n))
    (T : Trader) (hT : EfficientlyComputable T) :
    ¬ GatedExploits T P ψ DP
```

The theorem is correct and its proof is a real transfer through FAF's `conditionedTranslation` (I re-read `gatedNetWorth_sub_one_le_conditionedTranslation`: per-day case split on `locallyGatedConditionalContract_value_lower` / `_eq_zero_of_not_holds`, summed, with `sum_conditioningBudget_le_one`; then `noExploit` at `conditionedTranslation_preserves_ec`). Its conclusion is not trivially true: probe 2 (`AdvGatedExploitsSat.lean`, exit 0) proves `GatedExploits botSeller halfMarket (fun n => atom n) trivialDP` — against the constant-`1/2` market every conditional quote is the cap `1`, so selling `⊥` daily banks `1` on each day the world realizes, and the all-true world realizes every day. So the inductor hypothesis is doing the work. The problem is the domain the package assigns to the theorem.

**(i) On the stated domain the hypotheses are contradictory.** Let `ψ` be e.c. with each `ψ n` refuted by stage `n + 1` of `DP` (`∀ n v, v.ConsistentWith (DP.D (n+1)) → ¬ v.Holds (ψ n)` — "unchosen candidates refuted by the next stage", which is what the header and F14 say the conditions are, and what E7's own subject is: options the belief state has refuted). The day-wise seller of `ψ n` (one price-free trade `(const (−1), ψ n)` per day; e.c. by FAF's `ofSingleTradeBlocksBig` at the family's own certificate — the shape of the package's `oddSeller_ec`) has, on every world consistent with `DP.D n`, net worth `Σ_{i<n} P_i(ψ_i) + (P_n(ψ_n) − payout)`: every earlier candidate pays `0`, so the worth is bounded below by `−1` and is unbounded unless `Σ_n P_n(ψ_n) < ∞`. Probe 1 (`AdvCandidateRefutedFloor.lean`, exit 0): `summable_price_of_nextStage_refuted` (`Summable (fun n => P n (ψ n))` from `[IsLogicalInductor P DP]`, `hworld`, `hcode`, the refutation), `no_floor_of_nextStage_refuted` (a uniform `ε > 0` gives `False`), `candidate_floor_uninhabited_of_nextStage_refuted` (`¬ ∃ ε : ℚ, 0 < ε ∧ ∀ n, ε ≤ P n (ψ n)` — exactly the floor package of the theorem above), and `floor_summable_of_nextStage_refuted` (any nonnegative `ε_n ≤ P_n(ψ_n)` is summable). The class is real: probe 3 (`AdvRefutedFamilyInstance.lean`, exit 0) inhabits every hypothesis with FAF's LIA of the negated-atom prefix process (`negAtomDP := prefixProcess (fun i => ∼ atom i)`, computable by `prefixProcessComputation` at `machineSentenceCodes_atom.neg`, the all-false world at every stage) and the injective family `n ↦ atom n` — day `n`'s candidate, which the process refutes on day `n + 1`. So wherever the candidates are learned to be unchosen, `conditioned_candidate_not_gatedExploits` has no instance, and the sentence "the floor is a genuine hypothesis here (… nothing makes their prices tend to `1`)" is true for the wrong reason: nothing lets them stay above a summable sequence either.

**(ii) The source's "`Σ` constraints" cut against the floor, and are the regime the package cannot reach.** E7 l. 235 conjectures "a price floor `ε_n` with `Σ` constraints suffices". Probe 1's last lemma is the first precise statement of the constraint in this package: for next-stage-refuted candidates, `Σ ε_n < ∞` is *forced*. F14 records a decaying floor as "outside FAF's translator (one rational `ε`) and is recorded, not attempted", and says "whether a decaying `ε_n` with `Σ` constraints gives the same … remains open". The honest record is sharper: the uniform floor FAF's translator needs is impossible on that domain, the only floors available are summable ones, and no FAF route to gated non-exploitation under a summable floor is known (the translated coefficients scale like `1/ε_n`, which the conditioning budget does not absorb) — this is where the content of 063(a) in the source's reading sits, and the package's theorem does not touch it.

**(iii) Where the theorem is non-vacuous, and what the witness shows.** The floor is inhabitable in two situations. (a) The process never learns the candidates' fate: fresh atoms over `paperDP`, which `paperDP_spliceAtomFree` keeps undecided at every stage, so the LIA's prices converge to interior limits and a finite patch gives the floor — this is `paperCandidate_not_gatedExploits`. It inhabits the literal hypothesis package (a genuine inductor, a varying family), but the floor is maintained by the process's ignorance, with no exploration pressure at all: for the reading the row names (candidates whose prices exploration must hold up against the inductor learning they were not taken) it is **N−**, not N+. (b) The process learns the realized action each day and exploration takes each candidate at positive frequency, so that a uniform floor and non-summable confirmations coexist (the seller argument of (i) shows the takings must be non-summable — in fact of positive density infinitely often — for a uniform floor to survive). That is the source's setting; it needs an inductor over a process that learns a sequence pseudorandom relative to e.c. traders, which nothing in this package builds and which F13 does not list. The row's "N+ for the hypotheses" does not say which of (a)/(b) is exhibited; it is (a).

**Why blocking.** [STANDARDS](../../STANDARDS.md) §5 makes the finding the first-class output here, and F14's candidate bullet would steer the author into stating 063(a) as "gated non-exploitation under a uniform floor, proved" for a setting in which that hypothesis can never be met, while telling them the floor "does work" there. Round 1's B-2 and round 2's B1 were the same shape (a reading presented as settling the source's claim with the content elsewhere) and were treated as blocking; so is this. The "N+ claim that is really N−" criterion of the audit prompt also applies to the witness as graded for the reading.

**Fix I would accept.** (1) Adopt probe 1's lemmas into the package (`DayIndexed.lean`'s (B3) section is the natural home, beside `summable_schedule_mul_price_of_refuted`, of which the day-wise version is the varying-family analogue): `summable_price_of_nextStage_refuted` (P), its no-floor and summable-floor corollaries, with probe 3's `negAtomDP` instance as the N+ (and `daySeller_ec` as its certificate). (2) Prose in all four places (header and docstrings of `Candidate.lean`, the ledger (C) row, F14's candidate bullet and closing paragraph, report l. 7 and §Repair round 2; `Open.lean:33–45`): the theorem's domain is "candidates not learned by the process, or taken by exploration non-summably often"; on candidates refuted by the next stage the floor is impossible and every floor is summable, so the source's "`Σ` constraints" is forced rather than optional and FAF's uniform-`ε` translator does not reach it; the sentence "this is the only reading in which the source's floor does work" becomes "the only reading in which the floor is a hypothesis at all, and one it can satisfy only through exploration takings, which no witness exhibits". (3) Regrade `paperCandidate_not_gatedExploits`: N+ for the literal package, N− for the reading (no learning); say in F13 that an exploration witness (an inductor over a process learning a pseudorandom realized-action sequence with a uniform floor on a candidate family) was not built. (4) Either state the summable-floor target precisely as OPEN — "for an e.c. family refuted by the next stage and a summable floor `ε_n ≤ P_n(ψ_n)`, no e.c. trader's gated assessments are bounded below and unbounded above" — or record in F14 why it is not written (no FAF translator for a varying budget; nothing suggests it is true).

## 3. Non-blocking issues

**N5 — `paperCandidate_not_gatedExploits` disclosure** ("which disjunct holds for that trader is not shown") is honest and worth keeping; after B1's regrade the docstring should also say the base process never decides either atom (`paperDP_spliceAtomFree`), so the floor is free there.
