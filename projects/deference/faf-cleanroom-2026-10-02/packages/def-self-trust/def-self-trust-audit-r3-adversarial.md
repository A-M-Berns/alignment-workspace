## 3. Non-blocking issues

- **N1 — "On any decided source Theorem B is `provind`-implied for every forecast" is false as a generality and self-contradictory; machine-checked.** The sentence appears in the `Witness.lean` header ("on *any* decided source Theorem B is `provind`-implied for every forecast … so a content-exercising inhabitant needs an undecided source … the pseudorandom family over T7"), in `theoremB`'s docstring ("on a decided source (payout `∈ {0,1}` in every world) Theorem B is `provind`-implied for every forecast, and a content-exercising inhabitant needs an undecided source"), in the `RobustSelfTrust.lean` header, in the report (6e, "Not done", § Repair round 2) and in the ledger's load-bearing row 4. Under its own definition of "decided" (payout `0` or `1` in every completed-theory world), **the pseudorandom family of record is a decided source**: probe `DecidedPseudorandom.lean`, `truthStar_source_decided`, proves `∀ n v, ConsistentWithTheory (atomDP a (truthStar a g p) g) → v.payout (atomFamily a n) = 0 ∨ = 1` from `truthStar_theoryTruth` and `truthR_bool`; and the package's own `truthStar_uniformAccuracy_false` says the price does *not* track that decided payout (`decided_but_not_tracked` packages the two, under the T7 binder exactly as the package takes it). So the sentence, applied to the very family it names as the content-exercising witness, says Theorem B is `provind`-implied there — which contradicts target 7's whole point. The argument's own parenthetical gives the game away: "with payout `0` it reads `t·û_n ≤ (1+t)·e_n`, which the ramp arithmetic gives *once `Y_n → 0`*" — and `Y_n → 0` on the refutable days is `thm:provind`'s conclusion only when the refutable days form an e.c. family, i.e. when the decision *pattern* is machine-enumerable. A decided e.c. source whose pattern is not poly-time (every `φ_n` provable or refutable in `T`, which is which not poly-time computable) is one on which nothing forces `Y_n` to the payout, and Theorem B then says exactly what it is for: `E_n(⌜X_n û_n⌝) ≳ t·û_n − (1+t)·e_n` binds the market's *current* expectation from below on gate-live days where the forecast tracks the deferred price. What the parity source has and the family of record lacks is a decision pattern `thm:provind` can reach. **Fix I would accept** (wording only, no grade changes): replace "decided" by "decided with a `provind`-reachable pattern (the theorem-days and the refutable-days are each e.c. families, so `thm:provind` drives the deferred price to the payout)" and "an undecided source" by "a source whose decision the market cannot reach by day `f(n)` (a pseudorandom pattern)", in the four Lean places, the report and the ledger row; keep every N− grade and the "no content-exercising inhabitant constructible over `paperDP T` without T7" scope statement, which is a claim about what FAF can *prove* and is right.
