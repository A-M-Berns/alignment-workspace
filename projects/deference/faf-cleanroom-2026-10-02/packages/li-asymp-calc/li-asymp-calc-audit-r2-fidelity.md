## 3. Non-blocking issues

1. **B1′'s plain-words claim states the LI corollary, not the implication proved.** `DivergentWeighting.not_hasLimitPoint_weightedBias` (`LimitPoint.lean:156`) proves: `DivergentWeighting W P`, and from day `N` on `0 < (W n).denote P → c ≤ market n − truth n`, ⟹ `¬ HasLimitPoint (weightedBias (fun i => (W i).denote P) market truth) 0`. Its docstring's first sentence and the ledger's Claim column read "a divergent weighting **cannot be supported**, from some day on, where the bias is one-signed and bounded away from zero" — that is the contrapositive *after* composing with `recurringunbiasednessexp` (which supplies `HasLimitPoint … 0` for a generable weighting under an inductor), and the docstring itself says that composition is `fa-theorem-a`'s and no `IsLogicalInductor` is here. The wording is inherited from the mandate's B1′ text; the parenthetical "(the negation of `recurringunbiasednessexp`'s conclusion)" tells a careful reader what is proved, which is why this is not blocking. Fix: lead with the implication — "if a divergent weighting is supported, from some day on, only where the bias is `≥ c > 0`, then `0` is not a limit point of its `weightedBias`; against `recurringunbiasednessexp` this says such a weighting cannot be generable" — in the docstring and the ledger Claim.

2. **`WeightedApprox.trans`/`.add`: the divergence hypothesis is dispensable** (probe 1, `AuditR2.weightedApprox_trans_total`, `weightedApprox_add_total`). Because FAF's `weightedAverage` is `0` at zero mass, the additivity identity `weightedAverage w (x − z) n = weightedAverage w (x − y) n + weightedAverage w (y − z) n` holds at every day (both sides `0` on zero-mass days: `AuditR2.weightedAverage_sub_add_total`), so transitivity and additivity hold for **every** weighting, exactly as the earlier Lean's `ApproxW.trans` (`StreamlinedSS.lean:150`, no hypotheses) does. The package's `hdiv` is the mandate's convention ("state the calculus under `hw`, `hdiv`") and every consumer has it in scope, so nothing is lost; but "Fidelity: exact" is exact against the prose (`theorem-ss-streamlined` §1 defines `≈_{w̄}` only for `W_n → ∞`) and *weaker by a dispensable hypothesis* against the cited Lean source. Fix: drop `hdiv` (the probe's proofs port as they stand), or say in the docstrings that the hypothesis is the mandate's and dispensable. Finding 11 ("needs less than the mandate's package") would then say "nothing".

4. **Finding 9's "no truth stream at all" is specific to the undecided threshold; say so** (probe 2, `AuditR2.gridLUV_mesh_determined_atomDP`). `not_determined_mesh_ofLUV_onGrid` uses `DeductiveProcess.empty`, under which the atom at the grid point is never decided, and shows no truth stream makes the mesh exactly `DeterminedViaTheory`. With the deductive process that reveals `atom 0` on every day, `DeterminedVia gridLUV DP 0` still holds on every day and the precision-`(n+1)` mesh **is** exactly `AffineCombination.DeterminedViaTheory` — at the truth `1/(n+1)`, which differs from the mandate's grid-rounded truth `(n+1)⁻¹ · #{i < n+1 : i/(n+1) < 0} = 0` on every day.
