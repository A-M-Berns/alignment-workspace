## Closed by this package (the mandate's "known issues")

1. **"Continuous ⟹ legal trader" was a modelling substitution** (`AUDIT` §3.2; `faithful-acceleration` §9 "continuity is the legality property"; `deference-in-logical-induction-v6` §5.9; root-fa-004). Severity: *imprecision* in the sources' phrasing, now *closed in one market*. Legality over FAF is `EF`-expressibility plus ℙ-generability, and both are theorems about the doubly-soft construction: `dsFeature_denote` (the feature denotes `dsWeight` of the day-`n` prices) and `dsFeature_pgenerable` (a `PGenerableWeighting` for machine-coded `φ ψ`, constant rational `t ε δ`). Joint continuity in the pair (`continuous_dsWeight`) is a *consequence* of expressibility (`EF.continuous_denote`), and it is *necessary* — D3 `not_exists_ef_hardIndicator` shows a hard price indicator is no `EF` — but it is not the property itself: "continuous" is not the same as "an `EF`", and generability is a separate obligation the prose never states. **Residue:** the corpus's weight `Ind_δ(a_n > t) · Ind_δ(E^H_n(X) < t - ε)` mixes `A`'s quote with `H`'s expectation; it is a feature of `H`'s market only once the quote is a sentence priced in `H` — `li-quote-lane` target (4). Nothing here asserts cross-market legality.

## New findings

9. **Mandate G3 (c): "exact" mesh determination at the grid-rounded truth is false on the grid — refuted in Lean.** Severity: *local error* (in `li-asymp-calc-mandate`, not in a research note). FAF's `PCWorld.ValuesAt v X y` constrains thresholds strictly below `y` (affirmed) and strictly above (denied) and says nothing about a threshold **equal** to `y`; when `y n = i/(n+1)` for some `i ≤ n`, two completed worlds valuing `X n` at `y n` may disagree on `(X n).gt (i/(n+1))`, so the mesh values differ by `1/(n+1)`. Repair round 1 turned "may disagree" into a refutation: `not_determined_mesh_ofLUV_onGrid` (`Luv.lean`) takes `X n = gridLUV` (`⌜X > r⌝` is `⊤` for `r < 0`, `⊥` for `r > 0`, the atom `0` at `r = 0`), `y ≡ 0` and the empty deductive process `DeductiveProcess.empty`: every world values `gridLUV` at `0` (`gridLUV_valuesAt`), so `DeterminedVia` holds on every day, yet **no truth stream at all** makes the precision-`(n+1)` mesh of `ofLUV (X n)` exactly `AffineCombination.DeterminedViaTheory` — the worlds `fun _ => True` and `fun _ => False` value the day-`0` mesh at `1` and `0`. Proved positive forms: `DeterminedVia.approxDetermined_mesh_ofLUV` (approximate, error `1/(n+1)`, unconditional — FAF's `expectApprox_near_ofGrid`) and `DeterminedVia.determined_mesh_ofLUV_offGrid` (exact at `(n+1)⁻¹ · #{i < n+1 : i/(n+1) < y n}` under the off-grid hypothesis `∀ n, ∀ i ≤ n, i/(n+1) ≠ y n` — restricted to `i ≤ n` in repair round 1 so that `y n = 1`, which is not a grid point, is not excluded). Dependents wanting exactness must supply off-gridness or work at the approximate level, which is what FAF's own endpoints do (`ExpectationProperties.lean:1698`: "deliberately no exact mesh-determination lemma").

13. **G1's "`BoundedSequence` passes through for bounded `g`" is half true.** Severity: *imprecision* (mandate).
