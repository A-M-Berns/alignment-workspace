**Type-space reduction (used by the search, stated in prose).** For `Q`-measurable data only the pair `(Q w, Q_* P_w)` of a world matters; so both predicates are properties of a finitely supported measure `μ` on `C × Δ(C)`. Local Total Trust: for all `x ∈ ℝ^C` and `s`, `∑_t μ_t (x_{c_t} − s)·𝟙[⟨p_t, x⟩ ≥ s] ≥ 0` and the dual. Weak local Value: for every finite menu `{x_o}` the strategy `t ↦ argmax_o ⟨p_t, x_o⟩` (ties broken as a function of `p_t`) has `∑_t μ_t x_{o(t)}(c_t) ≥ max_o ⟨π̄, x_o⟩`, `π̄` the `C`-marginal. Two-option menus follow from local Total Trust (T4(a) in reverse is `valuesWrt_questionOf_core`'s two-option case; in general the two-option weak case is the threshold inequality at the crossing), so a counterexample needs three or more options.

**Search families and method** (`/tmp` scratch scripts `t4d_search{,2,3,4}.py`, NumPy + SciPy, 2026-10-01; **not committed and no longer present — the counts and slacks below are not reproducible from the repo** (audit r1 N3); the structural claims of the attempt log stand on their own and were re-derived by both round-1 auditors). (1) Random types: `n ∈ [3, 7]` types with Dirichlet-random `p_t ∈ Δ(3)` and random cells; local Total Trust tested by sampling 1,500–4,000 directions `x` (random, plus all pair-tie planes `x ⊥ p_i − p_j` and triple-tie lines, which is where violations live) and checking both forms at every attained threshold. Result: 0 of 150 random measures pass local Total Trust — random measures are never trusting, so the generator was changed. (2) LP-generated trusting measures: fix the types, collect the linear constraints in the weights `m` from ~600 sampled directions (each direction and attained threshold gives one row), solve LPs with random objectives for vertices of the trust cone, mix vertices randomly for interior points, re-verify local Total Trust with fresh samples (rejecting any failure), then search 3-option (and 4-option) menus for a local-Value violation by 1,200–2,000 random menus followed by 4,000 steps of random local descent on the slack `E[x_S(c)] − max_o ⟨π̄, x_o⟩`. **Exact slack, what was learned (rounds 0–2).** On every trust-feasible measure found, the minimal 3-option slack is 0, attained at menus where one option is optimal on a region containing no support type or where two options tie at a support type — i.e. the generic minimum is a boundary of the two-option case, not a new phenomenon. This was read as weak evidence for the weak reading (the LP vertices tend to be "reflected" measures, where the conjecture is trivial; the mixtures add interior points but the family is still biased toward the easy region), with the caveat that a failure to find is not evidence of truth. **Why the search missed it (repair round 3, from audit r3 adversarial B1).** The search generated trust-feasible measures first and menus second; the LP vertices of the trust cone are reflected measures, on which Value is automatic, and random mixtures of them rarely leave the easy region. Reversing the order finds a violation at once: fix a three-option menu with all three regions inhabited, make the forced strategy's Value slack the LP objective, and minimise it over the trust polytope (one linear constraint per closed half-plane class of the finitely many type points; the pair-line system is complete). The round-3 scripts (`audit-r3-probes/t4d_lp_search.py`, `t4d_exact.py`, `certify.py`) and the exact rational certificate (`t4d_certificate.json`) are committed; the first run's worst slack was `−0.10`, the certified instance's `−1/15`. **Why the conjecture fails (the structural paragraph of rounds 0–2, re-labelled in repair round 3: it was written as "where a proof breaks (attempt (ii))"; the counterexample confirms its wedge picture — four types, one interior — but not its claim that local Total Trust pins `π(· | p₀) = p₀` at every isolatable type: see the numerical note after it. The paragraph itself is unchanged.)**
 The global proof (DDB Lemma 7.3 via Theorem 4.1) needs two things at each candidate `ρ`: the informed decomposition `ρ = λ_ρρ P̂_ρ + ∑ λ_ρj ρ_j` over `ρ`'s *own* candidates, and New Reflection `π(· | P = ρ) = P̂_ρ`. In the type space a type `p ∈ Δ(C)` has no candidates of its own — "the candidates `ρ` leaves open" is defined through `supp ρ ⊆ W`, which `Q` erases — and local Total Trust pins `π(· | p)` only for *isolatable* types (those outside the hull of the others, T7's `addressable_iff_extreme` in the type space): for an isolatable `p₀`, the separating directions give `E_{p̂₀}(x) = E_{p₀}(x)` on an open cone, hence `p̂₀ = p₀`; for interior types no clause of local Total Trust mentions `π(· | p₀)` alone. The two-cell proof escapes because in one dimension every region of a menu is an interval, so an induction over upward-closed sets reduces every region to above/below cuts; in two or more dimensions the regions of a 3-option menu are wedges, which are not differences of half-space events, and the decomposition `E_π(S) − E_π(O) = ∑_k π(A_k)(ℓ_k − ℓ_O)(π(q | A_k))` has no sign control on a wedge `A_k`. A lift to a global frame on the type space (choose rows above each `p` with the right pushforward) would import Theorem 2.2, but the lift must satisfy global hull + modest informedness, and the natural lifts (`p ↦ p(1)·π(·|q) + p(0)·π(·|¬q)` in two cells; its `C`-fold analogue) fail modest informedness at extreme types unless `p̂ = p` there — which local Total Trust does give for isolatable types but not for a type on the boundary of the hull that is not a vertex. That is the precise gap: **local Total Trust controls the informed conditional at vertices of the candidate hull and nowhere else, while local Value needs it (or a substitute) on every wedge of every menu.**

**Numerical note (repair round 3; scripts and outputs in `packages/corr-legit-general/repair-r3-probes/`; register: numerical, not certified, flagged for the next audit).** Two quick runs of the round-3 LP machinery after the refutation.
