**N4 — F-8 under-reads the paper's "less than 1.75", and that bound is the route to the OPEN statement.** F-8: "(the paper's 'less than 1.75' at `θ = 1/2` is exactly `5/3`)". The exact interrupted value of always-`a` at `θ = 1/2` is indeed `5/3` (`fig2_int_values`), but the paper's `1.75` is not an estimate of it: it is the bound `1 + γ(1−θ) + γ²·2 ≤ 7/4` on the interrupted value of *any* continuation that plays `a` at `s₁` when `θ ≥ 1/2` (the transcription's display is garbled; `1 + ¼ + ½ = 7/4`), and `7/4 < 9/5` is how the paper gets int-optimality of `b` at `s₁` over **all** policies for every `θ_t ≥ 1/2` — a stronger statement than the package's `thm8_int_optimal` (four stationary deterministic policies, `θ > 3/10`), which the fidelity line discloses. Consequence for `thm8_sequence_form` (OPEN): for `θ_t → 1` pick `T` with `θ_t ≥ 1/2` for `t ≥ T`; then at every `t ≥ T`, `Q_t(s₁, a) ≤ 1 + ½·((1−θ_{t+1}) + ½·2) ≤ 7/4 < 9/5 ≤ Q_t(s₁, b)` (always-`b` attains `9/5` from any time), so an int-optimal non-stationary policy plays `b` at `s₁` at every `t ≥ T` along positive-probability histories, and from its first visit to `s₁` after `T` (at most one step later, since both actions from `s₂` lead to `s₁`) the history is constant with difference `1/5` — Cesàro mean `1/5`. What the formalization then needs is (i) the bound above for `VlimNS` and (ii) the dynamic-programming principle for non-stationary policies (optimal from `(0, s₁)` ⇒ optimal continuation on every positive-probability history), not a characterisation of the policy before `T`. Record this in the OPEN's reason (open list and docstring) in place of "what resists: characterising the nonstationary int-optimal policy … may play `a` while `θ_t < 3/10`" — it may, and it does not matter. Also fix F-8's parenthesis to "the paper's `1.75` is the bound `1 + γ(1−θ) + γ²·2` for `θ ≥ 1/2`; the exact value at `θ = 1/2` is `5/3`".
