## 2. Blocking issues

### B1. `Size.atomDay`/`Size.Sminus` — the "`≥` the day" claim on tag `2` reads a packing convention, not the day; `Sminus m m` contains decided claims about later market days

**Declarations and prose.** `Size.atomDayBase`/`atomDay`/`Sminus` and their docstrings (`Size.lean:486–537, 576–582`: "`atomDay a` is `≥` the day the atom quotes … the packed input, hence `≥` the day, for tag `2` … So `atomDay a < m` implies the atom quotes no day `≥ m`: **`Sminus n m` under-approximates the prose scope** … every predicate quantified over it … is correspondingly *weaker* than the source, never inconsistent with it"); the root module docstring (`BliFound.lean:31–34`); the `StateSentence` module docstring (`:60–69`, "Conservative (the tag-2 reading is `≥` the day, so `Sminus` is a subset of the prose scope and E2x is weaker, never inconsistent)"); the ledger's D3 row ("on every FAF-allocated family `atomDay a ≥` the day quoted, so **`Sminus` under-approximates the prose scope**") and D5 row for `E2x`/`E2i` ("the scope `Sminus m m` is a subset of the prose scope … so both are weaker than the source"); findings F-14 ("So now, on every atom of a FAF-allocated family, `atomDay a` is `≥` the day it quotes, and `Sminus n m` under-approximates the prose scope … never inconsistent with it — the claim the round-1 prose made, now true"); report line 43.

**What the Lean says.** `atomDayBase a` reads, for `a.unpair.1 = 2`, the number `a.unpair.2.unpair.2.unpair.2.unpair.2`, which for `quotationClaimCode pos neg w` with `w = ⟨code, input⟩` is `input` (`StateSentence.atomDay_quotationClaimCode`, verified against `Packages.lean:81`). The justification given is that "the day sits inside `input`" and `Nat.pair` dominates its components. That holds for quote codes that pack the day *as a component of the input* — FAF's `RationalQuoteCode` mesh convention `⟨n, ⌜r⌝⟩`, the package's `marketQuoteCode` (`⟨⟨m, ⌜φ⌝⟩, ⌜r⌝⟩`) and `cellQuote` (`⟨m, ⟨c, r⟩⟩`). It is not a property of tag `2`: `BooleanQuoteCode.ofComputable` (`Packages.lean:313`) takes *any* `ComputablePred`, and `paperDP T` decides every quotation claim of every code at every input (`QuotationTheoryPresentation.quote_positive_enters`/`quote_negative_refutes`, `Packages.lean:243–248`, through `paperQuotationPresentation`). A decider whose day lives in its program, or is computed from the input, is read below its day.

**Evidence (probe P1, `audit-r3-probes/Vacuity.lean`, all elaborated).** `laterDayTruth T i := halfRound (i + 2) (marketValue T ⟨i + 2, i⟩) = 1` ("on day `i + 2` the market's quote of the sentence with code `i` rounds to `1`") is a `ComputablePred` (`laterDayTruth_computable`: the package's own `cellTruth_computable` precomposed with `i ↦ ⟨i + 2, ⟨i, 1⟩⟩`); `laterDayQuote T := BooleanQuoteCode.ofComputable …` and `laterDaySentence T i := (laterDayQuote T).sentence i`, a single tag-`2` atom with `input = i` (`laterDaySentence_eq`, by `rfl`). Then:

- `laterDaySentence_reflected`: in every completed-theory world of `paperDP T`, `v.Holds (laterDaySentence T i) ↔ laterDayTruth T i` (FAF's `BooleanQuoteCode.reflected` at `paperQuotationPresentation T`); `laterDaySentence_decided`: the literal or its negation enters a stage of `paperDP T`.
- `laterDaySentence_iff_cellSentence`: `v.Holds (laterDaySentence T i) ↔ v.Holds (cellSentence T halfRound _ (i + 2) i 1)` in every such world — the probe's atom and the package's own day-`(i+2)` cell literal say the same thing.
- `atomDay_laterDaySentence`: every atom of `laterDaySentence T i` has `atomDay = i`.
- `laterDaySentence_small`/`laterDaySentence_mem_Sminus_eventually`: `∃ M, ∀ i ≥ M, laterDaySentence T i ∈ Sminus (i + 1) (i + 1)` (the index is below `(i+1)^16` once `i` dominates the three fixed shell constants, so the atom has at most `8(i+1)+2` base-4 digits and `8i + 11 ≤ 2^{2^{i+1}}` from `i = 2`).
- **`sminus_scope_is_packing_dependent`** (headline): `∃ M, ∀ i ≥ M, laterDaySentence T i ∈ Sminus (i+1) (i+1) ∧ cellSentence T halfRound _ (i+2) i 1 ∉ Sminus (i+1) (i+1) ∧ ∀ v, v.ConsistentWithTheory (paperDP T) → (v.Holds (laterDaySentence T i) ↔ v.Holds (cellSentence T halfRound _ (i+2) i 1))`.

So with `m := i + 1`, the day-`m` scope of faith `Sminus m m` contains a literal that `paperDP T` decides and that every completed-theory world reads as "the day-`(m+1)` rounded quote of `i` is `1`" — a claim about a market state **strictly later than `m`**, excluded by every reading of the prose (Notion line 207: "`φ` contains no info about market states later than `m`"; `bli-program` line 56: "quoting no market day `≥ m`"; bli-slides-030: "no claims about future market states"; the package's own `Sminus` docstring: "no information about market states on or after day `m`") — while the semantically identical `cellSentence` is excluded. Membership in the scope depends on how the quote code packs its input, not on what the literal says. Consequently `E2x S P` at `n < m` demands, for `q ∈ S.states m`, `P n (laterDaySentence ⋏ σ_m) = S.val m q (laterDaySentence) · P n σ_m` — faith in the day-`m` table's price of a day-`(m+1)` market fact, which the source does not ask for. `E2i`, `E3`, `E3fin` (scope `Sminus m m`) inherit this; so do the σ-forms. The same atom wrapped as an old-language copy `Nat.pair 6 a` inherits it through `atomDay_pair_six`.

**Why blocking.** The audit prompt's list includes "a ledger row that misstates" and "a false claim in the findings file"; both apply, to the same claim round 2 found false on tag `3` and repair round 2 asserted true "on every atom of a FAF-allocated family". The direction matters downstream exactly as round 2 said: seven dependents quantify faith over `Sminus`, and an *impossibility* result over `E2x`-style faith could rest on a sentence the prose scope excludes. No theorem of this package is affected (nothing uses `atomDay`'s value on a quote code other than the package's two, whose packing is day-first), which is why the repair is cheap.

**Why a definition change cannot fix it, and what I would accept.** The day of a tag-`2` atom is a property of the *program* the code names, not of the atom's index; no reader `ℕ → ℕ` recovers it (P1 exhibits a single fixed code whose literals quote day `input + 2`; the same construction quotes any computable function of the input). Tags `0`/`1` (halting claims about arbitrary computations, decided by `theoremDP`) and tag `5` (first-order primes: `𝗜𝚺₁` proves every true Σ₁ sentence, and "the LIA's program on day `d` outputs price `p` for `ψ`" is one, so `paperTheoryDP` decides such primes) carry a day only semantically, and `atomDay` reads `0` (probe P2: `atomDay_paperPrimeSentence`, `paperPrimeSentence_mem_Sminus_iff` — every prime that is small on day `n` is in `Sminus n m`, whatever it says). So the honest statement is: **`Sminus` renders the syntactic scope for the quote families that pack the day first (the run's families, FAF's product atoms and their copies, `marketQuoteCode`, `cellQuote`) and under-approximates it there; it is incomparable with the semantic prose scope in general** (tag-`2` codes with the day in the program, halting claims, arithmetic primes). The fix I would accept: (i) **required** — rewrite the `atomDay` and `Sminus` docstrings, the root and `StateSentence` module docstrings, the ledger's D3 row and the D5 rows for `E2x`/`E2i`/`E3`/`E3fin`, F-14 and report line 43 to say exactly that, with the consequence spelled out for dependents ("a satisfiability/consistency result over these predicates is at least as strong as the source's; an impossibility result must check that the sentences its argument uses lie in the prose scope"), and lift P1's `laterDayQuote`/`sminus_scope_is_packing_dependent` into the library (a `Scope` section of `StateSentence.lean` or a probe-derived module) as the record, the way round 2 lifted `productAtom_notMem_Sminus`; (ii) **optional, consolidation's call** — a definition-of-record change in the safe direction that excludes from `Sminus` every tag-`2` atom (and tag-`6` copy of one) whose code is not a *registered* day-first family, or every tag-`2` atom outright (the package's own quote atoms of day `≥ m` are already excluded, so the loss is day-`< m` quotes); either restores the under-approximation on tags `2`/`3`/`6` and the run's families but still not on `0`/`1`/`5`, so (i) is needed in any case. Do not claim "never inconsistent" again without restricting the quantifier to the families the reader actually handles.
