## Representation decisions that differ from the mandate (flagged, [STANDARDS](../../STANDARDS.md) wins)

1. **The ε-law is a signed weight function, not a `FinDist`, outside `ε ∈ [0,1]`.** The mandate's `law : Alg → Node → ℝ → FinDist Ω` cannot be differentiated two-sidedly at `0` unless it is constant or clamped, and Mathlib's `deriv` is two-sided; a clamped family makes every influence a junk value, which [STANDARDS](../../STANDARDS.md) §3 forbids as the definition of record. The natural ε-family (a mixture `(1−ε)·B + ε·A`) extends polynomially to all real `ε`; `PlaySpace.law` returns that extension, with fields `law_zero` (`= ℙ` at `0`), `law_nonneg` on `[0,1]`, `law_sum` (total mass one for every `ε`). The two-sided derivative at `0` equals Diffractor's one-sided limit.
2. **`A5` is stated for time-`n` precommitments, not only for constant algorithms.** Post 8 states Assumption 5 for "the algorithm which just selects an action from μ", but its proof of Theorem 1 applies it to `Ā_{h,n}` ("Use affineness of influence", `E_{a∼Ā_{h,n}}`), which as an algorithm reads the unplannables up to time `n`. `PlaySpace.A5 h` quantifies over algorithms whose action at `h` is constant on the time-`n` atom (`ConstOnAtom`); `A5_ofDist` recovers Post 8's literal statement. This is the exact form the proof uses and is disclosed as "Fidelity: stronger" in the docstring; see finding F4 for why the two readings differ in the model of record.
3. **Two positivity hypotheses the posts leave implicit are explicit:** `AllPosObs` (every next observation has positive probability at every positive atom; Lemma 1 divides by `ℙ_{h₁:ₙ}(o)`) and `ReachPos h` (every positive atom along `h` can still reach `h`; the Theorem 1 proof divides by `ℙ_{h₁:ₙ₊₁}(h)` at every time-`(n+1)` atom below `h_{n+1}`). Both are (a)-type regularity conditions, checked on the instances. Findings F2, F3.
4. **Post 6's Assumption 1 is derived, not assumed.** The abstract hypothesis is `SmoothLaw` (every coordinate `ε ↦ law A h ε ω` is differentiable at `0`, a theorem of the model of record where coordinates are polynomials); `A1_of_smoothLaw` proves Post 6's three-limit Assumption 1 on the support. Off the support the junk `0` at `ε = 0` can break the limit, so `A1` is stated on the support.
