## 3. Non-blocking issues

**N1 — The docstring of `Priv.theorem1_fails` claims "`privA ≠ Ā_{hT,0}` on the support"; it is false — `privA` *is* its own time-`0` average.** `privA` reads only the private root state `ξ₀`, which the time-`0` atom fixes, so its time-`0` conditional average at `hT` is `privA`'s own action on every atom. Probe `AvgPrivA.lean` proves `Priv.S.play (Priv.S.avg privA hT 0) hT ω = Priv.S.play privA hT ω` at **every** world (`play_avg_privA`, via `avgDist_w_of_pos` and `condExpJunk_const_on` on the positive averaging event `avgEvent_pos`). Consequences: Assumption 3 for `privA` at `hT` is the identity `x = x` (both clauses compare `privA` with itself), and the mechanism of the counterexample is not "`A` differs from its average" but "`A`'s on-atom action (`δ one-box`) differs from its global profile (fair)", which the module docstring and F4 state correctly. The N+ grade survives on the other two clauses of the same Fidelity line (`128` positive worlds; `IE_eq` depends on `A` through `prof`). Fix: delete the clause (Priv.lean l. 799) and say instead "`Ā_{hT,0} = privA` at `hT`, so A3 and A4 are trivial for `privA`; the failure is the A5 step alone".

**N2 — F4's headline "Post 8's Theorem 1, under the assumptions Post 8 states, is false" and the `post8_theorem1_counterexample` docstring's "the gap is real, not a matter of interpretation" need the register the house rules require.** The claim depends on how `Ā_{h,n}` is rendered. Post 6's definition (l. 115) — "the algorithm that, on any extension of `h₁:ₙ`, just plays action `a` with `ℙ_{h₁:ₙ}(A(h, S̄_h) = a | h)` probability" — admits two readings. (i) The package's: `ℙ_{h₁:ₙ}` is the belief of the *realized* epistemic state `𝕊_{h₁:ₙ}`, itself random from earlier viewpoints (Post 8's Theorem 1 feeds `f` "the actual sequence of epistemic states"), so `Ā_{h,n}` reads the unplannables up to time `n` — Post 6 §1.2's "destroy all of its ability to entangle its behavior with incoming unplannable information *past n timesteps*" (l. 142) — and a diffuse predictor sees its timeline-average, `prof Ā = prof A` (Post 7 Sub-Assumption 4, l. 281: "only depends on your average behavior when you get to the decision point, not which timeline you do it in"). Under (i), A3 is a theorem of every profile model, Post 8's literal A5 holds in `privModel`, and Theorem 1 fails: F4 as filed. (ii) The r1 adversarial audit's (N3): from the actual time-`n` state, `Ā_{h,n}` is the *constant* algorithm playing the number `μ_n`. Under (ii) the same instance violates A3's probability clause instead — probe `ConstReadingA3.lean`: `𝕀^ℙ_∅(privA, hT, full) = 0 ≠ 1/2 = 𝕀^ℙ_∅(ofDist (privA(hT, S̄)(ω₀)), hT, full)` — so it is outside Theorem 1's hypotheses rather than a counterexample. Reading (ii) is the worse-supported one: at `n = |h|` it makes A3 say the predictor responds to `A` as to the constant `A(h, S̄_h)(actual)`, i.e. knows the agent's actual mixed action — not Post 7's diffuse predictor — and it leaves Theorem 1's right-hand side ill-typed (`f^n_{h,S̄_h}` with `S̄_h` random against a constant `Ā`). I therefore agree with the r2 fidelity audit that F4's severity is right. But: (a) "X refutes P's theorem" is a bridge claim about the source and `research/CLAUDE.md`'s register rule requires **ATTRIBUTION-UNVETTED** on it (F6 carries the label; F4 does not); (b) "not a matter of interpretation" should be replaced by the one-sentence argument above (why reading (ii) is unavailable), since a reader of the docstring alone cannot see it; (c) F4 should record the relocation — under reading (ii) the instance violates A3 (`IP_privA` vs `IP_ofAct_true`) — so the author can vet which reading Diffractor intends. Presentation/register; the Lean is unaffected.
