## F2 — selection.md R1.3: "optional stopping is a special case of R1.1" is false under R1.1's hypothesis (imprecision; true in the per-`k` form)

- (i) [selection](../../sources/corrigibility/workflow-2026-09-14/followup/selection.md) R1.3 l. 31: "This is the bounded optional-stopping theorem and is a special case of R1.1 (a stopping time is a selection among the refinements $P(\cdot\mid\mathcal F_m)$ whose selection event $\{\tau=m\}$ is $\mathcal F_m$-measurable)."
- (ii) R1.1's hypothesis is a single partition `G` coarser than every candidate `ℱ_k` with `s` `G`-measurable (`retainsGrounds_of_refines`'s antecedent). A stopping time's `{τ = m}` is `ℱ_m`-measurable but `τ` is not measurable with respect to any partition coarser than every `ℱ_m` (for a two-step rule "stop at 1 iff the first flip is heads", `{τ = 1}` is not `ℱ_0`-measurable). So the literal "special case" claim fails.
- (iii) Survivor: the per-`k` retention hypothesis `RetainsGrounds f s := ∀ k w w', s w = k → f k w = f k w' → s w' = k`, which is exactly what Armstrong's A1 proof uses, implies the selection theorem (`selectFrame_eq_refineFrame`), is implied by R1.1's hypothesis (`retainsGrounds_of_refines`), and covers adapted stopping (`retainsGrounds_of_adapted`, `WitnessesC`).
- Severity: imprecision; R1.1's conclusion is unaffected.

## F16 — radical Claim I7.1 (the self-referential space exists): OPEN after an honest attempt (T13(c)); statement repaired in round 1

- **Round-0 statement was not the claim** (round-1 fidelity audit B1, adversarial 3): `∃ Ω, Nonempty (Ω ≃ₜ W × ProbabilityMeasure Ω)` is provable with `Ω := Empty` (no probability measure on an empty type; probe `audit-r1-probes/SelfRefTrivial.lean`), and true for other wrong reasons at an indiscrete or one-point `W`. Worse, the fidelity auditor's argument (not machine-checked, and adopted here as the reason the bare fixed-point form is *not* restated as open): for `Ω := W × Q` with `Q` the Hilbert cube, `ProbabilityMeasure (W × Q)` is an infinite-dimensional compact convex metrizable set, homeomorphic to `Q` by Keller's theorem, so `W × Q ≃ₜ W × ProbabilityMeasure (W × Q)` with no self-reference; the bare existence of a nonempty compact metrizable fixed point of `X ↦ W × Δ(X)` is therefore (modulo Keller) *true and empty*. What makes I7.1 a *type space* is universality.
- **Repaired statement** (`SelfRef.lean`): `SelfRefSpace W` (D) — a nonempty compact metrizable Borel `Ω` with canonical maps `state : Ω → W`, `cred : Ω → ProbabilityMeasure Ω` such that `ω ↦ (state ω, cred ω)` is a homeomorphism (`IsHomeomorph`); `SelfRefSpace.IsUniversal` (D) — the terminal-coalgebra property: every opens-measurable topological `Ω'` with a continuous coalgebra structure `Ω' → W × Δ(Ω')` factors through it by a unique continuous `h` with `state ∘ h = st'` and `cred (h ω') = (cr' ω').map h` (Mathlib's `ProbabilityMeasure.map`); the OPEN row is `universalSelfRefSpace_exists_open : ∃ X : SelfRefSpace W, X.IsUniversal` for `W` finite, discrete, nontrivial. Variant: the test coalgebras are topological with continuous structure maps, where Mertens–Zamir's belief morphisms are measurable; both directions of that difference are open here.
- What was tried (unchanged): Mathlib provides `ProbabilityMeasure Ω` with the topology of weak convergence and the continuous push-forward `ProbabilityMeasure.continuous_map`, but no universal type space; the Mertens–Zamir / Brandenburger–Dekel construction is the projective limit of finite-order hierarchies `X₀ = W`, `X_{n+1} = X_n × ProbabilityMeasure X_n` with coherence, and needs (1) compactness of `ProbabilityMeasure` of a compact metrizable space (Prokhorov — not in Mathlib in this form), (2) the Kolmogorov extension along the coherent hierarchies (Mathlib has Ionescu-Tulcea, not the projective-limit form needed), (3) the identification of the limit with `W × ProbabilityMeasure(limit)` and (4) the universality argument. Cardinality: `ProbabilityMeasure Ω` has the cardinality of the continuum as soon as `Ω` has two points, so no countable `Ω` works. Kind OPEN, credence unchanged (the cited constructions exist in the literature; the obstruction is packaging, not mathematics).
- Severity: presentation for the note (its claim is the universal space; the note's one-line statement `Ω ≅ W × Δ(Ω)^T` does not say so, and as printed admits the empty solutions above — an imprecision worth a sentence in the note).
