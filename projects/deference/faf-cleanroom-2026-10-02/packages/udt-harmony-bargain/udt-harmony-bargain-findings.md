## F1 — Theorem 11.1 is false as stated (blocking; §11)

**Quoted.** "In the bargaining game 𝒢 with a welfare selection rule, any trembling-hand equilibrium outcome is not subjectively Pareto-dominated."

**Reading formalised** (the source's own definitions; no choice among readings): `THPE` = SC Def. 10.4 verbatim (uniform floor `ε`, `ε → 0`, an *existential* over the perturbation sequence, Nash equilibria of the perturbed games converging in the weight vectors); pure profile `σ*`; outcome `outcome sel σ*`; "not dominated" = FAF's `Game.ParetoOptimalIn` with `ParetoDom` = Mathlib's `Pi.lt` (FAF's own strict Pareto improvement).

**Refutation** (`Thm111.thm111_refuted`, `Thm111.thm111_dominator`). Two players with actions `a/b` and `c/d`; `U₁ = (3, 0, 2, 0)`, `U₂ = (0, 0, 1, 3)` on `(ac, ad, bc, bd)`; `W = (3, 1/1000, 4.002, 6.003)`, injective and Pareto-consistent (`Thm111.paretoConsistent_W`, `W_injective`); `sel` the `W`-argmax (`isWelfareSel_selW`). The profile `σ* = ((a, {ac}), (d, {bd}))` is a trembling-hand equilibrium in exactly Def. 10.4's sense (`sStar_thpe`, via the proved Selten step `uniform_thpe_of_lex`): every alternative proposal of either player pays `0` against `σ*_{-i}` (`N0`, `N1`), and `σ*ᵢ` maximises the order-`ε` total against all 32 opponent proposals (`t0_max`, `t1_max`: `72` at `σ*ᵢ`). Its outcome is the batna profile `ad`, worth `(0, 0)`, strictly dominated for **both** players by `bc`, worth `(2, 1)`.

**Where the proof fails.** The proof's deviation "add `O* = bc` to one's acceptable set" ties at order `ε⁰` and loses exactly `4` at order `ε¹` (`Thm111.add_bc_loses_four`). Two mechanisms the proof omits, both of order `ε` already for `n = 2`: (a) in the proof's gain event the pre-deviation outcome is the *batna profile*, not the equilibrium outcome — when player 2 trembles to `(c, {bc})`, the no-deal outcome `ac` is worth `3 > 2 = U₁(bc)`; (b) *welfare displacement* — when player 2 trembles into accepting both `ac` and `bc`, accepting `bc` lets `sel` pick `bc` (`W = 4.002 > 3`) over `ac`. The audit's "`ε^{n−1}` vs `O(ε)` for `n ≥ 3`" suspicion aimed at the bookkeeping; the failure is at `n = 2`, where every term is of order `ε`, so no bookkeeping repair saves the statement.

**Consequences.** Thm 13.1(2) as proved ("by Theorem 11.1") and Remark 13.2's justification rest on it. What survives: the neighbour of F2 and Proposition S (F6, `PropS.harmonious_subset_optimal`), which proves Thm 13.1(2) in corrected form by a route that avoids 11.1.

**Surviving neighbour.** F2.

## F2 — Remark 11.2 and the surviving neighbour: the mandate's expectation is reversed (local error in the mandate / harmony ledger; §11 Remark 11.2 vindicated for the neighbour)

**What survives of Theorem 11.1** (`Neighbour.no_all_but_one_improvement`, `paretoDom_not_accepted_by_others`): for *any* singleton-faithful selector (`sel {O} = O`; every welfare rule is one) and any pure Nash profile `σ*` of the bargaining game — a fortiori any pure harmonious profile — an outcome `O*` accepted by every player other than `j` is worth no more to `j` than the realised outcome; hence no outcome that a player strictly prefers is accepted by all the others, in particular no all-but-one-accepted Pareto improvement is left on the table. The deviation is `(bⱼ, {O*})`, which realises exactly `O*`.

**Reversal.** The mandate (T5, after udt-rep-072) expected the neighbour to use Pareto-consistency of `W` and asked for a witness — a singleton-faithful non-welfare `sel` with a pure Nash profile leaving an all-but-one-accepted improvement on the table — to be recorded as "welfare-consistency is load-bearing, contrary to Remark 11.2". No such witness exists: `Neighbour.mandate_form_vacuous` proves that under any singleton-faithful `sel` the configuration is impossible. Remark 11.2's "the proof only uses that `sel({O*}) = O*`" is *correct for the neighbour*; it is moot for Theorem 11.1 itself, which is false. The mandate's suggested form of the neighbour ("if `U_j(O*) > U_j(outcome)` then a deal exists and `W O* ≤ W(outcome)`") has an unsatisfiable antecedent under welfare rules and is not shipped as a headline.

## F3 — Def. 10.3's normalised welfare divides by zero (imprecision; §10.3)

The suggested `W = ∑ᵢ Ũᵢ` with `Ũᵢ` normalised to `[0,1]` divides by `max Uᵢ − min Uᵢ`, which is `0` for a constant `Uᵢ` (e.g. a dummy player, or the constant game of `PropSWitness.constant_game_all_harmonious`). The unnormalised sum `wSum` needs no normalisation and is Pareto-consistent (`Defs.wSum_paretoConsistent`; no `Nonempty N` needed — a dominated pair supplies a player); `argmaxSel` + `wSum` gives the existence claim (`exists_welfareSel`). The tie-break "arbitrarily but consistently" is made a parameter `sel`; nothing here depends on how ties are broken except T7(c), which needs `W` injective on the optima (F6).

## F4 — Def. 10.4 presupposes a pure limit; the reading matters (imprecision; §10.4)

"The equilibrium outcome" of a trembling-hand equilibrium presupposes a pure limit. The limit may be mixed (matching pennies has no pure Nash equilibrium, hence no pure THPE), so every headline here says at which grade it is stated: pure (`HarmoniousPure`) or support form (every pure profile of positive `profWeight` under the limit). SC's uniform-`ε` floor is a special case of Selten's `η`-vectors (`THPE.selten`); the refutation targets SC's definition, and nothing is refuted against the Selten form. The strictly-perfect reading ("for every perturbation sequence") kills the F1 example but costs existence (Okada 1981 as reconstructed by the harmony ledger; not read) — recorded, not formalised.

## F5 — `Nonempty N` is a genuine hypothesis (imprecision; §9.2, §13.1)

With an empty player set nothing is ever subjectively Pareto-dominated (`Defs.not_paretoDom_of_isEmpty`), so every outcome is "Pareto-optimal" and Thm 13.1(1)'s "dominates iff `V O < V O'`" fails. `paretoDom_commonGame_iff` carries `[Nonempty N]`; so does Proposition S (through `nonopt_lt_opt_W`).

## F6 — Thm 13.1(2) and Remark 13.2: what is true, and at which grade (local error / imprecision; §13)

- **"The unique outcome surviving Theorem 11.1 is `argmax`"** — with 11.1 false this needs a new proof: Proposition S(a) (`PropS.harmonious_subset_optimal`): in every common-payoff game, under every welfare rule, every support outcome of every trembling-hand equilibrium is `V`-optimal. Uniqueness fails literally: with ties in `W` on the optima, or with a **safe batna** (a player whose batna coordinate forces an optimal outcome), other optima can arise; the corrected statement (`PropS.unique_without_safe_batna`, `outcome_eq_of_injOn`) is: with no safe batna and `W` injective on the optima, every support profile of every THPE accepts every optimum, and the outcome is the `W`-maximal optimum. With one player "no safe batna" is contradictory (the batna profile is the only outcome with that coordinate), so (c) carries `[Nontrivial N]`.
- **"`O*` is itself harmonious"** is asserted with no argument. It is delivered in the support form only (`PropSWitness.exists_harmonious_optimum`: some optimum is the outcome of a support profile of some THPE, from Kakutani existence `Existence.exists_thpe`). No pure THPE is claimed.
- **Remark 13.2's "unique strategy profile ensuring a harmonious outcome"** is ill-posed: a profile is not a choice of priors. The defensible form is Theorem H′(b′) (`PropSWitness.homogeneousGame_harmonious_isOpt`, `exists_isOpt_treeValue`): the homogeneous translation *is* the common-prior collection (`Translation.U_homogeneous_eq_value`, `homogeneousGame_eq_commonGame`), its harmonious outcomes are `V_B`-optimal, and a `V_B`-optimal pure profile exists — at the pure / shared-seed grade.
- **Remark 13.2's mechanism "updating introduces disagreement"** has no instance in the witnesses: on TN-V2 both updated points *agree* that `(both, both)` is best (`TreeWitnesses.tn_bb_paretoOptimal`: `U_F = (4,4,5,5)`, `U_E = (0,1,0,1)`), it is harmonious in the updated game (`tnSigma_harmonious`), prior-suboptimal (`tn_bb_suboptimal`, `2 < 3`) and not harmonious in the homogeneous game (`tn_bb_not_harmonious_homogeneous`). The mechanism is event-conditioning (the updated point discards the term by which its policy moves the probability of its own observation; HA-12′(d)), not disagreement.
