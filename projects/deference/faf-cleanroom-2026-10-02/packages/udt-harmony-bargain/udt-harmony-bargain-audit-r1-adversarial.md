## 3. Non-blocking issues

1. **`TreeWitnesses.tnSigma_harmonious`, `tnSigma_outcome`: the Lean hypothesis is stronger than the ledger and docstring say.** Both take `hsel : ∀ S, S.Nonempty → sel S ∈ S` (membership-faithful), while the docstring, the report and ledger row 35 say "for every singleton-faithful `sel`". The stated claim is true — `d_F`'s and `d_E`'s acceptable sets are singletons, so `sel` is only ever consulted on `{bb}` — but the theorem as written is the weaker one. Probe: `AuditR1Adv.outcome_coord_of_singleton'` proves the helper from `SingletonFaithful sel` alone, and `tn_ll_not_bargainNash` (below) uses it. Fix: replace the hypothesis by `SingletonFaithful sel` (the proof of `outcome_coord_of_singleton` goes through after `Finset.subset_singleton_iff`). The fidelity audit flagged the same line independently.
2. **Findings F6 asserts a claim the package does not kernel-check.** "Uniqueness fails literally: with ties in `W` on the optima, or with a **safe batna** …, other optima can arise" — the safe-batna half is T7(d), recorded as not started, yet the findings file's header says every claim is kernel-checked unless marked, and this one is not marked. Probe `AuditR1Adv.FR12.two_harmonious_optima_welfare` now checks it: in the common-payoff game `V(in,y) = −1`, else `0` (row `out` and column `x` are safe batnas, `FR12.safe_row`, `safe_col`), the pure profiles `σ₁ = ((out,{(out,y)}), (x,{(out,y)}))` and `σ₂ = ((out,{(in,x)}), (x,{(in,x)}))` are both `HarmoniousPure (commonGame V) (argmaxSel W)` for the welfare rule `W := V + ½·[O = (out,y)]` (Pareto-consistent, `W (out,y) ≠ W (in,x)`, so injective on the optima), with outcomes `(out,y) ≠ (in,x)`, both optimal. So Thm 13.1(2)'s "unique outcome" fails with a safe batna even under an injective-on-optima welfare rule, and the `hns : ∀ j b, ¬ Safe V j b` hypothesis of `unique_without_safe_batna` / `outcome_eq_of_injOn` is load-bearing. Fix: cite the probe (or port it as T7(d)'s first half) in F6, or mark the sentence "not kernel-checked here".
3. **Proposition S(a)'s N+ witness does not show the THPE hypothesis is doing work.** Ledger row 29 cites the Stag Hunt, but `stag_outcome_eq_SS` and `HH_not_harmonious` are *consequences* of the theorem; `HH_nash_actionGame` is about the *action* game, not the bargaining game. Nothing in the package rules out that the theorem holds for the trivial reason "every pure Nash outcome of the bargaining game is optimal". Probe `AuditR1Adv.stagHunt_HH_bargainNash`: `((H,∅),(H,∅))` is `BargainNash (commonGame StagHunt.V) sel` for **every** selector, with outcome `(H,H)`, which is not optimal. So Nash ⇏ optimal in the bargaining game and the trembling-hand refinement is exactly what the theorem uses. Suggest adding this two-line lemma to `PropSWitness` and citing it in the witness column: it is what makes the N+ grade meaningful.
4. **T8(d) proves the weaker half of "updating breaks harmony".** The package shows `(both,both)` *is* harmonious in the updated game and *is not* in the homogeneous one; it does not show that the prior optimum `(large,large)` is *not* harmonious in the updated game (it could, for all the package says, merely have gained a second harmonious outcome). Probe `AuditR1Adv.tn_ll_not_bargainNash`: for every singleton-faithful `sel`, no profile with outcome `(large,large)` is even a Nash equilibrium of `bargain tnUpd sel` (`d_F` grabs `5 > 4` with `(both,{bb})`). So updating *replaces* the harmonious set rather than enlarging it. Suggest adding it to `TreeWitnesses` beside `tnSigma_harmonious`.
5. **`Mugging.veto` would also be true if no profile were Nash.** The ledger row grades it P/N+ but the package exhibits no Nash profile of `mugGame`'s bargaining game. Probe `AuditR1Adv.mug_refuse_bargainNash`: `((refuse,∅),(refuse,∅))` is `BargainNash mugGame sel` for every selector. Suggest adding it as the N+ half of the row.
6. **`Neighbour.no_all_but_one_improvement` is graded P.** Its body is one instantiation of the Nash inequality at the deviation `(bⱼ,{O*})` plus the computation `inter_update_singleton` + `sel {O*} = O*`. That is composition of two cited facts, not "real content" in STANDARDS §6's sense; C (or L) is the honest grade. The statement itself is exactly what the docstring says (checked; and `mandate_form_vacuous` is correctly N−: I confirmed by hand that the mandate's antecedent is contradictory at any Nash profile under any singleton-faithful `sel`, since the singleton deviation realises `O*` outright).
7. **`Mugging.slogan_refuted` is at the pure grade only** (third conjunct via `veto_harmoniousPure`), although `veto_support` exists; adding the support-form conjunct would put the refutation row at the same grade as the rest of the package.
8. **Ledger witness columns name objects the witnesses do not instantiate.** Row 31 (`U_homogeneous_eq_value`) lists "TN-V2", but TN-V2 never uses `homogeneousPoint`/`treeValue`: `tnHom := commonGame tnV` with `tnV O := value (Proc.ofFun O) tnB` directly, tied to the translation only by `tnV_eq_sum_chanceLaw` (which is `sum_chanceLaw_payoff` restated). Accurate as "the same identity, stated concretely over `TnPt`"; the columns should say that. The report's "presentational identification" sentence covers the player set but not this.
9. **Ledger row 9 marks `THPESelten` Fidelity `exact`** while its docstring says Selten 1975 was "not read". Nothing rests on it (it is a D with one L consequence), but `exact` is not a claim the package can make about an unread source; `variant: recalled, not read` or `n/a` is honest.
10. **A research-grade question, not a defect.** In `harmonious_subset_optimal`, Pareto-consistency with common utility makes `W` strictly increasing in `V` (`nonopt_lt_opt_W`), so every welfare `sel` is a `V`-argmax on every set.
