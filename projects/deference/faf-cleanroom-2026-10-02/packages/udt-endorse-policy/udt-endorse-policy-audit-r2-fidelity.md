## 3. Non-blocking issues

**N1. The T3(d) plan-rule-3 "(i) refuted sentence" clauses name the wrong sentence for `reflective_not_endorsed_witness`, and F-4's wording compounds it.** The docstring and the ledger's Source/Claim columns of `reflective_not_endorsed_witness` say the refuted sentence is "the inventory's converse 'endorsement ⟹ the future belief is a conditional of the present one' (udt-rep-2-014) read as 'reflective ⟹ endorsed'". But those are different statements with opposite directions, and `as38` refutes only the second (`as38.Reflective ∧ ¬ BeliefEndorsedAll unif4 as38`: reflection does not force endorsement). The inventory's converse — endorsement ⟹ the future belief is `P₁(· ∣ 𝒢)` — is refuted by the *other* witness, `endorsed_not_calibrated_witness`: `as3` is belief-endorsed on every event, yet its kernels `κ₀ = P(· ∣ {0,2})`, `κ₁ = P(· ∣ {0,1})`, `κ₂ = P(· ∣ {1,2})` are conditionals on *overlapping* events, so they are not `P₁(· ∣ 𝒢)` for any partition `𝒢` (in SC terms: not calibrated), which is exactly the inventory's "any `Q` with `E[1_X ∣ Q] = Q` is endorsed, and such `Q` need not be `P₁(X ∣ 𝒢)`". F-4's sentence "The inventory's converse wording … is false in the stronger 'reflective ⟹ endorsed' form" is therefore mis-worded: "reflective ⟹ endorsed" is not a stronger form of the inventory's converse, it is a different claim; the inventory's converse is false in its own form, by `as3`. Statuses (`refuted (converse of T3(c))`, `refuted (converse of T3(b))`) and the Lean are right; only the attributions are crossed. Fix: in `reflective_not_endorsed_witness`'s (i) say "the converse of T3(c), 'reflective ⟹ belief-endorsed on every event' (SC Def 3.7 vs `BeliefEndorsedAll`; not a sentence any source asserts — the mandate's T3(d) question)"; in `endorsed_not_calibrated_witness`'s (i) add "this is also the refutation of udt-rep-2-014's converse 'endorsement ⟹ the future belief is a conditional of the present one'"; reword F-4 accordingly.

**N3. F-6(3) "'UDT is control-endorsed by its own prior' is … false for the pointwise rule" does not say which endorsement, and the corpus has two.** [agency-via-endorsement](../../sources/udt-representation-theorem/agency-via-endorsement.md) states both a policy-level control endorsement (§Formal Expansion / §Endorsement with Explicit Boundary Structure: "every policy in the support is optimal") and a *pointwise* one (§Pointwise Control Endorsement: `argmax_a E[U_ctrl(Ξ, b, a) ∣ B = b] = π(b)`), and its §The UDT Connection defines `U_ctrl(ω, a) := U(ω)` "when the action at observation `o` is `a`", which makes the pointwise UDT rule *equal* to pointwise control endorsement by construction — under that reading the Claim is definitional, not false.
