## 3. Non-blocking issues

**N1. (F-17; report §T4 and §Repair round 1; ledger rows `policyEndorsed_of_udtRule_of_separable`, `udtRule_iff_policyEndorsed_of_separable`, `CBD4.separable_dd_witness`) "True on the regime its own theorem is stated in" is one unproved step past the Lean, and the step needs a stronger independence than `AbstractDS` provides.** The Lean proves the equivalence on the *separable-policy-utility* regime (`hsep : E[U | Π̈ = π] = ∑_ö X(ö, π ö)`). The link "decision-determined with the input independent of the policy ⟹ `hsep`" is prose, and the independence it needs is *probabilistic* — `P(ö | Π̈ = π) = P(ö)` — which is strictly stronger than the support factorization `IB_fac` (`Ö ⊥ D_{I,B}` in support) that every `AbstractDS` already carries. Under DD clause (1) alone, with `Ö = D_E` and `Ä = Π̈(Ö)` (`polE_apply_oE`), the policy utility is `∑_ö P(ö | π) · u(π ö, ö)`, which has the form `∑_ö X(ö, π ö)` exactly when `P(ö | π)` does not depend on `π`. `CBD4` has uniform weights and is inside; a decision-determined structure with policy-dependent room weights is not covered by anything in the package. Whether the pointwise rule forces policy endorsement on *every* external-only decision-determined structure is therefore not settled here. I looked for a counterexample by hand in the three-policy family `{(0,1), (1,0), (1,1)}` with `U = [Ä = 1]` and arbitrary positive weights: writing `p = E[U | Π̈ = (0,1)]`, `q = E[U | Π̈ = (1,0)]`, the two tie equations the rule forces reduce to `C(p − q) = D(1 − p)` and `B(q − p) = D(1 − q)` (`B, C, D` the policy masses), which force `p > q` and `q > p`; so a counterexample, if one exists, needs the fourth policy or a richer `U`, and "open" is the honest status. Fix I would accept: in F-17 and report §T4 replace "true on the regime its own theorem is stated in" by "true on the separable regime, which decision-determination gives when the input is *probabilistically* independent of the policy (`CBD4`)", and either add the general decision-determined case to `udt-endorse-policy-open.txt` as a question (it has a precise statement: `DecisionDetermined ∧ hcons ∧ UdtRule → PolicyEndorsed`, with `sorry`) or say in F-17 that it is not settled.

**N2.
```lean
theorem bayesUpdateful_controlEndorsed (hw : ∀ ω, 0 ≤ w ω) (h : BayesUpdateful w U O Cupd) :
    ControlEndorses w U (fun ω => Cupd (O ω))
```

(a choice measurable in `O` is conditionally endorsed given `O` on each positive cell, then `controlEndorses_of_condControlEndorses`). The package formalized the lab's kernel rendering, as the mandate directed, and F-8 is careful to say "under the kernel reading"; but F-8 does not say that 2-016's own rendering was not formalized and is answered trivially, so a reader can take the ill-posedness verdict as a verdict on 2-016's reading. Fix: one sentence in F-8 — the inventory's sentence is ill-posed for two independent reasons: its stated type cannot carry the dependence it invokes (so the "false in general" half is "false always"), and once a policy-dependent utility is used, the base of the decoupled kernel decides the question.
