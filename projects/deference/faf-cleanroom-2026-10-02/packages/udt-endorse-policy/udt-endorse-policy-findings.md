## F-1. M&A's definitions need a positivity guard and a tie convention (presentation)

**Pointer.** M&A §Endorsement ("`P₁(X | "P₂(X) = p") = p`"), §Selection/Control Endorsement ("`C¹(A | "C²(A) = a") = a`"), §Conditional Endorsement ("`argmax … = y`"); udt-rep-2-012.

**What.** Conditioning on `"P₂(X) = p"` is undefined at null values, and read over all `p : ℝ` the definition is false for every report on a finite frame (uncountably many null values). `argmax` is set-valued; "`= a`" must be read "`a ∈ argmax`".

**Fix of record.** `Defs.lean`: every predicate quantifies over report values of positive mass and is written multiplicatively (`mass w (X ∩ {Q = p}) = p · mass w {Q = p}`), vacuous at null values; the division forms are lemmas (`beliefEndorses_iff_condProb` etc.), never definitions; `IsArgmax` is `udt-policy-calc`'s predicate. The tie reading is ATTRIBUTION-UNVETTED (the post does not say). The `A = ℝ` expectation reading of udt-rep-2-013 is outside the finite-action setting; T2(b) uses an injective finite grid `g : A ↪ ℝ` instead (F-2).

## F-2. "Control endorsement generalizes expectation endorsement" is exact over `ℝ`; a finite-action encoding needs the conditional means on the grid (presentation; re-filed in repair round 1)

**Pointer.** M&A §Control Endorsement, the sentence "Control endorsement generalizes selection endorsement (since U can ignore its first argument), expectation endorsement (since U can be some loss function), and belief endorsement (when that loss function is a proper scoring rule)"; udt-rep-2-013.

**What holds.** Selection ⟺ world-blind control (`control_of_selection`, T). Expectation endorsement of a grid report ⟹ Brier control endorsement (`brier_control_of_expectation`, P). Belief endorsement ⟺ expectation endorsement of the indicator (`beliefEndorses_iff_expectation_ind`, T).

**What holds, continued (repair round 1, audit r1 B1).** On the post's natural reading — `A = ℝ`, `U(ω, x) = −(V(ω) − x)²`, udt-rep-2-013's first — the sentence is an *exact equivalence with no hypothesis*: `brier_real_iff` (P), both directions being the grid theorems at `g = id`, the converse's grid hypothesis discharged by the conditional mean itself. M&A has no imprecision here; the earlier version of this finding filed one against it, which was wrong.

**What the grid witness shows.** A *finite-action* encoding of "control endorsement with a loss function" must put the conditional means on the grid: the converse "Brier control endorsement on a grid ⟹ expectation endorsement" needs `hmean` (`expectation_of_brier_control_of_grid`), and without it `brier_grid_refuted` (N+): uniform `Fin 4`, `V = 1_{\{0,2\}}`, grid `{0,1}`, report `C = (1,1,0,0)` — both classes have `P(X | C = a) = 1/2`, the two grid points tie under the Brier loss (control-endorsed on the grid), but `E[V | g ∘ C = 1] = 1/2 ≠ 1`. That tie is the grid's: the same report with the argmax over `ℝ` is not control-endorsed at all (`grid_refutation_is_artifact`), because `1/2` is then an available action. The mandate's T2(b) wording "exact only on a grid containing the conditional means" was the encoding artifact STANDARDS §3 warns about; STANDARDS wins. Also: the translation is proper in both directions for selection versus control (`knowsMore_control_not_selection`, `goodOnAverage_selection_not_control`) and for belief versus expectation (`beliefEndorsedAll_not_expectationEndorsedAll_witness`).

## F-5. Conditional endorsement is not transitive, but its strict part has a strict-partial-order closure (the extension of record; answers M&A Q1 in the belief form)

**Pointer.** M&A §Questions & Conjectures 1: "How well can we use conditional endorsement to characterize optimization power (or more generally, level of endorsement)? Is it transitive?"; udt-rep-2-015; trust-lab-070; scout Q4.

**Answer.** Not transitive: `condEndorse_not_transitive` (the eight-point frame, three non-constant reports, every positive pair checked; the mandate's hand computation confirmed), and `prec_not_transitive`: "strictly more trusted" is not a strict partial order. Acyclic: `condEndorse_acyclic` (three-cycles collapse to equality on the support). Surviving order: the transitive closure of strict trust is irreflexive and transitive (`strictOrder_of_closure`), with no calibration hypothesis — every index on a cycle is calibrated because it is the first index of some step. The engine is the resolution identity `E[V i²] − E[V j²] = E[(V i − V j)²]` under `CondR i j` and a calibrated `V j` (`m2_sub_eq`).

**Caveat (imprecision in the mandate's (d)).** "`R i j` implies `E[V i²] ≥ E[V j²]`" needs `V j` calibrated: with `V j ≡ c` uncalibrated and `V i = 1_X`, `CondR i j` holds while `E[V j²] = c²` is unconstrained. The theorem carries the hypothesis (`m2_le_of_condR`); the closure result does not need it.

**Not settled.** "Optimization power" — Q1 asks for a characterization; what is proved is the order-theoretic shape of the belief-form relation on finite frames. The control form (T6(e)) is not attempted: with ties, a constant `U` endorses everything (N−), so only the strict-argmax form is a real question; left in prose (report §Stretch and open).

## F-6. [agency-via-endorsement](../../sources/udt-representation-theorem/agency-via-endorsement.md)'s condensation gloss: an invalid step, a different `Π`, and a claim that is definitional at policy level and false pointwise (local error + imprecision)

**Pointer.** [agency-via-endorsement](../../sources/udt-representation-theorem/agency-via-endorsement.md) §Control Endorsement ("Since Π is independent of (B, Ξ) in support, conditioning on Π = π doesn't change the distribution of (B, Ξ)"), §The UDT Connection ("Claim: An agent following UDT is control-endorsed by its own prior"), §Summary ("every policy in the support is optimal"); udt-rep-050/051; [cleanup-audit-2026-08-05](../../sources/udt-representation-theorem/cleanup-audit-2026-08-05.md) item 1. ATTRIBUTION-UNVETTED: the condensation gloss is the AI's, not the author's (M&A itself has no condensation model).

**What.** (1) "Independent in support ⟹ conditioning leaves the distribution unchanged" is invalid as written (udt-rep-050's flag); `Structure.lean` does not reproduce it — `PolicyEndorsed` is stated directly over `E[U | Π̈ = π]` on realized policies. (2) The gloss's `Π` (residual of `A` given `E`) is not `udt-comm-trust`'s `Π̈`; the package uses `Π̈`. (3) "UDT is control-endorsed by its own prior" is definitional for the policy-level rule (`policyEndorsed_iff_isOptimal_on_range`, T) and false for the pointwise rule: `CB8.udtRule_not_policyEndorsed_witness` (Coordinated Buttons with prior weights `3,3,3,1` on the four policies: every score ties at `1/4`, so the pointwise rule holds, while the realized policies have utilities `1/2` and `1`). Plan rule 3: (i) the Claim sentence; (ii) `UdtRule` (pointwise, `E[U | Π*(ȯ,ö) = a]`) versus `PolicyEndorsed`; (iii) survivors: the policy-level restatement and `udtRule_of_policyEndorsed`.

**New (a finding the mandate did not anticipate).** With an external-only chosen policy (`Π* = (ȧ₀, Π̈)`), policy endorsement **does** imply the pointwise rule (`udtRule_of_policyEndorsed`, P): each realized action's score is the common maximal policy utility, and unrealized actions score the junk `−1`. So the mandate's "neither implies the other" is true on `AbstractDS` as given only because the pointwise rule sees the internal action: `IA4.policyEndorsed_not_udtRule_witness` fails the rule through `Ȧ`, which `policyUtility` (an external-policy quantity) does not see. The comparison is one-way on external-only structures — and, as F-17 adds, two-way once the policy utility is separable across inputs. Both implications run through ties: the proof of `udtRule_of_policyEndorsed` makes every realized action score the common `c`, and with two realized actions at one input the pointwise rule can only hold by a tie (`score_eq_of_udtRule`), so `CB8`'s universal `1/4` is the generic shape, not an artifact of its weights (audit r1 N4/N9). Scope caveat (audit r1 fidelity N1): `CB8`, `IA4` and `CB8X` are all **not** `DecisionDetermined` (`*.not_decisionDetermined`: two worlds share `E = (Ä, D_E)` and differ in `U`), so the pointwise refutation of the Claim sits outside the regime in which the corpus's UDT theorem is stated; inside it, F-17. The "UDT1.1 reading" of item (3) oversold: `policyEndorsed_iff_isOptimal_on_range` restates `PolicyEndorsed` as optimality among *realized* policies, and no all-policies rule is formalized (`AbstractDS` has no utility for an unrealized policy; audit r1 adversarial N3).

## F-8. udt-rep-2-016's precise reading of "updatelessness boosts endorsed agency" is ill-posed under the kernel reading — the decoupled kernel's base decides it; the post's sentence is a heuristic (imprecision, in the inventory; re-filed in repair round 1)

**Pointer.** M&A §Updatelessness ("Bob being updateless will boost his agency and any updateful behavior from Bob will be to the detriment of Bob's agency"); `udt-rep-2-inventory` udt-rep-2-016: "'updateful ⟹ not endorsed' is false in general and true exactly under cross-situation dependence" — the surveyor's reading, ATTRIBUTION-UNVETTED.

**What.** "Updateless ⟹ endorsed" is definitional (`IsOptimal` of the chosen policy; `PolicyEndorses` is that predicate). "Updateful ⟹ not endorsed", under the lab's rendering (an EDT assembly through a decoupled kernel `κ` with base `base`), is false under separability for every kernel (`edt_decoupled_globally_optimal`). Off separability it is **not a property of `U`** (audit r1 B2): an EDT assembly through the kernel decoupled at the policy itself is, definitionally, a local optimum (`edtAssembly_selfKernel_iff`), so every optimum of every `U` is an endorsed updateful assembly (`edtAssembly_selfKernel_of_optimal`) and every non-global local optimum of every `U` is an unendorsed one. The package's two coupled utilities each show both behaviours: the mugging fails at base `(0,0)` (`mugging_edt_misses`, `κsev = selfKernel (0,0)`) and succeeds at base `(pay,pay)` (`mugging_pay_base`); Coordinated Buttons succeeds at base `(1,1)` (`coordButtons_assembly_optimal`, now graded N− for exactly this reason) and fails at base `(0,0)` (`coordButtons_refuse_base`). The earlier version of this finding drew a contrast between two utilities that is really a contrast between two bases. Two quantifier readings of the inventory sentence, both answered: universal over kernels ("every updateful assembly is unendorsed"), it is false on every `U` that has an optimum, coupled or not; existential ("some updateful assembly is unendorsed"), it holds on every `U` with a non-global local optimum — and, with set-valued argmax, on some coupled `U` by ties alone (e.g. `tbl 0 1 1 1` at base `(1,1)`: every assembly is an argmax, `(0,0)` scores `0`), so whether it coincides with cross-situation dependence also depends on the tie convention. Either way "exactly under cross-situation dependence" is not a well-posed claim until the base (and the tie convention) is fixed; what is proved is the one direction and the base-dependence. The post's own sentence is a heuristic and is neither confirmed nor refuted beyond these facts.

## F-17. On external-only structures with a separable policy utility, the pointwise UDT rule and policy endorsement coincide; `CB8`'s escape is non-separability, outside decision-determination (result; repair round 1)

**Pointer.** [agency-via-endorsement](../../sources/udt-representation-theorem/agency-via-endorsement.md) §The UDT Connection ("Claim: An agent following UDT is control-endorsed by its own prior"); `udt-comm-trust`'s `DecisionDetermined` and the audit r1 fidelity N1 question "on external-only DD structures, does the pointwise rule force equal policy utilities across realized policies?".

**What.** Yes, whenever the policy utility is additively separable across inputs. `policyEndorsed_of_udtRule_of_separable` (P): if `Π* = (ȧ₀, Π̈)` and `E[U | Π̈ = π] = ∑_ö X(ö, π ö)` for some table `X`, then `UdtRule` implies `PolicyEndorsed`. The rule's forced ties (`score_eq_of_udtRule`) make the conditional mean of `f := E[U | Π̈ = Π̈(·)]` given `Π̈(·)(ö) = ä` the same for every realized `ä` at every `ö`; the covariance identity `Var(f) = ∑_ö Cov(f, X(ö, Π̈(·)(ö)))` then forces `f` constant on the support (`eq_on_support_of_condMean_const`, the finite form: `∑ w (f − c)² = ∑_ö ∑ w · X_ö · (f − c)`, each coordinate's sum vanishing class by class). With `udtRule_of_policyEndorsed` (F-6) this is the equivalence `udtRule_iff_policyEndorsed_of_separable` (C). Separability of the policy utility is exactly what decision-determination supplies in the Coordinated-Buttons pattern — `U = u(Ä, D_E)` with `Ä = Π̈(D_E)` and `D_E` independent of `Π̈` gives `E[U | Π̈ = π] = ∑_ö P(ö) u(π ö, ö)` — and exactly what `CB8` lacks: its policy utility is the Coordinated-Buttons table itself (`(0,0) ↦ 1/2`, `(1,1) ↦ 1`, mismatches `↦ 0`), non-separable, which is how its universal ties coexist with unequal policy utilities. So the corpus's Claim, read pointwise, is false in general (`CB8`) but **true on the regime its own theorem is stated in**, provided "following UDT" there means the external-only pointwise rule and the utility is decision-determined with the input independent of the policy. The "DD ⟹ separable" link is prose (not Lean), but the regime is inhabited: `CBD4.separable_dd_witness` (N+) is decision-determined (`CBD4.decisionDetermined`, clause (2) by the count identity), external-only, with the two anti-coordinated policies realized, `U = [Ä = 1]` and the action-dependent table `X(ö, ä) = ½·[ä = 1]` — the rule holds by the forced tie and policy endorsement follows. The restricted support is forced: `CB8X` inhabits the package on full support only with the inert table `X ≡ 1/4` (with every policy realized, a separable policy utility constant on the support has an action-inert table). Severity: a result, not an error in the source; it sharpens F-6's item (3).
