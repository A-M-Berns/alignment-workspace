## The representation of record

Finite `Ω` with a weight `w : Ω → ℝ` (theorems take `hw : ∀ ω, 0 ≤ w ω`; witnesses use explicit vectors, `udt-policy-calc`'s `FinDist`/`mass`, or `udt-comm-trust`'s `IntWeights`). Events are `Finset Ω`. **Reports are random variables on `Ω`** — a belief report `Q : Ω → ℝ`, a choice `C : Ω → A` — and a second agent is never a separate probability space (the post's quotation marks; module docstring of `Defs.lean`, fidelity `exact`). **Every endorsement predicate quantifies over report values of positive mass and is division-free**: e.g.

`BeliefEndorses w X Q := ∀ p, 0 < mass w {Q = p} → mass w (X ∩ {Q = p}) = p * mass w {Q = p}`,

vacuous at null values. The division forms (`P(X | Q = p) = p` with `condProbJunk`, `E[V | Q = x] = x` with `condExpJunk`, argmax of `E[U(·,a') | C = a]`) are lemmas. `argmax` is set-valued (`IsArgmax`); where uniqueness is needed `IsStrictArgmax` is used explicitly. Two workhorses make finite witnesses cheap: the **point forms** (`beliefEndorses_iff_forall_pt` etc.: quantify over positive-weight points instead of report values) and the **fibre lemma** `wsum_eq_of_fibres` (class-wise agreement of weighted sums lifts to the whole event, null fibres contributing nothing) — the latter is the engine of T2(d)(iii), T3 and T6.

**T3's `PMF`/real bridge** (mandate option (i)): `pw P ω := (P ω).toReal`, `futureBelief as X ω := (κ_{a ω}(X)).toReal`, `futureExp as V ω := ∑ x, (κ_{a ω} x).toReal * V x`, and the finite definition of record is applied to these. No second belief endorsement is defined over `PMF`; the bridge lemmas are `toReal_mass_finset` (`udt-supercondition`'s `ℝ≥0∞` mass of a finite event is the real sum), `pw_cls_eq_atomMass` and `calibrated_pointwise_real` (SC-internal calibration in real form). Witnesses are built with `pmfOfReal`, so the same rational vectors serve both sides (`pw_pmfOfReal`, `toReal_mass_pmfOfReal`).

**Decision problems** (T5, T7–T10) are `udt-policy-calc`'s (`Policy S A`, `IsOptimal`, `Separable`, `IsArgmax`, `coordButtons`, `tbl`, `forall_policy2`); the lab's weighted `Separable U p u` is `SeparableW` with `separable_iff_weighted` (plan §0.4 rule 10). Self-prediction kernels `κ : S → A → Policy S A`, `vNode`, `Decoupled`, `EdtAssembly` are new definitions of record. Decision structures (T4) are `udt-comm-trust`'s `AbstractDS` (import `Determination` + `Count` only; no FFS, no 64-world files).

**Enumerations.** `decide` only on `Fin n`, `n ≤ 8` (the eight-point frame; the `Fin 2 × (Fin 2 × Fin 2)` structure `CB8` for its factorization fields); everything numeric is `simp +decide only [...]` then `norm_num` per cell.

## What the load-bearing five establish

1. **T3.** Over `udt-supercondition`'s anticipation structures, `Calibrated ⟹ BeliefEndorsedAll ⟹ Reflective`, both steps strict, with the Bayes-updating future self endorsed on every event. The Reflection Principle is, in the finite same-ontology case, the descriptive theorem "a future belief that is a conditional of the present one is endorsed", and endorsement sits strictly between SC's calibration and SC's reflection.
2. **T6.** Conditional endorsement (belief form) is not transitive and does not make "strictly more trusted" a strict partial order, but it is acyclic and the transitive closure of strict trust is a strict partial order — with the second moment as the potential. This answers M&A Q1's "Is it transitive?" (no) and its "can we (partially) order random variables" (yes, by the closure), and says nothing about optimization power.
3. **T7.** The EDT node value through a decoupled kernel collapses to the local reward plus an `a`-constant, so in the separable regime every EDT assembly through every decoupled kernel is optimal; the coupled mugging misses the optimum by the severed acausal payoff, and the severance is the kernel's. Outside separability the answer is base-dependent, not utility-dependent: an assembly through one's own decoupled kernel is a local optimum, so every coupled `U` with a non-global local optimum has both an endorsed and an unendorsed updateful assembly (T5, repair round 1).
4. **T9.** Decision-flatness of a refinement is equivalent to "every cellwise assembly is optimal for every utility respecting the coupling graph", in both directions, with the mugging on a crossing edge as the constructive converse.
5. **T2 + T8.** "Control endorsement generalizes expectation endorsement" is exact over `ℝ` with the Brier loss (`brier_real_iff`); a finite-action encoding needs the conditional means on the grid, and the grid counterexample is the grid's artifact. "UDT defers to `u` iff `P` policy-endorses `u`" is definitional under the all-policies menu (kind S) and false under the constant-policy menu.
