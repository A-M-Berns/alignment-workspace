## F-1 — Faith scope vs Markov structure (Known issue 1) — **blocking for the full-scope bundle**

**Claim in the sources / dependencies.** Appendix B constraint 2 (`main.tex:432`) quantifies over "`φ`" without restriction; `bli-found`'s `E2x`/`E3`/`E3fin` quantify `φ` over `Sminus m m` (the day-`m` small sentences whose atoms quote no day `≥ m`), which *admits* a state atom `stateAtom k q'` of a day `n < k < m` once it has become small.

**What is true.** For the trajectory prior with the tent skeleton from **any** base whose day-`n` prices lie in the unit cube — coherent bases included — and `2 ≤ d_{n+1}`, `bli-found`'s full-scope `E2x` is **false**: `Refutations.e2x_full_scope_fails`. The mechanism: choose the day-`(n+1)` candidate `q'` coding the *face table* of `t = actualTable Q n` (`faceTable`: copies `t`'s `0/1` coordinates, `1/d_{n+1}` elsewhere); `σ' := stateAtom (n+1) q'` is small from day `tokenSize σ'` on (`smallOn_tokenSize`), so with `m := max (n+2) (tokenSize σ')` it lies in `Sminus m m`; take the day-`m` face table with `σ'` sent to `0` (`zeroAt`), coded `q`. Constraint 2 at `(n, m, q, σ')` says `𝐏_n(σ' ⋏ σ_{m,q}) = 0 · 𝐏_n(σ_{m,q}) = 0`, but the chain through `σ'` on day `n+1` and `σ_{m,q}` on day `m` has positive mass: each table on the path lies in the product face of the previous one (`tentLaw_pos_iff` pins only `0/1` coordinates, and `σ'` is not a day-`n` coordinate), so the tent charges every step (`chainProbH_pos_of_path`). No OPEN inhabitation row was needed: the day `m` is named explicitly. *Repair round 1:* the round-0 statement assumed every day-`n` small price strictly interior, which — since `⊥ ∈ smallSet n` — excluded every propositionally coherent base (`D_PC` forces `Q n ⊥ = 0`; audit r1 fidelity N3 / adversarial N2, probe `CoherentBase.dpc_not_hint`), i.e. exactly the program's intended base and B3's premise. The face path removes that: the refutation now needs only the unit cube. The round-0 form is kept as the corollary `e2x_full_scope_fails_of_interior`; the concrete instance `e2x_full_scope_fails_halfBase` uses the constant-`½` base (incoherent — it prices `⊥` at `½`), but the theorem itself covers coherent bases.

**The restriction of record.** The package's faith lemmas are `E2xScoped c`/`E3Scoped c`/`E3finScoped c`, with the extra hypothesis `NoFutureState c n φ` ("`φ` mentions no candidate state atom of a day `> n`"). **The mandate's restriction is too weak** (M1: "`∀ k q', n < k → φ ≠ stateAtom k q'`", bare atoms only): a compound `φ = stateAtom k q' ⋎ χ` or `stateAtom k q' ⋏ χ` with `n < k < m` is admitted by `Sminus m m` and creates the same conflict (for the conjunction the parser splits it into the chain; for the disjunction it is Tier B). The correct antecedent is "no such atom occurs in `φ` at all", which is what `NoFutureState` says.

**What the full-scope bundle still is.** It is *consistent*: `Refutations.recipe_isBLI_Roman` exhibits a non-Markov history satisfying `IsBLI_Roman` on the full scope (it prices `σ_k ⋏ σ_m` by faith in the later table). So the finding is precisely: **Appendix B's constraint 2 on the full scope and constraint 3's Markov structure (a trajectory prior) are jointly unsatisfiable** whenever some intermediate day's atom is in scope and the later table gives it a value other than the chain's backward conditional. Either the scope must exclude sentences mentioning intermediate-day state atoms (this package's choice), or the grid must pin past atoms to the chain's backward conditionals — no longer a product grid.

**Recommendation for consolidation (do not edit `bli-found`):** change the definition of record of `E2x`/`E2i`/`E3`/`E3fin` to exclude, from the scope at `(n, m)`, sentences mentioning a state atom of a day in `(n, m)` (equivalently, add `NoFutureState`-style hypotheses parametric in the state system's candidates), or document `IsBLI_Roman`/`IsBLI_AppB` as bundles no Markov prior satisfies. Pointer: `Defs.NoFutureState`, `Lemmas.bli_cond2_scoped`, `Refutations.e2x_full_scope_fails`.

Note also `bli-found` audit r3's separate blocking item on `atomDay` for FAF's tag-4 handles: a repair there only removes sentences from `Sminus`, so the scoped predicates here get weaker, never inconsistent (re-checked against the D3 row of [bli-found-ledger](../bli-found/bli-found-ledger.md) before starting).

## F-10 — The Tier-A parser must be coding-aware — imprecision in the mandate's D2 (definition of record here)

## F-13 — "LUV coherence plus conditional trust implies all the BLI conditions" (Notion ll. 215–225, bli-paper-060) — local error under the `FaithMarginal` + partition reading, refuted in Lean with three distinct coherent tables; the two-state case goes the Notion's way; the readings named (rewritten in repair round 2)

Quoted (Notion): "LUV coherence plus conditional trust … implies *all* the BLI conditions, if I'm not mistaken." Two readings have to be fixed, both ATTRIBUTION-UNVETTED. (1) "Conditional trust" is read as `FaithMarginal` (bli-slides-015 (ii), `P_n(φ | P_m(φ) = p) = p`); read as state-level trust it *is* `E2x` and the claim is circular (the second horn, a remark). (2) "LUV coherence" has no FAF object (bli-paper-060: undefined in this slice). The Notion (ll. 208–210) calls it "coherence with respect to our beliefs about our beliefs, which was not given by propositional coherence alone", and the 2023-09-11 email (journal ll. 858–860) restates the antecedent as "propositional coherence plus luv coherence". What FAF can state is `CoherentOn ∅ A`: a `PCWorld` mixture over the atom encoding, in which the state atom `⌜𝑸_m = q⌝` is a fresh atom (the *empty* stage is the weakest; "every `A`" is the only strong part — audit r2 adversarial N6). The package renders "LUV coherence" in two strengths: **(weak)** `CoherentOn ∅ A` for every `A`, on market, base and tables; **(partition / injective)** additionally every world of the mixture holds exactly one next-day candidate (the next-day states partition every world), every table is itself a world mixture, and distinct codes are distinct tables — in the sources a state *is* its price-list (`main.tex:431`), and B1's `bliStateSystem` is injective on candidates (audit r2 probe `IdenticalTables.bliStateSystem_val_inj`). Stronger readings — e.g. one on which the market's conditional on a state atom is pinned to that state's table — are not touched; that one *is* `E2x`.

**Three steps.** (a) *Without coherence* (`Refutations.faithMarginal_not_imp_e2x`): identical constant tables `p`, conditionals `p ± δ`, masses `½, ½` — `E1x ∧ E4 ∧ E5 ∧ FaithMarginal ∧ ¬E2x`; the scaffold is incoherent (prices `⊥` at `p > 0`; audit r1 adversarial B2, probe `CoherentBase.twoWitness_not_coherent`). It refutes bli-slides-015 (b) for `bli-found`'s abstract `StateSystem`, not the Notion. (b) *With weak coherence* (`Coherent.faithMarginal_coherent_not_imp_e2x`, repair round 1): the four-world mixture `cohHistory p δ` (`cohAtom` true/false × which of two candidates holds, weights `(p+δ)/2, (1−p−δ)/2, (p−δ)/2, (1−p+δ)/2`), tables copying the day-`n` market, base the market's small prices; `CoherentOn ∅ A` on every day for every `A`, `E1x ∧ E3 ∧ E4 ∧ E5 ∧ FaithMarginal` on the full scope, `¬E2x` at `(0, 1, c₁, cohAtom)`. **Audit r2 (adversarial B1) is right that this is a `(c)`**: the two candidates are one table (probe `IdenticalTables.cohSystem_val_eq`, `rfl`), and `(p+δ)/2 ≠ p/2` is only possible because two exclusive atoms share the value `p`; under any coherence that reads the state atom as its table, `σ_{c₁}` and `σ_{c₂}` assert the same thing and `E5`'s exclusivity is incoherent. Relabelled `weaker: (c)` in the docstring and the ledger (repair round 2). The auditor's probe, adopted as `Pinning.two_state_pinning` and extended to `Pinning.two_state_pinning_scope`, shows the point is structural: **with two distinct additive tables, faith in the marginals on a `⋏`/`∼`-closed scope pins constraint 2 everywhere on it** (where the tables differ at `χ`, faith pins directly; where they agree, split `χ` along a sentence `φ` where they differ — the tables then differ at `∼χ ⋏ φ` and at `∼χ ⋏ ∼φ`, those cells and `φ`, `∼φ` are pinned, and the cells of `χ` follow by subtraction). So in the two-state case the Notion's claim is *right* under its own antecedent, and the survey's bli-soto-a-009 ("the two-table counterexample satisfies conditional trust and LUV coherence") is wrong: with two distinct coherent tables there is no such counterexample. (c) ***Repair round 2 — the refutation under the partition / injective reading*** (`TriState.faithMarginal_coherent_distinct_not_imp_e2x`): three pairwise distinct coherent tables `t₁ = (½, ¼, ¼)`, `t₂ = (¼, ½, ¼)`, `t₃ = (¼, ¼, ½)` over the three cells `{a, b}`, `{a}`, `{b}` cut by the atoms `a = atom 0`, `b = atom 1`; the market `triHistory ε` is on every day a mixture of nine worlds (next-day candidate × cell) with weights `⅓ · c_{ij}(ε)`, the conditionals being the tables perturbed by a 3-cycle, `c₁ = t₁ + ε(0, 1, −1)`, `c₂ = t₂ + ε(−1, 0, 1)`, `c₃ = t₃ + ε(1, −1, 0)` (`0 < ε < ¼`, every world charged). Every world holds exactly one next-day state (`triWorld_unique_state`), every table is a world mixture (`triTableVal_coherent`; table `i` charges cell `j` with `t_{ij}` and within the cell carries the market's own posterior over the states), the tables are pairwise distinct (`triTableVal_ne`), `E1x ∧ E3 ∧ E4 ∧ E5 ∧ FaithMarginal` hold on the full scope, and `E2x` fails at `(0, 1, triCode 1 0, cohAtom)`: `⅓ (¾ + ε) ≠ ¾ · ⅓`. The mechanism (`triLevel`, the eight events of the cell algebra checked exhaustively): on each event the states whose table values agree have deviations summing to zero, which three states can do because different events pair them differently — at `{a}` the level sets are `{1}, {2, 3}`, at `{b}` they are `{2}, {1, 3}`, at `{a, b}` they are `{3}, {1, 2}` — whereas with two states the pairing is the same at every event where the tables agree, and the Boolean-closed scope then carries the pinning over from a cell where they differ (F-20). So, under the `FaithMarginal` reading and the partition / injective reading of LUV coherence, **the Notion's implication is false for three states**, with a witness the sources' framework admits (distinct price-lists, states partitioning every world, coherent tables), **and true for two**.

## F-16 — `⊤` in `stateConj` vs `⊤` as a small sentence — local error for `bli-found`'s scope (a price asymmetry; a `⊤`-coherent base is immune)

`stateConj l = sentenceConjunction (…)` ends in a trailing `⊤`, so `E3fin` at `[(m,q)]` reads `P_n(φ ⋏ (σ ⋏ ⊤))`; meanwhile `⊤ ∈ Sminus m m` (for `m ≥ 1`), so `E2x` reads `P_n(⊤ ⋏ σ) = Q̂[⊤] · P_n(σ)` with `Q̂[⊤]` an arbitrary grid value. The two are consistent only if `⊤` is treated asymmetrically: neutral as a chain tail, a priced small sentence elsewhere. The parser of record does exactly that (`Defs.parse`: `⊤` is the empty tail right after a future state atom, opaque elsewhere), so `P_n(σ ⋏ ⊤) = P_n(σ)` while `P_n(⊤ ⋏ σ) = Q̂[⊤] · P_n(σ)`. *Severity raised from "presentation" (audit r1 adversarial N6, probe `TopAsymmetry.top_asymmetry`/`_concrete`):* B1 therefore prices the two logically equivalent sentences `⊤ ⋏ σ_{m,q}` and `σ_{m,q} ⋏ ⊤` differently whenever a *charged* table has `Q̂[⊤] ≠ 1` (for the tent over `halfBase` at the day-`(n+1)` candidate coding the zero table, `𝐏_n(⊤ ⋏ σ) = 0 < 𝐏_n(σ ⋏ ⊤)`). This is forced by `bli-found`'s `E2x` at `φ = ⊤` with incoherent grid tables, not by this package's choice, and it matters for dependents: `bli-measure`'s `PCP` for B1 cannot hold at `⊤` over a base with `Q_n(⊤) ≠ 1`. A base pricing `⊤` at `1` makes the tent's face pin `⊤ ↦ 1` on every charged table, so the asymmetry vanishes there. Recommendation: define `stateConj` without the trailing `⊤`, or exclude `⊤` from `E2x`'s scope, or require `Q̂[⊤] = 1` of candidates.

## F-17 — The mandate's B0 positivity clause rounds to the wrong day — imprecision

M2 states `𝐏⁰_n(σ_{n+1, actual(n+1)}) > 0 ↔ actualState (n+1) = extendZero (actualState n)`, i.e. the day-`n` table rounded to the **day-`n`** grid. ## F-19 — A coherent `FaithMarginal` witness must be agnostic two days ahead — structural remark (from the repair of F-13)

## F-20 — Faith in the marginal pins two distinct tables and not three: the `k`-state question — finding, with an OPEN conjecture (repair round 2)

Two results fix the shape of "faith in the marginal vs faith in the state" over coherent systems. `Pinning.two_state_pinning_scope`: two states, distinct finitely additive (and commutative) tables, the market a world mixture, faith in the marginal on a family closed under `⋏` and `∼` ⇒ constraint 2 at every sentence of the family, for both states. `TriState.faithMarginal_coherent_distinct_not_imp_e2x`: three states, pairwise distinct coherent tables, the market a world mixture in which every world holds exactly one next-day state, faith on the full scope, and constraint 2 fails. The linear-algebra picture behind both: fix the cells of the event algebra; the deviations `d_i(cell) = c_i(cell) − t_i(cell)` of the conditionals from the tables have zero row sums, and faith in the marginal says that for every event `E` and every level set `G = {i : t_i(E) = x}`, `∑_{i ∈ G} m_i d_i(E) = 0`; constraint 2 on the scope is exactly "the only solution is `d = 0`". For two distinct tables the system has full rank (the theorem). For the three tables of `TriState` it has a one-dimensional kernel, spanned by the 3-cycle `(0, 1, −1), (−1, 0, 1), (1, −1, 0)`: the level sets `{1}, {2,3}` at `{a}`, `{2}, {1,3}` at `{b}`, `{3}, {1,2}` at `{a,b}` and `{1,2}, {3}`, `{1,3}, {2}`, `{2,3}, {1}` at the complements pair the states cyclically, and each pair constraint kills a different coordinate of the cycle's complement. Remarks not formalized: if the tables are pairwise distinct at *every* event (all level sets singletons) the kernel is trivial outright; the counterexamples need tables that agree on events in a pattern no single sentence separates. **OPEN (conjecture, no `sorry` row — FAF has no object for LUV coherence to state it against):** characterize the table configurations `(t_i)_{i < k}` for which faith in the marginals on a Boolean-closed scope forces constraint 2, i.e. for which the level-set constraint system has trivial kernel; and whether a scaffold-free witness (a genuine trajectory prior, mass one on every future day's partition) exists for the three-state failure, which F-19 suggests needs backward-conditional tables. Severity: a finding about the sources' claim (F-13) and a correction to the survey's bli-soto-a-009; pointer: `Pinning.lean`, `TriState.lean`.
