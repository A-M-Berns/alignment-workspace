## 2. Blocking issues

### B1. "The distinct-table refutation needs three states" is false for `FaithMarginal` as defined; the `TriState` headline's statement does not carry the scope at which it is true

**The claims.** Ledger, row `faithMarginal_not_imp_e2x`, Fidelity column: "with two *distinct* additive tables the converse holds (`Pinning.two_state_pinning_scope`), and the distinct-table refutation needs three states (`TriState`)". F-13 (b): "with two distinct coherent tables there is no such counterexample"; F-13's close: "the Notion's implication is false for three states … **and true for two**". F-20's title: "Faith in the marginal pins two distinct tables and not three". Report §M7 (iii) step 2: "so no two-state counterexample exists under the injective reading". `TriState.lean` module docstring: "With two states this is impossible (`Pinning.two_state_pinning` and `Pinning.two_state_pinning_scope`): the level sets of two distinct additive tables are singletons on some sentence of every Boolean-closed family". `TriState` ledger row, surviving neighbour: "`Pinning.two_state_pinning_scope` (two states go the Notion's way)".

**What the Lean says.** `Pinning.two_state_pinning_scope` takes `Γ` with `hΓand : ∀ φ ∈ Γ, ∀ χ ∈ Γ, φ ⋏ χ ∈ Γ` and `hΓneg`, and faith `hFM` on `Γ`. `Defs.FaithMarginal S P` is `∀ n m, n < m → ∀ φ ∈ Sminus m m, ∀ x, marginalJoint … = x * marginalMass …`, and `Sminus m m = (smallSet m).filter (day < m)` with `smallSet m = {φ | tokenSize φ ≤ sizeBound m}`. At `(n, m) = (0, 1)`: `sizeBound 1 = 4`, `tokenSize (atom 0) = tokenSize (atom 1) = 3` (so both are in `Sminus 1 1`), and `tokenSize (atom 0 ⋏ atom 1) = 8` (so the conjunction is not): probe `TwoStateGap.and_not_mem_Sminus`. In fact every sentence of `Sminus 1 1` is atom-free or a single atom (`TwoStateGap.shape_of_tokenSize_le_four`, by induction on the sentence from `tokenSize_and/or/imp/atom`). The pinning theorem's scope hypothesis is therefore not met by `FaithMarginal` at `(0, 1)`, and nothing in the package bridges the two.

**The counterexample** (`TwoStateGap.lean`, elaborated, standard axioms):

```
theorem two_state_distinct_coherent_gap (δ : ℝ) (hδ : 0 < δ) (hδ' : δ < 1 / 4) :
    ∃ (S : StateSystem) (Q P : History),
      (∀ n A, CoherentOn ∅ A (P n)) ∧ (∀ n A, CoherentOn ∅ A (Q n)) ∧
      (∀ m q A, CoherentOn ∅ A (S.val m q)) ∧
      (∀ m, ∀ q ∈ S.states m, ∀ q' ∈ S.states m, q ≠ q' → S.val m q ≠ S.val m q') ∧
      (∀ m, (S.states m).card = 2) ∧
      E1x Q P ∧ E3 S P ∧ E4 S P ∧ E5 S P ∧ FaithMarginal S P ∧ ¬ E2x S P
```

— the `TriState` headline's conjuncts verbatim, plus `card = 2`. Witness: two atoms `a = atom 0`, `b = atom 1` cut each day's worlds into four cells; the day-`n` worlds `gapWorld n k` hold the day-`(n+1)` candidate `c₁` (`k = 0, 1`: cells `{a, b}`, `{a}`) or `c₂` (`k = 2, 3`: cells `{b}`, `∅`) — so every world holds exactly one next-day candidate, the partition reading. The day-`(n+1)` tables are the unskewed cell mixtures `c₁ ↦ ½ w₀ + ½ w₁` (`a ↦ 1`, `b ↦ ½`) and `c₂ ↦ ½ w₂ + ½ w₃` (`a ↦ 0`, `b ↦ ½`): coherent, distinct at `a`, and each one holds its own state atom. The market on day `0` has weights `¼ + δ, ¼ − δ, ¼ − δ, ¼ + δ` — the conditional given `c₁` is skewed on `b` — and on every later day the unskewed weights (so constraint 2 holds there on every sentence, `gapHistory_cond2_pos`). On `Sminus 1 1` the skew is invisible to faith in the marginal: at `a` the tables differ and the conditionals equal the tables; at `b`, `⊤`, `⊥` and every other atom the tables agree and the two joints sum to the common value (`gapHistory_faith_zero`, through `faith_pair`). `E2x` fails at `(0, 1, c₁ 1, b)`: `𝐏_0(b ⋏ σ₁) = ¼ + δ ≠ ½ · ½` (`gapHistory_not_E2x`). And faith **does** fail at `a ⋏ b`, which is outside `Sminus 1 1` (`gap_faith_fails_outside_scope`): the witness lives on the scope gap, exactly as the pinning theorem predicts for a `⋏`-closed family. Same scaffold as the package's witnesses (no state atom of a day `≥ n+2` holds; F-19). N+ on the coordinates that matter: two charged states (`½` each), distinct tables (`a ↦ 1` vs `a ↦ 0`, interior at `b`), a market genuinely uncertain about both atoms, every world charged for `0 < δ < ¼`.

**Why this is blocking.** [STANDARDS](../../STANDARDS.md) §5 makes findings first-class output, and the audit prompt makes "a false claim in the findings file" and "a ledger row that misstates" blocking. F-13's "there is no such counterexample", the ledger's "the converse holds … needs three states", and the `TriState` docstring's "With two states this is impossible" are each false as statements about `bli-found`'s `FaithMarginal` and `E2x` — the objects the package's own refutations are stated over. The mathematics behind the three-state construction is right, and it is the right construction *for the Boolean-closed regime*; what is wrong is that the package states the positive result at one scope (closed family) and the refutations at another (`Sminus`), and presents them as being about one thing. Two readings of the Notion's "conditional trust" are possible, and the package's text is wrong under either: (A) faith on every sentence the day-`m` market could be asked about (Boolean-closed): then `TriState`'s *statement* — `FaithMarginal` on `Sminus` — is weaker than the claim it refutes, since a two-state witness refutes that weak form too, and the headline should carry the closed-scope faith its proof already gives; (B) faith on the day-`m` small sentences (`Sminus m m`, the predicate of record): then two states suffice and "needs three" is simply false. Under (B) my witness is a boundary effect of the size bound at day `1` — the day-`1` market prices only atoms and constants — and a reader may fairly say the interior (Boolean-closed) regime is the interesting one; that is an argument for stating the scope, not for the current text.

**Fix I would accept** (no new mathematics; the probes can be adopted as the round-2 probes were):

1. `TriState.faithMarginal_coherent_distinct_not_imp_e2x`: add the conjunct the proof already supports — `∀ n m, n < m → ∀ φ, (∀ a ∈ sentenceAtomCodes φ, atomDay a < m) → ∀ x, marginalJoint S P n m φ x = x * marginalMass S P n m φ x` (faith on the family of all sentences whose atoms refer to days `< m`, closed under `⋏`/`⋎`/`🡒`/`∼`: `TriStateStrong.triHistory_faith_dayFamily`, `dayFamily_and`, `dayFamily_neg`; the proof is `triHistory_faithMarginal`'s with the size bound dropped). With it, the headline refutes the implication at the strength where two states are pinned, and the three-vs-two narrative is carried by the statement. Optionally add the in-scope distinctness `∃ φ ∈ Sminus m m, val q φ ≠ val q' φ` for `m ≥ 1` (`TriStateStrong.triTableVal_ne_in_scope`) in place of distinctness as functions.
2. Ledger row `faithMarginal_not_imp_e2x` (Fidelity), `TriState` row (Fidelity and surviving-neighbour clause), Pinning row (Claim): say that two distinct tables are pinned by faith on a `⋏`/`∼`-closed family, that `FaithMarginal`'s scope `Sminus m m` is not such a family, and that on that scope two distinct coherent tables already refute the implication (`TwoStateGap`), so the three-state witness is what the closed-scope reading needs.
