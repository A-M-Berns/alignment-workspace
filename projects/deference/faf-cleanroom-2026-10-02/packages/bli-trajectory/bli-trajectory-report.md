## Chain probability vs `trajLaw` (deviation, disclosed and closed)

`bli-finite`'s `trajLaw` is snoc-oriented (last day split off); the update identities need the *first* day split off (restart from the conditioned table). The price of record is defined by the forward recursion `chainProbH`, in which the chain rule is built in (`chainProbH_cons_succ`). **`Marginals.chainProbH_eq_trajMass`** proves `chainProbH sk c l n t H = ∑ τ ∈ trajGrid n H, [τ passes through l's codes] · trajLaw sk n H t τ` (`trajMass`), by the front decomposition `Traj.cons`/`first`/`tail` (a bijection `grid (n+1) × trajGrid (n+1) k ≃ trajGrid n (k+1)`, `sum_trajGrid_cons`), `trajLaw_cons` (the law factors through it, using `Skeleton.law_castDay`) and `chainEvent_cons`. So the Tier-A price **is** the mandate's "mass `trajLaw` gives the event"; `bli_state` reads the one-step case as the kernel entry directly.
