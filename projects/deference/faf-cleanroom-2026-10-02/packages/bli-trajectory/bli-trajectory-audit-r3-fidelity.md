## 3. Non-blocking issues

**N1. "With two distinct tables the converse holds" is true on Boolean-closed scopes, not for `bli-found`'s `FaithMarginal` — the ledger drops the qualifier, and a two-state distinct-table counterexample exists at `(0, 1)`.** `Pinning.two_state_pinning_scope` needs a family `Γ` closed under `⋏` and `∼` (`hΓand`, `hΓneg`), and its docstring, F-13's step (b), F-20 and the `Pinning` ledger row all say so. But the `faithMarginal_not_imp_e2x` row's Fidelity column says "with two *distinct* additive tables the converse holds (`Pinning.two_state_pinning_scope`), and the distinct-table refutation needs three states (`TriState`)", report §M7 (iii) step 2 says "no two-state counterexample exists under the injective reading", and F-13 ends "and true for two". `bli-found`'s scope is `Sminus m m`, a *size-bounded* set, and at `(n, m) = (0, 1)` it is not Boolean-closed: `sizeBound 1 = 4`, `atom 0` and `atom 1` have token size `3`, their conjunction `8` and the negation `∼atom 1` `6` — probe `ScopeNotClosed.lean` (§5): `cohAtom_mem_Sminus`, `triAtom'_mem_Sminus_one`, `and_not_mem_Sminus_one`, `neg_not_mem_Sminus_one`. So the pinning argument (split `χ` along a `φ` where the tables differ, use `∼χ ⋏ φ`) has nothing to work with at `(0, 1)`, and a **two-state** witness with distinct coherent tables goes through there, by hand (not machine-checked — cost; it is `Coherent.lean` with a second atom): day-`0` worlds `{a₀, a₁, σ₁}`, `{a₀, σ₁}`, `{a₁, σ₂}`, `{σ₂}` with weights `(p+δ)/2, (1−p−δ)/2, (p−δ)/2, (1−p+δ)/2`; day-`1` tables `t₁ := p·⟦{a₀,a₁}⟧ + (1−p)·⟦{a₀}⟧`, `t₂ := p·⟦{a₁}⟧ + (1−p)·⟦∅⟧` (world mixtures, so coherent; distinct at `a₀`, equal to `p` at `a₁`); later days the same with `δ = 0`. Then each world holds exactly one day-`1` state; `E5` (`½ + ½`, exclusive); `E4` at `0` reads only `⊥` (`smallSet 0 = {⊥}`, since atoms have token size `3 > 2`) and at `n ≥ 1` holds because conditionals equal tables there; `E3` and `FaithMarginal` at horizon `≥ 2` by the usual scaffold; `FaithMarginal` at `(0, 1)`: at `a₀` the tables differ and the conditionals are `1`/`0`, pinned; at `a₁` the level set is `{1, 2}` and `(p+δ)/2 + (p−δ)/2 = p·1`; at `⊥`, `⊤`, `⊥ ⋏ ⊥` and every other atom in scope both sides agree (both tables are mixtures of the same worlds); no two-atom conjunction and no negated atom is in scope. `E2x` fails at `(0, 1, q₁, a₁)`: `(p+δ)/2 ≠ p/2`. So **for `bli-found`'s predicates the distinct-table refutation does not need three states** — it needs them only once the scope is Boolean-closed, which is the reading under which the package corrects bli-soto-a-009 and the Notion's "true for two" holds. The audit r2 adversarial B1 already called the `(0, 1)` size bound "a second encoding artifact"; the repair adopted the pinning theorem (with `Γ` closed) without carrying that caveat into the rows that cite it. Nothing downstream rests on "two states cannot do it" (the three-state refutation is strictly stronger and scope-robust: `triLevel` checks the whole cell algebra), so this is a labelling matter. Fix: in the `faithMarginal_not_imp_e2x` row, report §M7 (iii) step 2 and F-13's closing sentence, add "on a `⋏`/`∼`-closed scope; `bli-found`'s `Sminus 1 1` is not one (probe), and there a two-distinct-table witness exists — an artifact of the day-`1` size bound"; and say in F-13 that the correction of bli-soto-a-009 is under the Boolean-closed reading of "conditional trust".

**N2. The `TriState` row is labelled `Fidelity: exact`, `Hyps: —`, while the `Coherent` row for the same rendering of "LUV coherence" is labelled `weaker: (c)`, `Hyps: (c)`.** "LUV coherence" has no FAF object (bli-paper-060; F-13 (2)). `TriState` renders it as: `CoherentOn ∅ A` on every atom set for market, base and tables, every world holding exactly one *next-day* candidate, tables pairwise distinct. That is a modelling choice in the sense of [STANDARDS](../../STANDARDS.md) §3 (c) — a defensible one, and the strongest the package argues FAF can state — and the row discloses it in words ("the partition and injectivity readings … built into the witness; the scaffold as in `Coherent`"). But the scaffold is exactly where a reader of the Notion's own gloss ("small beliefs about future beliefs marginalize large beliefs about whole future market states", ll. 217–218, for every future day) will say the antecedent is not met: from day `n` the witness gives the day-`(n+2)` partition total mass `0` (`triHistory_stateAtom_ne`; r2 probe `Scaffold.lean` for `cohHistory`, same mechanism), i.e. its "distribution over tomorrow's-tomorrow states" is not a distribution. The package knows this (F-13's last paragraph, F-19, F-20's OPEN) and B1 itself satisfies the stronger reading (`bli_stateAlgebra_additive`: every future day's candidates carry mass one) — which also shows that reading *is* statable in FAF, contrary to the `TriState` module docstring's "a stronger reading of LUV coherence that FAF cannot state". Fix: label the `TriState` row `variant: (c)` in Fidelity with Hyps "(c) LUV coherence rendered as a `PCWorld` mixture with next-day partition and distinct coherent tables; mass `0` two days ahead (F-19)", matching the `Coherent` row; and in the module docstring replace "that FAF cannot state" by "not touched here (statable: `bli_stateAlgebra_additive` is its B1 instance; F-20's OPEN)". Status "refuted (… under the `FaithMarginal` + partition/injective reading …)" is accurate and can stay.

**N4. As noted in §1 the property is in fact implied by the statement (`E5`'s mass-one-and-exclusivity across a mixture with all weights positive forces each charged world to hold exactly one candidate), so nothing is lost; a sentence saying so in the docstring, or an explicit conjunct `∃ k W w, (∀ i, 0 < w i) ∧ (∀ χ, P n χ = ∑ w_i (W i).payout χ) ∧ ∀ i, ∃! q ∈ S.states (n+1), (W i).Holds (stateAtom (n+1) q)`, would make the "partition reading" visible at the statement.
