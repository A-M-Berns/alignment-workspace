## 3. Non-blocking issues

**N1 — `refreeze_dilemma` (ii) is the coin-true branch only; the coin-false branch reverses (probe `RecClass.lean`).** The Lean is explicit — the re-frozen prior is `conditionOn (scPrior p) (coinClass K) _` — but the docstrings of `refrozen_gap`, `refrozen_prefers_refrozen`, `refreeze_dilemma`, the report's T4(d) and the ledger row say "the re-frozen prior strictly prefers the re-frozen policy" without the qualifier. Under the prior re-frozen on the coin-**false** class (`recClass`, the `Rec_k` tables) the ex-ante value is `V · ∑_k γ_k [π Ask_k] + r₀ false` (`AuditR1.conditionOn_rec_exAnteValue_eq`), so that re-frozen prior strictly prefers `payAll` to `refrozen t` by `V · ∑_{k≥t} γ_k` (`AuditR1.rec_refrozen_gap`, `rec_prefers_payAll`, for `q < 1`, `V > 0`, `γ > 0`, `t < K`) — the same direction as the frozen prior. This does not weaken the dilemma: the coin-true branch is the one whose tables (`Ask_k`) carry a stake, and `refrozen t`'s decisions from `t` on are its two-step choices at exactly those tables. But "two-sided" is two-sided *at the `Ask` tables*, and the row and docstring should say "re-frozen on the coin-true class". Related wording: `refrozen t` is not the re-frozen prior's own one-step policy (that is `refrozen 0`, as `selfTrust_is_refreeze_gap` records); "prefers its own policy" means "prefers the policy that is its two-step rule from `t` on". Fix I'd accept: one clause in the `refreeze_dilemma` docstring and the ledger row; optionally adopt the probe's `recClass` lemmas as the recorded other branch.

**N2 — "`LocalUtility` fails here (cross-round terms)" is prose without Lean, and the reason given is off (probe `NotLocal.lean`).** The report (T4 Model, "Deviations"), the `SingleCoin.lean` module docstring and the root docstring say `LocalUtility` fails on `scPrior p`; no declaration proves it. `AuditR1.not_localUtility` proves `¬ (scPrior p).LocalUtility` for every `K ≥ 1` under `q < 1`, `0 < w j`, `V ≠ 0`, `γ j ≠ 0`, through `AuditR1.cellEU_rec : cellEU (Rec_j) π = V · roundSum π + r₀ false`: `payAll` and `payAll[Ask_j ↦ refuse]` agree at `Rec_j`, both cells are positive, and the values differ by `V · γ_j`. So the failure is the cross-**branch** term (`Rec_j` reads the `Ask_j` point — the mugging's own term, core F-13), present already at `K = 1`, before any cross-**round** term; the parenthetical should say so. Fix I'd accept: adopt the lemma into `SingleCoin.lean` (e.g. as a conjunct of `structure_facts` under the stated positivity) and correct the parenthetical in the three docstrings/report.

**N3 — Kind inflation on two rows.** `Omega.r1_coarse_optimal` is labelled **C** in the ledger and report; its body is `⟨fun _ _ _ => rfl, isPriorOptimal_const_pay r hr⟩` — a pairing of a `rfl` (constancy of a constant policy) with a core theorem: **T/L**. `policyFair_bitLayer` is labelled **P**; the bit is invisible to `U₀`, `state₀` and `small₀` by construction and the proof is two rewrites of `procEU_bitLayer`: **L**. Neither is a headline of the mandate's five, and `procEU_eq_exAnteValue_of_policyFair` (P, fibre averaging) is correctly labelled. Fix: relabel.

**N4 — The round law `w` is inert in every value.** `scU` ignores `now`, and the policy points are independent of the state, so `exAnteValue_eq`, `EU_diff`, `twoStepEU_eq`, `conditionOn_exAnteValue_eq` and every gap are `w`-free; `w` enters only cell/home positivity (`NDHOME` at `Ask_k`/`Rec_k` needs `w_k > 0`, as in my probe's `hw`). The `Params` docstring ("the law of the current round") and the model docstring could say that `w` affects which table obtains and nothing the headlines compute. Presentation.

**N5 — The vacuity boundary is implicit (probe `Vacuity.lean`).** `AuditR1.noStrictPrecommit_all_of_zero_weights` and `noStrictPrecommit_all_of_gain_zero`: with `γ ≡ 0`, or with `gain = 0` — attained at the exact threshold `q = V/(V+c)` (`gain_zero_at_threshold`) — every policy, every `refrozen t` included, has `NoStrictPrecommit` and is prior-optimal (all values tie at `res`). The headlines exclude both by `hγ`/`hg` (strict), so they are not vacuous; but `priorOptimal_payAll` is stated with `0 ≤ gain`, and a reader may expect `noStrictPrecommit_iff_payOnAsk` to extend to the boundary. One sentence in that docstring ("at `gain = 0` every policy ties and the predicate is trivial") would close it. Presentation.

**N6 — `IsRefrozen Sig t π` for a general `Sig` can include junk-tied classes.** `IsTwoStepChoice (scPrior p) Sig (Ask_k) a` compares `twoStepEU` values, which are the junk `0` under every action when the `Sig`-class of `Ask_k` is null (e.g. `Sig = {node1 K k}` with `w_k = 0`); then every action is a two-step choice and the class `IsRefrozen Sig t` is wider than intended. `stability_iff`'s conclusion is unaffected (it is `noStrictPrecommit_iff_payOnAsk` plus the before-`t` clause, true for any `π`), and the two instantiated `Sig` are positive (`{coin}` under `hq`, `∅` trivially). Disclose in the `IsRefrozen` docstring, as sist's `IsTwoStepChoice` docstring does ("honest scope: the Σ-class positive under every action"). Presentation.

**N7 — T1's row could say that in its package the one-step rule is the updateful rule.** The `Independent.lean` module docstring says it (`oneStep_iff_updateful` under `NoCrossBranch`, which `IndependentPoints ∧ LocalUtility ∧ ReflectivePolicy` deliver); the theorem's own docstring and the ledger row do not. **N8 — T5's N+ of record has fairness by construction.** `CorrFullBit.tiles` uses `withBit corrDataFull`, whose bit is invisible to the utility, so `PolicyFair (bitLayer _)` holds for every `IndepData` prior. The row could say "fairness by construction (the bit is inert)". The other N+ (`FairLayer.fair_layer_tiles`, `layerOf fairU`) is correctly recorded as outside T1's package (F10, `not_in_package` read). Presentation.

**N9 — `not_noStrictPrecommitAt_of_lt` does not guard `π` itself.** The lemma (L) takes positivity of the update only, so with a null `π` the strict inequality would compare the junk `0`. Every use in the package (`gap_not_noStrictPrecommitAt`, `updateful_not_tiles`, `refreeze_not_noStrictPrecommitAt`, `noStrictPrecommit_iff_payOnAsk`) is under `NDPOLICY`, and `¬ NoStrictPrecommitAt` is never obtained through the null-mass conjunct.
