### R-9 — `hdiv` is derivable at every content threshold, so T6/T7's hypothesis package is inhabited where the theorems have content (result; audit r1 fidelity B2 / adversarial B1)

The repair goes past both suggested fixes: `schedGate_divergent_of_realized_tendsto_one` (`Witnesses`) proves, for any inductor `P` quoting `H`'s deferred expectation through a `CrossQuotePackage`, that if the realized value `𝔼^H_{f n}(X_n)` tends to `1` then the scheduled upper quote gate `1[n ∈ im d]·Ind_δ(a_n > t)` has divergent mass in `P`'s market at every `t + δ < 1`. The engine is FAF's own `recurringunbiasednessexp` (via fa-theorem-a's `quote_unbiased`) on the *bare schedule indicator*: the scheduled average of `a_n − 𝔼^H_{f n}(X_n)` has limit point `0`, so with the realized value `→ 1` the scheduled average of `a_n` has limit point `1`; the ramp bound `Ind_δ(a > t) ≥ 1 − (1 − a)/(1 − t − δ)` then makes the gate's mass frequently at least half the schedule's, and monotonicity of prefix sums turns "frequently" into divergence. On the paper LIA with `X ≡ 𝟙(⊤)` the realized value does tend to `1` (`lic_provind_true` on the constant family `⊤ ⋏ ∼∼⊤`, which every world holds — no deducibility premise — plus the arithmetic identity `𝔼_n(𝟙(φ)) = P_n(φ ⋏ ∼∼φ)`), so `theoremSS_paper_self_top` / `schedThresholdAbove_paper_self_top` inhabit the **full** package of `theoremSS_limitPoint` / `schedThresholdAbove_of_theoremSS` at, e.g., `t = 1/2`, `δ = 1/4`. The grade is honest: N+ for the package at a content threshold, N− for content (same market, and `𝔼^H_n(𝟙(⊤)) → 1` makes T7's conclusion immediate). What is still not shown — and would need facts about an LIA's prices — is a content-threshold inhabitant on an *undecided* family such as `cleanX`. The sub-zero completion (`theoremSS_paper_self_negOne`, `_negOne` for T7) is also in the library, graded N−. ### R-14 — the T4 witness's return vanishes without the trader (audit r2 adversarial N6; sharpens F-A10)

F-A10 and the ledger said the return's non-vanishing on `A.hSideBridge_w2` was "not shown". It is false: on a constant indicator family the return is provably zero in the limit, with no trader. `indicatorOf_expectApprox_eq` (`𝔼^V_k(𝟙(φ)) = V(φ ⋏ ∼∼φ)` for every valuation and precision `k > 0`, since every threshold `i/k` lies in `[0,1)`) with FAF's `expectAffine_priceAt` gives `bundle_indicator_price`: the day-`n` mesh of `𝟙(φ)` is priced at `P_m(φ ⋏ ∼∼φ)` on **every** day `m`. So the round trip banks `P_{n+1}(φ ⋏ ∼∼φ) − P_n(φ ⋏ ∼∼φ)`, which tends to `0` by the convergence of a fixed sentence's price (FAF `thm:con`, `lic_limitingBelief_tendsto`): `bundle_indicator_return_tendsto_zero` on any inductor, `hSideBridge_w2_return_tendsto_zero` on the witness's instance (`Witnesses` §E). Consequence for the grading: `A.hSideBridge_w2` is N− for content in the strongest sense — T4's conclusion on it follows for every weighting from convergence alone, and `lic_not_frequently_positive_feedback_return` is never needed. A witness that exercises T4's mechanism needs a family whose day-`n` mesh is **not** priced constantly across days, i.e. a varying `X_n` (e.g. `𝟙(atom n)` on the decided-atom inductor, where the mesh's day-`(n+1)` price is the decided value and the day-`n` expectation is the pre-decision price); that needs an e.c. certificate for a varying indicator family and an argument that the realized cash is not Cesàro-null, neither of which the package has. Severity: imprecision in F-A10 (corrected here; the row and the T4 section say "vanishes").
