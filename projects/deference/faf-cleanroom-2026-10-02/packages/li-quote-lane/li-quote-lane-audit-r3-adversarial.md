## 3. Non-blocking issues

**1. `readability_ofApprox`'s existential hypothesis is equivalent to its conclusion for every rational-priced reader, so the theorem is an iff; state it as one, and say in the docstring which instances carry the content.** The hypothesis is `∃ ẑ, PGenerableRat P ẑ ∧ ẑ_n − a_{j,n} → 0`. For a reader whose prices are rationals — every FAF `liaHistory`, by `liaHistory_eq_quote_cast` — the reader's *own* day-`n` expectation of the ledger LUV is such a `ẑ`: it is a rational (a finite average of rational prices), and `ledgerFeature j n` (T4.2) is a `PGenerableWeighting` denoting it (`ledgerFeature_denote`), which is exactly a `GeneratedRatFeature`. So `𝔼^P_n(α_{j,n}) ≈ₙ a_{j,n}` gives back the hypothesis at `ẑ := 𝔼`. Probe `AdvR3ApproxIff.lean`: `expect_pgenerable (hP : RationalPriced P) (j) : ∃ ẑ, PGenerableRat P ẑ ∧ ∀ n, (ẑ n : ℝ) = (ledgerLuv j n).expect P n` (feature `ledgerFeature j`, fields from `ledgerFeature_pgenerable`), then `readability_ofApprox_iff` — the package's hypotheses plus `RationalPriced P` — proves `(∃ ẑ, PGenerableRat P ẑ ∧ Tendsto (ẑ − a j) → 0) ↔ (𝔼^P(α_j) ≈ₙ a_j)`, and `readability_ofApprox_iff_lia` instantiates it at `liaHistory (ledgerProcess base a e)`. This is **not** a squeeze in `AUDIT` §3.3's sense and I do not grade it one: the forward direction is FAF's provability induction (`affine_provind_theory_tendsto_zero`), and at the instances the package actually uses — `ẑ = a_j` (`readability`), `ẑ ≡ L` (`readability_ofTendsto`), `MachineRatCodes` approximants (`readability_ofMachineApprox`) — `ẑ` is given independently of `P` and the content is real. But the *existential* packaging is, for rational-priced readers, a restatement of the conclusion, and a dependent reading "any `P`-generable `ẑ`" should be told that the generable approximant must come from outside the market for the theorem to say anything (an approximant that is itself a feature of `P`'s prices of `α_{j,n}` is circular). Two consequences worth recording: (i) over a rational-priced reader, L4 at a table holds **iff** the table is asymptotically approximable by a `P`-generable rational sequence — the honest FAF rendering of "L4 needs (L)" (F2), sharper than the one-directional `readability`; (ii) the OPEN `readability_fails_without_generability` is then equivalent (at a LIA reader) to: some computable `[0,1]`-table with a decidable cut is *not* asymptotically approximable by any `liaHistory`-generable rational sequence. Fix I'd accept: adopt the probe's two theorems (Kind P for `expect_pgenerable`, L for the iff), cite the iff in `readability_ofApprox`'s docstring and the T3.1 row, and add the sentence about circular approximants.
