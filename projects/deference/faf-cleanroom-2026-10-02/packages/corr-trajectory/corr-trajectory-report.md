## Design (T1) and what it does to the statements

**Layer F.** `Atoms Ω` (atoms contain their point, partition, nested in time), `FinFiltered` (law + atoms), `condSum μ A t X ω = ∑_{ω' ∈ fib t ω} μ ω' X ω'` — every conditional quantity in the package is this object against `c · atomMass`. The only *conditional* ratios are `ShutdownProc.epsH` (T3's `ε_t h_t`) and its dual `epsC` (`(1 − ε_t) c_t`, repair round 2), and they enter only through `expect_mul_condSum_div`, which is junk-safe (a mass-zero atom contributes `0` on both sides, no positivity hypothesis). Other ratios in the package — `a6`'s two conjuncts, `posterior_eq_epsHatA7`, `floorA7`, `complianceMargin` (logs), the parent's `complianceThreshold`/`epsStar`, `Basin.tv` — are each guarded: positive denominators proved, logs under positivity, homogeneity theorems handling zero denominators (audit r2, adversarial N10). The tower property appears twice: `expect_le_of_condSum_le` (atom comparison integrates) and `condSum_le_of_condSum_le` (nests across levels — needed for the backward induction).

**Two filtrations.** `ShutdownProc` carries `F` (`𝓕_t`, after the press) and `Fpre` (`𝓕_t^-`, before it) with `F.fib t ⊆ Fpre.fib t ⊆ F.fib (t−1)`. `pressed` is `𝓕_t`-measurable, `kappa` is `𝓕_t^-`-measurable (the agent's "would I comply?" is decided before the press). This is where F-5 comes from: a factorization of `P(Cat_{t+1} ∣ ·)` through the press rate must condition on `𝓕_t^-`.

**Derived, never fields.** `executed = ¬(Pr ∧ κ)`, `loss`, `Harm`, `Omit`, `Reg`; S2′'s `executedWL` from the absorbing `stopped`. `AgentView` adds `agentLaw t ω : Distr Ω` (`𝓕_t`-measurable in `ω`), and `Ψ_t = expect (agentLaw t ω) (R T t)` — so `Ψ_{T+1} = 0` is a lemma (`Psi_top`), `Z`, `drift` in product form, and the two Mart identities as two named predicates (`condMart` is (iv); `uncondMart` is the develop's).

**The toy as an instance of S2.** The 2-013 loss toy has no informative button; it is the `ShutdownProc` with the press always on and uninformative, so `executed = ¬κ` and `κ` is literally S2 step 4's rule (`ev q ≤ 0`). The A1/A8/A2/C6 and A3 refutations are therefore instances of the package's definitions, not of a side model (F-6).

**Latent target elided.** Wrongness enters as `W_t`, as in the source's own proofs; fidelity `variant` on `ShutdownProc`'s docstring.

**Undiscounted throughout.** No discount factor appears anywhere.
