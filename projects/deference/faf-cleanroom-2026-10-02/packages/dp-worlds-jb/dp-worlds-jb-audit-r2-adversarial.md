## 3. Non-blocking issues

### N1. T6's witness annihilates probabilities too: `Prob (Jω Dy)` is empty, so the appendix's regime has no witness in the package

**What is true.** Round 1 (adversarial N2) showed the dyadic witness is countable and that on a countable algebra `Jω`-worlds are exactly the atoms without any measure; the repairer lifted that into `Countable.lean` and disclosed in the ledger that `Dy` "does not separate the theorem from its measure-free countable special case". This round pushes the same diagonal argument one step further. Probe `NoProbCountable.lean` proves, for any countable Boolean algebra `E` with `hat : ∀ X ≠ ⊥, ∃ Y < X, Y ≠ ⊥`:

- `exists_small_below`: a `Prob (Jω E)` puts arbitrarily small mass below every non-`⊥` event (build a chain `a = Y₀ ≥ Y₁ ≥ ⋯` of non-`⊥` events dodging the enumeration, so its only lower bound is `⊥`; `cont` forces `inf P (Yₙ) = 0`);
- **`no_prob_of_countable_atomless : IsEmpty (Prob (Jω E))`** (build `⊤ = Z₀ ≥ Z₁ ≥ ⋯` dodging the enumeration while cutting out mass `< 2⁻ⁿ⁻²` each time, so `P (Zₙ) > 1/2` throughout; its only lower bound is `⊥`; `cont` forces `inf P (Zₙ) = 0`);
- `no_world_of_no_prob` re-derives `no_world_of_countable_atomless` through T1 (a world is a two-valued probability);
- **`dyadic_no_prob : IsEmpty (Prob (Jω Dy))`**, `dyadic_both_empty`, and `dyProb_not_Jω_continuous : ¬ ∃ Q : Prob (Jω Dy), Q.P = dyProb.P` — Lebesgue measure on the dyadic algebra is not continuous along the designated meets, and nothing else is either.

**Why it matters for the record.** Appendix A's annihilation runs on the Lebesgue measure algebra, where Lebesgue measure *is* a `Jω`-probability (σ-additivity on a σ-complete algebra), and v2 §1 line 24 (read) characterizes that pointless regime as one where "`Δ(Ω)` would name distributions on an empty set while `Δ(𝒜)` is nonempty". On `Dy` both are empty. So the worlds/probabilities contrast that the appendix's annihilation paragraph and the "world/probability mismatch" paragraph are about is exhibited by no witness in the package: `dyadic_no_world` shows "designating too much can leave no worlds", but on an algebra where designating that much leaves nothing at all. The same holds for the agenda witnesses finding 11 proposes for the halving theorem, as far as I can see (prose argument, not formalized): on the interval algebra of `[0,1)` and on the clopen algebra of `2^ℝ`, a decreasing sequence of members whose set-intersection is nowhere dense but of positive measure (delete a dense open set of small measure interval by interval; forbid an all-ones block on longer and longer blocks) has infimum `⊥` *in the subalgebra* while its finite meets keep measure bounded away from `0`, so Lebesgue and the fair-coin measure are not `Jω`-continuous there either. They would exercise the halving hypothesis on an uncountable algebra (finding 11's purpose) but not exhibit `Δ(𝒜) ≠ ∅`; only a σ-complete algebra — the quotient measure algebra, S1-type work — does.

**Fix I would accept** (no Lean statement changes): (i) lift `no_prob_of_countable_atomless` and `dyadic_no_prob` into `Countable.lean` (the probe's proofs are self-contained and elaborate against the library as is); (ii) the ledger's Witness cell for `no_world_of_halving` and the Fidelity line of the `Dy` row say that on the variant algebra the designation annihilates probabilities as well, so the appendix's regime (worlds empty, probabilities nonempty) is not exhibited; (iii) the report's "State of the package" sentence "each with an N+ witness" carries that caveat for load-bearing item 2, and §T6's "What the witness exercises" paragraph gains the sentence; (iv) finding 11 records the theorem and the consequence for the source: v2 §1 line 24's "`Δ(𝒜)` is nonempty" is specific to σ-complete (measure-algebra) instances — on countable atomless algebras with countable meets designated there are no probabilities at all, and on set-algebra stand-ins the natural measure is not `Jω`-continuous; severity presentation (the sentence is scoped to Appendix A's instance and is true there). ATTRIBUTION-UNVETTED that the appendix's author intends the contrast as load-bearing; the sentence at line 24 says it is why `Δ(𝒜)` rather than `Δ(Ω)` is the abstract notation.
