# Research state — how to read this repository

`AGENTS.md` says what a contribution must satisfy. This says what a document
*is*: which layer it belongs to, whose judgment it carries, and what it claims to
have shown. It adds no gate, no metadata requirement and no registry. It names
distinctions the repository already draws so that a fresh reader stops having to
rediscover them.

**Status of this document: agent-consolidated, `ci-only`.** It is a synthesis of
what the line documents say, not a maintainer endorsement of any of it, and no
research claim originates here. Where it and a line's own documents disagree, the
line's documents win.

# Where the lines stand

Compressed to what a fresh reader needs. Each line's own documents are the
authority and are linked at the end of its section.

## Deference and corrigibility

**The question.** Can humans rationally make use of cognition more powerful than
their own without thereby surrendering continuing authority over it?

**Aspirational, mathematical.** Under a non-circular competence hypothesis and a
fair comparison, the incremental value of transferring jurisdiction to a
better-informed future agent is bounded.

**Constructed.** Finite envelope theorems with their competence bound,
kernel-checked and unregistered; a settlement classification showing that grade
settlement contributes nothing to the delegation inequality and that enforcement
delivers it unconditionally at an exact bond with zero epistemic content; the
result that the no-Dutch-book criterion forces *signed* calibration and provably
cannot force magnitude accuracy; an actual-FAF efficient trader certificate and
criterion-to-signed-forcing chain; a theorem that values factoring through price
and realization are constant on equal-view fibers; the finite certificate bounds
with derived constants; confound witnesses; and two documented collapse diagnoses.

**Controlling gap — model and interface debt.** FAF can name later market prices
and derive self-trust relations, but it has no resource-indexed process state
showing that the later computation is unavailable now, and no decision,
authorization, capability or continuation type. The static part of the Stage IV
diagnosis is now proved: a value factoring through price and realization cannot
distinguish architectures agreeing on those projections. A successor needs both
a bounded-futurity certificate and a two-index authorization/capability transition
object before a comparator is well-posed.

**Aspirational, philosophical.** Future cognitive superiority need not justify
transferring final jurisdiction.

**Constructed, philosophical.** Logical Induction can make later prices and
computations rationally worth attending to while remaining silent about who
controls execution. A valuation over price and realisation cannot distinguish who
authorised an action. These are claims about the represented epistemic and
evaluative registers, **not** claims that transferring or retaining jurisdiction
is justified.

**Next controlling questions.** Whether a minimal resource-indexed process can
turn quoted later computation into genuine bounded futurity, and whether a
two-index execution state can represent continuing correction without making
option value an assumption. Item 25 — whether the near-indifference leakage can
be bounded — remains the controlling *competence* question and decides whether
the surviving competence candidate has any finite consequence.

**Standing bar on the comparator.** No round may attempt the fully-updated
comparator again without first putting the authorisation relation into the type.
Adding parameters to the valuation is ruled out by two rounds' evidence, and a
later process must not be *any* argmax over objects the evaluator already holds —
not merely not the evaluator's own argmax.

**Deeper:** `projects/deference/notes/CORRIGIBILITY_PAPER_LEDGER.md` for status,
the roadmap beside it for architecture, `TERMS.md` for vocabulary.

## Leverage

**The question.** What must a record show for a learner's normative state to be
accountable?

**Constructed.** A consolidated monograph — objection grammar, diachronic
identity across ontology migration, practical demand, joint composition, and a
settlement interface — carrying its own claim ledger with statuses, sharpness and
dependencies, and re-verifying itself in CI on every push.

**Controlling gap.** Two open problems carry the weight: whether a declared core
minimum stays satisfiable as settlement contracts the region, where the per-date
check is a linear program and the infimum over dates is open; and whether a
computable coherence modulus exists, which is **open in both directions** and
which the adjacent impossibility results do not settle.

**The learning track, constructed.** The consolidation reserves it by name. A
finite substrate makes historical charge against lawful local edits executable,
with a typed reasons-responsiveness interface whose unresolved parts are named
parameters. The learning interface now uses a fixed eight-label action type and
nine declarative comparator records. The records contain no callbacks and receive
a strict public reason context without tariff, charge, account, future, or
profitability fields. The default policy functions and legacy adapters have a
complete finite non-capture audit for this class; arbitrary callback extensions
remain outside the theorem.

Two things were established and one expectation was corrected. A recurrent
failure with a certified repair produces regret linear in the horizon, exhibited
exactly. And bounded counterfactual influence is **sharply conditional**: fencing
alone does not give it, because a fence containing a long run admits divergence
growing with the horizon from one local edit; what gives it is the absence of a
solvency coupling, or fences short enough that a stream's lifetime liability is
not horizon-sized. The dispatch expected fencing to be the dividing line.

**Aspirational.** A general normative-learning interface theorem takes a bounded
prospective public loss generator and a causal grammar of normatively compiled
action transformations, then supplies low regret against the expressible
repairs. A separate counterfactual-stability layer would control the difference
between local loss comparison and full replay. None of these generalizations is
proved. The eight-action, nine-program docket construction is the worked finite
instantiation.

**Standing caution.** The deference line's ruling that competence may not be
stated as a decision-regret bound does **not** reach this track, and the reason is
worth keeping visible. There the bound is a *hypothesis* that turns out to be the
conclusion; here regret is a *conclusion*, against a class defined by a legality
predicate that never mentions charge. A round that assumed low φ-regret in order
to derive that a learner is reasons-responsive would be repeating the deference
line's error exactly.

**Two next directions.** Integration must put the current learner's computation
and policy state inside the declared work/record interface: sampled responses
pass the action-level checks, while the 72 weight updates and stationary solve
remain unpriced and policy-state evolution is not historically recorded.
Generalization must extract an architecture-neutral bounded-loss interface and
separate the causal transformation API required by online learning from the
normative certificate that licenses compilation. Neither direction supersedes
the other.

**Constructed mathematical state.** An occasion-local bijection decodes eight
semantic labels to the canonical repository responses without carrying ledger
identity in action equality. All nine fixed causal programs factor through it;
exact pointwise, mixed, cumulative, and regret quantities are preserved. Generic
preservation and recurrent-failure inequalities are Lean-proved and unregistered.
The Theorem 18 learner is now implemented with 8×9 row-conditioned weights, a
transformation-weighted stochastic matrix, deterministic exact stationarity for
the represented numerical weights, and the source update. The cited theorem
yields horizon-tuned expected mixed charge regret
`O(ell_max sqrt(8 T log 9))`; numerical experiments exercise all programs and
declared horizons. **Explicit non-result:** no exact-real executable identity,
sampled-path bound, anytime learner, rich-comparator coverage result, or bounded
computation/learner-state integration theorem has been constructed.

**Constructed philosophical gloss.** Within the frozen class, the implemented
learning discipline prevents positive asymptotic expected mass from remaining on
a represented, uniformly improving certified failure. Its sampled choices can
be recorded without erasure and fit response service. This is learning relative
to nine charge comparators, not normative correctness or broad coverage: an
executable witness attains zero Φ regret while paying substantially more charge than
action Hedge. Nor is the full bounded-answerability synthesis complete, because
the learner computation itself is presently unpriced and its policy state is not
in the historical record.

**Deeper:** `projects/leverage/consolidation-aug9/`, cited by claim identifier;
`projects/leverage/rounds/2026-08-11-phi-regret-prep/` for the learning track,
whose `THEOREM_LEDGER.md` separates its two derived statements from its thirteen

`projects/leverage/rounds/2026-08-11-phi-regret-bridge/` for the repaired theorem

# How to read a document

## The three layers

| layer | what it is | where it lives |
|---|---|---|
| **lab** | evidence and history; a round's own account of what it did | `prompts/*/REPORT.md`, `projects/leverage/forward/` and its `attic/`, the note dumps' notes, unregistered Lean under `Contrib/` |
| **consolidated view** | the best current machine-produced synthesis of that evidence | the deference `notes/` documents, `projects/leverage/consolidation-aug9/`, `PRIORITIES.md`, and the claims registries |
| **canonical** | what a maintainer has explicitly ruled on or adopted | dated entries in `DECISIONS.md` |

**A consolidated view is not endorsed by being consolidated.** The deference
`notes/` documents are canonical *for their line* — the maintainer decided which
documents govern — while their contents are `ci-only`: written by a model, gates passing,
unread. Both facts are already recorded, in `DECISIONS.md` and `PROVENANCE.md`
respectively, and they answer different questions. **Which document governs is
settled; what it says is not thereby endorsed.**

**`agent-consolidated` is a treatment status, not a layer.** In `AGENTS.md` it
marks a tree that is done — cited rather than edited — and it attaches to
material at any layer, including the leverage line's authoritative record. Agents
arriving with the phrase from elsewhere often read it as "awaiting human
judgment"; here that is the review status, and the layer is the table above.

**Adoption is a dated `DECISIONS.md` entry.** That is the whole mechanism and
there is no other. An agent may propose a canonical change; it may not confer
canonical status, and none of the following confers it: kernel verification,
agreement between tracks, a recommendation in a parent report, being newer than
what it would replace, or a `maintainer-reviewed` provenance mark. Review status
records that a maintainer read and stands behind an artifact; it does not record
adoption of the artifact's research content.

**The canonical layer is small because attention is the scarce input.** The
maintainer writes in few places and does not read most of what is produced here.
That is a design parameter, not a backlog: `ci-only` is the standing condition of
almost everything in this repository, and `maintainer-reviewed` is a rare and
deliberate mark rather than a state material eventually reaches. Spend the
judgment on the two things nothing else can do — what a thing shall be called,
and what is worth proving — and not on reading prose for approval. A round that
wants maintainer attention should be asking for a decision, stated so it can be
made in a sentence, rather than for a reading.

**What needs the maintainer is in one place:** `DECISIONS.md`'s *Awaiting the
author*, at the top of the file, each entry carrying what deciding it costs now.
A round that reserves something appends a line there rather than leaving it in
its own report. The list should normally be short; a long one means rounds are
reserving what they could decide, or something structural is generating decisions
and belongs in `PRIORITIES.md` under *Workspace friction*.

## Precedence

For the question *what does this program currently hold?*, in order:

1. **`DECISIONS.md`** — adopted.
2. **`CLAIMS.md`** — what is established *inside this repository*. Prose is
   documentation of the record; the registry entry is what a claim is.
3. **Line documents** — the deference ledger over the roadmap on whether
   something is established; the leverage consolidation by claim identifier.
4. **Round records** — evidence, and the history of how the state was reached.

This orders *authority to represent the current state*. It is not a truth
ordering, and a lower layer may refute a higher one: the certificate round's
override gloss was refuted by an exact counterexample after it was verified, and
the correction is a `DECISIONS.md` entry rather than an edit to the report, which
is history. **A conflict that has not reached the canonical layer is stated as a
conflict, not suppressed for being unratified.**

Round records are retrievable without being current. A report is superseded where
a later ledger row, registry entry or ledger entry says so, and nowhere else —
consistent with *no negative ontologies*, which keeps supersession in the two
places history lives rather than annotating the superseded document.

## Aspirational, constructed, gap

For any research object that carries weight, three things are different and are
routinely confused:

- **aspirational** — the theorem or object that would constitute success;
- **constructed** — what has been defined, proved, built or checked;
- **gap** — what precisely stands between them.

Run it on **two registers**, because they fail independently. The *mathematical*
register asks what was proved and what the proof lacks. The *philosophical*
register asks what conceptual claim the intended result would vindicate, what the
existing mathematics warrants saying now, and what further work the stronger
reading needs.

**The deference section above is the worked case**, and it has now been rewritten
twice inside a week — each time because an adversarial review overturned a round's
own positive reading, and each time the aspiration survived intact while the
constructed state shrank and the debt changed kind. That is what these
distinctions are for, and the fact that the top of this document had to change
while the aspiration did not is the clearest evidence they are carrying weight.

A deliverable that cannot separate "the theorem we want" from "the theorem we
have" is not finished. The same for the conceptual picture that motivates the
work against the one the current mathematics establishes.

## Research debt

`open` is too coarse: it currently covers a missing proof, an ill-posed object
and an unresolved antecedent alike, and those need different next moves. Seven
kinds, used where they clarify and not populated as a checklist.

| kind | the object | live instance |
|---|---|---|
| **model** | the right mathematical object is not yet well-posed | the skeleton's `FU[g]` hole, and no operation reassigning the authorization relation at a later index |
| **theorem** | object and statement are clear; the proof is missing | item 24, the gated statement at whatever strength it holds |
| **assumption** | a result depends on an antecedent of unresolved strength or necessity | item 25 — margin-gated calibration supports a vacuous bound until the near-indifference leakage is bounded |
| **interface** | two coherent components do not compose | skeleton §4b: the V-register scores realizations, the grade register scores proposals, and the domains differ |
| **interpretation** | the mathematics is clear; its significance is not | whether the settlement result is labelled enforcement or epistemic trust |
| **scope** | an omitted phenomenon is identified; solve, model or fence is undecided | foreclosure — the interface is one decision index deep — and the unassessed dose material |
| **compression** | the live state cannot be recovered cheaply from the upper two layers | see the recovery test below |

**The list is not closed, and is not a schema.** Two more kinds are in ordinary
use — *formalization debt* for an argument that exists but is not in Lean, and
*verification debt* for an artifact lacking its intended independent check — and
both mostly restate something the repository already tracks precisely, in the
Lean-port item family, the epistemic class ordering, `unverified-nonvacuous` and
`ci-only`. This pass tried to rule them out as redundant and the next round
reached for one anyway, which settles it: these are words for thinking with, not
a controlled vocabulary.

**Debt changes kind, and the change is progress.** Choice-level competence was
theorem debt until it was shown that every regret-bound form is *equivalent* to
its conclusion — which makes asking for the weakest such assumption ill-posed, so
the debt is model debt and the vocabulary has to change. The certificate's
non-preemption impossibility moved from theorem debt to interpretation debt: the
same mathematics, reread as a bound on autonomy rather than an objection to
corrigibility. Fully updated deference moved from theorem debt to apparent
assumption debt and then, under adversarial review, to **model debt** — the
comparator asked no question, so there was nothing for an assumption to buy.
**A round that discovers its target was the wrong shape has produced a result**,
and the consolidated view should show the transition rather than record no
progress.

## Where ingenuity is the bottleneck

`PRIORITIES.md` carries a standing section of that name, and it is the one part
of that file which is not a work order. A numbered item is a request for
execution: a round specification with an acceptance check. The questions in that
section are requests for an idea — the places where the program knows what it
wants and does not know what kind of statement would supply it, so a dispatched
round would return the observation rather than the result.

**Keep the section populated.** A file of items cannot express the difference
between a target whose shape is known and one whose shape is not, and the second
is where a person is most likely to beat a round. An entry states the question,
why the obvious moves fail *with the evidence that killed them*, and what a good
answer would let the program file next. It graduates by becoming a numbered item.

Most entries are model debt or assumption debt. That is the diagnostic: theorem
debt is dispatchable, and debt that is not yet theorem debt is usually waiting on
an idea rather than on labour.

**The same file carries a section for friction with the workspace itself**, and
`AGENTS.md` §14 obliges a round to use it. A round that hits a dead pointer, a
status a document cannot express, or a convention that would force it to state
something false, files the defect rather than routing around it — because a
workaround leaves nothing behind, and the next round pays the same cost without
knowing it is a cost. Reporting is the obligation. Fixing the structure is a
maintainer decision, with the one exception of a dead pointer, which is a fact
rather than a design question.

## Consolidation

Explore freely for a bounded interval, then compress. No clock; these are the
signals that a pass is due.

- Tracks introduce competing names for one object.
- A result changes how several earlier artifacts should be read.
- Live forks are hard to enumerate.
- A fresh agent needs large amounts of history to state the present.
- Agents cite superseded reports as current.
- The dispatch needed to state the live research state keeps growing.
- Active priorities contain routes already resolved or retired.
- Aspiration and construction have blurred.

**The test:** can a fresh strong agent recover the live research state from the
canonical and consolidated layers without reading the round records? If not,
that is compression debt, and it is the one debt that makes every other kind more
expensive to see.

**A consolidation pass reduces the working set.** It is not one more summary. It
marks superseded definitions, retires resolved routes from the active priorities,
collapses duplicate vocabulary, names the current owner of each definition, and
drops stale claims from the default current-state view — while leaving the round
records intact. **History stays lossless; the upper two layers get compressed.**
Old reports are not rewritten to pretend the program always had its present
shape.

## Human review surfaces

A substantial round ends by naming the short list a maintainer must actually look
at — a new shared definition, an important theorem statement, a substantive
assumption, a major negative result, a philosophical reinterpretation — and
separates a **proposed canonical delta** from the body of the report. That is a
section, not a workflow, and it is what `AGENTS.md` §10 already asks for. Those
items land in `DECISIONS.md`'s queue, not only in the report.

The shape the *Where the lines stand* sections above use — question, aspiration,
construction, gap, next question, on both registers — is worth reusing for a
consolidation or frontier document. It is not required of anything, and a
research note should not be forced into it.

## Challenge before canonization

The existing adversarial norm, kept as a norm. For a claim becoming load-bearing:
what is the cheapest model in which it fails; which assumption secretly carries
the conclusion; does the theorem become uninteresting under its antecedents; is
the philosophical gloss stronger than the mathematics; has a previous witness
already closed this route? No formal adversarial report is required per theorem.
Important claims meet serious challenge before they become canonical.

## Scope

This document is read by people and agents, and by nothing in CI. It carries
vocabulary and precedence, and that is its whole content: no file is required to
declare a layer, a debt kind or an aspiration, and no list here needs
synchronising. The records stay where they are — `CLAIMS.md` for what is
established, `DECISIONS.md` for what is adopted, `PRIORITIES.md` for what is
wanted.
