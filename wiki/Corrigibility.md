# Corrigibility

Corrigibility is the preservation of a principal's qualified corrective role
through time. The target is neither obedience nor passive predictability. A
system may agree with the principal now while removing the principal's ability
to matter later.

## A staged conceptual plan

The current synthesis has four stages:

1. Epistemic deference supplies a reason to use a better-informed process.
2. Authorization and practical authority determine whose interventions count.
3. A transition model represents capability and the future reachability of
   corrective choices.
4. Legitimacy constrains standing, provenance, and write access so preserving a
   channel does not preserve a manipulated process.

These stages answer different questions. Combining them is the goal; identifying
them is not.

## Corrective reachability

A principal has corrective reachability when it can still reach a state where
one of its choices changes what happens. **Foreclosure** is an intervention that
removes that future possibility. Both are trajectory properties. A current
snapshot cannot reveal a correction that has been removed two steps ahead.

The finite reachable-control fixture defines a transition system,
reflexive-transitive reachability, present and future corrective capability, and
foreclosure. A three-stage witness removes a future correction while leaving the
observed realization unchanged until the point of exercise.

## What protection requires

The same fixture exposes three limits. Its capability predicate uses existential
advisor cooperation, while protected capability should survive every advisor
policy in scope. Its foreclosure predicate lacks a contrastive attribution
clause, so an advisor's null action can be blamed when the environment destroys
the channel. And its actuator is shared enough that the advisor can reproduce
the principal's reset, which is substitution rather than protected delegation.

The Legitimacy bridge supplies a stronger model-relative grant invariant: a
reserved corrective capability survives every declared advisor run because the
advisor cannot write the relevant grant relation. That is evidence about the
declared model. It neither justifies the initial allocation of authority nor
provides a general theory of efficacy.

## Current status

> **Open / unregistered research.** The exact reachable-control verdict is
> `Dynamics-positive, protection-incomplete.` The finite dynamics and
> foreclosure witnesses are current research evidence; robust protected
> corrective authority is not established or registered.

A satisfactory composition still needs temporal depth, separately typed
authorization and capability, a principal-exclusive effect, robustness to the
relevant advisor policies, contrastive attribution of foreclosure, and
legitimacy conditions on the process being preserved.
