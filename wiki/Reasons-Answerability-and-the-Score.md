This page asks a static question: before a learner can improve, what makes a

## Reasons, warrants, and defeat

The finite model separates three relations. A **committive inference** says what
follows from an undertaking. A **permissive inference** says how entitlement may
transmit. An **incompatibility** says when commitment to one content defeats
entitlement to another. Applicability is itself contestable content: asserting
that a rule applies does not install the rule.

This separation is influenced by Brandom's distinction between commitment and
entitlement and his perspectival scorekeeping. The finite implementation and its
consequences are this program's. Structured argumentation and epistemic-graph
work provide nearby formal comparisons, not this project's statics; see
[Sources](Sources).

## A perspectival score

Let participant `j` supply public acknowledgments and participant `i` supply the
practice used to assess them. The human-facing notation is:

- `C_i(j)`: commitments `i` attributes to `j`;
- `E_i(j)`: entitlements `i` attributes to `j`;
- `K_i(j)`: incompatibilities relevant to `i`'s assessment of `j`.

The diagonal `i = j` is self-assessment, not a universal standard. A challenge
gains force from the challenger's entitlement to an incompatible ground.
Agreement therefore does not settle a matter automatically, and challenge does
not make the challenger an oracle.

**Answerability** begins here: another participant with standing can attribute
consequences and raise a challenge one owes an answer to. Its record and causal
companions—**auditability** and model-relative **efficacy**—are separated on
[Legitimacy](Legitimacy).

## The quantitative book

The book projects qualitative scorekeeping into exact rational constraints; it
is not a second source of normative truth. Logical constraints, settled records,
and active endorsements determine a feasible set of credal states. The credal
interval of a target is the exact minimum and maximum of its probability over
that set.

**Leverage** is the distance by which a forced endpoint clears a decision
threshold. If the feasible set is empty, the model supplies no merits direction;
the inconsistency instead supports an objection against the book. Charges track
declared liabilities, caps bound accumulated quantities, and fencing separates
accounts. Fencing alone does not guarantee that later system effects remain
bounded.

Williams's desirable-gamble approach and Walley's imprecise probability supply
relevant mathematical background for intervals and coherent books. The specific
accounting interpretation is this program's; see [Sources](Sources).
