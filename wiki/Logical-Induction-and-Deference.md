# Logical Induction and Deference

How can a bounded reasoner form rational beliefs about a computation it has not
yet finished? Logical Induction models a sequence of prices for logical
sentences. Efficiently computable traders may trade contracts at those prices;
the criterion prevents any such trader from achieving unbounded upside with
bounded downside. This gives a framework for bounded beliefs about logical facts
before deduction has settled them. See Garrabrant et al. in [Sources](Sources).

## From later computation to epistemic deference

A present sentence can name a later quote or recommendation without making that
future value legally available as a present price-responsive coefficient. This
distinction lets the deference line ask about a present market's opinions over a
later process while keeping explicit which admissibility and resource-separation
assumptions are still owed.

Logical Induction supplies prices, expectations, self-trust phenomena, and
no-exploitation constraints. It does not supply an action type, controller,
authorization relation, practical authority, capability, or efficacy. Those are
additional structures, not interpretations smuggled into a price.

## The motivating decision problem

*Deference Done Better* begins from epistemic modesty. An expert may be worth
consulting without being certain of its own worthiness. Strong reflection can be
too demanding, while weak reflection can count apparent deference to an
anti-expert. Dorst, Levinstein, Salow, Husic, and Fitelson propose comparing the
decisions made from the expert's opinions with decisions made from one's own.
See [Sources](Sources).

The repository inherits that target shape rather than a finished theorem:
connect epistemic deference to a decision comparison, then ask when a bounded
process may rationally use a later or better-informed process. Total Trust,
Value, and tower-style expectations are candidate mathematical faces of the
connection.

## What the audits retain

The current corpus audit retains a two-option finite exact identity connecting
Value and Total Trust, and it keeps a soft asymptotic route as conditional source
evidence. It treats varying-question results as strengthening the forward
epistemic relation rather than creating a separate research line.

The audit also keeps distinctions that limit the claim. A hard-selector
inference from a tower condition to Value fails on selection-referencing
punishment menus. That does not refute the soft construction. Conversely, the
workspace's vanishing-gap mixture and the source's fixed-margin hedge have not
been shown to carry the same hypothesis package.

## Current status

> **Open / unregistered research.** The finite identities, soft route, and
> Logical-Induction-native forcing chain have not been promoted. Cross-process
> calibration, admissibility, world assumptions, and resource-indexed separation
> remain explicit debts.

Even a successful epistemic-deference theorem would not confer practical
authority. A favorable prediction or decision comparison does not say who is
authorized to act, who retains a corrective capability, or whether a demand is
efficacious in a transition model.

**The bridge to accountable claims.**  Logical Induction predicts; the learning layer
that allocates realized experiments among temporally extended continuations is
[Continuation BRIA](Continuation-BRIA), which scores accountable claims on realized block
outcomes only.  A deferential value prediction — the activated future evaluation of the
legitimate-deference stack, or an LI price of it — can potentially be the claim a
continuation hypothesis is held to, and if such claims are near-tight for the
continuation's realized value (the `SLACK` bridge) continuation competence becomes
competence against that value.  This is an interface with a named open realization, not
a theorem; the two wealth accounts stay semantically distinct.

exact statement: Expectation Provability Induction.  The corrigibilization inequality
between the activated securities of a raw continuation and its corrigibilized
counterpart, with the mediation discrepancy, the decline regret and the directional
activation mismatch as its explicit costs, compiles into a bounded combination of gated
logically uncertain variables whose generability certificate is constructed at the
pinned interface; if the architecture's specification makes that combination
nonpositive in every world consistent with the inductor's theory, the inductor's own
expectations respect it asymptotically — before any instance is proved, with no
calibration hypothesis, and uniformly over any polynomial-size efficiently generated
menu through a continuous near-argmax weighting, since a hard argmax is not an
expressible feature.  Logical Induction contributes the passage from validity in every

smallness of the costs, and nothing about non-settling quantities.  Expectation

computable deductive process does not provide.  The deliberative half uses the same theorem once more: the trace-steering package
