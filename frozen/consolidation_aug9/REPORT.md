does not show.

## 3. Coverage: the discard test, closed

The completing pass closed the debt this section previously recorded. The
standing is now uniform across both bodies of work.

| body | source rows | disposition |
|---|---|---|
| settlement-interface era (Theories 11, 12) | — (produced in this era) | 46 claims, all stated and proved here |
| earlier layers (Theories 7 through 10) | **134** in the source tree's ledger | 38 already restated, **93 restated in the completing pass**, 3 deviations quarantined verbatim |

The arithmetic closes exactly: 38 + 93 + 3 = 134. The reconciliation, row by row
with its disposition, is the table in `COMPLETING_PASS_REPORT.md`.

**The earlier figure of 133 was wrong**, and is corrected here. It came from a
regular expression that missed two claim families — the local-to-global and
answerability-ledger namespaces — while counting some section prose as rows. The
exact figure, parsed from the ledger's tables, is 134.

**Three rows were not transcribed**, and none is silently omitted. One records a
conjecture, which the standing rule forbids promoting to a claim; two are
proposals about a future interface whose compatibility the source itself records
as unchecked. Each is quoted verbatim in `DEVIATIONS_ANNEX.md` with its obstacle
and with what a resolution would require.

**What closes the test.** Every transcribed row carries its hypotheses,
conclusion, status and sharpness into a ledger row and into its theory part.
Where the source's fuller witness display lives in a source-tree document rather
than in the row that names it, that document is vendored byte-for-byte under
`vendor/source_theory/` and frozen by digest — 18 documents, including the
source's complete ledger. So every item of retained value is either **restated**
here or **preserved verbatim and flagged** here.

**Consequence, stated plainly: the source tree is no longer needed as evidence
for anything.** It may be changed or deleted wholesale.

**What this does not claim.** The vendored source documents are quarantined
material, not claims of this package: nothing in them is proved, endorsed, or
counted in the ledger, and no theory part cites them. And a transcribed row's
folder-local evidence is the text displayed in its theory part, which for many
drop-contract rows is the source's own compressed description of a witness
rather than a re-derivation of it. Transcription is faithful carry-over; it is
not re-proof, and the ledger's verification column says so on every such row.

## 6. What this does NOT show

**The interface is a definition, and a definition cannot be true.** Every result
is of the form *given an engine satisfying these clauses*. That the clauses
carve the right joint is supported only by the observation that existing
frameworks inhabit different subsets of them, which is suggestive and is not an
argument.

**The parametric composite is a substitution.** It inherits every hypothesis the
source composite inherits, proves nothing the source did not, and does not
establish the composite guarantee — satisfying the interface is that
conditional's antecedent, not the conditional. Its content is that the
antecedent is evaluated rather than assumed.

**The strongest evidence about a non-trivial engine is a reading audit.** Not a
theorem, not machine-checked, not derived from these definitions. It is labelled
in its ledger row, in its theory part, and in the trust audit, and the instance
corollary inherits that standing. Nothing in this package constructs the pair it
describes.

**Machine checking is over displayed finite instances only.** The core-geometry
sweep is four endorsed rows against nine settlements on three worlds; the
trajectory reports are over searched finite trajectories. No asymptotic claim
rests on any of it, and the two open questions are open.

**The typed obstructions are disclosures, not proofs.** A signature that cannot
express a dependence constrains the construction displayed here, not every
possible engine. The faithfulness axiom and checker soundness remain assumed and
named.

**Two questions are open and one of them in both directions.** Persistence of
the core minimum; and whether a computable coherence modulus exists — for which
neither a construction nor an impossibility proof is known, and for which the
two adjacent impossibility results both turn on a desideratum the question does
not mention.

**The deduction-budget clause has no formal content here**, deliberately, and
its two inequivalent readings are not chosen between.

**No incentive, behavioural, convergence, or learning claim is made anywhere.**
Every result says what the record must show. Tariffs, subsidies and liabilities
are accounted quantities, never motives. It would be a mistake to read the
composite's cap as a safety property: it limits what a record can show about
gain under stated hypotheses, and predicts nothing about what any system does.

**The vendored Lean does not cover the new mathematics.** Nothing in Theory 11
or Theory 12 is formalized.

**The discard test is not yet met for the four earlier layers.** See §3. The
source tree remains necessary for about a hundred witness rows, and this package
does not claim otherwise.
