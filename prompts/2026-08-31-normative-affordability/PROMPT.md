# Current candidate theorem factorization

The provisional picture is:

### Settlement

Settlement supplies a filtered learner-visible history

$$
\mathcal F_0\subseteq\mathcal F_1\subseteq\cdots
$$

together with enough semantics to determine:

* which continuation/assessment possibilities remain live;
* how historical control exposures are evaluated as settlement arrives.

A normative controller must be adapted/predictable: round-\(t\) control is chosen from \(\mathcal F_{t-1}\), not after seeing round-\(t\) settlement.

The schematic theory should not assume direct access to hidden “truth.” In LI, the relevant assessment set is something like \(PC(D_N)\); in empirical systems it may be generated by settled report variables.

### Answerability

Answerability determines what remains historically owed.

The rich primitive may be a service/trace specification, not a scalar quota.

For the quantitative learning interface, however, suppose a persistent reason \(r\) induces a predictable nonnegative **claim/exposure process**

$$
c_t^r\ge 0
$$

with

$$
C_N^r:=\sum_{t<N} c_t^r\to\infty
$$

when \(r\) persistently retains a claim to service.

Normalize this into a **normatively owed exposure measure**

$$
\mu_N^r(t)
:=
\frac{c_t^r}{C_N^r}.
$$

Interpretation: where across the first \(N\) rounds did \(r\) genuinely have claims on the reasoner?

The \(c_t^r\) representation may be too restrictive. Determine the minimal object actually needed.

### Control versus action

Keep distinct:

$$
U_h=\text{normative control inputs}
$$

and

$$
A_h=\text{concrete agent-side continuation policies}.
$$

The controlled decision engine converts normative pressure into behavior:

$$
D_h:U_h\to\Delta(A_h)
$$

or some more general analogue.

In LI, \(U_h\) contains added trading pressure; in an online learner it might contain comparator/loss pressure. A norm does not in general directly choose the physical action.

### Actionability

For each reason, we need a semantic connection from normative control to recognizable improvement.

The old one-reason form was:

$$
g_t^r\ge\gamma_r d_t^r,
$$

where \(d_t^r\ge0\) is recognized defect/nonresponse and \(g_t^r\) is the reason-relative advantage presented to the learner.

But **do not assume individual Actionability composes**.

If controls for reasons \(r_1,r_2\) interfere, each reason may be actionable alone while their aggregate pressure destroys both advantages.

Therefore investigate a **joint response correspondence**, provisionally something like

$$
\mathcal J_t(h)
\subseteq
\mathbb R_+^{R_t}
\times U_t
\times Q_t^{R_t}.
$$

An element

$$
(w_t,u_t,q_t)\in\mathcal J_t(h)
$$

would certify that:

* \(w_t^r\) is the amount of actual service assigned to each reason;
* \(u_t\) is the one aggregate normative control actually applied;
* \(q_t^r\) is whatever reason-indexed comparison/challenge Uptake scores;
* this joint intervention really preserves the claimed Actionability inequality for the serviced reasons.

Find the weakest sensible type. Perhaps \(\mathcal J_t\) should not have this form at all.

Construct explicit counterexamples showing why individual Actionability does not imply joint Actionability.

Then identify sufficient composition assumptions such as:

* linearity;
* convexity;
* positive homogeneity;
* separability;
* noninterference;
* superposition.

Determine exactly what traderized LI gets “for free” here because traders/payoffs aggregate linearly, and which parts do **not** generalize.

# The Service Transfer problem

This is one of the central mathematical questions.

Suppose the affordable controller actually services reason \(r\) with intensity

$$
w_t^r\ge0,
\qquad
W_N^r=\sum_{t<N}w_t^r,
$$

and define the normalized **actual service measure**

$$
\nu_N^r(t)
=
\frac{w_t^r}{W_N^r}.
$$

Actionability + Uptake may first give

$$
\mathbb E_{\nu_N^r}[d^r]\to0.
$$

But what we need normatively is

$$
\mathbb E_{\mu_N^r}[d^r]\to0.
$$

The scheduler must not be able to service \(r\) only at the easy/low-defect dates.

A candidate minimal condition is **contiguity**

$$
\mu_N^r\triangleleft\nu_N^r:
\qquad
\nu_N^r(A_N)\to0
\Longrightarrow
\mu_N^r(A_N)\to0
$$

for every sequence \(A_N\subseteq\{0,\ldots,N-1\}\).

Investigate this carefully.

### Target Service Transfer theorem

For bounded defects \(0\le d_t\le D\), is the following correct?

$$
\mathbb E_{\nu_N}[d]\to0
\quad+\quad
\mu_N\triangleleft\nu_N
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\mathbb E_{\mu_N}[d]\to0.
$$

The obvious proof uses the level sets

$$
A_N^\varepsilon=\{t:d_t>\varepsilon\}.
$$

Prove it cleanly.

More importantly, investigate a converse:

> Is contiguity exactly the weakest condition on sequences of probability measures \((\mu_N,\nu_N)\) such that for **every bounded nonnegative defect array**,
>
> $$
> \mathbb E_{\nu_N}[d]\to0
> \Rightarrow
> \mathbb E_{\mu_N}[d]\to0?
> $$

Be careful about triangular-array versus one fixed infinite defect sequence. The naive indicator counterexample may require choosing \(d^{(N)}\) depending on \(N\). Determine the exact statement and the exact necessary-and-sufficient condition in the version relevant to our theorem.

Compare contiguity with stronger sufficient conditions:

$$
\eta c_t\le w_t\le Kc_t;
$$

uniform absolute continuity;

bounded Radon–Nikodym densities;

prefix discrepancy/backlog bounds;

queue stability;

bounded-delay service;

fair scheduling.

Determine which formulation is most natural as the **schematic Answerability-to-Progress interface**.

# Deferral and transport

Same-round servicing is likely too restrictive.

A claim incurred at time \(t\) might legitimately be serviced at time \(s>t\).

Investigate an explicit transport/matching object, e.g.

$$
T_r(t,s)\ge0,
$$

sending owed-service mass at claim time \(t\) to actual service at later time \(s\).

Then ask what additional condition is needed to infer improvement of the **original defect at claim time** from learning at service time.

Likely we need a stability/transport inequality such as

$$
d_{t,r}
\le
L\, d_{s,r}^{\mathrm{transported}}
+
\epsilon_{t,s}.

Try to identify the minimal theorem.

This may connect directly to **cross-era Actionability**: transporting a reason through delay, semantic change, evaluator succession, or ontology revision may be one common mathematical problem.

# Settlement-relative robustness / SafeCert

Affordability should not itself hard-code trader liability.

A controlled learner should expose some replaceable robustness contract:

$$
\operatorname{SafeCert}_{D,\Sigma}(\kappa,C)
\Longrightarrow
\operatorname{Uptake}(D^\kappa).
$$

Here \(\kappa\) is the aggregate causal normative controller and \(C\) an engine-specific certificate.

For LI:

$$
\operatorname{SafeCert}
$$

is instantiated by bounded cumulative downside of the added enforcement trader over every still-plausible world, and the preservation theorem is:

$$
\text{bounded enforcement liability}
\Rightarrow
\text{original LIC preserved}.
$$

Map this exactly to the traderization results.

But investigate the **minimal schematic type**.

Possibilities include:

* an abstract prefix-closed safe class of control histories;
* a cumulative potential/account;
* a dynamic robust risk functional;
* an admissible perturbation ideal;
* some ordered monoid of signed burdens rather than \(\mathbb R\).

Avoid unnecessary finance vocabulary if a weaker abstraction suffices.

### Important robustness question

Should safety be actual-path or robust over all settlement-consistent continuations?

LI strongly suggests the latter:

$$
W(E_{\le N})\ge-B
$$

for every \(W\in PC(D_N)\).

Formulate the analogous history-relative notion:

$$
\operatorname{Risk}_{h_N}(\kappa_{\le N})
$$

with assessment over all continuations still compatible with settlement at \(h_N\).

Investigate what consistency properties this family of risk/safe sets needs as settlement refines the continuation set:

* monotonicity;
* prefix closure;
* time consistency;
* locality;
* concatenation/pasting closure.

Look at dynamic risk measures / viability / superhedging literature only if it actually helps sharpen the theorem. Do not import terminology merely because it sounds related.

# Candidate definition of affordability

Try to vindicate, weaken, or replace the following:

An **affordability witness** is a causal sequence

$$
(w_t,u_t,q_t)_{t\ge0}
$$

plus a settlement-relative safety certificate \(C\) such that:

1. **Joint Actionability**

   $$
   (w_t,u_t,q_t)\in\mathcal J_t(h_{t-1})
   $$

   on every round.

2. **Faithful service**
   for every persistent reason \(r\),

   $$
   \mu_N^r\triangleleft\nu_N^r,
   $$

   or whatever weaker correct Service Transfer condition replaces this.

3. **Aggregate safety**

   $$
   \operatorname{SafeCert}_{D,\Sigma}(\kappa,C),
   \qquad
   \kappa=(u_t)_t.
   $$

Then **Normative Affordability** at a history means that such a witness exists.

Conceptual reading:

> The standing normative load admits a causal joint realization which does not selectively starve the occasions on which its reasons are owed service and whose aggregate settlement-contingent exposure remains inside the controlled learner's certified robustness envelope.

# Sustainable Progress theorem

Try to prove the cleanest possible composition theorem of approximately this form.

* Answerability supplies persistent-reason claim measures \(\mu_N^r\);
* an affordability witness supplies actual service measures \(\nu_N^r\);

  $$
  g_t^r\ge\gamma_r d_t^r

$$
