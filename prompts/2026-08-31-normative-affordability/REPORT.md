## The central composition is the real architecture, with three amendments

The dispatch asked whether the arrows from claim measures through affordability to
claim-weighted Progress are the theorem architecture. They are, and each arrow is
made exact. Three things had to change.

**Contiguity is exactly right and in the wrong place.** It is necessary and
sufficient for the transfer, for triangular arrays — and a settlement-relative
defect is a triangular array, so the strong version is the one in force. But it is
not checkable at a finite horizon, not quantitative, and it is false of
bounded-delay service. The transport theorem derives it from a feasible plan, a
stability inequality and a service-to-claim cap, and gives a quantitative bound.
The definition's clause 2 becomes the plan.

**The conclusion is weaker than boxed.** The composition gives
`limsup E_mu[d] <= eps`, the transport stability error. Only exactly
defect-preserving deferral closes it, and nothing in the premises supplies that.

**The inherited service hypothesis does not support the conclusion.** Bounded-
deficit Surface Fairness is a condition on total mass. A two-surface fair rotation
satisfies it while the service-weighted defect is exactly zero at every horizon and
the claim-weighted defect tends to one half. Of the four Persistent Relevance
interfaces the merged schematic offers, only "a registered surface exposed on every
service date" transfers; the recommended pairing does not. This is the round's one
finding against settled-adjacent material, and it is exhibited rather than argued.

## Joint Actionability

Individual Actionability does not compose when the gain is read against
reason-relative regions, and the countermodel has jointly satisfiable demands — so
conflict is not the mechanism, the choice of scoring set is. Read against the
common region it composes with no convexity and no separability: superadditivity of
an infimum over a smaller set is the entire proof. Traderized Logical Induction
supplies the common scoring set, additivity and per-reason Uptake for free; the one
thing it does not supply is nonemptiness of the intersection, which is covered
compatibility and already carries an exact failure certificate.

## Deletions

`J_t`'s third coordinate; contiguity as a primitive; the safety certificate as an
argument of the definition. The ordered monoid of burdens is not adopted: `R`, or
`R^k` for incomparable budgets, is what every realization uses, and a monoid would
be vocabulary without a consumer.

## The second dispatch: the realization map does not survive intact

The follow-up audited the mapping into the traderized mathematics rather than the
schematic. Three claims are withdrawn and one is repaired;
`FOLLOWUP_REPORT.md` in the round carries the audit and `CORRECTED_STACK.md` the
result.

**Progress is staged, not redefined.** The reserved question had a false disjunct.
The base theorem stays service-weighted; a second theorem, mentioning no engine,
carries it to the claim-weighted conclusion at the transport residual. No settled
definition changes, and the queue entry is answered rather than ruled on.

**Contiguity is not derived by the transport theorem.** What the theorem bounds is
the density of the *transported* claim measure. On the round's own repaired-rotation
trajectory the original pair is not contiguous while the theorem's hypotheses hold
and its conclusion is true, so the two routes are incomparable.

**Uptake comes from the market maker, not the criterion.** The criterion quantifies
over efficiently computable traders; the enforcement position sits in the
price-setting aggregate, where the source says it is not required to be efficiently
computable and, for a coherence-polytope presentation, is not. What bounds its
cumulative value is the maker's cumulative cap less the ordinary trading firm's
floor. The criterion's preservation theorem is *substrate* preservation — the
ordinary trader class's guarantee survives — and supplies no bound on the
enforcement position at all. Per-reason accounting then costs the other reasons'
liability floors, which replaces the round's share condition.

**"Self-financing" is deleted.** The claim that persistent service must be
self-financing interchanged a supremum with a sum. One priced sentence and two
worlds refute it: a norm alternating which world it excludes is robustly
loss-making at every date, keeps its signed account inside a band of width one, and
applies unbounded force. The account is signed and cumulative, so the necessary
condition is worldwise viability; the existing liability-regime taxonomy already
covers the sufficient certificates, and the existence theorem survives as a strong
sufficient result.

What the corrected realization gives is sharper and weaker than the schematic: the
service-weighted defect converges to the service-weighted misfit between the norm
and the still-live worlds. Force drives conformance exactly to the level at which
the norm is consistent with what can still be true.
