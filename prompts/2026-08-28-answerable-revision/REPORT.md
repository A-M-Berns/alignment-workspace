# Answerable Revision — report

**Verdict:** `ANSWERABLE-REVISION-SURVIVES`.

## What was asked

Whether substantive standards for what counts as a revision reason can themselves
change while reasons legitimately incurred under earlier standards remain
diachronically answerable.

They can.

## What survives

At the strict pre-state of `t`, with `W` standing, `E` admissible and
`Promote(W,E) = rho`: for every later `s`, `rho` is incurred and is either
outstanding or resolved — however the policy, the evaluator and the warrant
change in between.

**The proof is three lines and almost all of it is inherited.** Promotion puts
the reason in the frozen package's `opens`; `Incurred` never shrinks; `A1` lets a
claim leave the outstanding set only through an accepted `Resolve`; a warrant
revision is not a resolution and appears in neither fold. Saying this plainly is
part of the result — the round did not discover a new closure argument.

## Where the content actually is

**Promotion permanence (`P1`).** What was promoted at `t` is a function of the
state at `t`. A process that re-derives its own past under present standards
violates it, and **the frozen package would not notice**: its premises govern how
claims leave the outstanding set, and this attack works by preventing them from
arriving. `AR4` is that process, and a test asserts the closure theorem, `A1` and
Grounded Replay are all clean on it while the reason simply never exists.

**Historical validity against current endorsement.** Two predicates, and they
diverge in three fixtures. This lets a process say *that really was a reason we
incurred, and we now reject its force*, while forbidding *our standards changed,
so there never was an answerability relation*.

## The reflective case

`AR10`: a warrant admitting comparisons about warrant protocols promotes a reason
criticising itself; the warrant is then replaced; the reason survives. **No
meta-warrant is needed** — `target` is an ordinary identifier that may name a
warrant, and the module contains no level, rank or hierarchy notion, asserted by
parsing. That was the failure mode the dispatch asked to watch for and it did not
occur.

## The improvement round, recovered

Its demonstration threshold is a promotion rule and its withdrawal challenge an
ordinary instance; its boundary is recovered by the same route. The
specialization is **one-directional**: this round begins at promotion and
inherits nothing about what happens while a repair is live. The two compose by
sitting on either side of the promotion event, not by one containing the other.

## The boundary

`AR9`: a warrant narrows itself before the criticism can be promoted. Nothing
promotes, nothing is answerable, and the narrowing is legitimate under every
premise here and in frozen LE.

This is structurally the same boundary the improvement round found one level down
— a repair retired before it was demonstrated. The same shape at two levels is
some evidence it is the real boundary of this family rather than an artefact, and
it names the next frontier: **reflective openness**.

## What this round does not establish

That the `Warrant` tuple is minimal. It is the smallest that carried AR1-AR10,
which is not the same thing.

That a defeat is any good. `AR6` accepts a bare refusal and is answered.
