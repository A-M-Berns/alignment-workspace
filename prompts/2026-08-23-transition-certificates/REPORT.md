## Findings

1. PR #48's incompatibility semantics was defective in exactly the direction
   the dispatch predicted, inverted: an adopted ternary `Incomp` implied
   every pairwise conflict, so joint impossibility with pairwise
   compatibility was unrepresentable. Repaired with the set-level
   `Conflict_B(S)`; binary rebuttal is its two-element case and is
   deliberately incomplete for conflict. `Incomp` itself was the right
   abstraction.
2. Double negation was constructible as a distinct claim; it is now
   unconstructible, so the involution holds by there being no second object.
   The floor resists shrinking further: encoding negation as paired atoms
   under learnable incompatibility would make the correction reading of
   staged applicability itself revisable content.
3. `c@n` was a bare tag. It now has prefix-determined external semantics —
   the case-restricted arrival prefix — under which two histories with equal
   case restriction induce equal views, and delayed evidence extends later
   views while grounding ordinary corrections about old staged claims. The
   record-time choice survived its attack; `Case` stays thin.
4. Applicability-in-source is now enforced well-formedness over constitutive
   `instantiates` declarations, refused at minting when the staged `App`
   source is absent. The unconditional form is too strong — seed and brute
   reasons declare nothing — so the condition is declared-implies-cited, and
   basis-declaration requirements are record-side policy. Occurrences gain a
   constitutive birth index, without which strict pre-state citation is
   inexpressible.
5. The certificate needs exactly three citation sorts — grounds (particular
   occurrences), license (one prior scoped authority act), lineage (existing
   commitments) — checked strictly against the pre-state prefix. Citing
   conclusions instead of occurrences fails on the equal-content fixture;
   pooling the sorts fails on the conflation kill test.
6. No-self-grounding is a theorem, not a postulate: the checker has no
   self-grounding clause, and every self-certification and mutual-licensing
   attack is refused by strict priority and genealogy codes alone. Of the
   dispatched five postulates, two fold into strict pre-state citation, one
   is the record's account law, and one decomposes across certificate,
   substrate, and record. Three principles survive, each with a minimal
   failure witness: strict pre-state citation, constitutive immutability,
   answerability continuation.
7. Basis loss after certification is detected at the frozen citation in all
   five dispatched variants — substitute support, post-certification
   reclassification, later-stage persistence over a withdrawn earlier stage,
   and a defeated underlying incompatibility included — and the
   frozen-citation locality of the report is exhaustively checked over every
   stance on a small universe.
8. `Licensed(S,d,a)` is witnessed by a valid certificate and is nontrivial:
   grounds-only and authority-only fixtures fail on disjoint codes, so
   licence is not derivable from support. What it establishes is licence
   within the current accountable practice. The exact remaining blockers are
   named: the `May`-rule-to-scope compiler is record-side work the
   `AuthorityAct` stand-in does not do; defeater-uptake completeness moved
   to the record's inquiry obligations rather than vanishing; `Due` is
   untouched.
9. The checker does not adjudicate stance coherence: a licitly certified
   transition into a criticizable stance is accepted and left to the
   criticism machinery, since refusing it would smuggle a norm into the
   gate.
10. Closure phase, subtraction: every surviving primitive's removal loses a
    fixtured distinction — notably, compiling hyper-sources through
    conjunction vertices is nonconservative (it invents claims the learner
    never adopted and reason applications the practice never performed),
    and removing the floor makes a correction unstatable as a correction.
    The multi-hypergraph-plus-stance characterization is accurate only as a
    labeled structure carrying `born` and `instantiates`.
11. Closure phase, addition: all nineteen candidate primitives classify as
    existing content, record facts, derived queries, or learner policy —
    none as genuinely missing — so the interface has a negative boundary.
    All five consumers answer through the public interface; the one honest
    qualification is that this round's own consumer forced `born` and
    `instantiates` before closing.
12. Every remaining open problem classifies as semantics-open (App truth
    conditions against the view, view completeness, content typing) or
    policy/theory-open (revision, priority, uptake, the rule-to-scope
    compiler, `Due`, learning); none is representation-open. Freeze
    recommended, with reopening gated on a concrete inexpressibility
    artifact.
