# Answerable challenge service — report

**Verdict:** `CHALLENGE-CONTINUITY-SURVIVES-SERVICE-IS-THE-NEW-CONTENT`.

## The pinned episode survives

The round's central hypothesis, and it holds. An open challenge is adjudicated
under the terms fixed at its registration. The moving-goalpost attack —
threshold 10, evidence reaches 9, threshold becomes 100, evidence reaches 90,
threshold becomes 1000 — never promotes anything when terms float and promotes at
`t = 11` when they are pinned. The two fixtures differ in exactly one flag, and a
test asserts that.

Pinning binds the **episode**, not the process. `terms_in_force(25)` is still the
new threshold for anything registered then; only the open episode keeps its own.
The legitimate route for moving an open challenge onto new terms is an explicit
transfer, which frozen carry already expresses.

## Service is the new content

The clearest result. `ACS5` leaves a challenge open forever with opportunity
recurring forever and nothing ever served. Every premise in this round, every
premise in the frozen package, `Answerability Resolution` and `Grounded Replay`
are all satisfied. The claim sits outstanding — which is `A1` working exactly as
designed, and is precisely the problem.

So the genuinely new mathematical burden is service rather than another closure
theorem. `S1` is deliberately the weakest form the consumer needs: unbounded
cumulative service, no rate, no fairness, no deadline. The starvation-debt
quantity is reported for comparison and is **not** claimed to be the right
measure.

## What it does not solve, stated rather than engineered around

`ACS9` installs, before any challenge exists, the rule *criticisms threatening
authority are rejected*. Temporally clean, inferentially relevant, and every
structural condition here accepts it.

> Temporal and inferential integrity do not imply substantively non-self-sealing
> criticism.

No premise was added to make it fail, because every candidate considered would
also have refused `ACS6` — a standing resource-priority rule deferring one
challenge for another — which is legitimate. That is the Reflective Openness
frontier and it is not closed here.

`ACS11` shows the machinery is reflective *in form*: a challenge targeting the
sealing rule is registered, served and promoted with no meta-hierarchy. It does
not show such a challenge would be admitted.

## The closure half is inherited, for the third time

Challenge continuity is two lines of frozen `A1`. This is the third round running
whose closure argument is a restatement of the same premise over a different
object, and the document says so in three places rather than presenting it as
discovery. The load-bearing content across the three rounds has been the
**arrival** conditions — `C1` here, `P1` one layer up — the episode discipline,
and service. The recurring vulnerability is not erasure but non-arrival, and
frozen Legitimate Evolution structurally cannot see it.
