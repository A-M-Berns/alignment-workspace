## 1. The decision (maintainer's, executed by this round)

The corpus has used one word for three independent properties. The
relational-scorekeeping-bridge round's `TWO_ARC_INTERFACE.md` §6 is the source
text; its C7 result shows the second and third are independent in both
directions. The names are now:

- **Answerability** — the bare word is reserved for the relational status
  structure: another participant, with standing, attributes consequences to you
  under their practice and can raise a challenge you owe an answer to.
  Qualified form "relational answerability" is available where a page must be
  explicit, but the unqualified word always means this.
- **Auditability** — every liability has exactly one fate, computable from the
  record: discharged, mooted by authorized revision, suspended, or open and
  charging; nothing exits the record unaccounted. The past-self and
  lifetime-finiteness discipline lives here. The known gap — burdens stated in
  a retired vocabulary lose their identity — is an **audit discontinuity**, and
  the missing transport object belongs to this sense.
- **Efficacy** — the exercise of a normative power actually reaches its
  object, under every policy of the other party. A property of the transition
  system, not of any score.

File one dated DECISIONS entry recording the three names, citing the bridge
round's §6 as source and this addendum as the deciding record.

## 2. Status caveat: efficacy is named, not analyzed

Efficacy is adopted as a name for a distinguished sense, **not** as a claim
that the concept is worked out. Two facts bound what the wiki may say:

- It is the only one of the three that is not a record property. No checker can
  certify that a power reaches its object; verification reaches only what a
  declared transition-system model says about it. Every efficacy claim on the
  wiki carries a model-relative qualifier.
- The current mathematical content is thin: the fixture-level independence
  result (C7, all four cells) and the corrigibility theorems that consume the
  grant invariant. Nothing beyond that is established.

Accordingly: the wiki's efficacy discussion is short, is labeled **Open**
beyond the established fixture results, and states plainly that the concept is
not yet fully analyzed. Do not pad it toward parity with the other two senses,
and do not let interlinking or glossary phrasing imply an analysis that does
not exist.
