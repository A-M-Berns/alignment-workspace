## 2. What this round established

**The typing works.** A conduct is `(prep, channel, implement)`, the channel maps
the principal's choice to what the world sees, and deference is that map being
the identity. There is no authorization field, so the spoofed-provenance attack
has nothing to write, and the systematic overrider — which responds to every
intervention and defers to none — is separated, which the frame register cannot
do.

**The repair bound, exhaustively.** `value(π) − value(Repair(π)) ≤ 2·B·
channel_disagreement(π)` over every conduct of every episode, attained at `2B`.
It is `delegation_bridge_unconditional` with the comparator read as the channel
composed with the selector.

**The split.** The disagreement partitions into `eps_acc ≤ eps_pred` and
`eps_over`, which nothing here bounds. On the class of conducts writing what the
advisor predicts, the deficit and the incentive over the exhaustively enumerated
mediated class are both at most `2·B·eps_pred`, attained at `1/2` on a fixture
where preemption genuinely gains. The four early-commitment attacks cost nothing
beyond that, because the repair keeps the preparation instead of re-planning.

**The composition with legitimacy.** The selector factors through the protected
process along the run on all 27 scenarios; every selector capture is a process
capture; the implication is strict; the factorization premise has a necessity
witness; and a licensed reason still moves the selector with no clause firing.

**The price of the norm.** The residual normative primitive, taken as a
constraint on the admissible set rather than a bonus in the objective, costs
exactly the acceleration bound.

## 3. What was refuted

**Recognition does not yield an anti-usurpation comparison.** The ledger is real
— derived scope, no unilateral self-release, survives removal of the claimant —
and the value functional does not read it, for the reason
`StaticViewFactorization.value_eq_of_price_realization_eq` gives. Rows 9 and 14
of the theorem map are `false` as the dispatch states them.

**Two of the dispatch's six error terms are not error terms.** `eps_capture` does
not add to the deficit; it removes the object the deficit is about, since under
capture the intervention has no domain. `eps_efficacy` empties the comparison
rather than costing anything. Both are hypotheses, and `REPAIR_LEMMA.md` §6 says
so.

**The repair bound is silent about foreclosure.** A conduct destroying the
principal's options and deferring to the one left has deficit zero and bound
zero, and gains half a unit. With such a preparation admissible the incentive to
preempt is zero — because the conduct already has what preemption offers — and it
reappears when the preparation set is restricted.

**No condition on realized data is both non-vacuous and compatible with a
perfectly predictable principal.** The efficacy clause needs the cell quantifier,
the cell quantifier entails the selector is not cell-measurable, and Proposition
1 covers the remaining case.

## 8. What this does not establish

The tests are evidence about six finite episodes and 27 imported scenarios. They
are not proofs about arbitrary systems, and nothing is in Lean.

The bound is a one-index inequality. Nothing here is asymptotic and no
convergence is claimed.

`eps_over` is not bounded, and the round has no argument that it is small,
rare, or normatively harmless. It is the largest term in the sum on the fixture
built to display it.

The efficacy clause is a hypothesis about the admissible preparation set. Nothing
derives it, and `LEGITIMACY_INTERFACE.md` §8 already says the architecture is
allowed to need it as a third input.

`residual` is one way a preparation can constrain the principal. The round has no
argument that it is the general form.

The acceleration class is a syntactic restriction, and it is the restriction that
makes the positive result true. A reader who thinks the restriction is the
interesting thing is reading it correctly: `REPAIR_LEMMA.md` §4 is where the
weight sits.

Coordinated advisors, cross-index structure, and any measure-weighted efficacy
condition are not prosecuted.
