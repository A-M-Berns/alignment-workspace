# Addendum to the third follow-up on PR #145 — the adviser as one of her parts

Sent on 2026-11-12 while the third follow-up was running. It adds one question to that
dispatch and changes nothing else in it. The idea below is the prompt author's guess, which
the maintainer found interesting and asked to have considered; it is not part of the account
of §1 and is not to be written into `decomposition/ACCOUNT.md` as if it were.

## The idea

The account of §1 places the adviser wholly outside the principal, and relates her parts to
one another only through her overall goal. Suppose instead that her AI adviser is treated
as one of her parts: something she has taken on as part of how she pursues her goal, with a
purpose fixed the way any part's is, as the feature of its output on which the rest of her
depends. A stranger (an advertiser) stays outside her.

A possible further element, held more tentatively: part of what a part is for is keeping
the conditions the other parts need, so that a part's normal conditions are, apart from the
outside world, what the other parts are for providing.

## What to do

After statements 1–9 are done, and only then, write `decomposition/ADVISER_AS_PART.md`. For
each difficulty the pass has by then found in the account of §1 — the soft spots listed in
§1, the divergences on the bank, and whatever statements 1–9 refuted or left open — say
whether treating the adviser as a part resolves it, leaves it unchanged, or makes it worse,
with the reason and, where the model permits, a computed case. In particular:

1. **A part that achieves its own target while harming her.** Whether manipulation by her
   adviser is then an instance of this case, and whether that gives "never opposed" a
   definition.
2. **The comparison action.** Whether the adviser's best move by its own purpose as a part
   supplies the comparison that "the adviser widened a gap" lacks in statement 6.
3. **Two standards.** Whether a stranger and her own adviser come out held to different
   standards, and how these compare with the arm's-length and faithful-adviser standards of
   the first follow-up (`steering/`).
4. **Oversight as maintenance.** Whether her correcting the adviser is expressible as the
   same thing as her maintaining her own parts, and whether "the adviser stays correctable"
   then follows from the adviser serving its purpose or must be added.
5. **Determinacy.** Whether the adviser's purpose as a part is fixed by the constraints in
   the model of statement 1, or is free.

Test the further element (a part's purpose includes the conditions other parts need) only
as far as these five questions call for it, and say whether the definitions it gives are
circular.

Three objections to answer directly: that calling the adviser her part assumes the
alignment the account is meant to secure, since an AI has a developer, other users and
possibly aims of its own; that if the adviser is her part, its going round her judgment is
one part doing another's work and the account loses any ground to object; and that the
mutual definition of purposes and normal conditions is circular.

The report section gets a short subsection for this, with one line per difficulty and the
answers to the three objections. A finding that the idea solves nothing is a result. If
time is short, this addendum is the first thing dropped; say so.
