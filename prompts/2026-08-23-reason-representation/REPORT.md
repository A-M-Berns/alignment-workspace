## Findings

1. The dispatched structure fails three prosecution points as stated: no way
   to state `¬App` or pose rebuttal (negation floor forced); no way for
   evidence to enter sources (receipt sort forced); nothing connecting an
   undercutter to what it disables (`Enabled_B(e) ⟺ s(e) ⊆ B` survives only
   under the applicability-in-source convention).
2. Staged `App(σ,c@n)` is forced by the changed-world versus corrected-belief
   distinction, with `n` a record time. Its cost is an applicability frame
   problem: persistence must be earned by ordinary defeasible schemas, and
   making it automatic would hide substantive normative work.
3. The right substrate abstraction is not a maintenance system: five total
   stateless queries — `Enabled`, `Reasons`, `Dependents`, `Explain`,
   `LostBasis` — over (structure, stance, transcript). Hypothetical queries
   are the same functions at another stance. JTMS and ATMS functionality
   reappears as one named stance policy plus caching.
4. Nogood-hood is policy-relative once incompatibility is reified and
   learnable, and a newly supported incompatibility claim flips previously
   good environments; classical ATMS consistency caching is unsound in this
   regime. Two label backends were checked extensionally equal on the finite
   fixtures.
5. Doyle's odd loops become stance-policy pathologies rather than substrate
   failures: the self-undercutter is total under every stance and simply
   reported.
6. `Case` needs only opaque persistent identity; questions live in docket
   items, staged views carry the temporal load, procedural provenance `T`
   generates no reasons, and case split/merge is record acts over new
   identities.
7. `Inst` never feeds enabledness; sources and targets are constitutive, so
   schema reclassification mints new occurrences instead of rewiring what
   past reliance depended on. Schema organization stays one graph — no
   second-order hypergraph.
8. Incompatibility splits into a constitutive logical floor and reified
   n-ary `Incomp` claims that are learnable and defeatable; rebuttal is
   derived and stance-relative; a stance violating an adopted incompatibility
   is criticizable, not unrepresentable. Binary incompatibility is
   insufficient.
9. Reason priority has no seat in a substrate that never adjudicates; it is
   reified as ordinary claims, with Horty's variable-priority default
   theories as verified precedent.
10. `Due` stays record-side. `Licensed` is where the reason state pays rent:
    certificates can cite occurrence identities whose `App` sources supply
    the finite defeasible dependency keys the internal-answerability kernel's
    R7 requires, narrowing that memo's blocker 1.
