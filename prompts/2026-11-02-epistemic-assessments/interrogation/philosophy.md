# Philosophy layer and cross-line structure — audit of state/concepts.json

Scope: the philosophy concepts, the eleven goal/thread entries, and every line-to-line
edge. All paths relative to the repo root. Each item marked GROUNDED carries the path(s)
that establish it; nothing here rests on memory.

The headline: the network records each line's internal structure well and the
philosophy-to-construction edges inside the normativity line well. What it almost
entirely misses is **consumption across lines**. The legitimate-deference stack runs on
the account calculus, Robust Openness and authorship; the corrigibility nucleus is gated
by legitimacy and bounded by the openness obstructions; the corrigibility thesis's
legitimacy criterion *is* the deference line's trust mathematics. None of that is an
edge. The lines connect only through the thin chain
`normativity-line -> legitimacy-line -> deference-line -> corrigibility-line`, which
reads as org-chart adjacency, not as the actual flow of hypotheses.
