## Findings

1. The account checker is equivariant under coherent party relabeling. A minimal
   diachronic history and Alice/Bob history receive the same verdict.
2. The earlier claimant conclusion needed a type distinction. Creditor is not a
   common account-kernel primitive, but it is constitutive of Singh's directed
   social commitment. Creditor still differs from beneficiary, source, and
   evidentiary audience.
3. Respondent identity is not inert metadata. Actor-sensitive specifications and
   transfer authorization are independently necessary.
4. Private Alice/Bob reason states do not determine one `BasisLost` verdict. The
   reusable trigger must read a recognized state indexed by answerability context.
5. Raised challenge and successful challenge differ. Only an admitted challenge
   has the same formal effect as an admitted diachronic defeater.
6. Delegation is semantic account transport plus authorized role change, not
   ordinary transport alone.
7. Mutuality needs no new account primitive, but conflicts and feasibility remain
   separate.
8. Answerability, authorship/non-capture, and cooperation separate in both
   relevant directions.
9. Singh's `G` supplies the named social/normative and appeals locus but not a
   formal time-indexed state, evidence interface, or adjudication transition.
   The reusable `kappa` is therefore `G` plus such a protocol, not a renaming.
10. Create/Discharge/Cancel/Release/Delegate/Assign replace generic disposition
    and transfer predicates at the wrapper, while compiling to generic fresh,
    closure, and role-changing rewrites in the account kernel.
11. Context power and semantic adequacy remain independent. A policy can make an
    operation institutionally effective without establishing environmental or
    stronger normative correctness.
12. Singh provides named directed commitments, contexts, operations, and policy/
    metacommitment structure. He does not provide immutable account lineage,
    per-input semantic transport, undertaken basis receipts, basis-loss
    reopening, or a composition theorem; his conclusion leaves model-theoretic
    semantics for future work.
