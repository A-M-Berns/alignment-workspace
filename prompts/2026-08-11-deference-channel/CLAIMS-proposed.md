# Proposed registry entries — simulator-substitution divergence witnesses

**Proposed, not registered.** `CLAIMS.md` does not exist in this repository yet, and
filing a registry entry is a maintainer act under the demand-gating rule. These four
entries are in registry format so the house checkers can adjudicate them today:

```sh
python3 -m checkers.run prompts/2026-08-11-deference-channel/CLAIMS-proposed.md
```

All four instances are the same finite model at four settings; the model, its
readings and what each instance shows are in `REPORT.md` §1 and §6. The point vector
is, in order,

`P(w0) P(w1) P(w2) | X(wi,pj) | v+(wi,pj) | v^+(wi,pj)`,

each block in the order `(w0,p0) (w0,p1) (w1,p0) (w1,p1) (w2,p0) (w2,p1)`.

The constraint rows encode, in this order: the credence is a probability vector and
is pinned to the values used as coefficients in the valuation rows; every valuation
carrier is bounded by `B = 1`; the quantity equals the grade (except where the entry
declares otherwise); `v^+` is `t(n)`-measurable for the coarse reading of `F_0`; `v^+`
agrees with `v+` off the critical cell `{w2}` and differs on it; `J` and `J-hat` are
the least maximisers claimed, by strict margins; and the two valuations and their gap
are the stated exact rationals.

Names are provisional (`AGENTS.md` standard 6); `REPORT.md` §8 lists them.
