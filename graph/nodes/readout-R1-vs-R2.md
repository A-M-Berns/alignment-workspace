## Statement
Each option is scored by the humans of the terminals where it was taken (the
diagonal readout), rather than every option by the humans of the one realized
terminal.

## Source
Stated from its content: a floor-rule objective in which the humans of each
terminal score the options leading there admits two readouts. Under the
diagonal readout the floor, the lexical tier and the gap steer; under the
realized-terminal readout they cancel, and the floor rule's protection then
depends only on the off-diagonal scores.

## Notes
A question about which readout the floor-rule objective is defined with;
stated as the diagonal reading. Under sealing with selection-blindness nothing hinges on it.
