## Statement
Theorem 1 of the off-switch game (Hadfield-Menell et al.) is a corollary of a
general value-of-information theorem, with the agent's gain from deferring
equal to the smaller of the positive and negative parts of expected utility and
strictly positive exactly when utility has mass on both signs.
