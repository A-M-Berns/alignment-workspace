## Statement
No logical-induction market supplies the coalition's decision layer: a
Critch-faithful policy uses each member's own expectation with the aggregate as
bookmaker, while using the aggregate's expectation is the futarchy form and
leaves Pareto optimality.

## Source
Critch 2017, Toward Negotiable Reinforcement Learning (the Pareto-optimal
policy uses each member's own beliefs, with weights shifting by each member's
predictive success), set against aggregate-logical-inductor: the market
supplies prices, not a policy, and a policy that maximizes expected utility
under the aggregate's prices is the futarchy form, which Critch's theorem does
not cover. An AI derivation in prose; no proof is in this repository.
