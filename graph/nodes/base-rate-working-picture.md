## Statement
A calibrated agent complies with a press exactly when its probability of being
wrong given the press is at least c/(c+h), so compliance survives capability
growth only while the ratio of false to true presses stays at or below the odds
of the agent being right times the ratio of the harm h to the cost c, eps the
agent's residual error.
