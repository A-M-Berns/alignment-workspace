## Statement
For an observable coherent expert, soft Total Trust of the novice over a class
of bounded variables is equivalent to asymptotic Epistemic Value over the same
class under the generalized strictly proper scoring rules.

## Source
Dorst, Levinstein, Salow, Husic and Fitelson, "Deference Done Better",
Philosophical Perspectives 35 (2021), Theorem 3.2 (totally trusting an expert
with respect to a question is equivalent to expecting its estimates of
quantities determined by the question to be more accurate).
