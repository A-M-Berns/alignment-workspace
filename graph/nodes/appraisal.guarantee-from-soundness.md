## Statement
The guarantee from soundness: on a finite space with a probability weight, where for each move what she observes is determined by the adviser's information together with her valuation of that move, any value some move attains in expectation, less her expected regret by her own lights and the square root of twice the summed miscalibration of her valuations given the adviser's information and themselves, is at most what she attains.
