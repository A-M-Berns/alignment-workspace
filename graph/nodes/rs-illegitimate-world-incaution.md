## Statement
Both the floor rule and conditioning on legitimacy ignore harms of the agent's
actions that fall on illegitimate worlds, so their choices resemble those of an
agent deluded into certainty of legitimacy.

## Source
projects/deference/faf-cleanroom-2026-10-02/packages/legit-neg-static/legit-neg-static-report.md,
targets 8 and 9 (A0, A1): in the sealed regime both proposals read scores only
on legitimate terminals, and the regret against unconstrained expected utility
is bounded by the harm bound times the probability of illegitimacy, with an
instance attaining it.
