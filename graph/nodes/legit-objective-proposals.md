## Statement
The legitimacy-weighted decision objective the programme adopts defends
legitimacy strategically when it is threatened and is cautious about the
consequences of its actions in illegitimate worlds.

## Source
The five proposals are the variant matrix formalized in
projects/deference/faf-cleanroom-2026-10-02/packages/legit-neg-static/legit-neg-static-report.md,
Target 3 (P1 the floor, P2 conditioning, P3 graded, P4a lexical and P4b
cardinal gap, P5 the decomposition), with the verdicts of that package and of
legit-neg-pricing and legit-neg-dynamic beside it. The two requirements in the
Statement, strategic defence of legitimacy when it is threatened and caution
about consequences in illegitimate worlds, are the filing session's statement
of what those results test.

## Notes
The crux of the legitimacy step: the Statement is the two requirements,
strategic defence and caution in illegitimate worlds, as one claim, and the
pictures are the five objectives plus a residual. Three rows are conditioned on
the packages' strongest results (the deterrence floor, the news-management
theorems) or on the ordering crux crux-illegitimate-ordering (whether an
illegitimate world is ever better). The packages' register: no result refuted,
every informal reading of a proposal unvetted. The same five proposals are filed
by the corrigibility branch under its own id; the merge keeps one.
