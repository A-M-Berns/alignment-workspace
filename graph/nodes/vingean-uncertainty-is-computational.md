## Statement
Vingean uncertainty, trusting a decision procedure to perform well on general
principles rather than by predicting it precisely, rules out computationally
omniscient models, so it is uncertainty about computations.

## Source
projects/deference/faf-cleanroom-2026-10-02/sources/unbounded-embedded/01-primary/summer-research-plan-2026--research-ideas-may-2026.md:
"it is crucial that it be based on "Vingean" reasoning; that is: we must trust
decision procedures to perform well on general principles, not by virtue of
being able to predict them precisely" and "the Vingean principle rules out
models such as those based on reflective oracles, where beliefs are
computationally omniscient".
