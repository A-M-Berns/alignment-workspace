## Statement
Petersen's theory of abstraction, Wentworth's natural abstraction and natural
latents, and Eisenstat's condensation are related precisely: Petersen's theory
to Wentworth's, natural latents to condensation, and Petersen's theory to
condensation, each relation stated as a reduction or a complementarity.
