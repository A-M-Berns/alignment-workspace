## Statement
Each published argument that corrigibility is anti-natural rests on at least
one assumption that fails for an agent whose utility on the algebra containing
the overseers' corrections depends on those corrections.

projects/deference/corrigibility-note-dump-2026-10-01/value-change-as-epistemic-update.md,
