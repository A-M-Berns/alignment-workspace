## Statement
Corrigibility is a property of preferences: the agent prefers every course of
action it knows to be faithful to the allocation of authority over every course
it knows to be unfaithful, accepts a risk of unfaithfulness only at a fixed
exchange rate, and has the fidelity score as its canonical objective, so that
fully updated deference is dissolved by the typing rather than repaired.
