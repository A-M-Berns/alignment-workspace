## Statement
The system satisfies the coherence conditions of the money-pump theorems with a
utility over action histories, so it has no objective over outcomes that
implies a convergent instrumental subgoal.

## Source
Shah 2018, "Coherence arguments do not entail goal-directed behavior" (AI
Alignment Forum): coherence theorems say nothing about which utility function,
so they cannot supply the assumption for free.
