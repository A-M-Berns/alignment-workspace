## Statement
The li-deference model extends to an embedded-agency model that says when to
model something as an agent, where agent boundaries lie, and how agents
cooperate and merge into one agent with communication channels in the role of
the market.

## Source
projects/deference/note-dump-2026-08-11/notes/li-deference.md, Some other open
questions, Embedded Agency (human-written by the maintainer): "Can this model
be extended into a more serious embedded agency model, containing an account of
when we should model something as an agent? Does this model of agent
identification also say something about agent boundaries? Can we model agents
cooperating and merging into one agent, perhaps with communication channels
playing the role of the market?"

## Notes
The legitimacy-reasons branch lists this open question of li-deference.md and
links it to this programme, which files it. The Stage 5 representation theorem
answers its first part (when to model something as an agent) and the coalitions
sub-frame under Stage 6 its last part (merging through a shared market); that
reading of the connection is the filing session's.
