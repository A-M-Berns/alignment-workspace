## Statement
What is the policy-level analogue of Total Trust for an AI trained to act and
trusted directly, rather than an estimator run through an argmax rule, and
does the bounds-transfer and self-endorsement factorization exist without a
selection to condition on?

## Source
projects/deference/note-dump-2026-08-11/wiki/open-problems.md, Research
direction: trust the policy, not the decision rule: "model an AI trained to
act — a policy the humans can trust directly — rather than an estimator run
through an argmax rule"; "what is the policy-level analogue of Total Trust,
and does an analogue of the bounds-transfer/self-endorsement factorization of
[total-trust-implies-value] exist without a selection to condition on? It is
not known whether this is doable".

## Notes
A question: Value is instrumental and no decision rule is optimal across
embedded environments, so any argmax-shaped statement inherits a decision
theory; the shape of a statement that does not is unknown. A shared claim of
the bounded-agent programme's sub-frame: while it stands as a question it has
infinite cost to establish and no ranked unit, and the steps that need it can
only be broken or narrowed.
