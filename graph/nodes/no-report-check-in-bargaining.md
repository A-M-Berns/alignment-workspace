## Statement
The Geometric UDT bargaining rule checks no hypothesis's reports against its
bets, so a divergence between them is not penalized.

## Source
The maintainer's post Geometric UDT
(https://www.lesswrong.com/posts/Gmaq7vMj9NWLyQkEx/geometric-udt), where the
bargaining is over reported utilities; no countermeasure is specified in the
post; the finite form of the aggregation is geometric_udt_identity in
projects/deference/faf-cleanroom-2026-10-02/packages/udt-endorse-policy/udt-endorse-policy-report.md.
