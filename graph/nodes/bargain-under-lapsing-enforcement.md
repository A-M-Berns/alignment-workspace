## Statement
An updateless coalition member keeps a bargain struck while the humans held
leverage after the leverage lapses exactly when the bargain's benefit exceeds
the lapsed-branch cost by the stated ratio, with the humans' live cooperation
as the commitment device, for LI or BRIA agents as in the finite model.

## Source

updateless route honors the bargain iff the benefit is at least the lapse ratio
times the cost (an AI derivation in prose; no proof is in this repository).
