## Statement
The statement of record of `corrigibility.feedback-transfer` formalizes the registered claim as the registry reads it: the feedback hypothesis transfers: if the average excess of the estimates over the scores built from a feedback sequence is eventually below every positive bound, and the average excess of the feedback over the evaluation has limit superior at most zero, the post's Unbiasedness From Feedback holds for the scores built from the evaluation.
