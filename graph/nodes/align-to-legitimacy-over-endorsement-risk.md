## Statement
An AI aligned to a notion of legitimacy designed to override endorsement in
selected cases imposes its builder's guess at legitimate values unilaterally.

## Source
Demski, Meaning & Agency (LessWrong, 2023-12-19), section on legitimacy:
"anyone who purposefully built a
superintelligent AGI aligned to some notion of legitimacy designed to trample
on endorsement in select cases would be unilaterally imposing their own guess
at legitimate values. I claim this is bad behavior."

## Notes
A risk story from that post, filed with role risk-story in this frame:
the lexical and floor objectives rank illegitimate worlds by the AI's
legitimacy concept, which this story says must not override endorsement. Its
outcome class is unplaced (no human has set one); the corrigibility branch may
place it.
