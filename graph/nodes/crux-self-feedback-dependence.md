## Statement
The asymptotic collapse of the trust principles holds for logical inductors
whose feedback about their own beliefs is poor.

## Source

computable and knowing its own mathematical definition; or to put it a
different way, having good feedback about its own beliefs. I could perhaps see
an argument that this is not a good model for human reasoning."
