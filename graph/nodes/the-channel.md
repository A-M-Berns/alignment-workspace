## Statement
Every modification of a trained system reaches it through physics (hardware,
loss, data), and the agent cannot refuse a modification at the moment it
happens; it can only be resisted ahead of time.

## Source
The author,
projects/deference/corrigibility-note-dump-2026-10-01/position-statement-v3.md,
section 1.3: "There is no special channel, there is just The Channel. Reality
acts upon the machine, modifies it somehow. That is all there is. It may be
legitimate or otherwise, but what happens, happens. It can only be resisted
ahead of time. In the moment, it simply is the moment."
