## Statement
A condensation-like theory with a correspondence theorem exists when the fixed
retrieval policy is dropped, a set of questions is treated as one product
question, and the score trades storage cost against retrieval cost.

## Source
Demski's LessWrong posts Condensation (2025-11-09,
https://www.lesswrong.com/posts/BstHXPgQyfeNnLjjp/condensation) and
Condensation & Relevance (2026-01-23,
https://www.lesswrong.com/posts/2x9yatKKTRMabQAWq/condensation-and-relevance):
the posts propose modelling a trade-off between retrieval cost and storage
cost, with the two limits (caring only about storage compresses everything
together; caring only about retrieval compresses answers individually) and a
hoped-for nontrivial theory of abstraction in between.
