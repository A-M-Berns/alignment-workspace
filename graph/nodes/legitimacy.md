## Statement
Legitimacy is internal legitimacy (Integrity of the accounted obligation state and authorship of verdicts) together with external legitimacy (Robust Openness at every state and transparency of every channel), it satisfies the thin specification, and corrigibility, as faithfulness to an allocation of authority, consumes it at the segment gate.
