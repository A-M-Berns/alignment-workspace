## Statement
The murder-pill inequality, that the utility of the best act under U is at
least the utility under U of the best act under the changed utility, yields
resistance to value change only for a utility that does not depend on the
modification event; in the note's two-step example an expected-utility agent
strictly prefers having its values changed, 0.9 for accepting against 0.6 for
declining.
