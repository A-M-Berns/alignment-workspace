## Statement
A theory of abstraction compatible with logical induction yields a
representation theorem stating when a system is interpretable as a logical
inductor, a decision theory formulated in that setting, and a notion of
scale-free agency under which approximate cooperative equilibria are agents.

setting to do decision theory in" and "A notion of scale-free agency based on a
solid theory of the abstraction of agency"; "A characterization of approximate
cooperative equilibria as agents, with meaningful advice about how to steer
towards such equilibria".
