## Statement
Power-seeking tendencies survive the relaxation from optimal policies to
retargetable decision functions: retargetability is sufficient to cause
power-seeking tendencies for a range of decision-making procedures.

## Source
Turner and Tadepalli 2022, Parametrically retargetable decision-makers tend to
seek power (arXiv 2206.13477), abstract: "We discover that many
decision-making functions are retargetable, and that retargetability is
sufficient to cause power-seeking tendencies."
