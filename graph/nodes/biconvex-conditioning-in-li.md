## Statement
DDB's biconvex form of Total Trust, π(· | P ∈ B) ∈ B for biconvex B, is
expressible in logical induction when the conditioning event is a ramp on the
expert's estimates rather than the expert's identity.

## Source
Dorst, Levinstein, Salow, Husic and Fitelson, Deference Done Better (2021),
Theorem 5.1;
projects/deference/note-dump-2026-08-11/wiki/deference-notions.md, Reflection
paragraph: "in LI the soft indicator can only ramp over *estimate*-thresholds,
never over identity".
