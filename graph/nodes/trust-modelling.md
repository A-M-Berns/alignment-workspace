## Notes
This aim is a plan under the maintainer's broad question at the top frame, not
the frame itself: the top frame keeps its broad question so that other agendas
serving it can be recognized beside this one.

The roots are ordered as follows. First the decision-theoretic stance: the
updateful line at the logical-induction level, and the updateless programme
with its two cruxes (how hard updatelessness is at the logical-induction level,
and whether the updateless side matters to engineering). Then the levels of
computability: super-Turing, above the quantifiable (Jeffrey–Bolker and
Deference Done Better), and poly-time, a line recorded and not opened; the
crux on whether the unbounded level represents the important phenomena; the
family of claims that work at each level guides engineering; and the claim that value uncertainty is partly computational uncertainty. Then the
later stage, the abstraction arc of the roadmap's Stages 4 to 6. Last, as a
sub-frame of its own so that it stands below the named items and not beside
them, the enabling work the repository's activity serves: the vetting and
prioritization system, the machine-checked corpus, the publication pipeline and
corpus hygiene, with two cruxes about how to spend effort between them.

Every decomposition under this frame is the reading of the filing sessions,
drawn from the roadmap, the maintainer's public notes and the repository's
activity; every number on it is an AI estimate at J 0.
