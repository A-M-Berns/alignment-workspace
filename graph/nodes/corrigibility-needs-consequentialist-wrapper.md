## Statement
A legitimacy-weighted consequentialist objective is the right frame for
corrigibility, as against a system that is consequentialist by default and
behaves corrigibly on legitimate modification attempts.

## Source
Stated from its content: a design alternative to the legitimacy-weighted
objective is a legitimacy-respecting system that is consequentialist by
default and corrigible on legitimate modification attempts; the case for the
objective is that such a piecemeal system may manipulate the humans while
acting as a consequentialist.

## Notes
A crux between two designs. The legit-neg
clean-room packages attack the consequentialist objectives only; the piecemeal
alternative has no finite test, which is the action on the route under the
improvement claim. Under the second picture the legit-neg negatives lose
standing as attacks on the design and keep it as attacks on one design.
