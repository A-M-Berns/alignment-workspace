## Statement
A model of the joint agent-and-oversight process exists in which conditions on
the trajectory (capability bounded by understanding of the general welfare,
value learning ahead of capability, caution growing faster than the risk from
capability) are formally established as bringing the trajectory to an outcome
that counts as going well, with corrigibility as one subgoal.

## Source
The author,
projects/deference/corrigibility-note-dump-2026-10-01/position-statement-v2.md,
section 1.3: "In order for the future to go well, or at least for it to
reliably go well, we'll need to increasingly respect rules that upper-bound an
agent's power by its wisdom. ... Of course this isn't convincing as-stated; we
need to provide some sort of model in which this can be formally established in
order for it to be convincing. ... ultimately we need to understand the dynamic
conditions needed in order for things to turn out well, with corrigibility
being only a subgoal of that."
