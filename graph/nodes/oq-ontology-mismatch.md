## Statement
The li-deference framework models only the AI's outwardly expressed beliefs,
forced by training into the humans' language, so it does not model ontology
mismatch between humans and AIs or ontology-shift problems.

## Source
projects/deference/note-dump-2026-08-11/notes/li-deference.md, Some other open
questions, Ontology mismatches between humans and AIs (human-written by the
maintainer): "Since I'm only doing outer-alignment here, the "beliefs" of the
AI are only its outwardly-expressed beliefs, which are forced by the training
to be expressed in the same language as the humans use. This does not model
deep problems of ontology mismatch between humans and AIs, nor does it have
much to say about ontology-shift problems."
