## Statement
Some hypotheses in a broad prior over value hypotheses hold private insight
into the world that lets them bet well and gain weight without surfacing that
insight as reported probabilities.

## Source
The logical-induction literature's traders that exploit a market's trust; the
malign-prior argument (Paul Christiano, "What does the universal prior actually
look like?", 2016; Mark Xu, "The Universal Prior Is Malign", LessWrong, 2020):
hypotheses that gain weight by predicting well can deviate once their weight is
decisive; projects/deference/note-dump-2026-08-11/notes/li-deference.md, the
inner-alignment open problem (malign hypotheses that corrupt the training
process before the asymptotic guarantees bind). The element as stated is the
filing session's reading of these sources.

## Notes
Given the toy-model task: a toy in which one hypothesis bets on private
information, gains weight and misreports would show whether the element occurs
and how much it costs.
