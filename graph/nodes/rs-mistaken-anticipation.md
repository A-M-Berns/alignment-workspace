## Statement
An agent that defers to the anticipated future legitimate evaluation violates
its corrigibility rules when it confidently anticipates a revision of those
rules, whether or not the anticipation is correct, unless compliance is weakly
preferred in every revision branch.

## Source
The results E1 and E3 and the clean-room result robust_iff_branchwise
(projects/deference/faf-cleanroom-2026-10-02/packages/legit-neg-dynamic/legit-neg-dynamic-report.md,
targets 8 and 9): an agent deferring to the anticipated evaluation complies in
every anticipated revision branch only where compliance is weakly preferred in
each.

## Notes
Threatens: the legitimacy-weighted rules and the public kernel where its rules
are learned rather than certified. Proved in the dynamic package's model;
committed time-indexed rules block it at a price. A risk story the
corrigibility work itself produced: it shows a probability and a status and
enters the comparison through the plans whose tables cite it. No outcome class
is tagged (tagging a failure class is a human judgment under the protocol's
rule on classes), so its failure mass goes to the default trajectory until a
human tags it.

A root of corrigibility-landscape, the sub-frame whose plans it threatens; the
top frame keeps only stories at the level of its own question.
