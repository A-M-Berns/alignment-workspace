## Statement
An agent's beliefs about which modifications are legitimate can be made
accurate enough at high capability for the legitimacy-conditioned criterion to
be the right target.

## Source
The author's thesis in
projects/deference/corrigibility-note-dump-2026-10-01/corrigibility-discussion-outline.md:
it is crucial that such an agent have quite accurate beliefs about legitimacy,
particularly at high capability; the author in
projects/deference/corrigibility-note-dump-2026-10-01/position-statement.md,
section 2.12: "because the absolute probability of legitimacy is in fact high
... the agent's conditional estimates of legitimacy can be high everywhere";
the negative result on forced trust between inductors (see
no-forced-other-trust).

## Notes
The crux the whole line turns on; the landed step
step-legitimacy-conditioned-trust's accuracy clause is this claim. Pictures:
learnable via retractions plus one unlearnable constraint; learnable because
legitimacy is in fact high; attainable only with a human-side law of motion;
not attainable because learning selects for undetectable manipulation;
residual. The fourth row conditions on the no-forced-trust result.
