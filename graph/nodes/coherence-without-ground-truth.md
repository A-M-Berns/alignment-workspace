## Statement
Coherence guarantees (no sure loss, calibration, logical coherence, reflective
stability) give checkable confidence in an AI system's reasoning where
alignment lacks ground-truth labels.

## Source
The filing session's construction, on public grounds: the coherence notions
are de Finetti's no sure loss, calibration (Dawid 1982, The Well-Calibrated
Bayesian) and the logical-induction criterion with its reflective properties
(Garrabrant et al. 2016, Logical Induction), each checkable without access to
the truth of the beliefs it constrains.
