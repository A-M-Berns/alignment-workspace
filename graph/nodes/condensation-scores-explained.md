## Statement
Each of condensation's three scores is interpreted as an expected cost under a
stated convention: the simple and conditioned scores as explanation lengths,
and the reconstruction score as a measure of over-parameterization given the
givens.

## Source
Eisenstat's condensation paper, Definition 3.3 (the three scores). Demski's
LessWrong posts Condensation (2025-11-09,
https://www.lesswrong.com/posts/BstHXPgQyfeNnLjjp/condensation) and
Condensation & Relevance (2026-01-23,
https://www.lesswrong.com/posts/2x9yatKKTRMabQAWq/condensation-and-relevance):
the first post's Score section.

## Notes
A mid-level plan of Stage 4, an and of the two interpretations its Statement
names. The plan states a two-plus-one reading rather than reading all three
scores as explanation lengths, because the reconstruction score is not about
explanation cost.
