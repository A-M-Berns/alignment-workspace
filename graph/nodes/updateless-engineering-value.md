## Statement
The updateless side is high value to AI safety engineering work even where the
theory of computational updatelessness is easy: the engineering decisions
updateless reasoning changes are large enough that the programme's expected
contribution to a formal understanding of trust exceeds the updateful plan's at
equal cost.

must also ensure that it will not be motivated to modify or remove safeguards,
