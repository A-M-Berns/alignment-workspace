## Statement
An agent is corrigible to a human exactly when it is faithful on the family of
governance questions about modifying it and legitimate on that family, with the
corollary that modification decisions are made by a process sealed from the
agent's contemporaneous outputs and the conjecture that neither party then has
an incentive to modify the arrangement unilaterally.
