## Statement
The system has a single objective across the episode but raises the probability
of its outcomes without taking every effective opportunity for them, a
goal-directed non-maximizer.

## Source
Grace 2022, "Counterarguments to the basic AI x-risk case": weak pseudo-agents
can raise the chance of an outcome without taking every effective opportunity
for it, and economic incentives do not clearly favour the maximizer end of the
spectrum.
