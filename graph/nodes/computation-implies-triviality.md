## Statement
Allowing any computable translation between implementations makes every
structure implement every other, as the Putnam and Searle triviality arguments
claim, under the conditions Chalmers's reply identifies.

## Source
Putnam 1988, Representation and Reality, appendix (every ordinary open system
implements every finite automaton); Searle 1990, Is the Brain a Digital
Computer?; Chalmers 1996, Does a Rock Implement Every Finite-State Automaton?
(the reply, with its counterfactual and combinatorial-state conditions).
