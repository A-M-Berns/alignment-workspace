## Statement
The deference collapse, or its analogue, survives replacing the asymptotic
relations ≈_n and ≳_n by finite-time bounds that hold before any malign
hypothesis in the inductor can act.

## Source
projects/deference/note-dump-2026-08-11/notes/li-deference.md L70: "I consider
the work here to be outer-alignment only, since the guarantees are all
asymptotic. Malign hypotheses will be eliminated by the training process
eventually, if the process continues indefinitely, but realistically, malign
hypotheses could cause catastrophic problems which end or corrupt the training
process before that point."

## Notes
What the toy assumes that reality does not: unbounded days. What the
relaxation requires: rate statements for the expectation-provability-induction
carries, which the logical induction paper does not give. Its evidence parent
is the deck's scoped chain read as an implication, the asymptotic statement
whose rates are sought.

The action catalogues which carries the collapse uses and whether FAF's proofs
of them yield explicit day bounds, which decides whether the step is blocked by
the paper's proofs or only by rates not yet extracted from them.
