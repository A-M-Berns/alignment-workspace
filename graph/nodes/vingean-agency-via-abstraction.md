## Statement
The epistemic position of predicting that a more skilled agent will win without
predicting its moves is characterized by a theory of abstraction.

## Source
projects/deference/note-dump-2026-08-11/deference-trust-lab/AGENDA.md, Agency &
Abstraction (the maintainer, May 2026): "My main idea here is to start with
Vingean agency, by which I mean the sort of epistemic position we have when
watching someone who is more skilled than us at chess: we cannot predict
exactly what they will do, but we can predict that they will win. Can such a
thing be characterized well by a theory of abstraction?"
