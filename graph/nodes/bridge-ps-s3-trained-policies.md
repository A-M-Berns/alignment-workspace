## Statement
The power-seeking tendency of retargetable policies over a training-compatible
goal set survives the relaxation from that goal set to the policies produced
by the training procedures actually used.

## Source
The gap the toy's authors name, Turner et al. 2021: "We look forward to future
work which addresses partially observable environments, suboptimal policies,
or 'almost similar' RSD sets"; disputed by the first author, Turner 2024: "Due
to the 'RL->reward maximizing' meme, I personally misdirected thousands of
hours on proving power-seeking theorems."
