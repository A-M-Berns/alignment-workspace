## Statement
Bargaining between value hypotheses uses an improvement notion weaker than
Pareto, such as Garrabrant's stratified Pareto optima from cooperative oracles,
so that adversarial anti-values are excluded from the coalition while keeping
positive probability.

## Source
Scott Garrabrant, "Cooperative Oracles: Stratified Pareto Optima and Almost
Stratified Pareto Optima" (Agent Foundations forum and LessWrong, 2017):
stratified Pareto optima allow mutual-benefit improvements that impose
negative externalities on others, an improvement notion weaker than Pareto.
