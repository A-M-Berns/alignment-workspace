## Statement
A research programme on the updateless side develops decision theory for
computationally uncertain agents, counterfactuals, computational
updatelessness, decision problems, open-minded updatelessness and geometric
rationality, far enough to settle whether it is tractable and relevant to AI
safety engineering, and unfolds in full only where both hold.
