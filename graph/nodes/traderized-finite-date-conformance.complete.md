## Statement
The one conjunct is the registered deductive end-to-end theorem: for a deductive process with a computable fragment schedule, a computable positive tolerance schedule and satisfiable stages, the compiled market is a logical inductor over the process and conforms to the day's deductive region within the tolerance at every date. That establishes the claim for deduction, the case the claim's Source names; it does not establish the claim for an assessment process in general, any trust or gap-bet bound read from the conformance, or an efficient compiler.
