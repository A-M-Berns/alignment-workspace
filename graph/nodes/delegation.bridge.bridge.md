## Statement
The claim asserts the local delegation bridge: under grade trust at level η over an admissible partition, delegating to the principal's judgment beats any measurable comparator by the principal's grade margin less twice η on the disagreement region. The declaration certifies the inequality exactly, over a finite cell type with exact rational masses, expectations and grades: the comparator's valuation plus its grade margin minus 2η times the disagreement mass is at most the valuation of delegating. The gaps: the source states the bridge over an admissible conditioning partition of a finite outcome space, and the declaration carries the partition by its cell type, so measurability of the judgment and the comparator is true by construction rather than a hypothesis discharged (the file's own words: a re-typing, not a transcription); grade trust is imported, and the source argues no settlement in the skeleton produces it; the uniform form, which trades the trust hypothesis for a bound by twice the magnitude, is deliberately not ported because it rests on a grade-to-quantity link the programme derives rather than assumes; and the declaration is free of any logical-induction fact, where the claim's home is the logical-induction setting the deference line asks about.
