## Statement
Total Trust, the notion of alignment in Deference Done Better, is an updateful
notion: total trust and deference come apart when actions have acausal
consequences.

## Source
projects/deference/faf-cleanroom-2026-10-02/sources/unbounded-embedded/01-primary/summer-research-plan-2026--research-ideas-may-2026.md,
section Updatelessness: "The notion of alignment as total trust appears to be
an updateful one; total trust and deference come apart when actions can have
acausal consequences, since a more-knowledgeable future self might refuse a
profitable counterfactual mugging, get the wrong answer in a transparent
newcomb, etc."
