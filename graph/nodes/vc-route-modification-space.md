## Statement
A Bayesian agent that reasons about every way it could be modified is
represented by a belief space rich enough that the regress of modifications of
modifications closes, with a richness assumption that excludes the
impoverished-space case of Soares et al. 2015 section 1.

## Source
The author in
projects/deference/corrigibility-note-dump-2026-10-01/position-statement.md,
sections 2.2 and 2.4: "The main problem here, I think, is merely
representational: it is not obvious how to represent a Bayesian agent thinking
about all the possible ways it could be modified. We need to make assumptions
about the richness of the hypothesis space to rule out cases such as the
oscillation between opiates and stimulants." The clean-room package
projects/deference/faf-cleanroom-2026-10-02/packages/corr-reflect-frames lists
the open statement universalSelfRefSpace_exists_open.
