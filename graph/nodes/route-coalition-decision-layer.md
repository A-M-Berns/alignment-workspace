## Statement
A decision layer in which each member's own expectation drives the policy with
the aggregate as bookmaker keeps Pareto optimality where the aggregate's
expectation loses it.

## Source
Stated from its content, as a route under the given parent
aggregate-logical-inductor: a policy driven by the aggregate market's own
expectation (the futarchy form) need not be Pareto optimal for the members,
and letting each member's expectation drive the policy, with the aggregate as
bookmaker, is the proposed repair.
