## Statement
Where the ledger has uncollateralized credit, incompatibility and a payment the
world exhibits, the market has collateral, hedging and wealth, and an
infinitely wealthy trader is an unscored page only when it is the sole
settlement channel.

## Source
Abram Demski, the Market Logic sequence (LessWrong,
https://www.lesswrong.com/s/KMi4cws48eyeDtLiQ), for the market side
(collateral, hedging, wealth). The ledger side and the comparison are the
filing session's; no public text states them yet.
