## Statement
For most reward functions under a distribution invariant under the
environmental symmetry, in a finite fully observable Markov decision process,
it is optimal to seek power by keeping a range of options available and, when
maximizing average reward, by navigating toward larger sets of potential
terminal states.

## Source
Turner et al. 2021, abstract: "most reward functions make it optimal to seek
power by keeping a range of options available and, when maximizing average
reward, by navigating towards larger sets of potential terminal states";
Theorem 6.13.
