## Statement
Members entering the logical-induction paper's fixed-point market as
Kelly-demand traders with every budget, beside the enumerable trading firm,
yield a logical inductor over the union of the members' deductive processes,
and on the settled fragment its normalized member weights follow Critch's
weight path whatever else trades.

## Source
Garrabrant et al. 2016, Logical Induction (the fixed-point market and its
trading firm); Critch 2017, Toward Negotiable Reinforcement Learning (the
weight path); the claim identifies Critch's weight path with the Kelly
wealth-share path inside the market.
