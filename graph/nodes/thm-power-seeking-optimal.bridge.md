## Statement
The theorem's conclusion, that average-optimal policies tend toward larger sets
of recurrent state distributions, is what the informal thesis calls seeking
power: keeping options open and staying alive.

## Source
Turner et al. 2021, abstract: "optimal policies to tend to seek power over the
environment"; Turner and Tadepalli 2022 (arXiv 2206.13477), abstract: "most
reward functions have an optimal policy which seeks power by keeping options
open and staying alive".
