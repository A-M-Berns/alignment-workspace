## Statement
Theorem 6.13 of Turner et al. 2021, average-optimal policies tend to end up in
larger sets of recurrent state distributions, is proved in the paper: when a
set of recurrent state distributions contains a copy of another via an
environmental symmetry and the stated containment conditions hold, most reward
functions make it average-optimal to end up in the larger set.

## Source
Turner et al. 2021, Theorem 6.13: "Average-optimal policies tend to end up in
'larger' sets of RSDs".
