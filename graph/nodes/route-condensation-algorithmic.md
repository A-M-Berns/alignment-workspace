## Statement
An algorithmic-information version of condensation, with description length in
place of entropy and variable-cost symbols for single events, carries a
correspondence theorem.

## Source
Abram Demski's LessWrong posts "Condensation" (2025-11-09) and "Condensation
& Relevance" (2026-01-23): the first describes an algorithmic version of
condensation, and the second proposes variable-cost symbols to rescue it.
