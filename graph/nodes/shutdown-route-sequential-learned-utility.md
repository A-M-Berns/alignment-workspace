## Statement
The shutdown model is refined to sequential settings with learned utilities and
multiple principals, the direction Armstrong's indifference line and Holtman's
work pursue.

## Source
The published corrigibility literature: Armstrong's utility indifference
(Armstrong and O'Rourke, "'Indifference' methods for managing agent rewards",
arXiv:1712.06365) and Holtman's correction-function agents ("Corrigibility
with Utility Preservation", arXiv:1908.01695) take the shutdown problem to
sequential settings with learned utilities.
