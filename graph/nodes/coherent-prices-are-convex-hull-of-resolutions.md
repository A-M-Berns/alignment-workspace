## Statement
The de Finetti Dutch-book argument run with arbitrary terminal values gives
that coherent prices are the convex hull of the admissible resolutions, so the
argument's classical-logic assumption is a choice of which resolutions are
admissible; the Łukasiewicz instance is prior art.

## Source
de Finetti, Theory of Probability (1974), the coherence theorem (coherent
previsions are the convex hull of the admissible valuations); Mundici,
Bookmaking over infinite-valued events (2006), the Łukasiewicz instance.
