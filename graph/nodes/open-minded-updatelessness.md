## Statement
A formal account says which updates an updateless agent accepts, what it is
updateful about and what updateless about, so that an updateless agent can
defer to a better-informed successor, by at least one of: a non-definitional
deference relation on the canonical examples; the coupling-graph
characterization of decision-flat refinements and its extension beyond pairwise
coupling; a superconditioning bridge from endorsement to evidence-indexed
policies.
