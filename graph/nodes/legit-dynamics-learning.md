## Statement
The AI learns the humans' concept of legitimacy from them, legitimacy of the
process can be lost and sometimes repaired, and the theory characterizes when
the AI learns enough about legitimacy to avoid wireheading and manipulation
without assuming the humans live forever.

## Source
projects/deference/note-dump-2026-08-11/related-chats/2026-07-05__theory-of-legitimacy__c1ab54d9.md,
turn 17, the maintainer: "The humans have their legitimacy concept for
themselves, and the AI is trying to learn about that. Ideally we'd have some
nice theorems characterizing situations where the AI learns enough or doesn't
learn enough, telling us about wireheading, human manipulation, etc. This is
the version of the theory where we don't just get to assume the humans are
immortal and so asymptotic learning can eventually work. The legitimacy of the
process can die, and we're trying to address the accumulated odds of that as
things evolve."
