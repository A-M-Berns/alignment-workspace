## Notes
The improvement claim on uea-theorem-1. The reality phrase gathers
the issues the maintainer's review names: the self-knowledge premise rules out
rational self-modification, the theorem is updateful, and oracle calls are
modelled as independent so predictors are mishandled. The routes are his three
directions (self-knowledge split, updateless variant, correlation-allowing
oracles), the floored-agent repair the finite-shadow lab recommends, the
ε-exploration route of Kim and Lee's ε-asymptotic optimality result for
Self-AIXI (arXiv:2602.23242), and the residual route. Routes condition on the
theorem itself (a leaf) or on the refinement uea-floored-agent-theorem.
