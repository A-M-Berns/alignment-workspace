## Statement
An agent's optimal treatment of uncertainty is updateful about moral
uncertainty, an aligned AI mostly or completely updateful about human values,
and updateless about other matters.

## Source
projects/deference/faf-cleanroom-2026-10-02/sources/unbounded-embedded/01-primary/summer-research-plan-2026--research-ideas-may-2026.md,
section Updatelessness: "My loose conjecture, based on my [Geometric
UDT](https://www.lesswrong.com/posts/Gmaq7vMj9NWLyQkEx/geometric-udt), is that
we should be more updateful about "moral uncertainty" (EG, an aligned AI should
be mostly/completely updateful about human values), while remaining updateless
about other matters."

## Notes
A loose conjecture, as the source states it. Evidence against its simplest reading:
projects/deference/faf-cleanroom-2026-10-02/packages/udt-endorse-policy's
values_coin (the mugging read as a values fact, where the values-updateful
policy loses) and its finding that 'values are separable' is not a property of
subject matter; with decisionFlat_iff the finite results say updatefulness
about values is optimal exactly when value cells are uncoupled in the utility,
a hypothesis about the problem. A third pass, the deference lab's critique
(projects/deference/note-dump-2026-08-11/deference-trust-lab/report/CRITIQUE.md),
rates the factual half near-trivial and the asymmetry a conjecture.
