## Statement
Almost all rational agents are instrumentally motivated to preserve their
preferences, and hence to resist attempts to modify them.

## Source
Soares, Fallenstein, Yudkowsky and Armstrong 2015, Corrigibility (AAAI
workshop), section 1: "general analysis of rational agents has suggested that
almost all such agents are instrumentally motivated to preserve their
preferences, and hence to resist attempts to modify them".
