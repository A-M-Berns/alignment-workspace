## Statement
DDB's question-relative principles (reflecting, totally trusting and valuing
an expert with respect to a partition) have logical-induction counterparts in
which the question is a family of sentences, and question-relative Total Trust
is equivalent to question-relative Value there.

## Source
Dorst, Levinstein, Salow, Husic and Fitelson, "Deference Done Better",
Philosophical Perspectives 35 (2021), §5: "every theory we've explored here is a theory of
complete deference … We defer to Nate Silver about who'll win the election,
but not about what the weather will be".
