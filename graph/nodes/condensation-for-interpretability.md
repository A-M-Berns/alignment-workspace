## Statement
Condensing a neural network's internal activations as given variables yields
more interpretable features, and models built to resemble condensation are more
interpretable, within the limits that condensation is less tight than
information theory and is not a model of cognition.

## Source
Demski's LessWrong posts Condensation (2025-11-09,
https://www.lesswrong.com/posts/BstHXPgQyfeNnLjjp/condensation) and
Condensation & Relevance (2026-01-23,
https://www.lesswrong.com/posts/2x9yatKKTRMabQAWq/condensation-and-relevance):
the first post's section 'Interpretability Solved?' (treat the internal
activations of machine-learning models as givens and condense them; or take
condensation as inspiration for new models), with the two limits it names; the
second post relates its notion of 'symbolic' to interpretability.
