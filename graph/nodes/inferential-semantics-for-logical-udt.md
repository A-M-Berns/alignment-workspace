## Statement
A tiling theorem for logically uncertain updateless decision theory establishes
trust in a future self through three parts: a market-derivative algebra of
logical uncertainty giving an inferential semantics, a notion of treating
things as real linking it to map-territory semantics, and the tiling theorem
built on that link.
