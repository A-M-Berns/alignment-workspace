## Statement
In finite decision problems with several situations, where the agent
conditions evidentially, situation by situation, on a mixture of policies,
each δ in (0, 1) has a two-situation instance in which the agent (1 −
δ)-believes it is the optimal policy and satisfies the trust bound everywhere,
yet its realized policy loses utility 1.
