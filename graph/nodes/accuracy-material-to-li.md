## Statement
DDB's Epistemic Value (expecting the expert's estimates to be at least as
accurate as one's own under every generalized strictly proper inaccuracy
measure) and its equivalence with Total Trust have logical-induction
counterparts.

the accuracy material of DDB to LI."

§3.
