## Statement
Condensation models retrieval cost only and ignores computational cost, so it
does not represent bounded computational power.

## Source
Demski's LessWrong posts Condensation (2025-11-09,
https://www.lesswrong.com/posts/BstHXPgQyfeNnLjjp/condensation) and
Condensation & Relevance (2026-01-23,
https://www.lesswrong.com/posts/2x9yatKKTRMabQAWq/condensation-and-relevance):
the first post's remark that condensation completely ignores computational
cost, the only cost it models being retrieval cost, and still ignores bounded
computational power like many other models of rationality.
