## Statement
The argument map under graph/ files the research agenda from deep goals down
to concrete tasks, ranks the next work so that human reading goes first to
central claims and to claims with high estimated promise, logs every design
change and user confusion met in use, and has the maintainers' answers to the
design points it leaves open.

## Notes
An enabling goal, filed by the activity sweep of 2026-10-05 as a shared claim
of the-program, because every line of the agenda is ranked and
vetted through it. The four conjuncts are the filing session's reading of the
protocol paragraphs that shape the map, not a list the maintainer wrote. They
are jointly necessary in the sense of the Statement: a map that files the
agenda but ranks it against the maintainer's stated priorities, or that is
never revised in the open, is not the scheme those paragraphs describe.
