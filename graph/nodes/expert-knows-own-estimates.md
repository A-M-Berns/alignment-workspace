## Statement
For every formula that case-splits on the expert's own published estimates,
the expert's estimate of the formula equals the value obtained by resolving
the case-split with those estimates.

## Source
projects/deference/note-dump-2026-08-11/wiki/mart-implies-value.md, Remark:
"the expert must correctly resolve that case-split — it must know its own
estimates".

projects/deference/note-dump-2026-08-11/wiki/total-trust-implies-mart.md, What
the theorem costs, honestly, H2: "If the expert's self-knowledge is only
asymptotic (an LI stage knowing its own prices approximately), the weight is
not provably 1".
