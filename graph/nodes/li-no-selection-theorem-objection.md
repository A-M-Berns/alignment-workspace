## Statement
Because a logical inductor's own complexity class differs from the class of
traders it is unexploitable by, no statement says that nothing better is
achievable with the same compute, so logical-induction-based agency admits no
selection theorem and is not a robust foundation.

## Source
An objection stated here on its content: Garrabrant et al. 2016, Logical
Induction, prove that their inductor is unexploitable by efficiently
computable traders, while the inductor is computable but not itself
efficiently computable, and they state no optimality result relative to the
inductor's own class.

## Notes
