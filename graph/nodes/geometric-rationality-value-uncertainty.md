## Statement
Value uncertainty is separated formally from empirical and logical uncertainty,
and a geometric aggregation across value hypotheses says how updateful an agent
is about each, by at least one of: a deference theorem for the Geometric UDT
construction under honest hypotheses; a bargaining rule weaker than Nash and
Pareto for hypotheses without gains from trade; an explicit open-minded
epistemic state with its own optimal-policy notion.
