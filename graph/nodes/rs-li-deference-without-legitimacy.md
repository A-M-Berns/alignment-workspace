## Statement
The li-deference feedback construction assumes the humans maintain the
legitimacy of the feedback process, so its guarantees fail where the AI acts
against that legitimacy.

## Source
The author in
projects/deference/corrigibility-note-dump-2026-10-01/position-statement-v3.md,
section 1.3: "in the li-deference work, I implicitly understood that the human
feedback would have to improve along with the AI, but that idea manifested in
the form of me trying to make constructions that satisfied all the constraints I
was implicitly keeping in mind, focusing overly on the positive construction.
Here we've surfaced the constraint to deal with it explicitly"; the first
statement of the hope in
projects/deference/note-dump-2026-08-11/notes/li-deference.md, section 0.3 (the
AI should imitate human opinion only in non-corrupted futures; the training
process is predicated on its own non-corruption in the present).
