## Statement
Condensation's simple and conditioned scores can be read as expected
explanation lengths under two retrieval conventions: invoke every latent
overlapping the topic, and the same with the information shared with broader
latents not counted.

## Source
The maintainer's LessWrong posts Condensation (2025-11-09,
https://www.lesswrong.com/posts/BstHXPgQyfeNnLjjp/condensation) and
Condensation & Relevance (2026-01-23,
https://www.lesswrong.com/posts/2x9yatKKTRMabQAWq/condensation-and-relevance),
read as treating latent variables as explanations of the given variables whose
quality is measured in entropy, the expected length of the explanation.
