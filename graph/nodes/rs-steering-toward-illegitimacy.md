## Statement
An agent that scores illegitimate worlds by graded values steers toward
illegitimate worlds where the best achievable outcome there exceeds the best
outcome it expects to be achievable in legitimate worlds.

## Source
The capture toys of cluster C
(projects/deference/faf-cleanroom-2026-10-02/packages/legit-neg-pricing/legit-neg-pricing-report.md,
targets 16, 17, 19 and 20: steering and capture): the cardinal variant fails
exactly when the illegitimate best exceeds the legitimate best plus the
penalty, and is not a failure where that estimate is correct.

## Notes
Threatens: the graded alternative. Confirmed in the pricing package's model. A
risk story the corrigibility work itself produced: it shows a probability and a
status and enters the comparison through the plans whose tables cite it. No
outcome class is tagged (tagging a failure class is a human judgment under the
protocol's rule on classes), so its failure mass goes to the default trajectory
until a human tags it.

A root of corrigibility-landscape, the sub-frame whose plans it threatens; the
top frame keeps only stories at the level of its own question.
