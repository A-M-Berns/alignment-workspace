## Statement
A future evaluator judges an earlier act by the standard in force when it was
taken rather than by her revised standard.

## Source
Stated from its content, as the sibling of the readout question
readout-R1-vs-R2: a future evaluator whose standard has changed can judge an
earlier act by her revised standard (retroactive) or by the standard in force
when it was taken (prospective).

## Notes
Stated as the prospective reading. Negatives that turn on the evaluator's
standard being revised after the act need retroactive judging, and
time-indexing the standard removes them.
