## Statement
Legitimacy is a condition under which a trust principle (Reflection, Tower or
Total Trust) holds for an embedded agent toward its own future beliefs or
toward a teacher, where the unconditional principle licenses wireheading,
manipulation and self-fulfilling prophecies.
