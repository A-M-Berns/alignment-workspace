## Statement
In the two-step, two-utility, one-button shutdown model of Soares et al. 2015,
the stop-world precondition is a necessity-and-sufficiency pair with exact
margins only for sufficiency, and both directions of the corpus's iff statement
of the stop-hypothesis theorem fail inside the model's own beta-above-alpha
package.
