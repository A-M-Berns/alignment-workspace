## Statement
Legitimate deliberation has its own fixed-point selection problem, and the
theory says which reflective equilibrium it selects.

## Source
A question stated here. Reflective equilibrium is Nelson Goodman's (Fact,
Fiction, and Forecast, 1955) and John Rawls's (A Theory of Justice, 1971); a
logical inductor's limit beliefs on undecidable sentences are one fixed point
among many (Garrabrant et al., "Logical Induction", 2016).
