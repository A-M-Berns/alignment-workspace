## Statement
Quantitative limitations on an agent's self-knowledge imply quantitative
limitations on epistemic endorsement, so that reflection-type trust properties
hold only to within a tolerance matching the precision of self-knowledge.

## Source
projects/deference/faf-cleanroom-2026-10-02/sources/unbounded-embedded/03-journals/2026-03-26.md:
"Complete self-knowledge (immodesty, in the paper's parlance) leads to
paradox"; "We can only demand that the self-knowledge be very precise, accurate
to within some small tolerance."; "Relatedly, we can only demand that
reflection-type trust properties hold to within some tolerance, also"; "Do
quantitative limitations on self-knowledge imply quantitative limitations on
epistemic endorsement?"
