## Statement
Legitimacy is an event on which the agent's update satisfies reflection or
Total Trust with nothing required off it, and for the global
legitimacy-conditioned criterion, Total Trust, Value,
hull-and-modest-informedness and accuracy increase on every proper score are
equivalent on a finite frame, with locality forced.
