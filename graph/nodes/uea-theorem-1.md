## Statement
There is a reflective oracle relative to which, for every ε > 0, whenever the
self-reflective agent π_S assigns posterior above 1 − δ to its own
self-hypothesis at a history, its value at that history is within ε of the
optimal value, so π_S stays ε-optimal for all time with high probability.
