## Statement
Jeffrey–Bolker-style models are not less computable than Solomonoff induction
or AIXI: they are specified at a level where computability is not quantified,
and some instances are poly-time computable.

## Source
Jeffrey 1965 and Bolker 1967 specify the models without any computability
constraint, so they are not ranked against Solomonoff induction or AIXI, which
are specified as uncomputable; a finite Jeffrey–Bolker model over a
polynomially sized algebra is an instance computable in polynomial time.

## Notes
The axis has two orderings, abstraction and computability, and this claim
keeps them apart.
