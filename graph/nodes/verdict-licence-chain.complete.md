## Statement
The one conjunct is the registered licence chain: at a record state, a licensed verdict is supported on the record's grounds and admissible in their settled region. That establishes the claim's chain for a record state at one step, with its first arrow definitional and its second by convexity; it does not establish that a licence survives a record transition, that the authorized-event clause does any work, or either converse, which the round's countermodels refute.
