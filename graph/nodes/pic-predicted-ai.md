## Statement
AI systems are predicted, simulated and selected on their policies by their
developers, so human–AI situations have Newcomb-like structure, and trust
theorems stated updatefully mis-describe them.

## Source
projects/deference/note-dump-2026-08-11/notes/li-deference.md, open problem
Decision theory: "it does not seek to model the decision-theoretic consequences
of environments containing powerful predictors, and it ignores updatelessness."
projects/deference/note-dump-2026-08-11/wiki/open-problems.md, Research
direction: trust the policy, not the decision rule: "no decision rule is
optimal across all embedded environments (Death in Damascus and Counterfactual
Mugging are both in the family the scope condition excludes)."
projects/deference/faf-cleanroom-2026-10-02/sources/unbounded-embedded/01-primary/summer-research-plan-2026--research-ideas-may-2026.md,
section Updatelessness: "total trust and deference come apart when actions can
have acausal consequences".
