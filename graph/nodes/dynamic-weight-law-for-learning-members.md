## Statement
A law for how bargaining weight moves between members who are logical inductors
replaces Critch's fixed-prior likelihood recursion and freezes under full
deference.

## Source
Andrew Critch, "Toward Negotiable Reinforcement Learning: Shifting
Priorities in Pareto Optimal Sequential Decision-Making" (2017): for Bayesian
parties with fixed priors, each party's bargaining weight is multiplied by the
likelihood its beliefs gave the observation. No analogue for parties whose
beliefs are logical inductors is stated there.
