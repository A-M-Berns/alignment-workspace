## Statement
The property the utility-indifference designs aimed at is non-interference with
the feedback channel, which is compatible with a positive desire to learn from
that channel.

## Source
The author,
projects/deference/corrigibility-note-dump-2026-10-01/position-statement.md,
section 2.7: "I agree with your idea that indifference was simply the wrong
target -- I can be far from indifferent about a fact, but still eager to learn.
The real intuition behind indifference is
not-wishing-to-interfere-with-the-feedback".
