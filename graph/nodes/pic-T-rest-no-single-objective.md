## Statement
The system is a trained policy that is neither a reward maximizer nor the
holder of any single learned goal: its behaviour decomposes into
context-dependent heuristics with no objective fixed across the episode.

## Source

sentence as the picture this one refines; the reading of "not reward
maximizers" as "no single objective" is the filing contributor's.
