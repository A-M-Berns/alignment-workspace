## Statement
No explicit counter-drive is installed, or an installed one fails to bind at
high capability.

## Source
Reconstructed from Omohundro 2008, abstract: "tendencies which will be present
unless explicitly counteracted"; the paper does not state it in this form.

## Notes
The premise that makes the drives a risk rather than a description; the
shutdown-problem results bear on whether an installed counter-drive binds.
