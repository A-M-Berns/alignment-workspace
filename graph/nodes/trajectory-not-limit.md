## Statement
The guarantees this line seeks are invariants of the joint agent-and-oversight
trajectory over finite time rather than properties of a limit point.

## Source
The author,
projects/deference/corrigibility-note-dump-2026-10-01/position-statement-v2.md,
section 1.4: "we care more about conditions of the trajectory than where it
ends up. The formal model is going to have a limit point, but in reality we
think probably everything breaks down in the heat death of the universe if not
earlier. Properties of the limit point can be reassuring, but properties of the
dynamics more so." AGREED there with the drafting session's proposal.
