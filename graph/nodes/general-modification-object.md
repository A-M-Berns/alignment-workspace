## Statement
The general correction is a push of the agent's state to a distribution
absolutely continuous with it, with per-modification stakes c(Q) and h(Q),
compliance exactly when the probability of legitimacy given the push is at
least c(Q)/(c(Q)+h(Q)), and shutdown the special push under which the null
action is optimal.
