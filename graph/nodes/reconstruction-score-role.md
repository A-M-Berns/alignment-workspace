## Statement
The reconstruction score of Definition 3.3 measures over-parameterization of
the superset latents given the givens, a version of Occam's razor against
unnecessary postulates, rather than explanation cost.

## Source
Eisenstat's condensation paper, Definition 3.3 (the three scores); stated from
its content as an open question about the reconstruction score's role.
