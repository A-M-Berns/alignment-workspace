## Statement
Market Logic II's turnstile, read as pointwise dominance of payoffs, is the
degree-preserving Łukasiewicz relation rather than Łukasiewicz consequence;
tonk is a sure loss for the maker; the two disjunctions are Girard's additive
and multiplicative pair; and the shared-context rules price 'if' as Gödel's
conditional.

## Source
abramdemski, Market Logic II (LessWrong, 2025-11-23,
https://www.lesswrong.com/posts/2dsTdcZL6a3EAxdbS/market-logic-ii), the
turnstile and the connectives' rules. The four identifications
(degree-preserving Łukasiewicz relation, tonk as a sure loss, Girard's additive
and multiplicative disjunctions, Gödel's conditional) are the filing session's
reading, with the literature recalled and not checked.
