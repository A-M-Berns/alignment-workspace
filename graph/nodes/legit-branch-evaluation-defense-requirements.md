## Statement
The defense that the humans in the legitimate branch evaluate the quality of
actions correctly needs inferability of illegitimate-world consequences from
legitimate-world observables, ex-ante rather than posterior conditioning of the
illegitimate term, evaluators who model the counterfactual for voiding actions,
reference information independent of the agent's information gathering, and
off-diagonal grades under the realized-terminal readout, each condition
necessary in some fixture.
