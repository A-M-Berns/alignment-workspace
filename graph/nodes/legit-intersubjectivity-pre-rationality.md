## Statement
Hansonian pre-rationality connects legitimacy, an agent's endorsement of its
own reasoning, with intersubjective objectivity, agreement reached by
exchanging reasons, so that reason exchange arrives at a common prior by
modifying the prior.

from conversation, the Hansonian pre-rationality picture in which reasons are
exchanged to arrive at a common prior, the remark that this requires modifying
the prior, and the connection to legitimacy and endorsement through the
Hansonian argument.
