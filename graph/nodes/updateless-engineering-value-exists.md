## Statement
At least one situation that arises between humans and AI systems, developers
selecting systems on their predicted policies or a shutdown commitment a fully
updated system would not renew, is one in which the updateful and the
updateless prescriptions differ, so at least one engineering decision depends
on which is followed.

across all embedded environments (Death in Damascus and Counterfactual Mugging
are both in the family the scope condition excludes)."

projects/deference/note-dump-2026-08-11/notes/li-deference.md, open problem

not seek to model the decision-theoretic consequences of environments
containing powerful predictors, and it ignores updatelessness."
