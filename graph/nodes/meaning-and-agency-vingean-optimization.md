## Statement
Optimization, defined through endorsement and split into selection and control,
characterizes Vingean agency in the maintainer's Meaning & Agency account, and
that definition is an abstraction-theoretic one.

## Source
The maintainer's post Meaning & Agency (LessWrong, 2023-12-19,
https://www.lesswrong.com/posts/bnnhypM5MXBHAATLw/meaning-and-agency):
optimization as a type of Vingean agency, split into selection and control
variants, with endorsement and legitimacy as its new concepts.

## Notes
The second clause (that the definition is abstraction-theoretic) is the filing
session's reading, which the reconciliation task tests.
