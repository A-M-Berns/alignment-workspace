## Statement
Geometric UDT selects an updateless policy as a Nash bargaining solution
between value hypotheses with an updateful policy as the disagreement point, so
that an agent uncertain about human values does no worse for any hypothesis
than an updateful learner would, for a broad class of hypotheses.

## Notes
The published construction, stated with its 'broad class' clause. Where some
hypotheses are exact negatives of others, one gains exactly what the other
loses, so the product of gains from trade gives no usable optimum; this is a
candidate obstruction to the clause, and the stratified-Pareto route a
candidate repair.
