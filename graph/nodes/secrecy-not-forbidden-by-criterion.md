## Statement
Over the announced record the logical induction criterion forbids no secrecy;
over the union of what members know it fails for any permanently unannounced
independent fact; withholding policies visible in schedule but hidden in
content are permanent, permitted and profitable.

## Source
Stated from its content. The criterion constrains only traders whose
information is the announced record, so a fact never announced is invisible to
it (first clause); a trader with access to an unannounced independent fact can
exploit a market that never learns it (second clause); the third clause is an
AI reading that follows from the first two and is unchecked. The given parent
secrecy-props-lean-checked is the check of the first two.
