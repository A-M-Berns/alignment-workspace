## Statement
A requirement that the humans benefit at least moderately from a coalition is
best stated as a lower bound on the realized value gap between the agreed
policy and the executed one, under the humans' expectation, rather than on the
humans' bargaining weight.

## Source
Stated from its content: zero bargaining weight for the humans is harmless when
the AI's term is aligned, and preserved weight is worthless when that term is
corrupted, so weight is not the quantity a benefit requirement bounds.

## Notes
A question about how a coalition's benefit condition is formalized.
