## Statement
The given variables two latent-variable models share do part of the work that
makes their correspondence meaningful, in a form that generalizes to models
whose givens are related by a morphism rather than identical.

## Source
projects/deference/note-dump-2026-08-11/notes/roadmap.md, Stage 4: "Suspicion
to investigate: the observables are doing some of the work of making sure
correspondence is meaningful, in a way that could possibly be generalized." ||
The maintainer's LessWrong posts "Condensation" (2025-11-09) and "Condensation
& Relevance" (2026-01-23): the second post says condensation, like natural
latents, establishes corresponding world-models by assuming the correspondence
of a few given variables.

## Notes
The claim has two halves. The structural half (the comparison quantifies over
latent models of one fixed random-variable model) is in the published account
and is near certain; it is the hypothesis
condensation-shared-givens of the theorem. The open half, that the role
generalizes, is what this node claims.
