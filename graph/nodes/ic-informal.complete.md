## Statement
No premise beyond the four listed is needed for a system satisfying them to
pursue resource acquisition and self-preservation.

## Source
Omohundro 2008, The Basic AI Drives, sections 1 to 6, read against the four
premises.

## Notes
A story in which a system satisfies all four premises and does not pursue the
drives is a picture under this leaf; that is where a class-level attack on the
informal route lands.
