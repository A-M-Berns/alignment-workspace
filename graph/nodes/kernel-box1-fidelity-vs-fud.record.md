## Statement
An outcome-scoring agent's margin for asking is at most 2r where nothing is
left to learn, while the fidelity scorer's margin is at least the
recognized-count weight minus the deferral cost less its compensation, for
every estimate, price and credence, at every finite day of a logical inductor.
