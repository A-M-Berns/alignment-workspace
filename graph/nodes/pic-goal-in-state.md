## Statement
The operator's correction carries no information about value but writes a goal
register the agent's utility is indexed by, so the agent is corrigible to
modifications through that register while its objective over end states is
fixed.

## Source
Hudson 2024 (a lack of resistance to having goals modified is what cannot be
captured in a ranking of end states) and Hudson 2025's transformation; the
clean-room package
projects/deference/faf-cleanroom-2026-10-02/packages/lit-mdp-corrigible,
findings F-1 (Theorem 3.1's corrigibility half is definitional under one
reading) and F-17 (the transformation is inert on a physical channel).
