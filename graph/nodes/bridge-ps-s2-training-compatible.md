## Statement
Power-seeking incentives survive the relaxation from a symmetry-invariant
reward distribution to the training-compatible goal set: an agent that learns
a goal from that set is likely to avoid shutdown in a new situation, under the
paper's simplifying assumptions.

## Source
Krakovna and Kramar 2023 (arXiv 2304.06528), abstract: "we investigate how the
training process affects power-seeking incentives and show that they are still
likely to hold for trained agents under some simplifying assumptions".

## Notes
The same paper is the content of the training-compatible-goal picture under
the shared assumption.
