## Statement
The trust principle holds under the additional condition that the teaching
channel is legitimate and not otherwise, and the agent's beliefs about which
modifications are legitimate are accurate enough, at the relevant capability,
for the conditioned principle to be the right target.

outside this repository): "some trust principle (eg the reflection principle)
holds under the additional condition of legitimacy, but not otherwise". The
accuracy clause is a reconstructed assumption, not the maintainer's words.
