## Statement
The trust principle holds under the additional condition that the teaching
channel is legitimate and not otherwise, and the agent's beliefs about which
modifications are legitimate are accurate enough, at the relevant capability,
for the conditioned principle to be the right target.

## Source
projects/deference/corrigibility-note-dump-2026-10-01/position-statement.md,
"corrigibility as legitimacy-conditioned learning": reflection is required
with respect to the plausible future beliefs, conditioned on legitimacy, not
absolutely, and capability should grow only as the agent's concept of
legitimacy improves. The accuracy clause is a reconstructed assumption drawn
from that last condition.
