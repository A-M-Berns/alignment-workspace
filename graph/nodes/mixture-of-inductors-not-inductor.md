## Statement
The wealth-weighted mixture of logical inductors is a logical inductor only if
the heavy members predict one another's prices on every efficiently computable
family, and relative to a lagging member's own deductive process no aggregate
is an inductor.

## Source
The filing session's statement about the wealth-weighted mixture of the
logical inductors of Garrabrant et al., "Logical Induction"
(arXiv:1609.03543); no public write-up of its proof exists yet.

## Notes
Neither half has a public proof; the lagging-member half is argued from the
criterion's dependence on the deductive process, and the generic-pair
counterexample is predicted, not built.
