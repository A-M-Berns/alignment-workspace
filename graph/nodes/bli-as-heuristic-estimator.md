## Statement
A Bayesian logical inductor can be read as a heuristic estimator whose
arguments are of the form that the logical inductor thinks a sentence true, and
broken down into finer arguments.

## Source
projects/deference/faf-cleanroom-2026-10-02/sources/references/bli/logseq-journals/bli-journal-2025-04-22-arc-visit.md:
"In some sense, BLI gives us a heuristic estimator
where the "arguments" are just "LI thinks this". Perhaps there is some way we
can start with a BLI and break down the arguments a lot to get something
resembling heuristic arguments?"
