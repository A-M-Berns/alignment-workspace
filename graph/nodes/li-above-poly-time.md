## Statement
Logical induction is computable but not polynomial-time, so it sits above the
poly-time boundary of the computability axis.

## Source
Garrabrant et al. 2016, Logical Induction, §5 (the construction is computable;
its running time is not polynomial).

PRIORITIES.md: "The modified market as a computable belief sequence"

## Notes
A property of the published construction. It places work at the
logical-induction level above the polynomial-time level of the computability
axis (level-poly-time).
