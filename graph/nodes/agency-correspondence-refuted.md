## Statement
Under determination of actions by policy and observation and recoverability of
the policy from behaviour alone, two agency attributions to the same behaviour
need not agree on the policy; agreement needs perfect condensation of both
models.

## Source
projects/deference/faf-cleanroom-2026-10-02/packages/udt-condense-dd/udt-condense-dd-findings.md,
item 6 ("Correspondence theorem for agency"): "Under the note's two formal
conditions (determination `H(A_i | Π, O_i) = 0`, recoverability `H(Π |
behaviour) = 0`) the conclusion is false: `agency_correspondence_fails` on four
uniform worlds with `Pol₂ = (Pol₁, one more bit of behaviour)`. Eisenstat's
Theorem 4.15 needs perfect condensation of both models".
