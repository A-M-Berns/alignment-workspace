## Statement
For the self-expert, the inductor's day-n price is an average of its day-f(n)
informed price and a residual, and the residual is the pull-back that
def-self-trust builds, so DDB's modestly-informed decomposition has a
logical-induction form.

## Source
Dorst, Levinstein, Salow, Husic and Fitelson, "Deference Done Better",
Philosophical Perspectives 35 (2021), §4: "Say that a candidate $P_i$ is modestly informed
iff their opinions are an average of their own informed opinions … along with
the (uninformed) opinions $P_j$ of the other candidates".
