## Statement
A system that selects actions by predicted consequences for a fixed objective,
whose objective gains from resources and continued operation, and which models
itself and its operators, pursues resource acquisition and self-preservation
unless a counter-drive binds.

## Source
Omohundro 2008, The Basic AI Drives, abstract: "We identify a number of
'drives' that will appear in sufficiently advanced AI systems of any design.
We call them drives because they are tendencies which will be present unless
explicitly counteracted." The four premises are a reconstruction of sections 1
to 6.

## Notes
The fourth premise separates the reading on which the drives are a risk from
the theorem, which says nothing about counter-drives.
