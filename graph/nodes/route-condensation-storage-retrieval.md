## Statement
A version of condensation that trades storage cost against retrieval cost,
without a fixed retrieval policy, carries a correspondence theorem.

## Source
Abram Demski's LessWrong posts Condensation (2025-11-09,
https://www.lesswrong.com/posts/BstHXPgQyfeNnLjjp/condensation) and
Condensation & Relevance (2026-01-23,
https://www.lesswrong.com/posts/2x9yatKKTRMabQAWq/condensation-and-relevance),
whose condensation charges retrieval cost; the route, stated from its content,
adds storage cost and drops the fixed retrieval policy.
