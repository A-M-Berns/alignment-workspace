## Statement
The system is a policy produced by reinforcement learning, and such policies
are not reward maximizers: the trained network's behaviour is shaped by reward
without the network selecting actions for predicted reward.

## Source
Turner 2024, Many arguments for AI x-risk are wrong (LessWrong): "Due to the
'RL->reward maximizing' meme, I personally misdirected thousands of hours on
proving power-seeking theorems."
