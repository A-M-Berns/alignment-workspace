## Statement
For every efficiently describable question sequence, rational t and δ > 0, the
human's soft total trust in a fallible forecaster of its own future self falls
short by at most (1 + t) times the human's expectation of min(1, |a_n −
Y_n|/δ), so composition closes under per-day accuracy.
