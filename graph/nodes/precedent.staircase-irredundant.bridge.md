## Statement
The statement of record of `precedent.staircase-irredundant` formalizes the registered claim as the registry reads it: over one pair of kinds no case of a staircase of n cases at strictly increasing strengths is redundant: the reverse decision at the i-th strength is forbidden by the staircase and permitted without its i-th case.
