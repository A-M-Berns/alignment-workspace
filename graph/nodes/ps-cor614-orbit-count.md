## Statement
In the five-state Markov decision process of Corollary 6.14 of Turner et al.
2021 with an injective reward, keeping the human's grip is average-optimal for
exactly 40 and eroding it for exactly 80 of the 120 reward permutations, and
erosion is power-seeking in the one-shot value-certain form.
