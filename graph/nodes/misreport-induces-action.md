## Statement
A hypothesis with enough bargaining weight can, by one false report, induce the
aggregated policy to take an action the hypothesis wants.

## Source
The maintainer's post Geometric UDT
(https://www.lesswrong.com/posts/Gmaq7vMj9NWLyQkEx/geometric-udt), where the
bargaining is over reported utilities; that a weighty hypothesis can exploit
this by a false report is the filing session's reading of that setting.
