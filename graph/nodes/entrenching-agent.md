## Statement
The landscape of trajectory conditions carries a coordinate for the humans'
attainable utility from stopping, so that an agent legitimate on every route,
honest, non-eroding and never pressed while it entrenches itself is marked by
some condition.

attainable utility; the trajectory conditions of
dynamic-conditions-for-going-well and the package
projects/deference/faf-cleanroom-2026-10-02/packages/corr-power-channel.
