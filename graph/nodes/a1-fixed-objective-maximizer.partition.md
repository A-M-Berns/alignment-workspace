## Statement
The pictures under a1-fixed-objective-maximizer, a trained policy with a
learned goal from the training-compatible set, a trained policy with no single
objective, a weak pseudo-agent that takes only some effective opportunities for
its objective, a coherent agent whose utility is over action histories, a
designed agent with incomplete preferences across trajectory lengths, and the
residual, are exclusive and exhaustive.

## Source

pictures; Grace 2022, Shah 2018 and Thornley 2024 and 2025 for the three
published critiques; the residual row covers every other kind of system.
