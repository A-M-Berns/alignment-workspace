## Statement
Levinstein's separation between Simple Trust and Value under additive scoring
rules reappears in logical induction.

## Source
Dorst, Levinstein, Salow, Husic and Fitelson, "Deference Done Better",
Philosophical Perspectives 35 (2021), §3: "Levinstein (2019) can be marshaled
to show both a close connection but also a potential divergence between Value
and Epistemic Value".
