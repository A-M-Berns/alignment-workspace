## Statement
For a human inductor H and an AI inductor A whose chooser satisfies REGULARITY
on an efficiently describable sequence of finite menus, soft Total Trust of H
in A implies Value on that sequence, and Value on regular menu sequences
implies the tower E^H_n E^A_n V_n ≈_n E^H_n V_n.
