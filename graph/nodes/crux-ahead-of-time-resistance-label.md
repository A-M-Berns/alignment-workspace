## Statement
An agent's ahead-of-time resistance to a modification is a corrigibility
failure exactly when the modification is legitimate, judged before the
modification against the no-modification baseline.

## Source
The label turns on two definitions, the anchor and the judging moment and
baseline of "accuracy-inducing"; the clean-room result y1_dodge in
projects/deference/faf-cleanroom-2026-10-02/packages/corr-legit-modif (a
legitimate overwriting modification dodged at P(L) = 1 with gain 9/200) and
finding F3 there (blocking the position statement's "correct iff the
anticipated modification is illegitimate" as stated).

## Notes
The crux that decides whether the overwriting-dodge risk story is a failure of
the thesis or part of it. The claim is the before-the-modification,
no-modification-baseline reading; the other pictures are the additive-variant
baseline and the reflection-conditioned reading. Settling it either way is an
admission and a reweighting.
