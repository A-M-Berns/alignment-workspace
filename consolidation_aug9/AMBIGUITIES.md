# Ambiguities

Per-line contextual judgment calls made while consolidating: places where a
source word or arrangement admitted more than one reading and one had to be
chosen. Each row records what was ambiguous, what was chosen, and why. None
changes a mathematical content.

| where | ambiguous item | reading chosen | why |
|---|---|---|---|
| interface draft, tolerance clause | "within `e` of the feasible polytope" — distance in price space or in credal-state space? | **price space**, uniform over the displayed sentences | the clause governs price states; and the required normalization then falls out of duality instead of being stipulated |
| interface draft, enforcement clause | the worldwise loss guarantee and the tolerance certification were called by the same retired noun | **two different words**: downside limit for the loss guarantee, certified tolerance for the tolerance | they are different quantities — a lower guarantee on wealth and an upper limit on incoherence — and sharing a word had already caused one conflation |
| interface draft, adequacy clause | window inequality summed by deadline alone | **release-aware windows** | the deadline-only form is not the feasibility condition; the corrected form is stated and proved in Theory 12 §4 |
| reading audit, SI-minus | write-once and the enforcement clause listed inside the set with qualifying bullets | **outside, as named conditionals** | a conditional inhabitation is not an inhabitation, and with write-once inside, the consistency hypothesis goes missing from the sufficiency statement |
| reading audit, delta ordering | "order two" for the delta list | **retained, with its two conditions stated** | the count depends on pricing one delta at its true negligible cost and setting the reserved one aside; both conditions are stated where the count is used |
| source tree, retention predicate | an earlier framing said "point-excluding versus constraint-adding" | **core-invalidating versus constraint-adding** | the earlier framing is the unsound one that `NL-N-J2A` refutes |
| source tree, transport layer | the lowercase prose term versus the frozen identifiers | **prose flipped, identifiers frozen as fossils** | the identifiers are load-bearing across documents; the prose is not |
| previous consolidation, its retired structural noun | the mechanism object versus an external proof checker | **mechanism** for the object, **proof checker** for the checker | the previous consolidation's own glossary makes exactly this distinction and states that the external phrase is not a name of its object |
| this package, theory partition | several groupings were possible | **six parts grouped by shared definitions** | discretionary; recorded as decision C-9 |
| this package, claim identifiers | renumber into a fresh namespace, or retain | **retain** | renumbering breaks traceability of the reclassification history for no mathematical gain; recorded as C-10 |
| this package, suffix letters | `X` and `T` are reused across families with different readings | **retained, recorded** | the families are disjoint so no identifier is ambiguous; recorded in `ID_EXPANSIONS.md` |
